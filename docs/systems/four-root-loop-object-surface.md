# Four-Root Loop, Object, and Surface Contract

## Purpose and status

Define how Vesper behaves as one product across **Home, Chat, Places, and
Life**: how a person contributes, how information becomes governed state, how
the product returns value, how Vesper helps something happen, and how the
result carries forward without duplicating objects or turning every root into
a feed.

This is **target product and architecture doctrine**, not a shipped-behavior
claim. It promotes stable conclusions from the three parallel research lanes
while leaving their fixture packs, implementation audits, and visual studies
as supporting evidence.

## 1. The integrated model

The four roots are four orientations toward one governed lived-world model:

| Root | Human orientation | Primary responsibility | Default posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Now | Return the most valuable current composition across the person's world | Substantial, selective, attention-ranked output |
| **Chat** | Dialogue and agency | Receive contribution and intent; interpret, compose, coordinate, operate, and repair across owners | Clean, contextual, input- and steering-oriented interaction |
| **Places** | World | Make spatial truth, current affordance, personal relationship, human perspective, and possible movement intelligible | Substantial, explorable, spatially anchored output |
| **Life** | Continuity | Organize governed evidence, episodes, relationships, Occasions, Journeys, compositions, and repair across time | Stable, searchable, object- and period-oriented organization |

The compact operating law is:

> **Chat receives and operates. Home and Places return value. Life preserves
> and organizes. The agent and consequence engine connect all four.**

This is a dominant-posture rule, not exclusive ownership. Home and Places may
open actions; Life may be corrected directly; Chat may return substantial
answers. No root becomes a clone of another.

## 2. Why four roots are sufficient

The roots cover the four durable orientations a person needs:

```text
What matters now?            Home
What do I want to give/do?   Chat
What is possible where?      Places
What exists across my life?  Life
```

Trips, Journeys, Occasions, Plans, artifacts, people, social participation,
generated media, notifications, monitors, and provider operations are objects,
capabilities, or entry paths. They do not require additional root tabs.

A proposed fifth root is rejected unless complete experiences repeatedly need
a fifth durable orientation that cannot be expressed through a focused owner
view launched from these roots.

## 3. The core compounding context loop

The product-level loop is not ingestion alone. It closes only when contribution
produces visible return and increases the person's desire to contribute again.

```text
Give, ask, point, or authorize
  -> provide useful immediate return
  -> admit only justified evidence and state
  -> recompose Home and Places
  -> help an opening, Plan, or Occasion work
  -> reconcile what actually happened
  -> organize the result in Life
  -> improve the next answer, opening, Place view, or social experience
  -> make future contribution feel worthwhile
```

Every meaningful contribution should create two forms of value when evidence
and authority permit:

1. **Immediate value** — answer, explanation, connection, prepared option,
   correction, or authorized consequence now.
2. **Compounding value** — a better future Home, Places, Life return, agent
   operation, or multiplayer experience.

Deferred value alone turns contribution into homework. Immediate value alone
reduces Vesper to a disposable assistant.

### 3.1 Session and situational loops

The meta-loop contains several loops with different timescales:

| Loop | Trigger | Core movement | Return |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Contribution and inquiry** | The person notices, has, or asks something | Chat or contextual capture -> immediate job -> authorized residue | Useful answer now; richer governed context later |
| **Daily value** | The person opens Vesper without a specific request | Home or Places -> complete-value contribution -> optional depth or action | Understanding, possibility, capability, or reduced assembly work |
| **Real-world assistance** | A condition, commitment, or plan changes | Assess -> propose or act -> monitor -> reconcile | Stable next step and verified receipt |
| **Return and re-finding** | The person remembers vaguely or wants to revisit | Life search/lens -> episode/source -> See anew or Continue | Restored context without archive work |
| **Multiplayer** | A person shares, invites, or contributes to an Occasion | Attributed contribution -> governed shared state -> coordination -> plural outcomes | Better gathering and richer future context for each authorized participant |

No single screen must display the entire loop. Context and identity must survive
the handoffs that complete it.

## 4. Stable owners, projections, and derived assessments

Vesper must distinguish durable truth from how a root renders that truth.

### 4.1 Durable owner families

The contract assumes existing or separately governed owners for concepts such
as:

- Person and relationship/audience state;
- Place and current provider/world truth;
- Source custody;
- Plan and Commitment;
- Occasion and membership;
- Occurrence and Outcome;
- Journey or other governed episode-bearing container;
- Conversation and agent turn;
- external action, delivery, and monitoring jobs; and
- versioned generated composition when deliberately saved or shared.

This contract creates no universal writer above those owners. It authorizes
cross-root read projections and handoff envelopes.

### 4.2 User language and internal evidence language

- **Artifact** is the user-facing thing: photograph, ticket, receipt, note,
  message, film, book, link, recording, or imported document.
- **Source** is the internal evidentiary role through which an Artifact,
  provider, person, or external publication supports a Claim.
- **Claim** is a bounded assertion with evidence, author, time, certainty,
  audience, correction, and expiry.
- **Composition** is a generated or assembled return based on Sources and
  Claims. It does not become historical evidence merely because Vesper made it.
- **Episode projection** is a viewer-relative organization of governed owners.
  It is not automatically a new durable object.

The interface may say **Artifacts** while architecture and provenance use
**Sources**. These are complementary, not competing nouns.

### 4.3 Derived, non-owning assessments

The following normally remain expiring read assessments until repeated fixture
and implementation evidence proves a durable owner is necessary:

- Opening or possibility;
- situated affordance;
- temporal posture;
- interval composition;
- transfer candidate;
- human-route candidate;
- Place Horizon path; and
- Home contribution candidate.

Persist the Sources, Claims, authoritative decisions, actions, Occurrences,
Outcomes, corrections, and monitoring jobs that justify them—not every
assessment itself.

## 5. Object state and movement

One object keeps one identity while roots render different projections of it.

```text
captured or referenced
  -> interpreted
  -> admitted, kept ephemeral, or rejected
  -> linked to governed owners
  -> projected into Home / Places / Life / Chat context
  -> proposed, acted on, shared, or ignored
  -> reconciled against owner and world truth
  -> carried forward, degraded, corrected, revoked, or released
```

### 5.1 Cross-root projection matrix

| Object or result | Canonical posture | Home | Chat | Places | Life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Artifact / Source** | Source custody and related owner | Appears only when newly consequential | Captured, questioned, transformed, corrected | Appears when it supports a Place relation or current possibility | Nested in the episode/owner it supports; globally retrievable through Artifacts/Sources |
| **Place** | Place/world owner | Timely Place-based contribution | Asked about, compared, or used in an operation | Full spatial, current, plural, relational, and horizon depth | Autobiographical relation and episode membership |
| **Journey** | Journey and constituent owner truth | Current phase, consequence, or substantive return | Reconstructed, changed, searched, or continued | Route and constituent Places | Stable period/episode door when eligible |
| **Occasion** | Occasion owner | Material current change, invitation, or consequence | Created, invited into, coordinated, corrected | Venue, reachability, nearby possibilities | Prospective/live/historical continuity with attributed contributions |
| **Plan / Commitment** | Plan or Commitment owner | Foreground only when attention or action is warranted | Composed, proposed, authorized, changed, or repaired | Situated against current conditions | Preserved in its relevant episode and lifecycle |
| **Opening** | Ephemeral read assessment | Primary distribution surface when timely | Shaped, challenged, accepted, or dismissed | Spatial depth and alternatives | Not durable unless it becomes a saved intention, Plan, Occasion, or later Outcome |
| **Generated composition** | Ephemeral unless saved/shared | Distributed when timely | Generated, challenged, edited, or transformed | Distributed when spatially anchored | Stored only as an explicitly saved/shared versioned composition linked to its Sources |
| **Social contribution** | Author and audience owner | Appears when it changes current value | Contributed, responded to, invited, or released | Appears when it changes Place understanding or possibility | Preserved with author, scope, precision, epoch, correction, and withdrawal |
| **Monitor / external action** | Monitor, action, provider, and destination owners | Material state change or stable next step | Requested, redirected, authorized, or repaired | Current constraint and alternatives | Commitment, receipt, Occurrence, and Outcome lineage |
| **Carried-forward receipt** | Prior evidence owner plus current consequence owner | Compact proof when a prior contribution materially changed the present | Inspected, challenged, corrected, or released | Appears when the consequence is spatial | Durable causal lineage under the applicable episode or `What carried` return |

Root projections must read owner truth. A Home card, Places path, Chat
instrument, and Life episode are not four copies of an object.

## 6. Root contracts

### 6.1 Home — temporal composition

Home answers:

> **What is worth my attention now?**

Home may be informationally substantial, but its visible set is selective. It
may combine Places, people, Occasions, commitments, Artifacts, current
conditions, social contributions, and prior episodes when the combination
creates timely value.

Home admits:

- zero or one dominant foreground;
- complete-value openings;
- material changes to something already moving;
- new source-grounded synthesis or generated media;
- social contribution that changes the current possibility;
- practical recovery or expiring consequence; and
- continuity doors whose current relevance is already clear.

Home excludes:

- a general archive;
- generic recommendations;
- every unresolved thread;
- prompts that transfer synthesis or reflection back to the person;
- duplicated Places depth;
- stable object organization; and
- content whose marginal value does not beat silence.

Home ranks by current consequence, usefulness, novelty to the person,
receptivity, evidence, authority, and attention cost—not engagement probability.

### 6.2 Chat — contribution and agency

Chat answers:

> **What do I want to give Vesper, understand, shape, change, or cause?**

Chat is functionally broad and visually sparse. Its default state should
normally contain:

- one prominent multimodal composer;
- effortless text, voice, image, location, link, and Artifact contribution;
- a small number of concrete, state-aware ways to engage;
- attached origin context when entered from another surface; and
- no generated discovery feed.

Once a turn begins, Chat may inspect, compose, propose, commit, monitor, and
reconcile through canonical owners. It returns answers, instruments,
previews, receipts, owner navigation, or silence. It does not become the
canonical database or duplicate the complete UI of each owner.

The agent may be capability-dense. The empty Chat surface must remain
attention-light.

### 6.3 Places — spatial composition

Places answers:

> **What does the world afford me here, now, or next—and why?**

Places may be as substantial as Home, but its depth remains spatially
anchored. A projection may compose four faces as needed:

1. **World** — stable identity, geometry, durable characteristics, and sourced
   explanation.
2. **Current affordance** — conditions, hours, reachability, constraints,
   alternatives, fallbacks, and freshness.
3. **Relationship** — the viewer's governed Artifacts, Occurrences, prior
   episodes, and demonstrated continuity with the Place.
4. **Human and horizon** — attributed human perspective and a sparse typed
   path to another Place, person, mechanism, event, or future possibility.

These are projection roles, not four permanent modules. Places shows a small
number of meaningful paths rather than exposing a raw graph.

The placement boundary is:

- valuable primarily because of **when** -> Home foreground;
- valuable primarily because of **where** -> Places depth;
- both -> Home previews the timely value and Places owns spatial explanation,
  alternatives, and exploration.

### 6.4 Life — continuity and organization

Life answers:

> **What exists in my Vesper world, where does it belong, and what remains
> true and usable?**

Life uses a **period-and-episode spine with contextual lenses and bounded
returns**:

```text
Life
├── Find and scope
├── In motion                    when a governed object is active
├── Lived periods
│   └── eligible episode doors  Journeys, Occasions, bounded episodes
├── Across time                 earned Place/practice/thread continuity
├── Returns                     saved, addressed, or newly useful derived value
└── Timeline · Map · Artifacts/Sources · People/category filters
```

Containers organize experience:

- Journeys;
- Occasions;
- eligible bounded episodes;
- earned longitudinal Place or practice relationships; and
- deliberately authored collections when the person creates one.

Lenses reorganize the same identities:

- **Timeline** — represented time, distinct from planned, captured, authored,
  and generated time;
- **Map** — where the person's life happened, including occurred,
  planned-only, source-only, Mine, and Together states;
- **Artifacts/Sources** — heterogeneous retrieval and custody inspection;
- **People and scope** — attributed shared cores and viewer-relative access;
  and
- **Category/media filters** — photo, ticket, food, film, book, note, article,
  audio, and other facets without rigid folder ownership.

Artifacts normally appear where they support an episode or owner. They remain
globally searchable without becoming equal cards in a universal library.

Life's bounded Returns layer is subordinate to the record. If present urgency
or attention is the reason something surfaces, Home owns the foreground. Life
owns deliberate return, lineage, source inspection, saved compositions, and
Continue.

The entrance therefore has two layers:

1. a stable, searchable period-and-episode spine whose truth and identity do
   not move with engagement; and
2. at most a small finite set of earned adaptive Returns whose ordering may
   respond to current usefulness and explicit controls.

Life primarily delivers retrieval relief, better representation, experiential
return, and inspectable accumulated understanding. It preserves the evidence
behind capability, relationship, and possibility, while Home owns current
cross-domain consequence and Places owns current spatial application.

Evidence sufficiency is operation-specific. One governed Source may support
recovery; reconstruction needs a governed container or connected supported
Occurrences; understanding needs independent support for a new relation;
carrying needs a shared mechanism and a material difference between past and
present; Together needs an authorized attributable human contribution.
Multiple traces from one event do not establish a durable pattern or trait.

## 7. The real-world engine is cross-cutting

The engine is not a fifth product surface. It compiles the current Moment,
relevant owner state, usable evidence, affected people, authority, and current
world truth into a bounded next treatment.

Its lifecycle is:

```text
inspect -> compose -> propose -> commit -> monitor -> reconcile
```

Not every job uses every phase. A practical answer may inspect and stop. A
disruption may begin at propose or monitor.

**Temporal Posture** remains a derived assessment:

```text
ACT_NOW · HOLD · WAIT_FOR_SIGNAL · MONITOR · RELEASE
```

A durable **Monitor job** may own an authorized wait for an external condition.
The posture is not itself a permanent personality, preference, or universal
Option object.

The engine becomes legible through:

- concrete state-aware Chat affordances;
- current tradeoffs and fallbacks in Places;
- meaningful changes and stable next steps in Home;
- compact receipts stating what changed, what did not, who can see it, and
  whether monitoring continues; and
- reconciled continuity in Life.

This must operate across ordinary weekends, gatherings, reservations,
transport, weather, errands, local plans, and social coordination—not only
during infrequent live trips.

## 8. Multiplayer is inside the loop

Social and multiplayer are cross-cutting, not a fifth root or engagement feed.

```text
person creates, shares, or invites
  -> others contribute with attribution and bounded scope
  -> combined context improves an opening, Place view, or Occasion
  -> Vesper helps coordinate under plural authority
  -> the real-world event produces plural Outcomes and new Artifacts
  -> each authorized participant receives a viewer-relative Life projection
  -> later Home and Places returns may improve
```

Multiplayer invariants:

- every contribution keeps its author;
- may-use, may-name, may-contact, may-retain, and may-show remain distinct;
- shared constraints may affect coordination without exposing a private source;
- one participant's Outcome never becomes group sentiment;
- withdrawal and precision changes recompile dependent projections;
- another person's trace modifies the object or possibility it changes rather
  than creating generic activity; and
- passive or inferred live friend location is prohibited without explicit,
  time-bounded sharing.

Occasion ownership remains in the Occasion domain. Home foregrounds material
state, Chat coordinates, Places situates, and Life preserves the governed
shared episode.

## 9. Cross-root handoff contract

Every meaningful handoff carries a typed context envelope rather than forcing
the destination to reconstruct intent from navigation history.

Required semantic fields are:

- origin root and origin object;
- immediate job;
- selected Resource references;
- viewer and audience scope;
- usable contribution grant;
- action-authority ceiling;
- current Moment and freshness;
- unresolved decision or question;
- target owner or capability;
- return destination; and
- expiry and correction lineage when applicable.

Examples:

- Home opening -> Places with focal Place, interval, constraints, human note,
  current conditions, and unresolved tradeoff;
- Places route -> Chat with route options, threshold, companion/Occasion,
  current conditions, and requested adjustment;
- Chat result -> Life with canonical owner readback and authorized Artifact or
  composition identity;
- Life episode -> Places with historical Place relation separated from fresh
  current world truth;
- Occasion -> Home with material change only, not a duplicate Occasion card.

The destination owns depth and action. The origin retains a return path and
refreshes from canonical readback.

## 10. Action placement and consequence boundary

### Home actions

- open or inspect the current value;
- compare alternatives;
- continue in Chat;
- open the relevant Place, Journey, Plan, or Occasion;
- save a generated composition; and
- dismiss or correct relevance.

### Places actions

- inspect current feasibility and alternatives;
- open a Place relationship or horizon;
- start or adjust a route;
- add to a Plan or Occasion;
- ask or operate through Chat;
- contribute a situated Artifact or human note; and
- share a bounded Place projection.

### Chat actions

- Ask, Point, Bring, Keep, Share, Invite, Decide, Act, and Correct;
- search, compare, shape, coordinate, operate, monitor, reconcile, and repair;
- create or mutate owner state only through the owning capability; and
- preview exact material boundaries before social, provider, public,
  financial, sensitive, or weakly reversible effects.

### Life actions

- find, browse, change lens, and inspect provenance;
- open a Journey, Occasion, Place relationship, Artifact, or composition;
- correct, detach, suppress resurfacing, release use, change scope, revoke, or
  delete through the owner;
- Continue into Home, Chat, Places, a Plan, or an Occasion with selected
  context; and
- save/share a composition without converting it into occurred truth.

When prior experience materially changes a later suggestion, preparation, or
Outcome, the consequence-owning surface may emit a compact `Carried forward`
receipt. Life preserves its prior evidence, present consequence, known result,
and correction lineage. The receipt is proof of compounding, not a claim that
Vesper caused the experience's meaning or success.

The [Contribution and Consequence Contract](contribution-and-consequence.md)
governs every retention, inference, audience, action, receipt, and causal
repair. Surface location never grants authority.

## 11. Information placement test

When a proposed unit or feature could appear in several roots, apply these
questions in order:

1. Is the person primarily **giving, asking, shaping, authorizing, or
   repairing**? -> Chat.
2. Is Vesper returning cross-domain value because it matters **now**? -> Home.
3. Is the value primarily about **where, reachability, current conditions, or
   spatial relationship**? -> Places.
4. Is the person finding, organizing, inspecting, or governing something
   **durable**? -> Life.
5. Is the unit primarily proof that prior evidence changed a current
   consequence? -> Show the compact receipt at the consequence-owning surface
   and preserve its lineage in Life.
6. Is a focused owner view required to complete the job? -> Open the owner from
   the relevant root; do not create another root.

If several answers apply, one root owns the full representation and the others
receive bounded projections and context-preserving handoffs.

## 12. Promotion and implementation boundary

The three parallel lanes may independently produce research, fixtures,
architecture audits, and lane-local plans:

| Lane | Owns exploration of |
| --- | --- |
| **Input / Chat** | contribution, interaction entry, agent capability, control plane, authority, receipts, and repair |
| **Life / Artifact** | continuity, episode eligibility, organization, re-finding, lenses, provenance, and social lifecycle |
| **Home / Places value** | contribution admission, attention arbitration, temporal composition, spatial depth, Place Horizon, and returned value |

The integration layer reviews any proposal that introduces:

- a new canonical noun or persistent owner;
- a new root or cross-root responsibility;
- a cross-root handoff contract;
- retention, inference, audience, or action-policy change;
- a generated-content truth or persistence rule;
- a user-visible multiplayer commitment; or
- production code that mutates shared owner contracts.

Working fixtures may proceed without synchronous central approval. Canonical
promotion requires a compact integration packet: decision, evidence, affected
owners and roots, conflicts, expiry, proposed files, and verification plan.

Implementation should use separate branches or worktrees per lane. Shared
canonical files are integrated deliberately rather than edited concurrently in
one checkout.

## 13. Acceptance tests

The four-root system is coherent only if complete fixture portfolios show that:

1. a contribution receives immediate value and only authorized residue;
2. the same object keeps one identity across all projections;
3. Home is substantial without becoming an archive or engagement feed;
4. Places is deep without becoming generic search or a graph UI;
5. Chat remains clean while supporting broad agentic work;
6. Life supports Find, Return, See anew, and Continue without homework;
7. Timeline, Map, people, categories, and Artifacts feel like lenses over one
   life rather than separate databases;
8. the engine is visible in ordinary life as well as live travel;
9. social participation improves possibility and consequence without leaking
   authorship, scope, location, or private Outcomes;
10. generated output remains distinguishable from human observation and world
    truth;
11. weak evidence, stale state, revoked authority, or low marginal value makes
    the projection degrade or disappear; and
12. no fifth root is needed to complete the full object lifecycle.

Before production shell migration, run these tests against at least:

- an ordinary New York week;
- a sparse/new-user state;
- a rich returned-home state;
- a live pressured situation;
- a local multiplayer Occasion;
- a Journey with mixed planned and occurred truth; and
- a social or Source revocation that forces cross-root recompilation.

## Compact standard

> **One lived-world model. Four stable orientations. One identity per object.
> Value returns through Home and Places, agency concentrates in Chat,
> continuity remains inspectable in Life, and every real consequence reconciles
> through its owner.**
