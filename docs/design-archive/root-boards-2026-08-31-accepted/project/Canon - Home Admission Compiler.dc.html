THE ELEVEN HARD GATES — REJECT BEFORE RANKING (§7.2)
G1Unresolved authorityowner, audience, custody, or truth state unsettled → out
G2Insufficient freshnessthe claim would present cached facts as current
G3Repetitionthe person already saw this and nothing material changed
G4Evidence→identity driftthe unit interprets the person, not the experience
G5Removable personanother person’s presence can be removed without changing the value — then it was never social material
G6Synthesis handed backthe unit asks the person to perform work Vesper could already complete
G7Authority overreachrendering would exercise authority the surface does not hold
G8Medium overclaimthe medium implies precision, equivalence, causality, or consensus the evidence does not support
G9No accessible alternativeno complete text/accessibility fallback exists
G10Simpler surface sufficesa push, a receipt, or the owner’s own page does the job with less attention cost
G11Silence is betterthe marginal value does not beat an emptier, calmer page
Only four contribution classes reach these gates at all — canonical state · operational instrument · human contribution · Vesper contribution — and silence is the fifth valid candidate. A generated artifact is not admissible merely because generation completed.
PRECEDENCE — WHAT OUTRANKS WHAT (§7.3)
1Safety and stranded-state recovery
2Expiring material provider state
3Affected-person decision or protected commitment
4Current physical guidance
5Grounded present possibility
6Strong semantic or social contribution
7Earned continuity
8Factual register
The chain orders classes, not units — inside a class the ranking doctrine applies. Urgent is the only posture allowed to suppress the field below rank 4.
