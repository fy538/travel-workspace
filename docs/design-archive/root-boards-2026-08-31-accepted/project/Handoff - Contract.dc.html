LAWS
1 — The tap pushes a Places-owned projection inside the origin root; it never auto-switches tabs. Owner and tab are different things: focused owner views do not require root tabs (four-product-moves decision). Task continuation is not a change of posture.
2 — The only tab crossing is the in-view door (“Open Red Hook in Places”) — the person’s own act of converting a task into an exploration. That door does switch, because the posture genuinely changed.
3 — The projection push is the minority case. Home units complete on view; scroll-past is a complete ending. Only a genuine instrument earns depth — the handoff is an exception, never the spine.
4 — The destination owns depth and action; the origin keeps the compact claim. The reason for the tap leads the destination (change claim → change register first, never buried).
5 — Provenance renders as one quiet label (“FROM HOME · …”), navigational, never a content card. Back restores the exact origin scroll; the claim refreshes from canonical readback — seen is a state, not a badge.
6 — Motion is the ordinary Workspace Transition within the stack; anchors (interval, constraint, Sources) persist. No cross-root animation exists to design (continuity-motion L5 stands untouched).
RULED · 2026-08-29 · FOUNDER
Push, not tab-switch. Tapping a Home proposition opens the Places-owned projection inside Home’s stack — like a notification opens the message, not the Mail tab. The Places root stays an orientation posture the person chooses; the in-view door is the only crossing. The one accepted cost: a pushed instrument is a deliberate dead end — it cannot widen to World Field except through the door.
