ADMISSION — ALL SEVEN, EVERY TIME
May-use permits this purpose · may-name permits the attribution · the trace is relevant here · it adds information or possibility, not decoration · nonresponse creates no debt · live location is never inferred from stale history · revocation or correction recompiles the projection.
Scope is the grant, never the trip. A note shared for “planning with me” does not silently become public expertise.
REJECTED — PERMANENTLY
Friends-nearby surveillance · a global friend activity feed · popularity counts as authority · “Mara saved this” without why it matters · synthetic claims about what a friend would like · social proof that pressures a decision · private contribution converted into public expertise.
