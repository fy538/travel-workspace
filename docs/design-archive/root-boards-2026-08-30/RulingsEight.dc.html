CANONIZATION · STEP 1 · THE EIGHT RULINGS · ALL RULED 2026-08-30 (FOUNDER)
Eight small doors to a clean canon
Every open board-level question, ruled in one sitting. Seven adopted as recommended; ruling 1 amended by the founder — the world read gained its voice back. The canon pair inherits these answers.
1 The world read — two tiers RULED · AMENDED BY FOUNDER
Home opens with its voice. Two tiers: the mono anchor (place-day stamp, freshness right) plus a two-line serif read at mast scale in the standfirst voice — a read, never a greeting: something true about today (“Storms this afternoon. Saturday opens clearer.”). Uses the registered mast role; no new type. One voice moment per page: when a posture’s dominant is itself a voice statement (Quiet, Returned · Day 0), it promotes into the read and the Now region yields. Places keeps the compact band except at genuine cold start (anatomy doc §3.2 stands — its objection was masts that describe the product, not reads that say something true).
2 Crownless Quiet RULED: ADMISSIBLE — REQUIRED, EVEN
May a Home posture render no crown at all (Quiet’s Now as a voice read on bare paper)? The containment law answers it: a card marks something you can complete here — and a calm statement is not finishable, so a crown would lie. Quiet keeps the Now region; it drops the container because the container would be a false promise. The crown returns the moment something is genuinely finishable.
3 Maya’s note: card or row? RULED: OBJECT CARDED, DOOR ROWED
The boards disagreed: carded evidence object on Place Focus, uncarded row on Home. Both are right, because they render different things. Where the note itself appears — author, quote, audience, inspectable — it is an evidence object and earns its container (Places, Life). Where Home’s With-people region references it, that is a door to the object, and doors are rows. Same law as “depth is not containment”: the object carries the card; a pointer never does.
4 Verdict-register containment RULED: UNCARDED IS THE LAW
May a Place verdict (“the arrival shapes the afternoon”) ever take a container? Only at conviction grade — and the treatment doctrine already rules conviction deliberately producer-less: an emitted conviction would be fabricated certainty. So the verdict renders uncarded serif until the evidence system can genuinely earn conviction, at which point the raised material comes with it. No board-level exception.
5 Envelope staleness RULED: ADOPT THE PROPOSED DEFAULT
The destination always re-reads world truth on open; the projection envelope carries intent and evidence, never cached facts presented as current. Freshness lines belong to the destination’s own read, not to what traveled.
6 “Seen” is canonical RULED: ADOPT THE PROPOSED DEFAULT
A claim seen on the phone does not reappear on any other device. Seen is a state on the canonical object, not a per-device flag — the temporal form of “one object, one owner.”
7 Tab-bar icon language RULED: DEFER, WITH AN OWNER
The four-root icons on the boards are placeholders and should stay that way — icon language deserves its own pass in the dedicated design environment during production design (Step 4), alongside Brand Identity’s parked iconography work. Ruling needed only on ownership and timing, not on glyphs today.
8 56pt illustration plates on Home RULED: RATIFY
Home’s Horizons rows carry 56pt illustration plates — the pre-pivot 44/56 row-plate gradient extended to a surface that never had plates. It is exactly the C6 fix the old canon drew and never shipped, and it is what keeps uncarded Horizons editorial rather than administrative (presence law: uncarded + imagery = editorial). One constraint carries over: 56 fits the row floor; 92 never will.

All eight closed. The posture matrix beside this board already renders the amended read tier and the merge rule; the full-fidelity Home boards carry it too. Next: the Places completion board, then promotion — one accepted decision doc + two surface contracts. Nothing is waiting on a ruling.
