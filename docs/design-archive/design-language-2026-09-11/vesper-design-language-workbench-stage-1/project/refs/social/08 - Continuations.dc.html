THE EXTENSION, EXACTLY
The September 5 amendment already gives a casual share with no Place two homes: a Status doorway on Home while featured, and Life · People after. 08 extends that permitted treatment in one step: the fuller original itself arrives once in Home’s addressed region when the share’s audience includes you (Friends eligibility as the arrival condition), under the same two-item rule 04.2 uses. That is the whole delta. It is not evidence that nonspatial social material had no home, not a new feed, not a social root, and no root is reopened. Place-bound shares still open in Places, and nothing is forced onto a map.
THE ORIGIN STACK
08.2 is a Life-owned source opened from Home, so it keeps its Home-origin stack: the tab bar stays on Home and Back restores Home’s scroll. It becomes a Life page only if the person deliberately crosses roots, which 08.3 is. The alternative, Places · From friends without a pin, is fair for a person who lives in Places, but it asks a photograph of pasta to stand near a map.
THE PULL ROUTE
Life · People · Nora holds everything between the two of them, attributed both ways, and Life search finds it by its words (04.5’s grammar). Nothing is written by looking. This is the recommended durable route; Home is the arrival, not the archive.
WHAT THIS DOES NOT DECIDE
The Friends audience remains the friends-audience proposal; this board assumes it as a fixture. The retention agreement behind “Keep this photograph” is proposed, not adopted, and no grant is implemented here. The Home review (September 8, “with people” kinds) keeps the addressed region for contributions addressed to the person and relocates casual status to Places by default; whether a friends-audience original arrives as addressed material or as the featured-Status doorway is Home’s call, and the delta above is written so either answer is a small change.

Every photographic plate on these boards is a labelled stand-in, and the frames therefore argue that a friend’s original is the whole benefit while showing nothing worth looking at. The four that matter are named on their plates: the pasta (Nora’s phone, Thursday 7:40 pm), the table (Maya’s phone, Saturday 8:25 pm), the Print Room (Maya’s own visit) and Dana’s ragù (Sorrento, Wednesday). The authored words beside each plate are real fixture material and carry what they can meanwhile.
