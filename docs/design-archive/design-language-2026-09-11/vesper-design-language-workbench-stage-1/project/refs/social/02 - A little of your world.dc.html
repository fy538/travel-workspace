RECEIVE AND LEAVE IS COMPLETE
02.3 ends after Maya’s photograph. No reply, save or summary is asked. The scope is the ledger’s cast (Maya, Priya, Dana) on the Places project’s own composition; the wider world sits under it so Red Hook is worth opening even with three friends.
TWO RECIPIENTS, TWO FRAMES
02.5 and 02.6 start from the same photograph. Reply is the reference’s in-context composer with Maya named at the send boundary. Ask is a Chat frame whose chip names Vesper and says Maya sees nothing; it answers from the listing and the gallery’s notes because the ledger’s scoped-use assumption (§8.3) is not taken here. Board 05 draws the granted variant beside it.
SHARING WITHOUT A PRELUDE
02.2 is a friend sharing from the object itself: Maya, from the Print Room’s page, one line, who, Send. No Chat first, no Occasion, no connection to accept. The audience is chosen per share; a send crosses an audience boundary and the one clear Send carries it. 02.1 stays as the private answer that keeps nothing.
THE SENDER
02.7 keeps R5’s exclusion: no “used in” report. Maya sees her share, one reply, Edit and Take it back. On board 04 the same page is where withdrawal happens.
NOT DRAWN
B0 (“thinking of Saturday”) is board 05’s D1 pair. A Paris/Rome contrast is omitted per the ledger. The KEPT receipt with Undo after 02.2 is the Home project’s kept-row form and is not repeated here.
