WHAT SURVIVES CLOSING, AND WHAT SHOULD NOT

The customer sees three things: “Draft from 12 minutes ago”, the actual unfinished words in the field, and Discard. Specification, not copy: a read-only answer closed → nothing kept (J1d); an unsent draft → recovered with its target on reopening, discardable; an unadopted alternative dismissed → gone, asking again regenerates it; an accepted change, then leaving → stays accepted, closing a sheet never undoes (J2f). The same rules apply on return from Maps, a provider page (05 D5), and backgrounding (J2e).

Proposal, not policy: where a draft lives and how long it lasts (on-device, per plan, about a day) is a design proposal to reconcile with the lifecycle contract and Chat ownership (07 · Q14). It is not an unlimited-retention promise, and no durable artifact or personal claim is created to make resume work.
