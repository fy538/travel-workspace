THE RECOMMENDATION IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Keep the itinerary’s body; change what it can hold.

Every arrangement — a loose Saturday, an eight-day trip, a shared evening, a day changing under people’s feet — is the same document: an identity block (kicker · serif title · one thesis line), rows with a hanging mono time gutter and a serif title, temporal chapters only when a day has real parts, and a date rail only when there are several days. Four things are new and small: times you can read (12pt), a complete Roman chapter opener, a preview card (Quiet Paper with a dashed planning-ink edge) for every not-yet-real alternative, and a one-line receipt with Undo where Undo is true. People appear in three places only — header avatars, one “with …” phrase on the row it belongs to, and attributed material attached under a row. Precision is weight and a word, not a legend: bold mono for a provider or agreed time, regular for our plan, a muted word (“after”, “option”) for a loose interval, and the source named in gold when a bold time comes from a ticket or confirmation. Exact-time dependencies and loose intervals sit side by side without borrowing each other’s model, and a sparse day is allowed to be three lines long. Assistance comes through one contextual pattern (08): an “Ask Vesper” pill or a tapped row opens the half-sheet with the target carried, leading with what the place is and what matters; the same field answers a question, returns an alternative, applies a clear private change with a receipt, asks one question only when ambiguity is consequential, previews a material effect, or delivers the supported part of a request with the boundary stated once — and the page returns clean. Private entry is the same for every role; direct controls stay only where the choice is genuinely bounded.
