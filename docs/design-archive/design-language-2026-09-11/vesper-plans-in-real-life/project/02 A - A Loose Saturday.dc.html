RULING 2026-09-05 · THE SAME GRAMMAR, ONE STEP LIGHTER

When no row carries time meaning, the time gutter is dropped and the rows are a plain list in the same serif and supporting-line type — the treatment Life already uses for “kept in mind”. The gutter returns the moment one row has a time or a loose word (“after”, “sometime”). No sequence is implied that isn’t real; no status buckets appear. §0.6: the collection is a complete useful state — keeping possibilities carries no scheduling obligation, so the page issues no “say a day” instruction; asking stays available through the pill, and Home or Places may surface a justified opening when one becomes useful.

Below this — a single kept place, a Move — there is no plan page at all: the thing lives on its owner (Place, Home, Chat) with a receipt.

RULING 2026-09-05 · “OR” IS A WORD IN THE ROW
