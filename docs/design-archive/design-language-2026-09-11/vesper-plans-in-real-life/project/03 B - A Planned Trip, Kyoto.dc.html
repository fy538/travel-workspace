PRECISION WITHOUT A LEGEND

Four registers in one gutter: 09:02 bold — a provider departure; 10:15 regular — our plan; “after” / “option” muted words — loose intervals; 19:00 bold — a reservation. Where a bold time comes from is shown two ways: the train carries its ticket stub (the kernel’s evidence plate, §11.7), and the reservation’s “from your confirmation” is a door to the evidence — gold text with the arrow, per the Door law (§11.1). Provider truth is attributed and inspectable, never implied.

Neither register borrows the other’s model: the loose afternoon is not given a fake 13:00; the train is not softened into “morning”. Transfer detail (platform, 35 min) stays in the supporting line — available, not a second plan.

The sparse Day 7 that follows is one row. It is finished, not empty.
