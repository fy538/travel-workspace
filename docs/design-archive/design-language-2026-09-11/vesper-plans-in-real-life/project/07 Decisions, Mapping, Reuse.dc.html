§7 COMPOSITION DECISIONS · OPINIONATED RECOMMENDATIONS
1 · Primary organizationSequence whenever there are timed things (rows in time order). Chapters (Morning/Afternoon/Evening) only when a day has two or more real parts — never to fill a day. A short undated composition (identity block + rows, no rail, no chapters) for a single arrangement such as a Saturday. Internal status (settled / flexible / open / changed) is never the reading order; the status-grouped CurrentShapeSurface composition is not adopted. Evidence: 02 A2 vs A8, 03 B1–B2.
2 · Current vs possibleA possibility sits in the sequence where it would happen, as a Roman-weight serif title with a supporting line that starts “Option ·” and a gutter word instead of a fabricated time. Not a separate “ideas” section, not a dimmed second-class row, not a card. When a friend suggests it, it is attached under the row it concerns until adopted (04 C3). Evidence: 02 A2, 03 B2, 04 C3.
3 · Read vs act REVISED §0.1–0.2The plan is for understanding, anticipation and coordination; contextual assistance — one recognizable Vesper entry — is the general route for questions, exploration and changes. Resting: the document plus one “Ask Vesper” pill (text or voice); no composer, toolbar, field system, permission dashboard, or second assistant door in the header. Active: tapping the pill or a row opens the existing half-sheet with the target carried, leading with value (recognition · practical context · who), a private kicker that is the same for every role, direct chips only where the choice is genuinely bounded (the two jazz sets) or after expressed intent, and the field. Resolving: a read-only question gets substance with no mutation or receipt; a clear authorized private change applies with a receipt and no preview; an exploratory ask returns an alternative; consequential ambiguity gets one question with the known implication (a conditional consequence, not a stage); a material effect gets the preview; a request beyond what the plan executes gets the supported part in place with the boundary stated once. A contributor asks privately and may then choose to send a suggestion to named people; an expressly opened shared door starts shared. Resolved: the clean page with one transient receipt. “More room in Chat” is an optional expansion of the same assistant, returning clean. Board 08.
4 · PrecisionWeight and a word, no legend. Bold mono = a provider time or an agreed shared time, with its source named in gold in the support line (“from your ticket”, “reserved · from your confirmation”, “agreed”). Regular mono = our planned time. Muted caps word (“after”, “option”, “later”, “~4:30”) = loose or estimated. Personal fixed point, agreement and reservation are stated in words only when they differ (05 D4). Evidence: 03 B2, 01 specimen.
5 · PeopleThree places, no more: header avatars for who shares the arrangement; one “with …” phrase on the row where participation differs; attributed material (mono name · kind, then their words or a 72pt photo) attached under the row it concerns. Editing capability shows once, in the chapter label (“Afternoon · with Maya”) and in the collaborator’s own thesis line. No per-stop avatars, RSVP controls, counters or attribution at rest. Evidence: 04 C3–C8.
6 · ChangeThe row moves in place; a one-line receipt (mono on gold wash for private, on ink wash for boundary-crossing or non-events) says what happened, with Undo where Undo is true. Alternatives are one preview card with keeps / drops / adds in a sentence and the resulting rows — not a side-by-side diff of the whole page. History stays behind “···”. Evidence: 02 A5, 03 B5, 05 D3–D6.
7 · ContinuityThe arrangement rests in one owner projection whose title is a sentence (“Dinner with Theo, and maybe jazz”). Home, Chat, Places and Life each show a pointer in their own composition — a card, a coordination card, a row on the place, a list row — that mounts the same action and opens the same page; Back returns to the origin. No Arrangements tab; roots stay with their lanes. Evidence: 02 A6, 06.
8 · Aesthetic densityThe row budget from the itinerary contract holds: time · title (≤2 lines) · one supporting line · one quiet affordance, plus at most one attached thing. Beyond that, detail moves to the sheet. Compared at equal scale (01 R1 vs 03 B1) with the same content, the proposal is denser in information (readable times, complete opener, attributed sources) and lighter in chrome. Sparse days shorten rather than float (03 B2 Day 7).

THE PLAN IN SEVEN SENTENCES · RULED 2026-09-05 · KERNEL §11.15 · REPLACES R-1–R-20, PR-1, PR-2
1The plan is a page you read. It is always current when you open it. (Itinerary is the default; structure earned upward and downward — §11.8 amendment.)
2There is one way to change anything: say it — the pill, Chat, or voice. A few direct controls remain only where the choice is finite and real — the set picker (02 A3), Tuesday/Monday and whole-morning/temple (08 P3–P4), Use this/Not now (02 A4), Send/Not yet (05 D3), I’m in/Can’t (04 C2), Undo. Discoverability (2026-09-05, from the gulf-of-execution research): before any answer the field’s placeholder carries one contextual example in the person’s own words (“Ask, or ‘move it to 10’”); after an answer, one “Or say …” line offers the next plausible phrase; never a command list, never a tutorial. “Something else…” is gone everywhere — the field stays.
3If it is yours, it changes, with one receipt sentence and Undo. If it is someone else’s, your words go to them, and you see exactly what they will see before you send. Two roles; a grant is “yours, for the afternoon”; a guest is the same page with fewer parts. Deferred (founder, 2026-09-05): whether, with two people and no named decider, either may resolve loose material. Until walkthroughs show the asymmetry matters, the owner decides and the other person says it in words.
4Everything that happens to the plan is a sentence on the row it concerns. No feed, no recent-events region, no status chrome. A fact carried from an earlier occasion is one attributed sentence with a door to its origin, never a statement about the person.
5When you come back, at most one sentence at the top says what needs you. A cue reaches you only for that, bundled per arrangement per day. A change made by a person is told by that person, in their words.
6Vesper states facts. It recommends when asked, or once, in one sentence, when a change defeats what you said you wanted. A recommendation carries no button — except that when acting on it means sending words to people already involved, one door may open that message, already addressed and editable. The person sends. It can be turned off. (amended 2026-09-09)
7If something did not go through, it says so once and keeps your words. Pending is silent; unknown behaves as pending; “nothing changed” only when known. The states live on appendix 90 and 92.
§0.7Amendments from the post-subtraction refinement (2026-09-05). Relaxation of accepted wording (applied; recorded alongside the proposed amendments, not as unchanged canon): sentence 2’s discoverability addendum said one “Or say …” line follows an answer; it is now selective — kept only where it reveals a non-obvious capability (08 P2b: a change can follow a question; P9a: a suggestion can be sent as a question), removed from P3, P4, 09 J2a, 02 A4. Compatible with the seven sentences (applied): sentence 3 — the host is not a courier: a known change is carried into a draft message that leads the preview — recipients, words, the one consequence sending does not settle, and an action named for its effect, “Update & send” (05 D3, §0.8 B); sentence 4 — say each thing once from the same state (11 I1 thesis corrected); planned vs happened — an account of what occurred does not rewrite the plan (11 F1). PROPOSED exceptions, each needing a new decision before adoption: sentence 6 — one possibility (not a recital) may follow the implication, and where adopting it means telling people, one quiet text door prepares a proposal to the people affected — a message that changes nothing until they answer (11 I1 → I1b → I1c, labeled PROPOSED; §0.8 E defines the effect and contrasts it with D3’s owner-instructed update); sentence 7 — “pending is silent” governs the resting page; the active interaction acknowledges a submission (appendix 90 J2c shows the form); sentence 5 — per-day bundling is a non-urgent default; a distinct urgent change is not suppressed.

Sept 9SELECTED · sentence 6’s prepared-message door (decision 2026-09-09). Adopted in full, covering 11 I1–I1c and 09 J2f under the existing recommendation conditions; not permission for unsolicited outreach or a button on every recommendation. The door opens private preparation: it does not send, change a Plan, accept participation, contact a provider or establish another person’s answer, and recipients and disclosed material must be eligible. Sent proposals stay proposals and replies alone adopt nothing; an owner applies an authorized change separately, and 05 D3’s Update & send stays the distinct instructed route. Still separately proposed: guest identity and delivery, retention, and board 12’s following terms. The request that produced this, retained for the record: The accepted 2026-09-05 decision says plainly: “Vesper says less; people must ask more,” and “no recommendation carries a button.” Later strategy expects practical preparation before continuing help is accepted. The request: allow one obvious contextual continuation to be offered as a door when it saves the person retyping a decision they have already expressed and re-coordinating people already involved — drawn as 11 I1’s “Prepare a proposal for Maya and Sam” → I1b → I1c. Bounds, all of which stay: Send is always human and the words are editable; nothing changes the arrangement, provider truth or anyone’s participation by being prepared; one door, never a set; no command catalog, recommendation dashboard or unsolicited management; recommendations still appear only when asked or once after a problem. The trade, in work rather than buttons: without it Nora retypes a decision Vesper has just stated and re-addresses two people it already knows (one composition, two recipients, one send); with it she reads a prepared message and sends or edits it (one read, one send). No prompt or check is removed elsewhere. The same choice on 09 J2f: Vesper names that Ben’s move lands after the quieter window and stops there; raising it with Ben means the person types the request and the message is composed from nothing (one reconstruction, one composition, one send). The amendment would offer that single continuation as a door with the message already addressed and editable (one read, one send). Both routes end in a human Send, neither touches Ben’s authorized edit, and sending a question changes no arrangement and no one’s answer — the difference is a reconstruction the person no longer performs. Kept distinct from 05 D3’s Update & send, which follows an owner instruction and does change the arrangement. The exact wording, if adopted: “Vesper states facts. It recommends when asked, or once, in one sentence, when a change defeats what you said you wanted. A recommendation carries no button — except that when acting on it means sending words to people already involved, one door may open that message, already addressed and editable. The person sends. It can be turned off.” Nothing else in the seven sentences changes. Three answers: adopt as written (11 I1–I1c and 09 J2f lose PROPOSED); adopt narrower — only where the person’s own request produced the result, which keeps I1 and drops J2f; or decline, leaving sentence 6 as it stands and the frames as a rejected option. Social receives: while pending, 03.5 keeps the owner-instructed change as its one direct action and keeping-and-telling stays words in the entry — the two never stand as equal defaults, and no independent default is adopted meanwhile.
