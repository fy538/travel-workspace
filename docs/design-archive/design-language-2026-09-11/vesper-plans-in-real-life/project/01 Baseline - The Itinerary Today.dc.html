STRENGTHS TO PRESERVE

One continuous paper document. Kicker → 31pt serif date → one-line thesis → rail → day rule → chapter → rows. The serif stop title leads each row; the mono time hangs in a predictable gutter; one supporting line; one quiet “···”. Floating chrome is compact (back · people · chat/filters/map capsule). Warm, restrained, no photo needed.

OBSERVED ISSUES

Chapter prose clipped mid-sentence (“…while t…”, “…for the ki…”) by a two-line clamp (numberOfLines). Times at 10.5pt are decorative microtype for essential information. Italic 13pt for the opener sits under the 17pt voice floor the repo itself documents. “OPEN” as a gutter word for an undecided stay works — that grammar is kept and extended.

OBSERVED · DAY IDENTITY MISMATCH

The heading says Monday, June 22 while the rows directly beneath (Honen-in 11:00, Dinner — Pontocho 19:30) belong to the previous day; Day 6’s own rule appears further down. One capture cannot say whether selection, scroll offset or overlay timing causes it — investigate before inferring a fix. The composition rule this family adopts (03 · B6): the header names the selected day and the day being read whenever they differ.

Also visible: repeated oversized gaps below the fold once rows lose supporting lines. Sparse rows should tighten, not float.

MATERIAL · WHAT THE FAMILY USES, AND HOW OFTEN
PaperThe page. Rows and sections are flat: alignment, proximity and hairlines do the grouping. No paper-on-paper for ordinary content.
Quiet Paper (quietPanel)Only for a bounded object that benefits from containment: the invitation as guests see it (C1), the resolved grant (C5), the “changing seven o’clock” note (D2), Home/Chat pointer cards. Card fill, hairline, r14, no lift.
Preview card PROPOSEDquietPanel + a dashed planning-ink edge = “not yet real”. Used for every private or scoped alternative (A4, B5, D1, D3, D7). Never for accepted content.
Raised paperOnce: the selected-place card over the map (B3). Sheets and floating chrome use the overlay vocabulary (blur, capsule, soft square).
Receipt bandOne mono line on a gold wash (private change) or ink wash (something crossed a boundary / a non-event), with a sans sentence under it and, where true, Undo. Never stacked; never a feed.
ColorGold: kickers, chapter labels, attributed source facts, the “Open …” door. Planning ink: preview edges and external-link chips. Oxblood: once on 05 (the real mismatch), once on 02 (a tight fit) and on “Let it go”. No amber for optionality; no violet anywhere.
