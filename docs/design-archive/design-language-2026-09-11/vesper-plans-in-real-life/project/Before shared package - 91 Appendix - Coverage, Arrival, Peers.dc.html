THE RULE

Order by consequence: first what needs the person (gold dot), then facts that changed and still matter (planning-ink dot), then what resolved or was done by others within their scope (ink dot). Within a class, newest first. Only the top item carries an observation and one continuation; the others are one sentence each, and each is a door to the row it concerns. Three lines at most; the rest fold into “and N more →”, which opens the same list, not a new screen. Events that resolve together are one line (N2). A line disappears when its row has been opened or the thing it asked for has been done — never on a timer.

The §0.5 hierarchy (result → observation → continuation) is the one-event case of this rule. Without it, a real Saturday would have rebuilt the task panel §0.5 removed. Status: illustrated; the rule is written on 07 as R-19.

WHAT THIS BOARD ADDS TO 07 · TWO RULES, TWO PROPOSALS, THREE QUESTIONS
R-18 · ArrivalA cue carries the receipt sentence and its one observation, titled by the arrangement, one per event that changes a relied-upon fact or asks something; resolved facts never cue; several within an hour group. Home shows the arrangement’s state plus at most one sentence about what changed. Opening lands on the plan at the recent region; no interstitial.
R-19 · StackingThe recent region orders by consequence: needs-you, changed-and-matters, resolved/done-by-others; newest first within a class. Only the top item carries an observation and one continuation. Three lines, then “and N more →” to the same list. Events that resolve together are one line. Lines leave on the person’s action (row opened, thing done), never on a timer. §0.5’s hierarchy is the one-event case.
PR-1 · Two peopleProposed. With two participants and no named decider, either may resolve loose material (held pair, loose time, optional stop) with attribution and mutual Undo; commitments keep their maker. Multiplayer §5.2 applied to authority.
PR-2 · PeersProposed stress case. In an arrangement everyone shapes, loose material changes freely with attribution; a thing everyone agreed to changes as a proposal to those affected, adopted by their answers, never by silence. No vote count, deadline or approval surface; the D3 register carries it. Scoped to explicitly agreed times.
R-20 · OutcomeAn Outcome surfaces on a later arrangement as an attributed supporting fact with a door to its origin, scoped to that arrangement and removable in a sentence — never as a statement about the person or a preference. The Plan shows it the way it shows a ticket stub; the record itself is Person/Relationship-scoped.
Q20 · NotificationsWhich engine decides channel, grouping and quiet hours for R-18 cues; the existing decisioning holdout applies. What “last seen” means per person for the recent region.
Q21 · AuthorityFounder ruling on PR-1 and PR-2; how “no named decider” and “everyone shapes this” are set without a roles screen (a sentence at creation, as C5 did for Maya’s grant).
Q22 · Outcome recordScope and provenance of an Outcome; which outcomes a later arrangement may surface and to whom (audience axis of the contribution contract); how a wrong carried fact is retracted at the source, not just hidden here.
