THE CITATION GRAMMAR
Numbers are web sources, in the order they first appear; the sources line resolves them. A face is a person: ink for a friend, umber for you. A friend’s words are always in italics and always carry her face. The listing is a number too, with its as-of. A sentence with no marker is Vesper’s own conclusion, and there should be at most one per body.
PARAGRAPHS BY JOB
Each paragraph carries one thing and, usually, one marker: the setting; the kitchen; hours and the walk; reserving; who has been; what to do. Two to four lines each, never one. Length grows with what is known, never to fill.
WHAT IS TEMPLATE, WHAT IS MODEL
Paragraph one is the cached web brief. Paragraph two is assembled by code from structured facts and verbatim quotes. Paragraph three may be the dossier’s verdict, or a sentence the model derives only from what is already cited. Markers are attached by the code that placed the sentence, never by the model.
