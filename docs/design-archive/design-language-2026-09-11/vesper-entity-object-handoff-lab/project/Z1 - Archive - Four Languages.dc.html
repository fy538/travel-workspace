WHAT THE MATRIX SAYS
No single language wins both rows. The reading: the plate’s chrome (it is the shipped shell and the most reviewable), the ledger’s slot typography (mono labels carrying provenance, serif only for speech), the instrument’s top (map thumb plus a grid of current truth and facts, which is what makes the sparse page a real object), and the letter’s voice reserved for one place: the interpretation. That hybrid is the hypothesis board 04 draws on three kinds.
WHAT SPARSE TAUGHT
A page with no words needs a shape. The map thumb and the known/unknown grid give Café Aurora a body without inventing a sentence; the letter and the ledger both end up saying “nothing here” in different fonts. Authored emptiness is not a nicer placeholder; it is a different top of page.
