A garden restaurant on the Sorrento cliff, set among lemon trees a few streets back from the piazza. People go for the courses rather than the view, which comes free with the table.12

The kitchen runs a set sequence in the evening. The provolone course is the one the regulars talk about; the fish is whatever came in that morning, priced accordingly.2

Worth reserving on weekends. On a weeknight you can usually walk in before eight.1

Dana was here in August and left a line for you: “the table at the edge, order whatever they caught, nothing else.”D You kept it a week later for the lemons.F

Book the edge table, go on a weeknight, and let them run the courses. The edge has the same view as the seats inside and the better light.

THE PLACE VS MY HISTORY
The page answers “what is this place, and what do I and my people know about it now”. Life answers “what has happened between me and this place”. The page shows one line of that and a door; Life shows the record and a door back. Neither pulls the other in.
LABELS AND RETURN · TO COORDINATE WITH LIFE
Proposed: the page’s door reads “Your history here”; Life’s reads “Open the place”. Each returns to exactly where it was tapped. The Life lane owns the record’s anatomy; this lane owns only the door and the one-line read.
