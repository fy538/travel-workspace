THE FOLD · 812
THE PAGE IS SHORT ON PURPOSE. NO PROGRESS METER, NO EMPTY SECTIONS, NO WARNING BANNER, NO BADGE.
THE LAW · TWELVE RULES
01Seven slots, one order, always. Identity → current truth → you → people → interpretation → actions → facts → stop. Density changes emphasis, never order. The same order on every kind.
02Above the fold is the decision. Everything a person needs to decide sits before 812pt; everything below is elaboration reached for only if the decision is not yet made.
03Absent, not empty. A slot with nothing renders nothing: no header, no placeholder, no “no reviews yet.” Shortness is correct. The sparse page ends above the fold.
04Each slot has one owner. Identity is Vesper’s canonical record; current truth is a source with an as-of; you is yours; people are named, by grant; interpretation is Vesper’s and only if authorized; facts are the listing. Nothing crosses from one slot into another’s voice.
05Current truth is a row, not a badge. Operating, open-now, hours: one line with its source and time. Unknown stays unknown; the only thing it may offer is an explicit check. It never implies tomorrow from today.
06Never generate to fill. Opening a page never produces a Take. A persisted Take renders; a stable brief renders; neither, nothing. Opinion cells (order, skip, energy, peak) live inside the interpretation or not at all.
07People sit above the interpretation. A friend’s line outranks a dossier and a listing at that place; it carries its grant and its relevance sentence; it is never merged, counted or ranked.
08Verbs are content, not chrome. Save is a reference verb and lives in the top bar on every kind. Forward-motion verbs (add, book, ask) sit inline directly under the interpretation — above the facts, never in a floating foot — and in the same position on every kind.
09Ask once. One Ask on the page, in the rail. Not a pill and a disc and a link.
10Facts are rows with provenance. Price, walk, reservation, hours: label, value, and a source that can be revealed. Cuisine and price come first because they are the only things the catalog reliably knows.
11The hero is honest. A real photo when there is one; authored emptiness when there is not; never stock, never a gallery of one. Photos are near zero, so emptiness is the default state to design, not the failure state.
12Stop means stop. No “more like this” unless a real similar exists; no onward links that die with a dossier; no world section as a stand-in for having something to say.
WHAT THIS CHANGES AGAINST TODAY
Hours leave the bottom of a collapsed drawer and become the second slot. Cuisine and price, which the wire already carries, become the first facts. The rail lands in the same place on venue, site and experience. Ask appears once. The world section, the opinion cells and the URL-carried reason go. The people slot is new.
WHAT IT KEEPS
The taper and the fold rule from the 08-04 canon. The inline rail and the reference-verb top bar from the shell decision. Omission safety. The itinerary strip as a mode that flips add to edit (it sits with the actions when present). The mapper’s refusal to invent.
THE TESTS
On the sparse fixture the page ends above 812 with nothing generated and no empty header. On the rich fixture the decision is reachable above 812. Every slot’s content can be traced to one owner. Removing any single data source removes exactly one slot and nothing else.
NOT SETTLED HERE
How the slots look (board 03 draws four languages). Whether container and spot kinds use the same seven slots (board 04). The people slot’s states (board 05). Where the itinerary strip sits when the page is in-plan, and what the resolving cue looks like before identity is known (board 06).
