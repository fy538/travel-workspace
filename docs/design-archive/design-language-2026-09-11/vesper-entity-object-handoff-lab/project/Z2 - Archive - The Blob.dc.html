WHAT THE PARAGRAPH MAY DO
Describe the place from the web; carry the dossier’s judgment when there is one; state today’s facts (open until, the walk); name who has been and quote them in their own words, in italics, attributed; end with a recommendation that follows from all of it. Never invent a fact, never paraphrase a friend as if it were Vesper’s view, never rank people, never say “you love places like this”.
PRESENCE, THREE WAYS
1 the strip is the cleanest read and the weakest link between a face and a sentence. 2 chips inline bind name and face but interrupt the type. 3 margin marks keep the prose unbroken and still point at the sentence. My pick is 3 for the page and 1 for the card on the map sheet.

WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE LAW
The seven slots become inputs to one paragraph rather than sections of a page: identity and the plate stay; current truth, you, people and interpretation compose; actions and facts follow as before. The rules survive intact — one owner per input, cite don’t absorb, absent stays absent (Café Aurora’s paragraph has no one in it) — they now govern a sentence instead of a box.
THE VERBS, AFTER THE PIVOT
No “Add to trip”: no trip picker, day picker or review sheet. Keep is the top-bar reference verb and is the pin. Under the paragraph, two text verbs always — Ask Vesper (Chat) and Leave for someone (the directed act, already dark in code) — and a third, Tonight?, only while an Occasion is live on Home. Absent, not empty, applied to actions.
WHAT IT COSTS
The paragraph is generated, so the “today” facts and the quotes must be injected verbatim by code, not by the model, or a sentence about tonight can be wrong tonight. The cached web brief is the model’s part; the rest is template. That is the same split as the composition decision, and it is what keeps a generated blob honest.
