THE ARRIVAL, QUIETLY · CORRECTED 09-04: EXPLICIT READ UP, NOT QUEUED BY OPENING
The person taps Read up (an explicit, idempotent request; nothing runs on a GET) and the brief lands seconds to a minute later. While it runs, the verb reads “Read up is on its way”; if it fails, “Try again”. When it does: one line above the body says so with the source count; the body swaps from the honest sentences to the cited paragraphs; the sources line fills. The plate, name, byline and pair do not move, so nothing jumps under the reader’s thumb. The line is gone on the next open.
WHAT THE WEB MAY PROMOTE
A web-sourced fact may rise to the pair only when the listing has none, and it says so: “from the web [2], not the listing”. The live check stays offered. Price stays unknown until a source says otherwise; the body never invents one.
LARGE TEXT RULES
Body and values scale; kickers, source lines and markers’ labels do not (they are labels, not reading). The pair stacks to one column at the accessibility size and so does the closing row. Faces scale with the text they sit in. The where thumb keeps its size; the address scales. Nothing truncates.
FIXTURE HONESTY
Café Aurora is the brief’s synthetic fixture; the two sources and the bakery copy are invented; the opening time is invented and labelled as web-sourced to show the rule, not to claim it.
