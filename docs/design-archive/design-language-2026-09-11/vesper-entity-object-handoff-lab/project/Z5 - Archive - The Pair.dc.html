WHY THESE TWO
A person opening a place page is deciding whether to go now. The two facts that answer that are whether it is open long enough and whether it is close enough. Price and table matter after the decision. So they end the page.
FROM YOU, HONESTLY
Reach uses your location as an explicit one-shot at open, never tracked (the spatial rule). The source line says so. Walk and drive are both shown because the second changes the answer in a hill town; the isochrone is a radius by rule. When you are not in the town, the column measures from the town’s anchor and says so.
