READING THE TWO COLUMNS
Struck-through blocks do not render on the real path; amber blocks render thinner than designed. Of eighteen: 5 ship as drawn, 6 degrade, 3 are dark, one needs wiring, 3 are gated off for ordinary viewers. The dark ones — the Take, the brief, Order/Skip, the Details — are the opinionated ones. What survives for an ordinary viewer on a real venue is: name and type, a photo or nothing, an itinerary strip if trip-scoped, the rail’s two verbs, a map, the Ask block, and the world links. No price, no cuisine, no hours above the fold, no opinion.
THREE DEPENDENCIES, NOT THIRTEEN
The dossier cluster (06, 11, 14): one missing dossier removes the brief, the narrative and every onward link — a page with no voice and no exits. The schema cluster (09, 12): one backend TODO. The wiring cluster (03): no data at all; the strip reads state that is not venue-specific.
THE MEASURED WORLD (LOCALHOST, 08-13)
Venue n=1134: type 100%, cuisine 49, price_range 43, hours 17, brief 12, photos 0, phone 0, website 2. Site n=116: hours 76, brief 23, photos 0. Experience n=57 is the rich kind: description 70, when 63, image 33. Accommodation: spec sheet, brief 0. Place/neighbourhood n=911: off the shell entirely. No universal body template exists; each kind’s one reliable field is different.
WHAT THE PAGE SAYS ABOUT ITSELF
Its own components have already ruled twice: SpotPlanningRail is “deliberately not fixed-position and not a floating rail”, and the mapper refuses to “present plausible defaults as facts.” The page is honest in code and dishonest in review, because review has only ever seen the mock.

WHAT MOVED SINCE THE 08-04 CANON (ITS OWN §8)
Blocking · the mode key. Curator Takes are unique on (entity, mode); pages ask for researching / reviewing_plan; only post_trip was ever generated (105 rows, three days in July). No stored Take reaches a browse page. Decision · personal Take withdrawn from the default view: it streamed into the most prominent block behind a 22-second deadline, reading as an answer nobody asked for. Shipped · site is a third face on the shell: hero always an illustration, accessibility as free text, no planning rail, no details drawer. Plumbing · status notice + entity envelope: two strings (“may be permanently closed”, “closed right now”) over one row of projections; envelope carries identity, lineage, geometry, brief, status, tail — venue and site migrated, experience and accommodation not.
WHAT THE ENTITY-SHELL BRANCH CHANGED (codex/entity-shell-resolution, READ 09-02)
The count moved. Eighteen render units and four action ladders today, not fourteen and two. Added since 08-04: a catalog-evidence disclosure (internal cohort only), an always-on Ask Vesper block with a 52pt disc, a Leave this place for someone action (renders only for viewers with a confirmed pair), and a photo-attribution caption. Order/Skip no longer reads the phantom fields; it parses the Take body for a “what to order” heading — so it is dark for a different reason now.
Three implementations, three orders. Venue is a bespoke 1,570-line route. Site renders through the newer generic page: seven units, no add / book / ask verb although the presentation carries those tokens, no hours, and a finished interpretation block that nothing imports. Experience shares the shell but puts the planning rail last, below the map, contradicting the rail’s own comment; it has no status notice and can never reach the not-found state. Accommodation stays outside on purpose.
Hours are not nulled — the canon line is stale. They pass the mapper and format correctly. What is wrong: they render as the last row of a collapsed drawer, the drawer’s tuck line announces them third, the status object’s own hours are never read, and the site face has no hours at all. The most decision-changing fact on a restaurant page is the least reachable thing on it.
The wire already carries what the page ignores. Cuisine, price range, price per person, duration and reservation-required are mapped and shown nowhere on the venue page. Status as-of and sources are never rendered. The presentation’s per-fact provenance (catalog / adjudicated / research, observed-at, expires-at) is thrown away at the mapper. Three Take suggested-actions have handlers and no button.
Owner-private is invisible. catalog_state produces no notice, badge or banner. Its single effect is the map surface: a provisional shell renders a provider tile, never Mapbox — which means the app already derives a map-surface rule from lineage even though the wire carries none.
The pin peek card disagrees with the page. On the Places map sheet, Ask Vesper is a tertiary text link and the rule is one primary button; on the object page it is a pill in the rail and, separately, a disc block. Same verb, three treatments.

VERDICT · WHERE THIS PROJECT STARTS FROM
KEEP · IT IS RIGHT AND IT SHIPS
The taper. Identity at the top, elaboration below the fold, and the rule that everything above the fold is the decision.
The inline planning rail. Verbs directly under the interpretation, never a floating foot; verbs survive when the opinion fails.
Omission safety. A null fact renders nothing; the details drawer hides itself. This is the one behaviour that made the real path honest.
The itinerary strip as a mode. Not a slot: it flips the page from a pitch to a record, and it is the only door back to edit.
The floating top bar as the only floating chrome.
The mapper’s refusal to present plausible defaults as facts.
KILL · IT WAS DESIGNED FOR DATA THAT IS NOT COMING
The fourteen-block body as the default page. It assumes a rich catalog; the catalog is name, type, cuisine, price. Design the short page as the default and let richness be the exception.
Order / Skip, energy dots, group fit, peak, pairs-well-with as blocks. They wait on a schema nobody has scheduled; if an opinion ever arrives it belongs inside the Take, not as cells.
The photo-first hero. Photos are near zero in every kind; the current hero is a one-photo scroller pretending to be a gallery.
The streamed personal Take on page open (already withdrawn).
WhyForYou carried in the URL. The referral’s reason belongs to the surface that recommended it, not on the object.
“In Vesper’s world” as an onward-links block that dies with the dossier.
MISSING · NEVER DESIGNED, NOW REQUIRED
Current truth as a row with a source and an as-of, distinct from the listing, distinct from the Take; unknown stays unknown.
The people section — a friend’s line, its grant, its relevance sentence — and its states (one, several, withdrawn, blocked, outside the circle).
The sparse owner-private shell as a first-class state, not a degraded rich page.
Container and spot as kinds on the same shell: a neighbourhood that contains, a person-made place with only its owner’s material.
The hero when there is no photo, designed as authored emptiness rather than a fallback.
Continuity from the map sheet past full and from a candidate tap, with the resolving cue and back to origin.
Share constrained for owner-private shells without the page feeling disabled.
The starting position, in one sentence: design the five-block real page first — identity, current truth, one interpretation if authorized, a few facts, the verbs — make it beautiful at that length, add people as the sixth section, and treat every richer block as something the page earns from data rather than something the shell promises.
