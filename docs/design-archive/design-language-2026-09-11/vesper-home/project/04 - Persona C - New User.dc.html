The shortcut on 04.2 and 04.6 now names its doorway beneath it (“Opens Chat · read for the answer, not kept”), and 04.3’s line carries the same clause. The admission sample on 04.3 lost the reserved violet (ink, as on 09) and its labels are at 10px. Gold doors on this board are the shared Door construction (vdl.css .vdl-door).
