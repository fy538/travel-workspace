THE RULE (REVISED 17.2)
When the last open is more than a few days back, the read names the one thing worth receiving and the page leads with it: a note in a friend’s words, a grounded possibility, or a complete finding. “Since you last looked” then renders once, as compact rows, one per material change; then they are ordinary rows or gone. Orientation does not consume the experience; the archive’s tidiness is never the promise.
WHAT QUALIFIES AS A CHANGE
A commitment resolved (the refund paid), an import finished, an occurrence that happened to someone in your rows (Dana’s Sunday), a withdrawn source (as an honest line). Not: things Vesper composed while you were away. Ignored suggestions leave no trace.
THE CHAIR
The waiting row’s dashed seat is the one open chair, the decision that is yours. It is not a badge and it does not count down. On Friday it is filled and the row says so.
