Vesper — Home
A Home-only design project. Selected 2026-09-09: the generous 02/03 compositions, a benefit-led read on a nonurgent open with weather in support, and 15.3’s receiving-first learnability through ordinary doors. Not adopted by that selection: the three proposed kinds on 01, Rule B on 08, and any claim about supply economics. The current boards in reading order, four reference boards, — the parts, three people’s weeks, the wedge, Places, the ledger — and four archived baseline boards. 02 and 03 open with the selected current scrolls (ordinary, quiet, and a thin-context unit study; day zero, the return after absence, and the trip day, on one Life-record fixture) and continue with their history; the alternatives that lost and the tests live on 07. Everything on every phone is a fixture.

The selected scrolls on 02 and 03 reappear inside their 08b sequences by design; nothing else is drawn twice.
