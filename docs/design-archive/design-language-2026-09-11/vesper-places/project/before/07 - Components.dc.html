EXCLUSIONS NARROWED · SEPTEMBER 9
Two universal exclusions are withdrawn: “surfacing what you were not looking for is Home’s job” and “never explain why a note matters”. The Product Model (§3) admits causal, historical, cultural, relational and counterfactual understanding on Places. The restraint stays: the flood mechanism, the access comparison and the returned understanding are the forms it takes; nothing explanatory is added everywhere, and social placement is unchanged.

NOTES
How to read this sheet
WHAT IT IS
Every piece the seven boards use, rendered once at phone width from the same generator that draws them (docs/working/design-gen/places/kit3.py and the instruments in gen19v3.py). The origin beside each name says where the piece comes from: Home’s kit, the a26e3228 canon’s kinds and instruments, or Places itself.
WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a design system and not a token rollout; nothing here is shared with Home, Chat or Life by being drawn here. The proposed Places-scoped tokens remain proposals in the response doc.
HISTORICAL, NOT CURRENT
The a26e3228 specimen sheets, The Page of August 8 (f4eee076) and the Entity lab’s invariant-body page (dd48304b 09, 11, 12) are references this sheet drew from, not current rules. The current rule for what a place page reads is the selected column of the table on 08 (purpose-responsive reading under an invariant shell). Where a drawing here and one there disagree, 08 governs.
RULES CARRIED
Photo or hatch, never a drawing. One instrument per unit, its labels off its lines, at the 10px mono floor. Serif names, mono facts. Solid discs for people, ink numerals for map places. Text arrow doors, one primary where the content calls for one. No design doctrine inside a phone.
