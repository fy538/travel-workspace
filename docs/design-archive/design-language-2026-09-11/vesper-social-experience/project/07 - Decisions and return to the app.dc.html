Casual sharing. Maya, on the Print Room’s own page, writes one line and chooses who sees it (02.2). No prelude, no occasion, no connection to accept. Enjoyable receiving. She may reply (02.5), ask privately without Maya knowing (02.6), or close it. An offline possibility, optional. On Saturday the afternoon is her only free stretch, and Maya’s reason for going is what makes four o’clock worth using: the side room empties after four, the shop shuts at five, twenty minutes home by 5:10, the pasta from six. It appears once, with the cost said plainly, and it can be left (03.5). If she goes, Maya is not told; if she does not, Maya is not told either. The equally valid ending. She enjoys the photograph on Thursday and does nothing else, ever. No reply, no Keep, no reciprocal photograph, no next gathering. Maya sees one reply if one comes and nothing otherwise (02.7). That ending is not a failure of the design, and 05 D7 says so on the board.
