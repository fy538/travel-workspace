THE REVISION
“What two people made visible”, “may-use”, “featured status · city precision” are gone. Each contribution is a person, a sentence in their own words, a place, and a picture when they took one. The pictures are illustrations, tagged as such on the plate; no real person’s photograph is implied.
DENSITY WITHOUT A FEED, AND NOT ONLY ADVICE (§9.7)
Twenty-odd contributions from six people across nine places, grouped by where. Not all of it is advice: Maya’s pier picture with two words, Alex’s first loop without stopping, Priya’s pigeons, Dana back at the bookshop where she met someone ten years ago, and two different experiences of the same recital side by side. The recipient gets enjoyable awareness and easy reasons to reconnect, not only better recommendations. Priya’s Print Room line is labelled as her own separate visit, never as a comment on Maya’s share.
WHAT STAYS OUTSIDE THE PHONE
The grants, the precision each person chose, the fact that nothing is where anyone is now. Those remain true and are recorded on 08; the phone does not recite them.

THE EMPTY SCOPE
Red Hook from friends has nothing. The phone says so in one sentence and gives Red Hook anyway, because the person’s intent was Red Hook, not the absence of friends. The way back to friends across the whole city is one door.
TWO WITHDRAWALS
A decorative share leaves without a word: the collection recomposes and nothing marks the gap. The exact item, reopened from a saved link, shows a short unavailable state. When something relied on the share (the Sunday sequence used Maya’s bakery note), the dependent unit is repaired under the correction contract and says in one line what still holds and on what authority. Quiet does not mean unrepaired.
WHAT WAS CORRECTED FROM THE FIRST EXPORT
The blanket “nothing else is recomposed” claim. Independently supported material survives; dependent explanations, ranking and practical consequences are recomputed. The sequence here is the worked example.
