THE REVISION
Internal copy is gone from the phones. The world packet is twelve things across food, music, outdoor, low-cost social, indoor and one spatial discovery, and the subjects are now named (§10.4): Playtime on the lawn, Reich’s Music for 18 Musicians at the hall, Bach on the organ. Illustrative subjects, not real programming. The newcomer here has no network: the From friends row says nothing is shared yet, without faces, a count or a demand (§10.3); the populated variant is on 04.
THE THREE LEADS, ON ONE EVIDENCE
B leads (§9.2): a recognizable possibility with atmosphere, time, cost and an easy onward action, and the spatial distinction folded in where it improves it: the pier right there faces west, sunset is 7:04, the harbor view is the other pier another night. A keeps the discovery as the lead for comparison. C is the exhibition, which must earn the lead with its plans and does not. Understanding supports the possibility here; it does not compete with it.
