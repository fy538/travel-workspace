WHY THIS IS THE WEDGE
Positioning is group coordination, not an AI planner, and the invite loop is both wedge and growth. Trip invites ask for a commitment; a friend’s city asks for a look. The map artifact carries the product’s taste (his words, real places, no ratings) before any account exists, which is what the exit-artifact work found converts.
THE LINK, HONESTLY
A link is an audience of “whoever holds it.” The five-axis grant applies anyway: use (read), retention (until revoked), inference (none), audience (link holders), action (keep, reply, useful). The sender sees that sentence before sending, can take the link back, and revocation blanks the page. No profile, no other city, no follower relationship is created.
WHAT SHE IS NOT ASKED
To install, to sign in to see it, to reciprocate, to allow location, to accept anything. Reply travels by the channel she already has with him. “This was useful” is optional and is the only thing that reaches him. No open-receipt; a link opened is not a signal.
OPEN · NOT DRAWN
The join flow itself and how the link’s grant re-binds to her account; whether pins without a line (the piazza) can be sent at all (this board says no); expiry defaults; what a forwarded link should say to a third holder; and the Brooklyn version, where twenty-eight pins would need reach and time (Ambient 7) before they are worth sending.
