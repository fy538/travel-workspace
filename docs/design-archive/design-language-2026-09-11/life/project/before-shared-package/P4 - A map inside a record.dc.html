P4 · A map inside a recordReview
The map, when it earns its place.
Where does a map appear in Life — and when does it stay away?
Proposed: the map is a conditional organ of a record, never a root or a world view — three optional layers (the plan’s route, evidence points with their own coordinates, a kept path), rendered only when spatial structure says something. One pin is a door, not a map.
Decisions: (1) adopt the admission test — two or more supported places with movement, a plan that was walked and diverged, or a kept path; otherwise no organ, no slot, no nudge; (2) adopt planned-vs-occurred as the map’s reconciliation (the dossier already owes that block); (3) route to the contribution contract: may a path recorded inside an active Occasion be kept afterwards as a Life source with custody, release, and deletion? Recommended: adopt 1 and 2 as design; 3 is a deliberate amendment to the contract’s location rule — per-Occasion consent, expiring, kept only by the person’s act. Drawn schematically; the basemap follows the existing Places map policy.

Where the layers come from
THREE LAYERS · ALL OPTIONAL
The plan’s route — from the Plan lane, for any trip, day trip, or gathering that had one. Nothing new is captured.
Evidence points — self-sourced coordinates already in the record: a photo’s own location, a ticket’s two ends, a reservation’s venue, a scan.
A kept path — tracked only inside an active Occasion with consent (per-occasion, expiring), and kept afterwards only by the person. Provider dots never.
Absent layers are air · the map opens in Places with identity preserved
