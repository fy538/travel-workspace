00 · Start hereVESPER — LIFE / CURRENT PRODUCT · SEP 6 2026 · FOURTEEN BOARDS
Life makes what Vesper has held from living findable, understandable, and useful again.
It is the continuity root of Home · Chat · Places · Life — neither a feed nor a passive archive. One corpus, four lenses; every held thing opens the same way and opens on what it gives; originals are always one tap from the thing they support; refinding is target-first; a friend’s words stay theirs and show first; contributing returns value before it asks for anything. It must work for an ordinary week as well as a rich journey. Nothing here asks the person to file, review, answer, or complete. Current design is not shipped design — implementation status lives in repository receipts.
