RETURNS
For cacio e pepe, “creamy” is partly a stability problem Beyond the emulsion you noticed: the cheese proteins clump when the water is too hot or too thin in starch — so “creamy” is the sauce holding together, not just fat blending in. That is why it sometimes breaks. It says nothing about what any kitchen did. To see it, if you ever want to: two plates from one pot, one finished on the heat and one off it — the off-heat plate should stay smooth. FROM THE SOURCE YOU SAVED · AFTER THREE PLATES
