What opens when I tap a night inside Bushwick, or a day inside the coast — and do I have to?
The dossier grammar is fractal: a row opens the same page one grain down until depth ends at the object. Exploring is one way there, not the only way — a chip in a drawer or a search result opens the same object directly, and Back returns to the door you used.
Decisions: (1) a night’s page does not list its sibling nights — the way back to “twice at the same door” is the PLACE row; (2) “where it lives” lists every containment whichever door you came through, and Back returns to that door. Recommended: both as drawn.
