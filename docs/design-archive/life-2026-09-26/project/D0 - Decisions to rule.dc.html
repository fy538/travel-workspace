What “release” means
Is releasing something the same as deleting it, and does releasing an intention mean what releasing a source means?
04.4 (Your originals; Withdrawn) · 04b.11 and 09 (Delete)
AOne meaning for both: release means it stops being used, and the original stays yours and findable until you delete it. Delete means gone.
BRelease means the original is no longer held (board 39’s reading), which makes release and delete nearly the same.

A
Three consequences, two words, no overlap. It matches what 04.4 already draws, “Release a source from use” beside “Delete permanently”, and board 10B. B would change retention policy to make one word simpler.
