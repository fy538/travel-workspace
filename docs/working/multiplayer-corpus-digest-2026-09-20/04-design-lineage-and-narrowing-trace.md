## DOC 1: Multiplayer Prominence in Home and Places (2026-09-01)

`/Users/feihuyan/travel-workspace/docs/working/claude-design-multiplayer-prominence-exploration-brief-2026-09-01.md`

### A. Social values and experiences proposed

- **Executive thesis (§2).** The strategic claim: "Vesper helps people give one another better access to places, possibilities, and shared experiences without requiring everyone to become a planner."
- **People as sources (§2).** Another person's situated attention, judgment or evidence changes what the viewer can understand.
- **People as participants (§2).** Who is involved changes what is feasible, protected or open, and who must authorize a consequence.
- **People as relationships (§2).** What was deliberately shared may create a later opening without merged identity or permanent permission.
- **Distributed attention (§3.1, Aug 18 lineage).** One person's trace changes another's encounter "without requiring co-presence, simultaneous use, or a shared Plan." The same section lists:
  - borrowed lenses;
  - asynchronous shared attention;
  - routing curiosity to another person;
  - divergence without consensus;
  - shared open loops;
  - recurring relationship patterns without averaged taste profiles.
- **The trace as social object (§3.2, Aug 21).** A trace may be kept private, addressed to a person, used as one bounded lens, contributed to an Occasion, carried into a governed consequence, or "explicitly elevated into a public Place contribution." These are separate transitions.
- **AI mediates relevance, not intimacy (§3.2).** Vesper cuts the cost of recall, search, timing and coordination. The person keeps the choice to initiate and the work of care and repair.
- **The whole Occasion lifecycle (§3.3, Aug 23).**
  - Before: invitation, social accessibility, a truthful way in.
  - During: presence, shared agency, the interface receding.
  - After: plural Outcomes, afterglow, a clean ending.
  - Later: relay, ritual, a second Occasion or earned silence.
  - The guiding line is "Invitation is not incorporation."
- **Object-native circulation (§3.4).** "Social material enters through a person but lands in the Place, Plan, Occasion, Source, or possibility it changes."
- **Five value families (§4):**
  - shared perception (§4.1);
  - social accessibility (§4.2);
  - shared agency (§4.3);
  - relationship continuity (§4.4);
  - the Place commons, exploratory and deferred (§4.5).
- **Social units (§5).** Pair, recurring circle, ephemeral party, host and guest, family or household, and solo, each with its own opportunity and risk.
- **Contribution is broader than artifacts (§6).** The nine kinds are intent, presence, perspective, initiative, situated judgment, practical labor, care and boundaries, commitment and evidence, and meaning and continuation.
- **Five social questions Home can answer (§10).**
  - What has someone given me?
  - What are we doing together?
  - What changes because of who is involved?
  - What could I open with someone?
  - What did we create or carry forward?
- **Contributor efficacy (MP1 questions; §15).** The contributor sees what their note changed: "contribution consequence as efficacy rather than engagement."
- **Withdrawal as real experience (MP5).** Withdrawing, narrowing, blocking and clean endings must be real, without making privacy a maintenance chore.
- **Final standard (§25).** Make perceptible "what another person helped me see, what became possible because of our relationship, what their contribution changed, what we are causing together."

### B. Named concepts, and what "causal, not decorative" means concretely

- **Central visual hypothesis (§9).** When a person materially changes understanding, possibility, action or continuity, they must appear "in the unit's primary semantic hierarchy rather than relegated to metadata."
- **Counterfactual test (§9).** "If replacing the named person with 'someone online' loses nothing, the social attribution is decorative."
- **Three-treatment ladder (§9).** Every board compares the same evidence three ways. The goal is to find when the third treatment is honest, not to apply it everywhere.
  - Metadata social: avatar, attribution or count.
  - Supporting social: a secondary row or strip.
  - Causal social: the contribution changes the dominant understanding, route or decision.
- **Concrete examples (§6).**
  - "Maya is bringing dessert," not "Maya added an artifact."
  - "Daniel's ferry knowledge changed the return branch," not "Daniel likes Red Hook."
  - A private constraint removes the late option without exposing who supplied it.
- **Reasoning unit (§2).** `person × relationship × Place × situation × Outcome`. The shared-life law is "Shared place, plural experience, governed common action."
- **Four projection lanes (§7).** These are `shared_core`, `audience_lane`, `personal_overlay` and `system_assessment`. Viewers see the same canonical truth with different elevation, explanation and actions, described as "a relaxed shared-view model, not inconsistency."
- **Five authority axes (§7.1).** Use, Retention, Inference, Audience and Action. "Audience alone does not authorize reuse."
- **Continuity gradient (§4.4).** The steps run from addressed, to Occasion-bounded recognizability, to optional private remembrance, to mutual or directional reachability, to confirmed recurring relationship, to dormant or ended. "Remember privately," "keep in orbit," "make me reachable," "invite again" and "share contact" each authorize something different.
- **Places relationship scopes (§11.1).** `For me`, `With Maya`, `For Friday dinner` and `From people I trust` are temporary, bounded scope handles over one World Field. They are not tabs.
- **Four admissible Places social forms (§11.5).** Attributed evidence, relational relevance, plural comparison and participation consequence.
- **Home prominence amendment under test (§10.5).** "multiplayer may own any Home region, including the crown."
- **`people_status_aperture` (§8.1, §23 q9).** An accepted Home specimen. The open question is whether it is a "useful opening or a disguised activity feed."
- **Board suite (§12–13).**
  - MP0: diagnosis.
  - MP1: pair handoff. Maya's ferry judgment changes Saturday's route.
  - MP2: relational opening. Status plus two separately kept waterfront traces.
  - MP3: one World Field under three scopes.
  - MP4: the Occasion seen through Places, with host, guest and contributor projections.
  - MP5: withdrawal, narrowing and block.
  - MP6: component disposition.
  - MP7: optional Place-commons specimen.
- **Reuse candidates (MP6).** Attributed evidence lane, social cause line, contribution consequence receipt, affected-people row, relationship scope handle, viewer-relative shared-state instrument, relay/handoff Door, and withdrawal treatment.
- **22 evaluation gates, G1–G22 (§18).** They include G1 Materiality, G7 No debt, G14 Earned prominence and G18 Fullness after removal.

### C. Cut, gated or deferred

- **Place commons (§4.5, §13 MP7).** "Do not make this a primary production recommendation… one clearly labeled horizon specimen only." It is called "a strategic horizon, not a V1 commitment," and is attempted only if MP1–MP6 stay coherent.
- **Social units (§5).** Recurring circle and family or household are de-prioritized. The brief "should prioritize pair, host and guest, and one ephemeral Occasion." The reason given is that "Pair-first value makes multiplayer visible in ordinary life without requiring several people to adopt the app simultaneously."
- **Value families (§4).** The brief requires "at least four of these five," so a family can be dropped. The commons is the obvious candidate.
- **"Prominent" must not mean (§1)** more social cards, facepiles, a permanent With-people chapter, a friend map, an activity stream, an invitation-growth module, forced group setup, a new notification class, public reaction metrics, or equal social density everywhere.
- **Rejected patterns (§17).**
  - Global friend activity and friends-nearby surveillance.
  - "People you know liked this" social proof and popularity as authority.
  - Synthetic claims about what a friend would like.
  - Merged pair or group taste, and compatibility scores.
  - AI-authored group meaning.
  - Occasion-specific profile labor, polls and preference homework.
  - Nonresponse read as rejection.
  - Automatic shared memory from attendance.
  - Private contribution converted into public expertise.
  - "a fifth social root hidden inside Home or Places."
- **Automatic contextual identity (§3.3).** Rejected. The target is "human-authored Status or no identity treatment."
- **AI-written intimate outreach, match scores, automatic Occasion creation and reminders after ignore (MP2).** All listed as hard failures.
- **Scope of authority (§0).** The brief does not authorize production implementation, schema work, a new root, or changes to the four-root model.
- **Validation (§19).** "Do not declare user validation."

### D. Ambitious ideas beyond pair coordination

- **Public Place commons (§4.5).** "A person may eventually leave a bounded, attributed contribution with a Place." It lists lens, access observation, identity correction, resident or expert interpretation, warning, and a question inviting plural perspectives. The reward: "useful downstream consequence and refinement, not likes or creator reach."
- **Strangers and residents in public correction (MP7).** "resident or institutional correction."
- **Trust scope wider than one friend (§11.1).** The `From people I trust` scope.
- **Distributed attention as the multiplayer thesis (§3.1).** "multiplayer as a consequence path, not a visibility default."
- **Household and care asymmetry (§5).** "Ignoring care, age, representation, or account asymmetry" is named as a risk.
- **Rituals and recurring circles (§4.4, §10.4).** "Our Place or shared ritual becoming useful again."
- **Mandates, delegation, private caucus and subgroup split (§4.3).** Shared agency that goes beyond two people.

### E. Constraints that caused narrowing

- The four-root model is fixed and no social root is allowed (§0, §17).
- Anti-feed, anti-surveillance, anti-profile-ritual stance (§0). The map "must not reveal live presence or infer availability" (§11.1).
- "Output before input" (§14.4). No reflections, rankings, relationship explanations, profile upkeep or performed gratitude.
- "Silence as an acceptable result" (§1). Ignore and decline complete silently (§16).
- The visual kernel is frozen (§15): no presence dots, hearts, likes, streaks, follower counts or large profile cards.
- Evidence is held constant across comparisons (§14.2). No new components from a single board (MP6).
- Pair-first, because several people will not adopt the app at once (§5).

## DOC 2: Social Aperture and Shared World handoff (2026-09-02, amended 09-06)

`/Users/feihuyan/travel-workspace/docs/working/claude-design-social-aperture-and-shared-world-exploration-handoff-2026-09-02.md`

### A. Social values and experiences proposed

- **The missing intention (§1.2).** "What is currently available through my people, what do we have in motion, and what could their deliberately shared lives open for me now?"
- **Six unowned intentions (§1.2).**
  - What have people deliberately made available lately?
  - "What are my friends noticing in Paris, Brooklyn, Shanghai, and Rome?"
  - Which Places matter through them?
  - What do Maya, Alex and I have in motion across unrelated Occasions?
  - "What have I made visible, to whom, and for how long?"
  - "Where are my friends, at the precision they deliberately chose to share?"
  - "I have no task. Let me browse my social world and find one real opening."
- **Three risks of fully distributed social (§1.2).** Multiplayer invisibility, where the app is "perceived as personal AI with occasional collaboration". Compiler dependence, meaning no way to pull. And no social mental model.
- **Multiplayer across four product moves (§2.2).** Make sense, Open possibility, Help it work, Carry forward. "Do not reduce multiplayer to either social discovery or group planning."
- **Social value precedes social labor (§2.5).** The surface should first return "a human perspective, a new connection, a spatial opening, a useful shared state, a vivid shared record, or a currently possible experience."
- **Sept 6 refinement (§1.4).** Enjoyable awareness, easier human initiation, and less unwanted work between wanting to meet and actually meeting. These are "independent benefits, not one conversion funnel."
  - "A friend's ordinary photograph may be worth seeing because it is theirs."
  - Four social-benefit overlays: an ordinary share with no reply, a directed perspective, a human-chosen gathering, and its adaptation to reality.
  - Test a recipient with little app investment, and an organizer whose unwanted work is actually reduced.
- **Social translucence (§3.6).** "the current doctrine may under-provide visibility in its effort to avoid social pressure". The answer is visible, attributable, bounded social state.
- **Pull-based attentional agency (§3.8).** A user-invoked view as an alternative to Home ranking.
- **Outward-sharing inspection (S7).** The author can see at a glance "what is visible, to whom, and until when."
- **Multi-party privacy (§3.7).** Subjects and co-participants need ways to object and correct.
- **Sparse, dense and silent social worlds as designed states (S9, §7.5).**

### B. Every shape, and its fate

The five competing information architectures (§5):

- **IA-0, distributed-only control.**
  - Shape: no new surface, only stronger entry points and cross-root doors.
  - Failure signature: the user asks "Where do I see my friends?"
  - Fate: a control that must get a fair treatment. The Sept 6 amendment says it must get "a strong discoverable pull path, not a deliberately weak control."
- **IA-1, Life People with one present-tense aperture.**
  - Shape: the stable People record plus one bounded door, "Available from people now →".
  - Failure signature: current Statuses dominate and Life reorganizes on every visit.
  - Fate: a credible alternative.
- **IA-2, focused Shared World owner view.**
  - Shape: a non-root, user-invoked surface covering available from people, what you share outward, active shared motion, a permissioned people-and-Places map, doors into relationship records, and person search or invitation as secondary infrastructure.
  - Failure signature: a card feed, an inbox, a directory, or a second Home.
  - Fate: the leading hypothesis before comparison (§6), explicitly "not a decision."
- **IA-3, fifth-root Together counterfactual.**
  - Shape: navigation parity with Home, Chat, Places and Life.
  - Failure signature: users cannot tell Home from Together.
  - Fate: a counterfactual to be given its best honest treatment (S11). It is not authorized.
- **IA-4, Places-led social world.**
  - Shape: no Together page. Places gets a `From people` relationship scope, plus Status and Occasion layers when they are spatially meaningful.
  - Failure signature: non-spatial social forced into geography.
  - Fate: a credible alternative.

**Later fate, per the Sept 7 banner on Doc 2 and Doc 3 §1, §4 and §5.**

- The aperture alternatives "do not reopen the accepted root split". They are kept as provenance only.
- Doc 3's placement table puts casual authored places in Places as `From friends` and addressed value in Home. Life · People is "Not a present-tense activity feed."
- "Do not start a fifth-root redesign from these older boards."
- In effect the outcome is IA-0 plus IA-4, with no Shared World owner view and no Together root. That last sentence is my inference from Doc 3's placement table. Neither document names IA-0 and IA-4 as the outcome.

Other named concepts:

- **Social aperture (§1.3, §2.4).** "a bounded, user-invoked social aperture that makes the currently permissioned social world inspectable without becoming its own engagement economy". It is an index or lens over existing owners and never a second owner.
- **Content-model semantic families (§4).**
  - Eligible for the aperture: Authored Status, Directed handoff, Shared motion, Shared Place perspective, and Operational update.
  - Shared occurrence only if newly useful.
  - Relationship continuity as a stable door.
  - Public Place contribution: "Horizon only in this phase."
  - Private behavior and Platform events: not eligible.
  - "The aperture admits humanly meaningful shared state, not database events."
- **Signal-contract meanings (§3.3).**
  - Someone deliberately addressed something to me.
  - Someone featured something for this audience.
  - We share an active bounded consequence.
  - A shared Place or Occasion materially changed.
  - A relationship-place continuity became newly useful.
- **Six map claim types (§3.5, S5).**
  - Live device location, which is out of scope.
  - Authored Status at a chosen precision.
  - Recently shared Place or handoff.
  - Place in an active Occasion.
  - Historical shared Place.
  - A Vesper-generated possibility.
  - Precision states are city, neighborhood and specific Place. "No device dot is permitted."
- **Semantic, not biometric map (§9.8).** Each mark answers: is this where they are, were, what they shared, or a Plan? Who authored it, at what precision, for which audience, and until when?
- **Common composer destinations (S7).** Keep private, Send to Maya, Share with selected people, Feature as Status, Contribute to Occasion, Contribute to Place.
- **Candidate Shared World regions (S3).** Available from people, in motion together, the shared world map, relationship continuities, and what you currently share.
- **Fixture world (§7).** Feihu, Maya, Alex, Dana and Theo, with Monday and Thursday states and governance transitions. The sparse variant has 2 people. The dense variant has 24 people, 6 Statuses, 3 Occasions and 8 handoffs.
- **Boards S0–S12 (§8), interaction laws (§9) and 20 negative oracles (§10).**
- **Research precedents (§3).** Apple Shared with You, Beli and Google Maps lists, Locket and Airbuds, Spotify Jam and Partiful, Snap Map, social translucence, selective sharing, and attentional agency.
- **Existing Life work (§1.1).** Boards 03E8, 06D, 17A–G and 21, summarized as "already a dedicated multiplayer continuity experience."

### C. Cut, gated or deferred

- **Not authorized (§0).** Production implementation, a fifth root, a public follower graph, a generic friend-activity endpoint, ambient or live-location sharing, engagement ranking, streaks, badges, reciprocity pressure, and schema promotion.
- **Live device location (§3.5).** "remains out of scope."
- **Public Place contribution (§4).** "Horizon only in this phase."
- **Private behavior and platform events (§4).** Saves, dwell, listening, bookings, follows, views and reactions are not aperture content. The reason given is that "automatic behavior" is not "automatic expression."
- **Legacy code (§12.2).** The `Your people` screen, the `follows.py` feed route and `social.ts` are covered by "Do not infer that these shapes should survive."
- **Person ranking (§9.5).** Closest friend, relationship strength, and "people you interact with most" are all barred.
- **Pressure mechanics (§9.3, §9.4, §9.7).** No infinite scroll, no chronological stream, no unread counts, no "Maya is waiting."
- **Broad voting (§3.2).** Doctrine is "resolve rather than poll."
- **Guest-list spectacle and public comment pressure (§3.4).**
- **Person search or invitation (IA-2).** "secondary infrastructure." A sparse state must not be "dominated by 'invite friends' acquisition UI" (oracle 18).
- **Oracle 20.** "A design whose only justification is that multiplayer deserves more prominence."
- **Contributions after attendance (§1.4).** "No image or retrospective contribution is required after attendance."
- **Implementation (§12.4).** Gated on the founder choosing an IA direction.
- **The whole aperture question (Sept 7 banner).** Superseded as the active assignment.

### D. Ambitious ideas

- **A pull-based social world (§1.2).** "Let me browse my social world and find one real opening."
- **Shared World owner view (IA-2).** "worth opening with no task, makes the product unmistakably multiplayer."
- **Together as a fifth root (IA-3).** "Is 'What exists among my people now?' a fifth durable human orientation…?"
- **A social map as the centre (IA-4, §14 q5).** "Should a social map lead, support, or exist only as a mode?"
- **A cross-city friend perspective (§1.2).** "What are my friends noticing in Paris, Brooklyn, Shanghai, and Rome?"
- **Followable shared lists (§3.2, Beli and Google).** Pins that stand for a "shared list, handoff, active plan, or authored Status."
- **Ambient narrow-signal awareness (§3.3, Locket and Airbuds).** "an ambient surface needs a legible signal contract."
- **Public Place contribution (§4).** "This attributed perspective is available to an allowed public."
- **Circles with epochs (§7.4).** "Alex leaves one recurring circle; the prior epoch remains correctly attributed."
- **Audience facets (§3.7).** People organize audiences "around life facets, tie strength, and interests."
- **Legibility as a social value (§3.6).** "consent may be technically correct while the social product remains illegible."

### E. Constraints

- Four roots are "the control" (§2.1). The aperture may only index existing owners (§2.4).
- Surveillance fear. "'friend map' is dangerously underspecified" (§3.5). "No device dot" (S5).
- Anti-feed. The surface is finite by design, not chronological, and ranks situations rather than people (§9.3–§9.5).
- Effort. No posting, replying, profile completion, contact invites, relationship classification or inbox clearing (§2.5).
- Multi-party privacy. The author alone cannot publish another person's identity, attendance or photograph (§3.7).
- Duplication with Home, Places and Life is a scoring criterion (S12). So is "technical complexity."
- Process. Four founder checkpoints, and no winner before the control gets a fair treatment (§11.4).

## DOC 3: Social experience design brief, "connection without obligation" (2026-09-07)

`/Users/feihuyan/travel-workspace/docs/working/vesper-social-experience-design-brief-2026-09-07.md`

### A. Social values and experiences proposed

- **Promise (§1).** "See a little more of your friends' worlds, find something worthwhile through them, and make it easier to do something together."
- **Assignment line (§1).** Make ordinary human gestures "more useful, easier to receive, and easier to continue—without requiring them to become something bigger."
- **Six benefits (§2).**
  - A glimpse of someone's world.
  - A perspective I did not have.
  - A better way into a place.
  - An easier shared possibility, where someone "welcomes company."
  - A more comfortable gathering.
  - Something worth keeping or continuing.
- **Positive bar (§2).** "would the recipient want this even if the app offered no action beneath it?"
- **The six decisions (§3 A–F).**
  - **A. Sharing versus inviting.** One café photo expressed four ways, from share, to possibility, to "happy for company," to addressed invitation. "Company welcome" is a design candidate.
  - **B. Unequal participation.** Private Reply first. No activity accounting. Protect the contributor. Absorb the host's coordination work.
  - **C. Awareness.** Draw on chosen expressions, not hidden availability. No "You're both free tonight."
  - **D. Gathering.** "hospitality before social profiling." This covers an arrival view, partial attendance and a comfortable departure.
  - **E. Receiving.** "Original human material first, enrichment when earned." Reply to Maya stays separate from Ask Vesper privately.
  - **F. Continuity.** "continue something together, not a relationship level." Examples are the promised boat photo and the boat acquaintance. "No continuation is a complete outcome."
- **Repository-alignment update (banner).** "Strengthen everyday friendship outside an Occasion and later value within an existing relationship." Also one understandable connection with deliberate audiences, and separate mute, disconnect, block and report.
- **Post-checkpoint update (banner).** Rebalance the author and recipient paths. Show Dana contributing and Sam receiving later. Compare occasion-bounded exchange with an optional, explicitly accepted ongoing connection.
- **Guest receiving without the app (§5).** A design aspiration, not a verified capability.
- **Power and difference (§8).** Coworker and host situations, and people with different language or accessibility needs.

### B. Named concepts

- **"Company welcome" affordance (§3A).** A lightweight, author-controlled way to signal openness. Not adopted.
- **Four café variants (§3A).** Share, possibility, authored opening and addressed invitation. "meanings to compare, not four object types."
- **Three receiving treatments (§3E).** Original-only, original plus compact context, and generated-led.
- **Placement table (§4).**
  - Places carries the casual `From friends` field.
  - Home carries addressed value, a conditional `Addressed to you` region, and a "one-social-ask budget."
  - Chat carries contribution and private Ask.
  - Life · People carries stable continuity.
  - Occasion and Plan objects are entered through a root.
- **Six comparison situations (§6).**
  - The café shared four ways.
  - Paris/Rome, then an ordinary NYC weekend.
  - A new guest at dinner.
  - Unequal participation over several weeks.
  - The boat acquaintance.
  - A coworker at a gathering.
  - Each situation has a "required comfortable stopping point."
- **Six out-of-interface annotations (§6).**
- **Ambient boards from the aperture project (§5).** `Ambient 5 – By-Product Not Behavior`, `Ambient 6 – The Interaction Layer` and `Ambient 8 – The Map as the Invite` are kept as comparison material only.

### C. Cut, gated or deferred

- **Retired as defaults (§5).** "universal-pin ambitions, mandatory post-save sharing, and any success definition that requires a changed decision."
- **Fifth root (§5).** "Do not start a fifth-root redesign from these older boards."
- **Structure (§1).** "Do not start six feature lanes, invent another root, or make every social gesture enter a planning funnel."
- **Separate acknowledgment system (§3B).** Not added unless it beats Reply. No automatic viewed, saved or used reports.
- **Availability and proximity (§3C).** No "You're both free tonight." Nothing inferred from calendar gaps. A historic place mark is not current presence.
- **Gathering profiling (§3D).** No compatibility pairing, assigned icebreakers or occasion profile.
- **Relationship mechanics (§3F).** No friendship ladder, neglected-contact ranking, "you're drifting apart" diagnosis or contact-maintenance feed.
- **Architecture (§7).** No universal relationship-status column, new ambient-social service or generalized Occasion architecture.
- **Guest delivery and reachability (§7).** A dependency "rather than drawing it as shipped."
- **Success measures (§8).** Equal posting, reply speed, view counts, dwell time and inferred relationship health are all barred.
- **Authority (§1, banners).** Nothing is adopted. The brief does not authorize implementation or amend permissions.
- **Life · People (§4).** "Not a present-tense activity feed." This closes off Doc 2's IA-1.

### D. Ambitious ideas

- **Places should stay generous (§4).** "A social scope is not a permanent 'four friends, four things' ceiling."
- **An open invitation to a wider audience (§3A).** "I'll be there Saturday; happy for company."
- **Weak ties (§3F).** "A person met once can remain reachable for one particular continuation without becoming an all-access friend" (the quoted line is from §1).
- **Role-spanning relationships (§3F, §6).** "A coworker joining one dinner does not gain access to all future activity."
- **Standing connection (banner).** "one understandable connection with deliberate audiences."
- **Casual status with no place (§4).** "a casual non-spatial Status can have its featured Home doorway and its later Life record."
- There is nothing on the public commons, creators, strangers beyond met-once acquaintances, reputation or community.

### E. Constraints

- The accepted root split, and "Existing canon and contracts retain authority" (§1).
- "Low pressure limits unwanted obligation, not eligible media or useful supply" (banner). This is a correction against over-narrowing.
- The Home "one-social-ask budget" (§4).
- Privacy. "City-level information can still be sensitive" (§3C).
- Cost. "Do not require fresh generation for every card" (§3E). "Supply and generation economics remain the content lane's responsibility" (§7).
- Research humility. The studies cited are small and correlational (§3, throughout).

## DOC 4: MVP Social Loop, "Social circulation and future distribution hypothesis" (created 05-20, verified 09-06)

`/Users/feihuyan/travel-workspace/travel-agent/docs/product/MVP Social Loop.md`

### A. Social values and experiences proposed

- **A portfolio, not a loop ("The current grammar").** The paths are keep private, Address a named person, feature as Status, create or join an Occasion, carry it through Decision, repair and afterglow, and "contribute a governed perspective to a public Place." The summary line is "They are not one funnel."
- **Eight branches ("The social portfolio").**
  - Answer only.
  - Private artifact.
  - Featured share / Status, described as "Low-friction chosen expression and a natural opener."
  - Directed handoff, described as "This made me think of you."
  - Occasion contribution.
  - Live repair.
  - Afterglow.
  - Public Place contribution, described as "Situated human judgment helps another person inhabit a Place."
- **No reciprocity quota ("Benefit and effort are not reciprocal quotas").**
  - No reciprocal quotas.
  - Receiving is not gated behind posting.
  - Optional private acknowledgment is kept.
  - One photograph, a dish, attendance or hosting can each be sufficient participation.
- **Common composer ("Status and the common composer").** Six destinations. Status is "the current featured share, not a separate content object or durable identity."
- **Occasion participation.** Created naturally in Chat. Quiet presence, labor, care and boundaries count as participation. No Occasion-specific profile.
- **Growth signals ("Relationship to growth").** Organizer relief, participant payoff, relevant directed handoffs, second-occasion lift, and participant-to-organizer conversion.

### B. Named concepts

- **Featured share / Status.** One audience-scoped, revocable featured projection, human-authored, "not a profile or feed."
- **Afterglow.** An Occasion-phase projection over Occurrence, contributions and plural Outcomes. "No automatic collage or synthetic group story."
- **Public Place contribution.** A governed Place projection with source, audience, correction and withdrawal. "No universal ranking or creator-status pressure."
- **Promotion gates by boundary.** Defined in section C below.

### C. Exactly what is gated, and by what

Eight items are gated ("What remains gated"):

1. Infinite friend, creator or Place feeds.
2. A follower graph as a primary product destination.
3. Engagement ranking, popularity counters or creator status.
4. Ambient live-location or co-presence discovery.
5. Public comments or a generic travel-post network.
6. Automatic public Trip Stories, reels or group scrapbooks.
7. Public-hosting discovery and second-order invitation policy matrices.
8. Marketplace or commission systems based on published artifacts.

Two qualifications follow.

- "Native share output, link viewing, Status, and public Place contribution do not automatically authorize those products."
- "Each wider surface needs its own human value, custody model, abuse controls, and proof."

A circulation surface is promoted only when all seven gates hold ("Promotion gates by boundary"):

1. Its private or relational value is understandable without a feed.
2. The author deliberately chooses the audience and can preview the exact projection.
3. Provenance, authorship, uncertainty and current truth stay legible.
4. Correction, expiry and revocation affect every served projection.
5. Other people's names, media, contributions and Outcomes remain separately consented.
6. The recipient has one useful next act (understand, respond, save, join or ask) "without joining a social network."
7. It "improves a lived-world or relational outcome rather than only increasing impressions."

Further limits:

- "Public discovery, following, ranking, creator tooling, and monetization remain independent later gates even if a link or public Place contribution ships."
- The archived May 2026 document keeps "the complete public-story, share-card, feed, creator, and Plan Similar proposal as hypothesis provenance, not roadmap authorization."
- Link-based recipient access is described as "a design target to verify."

### D. Ambitious ideas

- **Public Place contribution** is a full portfolio branch, not only a horizon.
- **The architecture is derived against the whole portfolio.** "A first mounted case… does not define the product's ontology."
- **The gated list is itself a catalogue of the larger ambition.** It names creator feeds, a follower graph, public hosting discovery, a marketplace, and second-order invitations. The last of those means friends-of-friends.

### E. Constraints

- The Privacy and ownership section: source custody, separate consent for anyone depicted, and "Engagement… must not silently author identity, relationship meaning, or social pressure."
- The growth strategy "does not depend on an infinite public network."
- Anti-feed gating, plus abuse controls and proof required for each surface.
