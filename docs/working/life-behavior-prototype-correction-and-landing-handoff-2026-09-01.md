# Life Behavior Prototype — Correction and Landing Handoff

## Executive ruling

The latest `Downloads/vesper-life-anchors` export is directionally successful.
Boards 17-20 turn Life from an archive grammar into a behavior system:

- other people may contribute without producing an activity feed;
- incomplete human cues can recover governed records;
- competing Returns are arbitrated before rendering; and
- a contribution may compound from immediate value into later value without
  creating homework.

Do **not** reopen the settled Life philosophy, four lenses, record-plus-opening
anatomy, or visual language. Do **not** start another wide design exploration.

The next phase is a bounded correction and landing pass. It has three jobs:

1. repair truth, authority, and fixture contradictions in the existing boards;
2. distinguish policy diagrams from complete 1:1 consumer experiences; and
3. land the narrow refinding implementation through the workspace's real
   cross-repository contract rather than a hand-maintained API mirror.

The existing Checkpoint 1-3 receipts remain valid as **directional founder
decisions at fixture scale**. This review does not erase them. It narrows their
status: accepted behavior is not automatically canonically integrated,
build-ready, implemented, or shipped. Where a newly found hard failure exists,
the affected specimen must be repaired before it can continue to carry the
stronger `consumer-proven` label.

## 1. What this follow-up does in plan language

This follow-up is not a new Life redesign. It is the convergence step between
the design project, the governed fixtures, and the code that now exists.

### Claude Design

Claude Design repairs the current board families in place and converts one
representative path from each family into a real 1:1 surface sequence. It
should not invent more components or alternate paradigms.

### Claude Code

Claude Code stabilizes, reviews, and lands only the already-authorized dark
refinding slice. It records the implementation distance for Together,
arbitration, and contribution lifecycle but does not build those broader
systems from the boards.

### Founder checkpoint

The founder reviews the repaired sequences and the exact refinding landing
diff. The question is no longer whether the philosophy is compelling. The
question is whether the product now tells one truth, preserves authority, and
behaves coherently across its owning surfaces.

### P0-2 — Remove the unsupported Da Adolfo recovery

**Claude Code fix**

  elevate an unsupported entity merely because it is geographically plausible.

### P0-3 — A movie ticket does not prove attendance

> Bring admits the Source. It does not create Occurrence by implication.

**Claude Design fix**

Choose one explicit path:

  or

  before Italy,” then record the authored attendance claim separately from the
  ticket.

The later Colosseum bridge may use a watched date only in the second path.

**Acceptance**

- Receipt names ticket custody and authored attendance as separate evidence.
- Removing the authored Point degrades the bridge without deleting the ticket.
- Ticket custody never becomes taste or genre preference.

### P0-4 — Keep safety overlays separate from grant state

`17G` correctly calls blocking a safety overlay, then says old grants were
closed when the relationship closed. The social lifecycle contract is more
precise:

- grants may continue to exist as governed records;
- the block prevents rendering through the relationship;
- unblock removes only the overlay;
- no membership, rank, pending action, or proactive Return is restored; and
- explicit retrieval is possible only under whatever authority remains valid.

**Claude Design fix**

Replace “the old grants stayed closed when the relationship closed” with:

> The boundary is lifted. Nothing returns on its own. Any explicit retrieval
> is evaluated against authority that is still valid; new sharing or a new
> relationship chapter requires newly authored grants.

Do not remove independently supported Feihu/Alex parts of the dinner merely
because Maya is blocked. Suppress only units whose rendering depends on the
blocked relationship or unsafe social periphery.

**Claude Code constraint**

Do not implement block as mass grant revocation or record deletion. The
Together audit's fixture envelope remains the target: overlay and grant are
separate state.

### P0-5 — Strengthen the cross-surface seat law

`19D` concludes that a cluster earns at most one seat **per surface**. That
still allows the same active Return to appear simultaneously in Home, Places,
and Life.

Adopt the stronger law:

> A cluster may have at most one active present-delivery seat across surfaces.
> Its durable dossier and lineage remain in Life. When Home or Places owns the
> present value, Life's active root projection yields.

This does not prevent search, the dossier, or custody views from finding the
underlying record. It prevents simultaneous active editorial delivery.

**Claude Design fix**

- Amend `19D` and the policy note.
- In the trigger-flip sequence, show the same identity moving from a Life root
  seat to Home or Places while the Life dossier remains available.
- Do not render an echo card in Life during destination delivery.

**Claude Code fixture**

Add a zero-count invariant:

> Active present-delivery projections for one cluster across Home + Places +
> Life at one time <= 1.

## 5. P1 corrections — clarity, evidence calibration, and consistency

### P1-1 — Reconcile Maya's record count

Earlier canonical boards say Maya has twelve shared episodes since 2019. New
boards `17A/17B` say seven episodes from 2024 to now.

Choose one fixture truth. If seven is intentionally a filtered subset, name the
filter in consumer language, for example `Seven since last summer · twelve in
all`. Do not present two unqualified totals for the same record.

### P1-2 — Remove invented causality from the dinner reconstruction

In `17D`, replace:

> The heat carried the conversation past midnight; the note she sent Sunday
> picks up where it stopped.

with evidence-calibrated language such as:

> Heat came up at the table. Maya returned to it in the note she addressed to
> you on Sunday. The conversation continued past midnight.

Do not claim that heat caused the duration or that the later note continued a
shared interior thread unless an authored source says so.

Replace `what the night cost him is his to have shared` with a literal custody
line: `Added Sunday · Alex's contribution · visible to everyone at the dinner`.

### P1-3 — Calibrate the restaurant photograph response

In `20B`, a photograph cannot establish `starch, not cream`.

Use:

> The finish looks similar to what you pointed out in Sorrento. A starch-water
> emulsion is one explanation consistent with that texture; the photograph
> does not establish what this kitchen used.

The later dossier may preserve `you noticed the same finish`. It may not
preserve `the same mechanism occurred` without additional evidence.

### P1-4 — Narrow the voice-evidence claim

In `20D`:

- replace `the record can cite forever` with `the record may cite later while
  this observation remains retained and authorized`;
- change `matches what your Rome traces showed` to `offers one authored
  explanation for the traces`; and
- replace `gold standard evidence` with `strongest evidence of what the person
  explicitly noticed`, not strongest evidence of external physical truth.

Release must remove the quotation and every dependent composition line.

### P1-5 — One door, one destination

`19A` renders `The comparison · or ask Maya` as one door. Those are two jobs.

The root door should be `Open the comparison`. The expanded comparison may
then contain `Ask Maya`, opening an editable, unsent human-authored draft.

Likewise, search results may show one primary target and at most one quiet
secondary continuation. Do not make every adjacent action co-equal.

### P1-6 — Correct the Ask null-case reference

`20A` says later search finds a museum ticket brought in a `20F-style` flow,
but `20F` is an airline booking email. Either add an explicitly distinct
museum-ticket Bring fixture or say that later search returns nothing from this
Ask.

Preserve the central law: an Ask with a Source delivers an answer without
creating new durable personal state.

## 9. Acceptance gates

### Truth

- One governed flight story across all canonical boards.
- No unsupported venue promoted to a lived Place.
- No ticket, booking, menu, receipt, or photograph proves Occurrence by itself.
- Authored observation remains distinct from external fact.

### Authority and social safety

- Mine, hers, and held-in-common remain legible without ontology language.
- Block overlay and grant state remain independent.
- Withdrawal causes dependency-bounded recompilation with no paraphrase leak.
- Independent history survives social suppression.

### Effort

- Value precedes classification or receipt.
- Life never asks the person to organize, complete, or maintain the record.
- Reply remains optional and unsent until the person acts.
- Honest absence renders as absence, not a prompt.

### Surface coherence

- Life owns durable record, dossier, lineage, and refinding.
- Home or Places owns present-tense delivery when a world trigger wins.
- Chat owns low-friction contribution, interpretation, correction, and
  continuation.
- At most one active present-delivery seat exists across surfaces for a
  cluster.

### Design proof

- Each workstream has one complete 1:1 sequence, not only a matrix or diagram.
- Consumer screens contain only consumer language; policy/debug material stays
  outside the screen frame.
- One primary door performs one job.
- Existing visual language is reused rather than broadened.

### Build integrity

- Refinding remains flag-off until explicitly enabled.
- OpenAPI and generated types agree with the backend route.
- New hard truth oracles pass.
- Focused tests pass and the exact commands/results are recorded.
- Commits contain no unrelated concurrent-session files.
