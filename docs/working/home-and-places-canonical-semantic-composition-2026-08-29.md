# Home and Places — Canonical Semantic Composition

## Decision

For the controlled post-return New York world, Home contains **three substantive
contributions**, not five research worlds and not a standing set of sections:

1. one prepared Red Hook afternoon that combines current spatial truth, one
   useful network distinction, and Maya's authorized situated judgment;
2. one compact dinner Occasion consequence whose human contributions have
   already changed timing and preparation; and
3. one evidence-bounded pasta transfer that makes Saturday's existing cooking
   plan easier without adding an ingredient, question, or assignment.

Home then ends with stable doors. It does not also show Rome–Paris, Aeneas, a
trip reconstruction, the Sorrento hotel system, flight recovery, generic nearby
recommendations, or another social unit.

The governing choice is:

> **The current New York life wins. Europe appears only inside two present
> differences it genuinely improves. Social value appears inside the route and
> Occasion it changes. Everything else keeps its proper owner and loses the
> Home competition.**

This is a semantic composition and content hierarchy. It deliberately leaves
exact geometry, typography, illustration, motion, color, and component styling
to a dedicated visual-design tool.

## Executable fixture status — 2026-08-29

The semantic decision is now executable without mounting a production screen:

- `travel-app/utils/homePortfolioComposition.ts` selects and suppresses typed
  contributions without selecting components, geometry, or navigation code;
- `A05_NEW_YORK_FORWARD_OPENING_DRAFT` is a claim- and Source-complete
  `AgentCompositionDraftV0` grounded in an authorized interval, ferry and land
  return truth, public waterfront context, Maya's direct-share fixture, and the
  bounded Sorrento reachability premise;
- the emerging state selects H1 Red Hook, H2 dinner, and H3 pasta while cutting
  A01 Rome, A02 Paris, and A03 reconstruction and absorbing A04 into H1;
- the quiet state still returns one complete opening rather than an empty state
  or prompt; and
- a live return disruption preempts the entire ordinary portfolio with one
  operational instrument.

The accompanying oracle rejects flat-feed, chronological-recap,
everything-visible, and silence baselines. This is fixture evidence for the
semantic composition, not a production ranking model, provider integration,
visual design, or release claim.

## 1. Controlled evidence world

All operational values and people below are **fixture assumptions**, not claims
about the founder's actual current calendar, friends, route, dinner, or service
state. Production must resolve current provider truth and participant authority.

The fixture contains:

- New York as the current city-level scope;
- Thursday as the current day;
- an explicit Saturday open interval beginning at 2:00 PM;
- a host-owned dinner Occasion at home at 8:30 PM;
- current dry-weather, daylight, ferry-service, walking, land-return, and
  accessibility evidence;
- Red Hook, Brooklyn Bridge Park, and Governors Island as three materially
  different waterfront structures;
- one saved Red Hook Place and no supported prior visit;
- Maya's fixture-authored Red Hook note, shared directly with this viewer:

  > I use the ferry to arrive. I don't plan around it to get home.

- the person's private Sorrento evidence about cliffs, marina, luggage, lift,
  ferry sequence, and practical reachability;
- the person's authored observation that pasta in southern Italy had a
  different texture, plus an authorized dish photograph and source-supported
  mechanism candidates;
- Maya's Occasion contribution, “I'll bring tiramisu”;
- Theo's Occasion contribution, “I can get there at 8”;
- a minimum-safe private timing constraint that may shape the shared serving
  time without exposing its owner or reason; and
- no supported current need to resolve the return-flight disruption, no active
  provider emergency, and no pending affected-person decision that should
  outrank the Saturday possibility.

If the final condition changes—an unresolved compensation deadline, disrupted
booking, safety issue, or affected-person decision appears—the operational
precedence rules replace the Red Hook foreground. This fixture does not suppress
more material state to preserve its composition.

## 3. Canonical Home full scroll

### H0 — Situational frame

```text
NEW YORK · THURSDAY
Saturday afternoon is open · dinner at 8:30 PM
```

This is observable temporal state. It does not say the person is restless,
empty after vacation, missing Europe, or in need of recovery. If the calendar
window is not authorized or does not affect the composition, the second line
disappears.

### H1 — Foreground: let the ferry begin the afternoon

```text
LET THE FERRY START THE AFTERNOON,
NOT CONTROL THE END

2:20  Ferry to Red Hook
       Harbor edge and waterfront interval
6:50  Flexible land return
7:25  Back in the home area

28 minutes walking · dinner at 8:30 · no booking needed now

Red Hook is close by distance and far by network. The crossing gives the
afternoon a beginning; the land return protects its end.

MAYA · SHARED WITH YOU
“I use the ferry to arrive. I don't plan around it to get home.”

Open Saturday in Places
```

#### Why it wins

The unit does four jobs with one coherent object:

- **Open possibility:** it turns an open interval into one feasible shape.
- **Help it work:** it reconciles ferry state, walking, return flexibility, and
  the dinner end constraint.
- **Make sense:** it distinguishes metric closeness from network reachability.
- **Carry forward:** the Sorrento evidence helped Vesper separate distance,
  elevation, transfer, and practical reachability, but does not become the
  headline or a preference claim.

The lead medium is a spatial sequence with an operational instrument. The
network distinction is supporting explanation, not a second card.

#### Social ruling

Maya's note is admitted only if it changes how the route is understood or
composed: ferry as deliberate arrival, flexible land return as situated
judgment. If current data independently produces the same decision and her note
adds no real understanding, it is removed. Her name is not decorative proof of
personalization.

The note remains human-authored, direct-share scoped, revocable, and complete
without a message or reaction affordance. Home does not offer `Ask Maya`.

#### Owner and lifecycle

- Places owns Place, route, service, burden, and fallback truth.
- The route is prospective; no Plan exists until the person explicitly uses or
  commits to it.
- Opening goes directly to the same Live Reduction or Place Path, preserving
  the interval, proposition, evidence, and Home scroll origin.
- Service, weather, hours, or dinner changes recompute the unit.
- When the interval expires, the live instrument disappears. The durable Red
  Hook network explanation may remain in Places if it still earns depth.

### H2 — Shared consequence: dinner still works

```text
DESSERT IS COVERED.
DINNER STILL WORKS AT 8:30.

Maya · bringing tiramisu
Theo · arriving at 8:00
Main · 8:30

Remaining for you · pasta · vegetables · drinks

Open dinner
```

#### Why it wins

This is not a social update. Maya's and Theo's contributions have changed
canonical preparation and timing:

```text
Maya's commitment -> dessert leaves unresolved shopping
Theo's arrival + minimum-safe timing -> main settles at 8:30
```

The host no longer needs to reconcile chat messages, ask everyone again, or
interpret whether the dinner still works. The private timing rationale remains
private; only the minimum-safe shared consequence appears.

#### Lane and demand

- `Maya · bringing tiramisu` and `Theo · arriving at 8:00` are attributed HUMAN
  contributions.
- `Main · 8:30` and the remaining shopping set are canonical Occasion state.
- The compact unit requires no response. `Dessert still open` would not become
  a Home prompt if dessert were unresolved.
- Opening routes to the Occasion with participant, contribution, commitment,
  audience, and correction context intact.

### H3 — Practical transfer: the last minute changes the pasta

```text
SATURDAY'S PASTA NEEDS NO NEW INGREDIENT

The texture you noticed in southern Italy can come from finishing pasta in the
sauce with starchy water: the last minute changes how the sauce clings without
changing the shopping list.

Your photograph is consistent with that mechanism, but it does not prove how
the restaurant cooked the dish.

2 min · evidence and alternatives
```

#### Why it wins

This is the one past-trip editorial contribution admitted after the current
route and Occasion. It passes because it:

- begins with the person's authored sensory observation rather than inferring a
  taste identity;
- adds a source-supported physical mechanism;
- transfers that understanding into an already-occurring New York dinner;
- removes potential shopping or recipe-search burden instead of creating a
  cooking assignment; and
- states the visual-evidence limit.

The compact contribution is complete on view. Opening the two-minute artifact
adds annotated evidence, alternatives, and source inspection; it does not
reveal the withheld answer.

If the photograph or sources cannot support the cues, or if pasta is not
actually part of the Occasion, the unit disappears. Home does not replace it
with another Europe story merely to preserve density.

#### Owner and correction

- The person's sensory observation remains person-authored and source-bound.
- Vesper owns only the mechanism candidate and practical transfer.
- Life holds the durable Source and artifact; a relevant Place Path may project
  the mechanism spatially or culturally later.
- “That wasn't the dish,” “I didn't eat it,” or “I already knew this” repairs
  only the affected relations and invalidates dependent projections.

### H4 — Continuity and doors

```text
Saturday in Places
Dinner occasion
August journey in Life

Chat · Places · Life
```

No counts, completion meters, “still open threads,” prompt card, or second
screen of recommendations follows. The page has delivered three different
powers—go, coordinate, and make/understand—and ends.

Brooklyn waterfront · harbor edge

USE THE SCHEDULED EDGE TO BEGIN.

Making both directions schedule-dependent would turn the afternoon into
timetable management.

## 8. Place Path — Red Hook and Sorrento thresholds

### 8.1 Proposition

```text
CLOSE ON THE MAP IS NOT CLOSE IN THE SAME WAY

Sorrento's town–marina threshold is vertical and transfer-dependent.
Red Hook is comparatively flat; its threshold is continuity across the harbor
and street-transit network.
```

This advances beyond “Sorrento has cliffs” and “both are waterfronts.”

### 8.2 Three distinctions

| Axis | Sorrento fixture evidence | Red Hook fixture evidence | Vesper contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Distance | Short horizontal distance between town and marina | Short city-scale distance from parts of Manhattan and Brooklyn | Metric distance under-describes both thresholds |
| Dependency | Elevation, lift/stairs/road, ferry sequence, luggage | Ferry schedule, transit discontinuity, land-return flexibility | Different dependencies produce different practical closeness |
| Fallback | A missed lift or ferry may change the entire transfer | A land return can preserve the interval | Fallback structure, not visual resemblance, determines the present judgment |

### 8.3 Analogy limit

```text
These Places are not culturally, historically, or experientially equivalent.
The comparison concerns only how movement systems change practical
reachability.
```

No “places like Sorrento,” “other cliffs,” or inferred waterfront preference
appears.

### 8.4 Evidence apparatus

Keep locally inspectable:

1. the person's Sorrento observation and movement questions;
2. supported Sorrento route/occurrence evidence and unresolved gaps;
3. current Red Hook map, transit, service, and access truth;
4. Maya's note only under its viewer-specific grant; and
5. Vesper's typed comparison and analogy limit.

The person's evidence, Maya's words, current world truth, and Vesper's
comparison remain different authorities.

### 8.5 Present consequence

```text
For Saturday, use the crossing as the opening—not the only return.
```

This is the one translation back into action. Opening it enters Live Reduction.
The rest of the page does not add Red Hook history, other cliffs, or a second
analogy merely to create depth.

### 10.4 Optional Place continuation

The Red Hook afternoon belongs to the host's private Home/Places state, not the
shared dinner Occasion. It does not appear to participants.

If the host deliberately contributes an after-dinner route or if the Occasion
needs arrival/access truth, the Occasion may reference the bounded Places
projection. Places continues to own route and access facts; the Occasion owns
whether the group adopts the consequence.

### 10.5 Actions

After the complete state:

```text

Invite someone
Change time
Correct a contribution
End occasion
```

Each action routes authority to the affected owner. The Occasion does not add a
poll, profile, vibe question, or automatic public share.

### 10.6 After the dinner

Planning evidence does not prove occurrence. If supported evidence later
establishes that dinner happened, the shared Occurrence remains separate from
plural Outcomes:

```text
shared: dinner occurred at the settled Place and time

Theo: no Outcome unless he supplies one
```

No “everyone loved it,” friendship score, Afterglow prompt, or public Status is
generated. A participant may later Feature or Publish a human-authored artifact
through a separate audience boundary.

## 13. Recurring product and engineering requirements

This composition reveals six requirements worth carrying forward:

1. **Home portfolio composer.** Select one foreground and a marginally distinct
   field across canonical state, instruments, human contributions, and Vesper
   contributions. It must be able to end after three units.
2. **World Field composer.** Select one spatial proposition, a shared map extent,
   one lead, at most two siblings, and a visible end.
3. **Viewer-relative contribution projection.** Preserve author, may-use,
   may-name, audience, purpose, expiry, and revocation inside the object changed.
4. **Typed Place Path and navigation envelope.** Preserve origin, focal Place,
   edge kind, selected Sources/claims, current interval, and exact return state.
5. **Owner-read Occasion projection.** Compile contribution, participation,
   minimum-safe private constraints, Commitments, remaining work, and receipts
   without creating a group profile.
6. **Cross-root causal repair.** A route, note, contribution, ticket, or
   occurrence correction must invalidate Home, Places, Life, and Occasion
   projections without becoming a manual cleanup task.

These are semantic producer and owner seams. They do not justify a universal
Home table, trace table, card schema, or server-driven layout ontology.

## 14. Acceptance tests

### 14.1 Whole Home

- In five seconds, the person can identify the Saturday possibility, dinner
  state, and pasta contribution.
- The page asks no question and requires no response.
- Removing Europe still leaves a useful New York page; adding the admitted
  Europe evidence makes two present judgments materially better.
- No unit can be mistaken for a trip recap, friend feed, content magazine, or
  generic recommendation shelf.
- The page visibly ends.

### 14.2 Social integrity

- Removing Maya's note either materially reduces situated route understanding
  or causes the note to be rejected as decorative.
- Removing Maya's tiramisu commitment reopens dessert in the Occasion and
  recomputes Home.
- Theo's private rationale never appears in shared copy.
- No human note gains a wider audience because it appears near public Place
  truth.
- No contact, reply, reaction, or reciprocity is required.

### 14.3 Places continuity

- Home, map pin, World Field row, and Place Focus resolve to the same Red Hook
  identity and revision.
- Home can deep-link to Live Reduction without losing the Place Path or return
  to Home.
- Place Path adds the three threshold distinctions and an analogy limit; it
  does not merely enlarge Home copy.
- Live Reduction removes historical explanation and shows one route, one burden
  receipt, one fallback, and one posture.

### 14.4 Correction and expiry

- A service change recomputes or removes H1 and Live Reduction.
- Revoking Maya's note removes it from Home and Place Focus without erasing
  independent public route truth.
- Correcting the Sorrento occurrence narrows the Place Path without deleting a
  valid private observation.
- Removing the pasta plan or invalidating the mechanism removes H3 rather than
  filling its slot with another trip story.
- Occasion contribution changes propagate to H2; after the dinner, planning
  projections expire into supported occurrence and plural Outcome rules.
