# Europe Menu Source Audit and Life Treatment

> Status: raw-source audit complete; treatment ready for founder and visual review

## 0. Result

All six original images were recovered from their originating ChatGPT threads
and visually inspected at original resolution. The cross-menu opening survives
the audit, but the strongest insight is not a list of regional ingredients.

It is:

> **The two menus made “local” visible at three scales: the garden around the
> table, the Sorrentine peninsula, and agricultural recovery across the Amalfi
> Coast.**

This is more novel and substantive than `the menus named the coast`. It depends
on comparing the person's Sources and then grounding the relationship outside
the menu.

## 4. The actual cross-artifact operation

The product is not merely extracting entities. It performs four bounded steps:

1. **Recover:** bind exact phrases to six original images.
2. **Resolve:** identify the two menus and their episode context.
3. **Compare:** classify geographic language by scale and type rather than
   treating every place adjective as provenance.
4. **Ground:** use authoritative sources to explain why selected names matter,
   with the limit remaining visible.

The resulting structure is:

```text
AROUND THE TABLE
Villa Zagara lemon · garden herbs · garden vegetables

THE SORRENTINE PENINSULA
Sorrento walnut · Monti Lattari beef · Provolone del Monaco

THE AMALFI COAST
Amalfi Sfusato lemon · Re Fiascone · ACARBIO recovery in Tramonti
```

`Genovese`, `alla scapece`, `Neapolitan basil`, and buffalo dairy stay outside
the primary comparison because they encode style or broad tradition more than
source-specific geographic proof.

```text
Six menu photos · two venues · Vesper compared and researched
```

Optional one-sentence depth:

> At Hortus, locality contracted to the garden around dinner. At Dei
> Cappuccini, it expanded into protected products and ACARBIO's recovery of a
> nearly lost coastal tomato.

Claim-local limit:

> **Menus and producers make these origin claims. They do not prove what you
> ordered or where every ingredient in these servings was grown.**

### Apertures

- `See the six menu photos`
- `How Re Fiascone came back`
- `Find one of these in New York`

The first two remain in Life. The third asks Places for current, origin-aware
possibilities. It creates no reminder, Plan, or inferred food identity.

## 6. Why this clears the editorial bar

- **Not restatement:** the founder supplied menus and asked for translation;
  the three-scale locality structure was not supplied.
- **Corpus-dependent:** no single photo supports the comparison.
- **Substantive:** external grounding reveals agricultural recovery and
  protected regional systems, not merely a shared ingredient.
- **Compact:** three spatial scales can be read as a small structured graphic,
  not an article.
- **Practical:** one branch can open current New York markets, restaurants, or
  producers without making the historical page current.
- **Non-invasive:** it interprets public food language, not the person's
  personality.
- **Source-visible:** every phrase can return to an exact image and every added
  explanation to an external source.

## 7. Failure conditions

Reject or suppress this treatment if:

- the venue resolution cannot survive later artifact reconciliation;
- Vesper says the person ate any named dish without order or authored evidence;
- the product labels all ingredients as locally sourced;
- `Provolone del Monaco` or `Amalfi Sfusato` is presented as certified in the
  serving when the photographed menu does not show the designation;
- restaurant marketing copy is repeated as independent fact;
- the visual becomes a decorative ingredient cloud; or
- the New York aperture returns generic Italian restaurants rather than a
  meaningful continuation of the selected ingredient or production story.
