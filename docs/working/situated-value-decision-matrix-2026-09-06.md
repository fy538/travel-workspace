# Situated value: how Vesper decides what is useful now

## 1. Purpose and status

**Working product-design recommendation, not a new canonical contract or an
implementation-completion claim.** The founder authorized this matrix after
reviewing the strategy discussion about understanding what a person is up to,
their upcoming plans, interests, relationships and current circumstances.

The central question is not how much Vesper knows about someone. It is:

> Given the available evidence and the person's present purpose, what useful
> contribution can Vesper make—and why is this the right moment and place for it?

Importance in someone's life, eligibility for use, prominence on a surface and
permission to interrupt are different judgments. An important reservation need
not become a repeated reminder. A loosely held wish can become timely. A
friend's contribution can be valuable without AI enrichment or a reply.

This document supplies six comparable situations, connected moments,
competing-value compositions, owner dependencies and a [week-long experience
manuscript](#10-experience-manuscript-one-week-three-strands). The manuscript
is the next review object; it demonstrates rather than replaces the framework.
Section 11 applies two lower-participation comparisons to that same week:
no new user contributions after Tuesday, and a recipient who declines app
installation, profile setup and reciprocal contribution. They specify received
value, remaining work and supply dependencies—not new runtime guarantees.
Section 12 supplies three concrete prepared-result specimens. [Section 13](#13-a-week-of-competing-purposes-changing-receptivity-and-repair)
is a separate week-long branch testing competing purposes, a suggestion that
misses the person's moment, scoped repair and return without unfinished work.
Examples are **synthetic design fixtures inspired by the founder's stories**,
not new claims about the founder,
real friends, live venues, provider conditions or demonstrated user demand.
Proposed copy is illustrative; factual outputs need their own supporting sources.
The September 6 revision adds a narrowly sourced geological example in S2;
its sources support those facts, not personal relevance or user demand. All
venue schedules, participants and routes explicitly marked fictional remain
design fixtures. No production certification or user study was performed.

The four moves remain **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** They describe value, not required steps, separate services or one move
per tab. A useful answer or enjoyable share may be the complete experience.

## 3. The context we need—not a new universal profile

Assemble a decision-specific view from existing owners. Do not create a second
biography, universal life-state table or inferred personality dashboard.

| Dimension | Useful evidence | Consequence for assistance | Invalid shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Present purpose | Current question, invoked surface, explicitly requested outcome | Leads the response; selects the relevant context | Historical interests override the question |
| Current circumstances | Qualified origin when supplied, clock/timezone, current conditions, route | Determines what is feasible and timely | Last known location means current presence |
| Commitments and developing intentions | Confirmed event, expressed consideration, retained desire under its actual authority | Distinguishes protect, prepare, offer and preserve | A saved place is a plan; a plan proves attendance |
| Practical room | Stated window, hard constraints, known travel burden, wanted effort | Fits help into the person's circumstances | Empty calendar means free time; no activity means boredom |
| Attention and familiarity | Authored observations, current questions, eligible sources and prior explanations | Avoids repeating the person's insight; supports new depth or contrast | One question establishes a permanent interest |
| Wanted assistance | Current request for brevity, explanation, alternatives or coordination; scoped preferences | Changes benefit, depth and participation | A topic preference specifies the desired kind of help |
| People and shared situation | Addressed contributions, authorized shares, invitation and arrangement state | Enables enjoyable reception, shared options and coordination relief | A share implies availability, location tracking or an obligation to reply |
| Continuity and opportunity | Supported occurrences, authored outcomes, still-eligible intent, current world supply | Reconnects useful material when conditions make it relevant | Recency alone earns prominence; absence creates a backlog |

For consequential evidence, retain or resolve the source/owner revision,
subject, purpose, observed time, relevant event time, validity, uncertainty and
dependencies through existing contracts. These are requirements for a judgment,
not a demand to persist every field in a new record. Confidence is per assertion;
one confidence score must not conceal a reliable ticket and an uncertain origin.

Separate **event horizon** from **decision window**. A trip two months away
can require a decision today; an event tonight may need no intervention yet.
Several horizons can coexist without separate modes or fixed page sections.

### 3.1 How context forms without maintaining a profile

The user contributes because the current interaction helps. Useful context is
a consequence of that interaction under its actual authority, not an entrance
fee. The acquisition routes below describe product contracts and intended
dependencies; they do not certify that every adapter is deployed.

| Natural entrance | Useful result before more input | What can become available | How it stays current |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A question or follow-up in Chat | Answer, comparison or practical relief | Current purpose, subject and session context; no new durable personal state by default | Later wording changes the current job; session expiry applies. Optional later conversational reuse still requires adoption of its policy |
| A deliberately forwarded ticket or selected photograph | Extract useful details or explain what is supplied before classification | Retained Source and bounded extraction under Bring authority; not attendance or preference | Source revision, cancellation, correction and freshness change dependent uses; missing updates remain unknown |
| A direct choice or ordinary owner command | Save a place, accept an invitation, change an arrangement | Exactly that authored relation or owner state | Read the owner revision; do not infer an additional intention from a save or group consensus from one acceptance |
| “Keep this recipe and these notes for next time” | A useful, refindable result with a compact scope receipt | The named Source/notes and permitted later application | Natural correction or release changes dependent uses; keeping does not start a watch |
| Invoked narrow connected retrieval | Answer a concrete question using the named source | Fields permitted by that adapter and current purpose | Respect source validity and retrieval scope; no full inbox/calendar ingestion by implication. Verify adapter support before offering it |
| An addressed note or friends-visible contribution | Enjoyable human material, a usable recommendation or shared perspective | The author's material within its current audience and use scope | Preserve original time, precision, attribution, withdrawal and expiry; no inferred present location |
| A supported world or owner change | Revise an affected assessment without asking the person to re-enter facts | A bounded current-state update | Named producer and consumer determine delivery and freshness; generic background monitoring is not assumed |
| A voluntary outcome or clarification | Better current help, such as “the lift was closed” or “I prefer choosing the place myself” | Only the authored fact or scoped assistance preference admitted by its owner | Preserve context and alternatives; no routine satisfaction questionnaire or promotion into personality |

For every acquisition route ask: **What useful interaction makes this evidence
available, who updates it, and what help remains possible if it is absent?**
Prefer a source that resolves the current job over a broad connection whose
value has not been established. Do not ask for a calendar just to display a
worthwhile city possibility.

### 3.2 Inference and updating are active work

Vesper may infer a likely current job, reconcile eligible evidence, calculate a
feasibility margin, or choose a low-burden starting point without asking the
person to confirm every intermediate thought. These are bounded judgments,
not durable declarations about the person.

Example: a retained event ticket supplies a scheduled start. A cancellation
from the same owner supersedes it. A recent statement “I'm still at home” can
invalidate a previously supplied route origin. A calendar without an event
establishes neither free time nor willingness to go out. Conflicts should narrow
the claim or cause a justified retrieval; only ask when the missing answer
materially changes useful help and cannot be resolved appropriately otherwise.

Separate custody, current eligibility and outcome. A passed date retires a
timing cue; it does not delete a saved place or establish what happened. A long
absence makes some context less current, not the person less interesting.

The intended optional continuity experience should not require Keep after every
question. Until its policy is adopted, use current-session context and already
authorized durable material; show the intended later-use branch as a proposal,
not a functioning agreement.

### 3.3 Evidence can arrive before its explanation

The founder's next case is explicitly longitudinal: evidence on day 1, an
explanation in the same conversation on day 2, and other related evidence on
day 5. Vesper should not treat these as three unrelated inputs or freeze its
day-1 interpretation. **Later context can revise what an earlier fragment
supports, and a new fragment can make that relation useful elsewhere.**

Keep these times distinct where relevant:

- **Event time:** when the described thing happened or is expected to happen.
- **Received time:** when Vesper received the evidence or clarification.
- **Revision time:** when an interpretation or owner state changed.
- **Validity/decision window:** when a fact or resulting help is applicable.

An explanation received on day 2 may describe day 1; it does not move the visit
to day 2. A receipt imported on day 5 can concern an older event. Newly received
does not mean currently true or more authoritative than all earlier sources.
These are semantic requirements for existing evidence/owner contracts, not a
new event-store schema.

| Later material | What Vesper should do | What it must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The person explains an earlier photograph or statement | Resolve the referenced source; qualify or correct the specific interpretation within authority | Rewrite the source, infer a lasting preference, or require the person to re-import it |
| Vesper explains a mechanism on day 2 | Preserve it as a derived explanation with its actual supporting sources and uncertainty | Treat its own explanation as a second independent observation or a belief endorsed by the person |
| A day-5 source corroborates an earlier claim | Check whether it is genuinely independent and about the same subject/time | Count a copied caption or repeated model summary as additional evidence |
| A day-5 source concerns a different place with a similar mechanism | Consider a bounded relation that could improve current help | Transfer the first place's hours, access conditions, participant needs or personal meaning to the second |
| New material contradicts an earlier interpretation | Preserve the originals, supersede the affected derivative, and repair dependent uses | Merge conflicting assertions into a confident average, or declare the newest message automatically correct |
| The relation is plausible but unresolved | Keep it provisional for the current job; ask only if ambiguity materially changes a consequence | Require a relationship-labeling workflow before giving useful help |

Conversation identity is a reference-resolution clue, not a truth boundary:
one conversation can cover several unrelated experiences, and related evidence
can arrive in different conversations or through an import. Resolve “that photo”
from available context when clear. Cross-conversation retrieval still checks
source identity, subject, time, purpose and audience; similarity alone cannot
establish that two records describe the same event.

Continuity must preserve the roles of original evidence, authored clarification,
AI explanation and current projection. A compact summary can aid retrieval but
must not erase disagreements, exclusions or source dependencies. Reassess the
affected relations, not the person's entire history or every Home contribution.

**Current-policy boundary:** the same conversation is not an exemption from
Ask/session expiry. Days-later reuse requires source and use authority that
actually survives. Clearly authored Point/Bring/Correct gestures can take their
existing owner paths without a new per-fact permission ritual. If earlier
material was transient and expired, do not reconstruct it from an unauthorized
summary. Use eligible surviving material, or request the needed source only when
it is necessary. The intended optional conversational-continuity policy remains
pending; this sequence does not adopt it.

### 3.4 What is developing across days and weeks

Understanding a life needs more than immediate tasks and durable preferences.
A family visit next week, an unfolding interest in photography, a possible trip
and an existing Saturday commitment can coexist. They need not become one
global “life mode,” a new container each, or personality traits.

Compile relevant evidence about these developing situations when judging a
contribution: the visit from its arrangement and authored context; the photography
question as an exploration under its actual use scope; the possible trip as a
consideration or retained intention only where established. Later statements
can refine one without changing the others. What is developing may influence
several contributions across time, but not every contribution or every tab.

For example, an expressed family visit can make a usable arrival suggestion
timely while an unrelated exhibition remains a worthwhile personal possibility.
If the visit is canceled, its preparation recedes; the person is not reassigned
to another permanent mode. Do not infer a need for accessible routes from age or
family role; use stated needs and real access evidence.

## 4. Recommended judgment sequence

1. **Resolve the present job.** A current question deserves an answer. Opening
   Home invites a broader composition; opening Places invites spatial value.
2. **Compile only relevant, eligible context.** Preserve unknowns and conflicting
   evidence. A useful small-context answer must not wait for a complete model.
3. **Consider distinct contributions cheaply.** Include immediate help, practical
   preparation, a worthwhile possibility, human material, understanding and
   useful continuity where warranted. These are candidate sources, not quotas.
   Context retrieval can expand incrementally as a promising candidate exposes
   a missing dependency; selecting relevant evidence is not assumed solved in
   one pass. World-first opportunities need not match an established interest.
4. **Evaluate contribution and timing separately.** What does this add? Why now?
   What happens if it waits? Is it feasible? Has its value already been delivered?
   Does another candidate make it redundant? How much participation does it ask?
   Distinguish help with an evidenced concern from an exploratory offer whose
   appeal is uncertain. Both can be valuable; a city-level offer is not strong
   personalization merely because the person selected that city.
5. **Compose, then choose expression.** One dominant treatment when justified;
   a generous, non-redundant field can remain below it. Select medium and depth
   after value, not a bespoke article for every candidate.
6. **Deliver under the actual service relationship.** Helping now, keeping,
   following and changing state are distinct responsibilities. None implies
   the next. Natural-language instructions can exercise existing authority.
7. **Reconsider on relevant change; recede when spent.** Reuse valid work.
   A source correction, changed commitment, explicit purpose, freshness boundary
   or supported world update can alter the result. Merely reopening the app
   does not justify expensive regeneration or treating old value as new.

This is a design sequence, not a new service topology or numerical ranker.
Hard truth, scope and feasibility limits are not weights that engagement can
outvote. No universal measure of meaning, welfare or fit is proposed.

An interruptive safety or commitment warning needs strong, current evidence;
an interesting low-stakes possibility can be offered without knowing the whole
person. Distinguish uncertainty about someone's taste from uncertainty about
whether they can make a departure.

## 5. Six situated experiences

### S1 — A casual question, almost no history

**Entry:** In Chat: “The pasta I made has sauce pooling underneath. How do I
get it to cling?” No setup, imported archive or durable preference is required.

- **Known:** The current cooking problem and the supplied wording/image, if any.
- **Provisional:** The person probably wants a usable technique first, not a
  long history lesson. This inference concerns the current answer only.
- **Unknown:** Exact recipe, ingredients, equipment and enduring interest in cooking.
- **Immediate value:** Give a short, applicable method—for example, finishing
  the pasta in the sauce while adjusting liquid incrementally—and explain the
  important mechanism at the depth warranted by the evidence. If the sauce type
  would materially change the technique, ask that one question or distinguish
  the relevant cases. Do not lead with a profile interview.
- **Root behavior:** Chat contains the complete answer. No mandatory Home card,
  Life artifact, cooking intention or trip reference follows. A place-based
  continuation is optional only if it adds value; it is not an automatic detour.
- **What changes judgment:** “Explain the science” changes depth; “dinner is on
  the table” changes brevity and rescue priority; “this is for my friend” changes
  the subject, not the person's durable preference.
- **When it recedes:** When answered. There is no follow-up duty or “try this” task.
- **Owner/dependency:** Chat/contribution current-use path; reliable subject
  evidence as needed. Later private reuse needs actual authority, not the fact
  that the question appears in history. Optional continuity policy is pending.

**Value test:** Useful even if the person never returns. Rich history may improve
the answer, but must not be necessary to receive it.

### S2 — An ordinary week, nothing pressing

**Entry:** Home. Fixture evidence: the person explicitly selected NYC as their
home city; retained material contains an authored observation about Sorrento's
cliffs; a cooking interest has appropriate ongoing-use authority. No weekend
availability, mood or current GPS position is known.

- **Known:** Selected city and eligible source-bound interests/observations.
- **Provisional:** These can seed possibilities; neither requires action today.
- **Unknown:** Free time, willingness to travel, budget and interest in revisiting
  the trip at this moment. “Home city” is not a verified current origin.
- **Immediate value:** A finite, worthwhile range: one sourced comparison that
  genuinely adds to the cliff observation; a practical cooking technique; a
  grounded NYC possibility with enough information to assess its appeal and
  burden. These are example candidates, not three mandatory sections. At least
  one contribution should open beyond the recent trip when evidence supports it.
  The specimen below supplies actual content rather than leaving these as labels.
- **Root behavior:** Home offers value on view and an optional way into depth.
  Places carries spatial comparison and alternative destinations. Life provides
  the retained source or journey when wanted. Chat is available to change the
  purpose, not required to unlock the contribution.
- **What changes judgment:** “I have 45 minutes near X” turns a broad possibility
  into a bounded opportunity; “not Italy again” changes present composition
  without erasing the trip; fresh evidence that a venue is closed removes that
  candidate rather than making the user discover the failure.
- **When it recedes:** A consumed explanation loses novelty, a venue suggestion
  loses eligibility with its conditions, and a released source loses dependent
  uses. Valid factual material may remain available without being recast as new.
- **Owner/dependency:** Governed source/memory readers, Place evidence and current
  supply, Home composition and Places depth. Supply coverage is a dependency to
  verify, not assumed city-wide competence. No open-window or mood inference.

**Value test:** Interesting without becoming a checklist. No generated personality
reading, demand to document life, or endless output merely because evidence exists.

**Concrete specimen—content order, not a visual layout:**

1. **“A cliff can begin underground—or on a seabed.”**
   The Hudson Palisades expose the edge of magma that cooled beneath other rock;
   erosion later uncovered it. Dover's chalk accumulated from microscopic marine
   remains on an ancient seabed. Similar dramatic edges, very different origins.
   **Cliff describes the landform, not one kind of rock.** These paraphrased facts
   are supported by [Palisades Interstate Park](https://njpalisades.org/nature/)
   and the [National Trust](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/kent/the-white-cliffs-of-dover/history-of-the-white-cliffs-of-dover),
   consulted September 6, 2026. The user's Sorrento observation is an editorial
   entrance, not evidence of Sorrento's geology or a claim that this is new to them.
   The comparison is complete on view; a two-place map can supply optional depth.
   **Added value:** a transferable distinction and a regional connection, not
   “you noticed cliffs.” **Rejected:** a list of ten beautiful cliffs or another
   summary of the trip. Do not automatically turn this into a hiking suggestion:
   access, conditions and route feasibility require separate current evidence.

2. **“Pull a print. Take it home.”**
   *Fictional opportunity fixture:* Canal Print Room, a Brooklyn studio, offers
   a 45-minute drop-in printing session Saturday 1–4 p.m.; $12 includes a print;
   no prior experience or reservation is needed. Show those details and the
   place, not a teaser that requires asking Chat what is possible.
   **Added value:** a concrete way to make something, independent of Italy or an
   inferred art interest. **Why now:** a supported upcoming opportunity in the
   selected city, not assumed availability. **Rejected:** “your ideal Saturday,”
   enrolling the person, or fabricating a trip from their unknown current origin.
   This venue and offer are invented for composition review, not real listings.

3. **“The sauce-clinging step, in three lines.”**
   If the person deliberately retained the S1 technique and eligible present
   context warrants practical cooking help, show the usable method rather than
   a new essay; an additional request is not required just to earn on-visit value. Otherwise
   prefer a different supported contribution; do not infer that a past question
   licenses ongoing cooking output. **Added value:** refinding/use, not novelty.
   **Rejected:** presenting the identical explanation as newly discovered insight.

These are not a required three-card recipe. Item 1 can lead when its comparison
adds substance; item 2 can lead when the time-limited opportunity is more useful;
item 3 can be absent. The speculative venue must be replaced by real supported
supply before release. Freshness affects the event offer more quickly than the
geological explanation. More scroll requires more worthwhile distinct value,
not more prose about these three items.

### S3 — A gathering is beginning, but not everything is decided

**Entry:** In Chat: “I'd like to have Maya and Sam over for pasta Saturday.
Help me work out something simple.” Maya and Sam are synthetic participants.

- **Known:** An expressed hosting intention, tentative day and desire for low
  complexity. They are named people, not accepted attendees.
- **Provisional:** Preparing a manageable suggestion is appropriate. Inviting,
  sharing private context and creating a commitment are separate effects.
- **Unknown:** Guests' availability, contribution preferences and constraints
  unless already supplied for this purpose.
- **Immediate value:** Prepare a simple meal/hosting suggestion and a concise
  invitation draft. Do not require an Occasion-specific profile, planning wizard
  or questionnaire. Resolve only information needed for the requested next effect.
- **Social benefit:** A recipient can enjoy the invitation and respond simply.
  If they choose to contribute a dish photo or suggestion, place it in the shared
  arrangement it changes. The host receives useful coordination, not an editing
  assignment; the guest need not contribute equal effort or adopt the whole app.
- **Root behavior:** Chat prepares and invokes authorized commands. If the person
  creates an arrangement, Life indexes its owner record. Home shows its currently
  useful consequence or addressed contribution. Places serves location choices
  and casual friend sharing—not an extra copy of the coordination queue.
- **What changes judgment:** “Don't invite anyone yet” leaves a private draft;
  one guest accepting changes only that person's participation. An authored
  contribution need not be converted into an AI article to earn value.
- **When it recedes:** A sent invitation stops being an unsent task. Unanswered
  invitations do not repeatedly ask the host to chase people. After the window,
  preserve records without asserting attendance or a successful evening.
- **Owner/dependency:** Existing contribution, relationship/audience, arrangement,
  invitation and commitment owners. Loose durable Keep without an arrangement
  depends on the unadopted retained-intention proposal. Until resolved, do not
  fabricate a Plan or promise a saved intention; current-session preparation
  and existing supported save paths can still provide value.

**Value test:** Makes getting together easier while leaving participation light
and optional. No sharing or retention merely because the model found it helpful.

### S4 — An outing is underway and conditions change

**Entry:** Home during an active outing. Fixture evidence includes a confirmed
7 p.m. show, an explicitly chosen prior stop and, when requested, a qualified
current origin plus route evidence. A supported fresh update makes the original
sequence impractical. This is not an assertion of live NYC conditions.

- **Known:** Commitment, relevant owner revisions and supported current facts.
- **Provisional:** Arrival margin and possible alternatives, with their evidence
  and uncertainty. A stale origin cannot sustain a precise “leave now.”
- **Unknown:** Unprovided mobility constraints and whether the person wants to
  abandon or preserve the earlier stop. Preserve the expressed purpose when possible.
- **Immediate value:** Explain the material change briefly and prepare the best
  intent-preserving alternative: reduce the detour, change the route, or move an
  optional stop. Supply practical details rather than only a warning.
- **Root behavior:** Home foregrounds the warranted live consequence under the
  accepted live-window rules. Places explains alternatives spatially. Chat handles
  questions or authorized changes. Life keeps the same plan/commitment identities;
  it does not fork the outing into another object.
- **What changes judgment:** An unchanged show several hours away need not
  dominate all of Home. A fresh tight arrival margin can justify prominence.
  An explicit cancellation removes the dependent urgency. An authorized shared
  edit must still preserve each participant's actual commitment state.
- **When it recedes:** A change to relevant owner state triggers reassessment,
  not automatic removal. Delivering or acknowledging a warning can change its
  prominence without resolving the concern. Retire the dependent intervention
  when its underlying concern is resolved or no longer applicable; preserve
  useful commitment access. A display-name edit or a sent proposal does not
  clear arrival risk. Expired live guidance cannot persist as a current warning,
  and its retirement is not evidence of attendance.
- **Owner/dependency:** Plan/commitment, movement/route, Place/provider evidence,
  live-engine judgment and root consumers. Confirm supported family readiness
  against the integration roadmap before implementation or release claims.
  Assessing now does not mean Vesper will continue checking. Push needs its own
  justified interruption and delivery authority; external booking is not resumed.

**Value test:** Protects the experience's purpose, not merely its schedule.
Operational prominence does not turn the rest of the product into a task manager.

### S5 — Returning after several weeks away

**Entry:** Home after an absence. Fixture evidence: an expired Saturday cue, a
still-valid saved place, an eligible addressed note, an old booking whose outcome
is unknown, and any current commitments from their actual owners.

- **Known:** Those records and their current eligibility—not why the person was away.
- **Provisional:** A present composition is useful; catching up on every prior
  suggestion is not. The person may want something entirely different now.
- **Unknown:** Whether old plans happened, current location and present availability.
- **Immediate value:** Current material commitments where warranted; a timely
  addressed contribution if still eligible; an immediately usable current
  possibility from supported context. Old explanations may remain retrievable.
- **Root behavior:** Home is today's value, not “what you missed.” Places remains
  available for the wider world and eligible casual friend sharing. Life keeps
  the record and an independently saved place even when its Saturday cue expired.
  Chat can answer a new question with no re-onboarding ritual.
- **What changes judgment:** “I'm in Boston now” changes current spatial context
  without overwriting home-city identity. A revoked note disappears from dependent
  uses. “I never went” corrects occurrence-dependent material while preserving
  independent booking evidence.
- **When it recedes:** Outdated cues are not revived on every visit. An unresolved
  real commitment is not silently marked complete; distinguish unknown outcome
  from a currently actionable obligation.
- **Owner/dependency:** Owner reads, lifecycle/correction, Home delivery and Life
  retrieval. The proposed intention owner's separation of cue and custody remains
  pending. Historical chat retrieval and reuse obey actual source/continuity rules.

**Value test:** Returning is useful without an obligation to explain the absence,
clear a queue, refresh a profile or report every past outcome.

### S6 — Seeing a friend's life is enough

**Entry:** Places, From friends. Maya deliberately shares a photograph of a
slightly lopsided homemade cake and writes, “First attempt. The kitchen smells
incredible.” The fixture marks it as a past, author-chosen Brooklyn share—not
Maya's live location. The image is hypothetical; this packet does not create it.

- **Known:** Maya authored this material, its permitted audience, chosen place
  precision and share time. No visit, taste, current availability or invitation
  needs to be inferred about its recipient.
- **Immediate value:** Maya's actual photograph and words, clearly attributed.
  No AI caption, analysis of the friendship or instruction to reconnect. The
  recipient can smile and leave; no view receipt or reciprocal post is required.
- **Optional branches:** A private chosen acknowledgment; a question to Maya in
  an available human channel; a permitted recipe handoff if Maya supplied one;
  or the recipient choosing to suggest baking together. None is an automatic
  next step, and unsupported channel actions must not appear as completed.
- **Juxtaposition branch:** A Paris place note deliberately addressed to a
  recipient can stand beside their eligible Rome material. Preserve each person's
  words. Add AI comparison only when it identifies a supported distinction not
  already supplied; human perspectives do not need that enrichment to qualify.
- **Root behavior:** Casual place sharing belongs in Places; material deliberately
  addressed to this person can qualify for Home under current composition rules.

## 8. Cost, delivery and learning without obligation

- A useful candidate can be an existing answer, exact owner state, an original
  human contribution, a small grounded comparison or fresh research. Do not make
  generation the default representation of value.
- Do inexpensive eligibility, dependency and redundancy checks before costly
  retrieval or production. Track serving, refresh, data and generation work
  across the complete experience; do not judge economics by one model call.
- Reevaluate on a current request or a supported relevant change. A list of
  desired reevaluation events does not authorize subscriptions, a scheduler or
  background location collection. General watch ownership is unresolved.
- Newly relevant is not necessarily newly generated. Already produced does not
  mean still true or appropriate for another audience.
- Home eligibility does not imply push eligibility. Curiosity and ordinary
  friend sharing should not be promoted to interruptions merely to drive return.
- Value already delivered can recede without a completion tap. Use permitted
  exposure/delivery evidence for repetition control without treating views or
  silence as durable preference or proof that the person understood something.
- Do not optimize for “all possibilities acted on.” Understanding, enjoyment,
  finding something and declining an unsuitable possibility can all finish well.

by the fixture's basic terms. A revalidated offer is dependable, not newly found.

**Value and receiving requirements:** real supply, current offer evidence,
source eligibility, bounded production where needed, supported Places depth and
the intention contract for the later branch. Check whether the preparation
actually reduces searching and uncertainty without adding planning overhead.

### 12.3 E3 — Let a friend's perspective become useful without giving them work

Use §11's Friday Riverside contribution as the ready specimen. Maya's river-window
remark remains her original expression; Riverside's access information is a
separate factual source. The Tuesday-authored requirement supplies Saturday's
6:30–8:30 window and step-free entry/exit need. Vesper connects and checks these
without asking Maya to research logistics or asking the organizer to repeat them.

**Pending/unsupported:** use §11.3's branches. Maya's note can be enjoyable
without enrichment, and her endorsement cannot stand in for missing access or
capacity evidence. Do not forward her private wording merely because it helped
the organizer select a venue.

**Optional next steps:** fit is not selection. “Use for Saturday” needs the
arrangement owner's supported command and readback; invitation, delivery and
acceptance remain separate. A recipient receives only authorized current details
and supported response choices. No contribution, app adoption or reciprocal
effort is required by the proposed experience; actual outside-app access still
depends on the social owner's accepted policy and implemented channel.

**Value and receiving requirements:** the friend contributes perspective, the
system carries contextual checking, and the organizer and guest can recover
the correct current details. Count copying, checking, sending, response handling
and correction across everyone. If a thin guest requires the host to manually
reconcile everything, record the remaining work rather than declaring success.

### 12.4 Receiving these cases in the existing program

[Home packages A–E](home-connected-experience-implementation-map-2026-09-04.md#useful-preparation-receiving-requirements)
own presentation/destination/return; [integration I2/I4](complete-system-integration-roadmap-2026-09-05.md)

No new universal work owner, Home generator or public job queue follows.

## 13. A week of competing purposes, changing receptivity and repair

**New comparison branch, not additional events in §10's original week.** This
section exercises judgment across time rather than adding another ideal output.
It is a proposed product experience, not a runtime ranking policy, permission
change or claim about observed user behavior. All local venues/offers remain
fictional fixtures. The person is in their selected NYC context; their exact
location, friends' availability and unrecorded calendar remain unknown.

### 13.1 Starting evidence and the actual work worth considering

The person has deliberately retained a Sorrento cliff observation, an eligible
addressed recommendation from Maya, and a flight confirmation for a later week.
An existing owner holds Saturday's family gathering and its scoped access
requirement. They also express a wish to make something this weekend. Durable
use of that wish follows the pending intention proposal, not an assumed new
agreement. This branch can be reviewed without pretending that owner is shipped.

| Purpose | Useful work Vesper could do | What does not follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand the cliffs | Answer a current question; reuse a substantive sourced comparison where it adds something not already explained | An ongoing geology research assignment or permanent topic preference |
| Make something this weekend | Supply a concrete offer and its basic practical terms during the request | A booked activity, a whole-day itinerary or continuing availability checks |
| Receive Maya's recommendation | Deliver her actual perspective; check fit when a permitted current job makes that useful | Ask Maya to research logistics, forward her private words or infer she wants to meet |
| Have Saturday's gathering work | Apply current requirements to candidates; preserve agreed details and report supported relevant changes | Turn every Home visit into a planning session or assume attendance |
| Recover the flight confirmation | Make exact entrusted evidence easy to find, with printed facts distinguished from current status | Monitor the flight, check in, buy anything or let distant travel dominate this week |

**Working judgment recommendation:** complete an invoked job; protect an
established consequence when current evidence warrants it; retain a varied field
of other useful material. These are not three fixed ranks. A distant flight does
not outrank today's curiosity just because it is a commitment. Compare what
actually changes if something waits, what additional work would accomplish,
whether good material already exists, and how much the person must do afterward.
An interesting low-cost read need not compete for the same execution budget as
an expensive fresh assessment. Cheap content still consumes attention.

### 13.2 Monday — a question is not an assignment for the week

**Person:** “Can you compare the cliffs I noticed in Sorrento with somewhere
else?”

Chat answers now using the eligible observation and a verified world comparison
such as the sourced S2 material. It delivers the actual distinction, not “you
noticed cliffs,” and does not invent geological claims for this specimen. The
user can open the same places in Places or retain a particular result through
a supported command. The original observation stays refindable in Life.

The unchosen workshop, family venue and flight do not interrupt this answer.
Home can contain their useful current references without reprinting the same
cliff explanation as a new insight. No new essay is commissioned simply because
the question revealed attention. **Stop when the requested comparison is good.**

**Expected responsibility:** answer this question. Nothing in it promises a
course, later discoveries or a watch. It needs no boundary lecture after an
ordinary complete answer.

### 13.3 Tuesday — one worthwhile possibility, not five new jobs

**Person:** “I'd like to make something Saturday afternoon, something I can
finish there. I don't want to plan the whole day.”

Within this invoked request, deliver §12.2's concrete print-session offer if
real sources and the supported capability justify it. Its stated timing is
distinct from the evening gathering. Do not claim the journey between them
works without route/origin evidence; an invitation to inspect an offer need
not require a complete transport plan first.

If the person adds “Keep that idea for Saturday,” the intended receipt after
a supported intention-owner success is plain: “Kept the print session as an
idea for Saturday.” That sentence must link to a recoverable result and must
not imply registration or ongoing checking. Until that owner is accepted and
implemented, give the useful offer without claiming successful retention.

Vesper does not also produce a restaurant guide, commission another cliff
essay, research every possible workshop and start flight monitoring. The
relevant benefit was to make one plausible activity understandable and doable.
Alternative breadth remains available in Places; preserving choice does not
require generating every alternative in advance.

### 13.4 Wednesday — worthwhile Home without manufacturing urgency

On a later Home read, a proposed composition leads with the eligible, concrete
print offer because the authored Saturday wish gives it relevance. It includes
Maya's original note and a compact gathering reference. The flight confirmation
is available in Life; it needs no prominence without a useful decision window.
The already delivered cliff comparison is retrievable, not disguised as new.
An independently worthwhile world contribution may appear if actual supply
exists; none is invented to make this specimen longer.

This order is an editorial hypothesis, not evidence that making something is
the person's dominant interest. The person opens Maya's note and ignores the
workshop. Nothing requires a reply or a save. **No click-based rewrite of their
preferences follows, and no new production starts because Home opened.**

The day's legitimate value may be enjoying the friend's recommendation. The
system is not failing because the workshop was not selected. Repeated unwanted
prominence, however, would be a problem; nonresponse is ambiguous, not a reason
to keep promoting the same offer until the person finally dismisses it.

### 13.5 Thursday — the suggestion is accurate, but the help misses

**Person, from the workshop's Chat continuation:** “That's a good suggestion,
but today I want something to enjoy reading, not another thing to decide.”

**Vesper:** “Got it—something to read, no planning.” It then supplies a complete,
sourced contribution that has not already been delivered, using existing world
material or work justified by this request. If no new cliff comparison earns
its place, choose another supported subject rather than repackaging Monday.
Do not require a preference questionnaire before giving the reading.

The mistake was treating a relevant future possibility as the right present
benefit. It was not a false listing or proof that the person dislikes making
things. If the exchange establishes that the person is steering Home, soften
optional planning prominence there for the stated period through a supported
scoped path. Otherwise change the current help without silently expanding it
to every surface: “today” establishes duration, not reach. It does
not remove Saturday's arrangement, cancel the wish or delete the workshop.
Important current shared consequences remain legible. The old suggestion was
not retrospectively factually wrong; its treatment was wrong for this moment.

**Scope:** “today” is not forever. Resolve the local-day boundary only with a
known timezone; do not silently persist this as a durable interest or mode.
The intended cross-root same-day effect needs a concrete consumer and lifetime;
current-session adaptation can work independently. If that receiving contract
is absent, report it as an implementation gap rather than claiming Home learned.
No broad mood, loneliness or personality inference follows this correction.
The [scope research](vesper-useful-preparation-and-initiative-research-2026-09-06.md#11-follow-up-research-assistance-that-has-a-scope-not-a-permanent-mode)
explains this distinction and compares current-response, explicit root/time and
named-episode instructions; none automatically becomes a global mode.

### 13.6 Friday — changed purpose, not a contradictory person

**Person:** “For Saturday, I'd rather catch up with someone than do a workshop.
Keep the workshop for another time.”

Answer the current social wish without diagnosing a hidden motive behind
Tuesday. Their present instruction changes what would be useful for this
Saturday; it does not invalidate the earlier wish. Under supported owners,
revise the optional Saturday association while preserving the independently
requested idea. Do not silently change the family gathering or create a friend
invitation. If those exact retention/revision effects are unavailable, make the
limitation clear rather than using a saved place as a substitute intention.

Maya's recommendation is a plausible place to consider, not evidence Maya is
free. A useful reply can recover her recommendation and give its practical
terms, with optional “Draft an invite.” It must not require sharing the private
note: a draft can name the place and the user's invitation without copying her
words. No sending, acceptance or shared-plan change occurs on drafting.
The person may choose a different friend or do nothing. No reply chase follows.

Separately, the existing gathering's access assessment can be completed within
an explicit assessment request or another permitted named trigger. Mere arrival
of a note, retention of an idea or Friday's clock is not permission to start
optional production. If no assessment was accepted, Home cannot promise that
Vesper is checking. This makes the product-policy gap visible without filling
the experience with permission prompts.

### 13.7 Saturday — practical help without swallowing the rest of life

When the actual gathering enters a qualified live window, supported current
conditions may justify practical prominence. Use the selected venue and actual
owner state, not a candidate someone merely viewed. If current evidence reveals
a consequential access issue, show the specific problem and supported options;
do not bury it under yesterday's reading. A current route needs a justified
origin. An unknown origin is not a license to monitor the person.

The person says, “I'll choose the alternative; just compare the access.” Vesper
absorbs the checking and exposes the meaningful differences. It leaves the
choice to the person and does not infer “never recommend anything.” A private
instruction changes how help is offered, not other participants' agreed state.
Any actual arrangement change uses the owner command/readback and its real
delivery semantics; prepare, send, receive and accept are not interchangeable.

Once the time-sensitive need passes, operational prominence recedes. Attendance
and satisfaction are unknown unless supported. No mandatory “How did it go?”
prompt, retrospective assignment or completion badge is owed.

### 13.8 Sunday and a later return — no debt, no permanent mode

Suppose there is no fresh message, new friend contribution or qualified live
consequence. Home can offer an existing substantive reading not yet delivered,
an eligible human contribution, or a grounded local possibility. These need
real supply and current eligibility, not fabricated newness. Places supports
deliberate exploration beyond the topics already noticed. Life keeps the
entrusted material organized; Chat can answer an unrelated casual question.

Thursday's “today” instruction has ended. That does not mean Sunday should
automatically promote the deferred workshop again. Its Saturday association
was explicitly removed; “another time” is neither next Sunday nor an urgent
uncompleted intention. If it remains useful in depth, keep it there without
making it a debt. Do not replace the former planning pressure with an endless
reading feed because one reading was requested.

If existing supply cannot support worthwhile Home value, say the composition
has a supply gap. The answer is not to ask the person to document more life or
claim an empty screen proves respectful restraint. Later, when the person asks
for the flight confirmation, exact retrieval is complete value; it need not
include an insight, upgrade pitch or new trip-planning project.

### 13.9 Recommendations exposed by the week

These are working recommendations for the existing owners, not newly accepted
policies. The week supports the following design direction; real use must still
establish whether people find the experience worthwhile.

1. **Choose work and choose prominence separately.** A useful prepared result
   can remain secondary; a prominent human contribution needs no model work.
   Protect actual reliance, not every item with a date. Reuse before optional
   production, while preserving original human material and exploratory range.
2. **Describe the responsibility accepted, not the internal process.** Ordinary
   answers need no ritual. Retention, finite preparation, continuing follow-up
   and external effects need short, truthful language when expectations differ.
   “Kept as an idea” and “Checked the published access hours” must correspond to
   actual readback; neither means “I'll tell you if anything changes.”
3. **Repair at the scope the person expressed.** Distinguish this answer,
   today, this Saturday and a deliberately stated durable default. A change in
   desired benefit need not change the topic interest or delete prior intent.
   Hard facts and shared state cannot be personalized away.
4. **Let curiosity and relationships remain ends in themselves.** Reading,
   seeing a friend's perspective and refinding an entrusted item can each be
   complete. No mandatory action closes the loop, and success is not maximum
   suggestion acceptance or daily participation.
