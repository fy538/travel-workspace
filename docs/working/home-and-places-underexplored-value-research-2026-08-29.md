# Home and Places — Underexplored Value Research

## Status and purpose

This is a research and strategy document, not a schema, implementation
authorization, or canonical surface specification.

It investigates ten candidate value areas for **Home** and **Places**:

1. cross-domain life choreography;
2. option value and timing intelligence;
3. capability compounding;
4. human routing and situated expertise;
5. negative knowledge, prevention, recovery, and rights;
6. plural and contested Place intelligence;
7. hidden-system legibility;
8. Place horizons as a navigable world graph;
9. reciprocal participation in the world; and
10. attention protection as value.

The goal is not to produce ten feature families. It is to determine:

- which ideas are genuinely new;
- which already exist in doctrine but lack product expression;
- what value belongs in Home versus Places;
- what common abstractions tie them together;
- what must remain constitutional rather than becoming visible UI; and
- what should be explored first.

This work deliberately does not redesign Chat intake or Life organization.
Those roots supply and retain evidence; this document asks what Home and Places
should return from it.

## Executive verdict

The ten areas do not imply a larger, less focused product. They reveal a
smaller set of value systems underneath the product:

```text
TIME AND POSSIBILITY
  compose a lived interval
  + judge whether to act, hold, wait, monitor, or release

WORLD LEGIBILITY
  reveal how a Place works
  + preserve useful plurality
  + connect the Place through a typed horizon

CAPABILITY AND RELATIONAL RETURN
  reactivate prior understanding when it becomes useful
  + route to human judgment when human judgment is the right source
  + return specific outcomes to people and Places that supplied value

governed by:

NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE
  prevent repetition, recover gracefully, preserve intent, and retire debt

ATTENTION PROTECTION
  admit only value that beats silence and recede when the contribution is done
```

This consolidation matters. Home should not become ten new shelves, and Places
should not become ten filters. Each visible contribution may combine several
of these powers.

For example:

```text
Rome heat observation
  → hidden urban thermal system
  → plural evidence about bodies, routes, and neighborhoods
  → New York Place-horizon comparison
  → capability transfer for this weekend
  → a coherent five-hour interval
  → wait until the forecast resolves
  → one shaded fallback if conditions worsen
```

That is one authored contribution, not eight features.

## 1. What is actually new

The August canon already anticipated much of this territory. The research
therefore distinguishes **new doctrine**, **latent doctrine**, and
**operationalization**.

| Area | Current state | Real gap |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-domain life choreography | Strongly implied across Home, Moment, Plan, Occasion, Place, and Commitment | A reusable interval-composition contract |
| Option value and timing intelligence | Partially implied by flexibility, expiry, fallback, and silence | An explicit temporal posture: act, hold, wait, monitor, or release |
| Capability compounding | Strong canonical ambition | A visible transfer loop from prior evidence to later capability |
| Human routing and situated expertise | Strong doctrine; bounded handoff work exists | Source selection, burden-aware routing, and existing-trace-first behavior |
| Negative knowledge, prevention, recovery, and rights | Strong failure lineage and recovery doctrine | A first-class value treatment rather than an error subsystem |
| Plural and contested Place intelligence | Strong truth and provenance doctrine | A legible consumer grammar for useful disagreement |
| Hidden-system legibility | Rich examples but weak unification | A first-class “how this Place works” value family |
| Place horizons | Mature concept | A consumer path grammar and typed read projection |
| Reciprocal participation | Mature constitutional research | A visible outcome-return loop that avoids extractive participation |
| Attention protection | Mature constitutional doctrine | Perceptible competence, evaluation, and enforcement across every treatment |

The most genuinely new conceptual move is **option value**: Vesper should know
that waiting can preserve a better set of futures. The most important latent
product spine is **capability compounding**. The largest Places opportunity is
the combination of **hidden systems, plural truth, and Place horizons**.

## 2. Home and Places must return different forms of value

Home and Places may project the same underlying Source, Claim, Place,
Relationship, Plan, Occasion, Commitment, or Outcome. They should not copy the
same card between roots.

### Home

Home is the finite, authored answer to:

> What from my actual world is most valuable to understand, consider, enjoy,
> protect, or carry forward now?

Home should:

- compile across domains and time horizons;
- state why something matters now;
- deliver value on view;
- elevate no more than one true demand;
- offer possibility without converting it into obligation;
- use prior evidence to create a present semantic or capability delta;
- reveal a human contribution through the consequence it changes; and
- stop when the contribution is complete.

Home is not a dashboard, archive summary, recommendation feed, or list of
unfinished prompts.

### Places

Places is the spatial and relational answer to:

> How does this Place or region work, what is available here, what connects
> outward, whose knowledge matters, and what becomes possible from here?

Places should:

- preserve stable Place identity and geometry;
- separate current operational truth from durable interpretation;
- show the observable cues behind an explanation;
- expose typed, sourced horizon paths rather than generic similarity;
- preserve useful plurality and disagreement;
- hold the spatial detail behind Home's compiled proposition;
- support progressive depth rather than demanding it; and
- return the person's attention to the physical and human world.

Places is not a conventional nearby-inventory map with personalized content
layered on top.

### The handoff

The root transition should preserve identity while changing the question:

```text
Home:   “A harbor afternoon works because it lands near dinner and remains
         reversible until noon.”

Places: “Here is the route geometry, the two feasible branches, the current
         weather and ferry thresholds, and the waterfront system connecting
         the interval.”
```

## 3. Consolidated product systems

### 3.1 Time and possibility

This system combines cross-domain life choreography with option and timing
intelligence.

Its job is not to fill free time. It should produce a coherent shape for a
lived interval while preserving the futures that remain worth preserving.

Core derived treatments:

1. **Interval Composition**
   - fixed anchors;
   - genuinely open time;
   - experiential intent;
   - transitions;
   - social dependencies;
   - flexible branches;
   - intent-preserving fallback; and
   - expiry.

2. **Temporal Posture**
   - `ACT_NOW`;
   - `HOLD`;
   - `WAIT_FOR_SIGNAL`;
   - `MONITOR`;
   - `RELEASE`.

The second is especially differentiated. Commercial systems are commonly
rewarded for conversion, selection, or booking. Vesper can instead optimize
experienced optionality: preserve good futures until commitment creates more
value than waiting.

### 3.2 World legibility

This system combines hidden systems, plural truth, and Place horizons.

Its job is to turn a Place from a container of inventory into an intelligible,
living part of the world.

Its basic contribution is:

```text
observable cue
  → supported mechanism, relation, or dependency
  → visible consequence
  → current relevance
  → optional way to notice, navigate, compare, or act
  → uncertainty, limit, or contested edge
```

The Place horizon makes this navigable. A horizon may contain spatial,
operational, causal, cultural, historical, biographical, relational, or
counterfactual relevance. Generic nearness and vague similarity are not
meaningful edges.

Plural truth keeps the system honest. It does not show “multiple viewpoints”
for engagement. It identifies which claim differs, why it differs, whose
authority applies, and whether the difference changes understanding or action.

### 3.3 Capability and relational return

This system combines capability compounding, human routing, and reciprocal
outcome return.

Its job is to ensure that lived evidence and human knowledge create more than
content. They should make a later encounter better.

```text
prior evidence or human contribution
  → supported distinction or situated judgment
  → present cue under relevant conditions
  → changed perception, decision, conversation, or action
  → observable Outcome or correction
  → specific return to the source, relationship, or Place
```

This is the most important longitudinal loop in the product. It is also where
Vesper can become more useful with history without turning history into a
profile or identity verdict.

## 4. Research findings by area

### 4.1 Cross-domain life choreography

#### User value

Vesper turns scattered conditions—free time, commitments, movement, people,
weather, Places, and unfinished intentions—into one coherent shape for a lived
interval.

The unit is not an itinerary or a recommendation list. It is an interval that
works as a whole.

#### Home expression

Home owns the composed proposition:

```text
SATURDAY · 2–8 PM

A harbor afternoon that lands near dinner

Ferry at 2:20 → 90 minutes along the water → flexible stop
→ 14 minutes to dinner

Outdoor until 5; the indoor continuation remains open.
```

The user should not have to assemble the day from a row of cards. The
composition remains a prepared possibility until the user commits.

#### Places expression

Places owns:

- route and transition geometry;
- fixed and flexible segments;
- reachable alternatives;
- hours, transit, weather, and access;
- participant or Occasion effects;
- intent-preserving exits; and
- the Place relationships that make the interval coherent.

#### Risks

- filling every open interval;
- optimizing logistics while flattening spontaneity;
- treating inferred energy or mood as a stable preference;
- manufacturing Plans unnecessarily;
- exposing private calendar or relationship evidence; and
- automating another person's participation.

#### Evaluation

- five-second comprehension of the interval;
- reduced cross-app assembly;
- preservation of fixed commitments;
- ability to change one anchor without reconstructing everything;
- realized experience retaining its intended character; and
- no reduction in valued unstructured time.

#### Priority

**P1, high.** Define an ephemeral Interval Composition rather than a new
canonical object or permanent Home section.

### 4.2 Option value and timing intelligence

#### User value

Vesper helps the user understand when commitment is beneficial and when waiting
preserves a better future.

The output should include:

- what becomes irreversible;
- which uncertainty is likely to resolve;
- when it will resolve;
- what remains feasible meanwhile;
- the material deadline;
- the cost of waiting versus acting; and
- the fallback that preserves intent.

#### Home expression

```text
WAIT UNTIL NOON

Saturday's outdoor start and indoor alternative both remain viable.
The forecast becomes materially more reliable at noon.
Nothing needs booking before then.

Next change: weather check at 12:00
```

The user receives closure now even when the right action is delayed.

#### Places expression

Places should expose a small branch field:

- two or three feasible paths;
- material differences;
- shared anchors;
- threshold and expiry;
- reversibility and cancellation conditions;
- the next information event; and
- the intent-preserving fallback.

It should not display an opaque best option or an exhaustive choice tree.

#### Risks

- missing a real deadline;
- representing inventory as held when it is not;
- encouraging chronic indecision;
- converting option preservation into branch overload;
- monitoring indefinitely;
- repeated notifications; and
- hiding commercial incentives that favor immediate conversion.

#### Evaluation

- fewer premature commitments without more missed deadlines;
- lower verification work;
- fewer unnecessary Plan objects;
- accurate comprehension of what changes next and when;
- low monitoring and notification regret; and
- useful option preservation: a later preferred consequence stayed feasible
  because Vesper correctly delayed or limited commitment.

#### Priority

**P1, high and genuinely novel, but operationally risky.** Begin with
deterministic fixtures and source-freshness discipline.

### 4.3 Capability compounding

#### User value

Vesper should make experience become usable perception and judgment.

The correct treatment is not “you learned about pasta in Italy.” It is the
reactivation of one supported distinction when a later dish, market, cooking
attempt, restaurant, or conversation makes it useful.

A bounded transfer unit needs:

```text
prior observable evidence
+ supported mechanism or distinction
+ evidence that Vesper actually exposed it
+ a materially different present context
+ applicability and important limits
→ one newly available perception, judgment, or action
```

The system should distinguish a transfer candidate, an activated capability,
demonstrated transfer, and a retired or contradicted transfer. Exposure is not
proof of learning.

#### Home expression

Home owns timely activation:

- a prior distinction improves this weekend;
- a mechanism learned elsewhere explains something now;
- a prior failure prevents a present mistake;
- a new context reveals the limit of an analogy; or
- a practical capability improves an active commitment.

Example:

> The sauce difference is partly a timing difference. This weekend's market
> route contains the ingredients needed to test it.

#### Places expression

Places should answer:

- What prior experience helps me read this Place?
- Where can the distinction be observed?
- What similar mechanism operates here?
- Where does the analogy break?
- Which route or action lets me use this understanding?

#### Risks

- educational homework;
- capability scores, badges, or identity claims;
- calling repetition continuity;
- surface analogy without shared mechanism and important difference;
- cultural essentialism; and
- assistance that reduces later unaided ability.

#### Evaluation

- later unaided noticing;
- correct explanation or application;
- recognition of the analogy's limit;
- improved judgment or action;
- reduced need for later assistance;
- correction rate; and
- whether transfer worked without asking for reflection.

#### Priority

**P0 product loop.** This is the clearest bridge from evidence to Home,
Places, lived Outcome, longitudinal value, and defensibility.

### 4.4 Human routing and situated expertise

#### User value

Vesper should choose the right source for the immediate job:

1. authoritative world evidence;
2. an already-shared human trace;
3. bounded Vesper synthesis;
4. a known person who may answer;
5. a positioned public or institutional expert; or
6. silence when asking would impose more burden than value.

Human routing is therefore source selection, not a people feed.

#### Home expression

Home should normally show returned human value, not request human labor:

> Giulia's transfer note changes the route: use Marina Piccola before 11;
> today's timetable independently verified.

“Ask Maya” is appropriate only when the question is concrete, Maya's relevance
is evidenced, reachability is authorized, no existing trace answers it, and
nonresponse creates no debt.

#### Places expression

Places can show:

- the human lens embedded in the claim or route it changes;
- whether the source is a known person, resident, operator, professional,
  scholar, or community-authorized contributor;
- the conditions and time under which the observation applies;
- current independent verification; and
- an optional direct path to the source when synthesis would erase important
  positionality.

#### Risks

- interrupting people when existing knowledge suffices;
- inferring durable expertise profiles;
- broadcasting questions to a network;
- social pressure from nonresponse;
- exposing private constraints in a shared explanation;
- treating “local” or “friend” as universal authority; and
- turning public expertise into an influencer marketplace.

#### Evaluation

- lower human burden without lower answer value;
- higher usefulness than network broadcast;
- source agreement that expertise stayed within scope;
- clear understanding of why a source was chosen;
- nonresponse without relationship harm; and
- a larger perception or action delta than Vesper-only synthesis when
  situated judgment matters.

#### Priority

**P1.** Begin with known pairs and already-shared handoffs. Existing trace must
beat new interruption.

### 4.5 Negative knowledge, prevention, recovery, and rights

#### User value

What did not work can create five kinds of value:

1. **Prevent repetition** — suppress or qualify a later path under comparable
   conditions.
2. **Recover value or rights** — identify a material deadline, preserve
   evidence, prepare the next step, and avoid fake eligibility claims.
3. **Preserve experiential intent** — replace the failed means without
   discarding why the person wanted the experience.
4. **Learn conditionally** — retain the conditions and causal evidence without
   promoting one failure into a stable preference or identity.
5. **Retire debt** — close stale intentions, failed branches, and expired
   obligations instead of repeatedly resurfacing them as unfinished life.

This turns negative knowledge into a product value rather than an error log.

#### Home expression

Home owns the expiring consequence and the prepared recovery:

```text
DECIDE BY 5 PM

The cancelled flight leaves two rights-dependent paths: refund or rerouting.
Your choice affects the later claim. The provider email and receipt are ready.

Vesper has prepared both paths; nothing has been submitted.
```

Or, for a less transactional failure:

> The steep route failed because the marina–town transition was misread, not
> because the coast itself was a poor fit. Saturday's waterfront option has a
> level return path.

The first example requires current, jurisdiction-specific sourcing. It is a
prepared aid, not legal adjudication.

#### Places expression

Places should retain:

- what failed;
- the relevant condition, route, direction, time, or body;
- confidence and freshness;
- whether the cause is known, inferred, or unresolved;
- what has since changed;
- alternatives that preserve the original intent; and
- corrections or outcomes that supersede stale advice.

#### Rights and recovery discipline

Passenger rights illustrate why this must be evidence- and choice-sensitive.
Official U.S. guidance ties automatic refund eligibility to provider action
and whether the traveler accepts an alternative. EU guidance may require a
choice among reimbursement, rerouting, or rebooking, after which other options
can change. Vesper must therefore preserve jurisdiction, provider state,
traveler choice, deadlines, and current sources rather than output a generic
“you are owed compensation.”

#### Risks

- confident but wrong rights advice;
- stale provider or regulatory state;
- fake success or fake recovery completion;
- turning every miss into a permanent aversion;
- inferring “did not click” or “did not visit” as dislike;
- repeatedly resurfacing closed disappointment; and
- replanning the logistics while losing the experiential intent.

#### Evaluation

- time to a stable next step;
- zero false success and near-zero false eligibility claims;
- preserved experiential intent;
- completed recovery when authorized;
- repeated comparable failure reduction;
- quick recommender response to explicit negative evidence; and
- user trust after correction or uncertainty.

#### Priority

**P1, situationally high value.** Keep the initial scope inside lived-world
transactions and Places rather than becoming a generic consumer-rights app.

### 4.6 Plural and contested Place intelligence

#### User value

Plural intelligence separates:

1. factual conflict;
2. temporal change;
3. conditional judgment;
4. value difference;
5. perspective difference;
6. authority conflict;
7. unresolved uncertainty; and
8. sensitive non-adjudication.

“People disagree” is not value. The product must explain what differs, why,
whose authority applies, and whether it changes action or understanding.

#### Home expression

Home should expose plurality only when it changes something now:

- one compressed contrast with an operational consequence;
- an attributed juxtaposition of comparable experiences;
- a current conflict that changes route, timing, or expectation;
- a correction that supersedes an older claim; or
- one authorized absent-person perspective that opens the situation.

Home should not host a general debate or elevate controversy for engagement.

#### Places expression

Places should own the full plural projection:

1. stable shared facts;
2. current operational truth;
3. what varies;
4. attributed perspectives by claim;
5. why they differ;
6. what remains unresolved;
7. relationship-specific evidence; and
8. correction, source, and deeper context.

A difference map is more useful than a testimonial carousel.

#### Risks

- false balance;
- controversy theater;
- laundering incentives through synthesis;
- extracting community knowledge without benefit;
- flattening a group into one voice;
- exposing private knowledge outside its purpose; and
- overwhelming the user with disagreements that do not matter.

#### Evaluation

- identification of agreement and difference;
- correct classification of why claims differ;
- source-to-claim authority matching;
- improved decision quality and calibrated trust;
- contributor judgment that the rendering is faithful; and
- correct recomputation after correction or withdrawal.

#### Priority

**P0 truth infrastructure; P1 visible surface.** Places requires this
foundation. Home should use it sparingly.

### 4.7 Hidden-system legibility

#### User value

The user stops seeing isolated venues and starts understanding the system that
makes an experience possible:

- why a route feels harder than its map distance;
- why a city remains hot after dark;
- how a hotel changes the usable city;
- why a market has its rhythm and selection;
- how terrain, labor, transit, water, tourism, and preservation shape a
  waterfront; or
- how preparation and service timing affect a dish.

Useful lenses include physical, movement, operational, economic, social,
institutional, historical, and relational systems.

#### Home expression

Home should surface a hidden system only when it explains a current condition
or opens a present possibility:

- a compact causal chain;
- an annotated piece of evidence;
- a here/there or before/after comparison;
- a small reachability or flow model; or
- a practical translation into a route, meal, activity, or decision.

Example:

> Sorrento's cliffs are not scenery around the transport system; they create
> the marina–town separation that the transport system must solve.

#### Places expression

Places can make systems progressively explorable through:

- observable cues;
- flows and dependencies;
- who maintains or experiences the system differently;
- current conditions;
- historical change;
- connections elsewhere; and
- contested or incomplete edges.

Native forms may include a causal route, layered spatial map, time sequence,
ingredient or material flow, reachability diagram, or perspective lanes. Every
arrow remains a claim with provenance.

#### Risks

- false causal arrows;
- aestheticizing labor, exclusion, poverty, or climate burden;
- authoritative-looking oversimplification;
- stale operational data mixed with durable history;
- generic educational content with no observable Place anchor; and
- moralizing rather than helping the person see or decide.

#### Evaluation

- accurate explanation of the mechanism;
- recognition of the cue in the world;
- improved navigation, timing, choice, or conversation;
- correct transfer to a new Place;
- expert and stakeholder fidelity;
- recognition of uncertainty; and
- whether a native representation outperforms prose without producing false
  certainty.

#### Priority

**P0 Places; P1 Home.** This is the best way to broaden Vesper beyond tourism,
art, and recommendations while remaining coherent.

### 4.8 Place horizons as a navigable world graph

#### User value

A Place horizon answers:

- What made this Place possible?
- What does it change elsewhere?
- Which route, system, person, or unvisited Place explains it?
- Which prior experience supplies a useful comparison?
- What differs across time, companion, or condition?
- Which connection opens action and which is complete as understanding?

#### Home expression

Home should expose one complete-on-view **horizon passage**:

> The Sorrento hotel did more than provide a bed: its elevation selected the
> marina, which selected the ferry rhythm, which determined which coast became
> reachable. Here is the same dependency shaping Saturday in New York—and the
> important difference.

The unit needs one focal Place or situation, a two-to-four-edge path, explicit
relation types, one consequential difference, and an optional transfer to
Places.

#### Places expression

Places should support paths under stable lenses:

- **Around here** — spatial, route, and operational edges;
- **What shaped this** — causal, material, and historical edges;
- **Across the world** — structural comparisons with stated differences;
- **Through people** — attributed relational and biographical edges; and
- **In your life** — prior encounters, future returns, and counterfactual
  branches.

The graph should remain invisible infrastructure. The person follows a
question, pathway, map field, or comparison—not raw nodes.

#### Risks

- generic “related Places”;
- mysterious connections without relation types;
- graph density that exceeds comprehension;
- private relational edges revealing another person's location or history;
- weak analogy without structural similarity and important difference; and
- unvisited Places becoming bucket-list pressure.

#### Evaluation

- five-second statement of the new relation;
- later unaided noticing or judgment;
- correct distinction among causal, spatial, operational, and relational
  edges;
- enrichment by an unvisited Place without visit pressure;
- measurable marginal value of each additional edge; and
- reliable removal or recomputation after correction.

#### Priority

**P0, highest Places opportunity.** Begin with a fixture-backed, read-only,
typed Place-horizon projection.

### 4.9 Reciprocal participation in the world

#### User value

The reciprocal loop should be:

```text
receive useful Place knowledge
  → encounter the world differently
  → return an outcome, correction, or refinement
  → source sees the specific consequence
  → Place knowledge becomes more trustworthy or plural
```

The key missing value is what the contributor receives. The answer should be a
specific outcome, correction, acknowledgement, support, stewardship, or
payment—not engagement statistics.

#### Home expression

Home has two legitimate reciprocal returns:

1. a contribution changes a current opening; or
2. a specific downstream consequence returns to the contributor.

Examples:

> A resident's entrance correction changes tonight's route; observed
> yesterday.

> Maya used the ferry caveat you left and added that the gate now closes at 6.

Avoid view counts, “people found this helpful,” streaks, moral prompts, and a
generic contribution module.

#### Places expression

Contribution belongs at the exact claim, condition, or horizon edge it changes:

- correct current hours or access;
- add a time-bounded observation;
- offer a positioned perspective;
- contest or refine a claim;
- return an Outcome to an attributed source; or
- support, thank, or compensate a person or institution where appropriate.

Private constraints must never become the public explanation for an
operational correction.

#### Risks

- crowdsourced free labor;
- creator status and performance pressure;
- sensitive-place amplification;
- extracting community knowledge;
- publishing visitor attachment as community authority;
- weak moderation and withdrawal; and
- measuring database growth rather than improved encounters.

#### Evaluation

- contributor understanding of purpose and withdrawal;
- higher perceived efficacy from outcome return than popularity counts;
- faithful positionality and plurality;
- reliable correction and revocation;
- net benefit to residents and institutions; and
- a real encounter or decision improved.

#### Priority

**P1 for bounded handoff outcome return; P2 for public participation.** Public
Place contribution requires moderation, community governance, supply, and
externality controls.

### 4.10 Attention protection as value

#### User value

Attention protection means confidence that important state is accounted for
without continuous checking, response debt, avoidable verification, or a stream
of decisions.

The benefit is not merely fewer notifications. It is:

- complete value without response;
- one cue instead of continuous narration;
- permission to ignore;
- fewer unnecessary choices;
- confidence about the next material threshold; and
- Vesper's ability to recede.

#### Home expression

Home should demonstrate protected attention through:

- zero or one dominant demand;
- complete-on-view contributions;
- a compact “settled enough” state;
- one honest next threshold;
- no CTA when none is required; and
- omission of weak archive or recommendation filler.

A rare quiet statement may help:

> Nothing needs repair tonight. Dinner is settled; Saturday stays open until
> noon.

Repeating that message would turn silence into content and defeat its purpose.

#### Places expression

Places should protect embodied attention through:

- one route cue that points to something visible;
- minimal live facts;
- materially distinct alternatives only;
- audio by explicit request;
- no autoplay or continuous narration;
- no recommendation layer while a route or encounter owns attention;
- “stay here” as a valid outcome; and
- interpretation that terminates in an observable cue or action.

#### Risks

- under-delivering and calling it calm;
- hiding capability so users cannot form a mental model;
- missing material consequences;
- treating low interaction as proof of correct silence;
- invasive receptivity inference;
- suppressing accessibility assistance; and
- allowing commercial content to bypass attention rules.

#### Evaluation

- lower interruption and treatment regret;
- no increase in missed material consequences;
- lower response debt and checking;
- more eyes-to-world time;
- better activity or conversation resumption;
- correct user prediction of when Vesper will speak; and
- healthy use after a one-week Vesper pause.

Clicks and session duration are anti-metrics here.

#### Priority

**P0 constitutional constraint; P1 experience and evaluation work.** Do not
create an attention-protection feature. Require every Home and Places treatment
to beat silence and recede at the right point.

## 5. Ranked opportunity portfolio

Ranking is based on thesis fit, differentiated value, relevance to both roots,
ability to compound with history, evidence maturity, operational risk, and the
danger of becoming a separate product.

### Tier A — define the product spine

1. **Place horizons as typed explanatory paths**
   - highest-leverage Places capability;
   - supplies differentiated Home material;
   - joins practical, cultural, social, and prospective value without a
     feature catalog.

2. **Capability compounding**
   - proves that ingestion and continuity create a better later life;
   - gives the product a longitudinal outcome beyond memory and
     personalization.

3. **Hidden-system legibility**
   - makes Places more than map inventory;
   - broadens the product beyond travel and culture into ordinary lived
     systems.

4. **Plural Place truth**
   - foundational to trustworthy horizons and hidden systems;
   - visible only where disagreement materially helps.

5. **Attention protection**
   - admission rule for all of the above;
   - not a standalone module.

### Tier B — create signature Home behavior

6. **Cross-domain interval composition**
   - lets Home compile a whole lived interval instead of ranking cards.

7. **Option value and temporal posture**
   - genuinely differentiated;
   - requires high source freshness and deterministic validation.

8. **Negative knowledge and intent-preserving recovery**
   - creates powerful situational trust;
   - should remain scoped to the lived world.

9. **Known-network human routing**
   - adds human knowledge without a friend feed;
   - existing trace should beat interruption.

### Tier C — extend trust outward

10. **Reciprocal participation**
    - begin with Outcome returns inside known relationships and Occasions;
    - pursue public Place participation only after governance and moderation
      are credible.

The numbering is sequencing, not a judgment that later areas are less central
to the philosophy. For example, reciprocal participation is philosophically
important but operationally premature as a public system.

## 6. Presentation strategy

### 6.1 Compose powers; do not allocate sections

Do not create Home sections called “Capabilities,” “Systems,” “Friends,”
“Recovery,” or “Optionality.” Do not create equivalent Places filters.

One contribution should earn its form from the value it delivers:

| Contribution | Likely Home form | Likely Places form |
|---|---|---|
| Whole interval works now | compact sequence or route proposition | branchable spatial field |
| Waiting is valuable | temporal posture with next threshold | feasible-set comparison |
| Prior understanding applies | annotated transfer or compact comparison | observable cues and analogy limits |
| Human judgment changes the situation | attributed consequence | claim-level human lens |
| Failure creates a next step | prepared recovery or corrected possibility | condition-bound failure and alternatives |
| Perspectives materially differ | compressed contrast | difference map with sources |
| A Place is shaped by a system | causal chain, flow, or reachability model | layered system pathway |
| A Place connects outward | horizon passage | typed exploratory path |
| A contribution mattered | specific outcome return | claim-level use and correction history |
| Nothing beats silence | omission or one threshold | minimal live cue and receding UI |

Detailed visual composition belongs in dedicated design tools. The product
contract should specify semantic structure, evidence, attention, authority, and
root-native treatment rather than prescribe decorative cards.

### 6.2 Home should feel broad without feeling unfocused

Home obtains breadth from the number of powers its finite composition can
exercise, not from the number of modules it displays.

A strong Home can contain:

- one dominant current contribution;
- one materially different available possibility;
- one capability, relational, or interpretive return; and
- one farther horizon without urgency.

Each item must perform a different human job. More evidence should increase the
quality and variety of candidate value, not force more items onto the page.

### 6.3 Places should feel deep without becoming a knowledge graph UI

Places should preserve a stable spatial world and offer progressive questions:

```text
What works here now?
What can I notice?
How does this Place work?
What shaped it?
Who knows or experiences it differently?
What connects outward?
What in my life changes how I can enter it?
```

The graph, provenance system, and plural evidence remain infrastructure. The
surface shows a small number of typed paths and representations chosen for the
current question.

## 7. Enabling contracts

The research suggests derived projections and decision receipts, not ten new
canonical nouns.

### 7.1 Interval Composition Brief

- temporal window;
- fixed anchors;
- open interval;
- experiential intent;
- participants and authority;
- transition geometry;
- flexible branches;
- current conditions;
- intent-preserving fallback; and
- expiry.

### 7.2 Temporal Posture

- posture: act, hold, wait, monitor, or release;
- material deadline;
- reversibility;
- source freshness;
- expected information event;
- cost of waiting and acting;
- current feasible set;
- monitoring authority and expiry; and
- next visible consequence.

### 7.3 Place Horizon read projection

- focal Place;
- connected entity;
- typed, directed relation;
- Claim and Source references;
- time and condition scope;
- certainty, disagreement, and correction;
- viewer and Occasion eligibility;
- current explanatory or operational consequence;
- important difference or analogy limit; and
- expiry.

A two-to-four-edge Horizon Path can be composed dynamically. It does not need
to become a durable universal object.

### 7.4 Transfer Candidate

- prior cue, Source, Place, and Outcome;
- supported mechanism or distinction;
- evidence of what Vesper exposed;
- current cue and target Place;
- shared structure;
- important difference and failure conditions;
- privacy and audience;
- transfer status; and
- expiry or retirement.

### 7.5 Human Route Candidate

- immediate job and question;
- Place, Occasion, and time scope;
- evidence for source relevance;
- expertise mode;
- relationship and recipient fit;
- existing-trace-first decision;
- reachability and expected burden;
- may-use, may-name, and may-contact grants;
- no-response completion state; and
- expiry.

### 7.6 Negative Outcome and Recovery projection

Use the existing causal lineage rather than collapsing failure into one record:

```text
opening
→ judgment
→ decision
→ action receipt
→ occurrence or provider state
→ outcome or correction
→ conditional later projection
```

Preserve jurisdiction, provider, choice, deadline, evidence, authority, and the
original experiential intent.

### 7.7 Contribution use and Outcome return

- eligible contribution projection;
- exact claim or encounter that used it;
- use purpose and audience;
- downstream Outcome, correction, or refinement;
- return type: acknowledgement, evidence, support, stewardship, or payment;
- moderation and contest state; and
- withdrawal and propagation.

## 8. First research portfolio

The next step should be a fixture portfolio, not a repository-wide
architecture build or one-loop tunnel.

Use the same evidence base to test several powers in combination.

### Fixture A — Sorrento cliffs, marina, hotel, and ferry

Tests:

- hidden system;
- Place horizon;
- movement capability transfer;
- interval composition;
- failure correction; and
- analogy limit against a New York waterfront.

### Fixture B — Rome heat to a New York weekend

Tests:

- current condition;
- urban thermal system;
- plural bodily and neighborhood perspectives;
- act/wait posture;
- shaded fallback;
- attention protection; and
- practical capability transfer without identity interpretation.

### Fixture C — Southern Italy pasta to New York

Tests:

- photo and menu evidence;
- service and preparation mechanism;
- ingredient or technique flow;
- cooking, restaurant, or market transfer;
- cultural plurality and lineage; and
- later unaided capability.

### Fixture D — Rome, Paris, and attributed friends

Tests:

- simultaneous but different Place experience;
- attributed human evidence;
- one consistent axis of juxtaposition;
- human routing versus existing trace;
- audience effects; and
- whether the social contribution creates a real semantic delta rather than
  friend activity.

### Fixture E — cancelled or disrupted movement

Tests:

- provider-state truth;
- rights-dependent branch preparation;
- preservation of evidence;
- explicit authority boundary;
- intent-preserving alternative;
- correction and outcome lineage; and
- time to a stable next step.

For each fixture, compare:

1. generic recommendation;
2. factual summary;
3. prose article;
4. Home-native compiled contribution;
5. Places-native depth treatment; and
6. silence.

## 9. Evaluation program

### 9.1 Common positive oracles

- the contribution is understood in five seconds;
- it adds a supported fact, mechanism, consequence, comparison, or option;
- it changes perception, action, conversation, capability, or justified
  continuity;
- it remains correct under current conditions;
- it exposes uncertainty and authority at the level needed for the decision;
- it preserves another person's privacy and positionality;
- it does not require reflection, saving, or response to become valuable; and
- it recedes once complete.

### 9.2 Failure oracles

- personalized paraphrase;
- vague “connection” without new substance;
- feature or inventory recitation;
- recommendation without mechanism or fit;
- false causal certainty;
- premature commitment;
- a task disguised as content;
- inferred identity from situational evidence;
- human knowledge stripped of source and conditions;
- public contribution without reciprocal benefit;
- duplicated Home and Places cards; or
- any treatment that loses to silence.

### 9.3 Longitudinal oracles

- later unaided noticing or application;
- better second Occasion or Place encounter;
- fewer repeated comparable failures;
- lower need for verification or re-explanation;
- useful option preservation;
- specific outcomes returned to human sources;
- corrections propagating to every dependent projection; and
- the person's world becoming more navigable without becoming narrower.

## 10. Strategic recommendation

The product should not choose one of the ten areas as a narrow behavior loop.
It should define a small number of reusable contracts and test complete Home
and Places compositions that combine them.

Recommended order:

1. define the Place Horizon read projection and path grammar;
2. define the capability Transfer Candidate and its evidence thresholds;
3. define hidden-system and plural-difference contribution grammars;
4. compose those into Home-native and Places-native fixtures;
5. add Interval Composition and Temporal Posture to the same fixtures;
6. add negative Outcome and intent-preserving recovery cases;
7. add known-network human routing and bounded Outcome return; and
8. defer public Place participation until governance is proven.

This is systematic without postponing the product experience. It starts from
the common seams that can generate several complete experiences, rather than a
repository-wide noun migration or an isolated micro-loop.

The resulting product promise is sharper:

> Vesper makes the world around you more intelligible and more available. It
> composes what can work now, connects Places through the systems and people
> that give them meaning, carries understanding into later life, preserves good
> futures until commitment is worthwhile, and gets out of the way.
