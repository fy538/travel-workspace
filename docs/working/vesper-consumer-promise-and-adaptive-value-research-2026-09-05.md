# Vesper consumer promise and adaptive value — research and study brief

## 1. Status and conclusion

This is research provenance and a proposed study, not a second product canon.
The founder requested this consolidation after discussing the consumer promise,
three positioning alternatives, and learning which kinds of help different
people want. No participant interviews, preference experiments, or adaptive
runtime validation were conducted in this research pass.

The approved product refinement is:

> Vesper learns what kind of help a person values, not only what interests
> them. It adapts to current purpose and revisable preferences, reduces
> unwanted work, preserves meaningful participation, and keeps worthwhile
> possibilities available.

The four moves remain **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** They are a repertoire, not quotas, personas, onboarding modes, or a
required journey. This refinement does not change the group-travel launch
wedge, four roots, shared owners, release boundaries, or action authority.

The proposed opening **More to discover. Less to figure out.** remains a
hypothesis alongside discovery-led and practical-help-led alternatives. The
[August messaging decision](../decisions/2026-08-24-adopt-experiential-responsibility-messaging.md)
still governs approved public wording. Research drafts must not silently replace
Brand Identity, app-store copy, the investor script, or release claims.

### Consumer-product precedents, not success evidence

- [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) uses a familiar meeting/notepad entrance
  while describing preparation, follow-ups, chat, and memory. The useful lesson
  is a recognizable transformation, not copying the meeting category. Life has
  no complete transcript and Vesper must not imply ambient recording.
- [Spotify exclusion controls](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/exclude-playlists-or-tracks-from-your-taste-profile/)
  reduce the influence of selected listening on recommendations.
  [Its March 2026 Taste Profile announcement](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-03-13/taste-profile-beta-announcement/)
  described a steerable beta initially for Premium listeners in New Zealand.
  This is evidence of a control pattern, not universal availability or measured
  benefit. Vesper should support excluding a trace's future influence without
  assuming that deleting its original is the same operation.

These sources support a direction, not a novel-category claim, a moat, an
engagement target, or proof that one adaptive policy works for all people.

## 4. Product synthesis: adapt the contribution

Separate four questions before selecting an output:

1. **Topic:** what subject or Place is relevant?
2. **Desired benefit:** understand, discover, obtain practical relief, connect,
   or retrieve useful continuity? These are descriptions, not new product moves.
3. **Wanted participation:** what does the person want to choose, make, explore,
   express, or delegate themselves?
4. **Current purpose and circumstances:** what is useful now, with these people,
   commitments, conditions, and available attention?

Current requests govern the response; authorized ongoing preferences supply
revisable defaults; situation evidence narrows their application. A request to
learn a pasta technique does not mean the person wants restaurant recommendations.
Someone who enjoys choosing restaurants may still want help with hours and routes.

Moment/session adjustments, Occasion-scoped working context, and durable
assistance preferences have different scopes. Repetition, silence, and convenience
do not grant retention, inference, audience, or action authority. Unknown remains
unknown; do not add a preference interview before useful help.

### Corrections that must stay distinct

| Human statement | Intended distinction |
| --- | --- |
| “Not this weekend.” | Time/situation constraint, not enduring dislike. |
| “That was for my parents.” | Correct subject or context; not a preference about the speaker. |
| “I like the subject; these are too long.” | Presentation/depth correction, not topic rejection. |
| “Help with logistics, but let me choose.” | Assistance/participation preference, not action permission. |
| “Keep it, but don't use it for recommendations.” | Retention and future influence differ; propagate to dependent uses. |

### Adaptation boundaries

Adapt value mix, depth, medium, candidate selection, and initiative within policy.
Preserve root meanings, capability access, grounding, correction, privacy,
authorship, and canonical shared truth. Important commitment changes do not
disappear because practical updates get fewer clicks. Different private
explanations may accompany the same shared arrangement; do not average people
into a group personality or rank whose interests deserve protection.

Do not generate every conceivable output before deciding what deserves to
appear. Select the contribution first; reuse eligible evidence and production
work when freshness and audience permit. Shared generation must never imply
shared private context. Economics and quality remain unmeasured here.

## 5. Consumer-promise hypotheses

The three openings are alternate entrances to the same product, not three
products, releases, personalities, or permanent user segments.

| Variant | Headline | Hypothesis | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — balanced | **More to discover. Less to figure out.** | Attraction and practical relief are both legible. | Pleasant but too generic to imply a use. |
| B — discovery-led | **Discover what you wouldn't have known to look for.** | Gives a reason to receive value beyond asking questions. | Reads as a content/recommendations feed; hides practical help. |
| C — practical-help-led | **Make the day work, without all the work.** | Makes immediate assistance recognizable. | Reads as a planner, productivity app, or autonomous concierge. |

Use identical supporting copy in the initial comparison:

> Vesper helps you find places worth going, understand the things that catch
> your eye, and make plans work—at home or away, on your own or with friends.
> It builds on the places, questions, and experiences you choose to share.

Hold examples, visual polish, presentation length, and capability claims
constant. A later comparison of rewritten full narratives is a different test.
If every variant implies input debt, revisit the common paragraph rather than
declaring the least misunderstood headline a winner.

Separate company promise, acquisition situation, and demonstrated build
capability. Travel invitations can introduce the product without making travel
the company boundary; local examples do not themselves establish a different
distribution strategy. Consult [Current State](../status/current-state.md) and
exact journey evidence before claiming any capability is shipped.

## 6. Proposed comprehension study

Start with 12–18 people unfamiliar with the strategy as a practical exploratory
round, not a powered experiment or statistically ranked market test. Include
organizers, participants, and people who explore/save/ask without organizing;
distribute these backgrounds and familiarity with AI across variants. Do not
assign discovery copy only to explorers or practical copy only to organizers.

Pilot the interview guide first. Explain that participants are evaluating a
concept, not being evaluated. Obtain consent for recording; private material is
optional and should not be required. Use a consistent neutral facilitator script.

### Phase A — opening without explanatory rescue

Each person initially sees one variant, assigned across the sample rather than
chosen to fit them. Let them read naturally; do not use a speed test as a proxy
for comprehension. Ask one question at a time:

1. “In your own words, what do you think this app does?”
2. “Does a recent situation come to mind where this might have been useful—or not?”
3. “What would you expect to see or receive when you first open it?”
4. “What would you expect to have to provide or do first?”
5. “How would you handle that situation today? Where, if anywhere, would this fit?”
6. “What are you unsure it can do? What would you still handle elsewhere?”

Do not begin with the founder story, the four moves, tabs, or a feature list.
Do not ask what it does that current apps cannot: that presupposes differentiation.
Do not promise “little setup” inside the question about required effort.
Record verbatim interpretations, not whether they used our preferred words.

### Phase B — the same honest examples

Use the same set for all variants, rotating order evenly where practical and
recording it. These are specifications for example materials, not completed
or verified runtime outputs:

| Example | Material needed | Fail condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sorrento attention becomes understanding | A sourced, substantive cliff comparison, including a material difference; enough of the answer appears to be useful. | Placeholder insight, paraphrase, unsupported geology, or mandatory reflection. |
| Another person opens a possibility | An explicitly shared Place or note shapes a plausible upcoming afternoon; author, Vesper addition, and relevance remain distinct. | Inferred location, private context leakage, fabricated friend, or avatar-only social value. |
| Practical relief under changed conditions | An existing plan and a grounded problem produce an appropriate recommendation, with the choice left to its authorized owner. | Magical rebooking, silent shared edits, unsupported live monitoring, or an operations dashboard substituted for help. |

For each material, record source facts, hypothetical facts, authored mock content,
actual versus proposed capability, inputs, and dependencies. Use neutral fictional
people where permission for real stories is absent. Label static concepts as such.
The [September 5 Plan decision](../decisions/2026-09-05-adopt-the-plan-in-seven-sentences.md)
remains binding; these examples do not reopen deferred operational mechanisms.

Then ask: “Which parts fit what you expected? Which surprised you? Did anything
feel like a different app?” Probe social value only after recording spontaneous
interpretation, so we can distinguish noticed from prompted understanding.

Optional side-by-side wording comparison comes last. It is comparative feedback,
not three independent first impressions.

### Readout and decision

Record: inferred category; actual situation; expected first benefit and burden;
existing alternatives; uncertainty and boundaries; spontaneous social/practical
recognition; and what changed after examples. Keep headline preference separate.

Prioritize accurate expectations, recognizable personal value, and coherent
breadth. Do not score participants down for using different language or preferring
one legitimate benefit. Do investigate mandatory journaling, universal autonomy,
or friends-first expectations. If examples repair every opening, improve the
functional explanation rather than merely polishing the headline. If different
entrances work for different contexts, consider acquisition-specific messaging
without inventing permanent user types.

This round cannot establish adoption, retention, willingness to pay, long-term
adaptation quality, or a statistically significant winning message. No results
are populated or simulated as human evidence.

## 7. Adaptive-experience comparison and design handoff

Evaluate complete experiences, not three isolated personalized cards. Use the
same person and core evidence across changing purposes; use comparable evidence
with explicitly different desired benefits across people. Keep fictional
preferences labeled rather than inferring them from founder anecdotes.

| Case | Desired behavior | What remains invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1: Sunday curiosity → station urgency | A rich explanation gives way to a direct practical answer when explicitly requested. | Prior interest is not erased; grounded facts and capability access remain. |
| A2: Enjoys choosing → wants logistics offloaded | Prepare comparisons and practical checks while preserving the person's choice. | No autonomous booking or changed shared commitment. |
| A3: Planning for parents → later personal use | Apply the family's context to the visit, not to enduring personal taste. | Subject, scope, and evidence stay distinct. |
| A4: Too long, not wrong topic | Change depth/medium within the expressed scope. | Retain topic access; do not infer disinterest. |
| A5: Rich history → unfamiliar new curiosity | Let a new request govern; offer other worthwhile dimensions when appropriate. | No compulsory exploration quota or identity assignment. |
| A6: Sparse context, no friends enrolled | Return useful grounded value without fabricated intimacy or setup debt. | Stable roots and optional participation. |
| A7: Same shared occasion, different private needs | Offer different private help while preserving one coherent arrangement. | No merged preferences, private leakage, or suppressed obligations. |
| A8: Keep but exclude; subsequent return | Preserve authorized custody while stopping excluded recommendation influence. | Dependent uses honor correction; no repeated quiz or explanation. |

Review wanted versus unwanted effort, appropriateness of help, ability to change
direction, coherence across roots, and correction burden. A short visit, no click,
or silence can be consistent with value but does not prove satisfaction. Use
voluntary accounts, observed task consequences, and research sessions rather than
routine in-product surveys. Longitudinal follow-up is needed before claiming the
system learns the right defaults over time.

## 8. Implementation implications, not an implementation mandate

Existing context compilation and contribution policy should carry these semantic
distinctions; composition should choose the useful contribution before production
and rendering. No new personality database, preference service, universal role
enum, fixed content ratio, ambient sensing, or rewritten shell is authorized here.

Before a runtime package, verify where current purpose, assistance preference,
scope, expiry, and correction can be represented in existing owners. Separate
source facts, situation ranking, durable claims, audience, and action. A prototype
that selects a good output does not prove memory conformance or live authority.

## 9. Matched sparse-context pass — September 6

### Status, evidence, and judgment

This section executes the founder-approved comparison across four experiences:
menu, open afternoon, friend handoff, and city-only Home. It extends A6 rather
than creating another canon or prioritizing one loop over the system. These are
worked product specifications and desk-review judgments, **not participant
findings, rendered designs, live recommendations, or verified runtime behavior**.
Detailed visual composition remains with the design tool and execution remains
in its roadmap lane.

The judgment from this pass is:

> Start with competence about the world; become relevant through the situation;
> become more personal through an earned relationship. History improves value,
> but must not be the source of all value.

Three independent obligations—not onboarding stages—apply throughout:

- **S0, no personal history:** deliver a complete worthwhile result from the
  present material or available world context.
- **S1, one added detail:** materially improve fit without a profile interview.
- **S2, authorized history:** reduce repetition or improve judgment where that
  history is relevant; otherwise return the same good result.

Keep the current job, world evidence, source quality, and output polish constant
within each comparison. S1 adds only its named detail; S2 retains S1 and adds
only the named historical evidence. This is a design contrast, not a claim
that more history necessarily causes better outcomes. Long-time users still
encounter S0 situations. Current purpose overrides historical defaults.

### Shared fixture rules

All menu wording, people, venues, prices, schedules, and travel durations below
are explicitly **synthetic fixture facts**. They are not advice about operating
New York venues or records of the founder's friends. Production examples need
real eligible sources, freshness, attribution, and rights. Prototype pages must
identify the fictional scenario; a sample never populates personal Life state.

S0 is no *personal history*, not zero information. A user-directed city, a ticket,
or an addressed note is real context when actually supplied. Never infer current
location from a city choice, attendance from a ticket, or preference from opening
an item. No example requires inbox connection, photo-library access, a friend
graph import, background location, or a new universal onboarding flow.

### C1 — a menu becomes understandable

**Entry and fixed job:** a readable fictional menu photograph accompanies
“What's the difference between these two?” It lists potato gnocchi with tomato,
mozzarella, and basil, and fresh ribbon pasta with lemon and butter. The person
wants to understand a contrast. Restaurant identity, attendance, and previous
travel are unknown. This is Ask, not a request to retain the meal.

**S0 — complete return:** show the two dish names beside their literal
descriptions, then the useful distinction:

> One is potato dumplings with tomato and mozzarella; the other is ribbon pasta
> with lemon and butter. The menu describes different bases as well as sauces.

The answer explains rather than choosing for the person. It need not add a
regional story. Texture, provenance, technique, or dietary-safety claims beyond
the menu require adequate evidence; uncertainty is not filled with confident
culinary prose. The person can order or close the app without a follow-up.

**S1 — one detail:** “Texture is what I'm curious about.” Reorganize the same
answer around dumpling versus ribbon, with a sourced explanation of the
textural distinction if available. If preparation details are absent, say that
the menu cannot establish the restaurant's exact result. Do not ask for a
culinary-interest rating. The added value is a better answer to the current
question, not a larger amount of content.

**S2 — relevant history:** the person previously kept an explanation of potato
dough versus wheat dough and explicitly allowed its reuse for cooking questions.
Use that vocabulary without reteaching the basics; make the current distinction
clear on its own. An optional reference can reopen the earlier explanation.
Do not add a cooking assignment, infer that they ate either dish, or announce
that Vesper has discovered their culinary identity.

**Movement and completion:** immediate answer in Chat; Places depth only if
requested and a Place is established. No Home sequel or Life record is owed for
an answer-only interaction. Explicit Keep may preserve the requested material
with quiet receipt and repair. The same answer need not occupy every root.

**Desk-review verdict:** independent S0 value is credible, but translation and
explanation alone are weak differentiation from general assistants. Retain this
entrance without making it carry the entire company story. If history is removed,
the useful contrast must survive; if no new distinction is justified, stop.

### C2 — an open afternoon around a real anchor

**Entry and fixed job:** “I have from 4:30 until this 7 pm event. What could I do
beforehand?” A supplied ticket identifies the venue and date. Starting location
is unknown. The fixed fictional source pack supplies two nearby options: a
bookshop open until 6:30, twelve minutes from the venue, and a garden open until
6, eight minutes from the venue. Those durations are between named places, not
from the user's assumed location. Tickets remain external-provider evidence.

**S0 — complete return:** present two alternatives, not numbered obligations:

> Books, then the event: browse until around 6:15; the venue is a twelve-minute
> walk away. The bookshop closes at 6:30.
>
> A garden pause: spend some time outside before its 6 pm close; the venue is
> eight minutes away. This leaves a larger gap before the event.

Show a small place-to-place map and the known 7 pm anchor, with any ticketed
entry requirement. Do not fabricate a route from the user, a dinner reservation,
or an adopted schedule. These are feasible candidate intervals from the named
places, not a guarantee that the person can reach them by 4:30.

**S1 — one detail:** “Indoors, please.” Elevate the bookshop and remove the garden
from this answer. Explain the useful close/arrival relationship without asking
why indoors. If no indoor option is supported, say so rather than quietly
relaxing the constraint. This is situation fit, not a permanent indoor preference.

**S2 — relevant history:** a still-applicable, explicitly retained preference
says “For performances, I like being at the venue half an hour early.” Adjust
the suggested departure to protect 6:30 arrival and make that assumption easy
to change. Do not book, schedule, or ask again for the same preference. Current
ticket instructions and current requests take precedence.

**Movement and completion:** Chat shapes the alternatives; Places carries
spatial depth. If the person adopts a bounded plan, Home returns its useful
present state and Life organizes authorized retained material through existing
owners. Merely inspecting an option creates no commitment or pending task.
Live help uses current hours, routing, and anchor constraints even on the first
occasion; monitoring later requires its own supported scope, not just a ticket.

**Desk-review verdict:** the strongest practical demonstration here because the
system combines an artifact, the world, time, and choice without requiring a
biography. With source facts unavailable, return what the ticket establishes and
name the missing check; do not pass an unverified interval off as complete help.

### C3 — a friend's contribution becomes usable

**Entry and fixed job:** fictional Maya sends a named recipient a permitted
bookshop link with her authored note: “I liked the back garden; it was quiet
when I went on a weekday morning.” The newcomer opens this handoff. Shared
context exists; personal history, live location, and a plan together do not.
The source pack supplies the shop's location and current published hours.

**S0 — complete return:** lead with Maya's actual contribution and place, then
add the useful practical context:

> Maya's bookshop, with a garden out back.
>
> “It was quiet when I went on a weekday morning.” — Maya
>
> See where it is and when it opens. Weekend quiet isn't established by this note.

In the specimen, the map and actual fixture hours carry that last practical job,
not a button hiding all value. The person can appreciate the recommendation and
leave. No reply, Keep, enrollment, or public performance is required.

**S1 — one detail:** “Could this work for two hours on Saturday?” Compare that
interval with published hours; preserve uncertainty about weekend atmosphere.
If the intended time falls outside the hours, explain that directly. Do not
ask Maya another question on the recipient's behalf or turn her past visit into
real-time availability. Optional spatial exploration remains available.

**S2 — relevant history:** the recipient explicitly kept a nearby café from an
earlier handoff and said to reuse it for outings in this neighborhood. Offer a
coherent bookshop-and-café possibility, checking current hours and distance.
The addition builds on permitted continuity; it does not infer a recurring
friendship ritual or invite either contributor. If the café conflicts with the
current interval, the history earns omission, not ceremonial resurfacing.

**Movement and completion:** addressed Home material or a deep link opens the
same attributed contribution; Places supplies its spatial context. Chat can
help shape an outing. Life exposes only material the recipient is entitled to
retain, and revocation still governs future projections. A possible outing is
not an Occasion until chosen participation warrants one.

**Organizer-side counterpart:** with no history, Maya can send an existing place
and a short authored reason; Vesper resolves the place and prepares a legible
handoff for her chosen audience. Audience action follows the existing boundary.
Her payoff is making her recommendation useful without composing a guide—not
first building a profile for each friend. Recipient context does not magically
solve this sender-side work, and a solitary user still receives C1/C2/C4 value.

**Desk-review verdict:** strongest social entrance, provided the actual human
contribution survives. Avatars and paraphrases are not enough. Joining, replying,
or creating a plan must not be prerequisites for receiving the first value.

### C4 — Home with a city, but no current intention

**Entry and fixed job:** first Home open after selecting New York. No friends,
ticket, personal history, current neighborhood, or physical presence is known.
The source pack contains sourced city possibilities and a clearly separate
sample. For this written exercise the places are fictional; production must
replace them with licensed/eligible, current real material. Browsing itself is
a legitimate job; do not force an intention before displaying anything useful.

**S0 — complete scroll, content order rather than visual specification:**

1. **A worthwhile possibility:** a bookshop courtyard in a named neighborhood,
   showing what makes it worth considering, location, published hours, cost,
   and a photograph with rights. Not “your perfect afternoon” or “near you.”
2. **A compact capability sample:** a visibly labeled fictional event ticket
   becomes two nearby ways to spend the preceding interval, with the resulting
   map visible. Optional “Try with yours” follows the demonstrated value.
3. **A different possibility:** a source-supported indoor activity, with its
   actual time, neighborhood, cost, and any registration requirement. This is
   not another paraphrase of the lead or a step in its plan.
4. **Something worth understanding:** a concise, sourced comparison of two
   local waterfronts that reveals a material difference in how they can be
   experienced. Supply the distinction on the page, not “Ask why they differ.”
5. **A natural end:** further spatial exploration remains available in Places.
   No profile-completion progress bar, friend invitation demand, or reflection
   prompt closes the page.

This is a candidate composition, not five mandatory shelves or a fixed quota.
Its first real offering must earn attention even if the sample is ignored.
The sample explains the exchange; it cannot count as a real personal payoff.
If no city is supplied, offer an optional city search and a labeled sample,
without claiming local relevance or demanding location permission.

**S1 — one detail:** “I'd like something indoors this weekend.” Reorder the same
eligible source pool toward the indoor possibility; expose supported weekend
timing and practical requirements. Preserve other world access rather than
converting the app into an indoor-only feed. This authored refinement happens
because useful options are already present, not as an admission quiz.

**S2 — relevant history:** authorized history includes a pending museum idea the
person asked to revisit on a suitable free weekend. Where current scope and
conditions support it, use that idea as one candidate and reduce re-explanation.
Do not assume the weekend is free, repeat a completed suggestion, or resurface a
revoked intention. Unrelated travel history changes nothing. History need not
make Home longer or make every item personal.

**Movement and completion:** Home delivers the bounded value; Places supports
deeper spatial browsing; Chat receives optional questions or material; Life
holds real retained records, never demo artifacts. Reading can be successful
without a click, though an unobserved silent session does not prove satisfaction.

**Desk-review verdict:** credible but least resolved. The hard dependency is a
reliable supply of worthwhile world material, not personal memory. The
[artifact-led response](claude-design-home-artifact-led-visual-value-response-2026-09-05.md)
already identifies missing content inputs. Sources, freshness, breadth, and
economics must be established before this composition is called deliverable.
Fallback is a smaller truthful page, not invented notices or repeated setup asks.

## 10. Casual questions and useful continuity

September 6 continuation: the founder proposed a more casual return motive—ask
Vesper a question ordinarily taken to a general chat app, because the answer
helps now and authorized context can actively improve later life. This extends
the sparse-context argument; it does not make documenting life the entrance fee.
The following is desk research and a product recommendation, not evidence of
Vesper retention, user preference, or implemented memory behavior.

### 10.1 The distinction is useful continuity, not implicit versus explicit

“Implicit memory” and “explicit memory” bundle independent product choices.
Here they describe software behavior, not the psychological distinction between
implicit and explicit human memory. Separate six dimensions:

| Dimension | Decision Vesper must make |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | What can enter automatically under an actual agreement, versus through a deliberate contribution? |
| Representation | What remains a source, question, fact, uncertain inference, intention, or human-authored meaning? |
| Application | Does authorized context improve understanding, retrieval, capability, coordination, or judgment? |
| Initiative | Is it used in an invoked answer, an on-visit contribution, a permissioned interruption, or an authorized action? |
| Visibility | Can the person understand relevant evidence and its influence without constant memory narration? |
| Control | Can they correct subject/scope, exclude a use, revoke authority, or delete without rebuilding a profile? |

Effortless acquisition does not require opaque representation. Inspectable
memory does not require manually saving every fact. Automatic application does
not imply proactive interruption, and neither implies action authority.

The defensible ambition is **better help through useful continuity**, not
“competitors recall; Vesper acts.” Current adjacent products already combine
automatic memory, explicit controls, and contextual application:

- Claude's current documentation describes automatic topic memory, explicit
  remembering, past-chat search, project boundaries, and memory controls. A
  summary-only description would miss its current behavior. Shared memory with
  cloud Cowork should not be generalized to local Cowork.
  [Official Claude memory documentation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11817273-use-claude-s-chat-search-and-memory-to-build-on-previous-context).
- Gemini documents past-chat personalization and controls, alongside eligible
  connected-app personalization. Cross-context suggestions are not unique to
  Vesper. Product documentation establishes capabilities and constraints, not
  that users find them useful or that they increase retention.
  [Gemini past-chat memory](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469),
  [connected-app personalization](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988?hl=en).
- The preceding research did not establish a sufficiently complete current
  consumer ChatGPT comparison from the official documentation available in that
  pass. Do not infer missing capabilities or label ChatGPT's memory purely
  passive. No competitive superiority claim follows from this review.

Vesper must demonstrate the difference in complete experiences: a good answer
now, governed continuity across relevant objects and circumstances, and a later
contribution that genuinely benefits from that continuity. Memory alone is not
the moat, and competitors can pursue these same benefits.

### 10.2 What the evidence supports—and does not

| Research | Useful finding and limit | Implication to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jones et al., *Users' Expectations and Practices with Agent Memory*, CHI EA 2025](https://brennanjones.com/media/documents/publications/chiea25-666.pdf) | Six interviews and analysis of 54 Reddit threads found uncertainty about memory behavior and differing needs for separation/control. Preliminary qualitative evidence, not population prevalence. | Make scope and use legible; avoid assuming everyone wants either total continuity or total separation. |
| [Zhang et al., *Understanding Users' Privacy Perceptions Towards LLM's RAG-based Memory*, 2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07664) | Interviews with 18 Chinese participants examine convenience, incomplete mental models, inference, and privacy concerns. Not a general consumer acceptance estimate. | Distinguish remembering supplied material from inferring a person; test comprehension of actual behavior. |
| [Aguirre et al., personalization paradox, 2015](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/15747/) | Advertising experiments connect covert collection and vulnerability to reactions to personalization. This is advertising, not longitudinal assistant use. | Test whether people understand how context arrived and why it is used; do not infer that more permission prompts produce trust. |
| [Gilbert, strategic offloading of delayed intentions, 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25404057/) | Four experiments with 1,196 participants investigate external reminders under memory demands. They do not establish AI's ability to infer unspoken intentions. | Remembering can help a future intention happen, but the intention and reminder authority need evidence. |
| [Gick and Holyoak, *Schema Induction and Analogical Transfer*, 1983](https://reasoninglab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/273/2021/04/Gick_Holyoak1983_SchemaInduction.pdf) | Comparing analogs supported schema formation and transfer in laboratory problem-solving. Not evidence that arbitrary personalized associations teach. | Show the shared mechanism and important differences; familiar references alone are not transfer. |
| [Memolet, official UIST 2024 presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KH9yn4pg2bY) | A 12-person within-subject study explores interactive objects for controlling conversational memory reuse. A bounded interface study, not validation of lifelong memory management. | Reusable, inspectable context is promising; do not require users to organize every conversation into objects. |
| [Huang et al., *Controllable Memory Usage*, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.670/) | Technical work examines balancing historical anchoring with new responses. It does not validate a particular consumer control or adoption strategy. | Test irrelevant history, changed purpose, and new interests; more memory influence is not always better. |
| [Zindulka et al., *The AI Memory Gap*, CHI 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11851) | A preregistered experiment with 184 participants examines delayed source-attribution errors in AI-assisted ideation. It is not a finding that all AI memory creates false autobiographies. | Preserve human and AI authorship across summaries and reuse; never return AI interpretation as the person's own statement. |

Together these support design cautions and testable hypotheses, not a proven
retention strategy. Appropriate treatment may matter more than conspicuous
recognition; that is our synthesis, not an independently established causal
result. Contextual inspection and correction are stronger candidates than an
always-visible inferred biography, but their usability remains to be tested.

### 10.3 The casual return loop

The proposed loop is:

> A question or thing has my attention → Vesper helps with it now → within
> existing authority, relevant context can improve a later answer or experience
> → that usefulness gives me a reason to choose Vesper again.

The first payoff must survive with no retention. A correct, helpful response
can end there. The later payoff is conditional, not guaranteed after every
question. Someone may return solely to ask another question, without visiting
Home or organizing Life. Neither daily use nor saving an artifact is required.

Use the existing Product Model's five behavioral memory services rather than
inventing another grammar. Examples are illustrative, not generated world facts:

- **Understanding/capability:** an authorized earlier cooking discussion helps
  explain a new technique through a real mechanism, including where the analogy
  fails. Merely saying “you liked Italian pasta” adds little.
- **Retrieval relief:** recover an entrusted ferry ticket or recipe without
  asking the person to reconstruct the original conversation. Novelty is not
  required for this benefit.
- **Prospective help:** apply an explicit, still-valid intention to suitable
  circumstances; asking about a place does not establish an intention to visit.
- **Relational judgment:** use permitted shared context to reduce repeated
  coordination while preserving each person's private perspective.
- **Enjoyment or possibility:** offer a worthwhile comparison or new opening
  from eligible evidence. It must add substance; it need not turn into a Plan.

Home may return earned value, Places may use spatially relevant context, and
Life may make retained material findable. These are projections through existing
owners, not four mandatory steps. An answer need not become any persistent
object, generated article, or notification.

Return cadence, production cadence, and interruption are separate. Compose for
the person's present after absence; do not accumulate optional content debt.
Unresolved real commitments still require truthful treatment. The aim is
reliable usefulness when attention, intention, or circumstances create an
opening, including casual daily questions—not engineered obligation.

### 10.4 Pending continuity decision

There is a real contract seam, not just a copy problem. The current
[Contribution and Consequence Contract](../systems/contribution-and-consequence.md)
defaults Ask to no new durable personal state and defines transient expiry.
We cannot promise that ordinary questions improve future context while silently
retaining them under another name. Existing authorized memory may inform an
Ask; the Ask does not itself authorize new memory.

**Proposal, not adopted:** explore an optional, understandable conversational
continuity agreement that avoids per-turn saving. Before changing the contract,
resolve and evaluate:

1. **Material and duration:** source conversation, selected facts, summaries,
   or some bounded combination; what expires and what remains accessible?
2. **Eligible uses:** same conversation, cross-conversation answers, Home,
   Places, or other consumers. Storage for chat history is not a general-use
   grant; resurfacing and recommendation can have different permissions.
3. **Exclusions and scope:** sensitive and third-party material, work versus
   personal contexts, temporary inquiries, and questions asked for someone else.
4. **Inference boundary:** a source-grounded question remains a question;
   interest, intention, identity, and human meaning require their own authority.
5. **Controls and repair:** inspect, correct attribution, exclude future
   influence, revoke, or delete; specify effects on dependent projections and
   on records that another canonical owner must retain.
6. **Comprehension and refusal:** explain the actual consequence at a suitable
   moment, without mandatory setup or repeated prompts. Declining must preserve
   immediate value. Continued use is not acceptance.

This proposal changes neither the Ask table nor T0, expiry, audience, action,
or inference authority. It does not establish that an agreement exists in the
current app. Implementation requires a separately adopted contract decision,
owner mapping, and verified repair paths—not a prompt-only memory instruction.

### 10.5 Comparison cases and success criteria

Extend the existing sparse-context cases with matched immediate and later
moments. Compare no history, an accurate callback, and a materially useful
application using the same source quality. Include:

- a casual question with no-retention, ending after a useful answer;
- eligible prior context improving a later question without a new Keep step;
- irrelevant history correctly omitted, or an old interest outweighed by a new
  purpose;
- “that was for my parents” correcting subject and dependent use;
- AI-generated interpretation that must not become attributed human belief;
- retained material excluded from recommendations but still retrievable;
- a month away with current value, no optional backlog, and honest unresolved
  commitments; and
- private context that must not enter an otherwise useful shared contribution.

Evaluate immediate usefulness, avoided repetition, benefit of the later return,
inappropriate associations, source attribution, perceived intrusion, and control
comprehension. Ask whether remembering helped without demanding memory work or
overinterpreting the person. Compare total effort, not just taps. Separate
voluntary repeat choice from engagement caused by prompts, curiosity bait, or
unfinished-work pressure. No participant results or success thresholds are
claimed here; these cases guide design and later evaluation, not a one-loop
gate on the system architecture.
