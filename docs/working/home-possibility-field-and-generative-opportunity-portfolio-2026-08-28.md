# Home Possibility Field and Generative Opportunity Portfolio

## Decision

Home should be the **point-in-time composition of what a person's current
situation, accumulated experience, relationships, and surrounding world make
possible now**.

It is not primarily:

- a trip report;
- a ranked notification queue;
- one recommendation;
- an archive of what Vesper knows;
- a representation of what the AI is thinking; or
- a generic feed dressed in personal context.

Its human question is larger:

> **Given where I am, what is moving, what I have lived, who is in my world,
> and what is happening around me, what can I now notice, understand, do,
> make, enter, share, continue, or leave alone?**

The prior Home work correctly established one dominant stage, current-life
priority, low coercion, contribution before extraction, and root-native
handoffs. It incorrectly promoted the atomic restraint of **one grounded layer
per intervention** into a whole-page ceiling of one world opening, one
relational opening, and one continuity return.

The corrected law is:

> **Home has a strict demand budget and a generous possibility budget.**

One consequence may dominate. One action may require a decision. Several
low-pressure, complete-on-view possibilities may coexist when they are
substantively different, grounded, and composed into a legible whole.

Possibility is not Home's only form of value. A complete composition may
**Make sense** of current or prior experience, **Open possibility**, **Help an
active consequence work**, and **Carry forward** authorized continuity. These
are recursive product moves, not four page sections. Home's job is to compose
their most valuable current expressions under one attention hierarchy.

## 1. The conceptual correction

### 1.1 One dominant thing is not one valuable thing

The experience constitution says one thing should dominate a surface. That is
an attention-hierarchy rule. It does not mean the remainder of Home should be
empty or restricted to one example from each domain.

The distinction is:

```text
dominance budget
  one unmistakable foreground claim, instrument, or consequence

demand budget
  very few decisions, prompts, confirmations, or unfinished obligations

possibility budget
  several grounded ways the current world could open

depth budget
  most comprehensive detail remains in Chat, Places, Life, Plan, or Occasion
```

The user may voluntarily open Home precisely because they want to browse what
their life affords. The correct comparison for each quiet unit is not push or
silence. It is whether the unit contributes enough value to deserve a place in
the composition without becoming a demand.

### 1.2 Atomic restraint versus page composition

The atomic World Opening contract remains correct for the subset of Home units
that actually render an Opening:

```text
one observable cue
+ one non-obvious grounded relationship or mechanism
+ why it matters in this Moment
+ at most one continuation
```

That contract governs one Opening: a grounded, optional possibility made
available now. It does not rename every answer, reconstruction, notification,
social contribution, or operational instrument an Opening. Home may compose
several actual Openings when they occupy different human modes, temporal
horizons, or relationships and do not repeat one another.

Examples:

- one operational stage protects a current commitment;
- one prepared local possibility makes Saturday inhabitable;
- one mechanism changes how a photographed meal can be understood or made;
- one friend-supplied perspective opens a human connection;
- one reconstruction makes the recent journey legible; and
- one farther horizon reveals something that does not require action now.

Six restaurant recommendations are repetition. Six different consequences
from one lived world may be a coherent product.

### 1.3 Compression removes redundancy, not possibility

Forty photographs should not become forty cards. They may support:

- one accurate movement reconstruction;
- two or three substantive curiosity threads;
- one practical capability that transfers home;
- one attributed social mosaic;
- one Place horizon that includes an unvisited Place;
- one future Occasion or return; and
- several dormant possibilities that do not surface today.

Compression should improve the ratio of evidence to value. It should not make
the product appear capable of only one transformation at a time.

## 2. Home renders a personal possible world at time `t`

The underlying relation is:

```text
Person
× Relationships
× Places and Place horizons
× Sources and attention traces
× Plans, Occasions, and Commitments
× current Moment and world conditions
× Occurrences and plural Outcomes
× authorized intentions and continuity
```

Home is a viewer-relative projection of that relation at a particular time:

```text
Home(viewer, t)
  = the most useful composition of current consequence,
    available possibility, relationship, capability, and continuity
```

This prevents several false modes:

- there is no sealed “trip mode” that hides work, health, friendship, or home;
- “returned” does not mean the page becomes a retrospective album;
- a quiet day does not mean the product has no value;
- a recent artifact does not outrank a current opening by recency alone; and
- a current commitment does not erase the wider life unless urgency requires it.

### 2.1 Four temporal horizons can coexist

| Horizon | Human job | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Immediate** | Protect or use the present | leave now, repair, translate, orient, decide, stay quiet |
| **Available window** | Make the day or week inhabitable | tonight, after dinner, Saturday, the next open hour |
| **Emerging continuation** | Let prior life change a near-future experience | cook, host, compare, ask someone, try a local expression |
| **Farther horizon** | Keep the world expansive without urgency | another Place, season, return, reading, unvisited relationship, future Occasion |

These are not four permanent sections or separate queues. They are candidate
dimensions the composer uses to avoid collapsing every possibility into
“nearby now” or “memory.”

## 3. The generative opportunity model

### 3.1 Evidence is not content; it is capability-bearing material

The same Source can support different consequences at different times. A
ferry ticket may first help someone board, then bind a journey, later explain
how a base shaped movement, and eventually contribute to a new waterfront day
or a better transfer decision. None of those consequences is automatic.

The candidate pipeline is:

```text
authorized evidence
  -> resolve Place, people, time, Occasion, and truth state
  -> enumerate credible consequence families
  -> join current world truth and present human context
  -> perform a new editorial or operational contribution
  -> compare each candidate with silence and with one another
  -> compose a diverse possibility portfolio
  -> choose native Home form and owned continuation
```

### 3.2 Candidate axes

The composer should be able to reason across at least these axes:

| Axis | Values |
| --- | --- |
| **Human mode** | attend, go, make, understand, relate, coordinate, contribute, continue, return |
| **Value family** | operational, spatial, interpretive, creative, social, prospective, reconstructive, reciprocal |
| **Time** | now, later today, this week, next occasion, seasonal, dormant |
| **Space** | here, nearby, along a route, home-city transfer, focal Place, Place horizon, elsewhere |
| **Social scope** | private, named person, pair, Occasion, recurring circle, public Place |
| **Direction** | deepen, connect, contrast, continue, socially translate, safely surprise, retire |
| **Effort** | complete on view, glance, walk, try, make, coordinate, commit |
| **Authority** | read, offer, prepare, confirm, affected-principal decision, delegated action |
| **Claim type** | world fact, occurrence, evidence relation, operational consequence, human-authored meaning |

This is an opportunity tensor, not a mandate for one database table or an
exhaustive grid in the UI.

### 3.3 Human-facing possibility modes

Home should make several modes legible without exposing the internal tensor:

1. **Attend** — something genuinely needs awareness, protection, or decision.
2. **Enter** — a Place, route, event, meal, neighborhood, or open interval is
   ready to be inhabited.
3. **Notice** — a grounded distinction changes what is perceptible in the
   present world.
4. **Understand** — a mechanism, history, comparison, or source makes an
   observed thing more intelligible.
5. **Make or practice** — experience becomes cooking, hosting, drawing,
   photographing, language, craft, movement, or another form of capability.
6. **Relate** — another person's perspective becomes available; something can
   be addressed, offered, asked, shared, or lived together.
7. **Contribute** — a useful correction, perspective, acknowledgment, or piece
   of evidence can responsibly return to a person, Occasion, or Place.
8. **Continue** — a prior question, intention, ritual, or unfinished thread has
   a fitting next state.
9. **Return or branch** — a Place may be revisited differently, or its horizon
   may make a related or contrasting Place available.
10. **Recognize** — a reconstruction, recurrence, anomaly, or factual register
    becomes valuable without demanding interpretation.

No Home needs all ten. A mature Home system should be able to generate and
compose from all ten.

## 4. What rich lived evidence can become

### 4.1 Movement: planes, trains, ferries, walks, and transfers

Possible contributions include:

- prepare departure, arrival, and connection consequences;
- repair disruption and preserve receipts or rights;
- reconstruct the actual journey, including changes and gaps;
- show how a temporary base reorganized reachability;
- make elevation, water, transfer, and arrival semantics reusable capability;
- compare two ways cities reveal themselves through arrival;
- prepare a local experience whose route—not merely destination—is valuable;
- recognize a repeated or anomalous movement pattern without psychologizing;
- open a future rail, ferry, walking, or neighborhood possibility;
- connect movement to a companion's experience or an attributed trace; or
- retire sensitive operational details while preserving useful structure.

### 4.2 Hotels, homes, and temporary bases

A base can support more than a booking card:

- how the base changed the usable city;
- which daily radius became familiar;
- how returning, resting, working, or meeting functioned there;
- hotel, café, lounge, library, and other third-space distinctions;
- a comparison between tourist center, residential base, and transport node;
- a home-city exploration through the logic of dwelling rather than sights;
- better future base selection for the people and Occasion present;
- hosting someone through a personally known version of a city; and
- acknowledgment of people whose service materially shaped the experience.

### 4.3 Menus, meals, ingredients, receipts, and dining

The same food evidence can open:

- translation, identification, dietary safety, and immediate choice;
- a technique or mechanism behind a sensory difference;
- comparison across dishes, regions, restaurants, or service styles;
- an ingredient, producer, market, tool, or craft behind the meal;
- something to cook or learn rather than somewhere else to consume;
- a local Place where a precise distinction is perceivable;
- a dinner to host or a ritual to continue with particular people;
- a question for a friend, chef, resident, or family member;
- an attributed cultural or artistic connection;
- a future Place horizon grounded in lineage rather than similarity; and
- expense, merchant, or occurrence reconciliation when practical truth is the
  real job.

### 4.4 Photographs and visual attention

Photographs can support:

- Place or object identification;
- orientation and visible geography;
- an annotated mechanism grounded in the image;
- a contact sheet revealing recurrence or anomaly;
- a route or sequence reconstructed from time and Place evidence;
- changes in one Place across time or viewpoint;
- a comparison with an attributed friend's image;
- an invitation to notice the same thing elsewhere;
- a future viewpoint, walk, or Place horizon;
- a chosen expressive artifact or Status; and
- a separately governed public Place contribution.

The system must not infer inner meaning, stable taste, attendance by every
depicted person, or permission from faces alone.

### 4.5 Films, books, music, tickets, exhibitions, and other media

Media can become part of the lived-world model when it changes a Place or is
changed by one:

- a political or historical mechanism visible in a current monument;
- a fictional geography complicated by the physical Place;
- a work's material, architectural, musical, culinary, or ritual lineage;
- a route through several physical manifestations of one question;
- a current exhibition, screening, performance, book, or conversation;
- a related medium the user did not already name;
- a companion's professional, family, or cultural connection;
- a contrast that prevents one familiar interest from becoming a filter; and
- a later return in which the work is understood differently.

The standard remains known-to-person novelty. Repeating the connection the
person already made is not a contribution.

### 4.6 Climate, heat, terrain, bodily effort, and restoration

These are not merely constraints around “real content.” They can reveal how a
Place works:

- shade, arcades, waterfronts, hours, materials, and street life as climate
  adaptation;
- experienced reachability rather than flat distance;
- why one route, meal time, or social pattern fits local conditions;
- how restoration, separation, and reunion protect a shared Occasion;
- a home-city possibility shaped by season and actual energy; and
- procedural capability for later hot, steep, crowded, or transfer-heavy days.

Vesper should interpret the physical and operational relation, not diagnose
the person's body, emotion, or identity.

### 4.7 People and relationships

Relationship context can open:

- a borrowed lens from someone who knows the Place;
- an asynchronous trace left for a later arrival;
- a grounded comparison of parallel experiences;
- a natural reason to address someone without drafting synthetic intimacy;
- a question routed to the human who can answer it best;
- a dinner, walk, visit, or other lightweight Occasion;
- temporary divergence and smooth reunion;
- a ritual, return promise, or unfinished shared question;
- recognition or thanks for someone who enabled an experience;
- a low-pressure Status or featured share that creates social legibility; and
- a better second Occasion produced from plural prior Outcomes.

The relationship is not content supply. The strongest multiplayer consequence
may be a conversation Vesper enables and then leaves.

### 4.8 Corrections, breakdowns, and things that did not happen

Contradiction and absence can also create value:

- a booked movement that was cancelled;
- a Place considered but deliberately skipped;
- a route whose apparent convenience failed in practice;
- a dinner or visit that changed shape;
- an unvisited Place that still explains the region;
- a source that was wrong or stale;
- an intention that should expire; and
- a group pattern that should not be reused with different people.

Home may use these to repair, prevent repetition, reveal a counterfactual
horizon, or remain silent. It must not turn every miss into unfinished debt.

## 5. Europe to New York: a broad opportunity portfolio

This section enumerates candidates before ranking. It is intentionally broader
than one Home page. Exact Places, schedules, exhibitions, events, prices, and
historical claims remain fixture placeholders until current authoritative
research supports them.

### 5.1 Enter New York again

- a water-led half day in New York whose transit and arrival are part of the
  experience, not merely a ferry-themed recommendation;
- a shaded or indoor/outdoor route selected against the present weather and
  post-travel energy;
- a neighborhood entered through how it was built, reused, or reached;
- one open Saturday expressed as two or three meaningfully different shapes:
  social, solitary, and restorative;
- a local movement sequence that ends near an existing commitment rather than
  creating a competing Plan;
- a familiar Place shown through one unfamiliar, source-bound layer; and
- an ordinary repeated route whose seasonal or historical change has become
  visible.

### 5.2 Do, make, and practice

- cook one pasta technique made legible by the photographed meals;
- build a market-to-table route around a specific ingredient or method;
- host friends using one learned distinction without staging a themed trip
  reenactment;
- compare how the same preparation changes across regional or diaspora
  contexts;
- learn how to read a menu term likely to recur;
- compose a small photo sequence, map, or route from actual sources;
- try a low, wind-stable floral arrangement whose practical constraints came
  from the Capri Occasion;
- notice boat, stone, ceramic, textile, or hospitality craft through one
  sourced local expression; and
- apply the trip's pacing lesson to protect a future evening without claiming
  a personality trait.

### 5.3 See and understand more

- explain a new political mechanism behind Aeneas, Augustus, Virgil, and Roman
  public order beyond the founder's original Troy connection;
- make accumulated reuse visible in one Roman object and one grounded New York
  contrast without declaring the cities equivalent;
- connect carpaccio's reported artistic naming to a broader question about how
  dishes, colors, and visual culture travel;
- show how cliff, marina, base, and ferry reorganized Sorrento spatially;
- distinguish “preserved ancient city” from a city repeatedly quarried,
  inhabited, rebuilt, and excavated;
- compare how two coastal Places staged working waterfront, leisure, and
  tourism;
- open the cultural logic inside meal times, shade, public space, service, or
  movement rather than treating culture as a museum category; and
- carry a question from Europe into an unexpected practical field such as
  climate adaptation, urban logistics, hospitality labor, or materials.

### 5.4 Through people

- align authorized Rome and Paris evidence along a real dimension neither
  person observed alone;
- let the friend in Paris add or challenge the comparison in their own voice;
- leave one Place-specific observation for a friend traveling later;
- ask the Sorrento-knowledgeable friend for judgment where human authority is
  stronger than generated synthesis;
- turn “just returned from Nice and Sorrento” into a temporary, audience-bound
  social aperture;
- invite several people into a dinner or walk that grew from the trip without
  requiring an Occasion profile;
- acknowledge the concierge, captain, photographer, host, or other person who
  enabled the Occasion where appropriate;
- make a shared Place or ritual available to the couple without merging their
  private Outcomes; and
- use an absent person's perspective when a current New York Place makes it
  newly relevant.

### 5.5 Carry forward and branch

- return to southern Italy in another season for a meaningfully different
  experience rather than a repeated itinerary;
- let an unvisited Riviera or Campania Place clarify the region without adding
  it to a bucket list;
- carry one food, movement, or urban-history question into a future journey;
- recognize when a film, exhibition, book, or public event provides the next
  piece of an existing thread;
- create a prospective cue that retires after the fitting Place or Occasion;
- use the proposal-day choreography as bounded procedural learning for another
  consequential Occasion;
- revisit a New York Place with a different companion and therefore a
  different relational projection; and
- preserve a return promise or group ritual without turning it into a reminder
  campaign.

### 5.6 Reconstruct and recognize

- the actual Nice–Sorrento–Capri–Amalfi–Rome movement graph;
- intended versus actual travel after cancellation and rerouting;
- a ticket-to-photograph sequence for one crossing;
- a map of temporary bases and what became reachable from each;
- a contact sheet of a real visual recurrence, with no personality conclusion;
- a meal sequence showing one technique or regional contrast;
- one shared Occurrence with separately attributed perspectives;
- a factual register of hotels, restaurants, and transport where factual
  utility is the honest product; and
- an unresolved gap or contradiction offered for correction only when the
  correction has future consequence.

### 5.7 Reciprocal and public possibilities

- contribute a verified, time-bounded port or entrance correction;
- offer a source-bound menu translation or cultural distinction to a Place;
- return an outcome to someone whose attributed lens helped;
- thank or recognize a service contributor;
- share a useful route caveat without publishing private movement history;
- contribute one contested Place question rather than one universal verdict;
- retain disagreement among resident, worker, historian, operator, and visitor
  perspectives; and
- decline to publish when the material's primary value is private or
  relational.

## 6. Adjacent products clarify the whitespace

The adjacent products validate pieces of the substrate while stopping short of
the Vesper opportunity.

### 6.1 Apple Journaling Suggestions

Apple's current developer contract can assemble recent personal events from
places, people, photographs, music, workouts, and other signals, then provide
high-level information only after the person selects a suggestion. This
validates cross-modal event formation and privacy-preserving selection.

Its dominant consequence is still a prompt into a creative or reflective
workflow. Vesper's opportunity is to perform the first useful intellectual or
practical move itself: explain, reconstruct, compare, prepare, connect, or open
the world before asking the person to author anything.

Source: [Apple Journaling Suggestions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/journalingsuggestions).

### 6.2 Google Photos and Google Maps Timeline

Google Photos can retrieve details, answer questions over the gallery, identify
themes, create highlights, and remix media. Google Maps Timeline can reconstruct
visited places, routes, transport modes, and trip summaries with correction and
privacy controls.

These products validate that large personal archives can become searchable and
composed. Their center remains recall, media, location history, or sharing.
Vesper's center is what the prior evidence changes in the current lived world:
the next Place, skill, conversation, Plan, Occasion, or deliberate silence.

Sources: [Ask Photos](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/photos/updates-ask-photos-search/),
[Google Maps Timeline controls](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/updates-to-location-history-and-new-controls-coming-soon-to-maps/).

### 6.3 Spotify daylist

Spotify's daylist shows that a home experience can recompose several times per
day around temporal context and prior behavior rather than remaining one static
profile. It also demonstrates the appeal—and risk—of labeling a person through
generated taste language.

Vesper should adopt temporal recomposition while rejecting psychographic
performance. The current Place, people, weather, commitments, and prior
experience should change the offered possibilities; Home does not need to name
which “version” of the person it believes is present.

Source: [Spotify daylist](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2023-09-12/ever-changing-playlist-daylist-music-for-all-day/).

### 6.4 Episodic future thinking

Research on constructive episodic simulation supports the broad proposition
that details from past experiences can be flexibly recombined in imagining
novel future events. It also identifies a source-confusion risk when details
from several episodes are combined.

The product implication is not that Vesper knows the user's desired future. It
is that prior evidence can supply truthful building blocks for prepared
possibilities, while the system keeps source, inference, simulation, and actual
occurrence distinct.

Sources: [Addis et al., 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19041331/),
[Schacter, Benoit, and Szpunar, 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5675579/).

## 7. A broader Home composition

### 7.1 Stable semantic regions

Home should have stable semantics without a rigid number of cards:

1. **World read** — where and when the person is, what shape the present has.
2. **Dominant stage** — the one thing allowed to command the first viewport.
3. **In motion** — compact durable state and affected people.
4. **Possibility field** — several distinct, grounded trajectories across
   entering, making, understanding, relating, and continuing.
5. **Continuity and recognition** — evidence-rich returns, reconstructions,
   capabilities, and farther horizons.
6. **Stable doors** — comprehensive Plans/Occasions, Places, Life, and
   contextual Chat.

The visible page need not label these literally. It may use an editorial lead,
route field, small multiples, attributed lanes, a compact mechanism, state
rows, and a horizontal horizon. “Card” is never the semantic unit.

### 7.2 Possibility-field composition rules

The composer should:

- prefer different human modes over repeated examples of one mode;
- allow several complete-on-view contributions because they create no response
  debt;
- limit simultaneous action-bearing asks more aggressively than readable
  contributions;
- preserve at least one aperture beyond obligation in all non-urgent states;
- let current life and recent continuity intermingle when the causal bridge is
  real;
- show both practical and interpretive value without forcing either into every
  unit;
- make people visible through attribution, affected state, invitation, and
  shared possibility rather than activity metrics;
- include farther horizons without converting them into a recommendation or
  bucket-list queue; and
- collapse toward utility during urgency, disruption, or protected immersion.

### 7.3 Post-return New York: broadened full scroll

This is one composition study, not a content quota.

Six alternative full-scroll compositions using this same evidence now live in
[Post-Return New York Home Possible Worlds Gallery](post-return-new-york-home-possible-worlds-gallery-2026-08-28.md).
They test prospective, interpretive, capability, multiplayer, reconstructive,
and adaptive whole-page theses rather than assuming the structure below is the
only correct answer.

```text
NEW YORK · THURSDAY
Home again. The weekend has several ways in.

[Dominant stage]
A fully prepared Saturday possibility fitted to current weather,
time, reachability, and existing commitments.

[In motion]
Two or three current Plans, people, or recovery rows.

[Ways into the weekend]
GO       one grounded local experience shape
MAKE     one practical or creative transfer from Europe
SEE      one substantial local lens or current cultural opening
WITH     one authorized human possibility

[Your world is connected]
One Place horizon, cross-place mechanism, or attributed social mosaic
that neither repeats known material nor requires action.

[What the journey now lets you do]
One or two capability returns: movement, cooking, hosting, noticing,
judgment, or another practical form.

[What actually happened]
An evidence-first movement, Occasion, or factual register.

[Farther horizon]
A future Place, season, return, source, or unfinished question with no urgency.

[Stable doors]
All plans and occasions · New York in Places · Europe in Life · Chat
```

The top remains glanceable. The full scroll demonstrates the breadth of the
product. A person can leave after the lead, browse several possibilities, or
enter the appropriate root without manually reconstructing context.

### 7.4 Density by situation

| Situation | Dominant posture | Possibility field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Urgent disruption** | restore control | mostly suppressed until recovery is stable |
| **Live and moving** | protect the present | one or two contextual apertures below practical truth |
| **Protected Occasion** | preserve privacy and choreography | viewer-safe and intentionally narrow |
| **Planning or anticipating** | turn intention into shape | rich in alternatives, people, Place, and future structure |
| **Returned** | re-enter current life | richest cross-time transfer, social, capability, and reconstruction state |
| **Ordinary open day** | make the local world available | broad present-world possibility across several modes |
| **Cold start** | demonstrate useful range without false familiarity | world-grounded possibilities plus universal Ask/Bring doors |

“Quiet” describes pressure and visual atmosphere. It should often be one of
the richest possibility states because there is room to browse without an
urgent consequence.

## 8. Root coherence under the broader model

Home can reveal more without owning everything.

| Root | Role after Home opens a possibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Select, compose, and make the aperture legible in relation to the whole life now |
| **Chat** | Explain, challenge, compare, imagine, author, negotiate, and shape |
| **Places** | Situate, map, verify, browse alternatives, and inhabit the physical world |
| **Life** | Inspect sources, relationships, reconstructions, plural Outcomes, corrections, and long continuity |
| **Plan / Occasion** | Own commitment, shared participation, authority, dependency, and live reconciliation |

The handoff should preserve the candidate's Place, time, people, evidence,
current question, and intended consequence. Home is valuable on view; the next
root provides depth or action rather than supplying the value Home withheld.

## 9. Ranking and diversity doctrine

The system should not use one score to pick the top `N` objects. It should:

1. remove false, stale, unauthorized, redundant, or known-to-person candidates;
2. identify the one candidate with the strongest present consequence;
3. reserve the dominant stage for it;
4. group remaining candidates by human mode, time horizon, social scope, and
   effort;
5. select a diverse portfolio whose members add different powers;
6. limit demands and decisions more aggressively than complete-on-view value;
7. preserve one or more expansive apertures in non-urgent states; and
8. route comprehensive inventory to the owning roots.

Useful composition constraints include:

- no two adjacent units whose only job is venue recommendation;
- no more than one unresolved user decision in the first viewport;
- no question when Vesper has not yet contributed value;
- no retrospective unit whose only justification is recency;
- no social unit whose only proposition is that someone else did something;
- no cross-place edge without a named dimension and important difference;
- no future simulation presented as prediction or memory; and
- no artificial empty state while grounded, complete-on-view value exists.

## 10. Evaluation

The broader Home succeeds when:

- the first viewport is immediately understandable;
- one thing clearly dominates without monopolizing the possible world;
- the full scroll reveals several distinct things the person could see, do,
  make, understand, share, or continue;
- past evidence changes present possibility rather than merely resurfacing;
- a person encounters value without being asked to reflect, classify, or
  respond;
- current life is not displaced by the most data-rich recent Occasion;
- people appear as sources of perception, care, action, and shared possibility;
- a Place can open outward spatially, culturally, historically, practically,
  relationally, or counterfactually;
- a person can enter Chat, Places, Life, or an owned object without losing the
  causal context; and
- the system can still become sparse when physical urgency, privacy, or
  presence makes restraint valuable.

Candidate measures:

- **possibility diversity:** distinct human modes represented, not card count;
- **contribution density:** proportion of visible units that add a mechanism,
  reconstruction, distinction, transfer, social mosaic, or prepared consequence;
- **demand load:** number and consequence of questions, decisions, and pending
  obligations created;
- **complete-on-view value:** useful units that require no tap or response;
- **branch quality:** whether a handoff preserves context and deepens the same
  opportunity;
- **present transfer:** whether prior evidence changes an action, capability,
  relationship, or understanding now;
- **second-occasion delta:** whether later judgment is materially different;
  and
- **expansion:** whether Home opens new dimensions without enclosing the person
  in prior preferences.

Anti-metrics:

- number of cards generated;
- time spent scrolling;
- prompts answered;
- memories resurfaced;
- Places saved;
- content opened for curiosity-gap reasons; and
- social response rate treated as relationship value.

## 11. Architectural implications, not authorization

The contextual engine should generate a **portfolio** of evidence-bound value
candidates before surface composition. Arbitration and composition are related
but not identical:

```text
cross-domain candidate generation
  -> authority, truth, freshness, and known-to-person admission
  -> consequence and interruption arbitration
  -> possibility diversification
  -> Home composition
```

An attention arbiter may choose one foreground treatment while a Home composer
admits several quieter read-only or optional openings. Treating both as the
same top-one ranking problem will recreate the current narrow surface.

Likely conceptual additions to the existing projection receipt are:

- human mode;
- temporal horizon;
- effort and demand level;
- complete-on-view status;
- diversity relation to adjacent candidates;
- simulated-future versus current-world claim type; and
- portfolio displacement reason.

This does not authorize a universal Opportunity table, a graph database, free-
form server UI, client-side independent recommendations, or automatic writes
from every Source. Existing Person, Relationship, Place, Source, Plan,
Occasion, Moment, Outcome, and audience authorities remain canonical.

## 12. Documents that must align

This direction requires narrow corrections rather than replacing the product
thesis:

- keep the Product Thesis's atomic “one grounded layer” rule;
- revise Surfacing Strategy from “one useful opening” to one dominant
  consequence plus a bounded possibility portfolio;
- revise Place Interpretation's Home row in the same way;
- revise the four-root study's “one or two grounded openings” ceiling;
- revise the Home Editorial Composition Pack's shared shell and its cap on
  world, relational, and continuity openings;
- clarify in the Editorial and Content Canon that “highest-value current
  contribution at a glance” governs the lead and first viewport, not the
  entire full scroll; and
- retain current-life-first, value-before-input, authorship, privacy,
  provenance, and complete-on-view doctrine.

## 13. Open design questions

1. What visual composition makes several possible trajectories feel like one
   authored world rather than a shelf system?
2. Which modes need stable spatial positions, and which can emerge only when
   evidence exists?
3. How should Home distinguish “available now,” “worth knowing,” and “possible
   later” without turning them into urgency tiers?
4. When can one lead instrument include alternate shapes without becoming a
   comparison task?
5. How many complete-on-view contributions can coexist before visual
   comprehension declines?
6. How should the composer preserve serendipity across domains while rejecting
   random novelty?
7. Which future possibilities may be composed from past evidence without
   implying desire, prediction, or identity?
8. How should the product expose why a surprising possibility exists without
   narrating surveillance?
9. When should a human source replace Vesper's explanation entirely?
10. How should the same possibility mature across Home, Places, Chat, Plan,
    Occasion, and Life without appearing duplicated?

## Closing principle

Vesper does not earn the right to collect more of life by producing a prettier
record of it. It earns participation when fragments from life return as new
ways to inhabit the world.

> **The past is not a content supply for Home. It is one set of truthful
> building blocks from which the present world can become more possible.**
