## 1. Start here: what you are being asked to do

Create a **new Claude Design project for the next-generation Chat experience**.
Suggested project name: **Vesper — Chat**. This is a fresh visual and interaction
exploration, not a patch to the old Chat export and not production implementation.
Find earlier design references through Design Sync as described below; do not
wait for recovery of a local Chat export.

The founder wants to see genuinely different, appealing possibilities before
selecting a composition. Start by drawing—not by producing another architecture
essay or a giant inventory of error states. The detailed cases below supply
meaningful content and later coverage; they are **not a prescribed page layout**.

**Design question:** What should it feel like to converse with an intelligence
that understands the lived world, can work across the things in your life, and
helps immediately without making you administer a system?

Chat is a first-class root alongside Home, Places and Life. It is not merely an
ingestion box, a travel-planning chatbot, an agent-job dashboard or a mandatory
gateway to information that is already visible elsewhere.

## 3. The product promise the drawings should make tangible

Vesper helps a person and the people around them understand and inhabit the
world more fully. Bring what already has attention: a question, photo, document,
place, invitation or changing situation. It can make sense, open possibility,
help something work and carry useful context forward. These are a repertoire,
not four buttons, four sections or four required steps.

The desired feeling is **easy to begin, interesting to continue, capable when
needed, and easy to leave**. The person should not need to know the right noun,
write an elaborate prompt, name a thread, choose a workflow or organize evidence
before receiving help. Clean does not mean empty, timid or incapable.

Three levels of value must be credible:

- Almost no context: help with the thing in front of the person.
- A little context: make a better comparison, preparation or connection.
- Accumulated authorized context: avoid re-explanation and improve judgment,
  including recognizing when old context should not influence this answer.

Do not collapse these into a maturity score or onboarding progression. The
first level must work on its own. A complete answer can be enough; the user
does not owe Keep, a trip, an article, a share or a follow-up.

## 4. Settled principles versus open design space

### Preserve these product principles

- Home returns current value; Places opens the spatial world; Life organizes
  continuity. Chat expresses, answers, composes, operates and repairs across
  those owners. All four can contain actions and useful output.
- Ordinary conversation and serious practical work belong in the same product.
  A general entry does not promise equal expertise in every domain.
- Use material only within its actual permission. Asking about an attachment
  does not automatically retain it or authorize sharing. Do not expose an
  Ask/Bring classification quiz to the person.
- Human Reply and private Ask are different destinations. AI should support
  relationships without sending words or implying another person's agreement.
- Keep the product's value visible without an ontology, tool catalog, memory
  dashboard or mandatory review queue on the main surface.

### Explore these freely; no answer has been selected here

- The resting Chat root: ready composer, ongoing conversation, recent topics,
  or another clean arrangement. What happens on first open versus returning?
- The relationship between Chat root, new conversation and history; navigation
  depth, discoverability and whether these need separate full screens.
- Message anatomy, human/AI identity, typography, spacing, attachment placement,
  answer width and the transition between conversation and a substantial result.
- How much capability is evident before typing, without a promotional carousel
  or feed of suggestions. Concrete engagement can be quiet and compelling.
- Context attached from another root: chip, heading, inline original or another
  treatment. Make scope legible without turning it into a permanent selector.
- Voice and visual input where supported; animation and keyboard posture;
  how much source detail belongs in-line versus behind an ordinary door.
- Which current work remains visible after leaving and how a returning person
  resumes or starts fresh without being forced to manage threads.

The existing System Sans transcript and rounded composer are useful donors,
**not an instruction to reproduce their exact layout in every alternative**.
Use the shared Vesper language as context. If an alternative changes a visual
role, show why and label the proposed delta; do not quietly create a new token
system or require all neighboring projects to change.

## 5. Round 1 — visual exploration before convergence

Produce **three genuinely different directions**, chosen and named by you.
Do not limit the differences to palette, border radius or copy. Vary meaningful
composition or interaction choices, while holding the evidence and capability
constant so that one direction does not win through invented intelligence.

For each direction, show the same four anchor moments:

1. First/resting Chat open, ready for an ordinary question.
2. A photo/question exchange with a complete useful answer and one natural
   follow-up. Show the actual content, not “AI response goes here.”
3. Entry from a visible place or arrangement, producing useful practical help.
4. Returning after leaving: preserve the relevant work and make starting
   something else easy. Include enough of the keyboard/input posture to make
   the composition credible on a phone.

Include one realistic longer exchange per direction—not only perfect first
viewports. Let the result determine whether a table, small comparison, source
preview, map fragment or plain paragraph is the best expression. No medium quota.

Show the directions beside one accepted Home/Places or Life frame as a family
check, not a demand for pixel sameness. Provide a short rationale, the tradeoff
and your recommendation. Do not assume the most minimal or most editorial wins.

**Stop for founder selection after Round 1.** Do not implement production,
migrate the six projects or exhaust every exception before the visual direction
is selected. Selection can combine strengths; it need not blend everything.

## 6. Shared situations and content for the exploration

These are design fixtures grounded in the founder's stories, not new claims
about the founder, friends or current conditions. Invented people, messages,
times and places must be labeled as fixture material in board notes. Do not
invent facts in the user-facing answer to make the product look capable.

| Situation | What the person brings / wants | What a convincing experience gives back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pasta, here and now | A dish photo and “Why does this sauce feel different from what I make at home?” | A useful mechanism or distinction, appropriate uncertainty about the specific kitchen, and an optional practical technique. Not a restaurant list or a claim about the person's identity. |
| Cliffs beyond Sorrento | “Those cliffs in Sorrento were striking. What else is like that?” | A substantive comparison with important similarities/differences, not merely “you like dramatic coastlines.” Research geographic/geological claims before treating them as factual. |
| A familiar reference opens something foreign | The person already connected a film and the Trojan/Roman story they encountered | Go beyond repeating that connection. Show a supported new distinction or question, and avoid assuming the historical/mythological account is straightforward fact. |
| An ordinary NYC weekend | Little context, limited available time; sometimes curious, sometimes tired | Useful options or an answer without demanding an import spree. Rest, familiar places or staying home can be good outcomes. No obligation to relive the Europe trip. |
| A friend's perspective | A deliberately shared Paris note or imperfect cooking photo | The exact original remains valuable. Optional AI comparison adds something real; Reply reaches the human, private Ask reaches Vesper. No reciprocal posting gate. |
| Saturday needs to change | A current arrangement, known people, a practical constraint | Grounded assessment, an optional prepared editable message where allowed, and a readable outcome. Knowing a name is not sharing authority; a proposal is not an adopted Plan change. |
| Ticket alone versus a ticket question | Identical source, different wording or invoked input door | Useful interpretation first, with the right quiet consequence. Retention is not inferred from file type and no classification ceremony is required. |
| Day 1, day 2, day 5 | Evidence arrives, then an explanation, then related evidence later | Context can be updated and used within an actual agreement; corrections keep their subject and time. No automatic biography, required daily entries or per-turn saving as the intended long-term experience. |

For Round 1 use the pasta/photo and Saturday cases across all directions; use
an ordinary first open and return as the other anchors. The remaining cases
pressure-test the selected direction in Round 2 rather than becoming eight
separate homes or modes.

## 7. Round 2 — complete the selected experience

After selection, develop a coherent screen/state family, reusing primitives
instead of assigning each bullet a new screen:

- Resting/new and returning Chat, useful starters if chosen, history/search and
  draft continuity. Current truth remains directly accessible outside history.
- Text, selected photo/file, pasted link and supported voice; remove attachment
  or context without losing words; cancel, denied access, unsupported material.
- Streaming and substantial completed answers; source inspection; partial help,
  failure/retry and interrupted work without fake progress or a filing queue.
- Contextual question, correction and action from Home/Places/Life/Entity/Plans;
  actual affected target and audience; result and return to the originating item.
- Finite requested work: acceptance, leave, later result and failure. Do not
  confuse it with background following; no internal job ledger as Chat's home.
- Private versus addressed/shared preparation, with explicit human Send where
  required and accurate readback if only part of an authorized operation succeeds.
- Relevant prior context, changed current purpose, an excluded conversation and
  an unavailable source. The same interaction must remain useful without history.

For a useful answer, showing no CTA is a valid completed state. For consequential
work, showing a button without its effect and return is not completion.

## 8. Product-policy boundaries without designing a governance console

Optional ordinary-conversation continuity is accepted **direction**, but its
material, duration and reuse agreement remain unadopted. It is legitimate to
explore the intended experience in a clearly labeled proposal alongside a
current-policy case. Do not present “we remember everything” as the service.
History, source custody, future influence, sharing and action are distinct.

Bookings, payments, rebooking and autonomous provider contact are not the
product's execution promise. Preserve provider handoff without pretending the
provider acted. A finite research request and a watch have different endings;
show only supported/accepted services as effective. Prices, limits and paid
expansion are not selected by this project.

Keep these conditions in board notes and at actual consequential boundaries,
not in every chat bubble. Trust should mostly be experienced through competent,
predictable behavior, not continuous permission narration.

Is the social audience understandable? Does the chosen direction work at larger
text and narrow widths? Do not reduce the review to button counts or copy nits.
