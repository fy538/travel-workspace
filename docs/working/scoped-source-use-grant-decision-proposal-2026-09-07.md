# Decision proposal: scoped use of a received contribution

## 1. Status and audit correction

**Proposed, not adopted or implemented.** This September 7 revision corrects
the earlier assertion that T0 is only a metric. Contribution admission and
effective-retention enforcement already exist and are called from pending Chat
and intake. An authorized cross-user source path and complete no-write coverage
still require verification; do not weaken owner checks to make a prototype work.

The proposed immediate-question scope is one capability, not the complete
social intelligence policy and not a blanket ban on future Home/Places value.

## 2. Three distinct receiving capabilities

The [September 9 decision](../decisions/2026-09-09-exact-original-recipient-display.md)
now adopts exact selected-original display following an explicit Send to one
eligible account recipient. It does not adopt this document's immediate-question
AI-use proposal, grant acquisition alternatives or later composition rights.
Original display implementation and AI-use policy must not block or silently
authorize each other.

| Capability | Value | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original human material | Enjoy the person's photo, words or eligible object without AI. | Effective delivery/display, audience and custody rights. |
| Independent world context | Useful venue information, explanation or practical help alongside the object. | Source it independently; do not pass restricted human content to the model or imply the person endorsed the result. |
| Recipient-specific composition using the contribution | Their perspective can improve a private answer or, under separately effective scope, a Home/Places possibility. | Resolve purpose, eligible consumer, retention, inference, audience and repair. Visibility alone is insufficient. |

These are internal distinctions, not three mandatory user settings or product
modes. A card may remain entirely original. A current world fact can be useful
without a friend-source grant.

## 3. Narrow immediate-question proposal

An effective author grant may permit eligible recipients to use the specified
source in an immediate private question while that grant and source are valid.
The recipient's question is not delivered to the author.

This scope alone permits no durable source-dependent answer, person-level
inference, onward sharing, later automated composition or new action authority.
Reuse the existing admission and retention boundaries; validate the named
source, recipient, purpose, grant and current revision before consumption and
publication/readback.

**Grant acquisition remains a design decision.** Compare an explicitly chosen,
reusable bounded sharing agreement with a contextual per-share choice. Neither
is accepted here. Until an appropriate agreement is effective, source use is
not granted. Do not require a checkbox on every post or infer agreement from
friendship, viewing, continuing to use Vesper, or a generic Share gesture.

The immediate-only grant does not authorize Home/Places composition. A different
existing or later accepted grant may do so within its actual scope. Show that
possibility as an explicitly labeled fixture assumption, not runtime permission.

## 4. Retention, withdrawal and correction

- A recipient cannot turn an immediate-only answer into a retained derivative
  merely by omitting the friend's image. Answer text may still depend on the
  restricted source.
- A Keep request must either obtain an applicable retention grant or create a
  genuinely independent answer from permitted world sources, explaining the
  limitation briefly. Do not silently retain the original question either:
  it can quote the restricted source.
- Revocation invalidates future use. Recheck during queued/in-flight work and
  before result publication/read. Do not unconditionally finish and deliver a
  source-dependent answer after its grant is withdrawn.
- Already delivered material follows the explicit lifecycle/correction
  contract; do not promise erasure of screenshots or human recollection.
- Source-independent material may remain. Dependency lineage, not similar
  wording or the surface it appears on, determines required repair.

These replace the earlier contradictory “no derivative survives the turn” /
“Keep the answer text” cases and unconditional in-flight completion.

## 5. Implementation and acceptance

Contribution/consequence owns the semantics; existing source/share owners hold
effective grants; Concierge resolves scoped context; root/content owners consume
only authorized material. The [capability map](social-experience-capability-map-2026-09-07.md)
requires tracing existing paths before adding storage or another gate.

Verify: original-only receiving; independent answer without friend-source use;
authorized private question with no author notification; no unauthorized Keep;
withdrawal before/during production and before delivery/read; and separately
authorized composition with correct audience, attribution and repair.

Outside-frame annotations must identify which capability a specimen uses.
Inside the phone, show useful content and understandable context—not a policy
essay announcing every inference that did not occur.

## 6. Open decisions

How grants are offered and understood; exact eligible consumers and lifespan;
retention of source-dependent answers; and any broader compositional agreement.
The separate conversation-history proposal remains unadopted. This document
does not expand runtime authority or amend the five-axis contract.
