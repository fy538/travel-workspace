# Vesper Visual Direction — Deep Research Dossier

> Research synthesis and exploration authority, not product or runtime canon.
> It interprets the current product canon and proposes visual hypotheses for
> testing. When this document conflicts with product, architecture, privacy, or
> contract authority, those authorities win.

## Executive decision

Vesper should feel like a **living field atlas**: precise enough to act from,
porous enough for the physical world to remain primary, and personal enough
that evidence can become relationship over time.

The visual shift is not from one card style to another. It is:

> From attractive containers for travel features to a living notation system
> for people, places, time, evidence, and consequence.

That system must do four things at once:

1. preserve the recognizable anatomy of different real-world artifacts;
2. make time and phase visible through transformation, not status decoration;
3. reveal what supports a claim, who may see it, and who may act without
   becoming an admin interface; and
4. let several people contribute distinct meanings to one shared experience
   without collapsing into a feed, poll, or merged scrapbook.

The recommended synthesis is:

- **Quiet Field Atlas** as the environmental foundation;
- **Living Ephemera** as the object and artifact language;
- **Relational Correspondence** as the multiplayer language; and
- a **bounded relational instrument** only when someone intentionally explores
  how artifacts connect across place, time, people, and themes.

This is a whole-product direction. It should be judged through complete
experience sequences, not one isolated card or one proof loop.

## 1. What changed in the product philosophy

The August pivot made the visual problem more ambitious and more coherent.
Vesper is **personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world**, with
four promises: See More, Live It Well, Carry It Forward, and Experience It
Together. Its subject is the relationship among a person, other people, and the
physical world; lived experience is the human unit, not the trip itinerary,
recommendation, booking, chat turn, or memory archive.

The visual system therefore cannot be organized around feature ownership. A
plane ticket, menu photograph, dinner invitation, hotel confirmation, movie
ticket, book passage, place interpretation, shared decision, and later memory
may be different forms of one experiential thread.

The most consequential product ideas for visual design are:

- **The world is primary.** Vesper should increase attention to a place or
  person, then recede.
- **Artifacts can change role without losing identity.** Evidence can become an
  actionable object, an occasion component, an Encounter, or a later bridge.
- **Time is constitutive.** The useful shape of an object changes before,
  during, just after, and long after an experience.
- **Meaning is contextual and revisable.** A movie ticket can become a bridge
  to Rome; it is not permanent proof of a fixed interest in mythology.
- **Multiplayer means plural agency.** People can coordinate around shared
  facts while retaining private constraints, authored perspectives, and
  different memories.
- **Practical and meaningful reinforce one another.** Translation, navigation,
  timing, heat, access, food, etiquette, history, literature, and memory belong
  in one experience rather than separate product categories.
- **Human remembering is the goal; platform retention is not.** The system
  should make recurrence and recognition possible without demanding constant
  capture or engagement.

### Visual consequence

The app needs a grammar capable of expressing one thing across several states
and relationships. It does not need a universal card that makes unlike things
look interchangeable.

### 2.2 Existing visual laws worth preserving

The current doctrine already establishes a strong foundation:

- one dominant object or proposition per surface;
- hierarchy and whitespace before containers;
- cards are bounded objects, not the default page layout;
- original material can provide character when provenance remains inspectable;
- beauty should come from place, movement, time, people,
  planned-versus-lived divergence, and sparsity;
- generic AI gradients, excessive pills, dashboards, and ornamental maps are
  anti-patterns;
- EB Garamond Roman is expressive and interpretive;
- system sans is operational;
- JetBrains Mono is for time, provenance, receipts, and compact facts;
- warm paper is the room, not a paper texture applied to every object; and
- vivid imagery needs a bridge into the quieter material system.

### 2.3 Existing semantic architecture worth preserving

The canonical artifact work has already separated:

- artifact identity from viewer-safe projection;
- source from occurrence and interpretation;
- Mine from Together;
- semantic family from visual family;
- artifact family from projection mode; and
- an artifact from its explicit relationships.

It also identifies four useful visual families:

| Family | Native structure | Experiential job |
|---|---|---|
| Movement | Origin → path → destination | Make transition, timing, and threshold legible |
| Dwelling | Center, enclosure, radius, return | Establish a temporary base and what radiates from it |
| Gathering | Shared core with plural approaches and margins | Coordinate people without merging them |
| Encounter | Focal fragment, interpretation, open edge | Join something noticed with a useful or meaningful opening |

### 2.4 The code is a semantic scaffold, not yet the desired system

The existing canonical artifact gallery is valuable as contract and fixture
work. In the current worktree, its renderer adds family-specific border
treatments and a bounded explicit-relationship list, but still applies one
rounded paper-card composition and mostly distinguishes families through
accent, icon, copy, and edge treatment. The fixture lab also presents objects
as a tab-filtered list of similar cards.

That is evidence that the semantic foundation exists. It is not visual
authority for the next design pass. The design task is to make family,
temporality, provenance, scope, and relationship structural.

## 3. Research method

This review did not ask “which travel app looks best?” It examined adjacent
systems for a specific set of problems:

1. How can many object types belong to one identity without becoming clones?
2. How can an object retain continuity while its relevance changes over time?
3. How can provenance and authority remain visible without creating interface
   bureaucracy?
4. How can digital mementos feel personal rather than like a database?
5. How can relational exploration invite discovery without becoming a graph
   hairball?
6. How can multiplayer support people in a place without stealing attention
   from them?
7. How should Claude Design be used for exploration rather than mistaken for a
   production design system?

Each precedent below is evaluated through three lenses:

- **What it demonstrates** — the transferable principle;
- **Borrow** — a behavior, composition, or system property worth testing; and
- **Do not borrow** — a surface style or product assumption that does not fit
  Vesper.

## 4. Research findings

### 4.1 Coherence through rules, not sameness

#### Whitney Museum responsive W

Experimental Jetset describes the Whitney identity as a set of strict rules
that can respond to content, format, and context. The value is not the zigzag
shape itself. It is the proof that recognizable identity can emerge from a
generative constraint rather than a repeated template.

- **What it demonstrates:** consistency can be procedural.
- **Borrow:** a small number of persistent spatial rules that every family
  interprets differently.
- **Do not borrow:** one conspicuous brand gesture stamped on every object.
- **Source:** [The Whitney Tapes](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/the-whitney-tapes)

#### Kunsthalle Mannheim

The museum's architectural grid becomes the basis for identity, signage, and
wayfinding. The system feels native because it comes from the institution's
actual spatial logic.

- **What it demonstrates:** a visual grammar gains credibility when it is
  derived from the product's real structure.
- **Borrow:** derive shapes from movement, dwelling, gathering, and encounter
  rather than from an arbitrary house style.
- **Do not borrow:** a literal museum-grid aesthetic.
- **Source:** [Kunsthalle Mannheim wayfinding](https://segd.org/projects/kunsthalle-mannheim/)

#### Action Stations

Three naval vessels received distinct visual identities derived from their own
markings, signage, photographs, books, and diaries, while remaining part of one
museum experience.

- **What it demonstrates:** source-specific character and family resemblance
  can coexist.
- **Borrow:** let the native anatomy and source material of each artifact
  contribute controlled variation.
- **Do not borrow:** themed decoration or nostalgia for its own sake.
- **Source:** [Action Stations branding and signage](https://www.aspect-studios.com/au/projects/action-stations-branding-signage-strategy)

#### London Transport Museum

Transport identity has historically turned operational material—maps, arrows,
signage, route geometry, seat textiles, and Johnston typography—into a coherent
public language.

- **What it demonstrates:** useful infrastructure can become expressive when
  its semantics remain intact.
- **Borrow:** route geometry, identifiers, intervals, and directional marks as
  working visual material.
- **Do not borrow:** transport nostalgia as a universal travel aesthetic.
- **Sources:** [London Transport at the Design Museum](https://designmuseum.org/london-transport),
  [London Transport Museum identity by Kit Studio](https://www.kit.studio/work/london-transport-museum)

#### Design law: structural skeuomorphism

Vesper should borrow the **information anatomy** of a real object, not imitate
its literal paper, leather, perforation, or chrome.

A boarding pass can preserve sequence, gate, threshold, and route. A menu can
preserve sections, source language, and annotations. An invitation can preserve
address, host voice, recipient, and response. A book passage can preserve
quotation, location, and marginal interpretation. None needs fake aging or
photorealistic skeuomorphism.

### 4.2 Consistency is tonal, functional, and gestural

The V&A's work on a new welcome experience treats consistency as more than
visual matching. Tone, function, timing, and gesture must align across
touchpoints. V&A East Storehouse extends this into an “operating system” for
searching, discovering, decoding, and contributing across physical and digital
space.

- **What it demonstrates:** a product can feel coherent because it behaves with
  the same manners, not because every screen shares the same container.
- **Borrow:** stable voice, motion restraint, provenance behavior, action
  placement, and timing across visually different families.
- **Do not borrow:** museum interpretation density or institutional distance.
- **Sources:** [Designing a new welcome experience at the V&A](https://www.vam.ac.uk/blog/digital/designing-a-new-welcome-experience-at-the-va),
  [V&A East Storehouse](https://www.fieldworkfacility.com/projects/storehouse)

The Museum of Fine Arts Boston offers another useful split: individual
exhibitions can have distinct visual treatments while ticketing and wayfinding
stay operationally consistent.

- **Borrow:** separate object expression from shared operational furniture.
- **Source:** [MFA Boston digital signage system](https://www.sosolimited.com/work/mfa-digital-signage-system)

### 4.3 Semantic artifacts prioritize context, not completeness

Apple Wallet pass styles use semantic fields and pass type to determine shape,
hierarchy, and what becomes prominent. Boarding-pass redesign research similarly
shows that one object serves different audiences and moments; chronological
organization can reduce stress.

- **What it demonstrates:** an artifact's form should be determined by its job
  at this moment, while identity and full details remain available.
- **Borrow:** phase-specific emphasis; stable anchors that survive across
  compact, live, and expanded forms; semantic fields rather than arbitrary
  layout slots.
- **Do not borrow:** wallet stacking, glossy ticket mimicry, or treating every
  imported item as a transactional pass.
- **Sources:** [Apple Wallet passes](https://developer.apple.com/wallet/get-started/),
  [Wallet pass metadata](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/walletpasses/defining-the-metadata-of-your-wallet-pass),
  [A chronological boarding-pass redesign](https://www.wired.com/2013/09/airlines-please-adopt-this-smart-boarding-pass-redesign)

Passport design provides a related trust lesson. Security, nation, document
identity, and inspection are layered into one object; trust is not expressed by
a generic green badge.

- **Borrow:** quiet, inspectable proof marks with more detail on demand.
- **Do not borrow:** security-theater ornament or state-document grandeur.
- **Sources:** [Australian passport design](https://www.passports.gov.au/help/australian-passport-design),
  [United States passport security and design](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/help/security-design.html)

### 4.4 Time should transform the artifact

Apple Live Activities and Android live updates both emphasize finite,
trackable, glanceable experiences. Information updates in place, critical
changes earn alerts, and the surface ends when the live task ends. Apple also
recommends preserving relative placement across expanded and compact states.

- **What it demonstrates:** temporal continuity is stronger than a stream of
  replacement cards.
- **Borrow:** stable object identity; phase-specific hierarchy; relative spatial
  continuity; explicit ending and settling.
- **Do not borrow:** persistent lock-screen urgency or sports-score density.
- **Sources:** [Apple Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities),
  [Android live updates](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/home-screen/live-updates)

#### Design law: phase is morphology, not a chip

The same flight should not be a static card with a changing label.

- **Upcoming:** route and preparation dominate.
- **Live:** next threshold, time, and disruption dominate.
- **Resolving:** ambiguity and needed action dominate.
- **Lived:** route compresses; what occurred and what remains open take over.
- **Remembered:** exact operational facts recede; personally meaningful anchors
  and relationships remain.
- **Expired:** the object becomes quiet, explains why it is no longer active,
  and does not pretend disappearance equals forgetting.

Every family needs its own transformation sequence. Motion should explain that
continuity, not decorate it.

### 4.5 Personal meaning lives in an ecology of objects

Research on visual mementos defines a design space for personal data used in
reminiscing and sharing. It identifies three especially relevant challenges:
evoking familiarity, expressing subjectivity, and obscuring sensitive detail
when sharing. Research on photo mementos further finds that meaning depends on
where an object is displayed, with what other objects, and for whom; only the
owner may know all its layers.

- **What it demonstrates:** a personal world is not a database of isolated
  memories. Arrangement, concealment, juxtaposition, and audience are part of
  meaning.
- **Borrow:** different levels of intimacy; personally authored significance;
  selective detail; constellation and timeline as alternative arrangements;
  quiet resurfacing through familiar patterns.
- **Do not borrow:** automatic sentimentalization, total life logging, or a
  scrapbook aesthetic.
- **Sources:** [Visual Mementos research record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26529711/),
  [Visual Mementos project](https://www.alicethudt.de/projects/visual-mementos),
  [Photo mementos in the family home](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S107158191300133X),
  [Remembering today tomorrow](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/152475/)

Physical memorabilia can also act as a social instrument: a souvenir may
invite storytelling and lead into associated media. This supports Vesper's
artifact-as-bridge thesis. The artifact should be able to open a relationship
without becoming a permanent taxonomy.

- **Source:** [Physical memorabilia and co-located photo sharing](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1071581909001293)

#### Design law: familiar before analytical

Personal visualization often tries to reveal an unexpected pattern. A memento
may instead need to show a recognizable shape of one's life, then create a
small opening for new interpretation. The design should permit “I know what
this is” before “the system has analyzed me.”

### 4.6 Relational collections need situated perspectives

“Generous interface” research argues that visual exploration can reveal the
character of a collection and support serendipity. It also cautions that there
is no universal visualization: the interaction must fit the nature of the
collection, and visual abundance can increase learning cost.

Research on situated relational perspectives starts from a selected artifact
and offers several bounded views around it. Monadic exploration similarly lets
someone move continuously between a whole and a selected part. Work on
synoptic collection views shows that map, set, network, and time views become
more useful when their relationships remain coherent across perspectives.

- **What it demonstrates:** the alternative to a list is not one global graph.
  It is a set of coordinated, situated views.
- **Borrow:** select one anchor; reveal a bounded neighborhood; switch among
  place, time, people, occasion, and theme; preserve selection across views;
  allow juxtaposition; provide list and timeline alternatives.
- **Do not borrow:** force-directed hairballs, unexplained similarity edges,
  encyclopedic coverage, or novelty as the only value.
- **Sources:** [Europeana on generous interfaces](https://pro.europeana.eu/page/issue-11-generous-interfaces),
  [Relational perspectives as situated visualizations](https://uclab.fh-potsdam.de/publications/relational-perspectives-as-situated-visualizations-of-art-collections),
  [Hausmann collection perspectives](https://uclab.fh-potsdam.de/hausmann/),
  [Monadic Exploration](https://uclab.fh-potsdam.de/projects/monadic-exploration),
  [Exploratory modes in digital collections](https://firstmonday.org/ojs/index.php/fm/article/view/6984),
  [Many Views Are Not Enough](https://repositum.tuwien.at/handle/20.500.12708/140415)

The Cooper Hewitt Art Clock offers a useful austerity lesson: removing chrome
and letting collection imagery become the interface produced a more direct
relationship with the material.

- **Borrow:** when source material is strong, reduce interface presence.
- **Do not borrow:** passive slideshow behavior.
- **Source:** [Art Clock design retrospective](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2022/06/02/design-retrospective-art-clock/)

### 4.7 Maps should be selective instruments, not owners of experience

Mapbox's “symbolic realism” argues for deliberate abstraction and hierarchy
rather than homogeneous photorealism. Stamen emphasizes that maps are both
structural and emotional. Semantic-zoom research suggests that meaningful
changes in representation can improve readability and clarity as scale changes.

- **What it demonstrates:** a map can situate a relationship without flattening
  everything into pins.
- **Borrow:** selective landmarks, routes, neighborhoods, and meaningful zoom
  transitions; preserve the currently selected artifact or occasion.
- **Do not borrow:** default pin clouds, ornamental maps, satellite realism, or
  map-first navigation for non-spatial questions.
- **Sources:** [Mapbox symbolic realism](https://www.mapbox.com/blog/symbolic-realism-3d-basemap),
  [Stamen on choosing a map form](https://stamen.com/when-creating-a-digital-map-know-your-options/),
  [Semantic zoom research](https://publica.fraunhofer.de/bitstreams/db3899b7-9ee3-4f2e-8a32-e1b034242f18/download)

### 4.8 Multiplayer should look like addressed attention

Invitation products demonstrate useful pieces of the before/during/after
lifecycle. Apple Invites combines expressive invitation, logistics, shared
photos, and music. Paperless Post separates an expressive invitation from
operational detail blocks. Partiful extends coordination into guest interaction
and a shared afterlife.

- **What it demonstrates:** a social object can persist across preparation,
  coordination, live experience, and remembrance.
- **Borrow:** a shared core; clear host or author; recipient-aware expression;
  phase-specific operations; deliberately contributed afterglow.
- **Do not borrow:** event growth mechanics, public guest performance, reaction
  counters, or one merged album as the meaning of Together.
- **Sources:** [Apple Invites](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/02/introducing-apple-invites-a-new-app-that-brings-people-together/),
  [Paperless Post features](https://www.paperlesspost.com/features),
  [Partiful](https://partiful.com/),
  [Partiful on direct social interaction](https://info.partiful.com/blog/post/bts-building-boops-dms)

Research on enjoyable co-located interaction highlights spontaneity,
authenticity, nonverbal cues, undivided attention, and use of the physical
environment. A practical framework for co-located mobile experiences also asks
designers to consider the relationship among social situation, device
mechanics, physical environment, and time.

- **What it demonstrates:** software should support the social situation, not
  replace the channels that make being together enjoyable.
- **Borrow:** brief handoffs, one-person-at-a-time address, shared orientation,
  peripheral status, and clear moments to put the phone down.
- **Do not borrow:** synchronized screen attention as the definition of shared
  experience.
- **Sources:** [Enjoyable co-located interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.01698),
  [Designing mobile experiences for collocated interaction](https://nottingham-repository.worktribe.com/output/992495)

#### Design law: addressed, not broadcast

A multiplayer contribution should visibly answer:

- who authored or proposed this;
- who it is for;
- what shared object or decision it concerns;
- whether a response is needed now;
- what remains private; and
- when the device can recede again.

This is correspondence around a real-world object, not content posted into an
audience stream.

### 4.9 Perceptual grouping should carry semantics

Gestalt principles such as proximity, similarity, connection, and common
region explain why a universal stack of rounded cards feels semantically flat:
the repeated container overpowers the differences inside it. Conversely,
careless graph edges or color matching can imply relationships that do not
exist.

- **Borrow:** use proximity for belonging, connection for explicit relation,
  common region for shared scope, and continuity for time or movement.
- **Do not borrow:** use the same enclosure for unrelated objects or decorative
  lines that imply causality.
- **Sources:** [Nielsen Norman Group visual-design glossary](https://media.nngroup.com/media/articles/attachments/Visual_Design_Glossary_-_Printable_PDF_-_B%26W.pdf),
  [Nielsen Norman Group visual-design principles](https://media.nngroup.com/media/articles/attachments/Principles_Visual_Design-A4.pdf)

### 4.10 Claude Design is an exploration room, not production authority

Anthropic describes Claude Design as useful for early ideation, collaboration,
and building shared understanding before implementation. Its own guidance notes
that a design system must be supplied to avoid generic output, and current
image generation limitations mean teams should bring real assets.

- **What it demonstrates:** Claude Design is well suited to parallel visual
  hypotheses and sequence exploration.
- **Borrow:** one constrained brief; one asset packet; the same fixtures across
  several directions; explicit critiques and decision logs.
- **Do not borrow:** asking it to infer the product from the whole repository,
  generate production-ready design-system truth, or evaluate its own aesthetic
  success without external criteria.
- **Sources:** [How the product designer who built Claude Design uses it](https://claude.com/blog/how-the-product-designer-who-built-claude-design-uses-it-to-explore-ideas-before-building-them),
  [Set up your design system in Claude Design](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14604397-set-up-your-design-system-in-claude-design)

## 5. The proposed visual grammar

The grammar should be small enough to recognize and expressive enough to
compose the whole product.

| Primitive | Meaning | Typical visual expression | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Field** | Where this is situated | Open canvas, local atmosphere, map fragment, temporal field | That the map or background owns the object |
| **Anchor** | The thing that matters now | Dominant fact, place, person, object, or question | That everything visible is equally important |
| **Thread** | An explicit relation across place, time, people, or consequence | Route, connective rule, alignment, sequence, bounded edge | Causality or similarity without evidence |
| **Proof mark** | Why this is known and what kind of claim it is | Marginal notation, source signature, observed/scheduled distinction | Generic certainty or a universal “verified” badge |
| **Threshold** | A phase crossing or action boundary | Gate, fold, crop, reveal, compression, directional transition | Urgency when none exists |

Three modifiers apply across the grammar:

| Modifier | Question answered | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Address** | Who authored this and for whom? | Named margin, directed handoff, separate authored layer |
| **Scope** | What is Mine, Together, or inspectable? | Spatial separation, common region, restrained audience mark |
| **Authority** | Who may propose, decide, spend, delegate, change, or undo? | Calm action boundary, role notation, consequence and reversibility cue |

### 5.1 Shared invariants

All families should share:

- a consistent typographic role system;
- a stable material and elevation logic;
- the same provenance and scope semantics;
- the same action hierarchy;
- the same phase rhythm;
- restrained motion based on continuity;
- source-aware imagery treatment;
- accessible contrast, dynamic type, reduced motion, and non-color cues; and
- one dominant current proposition.

### 5.2 Deliberate variation

Families should vary through:

- silhouette and internal geometry;
- where the eye enters and exits;
- how expansion works;
- which facts remain stable across phases;
- how source material is incorporated;
- how one object relates to another; and
- how human authorship appears.

The family must be recognizable in grayscale and without its icon.

## 6. Four artifact morphologies

### 6.1 Movement — directed continuity

**Geometry:** two anchors, a directional spine, and one current threshold.

**Stable identity anchors:** origin, destination, provider or source, scheduled
identity, and route.

**Transformation:** the current threshold travels along the spine. Before
departure, preparation sits near the origin. During movement, the next relevant
gate or disruption expands. After arrival, the route compresses into a trace
and the destination becomes a bridge into the surrounding occasion.

**Source character:** operator color or route texture may appear as a restrained
borrowed accent after semantic colors are protected.

**Avoid:** faux ticket perforations, airplane icon as family identity, every
field displayed simultaneously, and map as mandatory background.

### 6.2 Dwelling — center and return

**Geometry:** a center, enclosing field, radius, and paths of return.

**Stable identity anchors:** place, dates, access authority, host or provider,
and location relationship.

**Transformation:** before arrival, access and approach sit at the boundary.
While live, the center becomes operational and nearby relationships can radiate
outward. After departure, access secrets disappear and patterns of return,
neighborhood familiarity, and chosen memory can remain.

**Source character:** architecture, room material, threshold, or neighborhood
color can contribute through crop, line, or muted texture.

**Avoid:** generic hotel card, permanent display of entry codes, and treating a
booking as proof the person stayed.

### 6.3 Gathering — shared core, plural margins

**Geometry:** one common center with separately authored approaches, margins,
or leaves.

**Stable identity anchors:** occasion, place, time window, participants and
roles, shared decision state.

**Transformation:** invitation begins as address. Coordination gradually
clarifies a shared core. Live mode surfaces only what helps the group act. The
afterglow permits separate Outcomes, photographs, and meanings to remain
adjacent without being merged.

**Source character:** host voice, venue material, or invitation mark can shape
the shared center while personal contributions retain authorship.

**Avoid:** poll-first interaction, avatar pile as the primary expression of
multiplayer, reaction counts, and a single group-authored memory.

### 6.4 Encounter — focal fragment and open edge

**Geometry:** one focal fragment, one useful interpretation, and an incomplete
edge that permits continuation.

**Stable identity anchors:** the captured or referenced fragment, source,
place/time context when warranted, and the user's authorship.

**Transformation:** an immediate question can expire after it is answered. A
kept Encounter can gain an authored note, a place relationship, an explicit
bridge to a book or film, or a later continuation. It need not mature into a
formal Plan or memory.

**Source character:** crop, quotation, original language, object detail, or
source typography can lead, with provenance nearby.

**Avoid:** auto-generated essay, forced sentiment, a generic “insight” card,
and confident inference from capture alone.

## 7. Color, type, material, image, and motion

### 7.1 Color hierarchy

Use three color layers with strict precedence:

1. **Semantic color** — action, risk, live change, privacy, correction, and
   degradation. It must remain globally consistent.
2. **Family structure** — primarily geometry and value, not four arbitrary
   category colors.
3. **Borrowed color** — one restrained color sampled or interpreted from the
   source, provider, place, event, or object. It supplies character only when it
   cannot be mistaken for semantic state.

Borrowed color should usually be muted, bounded, and optional. The system must
work when the source offers no usable color.

### 7.2 Typography

- **EB Garamond Roman:** one dominant interpreted line, title, invitation
  voice, memory fragment, or authored observation.
- **System sans:** action, navigation, settings, correction, practical facts,
  and dense productive use.
- **JetBrains Mono:** time, route codes, source references, quantities,
  coordinates, versions, and proof marks.

Typeface alone must not distinguish Mine from Together, certainty from
inference, or one family from another.

### 7.3 Material

The global field can remain warm and editorial. Objects earn boundaries based
on custody and interaction:

- raised surface for a manipulable or inspectable bounded object;
- flat composition for a field, overview, or relational arrangement;
- overlay material for transient action or expanded inspection;
- source fragment for original evidence; and
- no enclosure when proximity and alignment already communicate belonging.

Paper texture should be subtle enough that screenshots do not resemble a stack
of stationery.

### 7.4 Images and source material

Use source material as evidence and character, not filler.

- preserve original crop or source context when trust requires it;
- provide a tonal bridge into the warm field;
- avoid color washes that make distinct places interchangeable;
- allow image density to drop sharply during live operational moments;
- distinguish original media from generated or illustrative material; and
- never infer audience, identity, attendance, or affection from an image alone.

### 7.5 Motion

Motion should describe one of four things:

- a threshold crossed;
- an object's identity persisting through a phase change;
- a relationship being intentionally revealed; or
- the interface receding after its job is done.

Prefer crop, compression, alignment, continuation, and reveal. Avoid ambient
floating, perpetual shimmer, decorative parallax, and cinematic transitions
that compete with the environment.

## 8. My World as a situated relational system

“My World” should remain a product hypothesis and a composed relationship
layer, not automatically become the app's root or a total archive.

### 8.1 The core interaction

1. Begin from a selected artifact, person, place, occasion, or question.
2. Show a bounded neighborhood with explicit relationships.
3. Let the user change perspective among time, place, people, occasion, and
   theme while preserving the selected anchor.
4. Reveal why each connection exists.
5. Permit correction, concealment, forgetting, and removal.
6. Offer timeline or list alternatives for accessibility and analytical clarity.

### 8.2 Semantic zoom

- **Far:** a few named regions or recurring relationships, not individual nodes.
- **Middle:** occasions, places, and meaningful clusters with visible scope.
- **Near:** artifacts, authored Outcomes, sources, and explicit edge labels.
- **Inspect:** provenance, correction, custody, and history.

Zoom must change meaning, not only scale graphics.

### 8.3 Relationship rules

- show only explicit or explainable edges;
- distinguish shared occurrence, authored meaning, similarity, and causal
  consequence;
- use juxtaposition to reveal contrast as well as resemblance;
- never infer a stable interest identity from one artifact;
- keep private Outcomes out of Together;
- cap neighborhood size and provide an explanation for omissions; and
- prefer a useful empty or sparse state over invented density.

### 8.4 The desired feeling

The user should feel “these parts of my life are becoming intelligible to me,”
not “the system has constructed a dossier about me.”

## 9. Multiplayer visual system

Multiplayer is not an add-on to the artifact language. It changes composition.

### 9.1 Mine and Together are spaces, not filter pills

Mine and Together should be distinguishable through compositional custody:

- a shared core has a clear common region;
- personal constraints and interpretations remain in separately authored
  margins or layers;
- a handoff visibly travels from an author to a recipient;
- a proposal can converge into shared action without exposing private reasons;
  and
- after the occasion, plural Outcomes can coexist without one canonical group
  story.

A compact scope control may still exist, but it cannot carry the full meaning
of privacy and plurality.

### 9.2 Phone-down moments

Every live multiplayer sequence should identify:

- what needs attention now;
- who, if anyone, needs to act;
- what everyone else can ignore;
- what remains available later; and
- the moment when the interface should recede.

The ideal live artifact may make itself smaller after delivering one useful
handoff.

### 9.3 Social tone

Prefer invitation, correspondence, margin, relay, and shared table metaphors.
Avoid feed, post, story, reaction, leaderboard, and engagement metaphors.

## 10. Three directions to explore

These are not competing product strategies. They are controlled visual
experiments using the same content and constraints.

### Direction A — Quiet Field Atlas

**Question:** Can the whole product feel spatial, calm, and world-facing without
becoming map-first?

**Traits:** large breathing field, restrained cartographic or temporal
structure, very few enclosures, strong hierarchy, one selected relationship,
quiet marginal proof.

**Best at:** Home, Life overview, place relationships, temporal orientation.

**Risk:** becoming austere, abstract, or too much like a cultural institution.

### Direction B — Living Ephemera

**Question:** Can digital artifacts preserve object-specific character and age
gracefully without kitsch?

**Traits:** structural skeuomorphism, native field hierarchy, source-derived
accent, folds/crops/thresholds used semantically, strong phase transformation.

**Best at:** artifact identity, trust, continuity, invitation, memory.

**Risk:** decorative ticketing, paper-on-paper, visual noise, or nostalgic
travel branding.

### Direction C — Relational Correspondence

**Question:** Can multiplayer and personal relationships become visually
legible without a feed or graph dashboard?

**Traits:** address, authored margins, shared center, directed handoff,
juxtaposed perspectives, bounded relationship neighborhoods.

**Best at:** Together, gathering, invitation, convergence, plural afterglow.

**Risk:** stationery mannerism, chat-like density, or diagrams that demand too
much attention.

### Expected synthesis

The likely answer is not to select one direction wholesale:

- Quiet Field Atlas owns environmental calm and whole-screen composition.
- Living Ephemera owns object morphology and time.
- Relational Correspondence owns address, authorship, and plural agency.
- The relational instrument appears only on deliberate exploration surfaces.

The exploration must still allow a surprising fourth answer.

## 11. Required fixture program

Visual judgment should use a fixed portfolio, not cherry-picked hero screens.

### 11.1 Five narrative sequences

#### F1 — Flight becomes arrival

Boarding pass from upcoming → live → disrupted or resolving → arrived → lived.
Test route continuity, glanceability, source degradation, and transition into a
Rome occasion.

#### F2 — Dwelling becomes temporary home

Hotel confirmation from upcoming → access moment → inhabited base → departed →
remembered. Test secret redaction, center-and-radius geometry, and relationship
to nearby places.

#### F3 — Sorrento menu becomes an Encounter

Menu photograph → immediate translation and practical answer → optional private
Encounter → later connection to pasta technique, ingredient, restaurant, or
home cooking. Test expiration without forced memory.

#### F4 — Movie becomes a Rome bridge

Movie ticket or watched-film artifact → Colosseum mention of Troy and Aeneas →
bounded explanation → link to a book passage or place → addressed share to a
partner. Test medium-crossing, familiarity, provenance, and non-deterministic
interest expansion.

#### F5 — Couple occasion remains plural

Invitation → private constraints → shared coordination → live handoff → phone
down → two separate afterglow perspectives → bounded constellation. Test Mine,
Together, authorship, shared facts, and plural meaning.

### 11.2 Staged coverage matrix

Do not demand full portfolio coverage from every early direction. That creates
many shallow screens before the underlying grammar is understood.

The **divergence round** should compare every direction using the same locked
F1 live/disrupted-flight moment, the same F5 private-input → group-safe-proposal
moment, and the same Home current-opening composition. Begin in grayscale and
compare silhouette, hierarchy, relationship, and transition before polish.

After human selection, the **selected system proof** must show:

- all four visual families;
- compact, standard, timeline, occasion, and constellation modes where
  relevant;
- at least four phases of one stable object;
- one truthful unknown or degraded source;
- one correction path;
- one deletion or forgetting consequence;
- Mine and Together as separate viewer-safe frames on the same occasion;
- Home, Vesper, Places, focused Occasion, and Life continuity;
- dynamic type and reduced-motion behavior;
- outdoor, one-handed, low-attention use; and
- a quiet state in which no further action is requested.

### 11.3 Whole-product compositions

At least three complete screens are required:

1. **Home:** one current opening with supporting context, not an artifact feed.
2. **Occasion:** one shared center with movement, dwelling, gathering, and
   encounter elements composed by relevance.
3. **Life:** one selected anchor across Mine/Together, timeline, place, and
   bounded relational perspectives.

## 12. Evaluation rubric

Score each direction from 1–5 using only the evidence required at its current
stage. Apply the complete rubric to the selected system proof. A beautiful
direction that fails a critical gate does not advance.

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| World-facing attention | 15% | Does the interface help the person notice, act, and return to the world? |
| Family morphology | 15% | Are families recognizable by structure without becoming separate brands? |
| Temporal continuity | 15% | Does one object's form mature intelligibly across phases? |
| Trust and custody | 15% | Are source, occurrence, inference, authorship, and scope calm but inspectable? |
| Multiplayer plurality | 15% | Can people share action while retaining private and authored meaning? |
| Whole-screen composition | 10% | Is there one dominant proposition with clear context and consequence? |
| Accessibility and situation | 10% | Does it survive dynamic type, contrast, reduced motion, heat, glare, movement, and one hand? |
| Implementation feasibility | 5% | Can the grammar be expressed with reusable tokens, primitives, and deterministic projections? |

### Critical failure gates

Reject a direction if it:

- becomes a feed, dashboard, global graph, or stack of equal cards;
- makes a ticket or capture look like proof of occurrence;
- merges private and shared meaning;
- requires constant capture or interaction to create value;
- obscures practical action for atmosphere;
- uses color as the only family, state, or privacy signal;
- makes the map the owner of every relationship;
- applies one decorative metaphor to all objects;
- invents semantic relationships for visual density; or
- feels like a chatbot wrapped in editorial cards.

## 13. What the visual system must not become

### Not a luxury travel magazine

Editorial calm is useful; aspirational destination consumption is not the
thesis. Ordinary, practical, local, and imperfect experiences must belong.

### Not a wallet of imported objects

Artifacts matter because they can participate in experience, relationship, and
time—not because the app stores passes.

### Not a quantified-self archive

The aim is intelligibility and remembrance, not completeness, streaks, counts,
or life logging.

### Not a social network

Together is governed shared action and plural meaning, not publishing,
audience growth, or engagement.

### Not a knowledge graph UI

The graph can support the product. The user sees a bounded, situated,
explainable perspective only when it helps.

### Not a museum guide

Interpretation must begin from this person, this group, this moment, and this
place. It should not assign a curriculum or force cultural homework in the
heat.

## 14. Decisions, hypotheses, and open questions

### Recommended decisions

- Adopt **living field atlas** as the visual north-star phrase for exploration.
- Use the five primitives Field, Anchor, Thread, Proof mark, and Threshold.
- Treat Address, Scope, and Authority as required multiplayer modifiers.
- Use geometry for family, semantic color for state, and borrowed color for
  source character.
- Require family recognition without icon or color.
- Treat phase as morphological transformation.
- Design Mine/Together as compositional custody, not merely a switch.
- Limit constellation views to selected, explainable neighborhoods.
- Use the same five narrative fixtures across every direction.

### Hypotheses to test

- Structural skeuomorphism will make artifacts more legible without feeling
  nostalgic.
- Stable anchors plus phase-specific hierarchy will create stronger continuity
  than status-labelled cards.
- Marginal proof marks can communicate trust without chip clutter.
- Addressed handoffs will feel more human and safer than feed-like shared
  updates.
- A selected-anchor relational view will produce satisfying recognition and
  discovery without becoming analytical work.
- Quiet world-facing composition will remain usable in stressful live moments.

### Open questions

- How much visual character can borrowed source color carry before the app
  loses calm?
- Which proof marks should be ambient, which should be visible by default, and
  which belong only in inspection?
- Can Gathering feel social without relying on portraits or avatar piles?
- What is the minimum relationship density at which a constellation becomes
  more useful than a timeline or list?
- How should an artifact visibly lose access to deleted source material while
  preserving an authorized consequence?
- Which parts of a lived artifact should become quieter automatically, and
  which changes require authorship?
- Can one set of primitives serve both practical live use and reflective Life
  use without becoming mannered?

## 15. Recommended workflow

1. Assemble a small, licensed asset packet for the five fixtures: real or
   realistic ticket fragments, a menu, place details, a dwelling image, and
   invitation material. Preserve source and license notes.
2. Give Claude Design the companion brief, a frozen fixture-content sheet,
   tokens, and a curated asset packet. Keep the long canon available for
   questions rather than loading it all at the start.
3. Calibrate the extracted foundation before visual exploration so current
   pre-pivot card patterns do not become accidental authority.
4. Generate a wide, low-fidelity divergence round against the same F1, F5, and
   Home moments; stop for human selection.
5. Remix named strengths into a smaller finalist set; stop again before system
   expansion.
6. Prove only the selected system across the complete fixture, phase, scope,
   accessibility, and whole-product matrix.
7. Select principles and compositional moves, not a single screenshot.
8. Recompose the selected synthesis in Figma with real constraints, components,
   responsive states, and accessibility.
9. Run outdoor/on-the-move and two-person tests on-device.
10. Promote only validated rules into the Experience Constitution, Type &
   Material Doctrine, artifact gallery contract, and implementation tokens.

## 16. Promotion and expiration

This working document should not silently become permanent authority.

Before its expiry:

- record which direction or synthesis was selected;
- preserve rejected hypotheses and why they failed;
- promote stable visual laws into the Experience Constitution;
- promote family and mode decisions into the artifact surface contract;
- promote token changes into the Type & Material Doctrine;
- update fixture oracles and visual QA requirements; and
- archive this dossier with links to the resulting decision and artifacts.

## Closing principle

Vesper should not make the world look like its interface. It should give the
user a coherent way to recognize how a ticket, place, person, question,
decision, and memory belong to the same life—then make itself small enough for
that life to continue.
