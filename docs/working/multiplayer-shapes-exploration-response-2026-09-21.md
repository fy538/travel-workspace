## 2. Recommendation

**Build six interactions, not eighteen features. Three exist, one is an extension, three are new, and none needs a new root, tab or feed.**

| Interaction | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send | Words, a photo or a place · to whom · until · where it waits · one thing they can answer | Extension of Social 09's share sheet |
| The reader | A person's own words, attributed once; reply, or ask Vesper privately | Exists, shared |
| The arrangement | What, when, who is in, settled and loose, who decides; a person outside it; the world changing it | InviteCard plus Plans' lightweight arrangement |
| A private word | Said to Vesper about a shared thing; shapes it; arrives unsigned | New, on shipped substrate |
| Mutual yes, silent no | Each person asked alone; all yes, or nobody hears anything | New |
| Together | What is between these people, in Life; arriving on Home or at a place when it can change something | New view |

If only three things, in this order: **a private word** (what no group chat can do; on the three strongest boards; guard already shipped) → **Send, extended** (the cheapest breadth: "until", "waits at", one answer) → **Together** (the stated moat, slowest to prove; prototype on a pair).

## 3. What each direction becomes

Retain: D1 (send + reader; held to ease, not novelty), D3, D5, D8 (the arrangement's changed-world state), D10, E1, E2, E3, E5, E7, E8.
Merge: D2 → Together, circle destination removed · D4 → a send with one answer and an end · D7 → a send with "until" and "I'll come".
No surface of its own: D6 (a sentence typed into Chat) · D9 (content inside Together, or a send that waits at a place).
Defer: E4 (public audience through a place; canon step 6 of 9) · E6's nod (needs an audience the canon does not have; its first frame is personal memory and fails the private-context test).

## 4. Remove

A circle or group destination · "Me and you" as a feature · offers, requests, status and availability as objects · a play surface, games, streaks, prompts to capture · any screen text whose job is to say what the product does not do · buttons for doing nothing · a headline that retells what a friend sent · anything that would be a fifth root or social tab (on the composed weekend Home carried five of eight frames).

## 5. Compositions

`03` is an ordinary Friday to Sunday and one Saturday in November. Six of eight frames are lifted unchanged from D1, D5, D7 and D8. Three frames end where they are: Friday's shares lead nowhere, Saturday's opening is taken up by nobody, and neither is a failed step toward Sunday. Four of the six interactions carry the weekend; a private word and the mutual yes do not appear because two friends and a market do not need them.

`04` is two friends in New York and Marseille. One sends a photo, the other enjoys it and does not reply, and a week later an earned comparison of their two coasts goes back. It never becomes a meetup, a trip or a plan, and both people receive something.

## 6. Needs a ruling, not a drawing

These, and four decisions about the shape itself, are laid out as cards with options and a recommendation on board `01 - Decisions`.

1. May a contributor learn that what they wrote helped someone? The prominence brief says yes; the social brief bans used/viewed reports. (E4, D10)
2. Which secrets will the product keep: duration, who may set one about whom, what it may never cover. (E8)
3. Is "people who also chose this, at this place" an audience? It is not one of the canon's four visibility spaces. (E6)
4. May something be delivered when a person arrives somewhere, on their prior yes? The source calls this a consent experiment. (E3, E7)
5. How is a line said out loud kept at all, and by whose act? (W1 frame 8, D10, E5)
6. Unchanged and owned elsewhere: guest identity and delivery, Friends scope, circle membership and historical access.

## 7. Needs people, not a drawing

Does a note from someone you love, arriving on the corner it is about, feel caring or watched? · Does saying nothing about an opening nobody took feel kind, or evasive? · Will three friends each write one line about themselves for a stranger? · Will anyone spend ten minutes handing over a city? · Does a nod that software arranged still feel like a nod? · Is "Swap numbers" too light for a step that needs her consent? · Would anyone play a second time without a reminder?

## 10. Limits

A static board proves no behavior, no demand, and nothing about whether any of this is enjoyable. The order in §2 is an order of argument, not an engineering plan. The copy rules rest on one person's ear across three rounds. The corpus digests are subagent extractions; verify against sources before relying on a claim.
