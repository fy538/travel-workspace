## Rulings requested (R1–R6)

1. **Distribution law.** Future material distributes across the four lenses
   by its nature; no fifth lens, no Upcoming tab, no intention dashboard.
2. **AHEAD.** One conditional Time section, exactly two degrees — ARRANGED
   (dated commitment, planning-ink) and KEPT SOFTLY (retained possibility,
   quiet at 62%, the person's words). Absent when empty. No third state; no
   overdue.
3. **The kept intention is a claim, not a task.** Identity = verbatim
   wording. Standing = kept-when · window-if-any · still held / taken up /
   released. A window expiring is a standing change that deletes nothing;
   release/delete are the person's verbs through the owner. Attendance is
   never inferred and never asked for its own sake.
4. **Three keeps, three readbacks.** Keep-the-place / keep-in-mind /
   keep-the-composition are distinct verbs with distinct one-line readbacks;
   no universal Save sheet.
5. **Object-less intentions live lean.** While dated: Time. Always:
   Everything kept (an accounted kind with custody verbs) and search (a
   result kind carrying standing). Nowhere else.
6. **Kept-not-lived counts apart.** Region bars may read "2 places ·
   1 kept"; statlines keep describing the lived record; all counts reconcile
   to the viewer's doors.

funnel by the back door). → codex thread `01a06e71-b35c-7ca0-8307-367db90a8a6a`.
