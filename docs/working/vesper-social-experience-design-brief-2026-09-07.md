## 1. The assignment and the product promise

**Make ordinary human gestures more useful, easier to receive, and easier to continue—without requiring them to become something bigger.**

For a person using Vesper: **See a little more of your friends' worlds, find something worthwhile through them, and make it easier to do something together.** This is proposed social-experience language, not a replacement for the whole product's promise.

A photograph can be enjoyable without a reply. A place note can improve an afternoon without becoming an invitation. A dinner invitation can provide practical clarity without requiring someone to maintain a plan. A person met once can remain reachable for one particular continuation without becoming an all-access friend.

The assignment is one coordinated design pass in a dedicated social-experience project, using the existing root projects as references and returning accepted deltas to them. Resolve six choices through complete, comparable situations. Do not start six feature lanes, invent another root, or make every social gesture enter a planning funnel.

**Status:** working recommendations for founder/design review. Existing canon and contracts retain authority. This brief does not authorize implementation, amend permissions, certify guest delivery, or claim that research has validated Vesper. It does not dispatch work to another thread or change the exported designs.

## 2. What social should add to the whole product

Social is not just another source of notifications. It can deliver:

| Benefit now | Concrete example | A complete outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A glimpse of someone else's world | A friend's imperfect photograph of the dish they made | Enjoy it; close the app |
| A perspective I did not have | Two people's different responses to a hot evening, with a useful, grounded distinction | Understand something new; no response required |
| A better way into a place | A friend's particular entrance, dish or time-of-day note alongside current world information | Use the tip alone |
| An easier shared possibility | Someone deliberately welcomes company for a Saturday outing | Ask about joining, or leave it alone |
| A more comfortable gathering | Know how to arrive, which part I am joining, and what is actually expected | Attend without becoming an organizer |
| Something worth keeping or continuing | Another person's photo of a moment I missed; the promised recommendation from someone I met | Keep it where authorized, or continue that particular exchange |

These benefits can express **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.** They are neither four social stages nor four separate tab assignments. One artifact may deliver one benefit or several; no minimum progression is required.

The positive design bar matters as much as removing pressure: **would the recipient want this even if the app offered no action beneath it?** Not every unit must pass that test—an actual invitation can require an answer—but casual material should not depend on producing more input to justify its existence.

## 3. Six decisions, with recommended starting points

### A. Sharing and inviting: optional openness, explicit participation

**Start with ordinary sharing plus an optional, author-controlled way to welcome company.** Do not make everyone classify their intent before sharing. Compare natural wording with one lightweight “Company welcome” affordance; the latter is a design candidate, not an adopted component.

Use the same café photograph in four variants:

- “Found this little place.” A share, nothing owed.
- “Thinking of going Saturday.” A possibility, not an invitation sent to every viewer.
- “I'll be there Saturday; happy for company.” An authored opening, with any actual limits understandable.
- “Want to go Saturday?” An addressed invitation whose answer serves a real purpose.

These are meanings to compare, not four object types. A save, reaction or “looks fun” does not enroll someone. “Can I join?” requests participation where confirmation is needed. An explicitly open invitation may allow joining within its offered terms; do not add redundant host approval to every case.

Keep conversational softness without ambiguous system effects. A private feasibility question can prepare a proposal; it does not tell the friend or change the gathering. Where language leaves a consequential action unclear, clarify at that boundary—not during every casual share.

**Research basis:** indirect language can serve relationship-preserving purposes, while online event responses can express support rather than reliable attendance. These findings come from scenario experiments and a small historical interview study, not tests of this proposed composer. [Lee & Pinker](https://stevenpinker.com/files/pinker/files/lee_and__pinker_2010_rationales_for_indirect_speech_psycholoogical_review.pdf); [Huang, Wang & Yuan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.0663).

### B. Unequal participation: private Reply first, no activity accounting

**Start with an optional private reply. Do not add a separate acknowledgment system unless it improves on Reply in the comparisons.** Receiving and not responding is a legitimate ending. A voluntary “We tried your suggestion” is welcome; an automatic report that a friend viewed, saved or used it is not appreciation authored by that friend.

The cook, photographer, organizer, guest and quiet recipient need not contribute equally in the app. Nor should Vesper record offline care to balance an account. Protect the contributor too: make stopping sharing easy and absorb actual coordination work so generosity does not mean maintaining five interfaces.

For an actual dinner, the host can see the responses and headcount required to host it. A necessary answer or deadline is not the same as a social-engagement nudge. Keep those facts beside the gathering, without contribution scores, guilt copy or “your turn” mechanics.

**Research basis:** communal care differs from exchange accounting, but perceived under-benefiting can still matter. Support-imbalance evidence is correlational and concerns emotional support, not posting frequency; it does not justify measuring friendship through uploads. [Clark & Mills](https://clarkrelationshiplab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/Interpersonal%20attraction%20in%20exchance%20and%20commual%20relationships.pdf); [Wang & Gruenewald](https://www.midus.wisc.edu/findings/pdfs/1655.pdf).

### C. Awareness: connect chosen expressions, not hidden availability

**Begin social suggestions from deliberately shared material, explicit invitations, and scoped practical requests.** “Back in NYC this week,” authored for friends, can make an invitation feel timely. A private return ticket cannot silently become that status. A historic place mark is not evidence of where someone is now.

Private facts may help the person's own decision. Separately authorized availability checks can help a group without disclosing private reasons. Neither access nor a calendar gap establishes willingness to meet. Do not offer “You're both free tonight” from inferred gaps.

Author-chosen place/neighborhood/city precision and time should be intelligible without making every card a permissions explanation. City-level information can still be sensitive. An explanation of a recommendation must not disclose the private fact that its map hides.

**Research basis:** small location-sharing studies emphasize who is asking, purpose, usefulness and deliberate communication. They do not show that coarsening coordinates alone solves privacy, or that all automation is unwelcome. [Consolvo et al.](https://web.mit.edu/21W.789/www/spring2007/papers/p81-consolvo.pdf); [Iachello et al.](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/ubicomp2005-controldeceptioncommunication.pdf).

### D. Gathering: hospitality before social profiling

**Start with a small arrival-oriented view of the existing Occasion, not a new profile or questionnaire.** Use known facts to answer what kind of evening this is, when and where to arrive, whom to find, whether bringing something is expected, and which part this person is joining. Clarify missing details only when they matter.

A guest's dish photo, a shared picture or a host-authored introduction can provide something natural to talk about. Do not infer why someone was invited, pair guests by psychological compatibility, or assign an icebreaker. Optional assistance requested by a host remains possible; “no default homework” is not a prohibition on all conversation aids.

Design arrival and departure together. Dinner-only participation is complete, not failed group consensus. Leaving early needs coordination when it affects a ride, reservation or other dependency—not because the app expects maximum attendance.

**Research basis:** small HCI studies suggest shared material can support conversation, but technology-delivered questions have not uniformly reduced awkwardness. Conversation-ending research motivates attention to exits; it does not validate an app that infers when guests want to leave. [Kytö & McGookin](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10606-017-9281-1); [Zhang et al.](https://tinghanlin.com/publication/ice-breaking-technology/ice-breaking-technology.pdf); [Mastroianni et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7958231/).

### E. Receiving: original human material first, enrichment when earned

**Use original-only as a valid baseline, not an incomplete AI experience.** Preserve the photograph, words and author. Add a small, separately attributed Vesper layer when it supplies missing context, a genuinely new connection or practical usefulness.

Compare the same inputs as original-only, original plus compact context, and generated-led. For Paris/Rome, “you both experienced heat” is insufficient; a supported distinction about the places or choices must add something neither quote already says. For a food observation, a technique or usable recipe may beat an essay. A route can make a friend's suggestion actionable without rewriting their voice.

Do not require fresh generation for every card. Original contributions, existing object state and reusable world material can provide value directly. Timing or route claims still need appropriate evidence; a friend's past experience is not a current provider guarantee. Keep **Reply to Maya** distinct from **Ask Vesper privately**. Enrichment does not expand the original audience or become Maya's endorsement.

**Research basis:** a small group-photo study found that recipients valued some images photographers would not have selected to share; this is not permission for automatic camera-roll publication. Storytelling experiments suggest shared background can help listeners appreciate a story; that is a reason to supply useful context, not repeat the obvious. [Patel et al.](https://amy.voida.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/mobiphos-ijhcs09.pdf); [Cooney, Gilbert & Wilson](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28140768/).

### F. Continuity: continue something together, not a relationship level

**Start with the existing legitimate contact path and the particular thing to continue.** Sending the promised boat photograph or inviting someone to another cooking evening is more concrete than requiring a friend upgrade. Where no path exists, compare a bounded in-product connection with an external contact exchange; neither is assumed implemented or automatically authorized.

Private remembrance, reachability, an invitation, and a broader sharing audience remain separate choices. Do not turn these distinctions into a settings wizard. A coworker joining one dinner does not gain access to all future activity. Someone privately remembered in Life is not notified or made contactable just by being remembered.

No continuation is a complete outcome. After access expires or is withdrawn, preserve only legitimate independent records and permissions; do not silently reopen contact through an old Occasion. No friendship ladder, neglected-contact ranking or “you're drifting apart” diagnosis.

**Research basis:** observational research links shared opportunities and time with relationship continuity. It does not establish universal friendship milestones. Workplace research also identifies costs when relationships span roles, cautioning against indiscriminate audience expansion. [Hall](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0265407518761225); [Mollenhorst, Völker & Flap](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/changes-in-personal-relationships-how-social-contexts-affect-the-/); [Methot et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/peps.12109).

## 4. Placement: one system, different reasons to open each root

| Surface | Social value it owns in the experience | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Places** | Casual authored places and perspectives in the same world field; `From friends`, useful place context and exploration elsewhere | Not inferred presence, a people popularity ranking, or a second invitation inbox |
| **Home** | Addressed value and shared consequences where they matter: a sent recommendation, invitation, practical change or prepared shared possibility | Do not copy the casual friends field; preserve the accepted one-social-ask budget, not a one-human-artifact limit |
| **Chat** | Easy contribution, private understanding and preparation, with explicit human-directed sending when chosen | Private Ask must not silently become Reply, publication, retention or acceptance |
| **Life · People / records** | Stable, recognizable continuity through people, times, places and authorized artifacts | Not a present-tense activity feed, inferred social biography or relationship-maintenance dashboard |
| **Occasion / Plan objects** | Inviting, contributing, participating and adapting together; entered through the relevant root | Not a fifth root or a requirement for every shared photograph |

Preserve the existing exception: a casual non-spatial Status can have its featured Home doorway and its later Life record. Do not force a place onto every share. The conditional `Addressed to you` Home region holds timely, deliberately addressed material not already shown in full above it; two or more can warrant the grouping under the accepted amendment.

Places should remain generous when useful supply exists. A social scope is not a permanent “four friends, four things” ceiling. Sparse social supply is also normal: world value does not require recruiting friends, and quiet friends do not become an onboarding problem to fix.

## 6. One comparison packet, not six disconnected demonstrations

Use six situations, sharing the same small fixture world. The first two can reuse one café/photo source. These are design coverage cases, not a product launch narrowed to one proof loop.

| Situation | Views to compare | Required comfortable stopping point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Café shared in four different ways | Author's gesture; recipient's reading; only the consequential variant continuing into an invitation | See or enjoy it without joining or replying |
| Paris/Rome, then an ordinary NYC weekend | Original material; compact added understanding; optional useful local continuation | Receive the value without making a plan or writing a reflection |
| New guest at dinner | Host preparation; guest decision and arrival; partial attendance; a real timing change; optional departure coordination | Decline without explaining, or attend only the offered part |
| Unequal app participation over several weeks | Frequent contributor; quiet recipient; actual host work | Either person can stop without guilt; offline care need not be logged |
| Boat acquaintance | Recognizable private record; promised artifact; permitted continuation or unavailable contact path | Keep the memory without expanding access or contacting anyone |
| Coworker at a gathering | Bounded invitation; occasion contribution; later sharing choice | Participate without granting a broader friend audience |

Carry withdrawal and a private-Ask-versus-human-Reply comparison through the shared fixtures. Test no friends, no reply and no AI enrichment as complete states, not just error states. At least one useful invitation should actually lead to an offline activity; at least one equally valid experience should end with simple receiving. These are proposed scenarios, not claims of observed participation.

For every comparison, annotate **outside the interface**:

1. What the author deliberately offered and to whom.
2. What the recipient gets before contributing anything.
3. What response, if any, is genuinely needed and why.
4. The exact effect of the chosen action, its owner, and what remains unchanged.
5. Where the person returns, including retained navigation context and independent records.
6. What is illustrated, what is an accepted contract, and what runtime support remains unverified.

Do not fill the consumer screen with these six annotations. The interface should convey the relevant distinction through clear content, recipient labels, ordinary actions and proportionate disclosure.

## 7. Architectural handoff: reuse owners, expose unresolved seams

This is a design-to-contract checklist, not a new schema or service plan. Existing owner mapping must be checked by the implementation lane before building:

- **Source, authorship and audience:** preserve the original contribution and permitted uses; separately attribute any Vesper enrichment. A new projection does not grant a new audience.
- **Human message versus private Ask:** route to the intended recipient, show outgoing material at the sending boundary, and do not treat a private question as automatic retention or publication authority.
- **Invitation versus interest versus participation:** use the existing multiplayer/practical owner. Host edits do not create another person's acceptance; a reaction is not an RSVP.
- **Practical assistance:** the live engine can prepare feasibility and help an authorized arrangement adapt. Access to social material is not itself authority to create or change a plan. Ordinary preparation should make the engine useful beyond travel, without escalating every share into a task.
- **Guest identity and delivery:** value-first access, replies, contributions, expiry and reachability require an explicit supported path. Mark unsupported behavior as a dependency rather than drawing it as shipped.
- **Withdrawal and continuity:** remove disallowed source-dependent projections and revalidate prepared consequences; preserve legitimate independent material. Do not promise to erase external screenshots or human recollection.
- **Return and cost:** keep object identity and root context across navigation. Original material and existing state must work without bespoke generation for each impression. Supply and generation economics remain the content lane's responsibility.

Do not add a universal relationship-status column, a new ambient-social service, or generalized Occasion architecture to resolve a composition question. If a comparison exposes a genuine contract gap, record the concrete missing behavior and existing owner first. The [complete-system roadmap](complete-system-integration-roadmap-2026-09-05.md) remains the engineering coordination document.

## 8. Review, decision and completion criteria

**Recommended order:** first resolve the gesture/recipient/action distinction and original-first receiving treatments together; then apply those decisions to gathering hospitality and later continuity. Review the cross-root packet as a whole before promoting any changed contract. Independent design work can proceed inside existing lanes without waiting for every visual detail elsewhere.

Ask authors and recipients separately: What did you receive? What do you think is expected of you? What can the other person now see or do? Would you comfortably do nothing? Did this remove unwanted work or move it onto someone else? For the host and guest, does it make the real gathering easier to enter and enjoy?

Include relationships with different power and intimacy, including coworker/host situations and people with different language or accessibility needs. Fewer controls cannot eliminate existing social power. The research motivates these comparisons; it does not prove a universal low-pressure default.

The pass is complete when the six situations have understandable author/recipient meanings, immediate value, proportionate practical consequences, legitimate stopping points, and named implementation dependencies. Record each proposed default as accepted, revised or still open; promote only accepted changes through the owning canon/contract process.

Do not use equal posting, reply speed, view counts, dwell time or inferred relationship health as substitutes for those judgments. Wanted offline participation and felt welcome require human evidence; they cannot be inferred from an invitation being sent. This document ends the research-to-design handoff, not the product validation or engineering work.
