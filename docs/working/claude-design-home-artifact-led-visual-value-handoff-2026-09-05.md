# Home — recognizable things that return useful value

## 1. Assignment and status

Work in `/Users/feihuyan/Downloads/vesper-home`, the existing Claude Design
project. Improve the design artifact only. Do not implement the mobile app,
change backend contracts, or redesign adjacent roots.

The founder's latest direction:

> “I feel like the artifact can uplevel our visual from home and potentially
> place later on.”

Explore this seriously. The ticket/pass work suggests that Vesper's output can
become more recognizable, tangible, and immediately useful, with less reading
needed to understand what is on the page. The opportunity includes practical
help, curiosity, human perspective, anticipation, and continuity. A Home made
entirely of travel documents would miss the point.

**Design hypothesis:** a small family of recognizable forms can give Home
stronger hierarchy and richer expression while making the value easier to
understand and use. Test this in complete pages, not only a component gallery.

This brief extends the [generous-value Home handoff](claude-design-vesper-home-generous-value-handoff-2026-09-04.md).
It authorizes exploration, not new canonical entities or automatic adoption of
every recommendation below. In particular, the crown-priority alternatives in
section 8 require an explicit decision before implementation.

## 3. The product value to preserve

Vesper turns things someone gives it and attention they direct into worthwhile
understanding, possibilities, practical help, and continuity. Home is a primary
return on that contribution. It must remain worthwhile without another input.

The four moves remain **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** Forms can serve several moves. They do not become four feed sections
or one move per tab.

- Deliver the useful distinction before a tap where possible.
- Keep ordinary life forward-looking. Europe is part of the person's context,
  alongside this weekend, other people, new interests, and everyday needs.
- A meaningful reconstruction of the past can also be valuable. Avoid flat
  ingestion counts, repeated known connections, and personality interpretations.
- Preserve a longer, varied Home. Reducing text does not mean reducing it to
  one priority and an archive door.
- Make participation optional. Reading, enjoying, ignoring, and leaving are
  legitimate endings; no reflection prompt or save requirement follows by default.

## 4. Clarify what “artifact-led” means

Separate four concepts in the handback:

| Concept | Meaning in this exploration |
| --- | --- |
| Source or held object | The ticket, photograph, authored note, or existing owner-backed object |
| Value result | What Vesper makes useful: a comparison, explanation, workable route, reconstruction, or current consequence |
| Visual form | How that object or result is expressed here: pass, comparison, sequence, media unit, compact row |
| Design artifact | The Claude project used to explore the experience |

They are not interchangeable. A card is not a new database entity. A generated
comparison need not be retained as a new Life object just because it appeared
on Home. A source document and a result derived from it remain distinguishable.

Use “artifact-led” as a design direction, not a compulsory consumer noun or a
proposal for a universal Artifact owner. The user should recognize a ticket,
a walk, a friend's note, or an interesting explanation without learning our
internal taxonomy.

## 5. Candidate forms: start with a small family

Explore the first four families below in depth. Use the last two where they
improve an existing composition. These are comparisons to make, not six new
production components to ship or six required sections.

| Family | Value and visible payoff | Home expression | Optional continuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Held-object instrument | What matters about this ticket or commitment now | Recognizable identity, a few decisive current facts, one useful consequence | Exact object or contextual instrument; provider handoff where appropriate |
| Prepared possibility | A credible way an available window could unfold | A legible route/sequence or compact alternative set with the distinguishing practical details | Inspect the option or shape it through the existing owner; opening adopts nothing |
| Authored human contribution | The actual perspective, image, or judgment someone offered | Their words/media and the useful context, with authorship integral to the unit | Relevant Place, addressed contribution, or shared arrangement |
| Comparison or explanatory instrument | Something the person had not already noticed | Two or three meaningful contrasts, annotated evidence, or a short causal sequence | Read the explanation, inspect sources, or explore the relevant places |
| Usable method | A concrete answer that can be used without a conversation | A compact method, useful proportions, or decisive step; minimal setup prose | Open the method itself before optional history or conversation |
| Evidence-backed reconstruction | Scattered material becomes a revealing, readable account | A short journey strip, photo sequence, or meaningful pattern in documented facts | Exact Life record or underlying material, without claiming unsupported occurrence |

Recommended starting portfolio: the existing ticket, one ordinary NYC
possibility, one directed friend's contribution, and one substantive comparison.
Include a useful uncarded finding alongside them as a control.

For the Sorrento-cliff example, do not repeat “Sorrento has cliffs.” Explore a
comparison with other coastlines around a genuinely informative distinction
such as formation or settlement shape. Research would be needed for the actual
claims and comparable visuals; do not fabricate heights, geology, or diagrams
that imply measured scale. Likewise, a friend's Paris experience could create
a revealing juxtaposition only when the shared evidence actually supports it.

Current canon keeps findings, techniques, and facts bare. Explore explanatory
instruments within that rule first. If a bounded, reusable method or comparison
needs a different containment treatment, show the benefit and flag the exact
amendment rather than silently extending the component union.

## 6. Visual rules to test

### Recognizable identity, appropriate density

Keep enough identity across scales that people recognize the same thing:
name, kind, author, a meaningful image, or a restrained signature. Adapt the
visible information to the job. A small row need not be a miniature full pass.

Test compact, standard, and expanded expressions only where useful. Preserve
Life's accepted restrictions on where chips and live passes appear on Home.
Do not add pass-shaped decoration to unrelated knowledge or social content.

### Form should reduce interpretation work

A sequence should explain order. A comparison should reveal a difference. A
map should clarify a spatial relationship. A ticket should make its event and
useful state recognizable. Remove visual ornament that performs none of these
jobs before adding explanatory paragraphs to justify it.

Avoid fake barcodes, perforations everywhere, arbitrary scores, diagrams with
no informative relationship, and dashboards full of metrics. Provider-backed
admission credentials must remain distinguishable from Vesper's presentation.

### One hierarchy across different forms

Retain the accepted section/title/metadata/support roles. Do not stack a section
kicker, object kicker, and headline that repeat the same information. On the
current live ticket, consolidate repeated “Tonight,” door time, and leave time:
each fact should have a clear principal location.

Cards mark coherent objects or experiences, selectively. Mix them with compact
rows, bare findings, images, and whitespace. Avoid an identical card grid and
avoid returning to an undifferentiated wall of editorial text.

### Legible before impressive

Review at realistic phone width and larger text. The accepted chip's small
“object print” exception is not evidence of readability. Essential identity,
time, and action must remain legible; show an expanded or reflowed alternative
where needed. Never solve excess content by shrinking type or contrast.

Use labels and shape as well as color for meaning. Put explanatory alternatives
for graphs/maps in the handback and specify reading order. Do not depend on
hover or precision taps to understand essential content.

## 7. Continuity across roots

The same source and owner can support different useful expressions. Do not
copy the entire Home feed into Places or turn Life into another ranked feed.

| Example | Home | Places | Life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ticket | Timely consequence; live pass only in its accepted window | Relevant venue or route context when useful | Held source and appropriate record |
| Prepared afternoon | A graspable possibility with practical distinctions | The same route and places spatially inspectable | Existing kept object only if deliberately retained under its owner |
| Friend's note | Directed material or its shared consequence | Authored place contribution in its permitted scope | Person-linked record and refinding |
| Coastline comparison | The new distinction is visible here | Places compared around that specific distinction | Governed source/result continuity if retained |

Preserve the accepted social split: Home carries directed and
shared-consequential material; Places' `From friends` scope carries casual
place sharing; Life People holds the record. Keep the existing Home exception
for featured placeless Status. A casual note may inform a grounded Home
possibility within its permitted use, but do not duplicate it as a standalone
casual social feed on Home.

Places marks represent authored content at the author's chosen precision,
not live positions. Facepiles must not imply agreement, participation, or
attendance that has not been established. Let human contributions remain in
their own voice rather than converting every one into assistant prose.

For the Places extension, show only two connected examples: a friend-informed
possibility and a comparison or route. Demonstrate what becomes easier when
spatially expressed. Do not commission a full Places redesign yet.

Life remains the durable refinding context, but the exact destination follows
the canonical owner. A recipe link should open useful recipe content before
requiring a person to search a conversation. Do not label a generic archive
landing “exact object opened” in a prototype or implementation report.

## 8. Priority must preserve freedom and richness

Board 08's lifecycle is a strong starting point. Its collision behavior needs
a separate comparison before the provisional arbitration rule is implemented.

**Current provisional canon:** recovery outranks live commitment, which
outranks a deadline decision; the other object's ask becomes available after
the crown's ask is spent. Preserve a labeled baseline illustrating this rule.

**Recommended alternative for evaluation:** rank immediate prominence by what
delay would cost, actual deadlines, dependencies, and available actions. Keep
one dominant treatment while allowing deliberate access to other objects and
their actions. Separate a demanded response from an optional available action.
This changes the interpretation of the one-ask rule and needs explicit approval.

Use the existing collision: the delayed flight now departs at 9:30; Dana needs
a train answer by 6. If the flight change needs no immediate corrective action,
it should not automatically block the earlier social decision. Show that the
person can inspect/respond to Dana without first sending the flight update to
five people. A “no door” row must not make one task the price of another.

Also compare an actual departure rush with a long, safe waiting window. A
live ticket alone should not be assumed to require a nearly empty Home.
During waiting, food, reading, human perspective, and future possibilities
may be particularly useful. Show the proposed breadth treatment separately
where it differs from the accepted posture rules.

Timeliness can come from context, not just a literal deadline on a note.
Curiosity and enjoyable content do not need invented urgency to earn space.

## 10. Interaction and production handback

For each selected form record, outside the phone:

- Source/result and canonical owner; actual versus fixture/proposed capability.
- Immediate visible payoff, what the tap opens, and the return position.
- Whether it is simply read, explicitly kept, or used for an owner action.
- Which parts are stable identity and which are live or replaceable facts.
- Human authorship, audience, and source dependencies where applicable.
- Retrieval/structured composition/reusable content/bespoke synthesis class.
- Trigger for new work, permitted reuse, freshness/invalidation, and fallback.
- Required renderer/data seam and any unknown owner destination.

Opening, reading, or rendering a form must not silently retain an Ask, adopt a
Plan, invite someone, share, or subscribe to monitoring. Keep those boundaries
under the contribution contract without putting an essay about permissions on
each card. Source correction and withdrawal must reach dependent expressions.

Prefer reusable native-ready visual templates over bespoke generated imagery
or arbitrary generated interfaces for every feed item. Model-produced content
can fill a constrained presentation; this brief does not mandate an unchecked
runtime UI generator or a new generic artifact platform.

Opening or scrolling Home should primarily compose available value. Preserve
useful results while valid; refresh live facts separately from durable
explanations. Show a worthwhile page when no new synthesis is ready. Cost is
unmeasured until engineering measures generation, retrieval, refresh, and media
delivery; fewer words or a static-looking card does not prove low cost.

External booking links and provider context are in scope as existing boundaries.
Restoring checkout, rebooking, or a parallel operational workspace is not.

## 11. Evaluation, decisions, and delivery

For each before/after, answer:

1. Can someone identify what this is and receive useful value at a glance?
2. What becomes understandable visually that previously required reading?
3. Does each treatment carry substance beyond repeating supplied information?
4. If the leading offer is uninteresting, is the rest of Home still rewarding?
5. Does the visual form fit the content, and do simple findings stay simple?
6. Are people present through real contributions, not decorative avatars?
7. Can the person leave, ignore, or open another object without an assignment?
8. Does identity and destination remain coherent across scale and root changes?
9. Are essential facts and actions legible at larger text sizes?
10. Can engineering explain how this appears without generating everything anew?

Report word count and scroll height as diagnostics alongside distinct payoffs,
repetition, and reading burden. Do not optimize a numerical target at the
expense of useful content, legibility, or a generous page.

Deliver revised existing boards, the two bounded Places examples, a small
selected-form sheet, and a per-form production/interaction ledger. Recommend
which forms to adopt first, which to defer, and where a plain row or sentence
wins. Return one coherent recommended system, with comparisons retained as
evidence rather than several incompatible aesthetic directions.

Reconcile board 07's current rulings versus historical entries, especially
D-H4 and D-H10. Mark already-resolved corrections as history. Keep proposed
crown/demand changes, Places chip use, new containment treatments, palette
extensions, and unresolved accessibility choices explicitly separate from
accepted rules. Update related canon only after an explicit decision.
