## 3. The product value to preserve

Vesper turns things someone gives it and attention they direct into worthwhile
understanding, possibilities, practical help, and continuity. Home is a primary
return on that contribution. It must remain worthwhile without another input.

The four moves remain **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** Forms can serve several moves. They do not become four feed sections
or one move per tab.

- Deliver the useful distinction before a tap where possible.
- Keep ordinary life forward-looking. Europe is part of the person's context,
  alongside this weekend, other people, new interests, and everyday needs.
- A meaningful reconstruction of the past can also be valuable. Avoid flat
  ingestion counts, repeated known connections, and personality interpretations.
- Preserve a longer, varied Home. Reducing text does not mean reducing it to
  one priority and an archive door.
- Make participation optional. Reading, enjoying, ignoring, and leaving are
  legitimate endings; no reflection prompt or save requirement follows by default.

## 4. Clarify what “artifact-led” means

Separate four concepts in the handback:

| Concept | Meaning in this exploration |
| --- | --- |
| Source or held object | The ticket, photograph, authored note, or existing owner-backed object |
| Value result | What Vesper makes useful: a comparison, explanation, workable route, reconstruction, or current consequence |
| Visual form | How that object or result is expressed here: pass, comparison, sequence, media unit, compact row |
| Design artifact | The Claude project used to explore the experience |

They are not interchangeable. A card is not a new database entity. A generated
comparison need not be retained as a new Life object just because it appeared
on Home. A source document and a result derived from it remain distinguishable.

Use “artifact-led” as a design direction, not a compulsory consumer noun or a
proposal for a universal Artifact owner. The user should recognize a ticket,
a walk, a friend's note, or an interesting explanation without learning our
internal taxonomy.

## 6. Visual rules to test

### Recognizable identity, appropriate density

Keep enough identity across scales that people recognize the same thing:
name, kind, author, a meaningful image, or a restrained signature. Adapt the
visible information to the job. A small row need not be a miniature full pass.

Test compact, standard, and expanded expressions only where useful. Preserve
Life's accepted restrictions on where chips and live passes appear on Home.
Do not add pass-shaped decoration to unrelated knowledge or social content.

### Form should reduce interpretation work

A sequence should explain order. A comparison should reveal a difference. A
map should clarify a spatial relationship. A ticket should make its event and
useful state recognizable. Remove visual ornament that performs none of these
jobs before adding explanatory paragraphs to justify it.

Avoid fake barcodes, perforations everywhere, arbitrary scores, diagrams with
no informative relationship, and dashboards full of metrics. Provider-backed
admission credentials must remain distinguishable from Vesper's presentation.

### One hierarchy across different forms

Retain the accepted section/title/metadata/support roles. Do not stack a section
kicker, object kicker, and headline that repeat the same information. On the
current live ticket, consolidate repeated “Tonight,” door time, and leave time:
each fact should have a clear principal location.

Cards mark coherent objects or experiences, selectively. Mix them with compact
rows, bare findings, images, and whitespace. Avoid an identical card grid and
avoid returning to an undifferentiated wall of editorial text.

### Legible before impressive

Review at realistic phone width and larger text. The accepted chip's small
“object print” exception is not evidence of readability. Essential identity,
time, and action must remain legible; show an expanded or reflowed alternative
where needed. Never solve excess content by shrinking type or contrast.

Use labels and shape as well as color for meaning. Put explanatory alternatives
for graphs/maps in the handback and specify reading order. Do not depend on
hover or precision taps to understand essential content.

copy the entire Home feed into Places or turn Life into another ranked feed.

| Friend's note | Directed material or its shared consequence | Authored place contribution in its permitted scope | Person-linked record and refinding |

not live positions. Facepiles must not imply agreement, participation, or
attendance that has not been established. Let human contributions remain in

## 10. Interaction and production handback

For each selected form record, outside the phone:

- Source/result and canonical owner; actual versus fixture/proposed capability.
- Immediate visible payoff, what the tap opens, and the return position.
- Whether it is simply read, explicitly kept, or used for an owner action.
- Which parts are stable identity and which are live or replaceable facts.
- Human authorship, audience, and source dependencies where applicable.
- Retrieval/structured composition/reusable content/bespoke synthesis class.
- Trigger for new work, permitted reuse, freshness/invalidation, and fallback.
- Required renderer/data seam and any unknown owner destination.

Opening, reading, or rendering a form must not silently retain an Ask, adopt a
Plan, invite someone, share, or subscribe to monitoring. Keep those boundaries
under the contribution contract without putting an essay about permissions on
each card. Source correction and withdrawal must reach dependent expressions.

Prefer reusable native-ready visual templates over bespoke generated imagery
or arbitrary generated interfaces for every feed item. Model-produced content
can fill a constrained presentation; this brief does not mandate an unchecked
runtime UI generator or a new generic artifact platform.

Opening or scrolling Home should primarily compose available value. Preserve
useful results while valid; refresh live facts separately from durable
explanations. Show a worthwhile page when no new synthesis is ready. Cost is
unmeasured until engineering measures generation, retrieval, refresh, and media
delivery; fewer words or a static-looking card does not prove low cost.

External booking links and provider context are in scope as existing boundaries.
Restoring checkout, rebooking, or a parallel operational workspace is not.

### 13.4 Draw the whole encounter, including stopping

For each specimen show a connected path, with only the steps that apply:

1. **Encounter:** a complete Home unit in a real full scroll; enough value is
   visible without a tap. The user may simply enjoy it and leave.
2. **Open:** the exact promised piece or object. An explanation opens useful
   reading, a photo opens the photo, a route opens its spatial expression.
   Do not route every form to Chat, sources, or a generic Life landing.
3. **Inspect:** one optional, direct original/evidence path and its return.
   The person need not traverse journey → chapter → day to reach a ticket.
4. **Retain or act, only when chosen:** show the named effect, owner readback,
   and appropriate quiet receipt/repair. Do not attach a save assignment to
   every piece or place four competing buttons below every Home unit.
5. **Find again, where supported:** show the recognizable entry and exact
   destination in the relevant Life context or search result. Reuse the title,
   author, or distinctive media; a saved explanation is not merely a source
   list under a new title.
6. **Return:** restore the originating root, selection, query/scope where
   relevant, and position. Back from a directly opened object returns to the
   door used, not automatically to its containing Life record.
7. **Changed return:** show what happens when the original Home unit has
   expired, moved, or changed while the detail was open. Preserve orientation
   without resurrecting stale content or silently substituting another item.

Also show one unsaved piece after its valid availability ends. It must not
reappear as an exact historical saved object without authorized persistence.
The design should communicate whatever remains honestly accessible and offer
an optional useful continuation, not a scolding “you should have saved it.”

### 13.6 Saved words and current facts

Use Life `P3` to distinguish a piece as saved from the current record and any
live practical facts. Preserve exact authored wording and selected-version
identity, subject to source rights and withdrawal. A frozen version is not an
exemption from correction, audience changes, or deletion dependencies.

Show one small, specific changed-information indication where it matters,
with optional detail. Do not produce a revisions inbox, ask the person to
reconcile every new photo, or overwrite their caption during regeneration.
An updated generation, when explicitly requested and supported, is visibly a
new version. Current hours or availability belong in a distinct current-state
treatment; an unavailable check must not read as “still current.”

Saved and unsaved versions should remain enjoyable to read. Put detailed
owner/revision reasoning in the ledger and optional inspection, not paragraphs
of reassurance on every phone screen.

### 13.7 Visual composition and breadth

compact refinding expression. These densities may use different layouts.
