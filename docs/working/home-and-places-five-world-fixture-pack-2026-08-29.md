# Home and Places — Five-World Fixture Pack

## Decision

Test the proposed Home and Places value systems through five complete evidence
worlds:

1. Sorrento cliffs, marina, hotel, ferry, and a New York waterfront;
2. Rome heat carried into an upcoming New York weekend;
3. southern Italian pasta carried into New York cooking and dining;
4. Rome and Paris through separately authored friend evidence; and
5. a disrupted Rome-to-New-York return.

The fixtures test complete semantic compositions, not final visual design.
They specify what the user receives, why it is valuable, what Home foregrounds,
what Places owns in depth, which evidence and authority are required, what
degrades or disappears, and which engine seams recur.

Exact geometry, typography, animation, illustration, and component styling
belong in a dedicated design environment after the semantic portfolio is
accepted.

## 1. Authority and evidence boundary

The founder stories supply product provenance, not production truth. Every
historical, scientific, geographic, culinary, social, provider, weather,
schedule, legal, accessibility, or availability claim in this pack is either:

- an explicit founder observation;
- a labeled fixture assumption;
- a proposed Vesper inference with stated limits; or
- a production claim that would require current authoritative evidence.

The fixtures must not be interpreted as claiming:

- that a ticket proves travel occurred;
- that a photograph proves what was eaten, liked, or prepared in the kitchen;
- that the named friend supplied or authorized any specific Paris material;
- that the same weather actually occurred in Rome and Paris;
- that one failed route proves a stable preference or bodily limit;
- that a traveler is legally entitled to compensation;
- that inventory is held;
- or that a generated possibility is desired or committed.

Production versions must preserve Source, Claim, author, audience, current
conditions, freshness, correction, and withdrawal.

## 2. Common fixture protocol

### 2.1 The six comparisons

Each evidence world compares six treatments:

| Treatment | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Infrastructure** | Map, calendar, weather, provider, message, or archive state without Vesper's cross-source contribution |
| **Flat report** | A factual recap that is accurate but creates no new understanding, capability, or possibility |
| **Homework** | A prompt, choice set, reflection request, or assembly task that transfers synthesis back to the person |
| **Overreach** | Identity interpretation, false causal certainty, unmandated action, decontextualized human knowledge, or premature commitment |
| **Recommended** | Complete, bounded value with evidence, root-native expression, authority, lifecycle, and stopping condition |
| **Silence or smaller** | No treatment, or a deliberately smaller one, when marginal value is insufficient |

The recommended treatment must beat infrastructure, flat report, homework,
overreach, and silence. More Vesper is not automatically better.

### 2.2 Common evidence ledger

Every fixture resolves:

| Field | Required resolution |
| --- | --- |
| **Immediate job** | What human job exists now, if any? |
| **Source** | What entered the system, from whom, and under what custody? |
| **Observation** | What did the person or provider directly establish? |
| **Claim** | What factual, operational, interpretive, or social claim may be made? |
| **Known-to-person check** | What did the person already notice or state? |
| **Added value** | What mechanism, relation, contrast, option, or prepared consequence does Vesper add? |
| **Current condition** | What makes the contribution timely? |
| **Authority** | May Vesper explain, propose, prepare, monitor, contact, commit, publish, or only remain read-only? |
| **Affected principals** | Whose participation, privacy, money, time, rights, or reputation may change? |
| **Owner** | Which canonical object or domain owns truth and revision? |
| **Expiry** | Which condition, correction, decision, or elapsed backstop ends eligibility? |

### 2.3 Common Home contract

Every Home composition uses four semantic regions:

```text
situational frame
  → zero or one foreground
  → bounded composed field
  → continuity and stable doors
```

It must obey:

- the first viewport delivers value before asking for input;
- at most one unresolved primary decision dominates;
- every other unit normally completes on view;
- one lead medium owns each unit;
- social material modifies the contribution it changes;
- a past trip appears only when it creates a present difference;
- opening provides depth, inspection, or action rather than revealing a hidden
  answer; and
- scroll-past is a complete ending.

### 2.4 Common Places contract

Every standard Places projection may contain four faces. These are semantic
roles, not permanent page sections:

1. **World face** — stable identity, geometry, durable characteristics, and
   source-bound explanation.
2. **Current affordance face** — what works now under current conditions,
   including freshness, alternatives, limits, and fallback.
3. **Relationship face** — the viewer's authorized Sources, prior Encounters,
   human contributions, and demonstrated continuity.
4. **Horizon face** — a sparse two-to-four-edge path connecting the focal Place
   to another Place, person, mechanism, event, or possible future.

Places shows paths and questions, not a raw graph. Current operational truth
must not inherit authority from durable editorial content.

### 2.5 Common value receipt

Each visible unit records:

| Receipt field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Product move** | Make sense, Open possibility, Help it work, Carry forward—or a real combination? |
| **Human power** | What can the person now notice, understand, judge, do, coordinate, or continue? |
| **Lead medium** | Evidence, sequence, comparison, spatial field, concise prose, or instrument? |
| **Trust footprint** | What attribution, freshness, uncertainty, and authority must remain visible? |
| **Demand** | Is the value complete on view? What constitutive effort remains with the person? |
| **Owner handoff** | Where does depth or action land, with which context preserved? |
| **Attention counterfactual** | Why does this beat silence now? |
| **Lifecycle** | What happens after ignore, open, correction, expiry, action, or withdrawal? |

## 3. Shared evidence world

The five fixtures assume an authorized test corpus containing:

- New York departure on or after August 14;
- Nice, Sorrento, Capri or Amalfi, Rome, and return to New York;
- transport documents and provider messages;
- hotels or temporary bases;
- menu and dish photographs;
- a founder-authored observation that Sorrento has cliffs and that town,
  marina, luggage, and ferry movement created practical friction;
- a founder-authored observation that pasta in Italy tasted texturally
  different;
- Rome heat, walking, nighttime abundance, and “tour as homework” observations;
- a separately authorized friend's Paris evidence for a comparable interval;
- return-flight cancellation, rerouting, lodging, meal, compensation, and work
  rescheduling evidence;
- current New York location at an appropriately broad scope;
- an upcoming open weekend window; and
- current weather, hours, transit, provider state, and availability only when a
  fixture or live authoritative source supplies them.

The corpus is intentionally richer than any Home composition. Data abundance
increases candidate quality; it does not create a content quota.

## F1 — Sorrento cliffs to a New York waterfront

### 4. Situation

The user is home in New York. Saturday from 2–8 PM is explicitly open. The
recent Sorrento evidence includes:

- town and marina Place identities;
- a hotel or temporary base at a verified elevation and location;
- ferry documents and provider truth;
- photographs showing the coastal edge;
- founder-authored questions about Marina Piccola versus Marina Grande,
  luggage, check-in, vertical movement, and walking into town; and
- correction or Outcome evidence showing whether the route worked.

The New York side contains a verified waterfront route, current transit,
weather, walking load, and an existing dinner anchor. The fixture uses Red Hook
as a candidate focal Place only if current evidence makes it feasible.

### 5. Value hypothesis

The value is not “Sorrento has cliffs” or “here are other cliffs.” It is a
portable spatial distinction:

> A waterfront can be close in distance while separated from daily life by a
> physical or network threshold. Sorrento's town–marina threshold is primarily
> vertical; Red Hook's candidate threshold is primarily network reachability.

The exact comparison requires verified topography and transit evidence. The
analogy matters because it changes how the user evaluates arrival, base,
transition, and the experience of getting there.

### 6. Comparative treatments

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Two maps, ferry schedules, a calendar window, and restaurant results | Accurate ingredients; the person assembles the relation and day |
| Flat report | “You visited Sorrento, took ferries, and stayed near the cliffs” | Retrospective inventory without new value |
| Homework | “Which part of Sorrento changed how you think about movement?” | Demands interpretation Vesper can already prepare |
| Overreach | “You experience travel through transitions more than destinations” | Converts situational evidence into identity |
| Recommended | One threshold comparison that produces a usable New York interval and a deeper typed Place path | Adds understanding and possibility |
| Silence | Correct if comparison evidence is weak, the New York interval is not feasible, or the analogy has no present consequence | Live alternative |

### 7. Home full scroll

#### F1-H0 — Situational frame

```text
NEW YORK · THURSDAY
Saturday 2–8 PM is open
```

No copy claims the user misses the coast or needs a post-travel cure.

#### F1-H1 — Foreground: one waterfront interval

```text
TWO WATERFRONTS, TWO DIFFERENT THRESHOLDS

In Sorrento, the short town–marina distance concealed a vertical transfer.
For Saturday, Red Hook has the reverse problem: flatter ground, but fewer
continuous ways in and out.

Ferry approach → waterfront walk → dinner near the return
2:20–7:40 PM · one fixed departure · land return remains open

[Open the spatial field]
```

The lead medium is a compact comparison supporting a spatial sequence. It
contains the new distinction and one complete possibility before opening.

#### F1-H2 — Composed field: what the hotel changed

```text
THE HOTEL CHANGED THE USABLE COAST

Its position did not merely change the view. It changed the marina threshold,
the luggage path, and which departures were comfortably reachable.

[small base-and-reachability diagram]
```

This is a hidden-system contribution, not a hotel memory. It is admitted only
if the actual base, route, and provider evidence support the relation.

#### F1-H3 — Composed field: the failure carried forward

```text
THE RETURN PATH IS THE IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE

Sorrento's difficult segment followed the ferry. Saturday's route keeps a
level, land-based return if the fixed crossing stops fitting.
```

The unit uses the failed or difficult route conditionally. It does not say the
user dislikes hills or ferries.

#### F1-H4 — Continuity and doors

```text
Open Saturday in Places
Sorrento · Red Hook · All plans and occasions
Chat · Places · Life
```

No additional Europe recap is added.

### 8. Places projection

#### F1-P1 — Sorrento world face

- town, coastal edge, marina identities, and verified topography;
- observable evidence from authorized photographs;
- the distinction among physical distance, elevation, transfer path, and
  practical reachability;
- stable explanation separated from live ferry state; and
- any historical or geological claim sourced independently.

#### F1-P2 — Sorrento relationship face

- the user's explicit cliff observation;
- arrival and luggage questions;
- the actual or corrected movement sequence;
- one Outcome or unresolved uncertainty; and
- no generated claim that the experience was meaningful or preferred.

#### F1-P3 — Red Hook current affordance face

- current route, ferry or land truth, weather, walking, access, and hours;
- fixed versus flexible segments;
- one intent-preserving return path;
- an honest statement if provider inventory is not held; and
- no Plan creation without commitment.

#### F1-P4 — Horizon path

```text
Sorrento town
  → vertical marina threshold
  → ferry-dependent regional reachability
  → comparison with Red Hook's network threshold
  → Saturday interval with a different fallback structure
```

Every edge names its relation type and important difference. Red Hook is not
presented as “similar to Sorrento” or as a recommendation generated from a
coastal preference.

### 9. Receipt and lifecycle

| Field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Product moves | Make sense + Open possibility + Carry forward |
| Human power | Read arrival and waterfront access as systems, then enter one usable interval |
| Lead media | Home comparison; Places spatial field |
| Trust | Source per topographic, provider, route, and Outcome claim; analogy limit visible |
| Demand | Complete on view; opening explores; commitment remains explicit |
| Owner | Places owns Place truth and horizon; Plan owns later commitment; Life owns retained trip Sources |
| Attention | Beats silence only while the Saturday interval is feasible and the comparison adds a real distinction |
| Lifecycle | Degrade to a durable Sorrento Place explanation when New York conditions expire; recompute after correction |

### 10. Evaluation oracles

- The user can state the threshold difference in five seconds.
- The New York possibility remains useful if the Sorrento comparison is
  removed, but loses a meaningful perceptual layer.
- The Sorrento explanation remains useful if the New York option expires.
- The user can identify where the analogy breaks.
- A later waterfront judgment improves without Vesper claiming stable taste.
- No route, provider state, or occurrence is inferred from a ticket alone.

## F2 — Rome heat to an upcoming New York weekend

### 11. Situation

The user is home. The weekend is approaching under a forecast whose outdoor
comfort implications remain uncertain. The Rome evidence includes:

- explicit observations that August heat made walking and cultural abundance
  feel like homework, including at night;
- route, time, and Place evidence where authorized;
- periods of shade, indoor recovery, or temporary divergence from a companion;
- current scientific and urban evidence sufficient to explain relevant heat
  mechanisms; and
- no durable claim that the user dislikes heat or has a fixed energy profile.

### 12. Value hypothesis

The value is not “Rome was hot.” It is the combination of:

- a current temporal judgment about when New York commitment is useful;
- a grounded urban mechanism that makes heat spatially legible;
- a route and fallback that preserve the desired character of the weekend; and
- a practical capability carried forward without psychographic inference.

### 13. Comparative treatments

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Forecast, heat index, transit map, venue list | Necessary truth, but no lived interval or transfer |
| Flat report | “You walked extensively in hot Rome” | Accurate but backward-looking |
| Homework | “How do you want to recover from vacation this weekend?” | Invents a need and asks for synthesis |
| Overreach | “You need shade because Rome exhausted you” | Unsupported inner-state and durable-trait inference |
| Recommended | A temporal posture, one heat mechanism, and an intent-preserving route pair | Immediate value with low demand |
| Silence | Correct when forecast uncertainty cannot change action or when ordinary infrastructure already provides the whole answer | Live alternative |

### 14. Home full scroll

#### F2-H0 — Situational frame

```text
NEW YORK · FRIDAY
Saturday remains open; the heat-sensitive branch resolves at noon
```

#### F2-H1 — Foreground: temporal posture

```text
WAIT UNTIL NOON

The waterfront start and indoor continuation both remain feasible.
The forecast becomes materially more useful at noon, and neither path needs
booking before then.

If the hotter branch holds:
start after 5:30 → shaded approach → indoor continuation near the return

Next change: forecast check at 12:00
```

This is closure, not deferred homework. Vesper owns the authorized monitor and
will remain silent unless the material branch changes.

#### F2-H2 — Composed field: the hidden system

```text
NIGHT DOES NOT COOL EVERY STREET AT THE SAME RATE

Water, shade, stored heat in materials, street geometry, and airflow can make
the same city feel different block by block after sunset.

[small here/there thermal-path comparison]
```

The wording and representation must remain within the evidence. A compact
diagram cannot imply more causal precision than the sources support.

#### F2-H3 — Composed field: what Rome changes now

```text
ROME'S USEFUL LESSON WAS PROCEDURAL

Resolve the physical burden first. Let interpretation return only after the
route, shade, water, and stopping point work.
```

This is a product capability return. It does not claim what Rome meant to the
user.

#### F2-H4 — Continuity and doors

```text
Open Saturday in Places
Heat-aware route · indoor continuation · current plans
```

### 15. Places projection

#### F2-P1 — Current New York affordance face

- current temperature and heat-risk sources with freshness;
- shade, water, indoor recovery, walking, transit, and access;
- two materially different routes, not ten venues;
- what remains feasible under both forecast branches;
- the decision threshold and next information event; and
- an intent-preserving fallback.

#### F2-P2 — Hidden-system lens

The user can follow one question:

> Why can two nearby evening routes feel physically different after sunset?

The projection joins observable cues to bounded mechanisms. It distinguishes
current forecast from durable built-environment explanation.

#### F2-P3 — Relationship and transfer face

- the Rome observation remains founder-authored;
- Vesper's procedural synthesis is labeled;
- no capability is marked demonstrated unless later evidence shows use; and
- a correction can retire the analogy without deleting the Rome Source.

#### F2-P4 — Horizon path

```text
Rome night route
  → stored heat, shade, and recovery constraints
  → New York block-level thermal differences
  → Saturday start-time branch
  → shaded or indoor continuation
```

The path includes the important differences between climates, urban form, and
the specific conditions. It is not a city ranking.

### 16. Receipt and lifecycle

| Field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Product moves | Help it work + Make sense + Open possibility + Carry forward |
| Human power | Preserve a good weekend while reducing heat burden and understanding why routes differ |
| Lead media | Home instrument; Places spatial comparison |
| Trust | Weather freshness, source-bound mechanisms, uncertainty, and no medical claims |
| Demand | Zero until a material commitment boundary; monitor authority expires after the window |
| Owner | Places owns route and current affordance; Plan owns commitment; Source and Outcome remain governed separately |
| Attention | One threshold replaces repeated checking; cultural content remains suppressed while physical guidance owns attention |
| Lifecycle | Monitor ends after decision or window; mechanism can persist with Place; transfer remains candidate until demonstrated |

### 17. Evaluation oracles

- The user understands why waiting is useful and when waiting stops being
  useful.
- No viable option is lost because Vesper delayed incorrectly.
- The route remains usable without reading the mechanism.
- The mechanism changes later unaided noticing without becoming a lesson.
- Home creates no more than one decision.
- If uncertainty remains immaterial, the whole foreground disappears.

## F3 — Southern Italian pasta to New York

### 18. Situation

The user explicitly observed that pasta in Italy tasted or felt different. The
authorized evidence may include:

- dish and menu photographs;
- restaurant, region, date, and dish identity where verified;
- the person's exact sensory description;
- authoritative culinary sources about ingredients, extrusion, starch,
  emulsification, finishing, service timing, and regional practice;
- current New York restaurant, market, or cooking possibilities; and
- no proof of a kitchen process from appearance alone.

### 19. Value hypothesis

The value is a bounded mechanism that the user can later perceive or apply:

```text
sensory anomaly
  → observable evidence
  → one supported mechanism or distinction
  → important uncertainty or alternative explanation
  → one current New York context where the distinction becomes usable
```

This broadens Vesper beyond art and history while avoiding a recipe lesson or
identity claim.

### 20. Comparative treatments

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Restaurant history, menu translation, recipe results | Useful fragments without evidence-led transfer |
| Flat report | “You ate pasta throughout southern Italy” | Inventory; occurrence may not even be proven |
| Homework | “Upload your favorite dish and tell us why it mattered” | Input tax after the user already supplied attention |
| Overreach | “You are becoming a more discerning eater” | Identity interpretation and flattery |
| Recommended | Annotated evidence plus one mechanism, its limits, and one optional present use | Substantive and transferable |
| Silence | Correct if the dish, sensory observation, or sources cannot support a mechanism beyond generic pasta advice | Live alternative |

### 21. Home full scroll

#### F3-H0 — Situational frame

```text
NEW YORK · WEEKEND
One southern-Italy question has a practical return here
```

This frame disappears if the contribution does not clear admission.

#### F3-H1 — Foreground: mechanism on the evidence

```text
THE LAST MINUTE MAY EXPLAIN MORE THAN THE INGREDIENT LIST

[authorized dish photograph]

1  Sauce clings as a thin film rather than pooling.
2  The finish looks integrated at service, not added afterward.

Those cues are consistent with a starch-supported emulsion and pan finishing.
They do not prove how this kitchen prepared the dish.

[See the evidence and alternatives]
```

The exact annotations depend on expert-supported visual evidence. If the image
cannot support them, the medium changes or the contribution is rejected.

#### F3-H2 — Composed field: practical transfer

```text
ONE WAY TO TEST THE DISTINCTION IN NEW YORK

Compare when the sauce and pasta come together, not only which ingredients are
listed. A current nearby possibility makes that process observable this
weekend; no reservation is required now.
```

This may open a restaurant, market, or cooking context. The initial value is
the distinction, not a task to cook or book.

#### F3-H3 — Composed field: plural lineage

```text
“AUTHENTIC” IS TOO BLUNT FOR THIS QUESTION

Regional practice, restaurant service, ingredient supply, and New York's own
Italian and Italian-American histories can produce different textures without
one becoming the universal standard.
```

This unit appears only with sources and positioned perspectives. It prevents a
technique claim from turning into cultural superiority.

#### F3-H4 — Continuity and doors

```text
Open the dish in Places
Southern Italy ↔ New York · Sources · current possibilities
```

### 22. Places projection

#### F3-P1 — World face

- dish identity and observed form;
- relevant regional and restaurant context;
- ingredient, technique, service, and supply claims separated;
- visual evidence distinguished from unobserved kitchen process; and
- source-specific cultural authority.

#### F3-P2 — Current affordance face

- one or two New York contexts where the distinction is perceivable or usable;
- current hours, availability, cost, access, and commitment threshold;
- no claim that the user wants to reproduce the dish; and
- a complete understanding path even if no current venue qualifies.

#### F3-P3 — Relationship and capability face

- the user's sensory observation remains authored by the user;
- the Vesper explanation names its evidence and alternatives;
- later cooking, ordering, explaining, or noticing may demonstrate transfer;
- a save or dwell does not demonstrate capability; and
- contradiction retires or narrows the transfer candidate.

#### F3-P4 — Horizon path

```text
observed southern-Italy dish
  → sauce–starch–finishing mechanism candidate
  → service and regional conditions
  → New York Italian and Italian-American transformations
  → one present context where the difference is observable
```

The path follows mechanism and history, not “similar restaurants.”

### 23. Receipt and lifecycle

| Field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Product moves | Make sense + Carry forward + optional Open possibility |
| Human power | Distinguish a physical mechanism and recognize its conditions elsewhere |
| Lead media | Home annotated evidence; Places evidence-led horizon |
| Trust | User observation, source per mechanism, inference limit, cultural positionality |
| Demand | Explanation complete on view; cooking, booking, or asking remains optional |
| Owner | Place or Encounter owns the explanation; Life owns retained Source; Plan owns later commitment |
| Attention | Beats silence only if it adds a mechanism beyond “Italian pasta tastes different” |
| Lifecycle | Current possibility expires; explanation persists with owner; capability status changes only on valid later evidence |

### 24. Evaluation oracles

- The user can state one mechanism and one alternative explanation.
- The user can identify which evidence is observed versus inferred.
- A later dish becomes easier to read without Vesper prompting reflection.
- The comparison avoids “authentic versus inferior.”
- The New York possibility is useful but not required for the explanation to
  complete.
- No photographed dish is marked eaten or liked without direct evidence.

## F4 — Rome and Paris through attributed friends

### 25. Situation

The founder proposed that a friend's simultaneous Paris experience could make
Rome more legible. The fixture assumes separately authorized evidence on one
comparable axis, such as:

- time and broad Place;
- temperature or weather from authoritative sources;
- route or movement evidence;
- photographs or authored observations;
- river, street, dining, transit, or nighttime use; and
- explicit audience and reuse grants.

The friend evidence is synthetic until the actual author supplies and permits
it. The friend is not contacted merely because Vesper can imagine a useful
comparison.

### 26. Value hypothesis

The social value is not co-presence, location awareness, or friend activity.
It is an attributed difference neither person could establish from their own
experience alone.

For this fixture, assume the supported axis is **how water entered the evening
under heat**. The production contribution must verify:

- the conditions were sufficiently comparable;
- the rivers or waterfronts actually affected the observed routes;
- city-scale claims do not exceed the evidence; and
- each person may see the other's material in this transformed use.

### 27. Comparative treatments

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Map, weather, and two message threads | Evidence exists but remains unjoined |
| Flat report | “Your friend was in Paris while you were in Rome” | Social coincidence without substance |
| Homework | “Ask Maya what Paris felt like and compare notes” | Creates labor and reply pressure |
| Overreach | “You and Maya experienced Europe in opposite ways” | Synthetic relationship and identity narrative |
| Recommended | Two attributed lanes on one verified axis, followed by the structural difference and its limit | Multiplayer increases perception |
| Silence | Correct if audience, comparability, attribution, or new semantic value fails | Mandatory alternative |

### 28. Home full scroll

#### F4-H0 — Situational frame

```text
ROME ↔ PARIS · THE SAME WEEK
Two separately authored evenings, compared with permission
```

#### F4-H1 — Foreground: attributed comparison

```text
THE DIFFERENCE WAS NOT SIMPLY THE TEMPERATURE

ROME · YOU · 9:10 PM
[authorized evidence lane]
The route remained inside dense streets; the river was not part of the evening.

PARIS · MAYA · 9:05 PM
[authorized evidence lane]
The riverbank became the place to remain outside.

Vesper's synthesis
The useful contrast is how each route made water available—not who handled
heat better. The city-scale explanation remains narrower than these two
evenings.

[Open the comparison]
```

The exact content is fixture-only. Each lane preserves author, evidence, time,
and audience. Vesper owns only the connective statement.

#### F4-H2 — Composed field: human routing decision

```text
NO NEW QUESTION IS NEEDED

Maya's existing authorized trace already answers the comparison. Vesper will
not ask her to restate it.
```

This sentence is primarily an internal receipt and may not need visible UI.
The user-facing composition simply omits an Ask control. It is recorded here
to make burden-aware routing testable.

#### F4-H3 — Composed field: present Place opening

```text
THIS WEEKEND, NEW YORK OFFERS BOTH RELATIONSHIPS TO WATER

One route uses the waterfront as destination; another uses a crossing as the
transition. Places shows the distinction without treating Rome or Paris as a
preference profile.
```

This unit appears only if the current New York spatial field supports it and it
adds marginal value after the comparison.

#### F4-H4 — Continuity and doors

```text
Open Rome ↔ Paris in Places
Original Sources · audience · New York water routes
```

### 29. Places projection

#### F4-P1 — Two Place faces, no synthetic city verdict

Each Place retains:

- stable geometry and independently sourced urban context;
- the author's exact evidence and observation;
- weather and time as separate current-world Sources;
- the route or Place relation actually observed; and
- a boundary against generalizing one evening into “how Paris works” or “how
  Rome works.”

#### F4-P2 — Difference map

The standard comparison shows:

| Question | Rome evidence | Paris evidence | What Vesper may add |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conditions | Current authoritative Source | Current authoritative Source | Whether comparison is reasonable |
| Route relation to water | User-authored or observed route | Friend-authored or observed route | Typed spatial contrast |
| Experience | Each person speaks only for self | Each person speaks only for self | No synthetic group feeling |
| City mechanism | Independent sources required | Independent sources required | Bounded mechanism and important limits |

#### F4-P3 — Human source routing

Places first checks:

1. current world evidence;
2. existing authorized human trace;
3. Vesper synthesis;
4. optional contact only when the unresolved question is concrete and the
   expected value exceeds burden.

May-use, may-name, and may-contact remain separate grants. Nonresponse is a
complete state with no rejection inference.

#### F4-P4 — Horizon path

```text
Rome evening route
  → relation to the Tiber in this observed interval
  → Maya's Paris evening and relation to the Seine
  → supported structural difference and its limits
  → two New York waterfront possibilities under current conditions
```

The friend is part of the explanatory path, not a social shelf.

- Existing traces reduce human interruption.

## F5 — Disrupted return, rights, and intent-preserving recovery

### 32. Situation

The return to New York is disrupted. The authorized evidence may contain:

- airline cancellation or significant-change notice;
- original and rerouted itineraries;
- provider state and timestamps;
- terminal, airport, luggage, lodging, and meal information;
- receipts;
- a work interview or home commitment affected by the delay;
- jurisdiction-specific passenger-rights sources;
- traveler choices already made or still open; and
- no assumption that an action receipt proves provider completion.

This fixture is intentionally live and material. Recovery outranks editorial
content and ordinary possibility.

### 33. Value hypothesis

The value is a stable next step across travel, money, work, and Place:

```text
provider state
  → feasible movement branches
  → choice-sensitive rights and deadlines
  → prepared evidence and action
  → affected commitment repair
  → occurrence and Outcome readback
```

Vesper should preserve the trip's immediate intent—returning safely and
reliably—without turning the disruption into a generic travel hack or later
identity claim.

### 34. Comparative treatments

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Airline app, airport map, email, hotel search, calendar | Each domain is useful; the person reconciles the consequence |
| Flat report | “Your Rome flight was cancelled and you were rerouted through Chicago” | Record without recovery |
| Homework | “What would you like to do next?” followed by all options | Transfers urgent synthesis to a stranded user |
| Overreach | “You are entitled to €600; Vesper filed the claim” | Potentially false rights advice and fake action success |
| Recommended | Current state, one material choice or no-action state, prepared recovery, and exact authority boundary | Restores practical control |
| Silence | Usually loses during stranded state; becomes correct after all material consequences settle | Lifecycle alternative |

### 35. Home full scroll

#### F5-H0 — Situational frame

```text

Current provider state verified 8 minutes ago
```

#### F5-H1 — Foreground: recovery instrument

```text

The first viewport reveals current state, material consequence, preparation,
and authority. It never represents prepared work as completed work.

#### F5-H2 — Composed field: immediate Place recovery

Two reachable hotel areas preserve the morning route.

```

Places owns the spatial branch, current transit, access, hours, and provider
relationship. It does not claim inventory is held.

#### F5-H3 — Composed field: affected commitment

```text

The draft states the verified cancellation and two available windows.
It has not been sent.

[Review in the interview plan]
```

The commitment remains compact because it is subordinate to physical recovery.
Contacting another person remains an affected-person boundary.

#### F5-H4 — Composed field: evidence custody

```text
RECEIPTS ACCOUNTED FOR

Lodging, meals, and provider messages are grouped by the disruption branch.
Eligibility is not yet asserted.
```

No receipt-upload request appears because the evidence is already present.

#### F5-H5 — Continuity and doors

```text
Return route · airport Places · interview plan · receipts and rights sources
```

All unrelated editorial content is suppressed.

### 36. Places projection

#### F5-P1 — Current airport and route affordance face

- verified current terminal or airport position when authorized;
- provider-backed route states and freshness;
- connection geometry, transfer burden, access, operating hours, and luggage
  implications;
- reachable hotel areas and morning return paths;
- alternatives that preserve reliable return; and
- explicit unknowns and provider handoff.

#### F5-P2 — Branch field

```text
reroute now
  → movement consequence
  → hotel and interview consequence
  → rights implication after traveler choice

seek reimbursement / alternate travel
  → movement consequence
  → independently purchased route risk
  → evidence and deadline requirements
```

Only legally and operationally supported branches appear. Places shows spatial
and current-world consequences; it does not adjudicate the legal claim.

#### F5-P3 — Negative knowledge and fallback

The Place projection retains:

- which airport, terminal, route, provider state, or transfer failed;
- what was known at the time;
- whether the failure was provider, weather, access, timing, or unresolved;
- which alternative preserved the return intent; and
- what later correction supersedes the failure state.

It must not create a durable “avoid Chicago” or “prefers direct flights” claim
from one disruption.

#### F5-P4 — Horizon path

```text
Rome airport
  → provider cancellation state
  → Chicago reroute and overnight spatial consequences
  → New York arrival and affected commitment
  → later conditional recovery or rights path
```

This path is primarily operational and causal. The compact Home instrument
owns urgency; Places owns the spatial truth and branch depth.

### 37. Causal and authority receipt

```text
opening_id
  stranded or disrupted return detected

judgment_id
  current best assessment, including abstention and uncertainty

domain_decision_ref
  traveler acceptance, rejection, or alternate choice

action_receipt_ref
  provider command, booking attempt, message send, or claim submission

occurrence_ref
  canonical provider readback, actual travel, lodging, or message delivery

outcome_ref
  arrival, reimbursement, rescheduled commitment, correction, or unresolved

learning_application_ref
  later conditional use or deliberate non-use of the disruption evidence
```

No reference implies the next. A callback is not completion; acceptance is not
travel; travel is not preference; and a prepared claim is not eligibility.

## 41. Shared engine seams revealed by the fixtures

### 41.1 Source–Claim–projection separation is non-negotiable

Every fixture needs:

- original Source custody;
- author or provider;
- observation versus inference;
- Claim-level evidence and authority;
- viewer- and Moment-relative projection;
- correction and withdrawal propagation; and
- no generated projection becoming independent evidence.

This is existing doctrine, not a new universal content object.

### 41.2 Place Horizon is the most reusable new read seam

All five fixtures need a sparse typed path, but none needs a raw knowledge-graph
surface.

The read projection needs:

- focal Place;
- connected entity;
- typed, directed relation;
- Claim and Source references;
- time, condition, and viewer scope;
- certainty and disagreement;
- current consequence;
- important difference or analogy limit;
- correction and expiry; and
- eligibility for Home and Places.

A `HorizonPath` can be composed dynamically from two to four edges. It should
not become a new universal writer or durable object until the fixtures expose a
real ownership need.

### 41.3 Contribution compilation precedes medium

The engine must decide:

```text
what changed for the person
  → whether the change beats infrastructure and silence
  → whether authority and receptivity allow it
  → which root posture owns the encounter
  → smallest adequate lead medium
  → compact or standard projection
```

It must not begin from an implemented card or renderer and search for content
to fill it.

### 41.4 Transfer Candidate is an evaluation seam, not a skill profile

F1, F2, and F3 need a bounded transfer assessment:

- prior cue and Source;
- supported distinction;
- what Vesper exposed;
- current cue;
- shared structure and important difference;
- current relevance;
- candidate, activated, demonstrated, contradicted, or retired state; and
- correction lineage.

Only later observable use supports demonstrated transfer. Exposure, dwell,
save, or acceptance does not.

### 41.5 Situated affordance and Temporal Posture should remain derived

F1, F2, and F5 require current feasibility, alternatives, thresholds,
reversibility, and fallback. These can be assessments over Place, Moment,
Plan, Occasion, Commitment, and provider state.

They do not yet justify durable `Affordance`, `Option`, or `LifeInterval`
entities.

The shared posture vocabulary is:

```text
ACT_NOW · HOLD · WAIT_FOR_SIGNAL · MONITOR · RELEASE
```

Every posture names source freshness, deadline, information event, authority,
and expiry.

### 41.6 Human routing must be existing-trace-first

F4 demonstrates a source resolver, not a social feed:

```text
authoritative world evidence
  → existing authorized human trace
  → bounded Vesper synthesis
  → optional known-person contact
  → positioned public or institutional expert
  → silence
```

May-use, may-name, and may-contact remain separate. The resolver should produce
an expiring candidate, not a durable expertise ranking.

### 41.7 Consequential lineage spans roots without centralizing writers

F5 requires the existing typed lineage across judgment, domain decision,
action receipt, occurrence, Outcome, and later learning. Home and Places read
the lineage through root-native projections; neither becomes a general mutation
owner.

### 41.8 Attention arbitration is a system result

Every candidate needs an explicit comparison against silence. F5 suppresses
editorial opportunity during recovery. F2 reduces repeated checking to one
threshold. F3 and F4 disappear if they fail to add substance. Attention
protection is not a visual mood or a quiet card family.

## 42. Architecture decisions supported by the portfolio

The fixtures support these working decisions:

1. **Promote a typed Place Horizon read projection for fixture and API design.**
   Do not promote a universal graph writer or graph UI.
2. **Use one Home contribution compiler across direct state, human
   contribution, operational instrument, and Vesper contribution.** Keep the
   classes semantic rather than component types.
3. **Represent transfer, temporal posture, interval composition, and human
   routing as derived briefs or assessments first.** Persist only their
   evidence, authoritative decisions, actions, Outcomes, and corrections.
4. **Preserve Places' four faces as projection roles, not permanent page
   sections.** The current question determines which faces appear and how
   deeply.
5. **Keep Home and Places projections distinct over stable underlying
   identity.** Home compiles present value; Places owns spatial, current,
   plural, and horizon depth.
6. **Use the existing consequence contract for every write or external
   effect.** No fixture authorizes silent retention, contact, booking,
   publication, or rights submission.
7. **Treat negative evidence as condition-bound Claim and Outcome material.**
   Do not infer durable aversion from non-occurrence, failure, or dismissal.

These are research conclusions. Canonical promotion still requires review in
the accepted product, system, and decision authorities.

## 43. Decisions not yet supported

The fixtures do not yet justify:

- one permanent Home section for each value system;
- a new `Capability`, `Option`, `Interval`, `Opening`, or `HumanExpertise`
  source-of-truth table;
- universal Place scores, familiarity scores, or skill profiles;
- server-authored component trees;
- one product grammar assigned to each tab;
- a public social or expert marketplace;
- automatic public Place contribution;
- a generalized consumer-rights product;
- cross-person comparison without exact use grants; or
- repository-wide noun migration before the read contracts are validated.

## 44. Evaluation matrix

Each fixture should be rendered semantically in at least four conditions:

1. full valid evidence;
2. one critical Source missing or stale;
3. one social, authority, or action grant absent; and
4. one current condition changed enough to invalidate the foreground.

Score and qualitatively inspect:

| Dimension | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Five-second comprehension | Can the person state the new value without opening depth? |
| Representational gain | Did Vesper add a mechanism, relation, distinction, reconstruction, or consequence? |
| Root fit | Does Home compose while Places situates and deepens? |
| No-homework | Is initial value complete without response, saving, or reflection? |
| Truth calibration | Can observation, inference, uncertainty, and authority be distinguished? |
| Attention dividend | Does the treatment reduce checking, search, assembly, or interpretation cost? |
| Capability | Does a later perception, judgment, or action improve? |
| Optionality | Are good futures preserved without missed material thresholds? |
| Social fidelity | Are author, audience, burden, and withdrawal preserved? |
| Consequence integrity | Do preparation, decision, action, occurrence, and Outcome remain distinct? |
| Degradation | Does the surface become smaller or silent when evidence or permission weakens? |
| Correction | Do dependent projections update or withdraw? |

Clicks, time in app, number of cards, number of contributions, and quantity of
retained evidence are not success metrics.

## 45. Next execution step

The next productive artifact is a **semantic prototype and test manifest** for
these five fixtures:

1. encode the common briefs as fixture data, not production schemas;
2. produce compact Home and standard Places text/structure outputs for the four
   evidence conditions;
3. run the comparison and evaluation matrix;
4. record which fields are required across all fixtures versus only one;
5. promote only the stable read contracts; and
6. hand accepted semantic compositions to a dedicated visual-design tool.

Do not begin with component implementation or a full engine migration. The
fixtures should first prove that the same small set of seams can express all
five worlds without losing root clarity, authority, or attention restraint.

## Closing principle

The five worlds should make one product more legible, not make the product feel
five times larger:

> Home composes the best current return from a lived world. Places makes that
> world spatially, operationally, historically, socially, and personally
> intelligible. Vesper earns breadth by transforming evidence through the same
> disciplined seams—not by accumulating features or demanding more from the
> person.
