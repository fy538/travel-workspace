# Life Editorial Contribution Briefs and Treatment Evaluation

> Status: content-definition evidence, not production copy
>
> This document evaluates what Life can contribute before a visual tool chooses
> typography, card geometry, illustration, or motion. Controlled founder-story
> facts still require owner confirmation before dogfood use. External claims
> require the cited sources and the uncertainty shown here.

## 1. Decision

Life should not be a feed of interpretations, a media library, or a poetic
autobiography. Its stable value is **governed continuity**: truthful clusters
and overlapping timelines make a life re-findable; earned threads reveal
relations across clusters; bounded editorial Returns contribute one genuinely
new representation, explanation, distinction, or transfer.

The content hierarchy is therefore:

```text
record and authored attention
  -> reconstruction where fragments need structure
  -> one bounded editorial contribution where evidence earns it
  -> one optional present consequence where current truth supports it
  -> otherwise silence
```

The main Life structure does not need a clever headline. A Journey can be
valuable because its actual and intended paths are accurately reconstructed.
Generated copy enters only where Vesper has added something the person did not
already supply.

## 2. Shared admission contract

Each candidate below must name:

- **known-to-person material** separately from prior Vesper exposure;
- the **new operation** Vesper performs;
- claim ownership and the **authority boundary**;
- the proof that must remain inspectable;
- uncertainty and alternative explanations;
- why the contribution belongs in this cluster now;
- the optional consequence, if any; and
- the condition under which Vesper says nothing.

A candidate fails if any one of novelty, evidence, yield, relevance, or
compression is effectively zero. “Accurate” is necessary but not sufficient.

## 3. C01 — Europe reconstruction

### Contribution brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Life posture | Primary reconstruction inside the Europe Journey cluster; not a generated cross-time thread. |
| Known to person | Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi, Rome, return to New York; supplied tickets, stays, photographs, notes, and explicit corrections. The person already knows the trip happened and remembers its broad order. |
| New operation | **Restructure:** reconcile provider records, occurrence evidence, planned-only movements, corrections, and disruption into one actual-versus-intended sequence. |
| Claim authority | Provider records own bookings; occurrence evidence supports what happened; the owner owns corrections and meaning. Vesper owns only the reconciliation. |
| Required proof | Every segment exposes whether it is booked, independently supported, corrected, changed, cancelled, or unknown. Ticket alone never becomes occurrence. |
| Proposed expression | Two aligned lanes—`intended` and `supported actual`—with Sources attached at divergence points. No paragraph is required to explain what the marks already show. |
| Bounded headline | **Your return became a second journey.** Use only if cancellation and rerouting records establish a materially different return sequence; otherwise use the descriptive cluster title. |
| New substance | Not “four cities” or a route recap. The contribution is the visible distinction between plan, occurrence, correction, and recovery—including where the trip did not end at the scheduled flight. |
| Uncertainty | Missing boarding or arrival evidence remains unknown. A photograph proves capture, not the complete surrounding episode. The fixture's movements are controlled examples until owner-confirmed. |
| Why now | Re-entry is when receipts and fragments are most available and the truthful record is easiest to repair. It does not justify dominating Home. |
| Optional consequence | `Review two uncertain segments` is an inspect-and-repair utility inside Life, never a reflective prompt or required completion task. |
| Silence condition | If Vesper can only display a city count, artifact count, or itinerary recap, show the record without an editorial Return. |

### Four-treatment test

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat report | “4 places · 12 artifacts · 18 days.” | Keep only as metadata. It contributes no understanding. |
| Poetic identity | “You experience travel through transitions.” | Reject. It converts sparse behavior into a creepy trait claim. |
| Evidence-led | Intended and supported-actual lanes split at cancellation, unused movement, and rerouting, with source states visible. | **Admit as reconstruction** once records support the deltas. The structure—not decorative prose—is the value. |
| Prepared consequence | Inspect uncertain occurrence or export the verified sequence. | Admit contextually; never make repair a debt. |

## 4. C02 — Rome, Aeneas, and Augustan political lineage

### Contribution brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Life posture | One dominant editorial Return nested in the Europe cluster beside the Colosseum note and film Source. It is not a separate stable Life door. |
| Known to person | The person already connected the Trojan War, Aeneas, Rome, the Colosseum encounter, and the earlier film. Repeating that connection is prohibited. |
| New operation | **Explain + relational map:** show how Augustan culture could use Aeneas both as an ancestor of Rome and, through Iulus/Ascanius, as an ancestor of the Julian line. |
| Claim authority | Virgil supplies the literary linkage; scholarship interprets its Augustan use; monuments provide public material context. Vesper owns only the bounded synthesis. |
| Preferred headline | **Aeneas gave Augustus a family tree inside Rome's origin story.** |
| Mechanism chain | `Aeneas -> Ascanius/Iulus -> Julian line -> Caesar and Augustus -> epic and public Augustan imagery`. |
| Visible proof | In *Aeneid* 1, Caesar's Julian name descends from Iulus; in Book 6, Anchises places Augustus among the “seed of Iulus.” Cambridge's study of Ascanius describes him as destined to found Alba Longa and the Julian family. The Ara Pacis places Augustus and his family in a public dynastic/religious program; a west panel has traditionally been read as Aeneas sacrificing. |
| Uncertainty | Do not imply Augustus invented the Aeneas tradition or that Rome simply “adopted the survivor's side.” The exact west-panel identification is disputed; say `traditionally identified as Aeneas` or omit the panel from the decisive proof. Virgil's political relation to Augustus is richer than simple state propaganda. |
| Why now | The contribution answers an attention already supplied and changes what the person can notice in Augustan literature or monuments without assigning homework. |
| Optional consequence | `See the lineage in three pieces of evidence` may open annotated excerpts; no quiz, journal prompt, or mandatory museum plan. |
| Silence condition | Suppress if the result only says Aeneas escaped Troy and helped found Rome, or if citations cannot be projected with the claim. |

### Source adjudication

- Virgil's *Aeneid* Book 1 links Iulus to the future Julian line, while Book 6
  places Augustus among the seed/progeny of Iulus. These are literary primary
  sources, not proof that every Roman accepted one political reading:
  [Book 1](https://classics.mit.edu/Virgil/aeneid.1.i.html) and
  [Book 6](https://classics.mit.edu/Virgil/aeneid.6.vi.html).
- Anne Rogerson's Cambridge study describes Ascanius as destined to found Alba
  Longa and the Julian family to which Caesar and Augustus belonged:
  [*Virgil's Ascanius*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/virgils-ascanius/A314256384723271C84FD9AA167C6195).
- The Uffizi account dates and identifies the Ara Pacis as an Augustan
  commission and describes its procession of Augustus and imperial family:
  [Ara Pacis Augustae](https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/ara-pacis-augustae).
- The panel beside the west entrance has a long scholarly tradition of being
  read as Aeneas sacrificing, but the object should expose that identification
  as interpretation, not settled fact:
  [Journal of Roman Studies extract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-roman-studies/article/abs/ara-pacis-augustae/94A9E9C923961A019628840142324DF1).

### Four-treatment test

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat report | “You watched *The Odyssey*, then encountered Aeneas in Rome.” | Reject as editorial content; the person supplied it. |
| Poetic identity | “Rome adopted the survivor's side of Troy.” | Reject. Broad, imprecise, and no mechanism. |
| Evidence-led | “Aeneas gave Augustus a family tree inside Rome's origin story,” followed by the Iulus lineage chain and source annotations. | **Admit.** It adds a bounded political-literary mechanism. |
| Prepared consequence | An annotated lineage or a short `notice this next time` monument comparison. | Optional. The explanation is complete without action. |

## 5. C03 — Rome and Paris social mosaic

### Contribution brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Life posture | Contextual, addressed social composition inside the Europe period; never a public social feed or stable door by coincidence alone. |
| Known to people | Each person knows their own evening. The owner proposed the Paris juxtaposition. Neither person has automatically authorized full-trip access, live location, or private interpretation. |
| New operation | **Differentiate through an authorized social mosaic:** compare one axis neither person could observe alone, while preserving two authors and two evidence lanes. |
| Claim authority | Each person owns their contribution; current public sources may own weather, transit, river access, hours, or city conditions. Vesper owns the bounded comparison. |
| Candidate axis | `How each city made the hot evening usable`—for example shade, river edge, closing time, transit, or abandoned plans. Choose exactly one axis after inspecting the grant. |
| Proposed expression | Two attributed lanes on the same scale plus one sourced contextual strip. No merged “you both felt” voice. |
| Current status | **Withhold.** The controlled fixture contains a grant-shaped placeholder, not Maya's actual authorized evidence or a sufficiently bounded date. |
| Uncertainty | Similar timestamps or weather do not establish comparable experience. Absence of a photo or plan is not evidence of absence. |
| Why now | Only if the addressed comparison is currently authorized and adds perception; not because both people happened to be in Europe. |
| Optional consequence | `Send this comparison` is available only if both the content grant and destination audience allow it. No reply is expected. |
| Silence condition | No valid grant, no comparable axis, no new public context, or a result reducible to “you were both in Europe” means no composition. |

### Four-treatment test

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat report | “Feihu was in Rome while Maya was in Paris.” | Reject; coincidence is not social value. |
| Poetic identity | “Two cities, one summer rhythm.” | Reject; it invents shared meaning. |
| Evidence-led | Two attributed evening lanes compare one authorized practical axis with dated public context. | **Conditionally admissible**, but correctly silent in the current fixture. |
| Prepared consequence | Share or use the learned axis for a future evening. | Withhold until grants and relevance exist. |

## 6. C04 — Red Hook across episodes

### Contribution brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Life posture | Earned Place thread across supported visits and an Occasion. This is the strongest current test of a cross-time thread and bounded local map. |
| Known to person | Saved routes, supported visits, a dinner Occasion, explicit evaluations, and prior access choices. The person knows the individual episodes. |
| New operation | **Differentiate + transfer:** distinguish saved, actually used, and currently available access conditions, then prepare one current route comparison for an open weekend. |
| Claim authority | Historical records own past states; explicit evaluation owns `would repeat`/`not for me`; live providers own present service. Vesper must not translate recurrence into affinity or identity. |
| Preferred headline | **Red Hook has not been one trip from your life.** Use only when at least two supported episodes show materially different access patterns. A more literal `Saved, used, and available now` is safer in sparse evidence. |
| Visible proof | A compact episode sequence; used-mode evidence; current official service status; a local neighborhood map only if it reveals the difference in access. |
| Current world truth | NYC Ferry's South Brooklyn route currently lists Red Hook/Atlantic Basin and directs people to real-time schedules; service changed during 2026. MTA's B61 serves Red Hook. These prove available infrastructure, not which route the user should take at a future moment. |
| Uncertainty | Traffic, service alerts, starting point, walking tolerance, weather, and opening hours are volatile. A save is not a visit; a visit is not a preference. |
| Why now | When an open weekend makes the learned access pattern consequential. Otherwise the thread remains a re-findable history in Life. |
| Optional consequence | Hand off to Places to compare **live** reachability from the user's actual origin. Life does not cache a stale recommendation as truth. |
| Silence condition | Fewer than two supported episodes, no meaningful access difference, no live refresh, or only a visit count means no editorial headline and no map. |

### Current-source boundary

- NYC Ferry currently lists Red Hook/Atlantic Basin on its South Brooklyn route
  and explicitly routes people to real-time schedules:
  [South Brooklyn route](https://static.ferry.nyc/routes-and-schedules/south-brooklyn/).
- NYC Ferry also documents 2026 schedule revisions, which is why a durable Life
  object should retain the learned route relation but delegate present service
  truth to Places:
  [May 2026 schedule update](https://www.ferry.nyc/blog/updated-south-brooklyn-schedules-effective-may-4-2026/).
- MTA Bus Time identifies the B61 as operating via Red Hook; exact arrivals and
  detours remain live truth:
  [B61 live route](https://bustime.mta.info/?search=B61).

### Four-treatment test

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat report | “You visited Red Hook three times.” | Reject as insight; a count may remain factual metadata. |
| Poetic identity | “Red Hook keeps calling you back.” | Reject; recurrence is not desire or identity. |
| Evidence-led | Past episodes distinguish saved, used, and changed access modes without claiming affinity. | **Admit** after recurrence and mode evidence pass. |
| Prepared consequence | Places refreshes ferry, bus, weather, and origin-dependent reachability for this weekend. | **Admit contextually**; this is where the thread becomes useful now. |

## 7. C05 — pasta creaminess as a bounded mechanism

### Contribution brief

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Life posture | A nested editorial contribution in the Europe cluster. It does **not** yet earn a cross-time thread; one observation is not continuity. A later cooking attempt or second supported observation could promote it. |
| Known to person | Pasta in Italy tasted different and seemed creamier; the person already suspected pasta water, emulsion, finishing technique, and serving conditions. |
| New operation | **Explain + transfer:** narrow the question to one testable sauce system and show how starch concentration and temperature affect cheese aggregation. |
| Claim authority | The 2025 experiment supports cacio e pepe and discusses starch-stabilized sauce systems. It does not explain every pasta dish, every restaurant, or an Italy–US difference. |
| Preferred headline | **For cacio e pepe, “creamy” is partly a stability problem.** |
| Mechanism chain | `starch-enriched water + controlled temperature -> less protein aggregation -> smoother sauce`; insufficient starch or excessive heat raises clumping risk. |
| Visible proof | A small phase-boundary or controlled comparison derived from the paper, with the dish and conditions named. Do not present a decorative food photograph as proof. |
| Practical transfer | Offer a bounded at-home comparison: same pecorino/water ratio, change one variable such as starch level or mixing temperature. The result is a capability, not an identity claim. |
| Uncertainty | The original observation may involve different sauces, ingredients, service timing, or technique. Sensory comparison across countries remains unproven. Cornstarch in the paper's optimized recipe is not evidence that restaurants used it. |
| Why now | It answers a supplied curiosity and can change the person's next cooking attempt. It need not be resurfaced merely because a trip ended. |
| Silence condition | Suppress any claim that “Italian pasta is creamier because of starch,” any generic recipe, or any output that merely repeats the owner's own hypothesis. |

### Source adjudication

The 2025 *Physics of Fluids* paper experimentally varied cheese, water,
starch, and temperature in cacio e pepe. It identified starch concentration as
a key stability factor; reported system-wide clumping below a bounded starch
regime in its experiments; and explains that higher temperature can promote
protein aggregation. It also notes a role for starch-enriched water in
stabilizing aglio e olio. This supports the narrow mechanism above, not the
broader country-level sensory explanation:
[Bartolucci et al., “Phase behavior of Cacio e Pepe sauce”](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/download/19670/19681/2025_PhysicsFluids_Bartolucci.pdf).

### Four-treatment test

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat report | “You noticed creamier pasta in Italy.” | Reject as editorial content; retain only as authored attention. |
| Poetic identity | “You are becoming a more discerning eater.” | Reject as invasive and unsupported. |
| Evidence-led | “For cacio e pepe, ‘creamy’ is partly a stability problem,” with starch–temperature evidence and limits. | **Admit as a nested explanation.** |
| Prepared consequence | A one-variable home comparison or saved cooking note. | Admit only when requested or timely; do not turn it into homework. |

This distribution is deliberate. A strong Life page contains truthful records,
one or two substantive Returns, and silence where evidence or authority is
insufficient. It does not require five generated cards because five candidates
were considered.

## 9. Copy rules carried into composition

1. Cluster names orient; they do not pretend to be insights.
2. A visual reconstruction can be the complete value. Do not narrate its
   obvious marks.
3. A Return headline must contain a new bounded proposition, not atmosphere.
4. Put claim limits near the claim, not behind an information icon.
5. Preserve `unknown`, `planned only`, `supported occurrence`, `human-authored`,
   `external source`, and `live truth` as distinct states.
6. Social content remains plural and attributed. Vesper never writes one group
   feeling.
7. Consequences are optional. Life never asks for reflection to unlock value.
8. One Journey, one dominant editorial operation. Additional content must do a
   materially different job.
9. Global maps are excluded. Map earns space only inside a bounded city,
   neighborhood, route, or episode where spatial form explains something.
10. If the best available copy is restatement, use the record or remain silent.

## 10. Fixture implications

Fixture v0.2 should encode, without choosing visual geometry:

- period clusters as the stable index;
- `intended`, `supported actual`, `people`, `attention`, `Sources`, and `truth`
  as optional lanes inside an opened cluster—not permanent global tabs;
- explicit promotion gates from nested observation to cross-time thread;
- C03's withheld state as a first-class success case;
- C04's local spatial context and live Places handoff;
- editorial exposure receipts so C02 is not regenerated under a new headline;
- source and uncertainty bindings per claim; and
- textual projections proving that the model works without card styling.

## 11. Research limits

This pass verified the narrow historical, culinary, and current Red Hook
claims needed to define the editorial boundary. It did not reconstruct the
founder's real ticket corpus, inspect another person's private Paris material,
or select a final visual treatment. Those are respectively an evidence-ingest
task, a grant-dependent social task, and dedicated visual-design work.
