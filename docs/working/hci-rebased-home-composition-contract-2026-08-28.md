# HCI-Rebased Home Composition Contract

## Decision

Keep the accepted value grammar unchanged:

> **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.**

Do not use those four moves as page sections, tab assignments, lifecycle steps,
or a quota. They describe why a contribution creates value. Home requires an
additional operative contract to decide what earns a place now, how several
contributions coexist, what they ask of the person, and where any consequence
lands.

The working definition is:

> **Home is Vesper's composed contribution to the present moment: one
> foregrounded consequence, understanding, or possibility when one earns
> dominance; a bounded portfolio of complete, low-demand value; and only the
> continuity that makes a named difference now or later.**

Internally, Home is the primary **return on entrusted attention**. A person has
already lived, noticed, imported, asked, saved, invited, corrected, and
contributed. Home should demonstrate what Vesper made newly understandable,
possible, workable, or durable from that material. It should rarely make more
input the price of receiving value.

This is a working product-and-surface contract. It does not authorize a route
migration, server schema, generated component tree, production ranking model,
or release claim. The four-root shell remains under test until promoted through
the accepted product and surface authorities.

## 1. The five-layer model

The product remains coherent when five different questions stay separate:

| Layer | Question | Current answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product thesis** | What change does Vesper seek? | Turn lived attention into greater understanding, capability, participation, and continuity in the world. |
| **Value grammar** | What kind of value was created? | Make sense; Open possibility; Help it work; Carry forward. |
| **Operative compiler** | How did something earn the right to appear or act? | Authorized evidence or canonical state becomes a bounded contribution, affordance, or consequence; admission, receptivity, authority, projection, and lifecycle remain explicit. |
| **Root posture** | Where and in what mode does the person meet it? | Home composes; Chat forms and works through; Places situates; Life governs continuity. |
| **Expression** | What form makes the value easiest to perceive or use? | Evidence, sequence, comparison, spatial field, concise prose, or instrument, with bounded modifiers and density. |

The compact rule is:

```text
the moves explain why
the compiler explains how
the roots explain where and in what posture
the medium explains how it becomes perceptible
```

Broad capability is therefore not the same as an unfocused product. A ticket,
photograph, weather change, friend contribution, booking, Place, article, or
conversation can all participate when they enter and leave through this same
transformation. They become unrelated features when the transformation is
absent or invisible.

## 2. Root postures after the HCI rebase

| Root | Human posture | Primary responsibility | Must not become |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Receive and enter | Compose the strongest current return on attention across life, people, Places, and active consequences | Generic feed, prompt surface, dashboard, capability gallery, or trip report |
| **Chat** | Direct and develop | Ask, show, challenge, author, negotiate, interpret, or create with Vesper | The only route to product value or the owner of all durable truth |
| **Places** | Orient and inhabit | Express spatial truth, current affordance, Place identity, relationship, and horizons | Ratings inventory, generic map, or recommendation list |
| **Life** | Hold and govern | Preserve Mine/Together continuity, authorship, custody, correction, release, and return | Archive dump, inferred identity profile, brag feed, or universal settings panel |

Plans and Occasions remain durable objects reachable from several roots. They do
not need a fifth root. A route transition must preserve the source object,
evidence, people, current job, audience, and unresolved consequence so the
person never reconstructs context by hand.

## 3. What Home is optimizing

Home optimizes five qualities together:

1. **Current consequence:** protect physical reality, affected people,
   commitments, and expiring state before editorial opportunity.
2. **Attention dividend:** produce meaningful value with little input,
   navigation, or response debt.
3. **Representational gain:** add a supported relation, mechanism,
   reconstruction, distinction, or contrast rather than repeat the evidence.
4. **Capability or possibility:** make something newly noticeable, feasible,
   judgeable, participable, or achievable in the lived world.
5. **Governed continuity:** preserve only what has an owner, authority,
   correction path, and named later use.

Home does not optimize session length, number of cards, number of product moves,
amount of generated content, quantity of remembered evidence, or conversion of
every opening into a Plan.

## 4. Stable composition regions

The seven regions in prior Home studies remain a useful audit vocabulary, but
they should not harden into seven permanent sections. People, continuity, and
editorial voice are conditional roles, not page quotas.

The stable composition contract has four regions.

### 4.1 Situational frame

A compact factual orientation establishes Place, time, current conditions,
active Occasion, or material temporal window when those facts affect the page.

The frame:

- prefers observable world or canonical state over authored atmosphere;
- never diagnoses mood, fatigue, attachment, or identity;
- may disappear when it adds nothing;
- does not count as the dominant contribution; and
- must not narrate that Vesper “knows,” “remembered,” or “understands” the
  person.

Good:

```text
NEW YORK · THURSDAY
Saturday 2–8 PM is open
```

Bad:

```text
Home again. You are still becoming yours.
Vesper knows the trip changed how you move.
```

### 4.2 Foreground

Zero or one contribution may dominate. A non-quiet composition should normally
have one. It can be:

- a material state or consequence;
- a working operational instrument;
- an authorized human contribution whose presence changes the situation; or
- a Vesper contribution with unusually strong current semantic or capability
  value.

The first viewport reveals the answer, current state, or useful instrument.
Opening provides depth, alternatives, source inspection, simulation, or action;
it must not reveal the value that the compact projection withheld.

### 4.3 Composed field

The field is a bounded portfolio of materially different contributions. It can
contain current state, complete-on-view explanation, a prepared possibility, an
attributed perspective, a reconstruction, an instrument, or earned continuity.

The field is diversified by the different powers it gives a person—notice, go,
understand, compare, make, relate, coordinate, continue—not by demonstrating
every renderer, data source, product move, or time horizon.

No fixed number is canonical. The smaller composition wins when it preserves
the strongest value and range. A visually quiet page may still be substantively
rich.

### 4.4 Continuity and doors

The final region keeps only compact owner state and context-preserving paths:

- current Plans, Occasions, invitations, or commitments that still matter;
- an earned return whose later difference is named;
- All Plans and Occasions while Plans is no longer a root;
- Chat, Places, Life, or canonical-object destinations; and
- correction, source, audience, and release paths where required.

This region is not an archive preview. It may be almost empty. Stable doors are
learned navigation; the composer does not rearrange them to increase
engagement.

## 5. Four admissible contribution classes

Home composes four semantic classes. These are not four visual card types.

| Class | Admission basis | Required value | Typical owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Canonical state** | Current owned truth | Material state is legible at the right scope and time | Plan, Occasion, Commitment, Invitation, provider-backed record |
| **Operational instrument** | Current state plus authorized capability | It absorbs real practical or coordinative burden and honestly exposes consequence | Plan, Occasion, booking/route adapter, temporary instrument until committed |
| **Human contribution** | Exact author, custody, audience, and current relevance | The person's presence changes understanding, participation, coordination, or possibility | Person-authored Source, relationship handoff, Occasion contribution, Place perspective |
| **Vesper contribution** | Authorized evidence plus admitted semantic delta | A new supported relation, mechanism, reconstruction, distinction, or prepared consequence becomes perceptible | Source-bound artifact, Place/Encounter projection, Life reconstruction, temporary editorial contribution |

A fifth valid candidate is **silence**. Silence is selected when no candidate
beats its attention, privacy, social, epistemic, or commercial cost.

An external article, map, podcast, video, or link remains a Source until Vesper
adds a bounded contribution. A generated artifact remains inadmissible merely
because generation completed.

## 6. The Home unit brief

Every eligible unit resolves the following working receipt before copy,
geometry, or renderer selection:

```text
HomeContributionCandidate
  candidate_id
  semantic_class
  dominant_job
  primary_product_move

  canonical_owner + revision
  viewer + audience + custody
  evidence_refs + truth_modes + freshness
  premises_already_supplied
  prior_exposure_or_semantic_receipts

  exact_value_delta
  editorial_operation_or_operational_capability
  known_to_person_posture + basis
  capability_or_perception_opened
  why_now + current_job

  attention_cost
  response_or_decision_required
  receptivity_basis + treatment_ceiling
  action_authority + consequence_preview

  smallest_adequate_lead_medium
  optional_modifier
  accessible_complete_alternative
  compact_value_on_view

  destination_owner + preserved_context
  correction + dismissal + release
  expiry + invalidation + fallback
```

The exact production storage topology is deliberately unresolved. This receipt
is a semantic test contract, not authorization for a universal Home table.

### 6.1 Contribution-specific extensions

A Vesper editorial unit also requires the Round 2 `ContributionBrief` fields:

- the precise new claim, relation, mechanism, reconstruction, or distinction;
- uncertainty, counterevidence, and analogy limit;
- `likely_known`, `uncertain`, `likely_new`, `explicitly_known`, or `corrected`;
- smallest adequate medium; and
- the optional consequence and invalidation rule.

A Place, route, Move, or current-world possibility with material burden also
requires a candidate-relative `SituatedAffordanceAssessment`:

- subject principals and temporary social constitution;
- experiential intent and hard bounds;
- per-field world facts and freshness;
- burden axes that matter now, such as time, heat, grade, cost, coordination,
  or attention;
- opportunity axes that matter now, such as utility, restoration, learning, or
  connection;
- unknowns and sensitivity; and
- an intent-preserving fallback.

Do not collapse those axes into one universal fit, welfare, attachment, or
meaning score.

## 7. Admission compiler

### 7.1 Pipeline

```text
authorized evidence or canonical state
  -> resolve owner, viewer, audience, truth, freshness, and current job
  -> produce direct-state brief, ContributionBrief, or SituatedAffordanceAssessment
  -> compare against infrastructure baseline and silence
  -> apply hard semantic, privacy, social, receptivity, and authority gates
  -> admit an eligible candidate
  -> choose foreground and compose marginally distinct field candidates
  -> select the quietest sufficient native projection
  -> render complete value, trust footprint, owner, and optional consequence
  -> observe occurrence, correction, withdrawal, expiry, or silence
  -> update continuity only when a valid later difference exists
```

Not every path uses every contract. A verified train cancellation can become an
operational instrument without an editorial contribution. A human reply may be
shown directly when the author and audience are already clear. A factual state
may complete on view and produce no continuation.

### 7.2 Hard gates

A candidate is rejected before ranking when any of the following is true:

- owner, audience, custody, or truth mode is unresolved;
- freshness is insufficient for the displayed claim or action;
- the contribution repeats what the person supplied or Vesper already exposed;
- the central claim moves from evidence to identity, mood, preference, or
  meaning without first-person authorship;
- another person's presence can be removed without changing the value;
- the unit asks the person to perform synthesis Vesper could already complete;
- the proposed action exceeds authority or hides who is affected;
- the medium implies precision, equivalence, causality, or consensus the
  evidence does not support;
- no complete accessible alternative exists;
- a materially simpler infrastructure surface already supplies the whole
  value; or
- silence is better than the likely value after attention, privacy, social,
  epistemic, and commercial cost.

### 7.3 Precedence before editorial opportunity

Use a precedence model, not one universal ranking score:

```text
safety or stranded-state recovery
  > expiring material provider state
  > affected-person decision or protected commitment
  > current physical guidance
  > grounded present possibility
  > strong semantic or social contribution
  > earned continuity
  > factual register
```

Confidence and authority can reverse apparent priority. A verified current
route can outrank a speculative emergency, and a permissioned human reply can
outrank generated editorial content.

### 7.4 Portfolio selection

Eligibility does not imply page admission. After selecting the foreground,
each additional candidate must create material marginal value relative to the
current composition.

Penalize repetition across:

- subject or Place;
- evidence source;
- editorial operation;
- product move;
- human power opened;
- temporal horizon;
- social scope;
- lead medium;
- durable owner; and
- response demand.

Do not add a weak unit merely to include social material, a past trip, a future
idea, a product move, or an implemented component. Richness comes from a
portfolio of different consequences, not a portfolio of feature families.

## 8. Four page budgets

### 8.1 Dominance budget

- At most one unit dominates.
- In a material live situation, the dominant unit owns the first viewport.
- On an ordinary rich-context day, one prepared possibility or unusually strong
  contribution may dominate without manufacturing urgency.
- In a genuinely cold or low-confidence state, no unit needs to dominate.

### 8.2 Demand budget

- At most one unresolved primary decision should define the page.
- Every other selected unit should normally complete on view or accept one
  optional continuation.
- Home never leads with a reflection prompt, preference elicitation, profile
  setup, poll, or empty composer.
- Opening Home is evidence of willingness to glance or browse; it is not consent
  to psychological interpretation, social publication, or unlimited response
  debt.
- Constitutive effort—choosing, authorizing, correcting, contributing—remains
  with the person. Instrumental effort—recapping, sorting, cross-checking,
  reformulating, re-entering known constraints—belongs to Vesper.

### 8.3 Possibility budget

- Several complete, low-pressure possibilities may coexist when they open
  materially different human powers.
- Ignore and scroll-past are complete, non-punitive endings.
- Possibility is bounded; Home must not become an infinite recommendation
  supply.
- Quiet is an aesthetic and coercion posture, not a synonym for empty.

### 8.4 Depth budget

- The compact projection contains the first real claim or useful state.
- Home does not own substantial reading, route exploration, source inspection,
  Plan editing, Occasion governance, or memory custody.
- Depth routes to the durable owner with object, evidence, people, audience,
  current job, and scroll origin intact.

## 9. Product-move admission on Home

The moves remain an audit after selection.

| Move | A Home unit qualifies only when… | Does not qualify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Make sense** | It produces representational gain: a supported mechanism, relation, reconstruction, distinction, or contrast becomes legible. | Retrieval, paraphrase, decorative personalization, generic “connection,” or identity interpretation. |
| **Open possibility** | A grounded way in is genuinely available, optional, and situated under current conditions. | Venue list, generic recommendation, curiosity-gap headline, proximity alone, or forced Plan creation. |
| **Help it work** | Real practical, physical, or coordinative burden is absorbed and any consequence lands with an owner. | Decorative controls, stale state, hidden tradeoffs, or an action beyond mandate. |
| **Carry forward** | A named later perception, capability, relationship, commitment, or return is different because of what persists. | Storage, recap, recency, activity history, or profile accumulation alone. |

One unit can perform several moves when the same evidence and form genuinely do
the work. The page has no coverage target.

## 10. Social composition

Social material normally modifies the object it changes. It does not earn a
standing “With people” section.

A social projection requires:

- exact human author or clearly labeled Vesper synthesis;
- current viewer and audience;
- source custody and revocation;
- the minimum common ground necessary for the shared consequence;
- no inferred consensus or synthetic group sentiment;
- one occurrence with plural Outcomes when experience is shared; and
- a substantive transformation in understanding, possibility, participation,
  or coordination.

Good forms include:

- an attributed friend's route or Place note inside a spatial possibility;
- Rome and Paris evidence on one permissioned, consistent comparison axis;
- separately authored contributions inside an Occasion instrument;
- a human reply shown directly without unnecessary Vesper commentary; and
- a private constraint translated into a minimum safe shared consequence
  without revealing its owner.

Reject friend activity for its own sake, public performance pressure, group
profiles, contact-list inventory, passive location leakage, and Vesper-authored
messages impersonating a person.

## 11. Memory and continuity

Memory appears at the moment of use, through the contribution it improves. Home
does not need to announce a profile or display everything retained.

Rules:

- explicit current self-statement remains authoritative for intent, preference,
  and personal meaning;
- behavior may confirm exposure, occurrence, selection, or outcome but does not
  silently become identity;
- prior evidence must have permitted use for this current job and audience;
- source and contribution lineage stay inspectable at the owner;
- “I already knew this,” “wrong context,” “not now,” “do not use this,” and
  “forget this” repair different parts of the system;
- forgetting invalidates dependent projections rather than merely hiding a
  memory row; and
- recent-trip density never grants automatic Home priority.

The strongest long-term signal is not repeated engagement. It is second-
occasion value: the person later notices, explains, judges, coordinates, or
acts better—sometimes without Vesper.

## 12. Current-world and Place composition

Affordance is relational and expiring, not a stored Place property. “Nearby,”
“accessible,” “worth it,” and “good for you” require the current subjects,
purpose, world conditions, commitments, and hard bounds.

Home therefore:

- states environment facts instead of diagnosing the person;
- presents the two or three tradeoff axes that distinguish a feasible set;
- prepares an intent-preserving fallback when burden is material;
- lets current heat, closure, route, safety, or affected people suppress richer
  editorial content;
- never lets a meaningful story silently override physical cost; and
- returns attention to the world after the useful cue or action.

“It is 91°F; the shaded route adds seven minutes” is an admissible world claim
when sourced. “You seem tired” is not an equivalent adaptation.

## 13. Medium and projection

Medium is selected after semantic admission.

```text
admitted contribution
  -> evidence shape and current job
  -> smallest adequate lead medium
  -> only necessary social, relational, or quantitative modifier
  -> compact Home projection with accessible equivalent
  -> durable owner for standard or immersive depth
```

Each unit has one lead medium:

- **evidence** for an observable source detail;
- **sequence** for order, movement, development, or provenance;
- **comparison** for a consistent axis and important difference;
- **spatial field** for place, reachability, route, or distribution;
- **concise prose** for a claim whose structure does not require another form;
- **instrument** for state, manipulation, preparation, action, or repair.

Social, relational, and quantitative are modifiers, not independent renderers.
Article, guide, reconstruction, audio brief, podcast, and social mosaic are
artifact formats, not Home sections. Their compact projections must deliver
value even if never opened. Audio and motion never autoplay.

## 14. Cross-root handoff

| Home aperture | Destination | Preserved context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why this, challenge, compare, help decide, change this | Chat | source object, evidence, current job, people, uncertainty, unresolved consequence |
| Map, route, Place horizon, local comparison | Places | selected scope, time, route, conditions, candidate set, relationship state |
| Artifact, reconstruction, return, Mine/Together continuity | Life | source, authorship, audience, lineage, relevant Place or Occasion |
| Active Plan, Occasion, invitation, commitment | Canonical object | revision, participants, authority, settled/flexible/open state, origin scroll |

Global object detail should open over Home and return to the exact prior scroll.
A root switch is explicit when the user enters the owner's workspace.

## 15. Composition receipt and evaluation

A prototype or future composer should emit a content-free composition receipt:

```text
HomeCompositionReceipt
  situation_ref + generated_at + expiry
  candidate_ids considered
  selected_foreground_id or intentional_quiet
  selected_field_ids in order
  rejected_ids + policy reason classes
  owner / audience / truth / freshness checks
  aggregate response and decision demand
  diversity dimensions represented
  infrastructure and silence counterfactuals
  projection family + accessible alternative
  exposures, corrections, actions, occurrences, outcomes, and withdrawals
```

Evaluate at three levels.

### 15.1 Unit

- five-second proposition comprehension;
- grounding and calibration;
- novelty-to-person posture;
- representational gain;
- value without CTA;
- creepiness and identity overreach;
- authorship/audience comprehension;
- correction and dismissal health; and
- capability or consequence opened.

### 15.2 Page

- foreground clarity;
- cumulative attention and response debt;
- marginal value of each additional unit;
- variety of human powers rather than renderer variety;
- present-life priority;
- social transformation without feed behavior;
- context-preserving owner handoff;
- comparison against a smaller composition and intentional quiet; and
- eyes-to-world time during live situations.

### 15.3 Longitudinal

- second-occasion improvement;
- unassisted capability transfer;
- outward consequence in people and Places;
- memory correction and causal forgetting;
- plural social Outcomes;
- withdrawal health and freedom from guilt or lock-in; and
- whether commercial exposure ever distorted the contribution or ranking.

Clicks, dwell, saves, and return frequency may describe exposure or use. They do
not prove knowledge, agreement, attachment, preference, meaning, or wellbeing.

## 16. Current implementation translation

The present code should be treated as substrate and migration evidence, not as
proof that this contract already exists.

| Current surface | Useful substrate | Contract mismatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plans/Trips Home | Server-authored ordering, grounding, state, render plans, telemetry, working trip instruments | Feature-section vocabulary (`crown`, `open_loops`, `standing-ask`, `trip-feel`, and others) is not a cross-domain contribution portfolio; prompt-first units violate the Home demand law. |
| Current Vesper Home | Composer, sessions, route/season/here context, immediate Chat access | Its posture belongs primarily to the future Chat root, not the return-on-attention Home. |
| Places | Current Place data, reasoned sections, bounded native families, spatial and availability foundations | Section/feed logic and standalone friend activity need candidate-relative affordance and integrated social authorship. |
| Adaptive composition lab | Stable semantic spine, environment degradation, root-native fixtures, client-owned geometry | Fixture-only and intentionally not a full Home composer; legacy treatment keys still conflate medium, modifier, and semantic role. |

Do not begin by converting every existing section into a new Home candidate.
First author and compare complete compositions. Then map the selected semantic
needs to existing authorities and identify the smallest migration seams.

## 17. Four-situation acceptance portfolio

This contract is not ready for promotion until one common fixture schema can
compose and compare:

1. an ordinary New York week with rich context and no urgency;
2. a recent return from Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi, and Rome into a life already
   moving forward;
3. live Rome under heat, walking, time, and attention pressure; and
4. a Brooklyn dinner with private constraints, exact authorship, one shared
   choice, nonresponse, and plural Outcomes.

For every situation, compare:

- infrastructure alone;
- an underreaching factual report or prompt;
- an overreaching personalized or autonomous treatment;
- the recommended Home composition; and
- intentional silence or a smaller composition.

The companion fixture pack owns the worked examples. Visual language and exact
geometry remain work for the dedicated visual-design tool after the semantic
and interaction comparison is accepted.

## 18. Promotion decisions still required

This working contract deliberately leaves five consequential decisions for
founder acceptance:

1. Promote Home's root posture from “What matters now?” to the stronger
   return-on-attention definition.
2. Replace the seven named Home regions as permanent anatomy with the four
   stable composition regions in this document.
3. Accept the four contribution classes and silence as the shared Home
   admission vocabulary.
4. Accept the Home unit and composition receipts as semantic requirements while
   leaving storage topology unresolved.
5. Promote Home, Chat, Places, and Life from shell under test into canonical
   navigation direction, with an explicit migration path from Plans and current
   Vesper Home.

Until those decisions are promoted, this document is a design oracle rather
than production authority.
