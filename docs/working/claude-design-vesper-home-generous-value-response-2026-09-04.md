## The recommended system

One skeleton, four budgets. Read → priority (zero or one; a crown only when
something operational is at stake, otherwise the strongest unit leads bare) →
the coming days (two to four grounded alternatives, one of them "stay in",
social material integrated where it lands) → in motion → *From your people*
(conditional) → understanding now → something to try → material made useful →
the city this week (marked not-inferred) → optional depth with a real payoff in
the preview → the week seam and one forward line.

Budgets: dominance 0–1; demand at most one ask per page (only H4 spends it);
possibility 2–4; depth behind doors. Length follows value: H4-during is shorter
by folding, not deleting; H3-first is shorter than H1 because the city is all
there is.

## Multiplayer: the comparison and the recommendation

Identical evidence in both treatments: Alex's arrangement and his words, Theo's
market note, Maya's bakery share (friends, through Sunday), Dana's addressed
bookshop, the show. Treatment 1 integrates every person into the unit their
contribution changes, with one pull door at the foot. Treatment 2 keeps the same
top and moves the two *authored* units (Maya's photograph and words, Dana's
words) into a **From your people** region, in full, once; Theo stays inside the
market unit because his note is spent there.

**Recommendation: Treatment 2, with the condition.** Authored material is warmer
and more legible in its author's form than reduced to a clause. The condition
keeps it from becoming a social module: the region renders only when two or
more authored units are not already spent above, never duplicates a note shown
in full above, and disappears when empty (the sparse variant shows this). Shared
*consequences* (the arrangement) still appear where they land, above the region.
"What else have my people shared?" routes to the existing Life · People owner;
no fifth tab, no new aperture.

**Tension reported, not resolved.** MP2's "one relational opening" law, read as
a page-wide cap on authored material, would forbid Treatment 2. This pass reads
it as a dominance rule (one social cause may lead) and a demand rule (one social
unit may ask), not as a count. Ruling requested: **D-H2**.

## Gaps, kept separate from the design judgment

**Content production.** A city-scoped notice source with freshness; bounded
mechanism explanations with one source each; world rhythms (market, tide, hall
set times) with owners; photographs for every plate. Every passage, person, and
world fact on these boards is invented and labelled so.

**Engineering verification.** Grant-scoped reads of authored shares with
audience and expiry; Occasion decision-by-deadline under owner-controlled
collaboration; a world notice linked to a held commitment; sample retirement;
fold-and-return with position preserved; exposure-history suppression without
inferred knowledge. None is claimed built.

**Not done.** Large-text renders; native behaviour; participant evidence. These
are design reviews, not validation. No static page proves autonomous checking,
guaranteed updates, or live re-checks.

# Revision 2 — composition and production (same day)

The handoff was updated after H0–H5 with a review: the content range improved,
but H1–H3 were text-heavy and visually flat. This revision keeps the richer Home
and changes how it is expressed and produced. H1–H5 were revised **in place**;
each board keeps the previous pass beside the revision (H1 and H5 also keep the
original study version). Board 1–4 are untouched.

## What changed on the boards

- **Typography, four jobs.** Section heading (sans 13/600, sentence case, with a
  hairline; only where it navigates), content title (serif, the first line of
  every unit), metadata (one mono line for time, distance, date, or source),
  supporting text (one or two sentences). The gold kicker stacked above every
  title is gone; "Understanding now" + "Thursday's cold · why your route splits"
  + the title collapsed to one title.
- **Containment, selective.** Cards for coherent objects and experiences: a
  friend's photograph and words (media-led, authorship inside the unit), a
  prepared afternoon ("Today"), a shared arrangement (Alex's Saturday, with its
  people and timing integrated), the market with its instrument and Theo's quoted
  judgment, a reading preview. Rows for commitments, instruments for comparisons,
  bare units for findings and techniques, fact rows for service and city notices.
  **D-H1 ("a crown only when something operational is at stake") is withdrawn.**
- **Rationale off the surface.** "Her words, kept hers", "nothing to reply to",
  "not inferred from you", "try it or don't", and every footnote explaining why a
  unit qualified were removed. Kept visible: authorship (name, when, audience),
  source metadata, the sample mark, and material uncertainty ("it does not prove
  how that kitchen made it"; "whether you went is yours to say").
- **H3 shows the result.** The sample is a ticket-shaped stub, an arrow, and the
  evening it becomes (title, span, two facts). The paragraph describing what a
  ticket could become is gone. Capabilities are rows; city material is one
  mechanism unit and two fact rows.
- **H4 and H5 checked** against the same rules; both regenerated from the revised
  components.

## Production ledger

H0 now classifies every visible unit by production class — human-authored,
structured state, current world information, reusable researched content,
personal relevance (select/connect/lightly adapt), bespoke synthesis, optional
depth — with likely trigger, reuse/audience scope, freshness or invalidation
condition, fallback, and the cost unknown per unit. The operating behaviours
designed toward: opening Home retrieves and composes; new work is justified by a
source change, a stale fact, a new authorized contribution, or deliberate
exploration; live facts are separated from durable explanation; results are
kept and reused while valid; the page stays useful with existing material when
deeper work is unavailable. Fixed sample demonstrations are prepared once.

**Cost unknowns named, not estimated:** generation frequency, token/tool usage,
reuse rate, and cost per active user. The producer's per-call limits and prompt
caching do not establish account-level economics.

# Revision 5 — the social split (2026-09-05)

Founder ruling, later the same day: multiplayer material divides by root.

| Material | Root | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Directed and shared-consequential (addressed to me, invited, a contribution to our arrangement, a decision asked of me, a plan edit under a grant, a change to a shared occasion) | Home | The consequence, never the notification; inside the object it changes, or in the `Addressed to you` region; one ask per page |
| Casual sharing (a friend's photo, note, or place made visible to friends; a featured Status) | Places, as a `From friends` scope over the same map and field | Finite, situation-ranked, never person-ranked; marks at the precision the author chose; city-level statuses in an `Elsewhere` band; ends |
| The record | Life · People | Stable, by person |

The friends map is semantic, never biometric: every mark answers where they
were or what they shared, who authored it, at what precision, for which
audience, until when. No live location, no presence inferred, no location
permission required of anyone.

**D-H4 closes.** Home's pull door ("What your friends have shared") lands on
Places in the friends scope. No present-tense band on Life People, no fifth
surface. The decision record's §2 carries the split; the Home root contract
rule 1 and the build manifest cite it. Local commits `d8c2b22` (workspace)
and `f8f9cea8e` (travel-app). Nothing pushed.
