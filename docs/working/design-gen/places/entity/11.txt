NOT DUPLICATE DETAIL PAGES
Places Focus and the entity page were two renderers for one thing. Under the page, the Focus lanes become inputs to one body and two fact rows; source and author distinctions survive as labels, faces and italics, not as sections. One renderer. The one deliberate exception is the container page’s map plate, named here so it is not introduced by accident.
THE PLACE VS MY HISTORY
The page answers “what is this place, and what do I and my people know about it now”. Life answers “what has happened between me and this place”. The page shows one line of that and a door; Life shows the record and a door back. Neither pulls the other in.
LABELS AND RETURN · TO COORDINATE WITH LIFE
Proposed: the page’s door reads “Your history here”; Life’s reads “Open the place”. Each returns to exactly where it was tapped. The Life lane owns the record’s anatomy; this lane owns only the door and the one-line read.
