TYPE AND CONTAINMENT (§6) · AS APPLIED ON 14–17
Upright serif titles at 22, 17 and 15; no italic anywhere, including work titles (Playtime, Music for 18 Musicians) and people’s words, which are upright in quotation marks. One mono label per section; units carry no kick that repeats it; the only small caps inside a group are the pair’s dimension labels and the day tile. Cards group related information (a pocket, a pair, a moment, a set) and claim no confidence; the lead and its siblings are uncarded, with scale as the hierarchy. Mono is reserved for section labels, day tiles and map labels. Proposed as a Places expression; not applied to Home, Chat or Life.
