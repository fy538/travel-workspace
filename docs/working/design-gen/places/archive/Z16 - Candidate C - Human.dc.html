THE HYPOTHESIS
Do original encounters, different perspectives and connections to someone’s life make exploration most compelling? The moment kept with Maya leads because it is kept, not because it is social; two voices on the organ and two on the Print Room show plural perspective without consensus; Theo’s line changes a read and the phone says so in four words; the person’s own record makes a change useful (the counter they go to closes earlier). The world arrives as a compact assortment after.
