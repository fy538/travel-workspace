THE ORGANIZING PRINCIPLE
A mixed-value field with one pocket (§11.2): ordered by kind of value, not by neighbourhood. People appear where their places are; two stand alone because their contribution is not spatial or is the opening itself.
KEPT · COMBINED · OMITTED
FROMWHATHERE
ALead + siblings; the compact assortment; the pair; the reading; the morning set with Theo inside; standalone momentsKept; the assortment moved up, right after the opening
BThe pocketKept for Red Hook only; the pool and the lunch counter are separate rows with their own targets; no route, no walk
BSunset Park, Canal Street and Downtown as pocketsOmitted: proximity there is one line (“three minutes from the hall”), so the hour leads with siblings and no map
CThe kept moment as the opening; voices on the organ; Priya seen up closeKept, populated only; the cold opening is the film
CAlex’s loop, the market rhythm, then/now on the counter, the Print Room as two voicesOmitted: the counter’s change and the loop belong to Stage 3’s situations; the Print Room already has Maya’s picture
THE FIVE CORRECTIONS (§11.3)
HOW IT IS ADDRESSED
1 · Options, not an itineraryNo “before that”, “after” or “ends at” anywhere; siblings are related by place (“the same park”) or distance (“three minutes from the hall”). The pocket has no route and no walk; its one line states proximity (“within twelve minutes of the landing”). The pool and the counter are two rows, two targets
2 · Repeated hierarchy, duplicate contributionsSection labels only where they group several things (Any day, Saturday evening, Worth understanding, Seen up close, Sunday); a card with an informative title has no label above it. Maya’s Print Room words appear once, with her picture; the pocket entry carries a compact reference to it
3 · Internal language“Any day” only. The reading’s preview delivers its own payoff (the creek bed, the sill, the tide) and the door is “The rest of the reading”; “complete as read” is gone
4 · Honest evidence“The first evening you are both free” is removed. The opening uses only the kept possibility and Maya’s picture from board 13. No fixture evidence was added
5 · Draw itBoth scrolls are drawn in full; nothing is referenced
THE SELECTION QUESTION
A coherent opening into worthwhile possibilities, with geography doing useful work where needed? Look at the opening with the assortment, the one pocket, and the cold scroll’s pacing. Not a usability finding.
