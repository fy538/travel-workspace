WHY THESE
The second look at the rendered boards (09-07, after §11) found cases the situations had skipped: every lead assumed a picture although photo coverage is near zero; the listening hour was never opened although the Print Room was; nothing in Places waited or failed; there was no way into another city and no state before a city. They are drawn here, with the same kit, rather than folded into 02–06.
THE LEAD WITHOUT A PICTURE
When there is no media the card does not fake a plate: the date tile sits beside the kick, the title is one step larger, and the substance, the time, the one uncertainty and the door are unchanged. Every unit below drops its thumbnail as well, so the page is honest about its world. Where a real photograph exists, the plate returns; the Entity lab’s photo-or-nothing ruling applies.
THE OCCURRENCE OPENED
The venue page (RE2) is a place; this is a dated thing that happens there. It keeps the series and the occurrence apart in copy (“this Saturday”, “next Saturday instead”), carries the one uncertainty, and offers the hall as a door. Whether a dated occurrence needs its own page or a variant of the venue page is a reuse decision for 08.
