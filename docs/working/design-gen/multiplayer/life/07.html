Reply, Keep and Ask are three separate choices, none required by the others: you need not keep it to enjoy it or to find it again, and opening it keeps nothing. “Keep a copy of this note” is not “Keep the place” — one retains a permitted copy of her original, the other adds Lilia to your places; an unqualified “Keep it” (Entity 14 R4) does not say which. The copy door is agreement-dependent: it needs a permitted-copy term that is still proposed. Ask is private and eligible material only.
What keeping a place does not do07.8
THE DISTINCTION
Lilia is kept by you — a place in your record since Aug 30.
Maya’s note is hers. Keeping the place does not keep her words, and her words do not become yours by being the reason you kept it.
Three different things: you can reach it while she shares it; the share can end on its own date; she can take it back. A copy you kept survives the second and not the third. What already left the app cannot be recalled by any of them.
