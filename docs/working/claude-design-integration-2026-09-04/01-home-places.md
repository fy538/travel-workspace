# Home and Places — deliver the value, not the promise of work

## Assignment

Work in `/Users/feihuyan/Downloads/vesper-home-places 3`. Refine this existing
design project; do not build or change the production app. Produce opinionated
consumer compositions and connected destinations, not another abstract strategy
essay, component catalogue, or root-navigation redesign.

Vesper's product moves are **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** Home and Places are its output-heavy surfaces. Chat supports input and
agency; Life holds continuity. Multiplayer is structural, not a separate feed.
The four roots remain Home / Chat / Places / Life.

The central task: make an ordinary open of Home and Places immediately worth the
person's attention, even without urgent arrangements or another input from them.
Quiet means low demand, not low substance. Current New York life must not be
subordinate to the recent Europe journey.

## Diagnosis to address

The ruled Quiet composition still spends prominent space explaining that the
week is handled and a future Sunday remains empty. Past-show and dentist rows
precede the market and reading. These elements are individually plausible, but
their combined hierarchy can make Home feel like administration before value.

A separate Life-to-Home sequence offers to check menus instead of providing the
useful result. Honesty about missing evidence is essential, but an offer to work
is not equivalent to work already delivered. Do not invent results; choose an
already supported contribution or show the useful result of an appropriately
authorized bounded check. Do not turn this into blanket background research.

Several reading units specify an interesting title or a reading duration without
composing the actual experience behind it. Complete the promise, not just its door.

## Work package A — full-value ordinary Home

Compose three complete states from one coherent ordinary-week fixture:

1. **Open time, mature context:** no urgent obligation; one useful practical
   possibility, one substantive understanding, and an authorized human dimension
   where it materially changes the result. Do not fill three slots to meet a quota.
2. **An actual commitment:** dinner, a show, or an appointment needs situated help.
   Practical relief may lead without converting the whole page into operations.
3. **The next open after no engagement:** time and relevant facts move forward,
   but ignored content creates no task, guilt, taste inference, or demand.

Include a thin-history variant using one deliberately supplied object and modest
world context. No account setup or documentation assignment is the admission price.

For every retained unit state, outside the phone: what was already known; what
Vesper adds; why it belongs now; whether value is complete on view; what opening
it does; and what would justify removing it. Do not copy this ledger into the UI.

Use real finished fixture copy and the smallest suitable medium. A concise
comparison, practical fact, route relation, attributed observation, or readable
passage can carry substance. Do not require an illustration or an article for
everything. No personality verdicts, empty reassurance, recap counts as value,
or repeated descriptions of how hard Vesper is working.

## Work package B — ordinary Places, beyond one curated waterfront

Preserve the valuable world → focus → path → live distinction. Exercise it with:

- an open local afternoon with no chosen destination or named Plan;
- an explicit practical question, such as somewhere suitable before dinner;
- a personal curiosity that can be understood without going anywhere;
- a friend-contributed perspective under an existing grant;
- another neighborhood or city with less personal history.

Draw how map, list, search, spatial comparison, and opening a place relate.
Changing scope must not quietly change facts or invent social access. Assess
scope-control legibility with several relevant relationships; do not solve this
by displaying every possible relationship pill permanently.

Current candidate-set and scope rules remain contracts, not casual UI choices.
If broader exploration requires changing them, name that proposed change and its
owner explicitly rather than silently filtering away facts to improve a mockup.

## Work package C — fulfill the doors and reconnect the roots

Complete these transitions, reusing neighboring projects' destinations:

- Home contribution → substantive reading/comparison → return to Home.
- Home possibility → Places context → entity page → optional retained intent.
- A retained possibility becomes useful later without becoming an obligation.
- A shared perspective changes a possibility without requiring a group workspace.

Specify immediate tap response, destination, back behavior, later re-entry, and
whether anything persisted. A destination need not be fully redrawn if another
project already owns it; show enough of it to make the transition evaluable.

## Boundaries and coordination

The Entity lane owns canonical object-page design. Its newer paragraph/citation
direction must be reconciled with older Places lane-based layouts; do not build
a competing venue detail screen. Life owns the durable personal relationship
view. The Components and Plan task owns prospective-model decisions; this project
shows their Home/Places consequences rather than inventing a Plan system.

Design toward external booking handoff, not checkout, holds, rebooking, or
automated provider calls. Existing booking-heavy fixtures remain history, not
an instruction to reproduce that capability. Do not add a social tab, fixed
people region, expense dashboard, or onboarding campaign.

## Deliverables and review bar

Return updated active boards, all full scrolls, the connected destinations,
before/after rationale, a reuse/removal list, and a short decision log naming
cross-project assumptions. Mark design proposals separately from accepted canon.

Review at phone scale and large text. The result should answer:

- What did I get before tapping or contributing again?
- Is this useful beyond our familiar Rome/pasta/waterfront examples?
- Can I enjoy or use it without turning it into a project?
- Does a friend change the value, rather than decorate it?
- Can I understand the practical capability without a rare travel emergency?
- Does the opened destination deliver what the first surface promised?

Do not claim a complete read based on a title and two teaser sentences. Do not
claim user validation from a beautiful board. Identify content-production and
engineering gaps separately from the design judgment.
