## The shared product direction

Vesper is personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world. It turns
attention and intention into useful understanding, grounded possibilities,
practical relief, and continuity. Its moves are **Make sense. Open possibility.
Help it work. Carry forward.** These are neither four tabs nor a required funnel.

- **Home:** substantial value for current life and the anticipated future.
- **Chat:** effortless contribution, immediate answers, expression and agency.
- **Places:** intelligible world, spatial possibility, practical context, and
  authorized human perspectives.
- **Life:** stable access to what is held, lived, intended, and shared.

The founder's latest contraction direction is to stop competing in booking
execution, simplify itinerary/change/permission surfaces, and make expense help
integrated rather than form-heavy. Use that as the design target. Do not delete
production infrastructure or silently rewrite accepted contracts under this brief.

## Shared delivery standard

For each lane return: an active-board map; completed consumer screens and
meaningful transitions; a concise rationale; a decision/delta log; component
reuse and removal candidates; unresolved cross-lane assumptions; and explicit
design-versus-build status. Keep annotations outside consumer phone frames.

Use realistic, explicitly labeled fixtures. Do not present invented venue facts,
friend contributions, permissions, transactions, or successful AI behavior as
real. Prototype state must follow user actions; label scripted language limits.
Render at ordinary phone size, inspect the full scroll, and check a narrow/large
text state. Drawings and scripted walkthroughs are not participant evidence.
