## Reuse and removal

**Reused, unchanged:** crown anatomy; day band, rhythm bars, tide curve, span
comparison, basis strip; the 44px in-motion row; the banded week seam; section
header with rule; the Door atom; the scope handle; Place Focus and Place Path
anatomies; the attributed human card (MP1); the coda; the four-root shell; the
Entity lane's page (as a stub); the Chat lane's reader and Keep door; Life's
kept drawers and People lens.

**Removal candidates:** "Kept for you" as a crown on Quiet (fold into the
person's row); past-event rows on Home; reading units with a duration but no
passage; the browse pair on World Field where the field already has three
branches; the "offer to check" unit shape (D3); the four-door foot on Place
Focus (already ruled, §12.7 i).

## Gaps, kept separate from the design judgment

**Content production.** Composed passages need a real source-to-passage
pipeline (a governed excerpt or a Vesper composition with Source lineage); the
fixture passages are invented. World rhythms (market peaks, tide tables, hours)
need an owner with freshness. Photographs for plates remain unsourced.

**Engineering.** A context handle echoed by map/list/search (landed 09-04 for
map). A viewer-relative grant read for one note. Active-seat arbitration so
two readings do not both appear (HPL-2, open). An expiring retained-intention
read (C3), not a store. Exact-return envelopes for the four transitions. None
of these is claimed built.

**Not done in this pass.** A large-text render of the boards; native
behavior; any participant evidence. Drawings and ledgers are not user
validation.

## Review bar

- *What did I get before tapping?* A1: a decision (before 9:30) and an
  understanding (the flood line), both complete. A3: a complete reading.
- *Useful beyond Rome / pasta / waterfront?* A market, a coffee route, a
  dentist, a bookhall, Jackson Heights; no Europe unit in the week.
- *Without turning it into a project?* No Plan is created anywhere in A or B;
  C3 keeps an option in the person's words and lets it go nowhere.
- *Does a friend change the value?* A1/B4/C4: Theo moves the window;
  withdrawal reverts it. The change is the attribution.
- *Practical capability without an emergency?* A2 (a show), B2 (dinner before
  it), B1 (a walk timed to the tide).
- *Does the opened destination deliver?* C1–C4 draw each destination and its
  return; the reader continues the composed passage rather than restarting it.
