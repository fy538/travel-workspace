# Chat — effortless expression, without making conversation mandatory

## Assignment and scope

Work in `/Users/feihuyan/Downloads/vesper-chat`. This is a **bounded design
integration pass**, not permission to redesign the entire Chat root or modify
production Chat. Preserve the cleaner entry, composer, card vocabulary, and
existing visual language where they work.

Vesper makes sense, opens possibility, helps it work, and carries forward.
Chat receives attention and intention, answers immediately, and lets the person
shape or act. Home/Places deliver ongoing value; Life preserves continuity.
Chat is neither a second feed nor the mandatory gateway to all current truth.

## What remains good

The target entry is substantially cleaner than a workbench feed. Useful concrete
engagement near the composer is better than generic questions. Coordination
cards should remain small; a simple evening must not acquire a planning console.
The current-workbench-to-target migration is a documented dependency, not an
instruction to slim production Chat before Home can carry the returned value.

## The specific assumption to revisit

Boards 10/12 make coordination message-only and the thread the operational
itinerary. They tie the broader pivot to retrieval by asking. The September 4
review recommends reopening that assumption—not declaring it already repealed.

We want less UI burden without requiring people to ask for information they
could inspect directly. Compare the current rule against a small stable
arrangement view accessible from a card, Home, and Life. Keep one owner and
current state; do not introduce a parallel planner inside Chat.

Produce a recommended amendment, the cases it fixes, and any remaining
tradeoff. Do not treat a proposed interaction as adopted product law.

## Work package A — input pays immediately

Compose short, complete consumer sequences for:

- a ticket sent alone: useful interpretation and the permitted quiet retention;
- that same ticket attached to a question: answer without assuming durable intake;
- a photograph with an authored observation: substance before a compact receipt;
- “Keep jazz in mind for Saturday”: optional intention without a naming wizard;
- a contextual change from a visible place or arrangement;
- an ordinary expense question versus an explicit shared-cost instruction.

Resolve the human job from wording and affordance, not medium alone. Never expose
Ask/Bring/Point as a classification quiz. Show pending processing, usable partial
results, and a recoverable failure for one ingestion case. Do not create a manual
filing queue or demand another contribution to justify the first.

## Work package B — refer to things cheaply

When arriving from Home, Places, Life, or an entity, show enough context for the
person to know what they are addressing. Exercise “later,” “not this place,”
“make it less rushed,” and a local correction. Ambiguous scope should be resolved
only when needed, not with a permanent ontology selector.

Direct and language changes act on the same owner. Show localized readback and
return to the originating surface. Navigation must not Undo an applied change;
Undo must not merely dismiss the message. Keep changes inspectable without
requiring everyone to operate a history or permissions screen.

## Work package C — leave and return without transcript archaeology

Show a person discussing unrelated topics, then returning to Saturday's current
arrangement. The current state must be accessible without searching old versions
or asking what was accepted. Explore the card-to-current-view seam alongside the
interaction project. Do not claim the card itself is a new durable source.

For shared participation, use the existing Invitation/Decision/Brief family.
The current arrangement may be a projection, not another group chat or a required
named Plan. Do not independently design membership or Occasion semantics.

## Work package D — fulfill a content opening

Reconcile Home-originated substantive content with the editorial family. Show one
complete reading/comparison, an optional contextual question about it, and its
return/Keep behavior. Home must already have delivered value; Chat should not be
the toll required to obtain the interesting part. Do not expand this into a new
generative-canvas or media-renderer research program.

Distinguish keeping an exact editorial composition from retaining a place or
intention. Do not automatically promote transient output into Life. If the
existing editorial-only Keep rule obstructs a desired behavior, name the narrow
conflict rather than adding a universal Save button.

## Coordination and exclusions

The interaction lab owns the arrangement and practical-help journey; the active
**Components and Plan** task owns its semantic questions:
`codex://threads/01a06e71-b35c-7ca0-8307-367db90a8a6a`.
Life owns retrieval; Home/Places owns output; Entity owns the place page.
Chat supplies expression, context, and honest readback to those owners.

No production edits, new feed, blanket memory capture, ambient inbox access,
booking execution, autonomous payments, or generic control center. Preserve
independent audience/action boundaries without rendering internal governance
terminology in ordinary messages.

## Deliverables and acceptance

Return a small set of connected phone-scale sequences, a before/after amendment
proposal for coordination/current-state access, and a reuse/delta list. State
exactly what a tap or utterance changes, where it lands, and how re-entry works.

Success means contribution feels easy, worthwhile replies arrive before
administration, visible things are cheap to refer to, and current truth does not
require conversation. Label simulated language and fixture facts. This pass
does not establish participant evidence or override existing migration gates.
