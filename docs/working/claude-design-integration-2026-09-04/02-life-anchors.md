## The proposal to explore, not silently canonize

The Components and Plan task is considering this product rule:

> A person can retain an intention before it belongs to an organized Plan.

It separates remembering, degree of structure, and obligation. This is a
proposal, not an accepted database type or permission to create an intention
service. Design its user-visible implications and identify where current canon
requires a deliberate amendment.

## Work package A — one mixed past/present/future corpus

Compose complete Life views containing these fixture situations together:

- A completed Europe journey and an ordinary recent local dinner.
- A retained restaurant, without intent to visit it on a date.
- “Keep jazz in mind for Saturday; don't build the evening around it.”
- “I'd like to make that pasta sometime.”
- A confirmed meeting with Maya at eight.
- “Maybe Portugal next spring,” still exploratory unless explicitly retained.

Do not automatically mint six containers. A question is not a saved intention;
a saved place is not a planned visit; a precise optional time is not a commitment.
Use actual user wording and invoked gestures to establish what was retained.

Show Time plus whichever other existing lenses are genuinely useful. Determine
how future material is discoverable without burying it among past chapters or
turning the root into a dashboard. Do not assume a fifth lens or an Upcoming tab
is required. Recommend the smallest coherent anatomy after trying it in context.

## Work package B — lifecycle without a completion funnel

Draw connected states, not just a state catalogue:

1. The person expresses a clear retained possibility in Chat or on a place.
2. Life gives it a quiet, findable home with no filing work.
3. Home or Places later makes it useful in a supported current situation.
4. It remains optional, is incorporated into an arrangement, or is released.
5. The date passes with no evidence of attendance and no further user response.
6. A later cue retrieves the original meaning and its current standing.

Distinguish expiry of a dated opportunity from deletion of an explicitly kept
intention. Do not auto-reschedule, mark overdue, infer rejection, or claim the
event happened. Do not ask a review question simply to finish the lifecycle.

Include one option relevant to two possible evenings. Explain which identity is
shared and which context is local without duplicating the underlying place or
automatically merging arrangements. Architecture remains the Plan lane's remit.

## Work package C — clear object and relationship destinations

Complete the distinction among:

- opening the original ticket/photo/message;
- opening the place itself;
- opening my accumulated relationship with that place;
- opening the current arrangement in which it participates;
- reopening an exact deliberately kept generated composition.

Use the same place across Life and the newer Entity design. Preserve exact
return context. A map, timeline, category drawer, or search is a route into the
same corpus, not a separate archive with independently changing truth.

Do not conflate keeping a composition, saving a place, and retaining an intention.
Keep user-facing labels simple, but show the smallest confirmation/readback that
makes their consequences understandable. No universal Save-everything sheet.

## Work package D — integrate existing social continuity

Keeping it privately does not invite the friend or republish their words.
Show my view, the shared arrangement's view, and a later withdrawal where useful.
