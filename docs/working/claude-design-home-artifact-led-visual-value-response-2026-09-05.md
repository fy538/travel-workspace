## 4. The eleven questions, answered per page

1. Identify and receive value at a glance: the sequence, the pass, the strip and the paired evidence each read without the caption. The bare method reads as a method.
2. Understandable visually where reading was needed: order (sequence), evidence and its gaps (strip), the difference (paired evidence), where the cold changes (map).
3. Substance beyond supplied information: the menus' three scales; the four-degree split; the ferry morning's gap; the method behind the photograph. Nothing repeats the person's own words as a claim.
4. If the lead is uninteresting: Sunday still has the cold, the method, the city, the reading; day zero has the menus and the strip.
5. Form fits content; simple stays simple: the city fact, the dentist, the refund claim, and the settled dinner stay rows on every page.
6. People through contributions: Maya's and Dana's words with the place they gave; no decorative facepile except where people are the subject.
7. Leave without assignment: no phone asks the person to keep, reflect, or cook. The waiting window (08) asks nothing.
8. Identity across scale and root: the ticket at four scales (08); the sequence on Home and on the ground (02, 06); the cold on Home and on the map.
9. Legible at larger text: 09 shows Sunday and the live pass at 1.3×; both reflow within 393 px. The chip's object print does not scale and is never the only carrier of its fact.
10. Without generating everything anew: every form on the ledger is structured state, retrieval, or reusable content except the pairing of paired evidence, which is bespoke synthesis over two sources.
