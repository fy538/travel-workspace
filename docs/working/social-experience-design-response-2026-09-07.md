## 5. What changed after comparison (historical: first pass)

The openness toggle (D1-B) was drawn and set aside: honest, but a second state to explain. The warm acknowledgment (D2-B) is kept as the one candidate and marked for observation. The generated-led afternoon (D5-B) lost on its own frame: it used A1 without a grant and invented quiet. The invitation-only day (D4-B) showed the guest texting the host. The occasion-bound ending (D6-A) turned out to be the default, with the connection as opt-in rather than the reverse. The Paris/Rome contrast was omitted (no research, no grant). The address boundary settled as: in Maya's view at once, in Sam's view after he answers.

## 11. The four contract decisions, drafted as proposals

Each remains proposed, not adopted. The repository-alignment pass revised all
four; these summaries replace the first handback's recommendations.

- [Friends audience](friends-audience-decision-proposal-2026-09-07.md): one understandable connection and deliberate per-share audiences, not a second friends-membership invitation ritual. Evaluate existing named-recipient authority before adding a circle kind.
- [Guest identity and delivery](guest-identity-and-delivery-decision-proposal-2026-09-07.md): extend existing Occasion/invitation owners where needed. Safe preview, attributed response, sensitive arrival facts and later material need separate capability/identity decisions; an unverified RSVP cannot establish identity.
- [Ongoing connection](ongoing-connection-default-decision-proposal-2026-09-07.md): eligible objects as well as words/photos; initiated rather than automatically prompted contact; mute, disconnect, block and report are separate choices without prerequisites.
- [Scoped source use](scoped-source-use-grant-decision-proposal-2026-09-07.md): original receiving, independent world help and authorized recipient composition are distinct. The immediate-only grant neither permits retained derivatives nor prohibits other separately authorized composition. Existing T0 enforcement must be reused and tested.
