## 1. The assignment

Design **one complete, current Places experience**, drawing together world discovery,
personal relevance, human perspectives, and practical help. Social is part of this
experience, not its entire purpose. This complements the destination thread's
September 7 investigation; it does not restart that work.

**Purpose of the standalone project:** unify the best existing Places ideas into
a generous everyday exploration experience, with strong low-context value and
effortless social discovery. This is completion and rebalancing, not a new product
grammar or a presumption that the existing implementation needs replacing.

Working distinction:

> **Home brings forward what matters in my life. Places lets me explore what the
> world could offer—from here, somewhere else, or through my people.**

Places must reward both “I know what I am looking for” and “show me something
worth encountering.” It must deliver value without a rich history, a populated
friend network, map manipulation, or a researched dossier for every candidate.
Everyday life is a first-class setting; travel is a richer situation, not an entry requirement.

Use the four moves—**Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.**—
as a repertoire within Places, not four mandatory sections or a sequential funnel.

## 3. What the page should give me

- **A worthwhile world:** places, events, areas, routes, useful explanations and
  comparisons—not only restaurants, saved items, or “open now.”
- **Relevant possibility:** something for an available hour, the weekend, a future
  city, or a curiosity. Known interests can be bridges, not restrictive filters.
- **Human perspective:** a friend's photograph or words may be enjoyable as-is.
  Enrichment should add substance, not paraphrase the person or manufacture insight.
- **Practical confidence:** enough timing, travel, availability and uncertainty to
  understand whether an option fits. The live engine helps exploration and preparation,
  not just active-trip alerts. Exploring a future visit is not an “open now” question.
- **An easy continuation:** understand more, compare, ask, keep, share, get directions,
  or propose doing something together—without a workflow menu on every item.

Aim for a generous, finite field, not an empty dashboard or an endless attention feed.
Use selective cards, recognizable objects and clear hierarchy; avoid repeated
section labels and walls of generated prose. Detailed visual composition belongs
in Claude Design. More value does not mean generating bespoke content for every row.

**Finite is not a short-page quota.** Give a satisfying first collection, then
let the person deliberately explore further, widen an area, change time or follow
a subject. Do not recycle the same material to simulate freshness. With thin supply,
preserve useful world information and a clear way onward rather than inserting
reflection prompts, inviting friends as an entrance fee or claiming that nothing
exists. A bounded initial page is not a bounded view of what the world offers.

## 4. Design five complete situations using one shared design system

A shared system does not mean one repeated section arrangement. The composition
revision handoff separates the value delivered from its presentation and compares
different arrangements before applying the selected language across these situations.

| Situation | What it must answer |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary NYC opening, little context and no friends' shares | What can I immediately enjoy, understand or consider without supplying more input? |
| Purposeful exploration: Saturday evening, a restaurant, exhibition or music event | How do search, time, comparison, relevance and practical uncertainty work together? |
| Through my people: sparse and dense, nearby and elsewhere | Can I enjoy ordinary sharing, find an absent friend's perspective, and explore beyond friends without social pressure? |
| Another city before arrival | Can I explore Sorrento from New York without pretending I am there or requiring location permission? |
| Taking a possibility forward | Can I open, compare, ask, keep, share or propose gathering, then return to the same exploration context? |

For each, show the full scroll, map/list relationship, one useful detail opening,
one continuation and its return. These are test situations, **not five modes or new tabs**.
Include no-location, thin supply, no useful social material, stale practical facts,
and a withdrawn share as variations—not a separate screen catalogue.

Within purposeful exploration and taking something forward, include a matched
comparison: **for me → with Maya → around an existing dinner**. This preserves
the old MP3/MP4 strength without adding a sixth mode or a mandatory scope toolbar.
Choosing a social viewing scope and exploring a shared possibility are different jobs.

## 5. Social and cross-root boundaries

is the baseline:

- **Places:** casual spatial sharing in a `From friends` scope over the same world.
  Authored place/neighborhood/city precision is not live whereabouts. No friend
  popularity hierarchy, passive viewer reports, or obligation to respond.
- **Home:** addressed value and shared consequences, within the object they change.
- **Life:** eligible retained material and people/place/occasion continuity.
- **Chat:** private asking and preparation. Clearly distinguish **Ask Vesper** from
  **Reply to Maya**; a human reply is not automatically a durable personal memory.

Current canon routes non-spatial casual material through a featured Status doorway
on Home and Life People afterward. Show an honest onward path from Places; if that
feels broken, propose an explicit amendment rather than inventing coordinates or
silently moving all social activity into Places.

The same material may have different useful presentations across roots, but not
different authorship, audience, identity or correction behavior. Keep is not visit;
share is not invite; contribution is not attendance or editing permission.

## 6. Decisions this lane should resolve

1. **Default composition:** what makes a no-query opening worthwhile, and how does
   purpose change prominence? Neither friends, walking distance nor popularity should
   automatically dominate every situation.
2. **Spatial interaction:** when do map, list, area and route help? Preserve the same
   exploration across transformations; deliberate changes to city/query/time may
   establish a new result set. Do not silently replace inventory on a map toggle.
3. **Content forms:** which objects deserve a card, row, comparison or detail? Pins
   are one spatial expression; events, routes, areas and non-spatial material differ.
4. **Social receiving:** how are original material, optional enrichment, human reply,
   private asking and optional gathering legible without a button wall?
5. **Continuation:** which existing owner/destination handles each action, and what
   context survives the return? Flag thin-guest and pre-Plan gaps instead of depicting
   unimplemented behavior as established capability.

### 6.1 Recommended starting composition: one exploration, several expressions

Use the existing **World Field → Place Focus → Place Path**, with **Live Reduction**
when appropriate. Do not introduce separate Discover, Social, Map and Search
products inside Places. The following is the first composition to pressure-test;
Claude Design owns geometry, type, motion and component treatment.

| Part of the experience | Recommended behavior | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation and controls | A compact selected place/area, applicable time context, search access and a discoverable `From friends` control. Map is directly available. No opening questionnaire. | A paragraph explaining Vesper on every visit; a toolbar requiring several choices before value appears |
| First substantive view | Lead with something worth understanding or considering in the selected scope. It may be one strong contribution or a useful comparison; no mandatory Home-style crown. | A saved-item count, generic welcome, blank map or several navigation tiles as the entire payoff |
| Main scroll | A coherent assortment of possibilities, events, useful place information, explanations and eligible human material. Group only when it helps compare or navigate. | A section per producer, equal representation of four moves, a prose article per item |
| Deeper exploration | Compact alternatives, related areas/questions and deliberate ways to expand the exploration. Preserve the selected context. | Requiring a Keep, plan, reply or contribution to see more |
| Selection and return | Open the appropriate existing entity, event, reading or route destination and return to the same exploration, unless changed evidence requires an explicit update. | A new detail-page system, an unrelated feed after Back, or silent restoration of withdrawn material |

**Default to a content-led field with an immediately available map.** A small map
preview belongs only where it adds orientation; do not spend the first viewport
on cartography by default. Deliberate map entry and map-based returns may remain
map-led. The design comparison can overturn this default if the same sparse and
dense fixtures show a better independent visit; it must not compare a rich map
with an intentionally impoverished field.

**Content-led does not mean article-led or culture-led.** The exhibition in §6.5
is one comparison fixture, not Places' permanent editorial personality. A restaurant,
event, outdoor possibility, useful spatial relationship or original human contribution
can lead when the question and evidence justify it. Nor must a generated unit outrank
ordinary useful world information. Compare equally rich map- and content-led treatments
using the same evidence before settling the default.

**Search is a way to express the question, not a replacement for Chat.** Support
ordinary place/category/time queries and useful natural-language constraints in
the exploration. Use contextual Ask for deeper explanation or assistance, carrying
the selected subject and applicable context where supported. No second conversational
history or Chat redesign is implied. Clearing search returns to its parent scope.

**Friends is an intentional scope, not an automatic personality mode.** Keep its
entry discoverable even when no social material deserves default prominence. In
the friends scope, show authorized human contributions first because the person
explicitly chose that question. Offer a clear return to broader exploration.
Outside that scope, social evidence can enrich a relevant place or possibility,
but the existence of two friend marks must not silently switch the whole page.
No-supply treatment says what is unavailable in this scope, not that the world is
empty; it must not pressure the person to invite or post.

**“From friends” and “with someone” are not interchangeable.** The former is
receiving what people shared; the latter is exploring a possibility together using
authorized context. An existing dinner can shape nearby options without turning
Places into its planning workspace. Keep the chosen context legible without
requiring an occasion-specific profile or assuming the other person is available.
Selection of a lens grants no new access, sends nothing and adopts no arrangement.
Actual shared changes remain with the existing arrangement/participation owner.

These are not four permanent feed sections. Keep geography, time, current question
and chosen social scope understandable while varying selection and depth.

### 6.2 Map/list agreement and changes of intent

- Toggling map and field preserves the question, selected scope, filters and
  represented result set. Different visual density need not mean different inventory.
- Not every useful unit has a point. An area can use an outline, a route a line,
  and a city-level share its stated precision; an explanation can remain in the
  accompanying list. Never fabricate a coordinate to make the map look complete.
- Panning is inspection until the person deliberately requests a new area. Make
  that transition legible; exact control wording and treatment are design choices.
  Do not refresh the inventory simply because the map moved.
- Changing city, query, time or friends scope may deliberately establish a new
  result set. Show which question now governs the results. Exploring Sorrento from
  NYC does not change physical origin or grant location access.
- Back restores scope, query/time, display form, selected item and position where
  still valid. Changed/withdrawn material updates locally without silently replacing
  the rest of the exploration or resurrecting the old share.

The [existing result-set contract](places-result-set-context-contract-2026-09-05.md)
and current implementation supply the receiving boundary. Design needs a visible
intent model, not a new client-side query owner.
