### Design question and donors

Can ordinary personal photographs coexist with Vesper's editorial and practical
value without being restyled into brand imagery or wrapped in unnecessary text?
Extend **13 · Photograph Treatments**, **09 · Forms**, the existing human-original
receiving example and selected **02/03** full scrolls. Board 13 already keeps
personal pictures untreated; do not reassign that decision as missing work.

Test the same eligible material in three treatments, then select by actual job:

- **Original-only:** the picture or a small set, recognizable author, and only
  useful context. Enjoying a friend's moment and leaving is complete value.
- **Light context:** an existing occasion, place, time or the person's own line
  helps the recipient understand the material. No machine-written restatement
  of the obvious or inference about what the photographer felt.
- **Earned enrichment:** a supported comparison, sequence, explanation or practical
  possibility contributes something beyond the image. Keep the original accessible
  and distinguish Vesper's addition from the human author's words or endorsement.

These are comparisons, not three required feed sections or a per-open quota.
Original-only is not a deficient baseline that AI must beat. If no new contribution
is warranted, show the original or leave it in its record; do not generate filler.

Use the same imperfect-photo fixture as Life/Social, including mixed aspect ratios,

## Overall judgment

Keep the direction. Home now delivers useful and enjoyable things rather than
primarily reporting what Vesper knows or asking the person to contribute more.
The remaining work is to make adaptive usefulness as strong as breadth and
continuity: what becomes easier, more interesting or newly possible because this
person used Vesper?

The risk is an elegant personal almanac: attractive explanations, dates and
familiar names without enough improvement in judgment or reduction in effort.
Several current encounters escape that risk; the revision should preserve them.

Home's primary question is:

> What is worth receiving, knowing or doing in my life now and next?

This includes practical help, possibilities, understanding, human contributions
and useful continuity. It is not one product grammar per tab, a compulsory daily
briefing, a trip dashboard or a retrospective archive.

## What to preserve

### Value arrives on the surface

The selected ordinary and post-return screens contain a concrete afternoon
possibility, a cooking mechanism, a coastal comparison, an addressed contribution
and compact practical updates. These are genuinely different benefits, not just
different headlines on recommendation cards. Preserve the substance of the
explanation and the usefulness of the possibility. [Boards 02–04, 08b]

The sparse Sorrento-note case is especially important. One observation can lead
to a comparison with somewhere the person never mentioned. It demonstrates an
opening into the world without requiring an extensive personal archive. Do not
replace it with a callback to a connection the person already made.

### Social contributions can be the whole reward

After dinner, Maya's photograph captures an hour the person's camera missed.
Alex's receipt identifies the wine. Neither requires an AI interpretation,
reflection prompt or reciprocal contribution. These are strong expressions of
lightweight participation with asymmetric effort and mutual value. [Board 08b]

Keep these alongside practical recommendations and coordination. Friends must
not become merely suppliers of local tips or people to organize.

### The encounters continue coherently

A photograph opens the photograph; a guide opens the route; a ticket opens the
pass. Saving the guide is distinct from arranging it with Maya. A saved guide
reopens as itself, with a separate current check rather than pretending its old
facts are current. These decisions materially improve the product. [Board 08b]

### Practical intelligence belongs in ordinary life

Tomorrow's ferry, the way home from a show and the quiet claim-status row make the
live engine understandable outside emergencies. Keep this capability visible
without turning the whole surface into operational management. [Boards 08, 08c]

## Recommended changes

### H1. Distinguish urgent attention from an available window

**Observation:** Board 08 compares a rigid live/recovery hierarchy with an
alternative that preserves accessible actions and restores breadth while waiting.
In the airport example, the ticket compacts when nothing requires attention for
several hours; food, a friend's note, reading and future possibilities return.

**Recommendation:** Select that alternative for a proposed canon amendment. The
important distinction is whether something needs the person's attention, not
simply whether a ticket is live. Keep the departure-rush treatment when it earns
dominance. Do not let an unchanged live object monopolize an otherwise free window.

Likewise, distinguish the response the app recommends from the actions the person
can still access. A lower-ranked decision should not become inaccessible until
the person completes a different recommended action. “Recovery never loses” is
an understandable fallback ordering, not a sufficient universal attention rule.

**Revision should show:** the same commitment during a rush, an uneventful wait,
and a wait with another time-sensitive decision. Make the primary demand clear
without disabling unrelated agency. Do not treat board 08's proposed alternative
as already adopted.

### H2. Make free time feel open, not finished

**Observation, qualified after the second cross-project pass:** The trip-day
strip deliberately stops at six and leaves the evening open. This bounds the
strip, not necessarily Home's value. Board 08c's morning page already contains
Worth knowing and continuity below it. No after-six receiving frame establishes
that the design suppresses all optional value. [Boards 03, 08c]

**Recommendation:** Preserve the uncommitted evening. Compare an evening open
with a worthwhile optional offering against a complete low-demand ending when
nothing else earns a place. Do not require another card to make free time valid.
No obligation, scheduled activity or required response follows. Quiet should
describe the aesthetic and absence of pressure, not deliberately withheld value.

The same applies after returning home: the trip becomes one source of context
among many, while the person's week and future remain open.

**Revision should show:** what Home offers on an ordinary evening with nothing
scheduled, and why that selection is worthwhile. This is an untested receiving
comparison, not an established defect in the strip's stopping rule; it must feel
complete if ignored.

### H3. Keep prepared possibilities from becoming unsolicited assignments

**Observation:** “Today, in order” makes the afternoon tangible but can read as a
prescribed itinerary. The bakery at 10:30 and low water at 1:40, connected by a
nine-minute bike leg, need to read as two windows rather than a continuous
optimized morning. [Boards 02, 08b]

**Recommendation:** Keep the specificity and useful preparation. Clarify the
optional relationship between the opportunities. Do not fill the intervening
hours simply to make the sequence look complete, or imply that a suggested route
is now an agreed plan.

**Revision should show:** what someone receives by glancing, what opens on tap,
and the separate action that would turn the possibility into an arrangement.

### H4. Lead first use with useful value, not mainly a demonstration

**Observation:** The cold Home screen prominently features a made-up ticket. It
communicates capability honestly, but occupies much of the first reward. Places'
cold field offers a stronger example of immediate value. [Board 04]

**Recommendation:** Deliver something worthwhile from the available context
first. Keep a compact, reusable, clearly marked demonstration of what bringing
one thing can unlock. Avoid making onboarding, setup or the demonstration itself
the main experience of Home.

**Revision should show:** a newcomer with only a chosen city; then the same
person after bringing one item. The second state should improve the help, not
merely add a receipt or more personalized wording.

### H5. Keep contextual conversation easy without adding input homework

**Observation:** Board 12 provides thoughtful explanation and provenance, but
secondary conversation can require opening a “Why this?” sheet first.

**Recommendation:** Preserve a clean output-oriented Home. On the opened article,
place or contribution, provide a quiet contextual Ask route that carries the
object and relevant context into Chat. An ordinary question should not require
inspecting the recommendation system. Do not add a chat prompt to every card.

**Revision should show:** one casual question from an opened result and a return
to the original position. This specifies the seam, not a redesign of Chat.

### H6. Remove repeated reading, not the substance of the feed

**Observation:** Some units repeat the same point through a heading, metadata,
explanatory sentence and another consequence or source line. Photographs, pass
anatomy and sequences help, but do not automatically remove repetition.

**Recommendation:** Keep a generous optional scroll. Remove duplicated
orientation and explanation before cutting useful mechanisms, human voice or
unexpected connections. A visual earns its place when it makes a relationship
easier to understand, not simply because a section needs an instrument.

The rendered 393px-wide ordinary Sunday interior measured approximately 2,041px
and 352 whitespace-separated tokens; quiet Thursday approximately 1,438px and
170. These include navigation text and are browser diagnostics, not readability
thresholds, native-device measurements or user-performance evidence.

### H7. Do not make photo treatment carry provenance by itself

**Observation:** The latest response selects warm-paper treatment for reference
images and leaves personal originals untreated. It also proposes that the grade
communicates “reference” without an explicit label. [Board 13; response §15]

**Recommendation:** Keep the aesthetic direction, but retain a small readable
author/source distinction where it matters. A friend's original is neither the
user's photograph nor a world reference. A filter is not a reliable ownership
language, and it must not obscure visual evidence in food, geology or other
explanations.

Many photo slots remain placeholders. Their completed emotional effect is still
unresolved. The 1.3× text study is useful, but small SVG labels and object-print
exceptions are not proven accessible by that study. Native text and layouts need
to adapt across supported settings; see [Apple's Dynamic Type guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10074/).

These are coverage judgments, not completion percentages. A capability shown on

such as every household mode or every medium be launch requirements.

### H8. Distinguish a useful answer from a workable outcome

Board 12's request to put the pier before the bakery receives an accommodating
answer while retaining a bread-availability risk. The better question is whether
the revised afternoon still accomplishes what the person wanted. If bread was
the reason for the stop, acknowledge the tradeoff and offer an actually suitable
alternative; do not make the user reconcile the original recommendation's
constraints after accepting a friendly answer.

The companion Places review identifies a larger version: dinner changes around
a performance whose seats remain unconfirmed. Home must receive the resulting
state honestly, not turn a conditional possibility into a settled evening.

Design implication: show the useful conclusion, the one dependency that changes
the decision, and the least-work next step. This is not a request for a booking
flow, itinerary editor or exhaustive operational dashboard. The live engine's
value is that the whole option makes sense, not that many individual facts have
been checked. [Home board 12; Places board 02]

### H9. Let the person steer the help without assigning them a questionnaire

“Not this again,” “not tonight,” “less walking,” and “I want to choose for myself”
are different instructions. Board 12 offers explanation, correction and private
continuation, but does not clearly demonstrate those differences in the resulting
Home. A rejection should not be the only cheap way to reshape assistance.

Show one optional, contextual continuation after an already useful result. For
example, “Closer and somewhere we can talk” should change the offered evening,
not ask the person to maintain a social or preference profile. Scope a temporary
request to the occasion unless the person actually expresses a broader preference.
Do not infer that an empty calendar means energy, receptiveness or permission.

This preserves Home as an output surface. It makes assistance steerable when
wanted; it does not add a standing input prompt or require a response to receive
the next useful thing. Efficient access to assistance is also an established HCI
principle, but the particular interaction remains a Vesper design recommendation.
[Microsoft HAX: efficient invocation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/support-efficient-invocation/)

### H10. Generalize practical care beyond the trip wrapper

Board 01 restricts the temporal strip to trip days and the work receipt to a
position underneath that strip, never alone. Those are overly specific rules
for potentially general value. A Saturday gathering, an ordinary appointment or
another entrusted commitment can also benefit from time orientation and a useful
check. This does not mean every day needs a timeline.

Allow the relevant object's practical result to appear where it helps, without
requiring a trip-shaped composition. Reuse the underlying presentation and owner;
do not invent a separate kind for every life situation. Preserve the first pass's
distinction between an optional prepared day and adopted commitments.

Also distinguish “checked at 6:12” from “watching until departure.” Board 08c's
checks and board 01's watching language imply different levels of ongoing help.
The person should understand what they have entrusted, what remains their job,
and how to inspect or stop ongoing help through its existing object. A timestamp
alone does not explain that service. Do not silently settle unsupported monitoring
or action promises through mock copy. [Boards 01, 08, 08c]

### H11. Show shared judgment, not only shared material

The trip-forming board already uses participant information, and the joiner
encounter already permits lightweight participation. It would be inaccurate to
say multiplayer intelligence is absent. The missing emphasis is an ordinary
shared experience in which that intelligence improves the outcome.

Use one existing evening: a friend can join dinner but not the earlier activity;
someone wants somewhere conducive to conversation; the host should not have to
translate every constraint into a new plan. Show a sensible shared shape and the
individual's useful next step, without exposing another person's private reasons.
The contribution might be presence, a small commitment or a boundary—not another
photo or a form. Keep the enjoyable human material alongside this practical care.
[Board 05; Multiplayer Product Strategy]

### H12. Make accumulated attention improve the next encounter

Board 08b supplies meaningful one-input value and exact-object recall; board 10
already transfers a Rome/Paris observation into a New York possibility. Extend
those strengths into one cross-day encounter:

1. A day-one observation receives a complete useful answer immediately.
2. A day-two explanation or new source changes what is understood.
3. A day-five situation receives better help because relevant context persists.

The day-five benefit could be a more suitable approach to a place, a different
comparison, a cooking application or a friend's contrasting experience. It should
contain new work, not repeat the original insight with a later date. The person
may ignore the intervening material without accumulating unfinished business.
Use existing kept material and the supported continuity model; do not settle the
remaining conversational-continuity policy by implication.

This is not a serialized lesson, reflection habit, feedback survey or mandatory
learning objective. Familiar pleasure, practical relief and doing nothing remain
valid outcomes. [Boards 08b, 10–11; Personal Experience Loop]

### What not to add in response

Do not add a section for every gap above. Do not equate a longer feed with more
work: optional depth and an immediately useful first reading can coexist. Do not
bring Life's archive management or Chat's input workspace onto Home to demonstrate
system breadth. Short audio may suit an encounter, but a podcast catalog, more
generated prose and more permanent card kinds are not required corrections.

The architecture implication is shared context-sensitive composition, continuing
objects and appropriate practical owners—not a new Home-owned planner, social
profile system or memory store. Native implementation was not re-audited in this
second pass; these are requirements to reconcile with the existing owners.

## Component-by-component product-fit audit

This audit covers all **38 named kinds** in Home board 01, its three explicitly
non-union specimens, and the additional forms on boards 09/09b. It evaluates
product fit, not the accuracy of the index's BUILD/ADAPT/EXISTS grades. The
export calls its union 38 kinds; the workspace build manifest still calls it 35.
The three additions are `now_temporal_strip`, `now_work_receipt` and
`motion_money_row`. Their presence in a drawing does not establish admission or
implementation.[^home-components]

The central distinction is **useful capability versus distinct component**.
Keep semantic distinctions where they affect truth, action or lifecycle, while
sharing rendering when the perceptual job is the same. “Merge” below means a
presentation-family recommendation, not permission to delete a wire kind,
collapse owners or replace the bounded union with arbitrary generated UI.

Assessment terms:

- **Keep:** strong fit; preserve the benefit and existing owner.
- **Reshape:** valuable job, but the specimen or rule undermines it.
- **Merge:** retain meaning using a shared visual family or composition policy.
- **Conditional:** appear only when the current job warrants it, not to fill a slot.
- **Relocate:** retain the capability, but not as a default Home unit.
- **Remove specimen:** this example does not yet deliver its claimed value; the
  underlying capability may remain useful.

### Recommended component architecture, not a schema migration

The index should become a **crosswalk**, not a mandate to build 38 unrelated
widgets. Reuse the following presentation families across roots, with semantic
adapters retaining the current owners and typed states:[^home-expression]

1. **Orientation and navigation:** root context, destination and exact object link.
2. **Object state and result:** commitment, occasion, money, changed status, receipt.
3. **Possibility and decision:** alternatives, preparation and consequence preview.
4. **Authored material:** original photo, note, offer and plural perspectives.
5. **Understanding and evidence:** explanation, comparison, excerpt and annotation.
6. **Sequence and spatial instruments:** order, timing, access and grounded geometry.

These are conceptual reuse groups, not six universal card templates or a new
canonical inventory. A family may need several bounded primitives. Meanwhile,
dominance, grouping, folding, “since you looked” and merged orientation are
composition policies; they should not spawn duplicate content producers.

Three component capabilities need clearer examples, not necessarily new kinds:

- **Steer a received result:** an optional contextual adjustment that visibly
  changes the help without turning Home into an input form.
- **Receive original non-text material and a useful excerpt:** a friend’s photo,
  a source passage or a map should not always become a serif quote or essay teaser.
- **Continue a practical or social consequence:** preserve the chosen subject,
  relevant participants and remaining work through the existing owner, then return
  to the useful updated state.

Cost follows the job, not the apparent simplicity of the component. State rows
mostly assemble owner data; human units retrieve authorized originals; sourced
world methods can be reused; private comparisons require selective synthesis;
live instruments need fresh checks. “Cheap to render” is not “cheap to supply.”
Separate these production classes from the presentation families and avoid
generating a new piece because a page has another empty slot.

### Highest-priority component revisions

1. Replace the capability-field specimen and remove the special voice-horizon
   slot; preserve their possible benefits through ordinary useful forms.
2. Generalize practical orientation and receipts beyond trip-only placement.
3. Make comparison, alternatives and methods do more perceptual work with less
   repeated prose; keep optional depth and wanted choice.
4. Consolidate overlapping occasion/status/receipt and human-material rendering
   without erasing action or audience distinctions.
5. Make the index identify selected form, visual family, semantic owner, relevant
   state and a complete receiving encounter. Do not use stale build grades as a
   priority or completion score.
