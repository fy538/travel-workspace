# Claude Design handoff — Life: value and continuity refinement

## 1. Assignment and overall judgment

Revise the existing **Life** project in place. It should become the primary
design reference for Life. Preserve **Vesper — Life & Anchors** as the supporting
archive; do not create a third project or another collection of alternatives.

The consolidation is a substantial improvement: fourteen boards make the product
much easier to understand than the older 121-canvas lab. The main remaining work
is inside the selected experiences. Some stronger behavior was lost when older
sequences were replaced by reused root and detail screens. Restore that substance
within the cleaner project organization.

**The intended result:** someone can open these boards and see what Life gives
them, how to reach something they remember, what another person adds, and how
their record evolves without management work.

This is a design revision. It does not authorize production implementation,
engineering activation, repository cleanup, new source-retention policy, or
approval of pending product decisions. Do not interpret Current as shipped.

## 3. Product direction to preserve

Vesper's moves are **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.**
They can appear across all four application roots; they are not a required
sequence or one move assigned to each tab.

Home and Places deliver useful output for current life and its possibilities.
Chat makes giving, asking, and expressing changes effortless. Life makes durable
experience recognizable, findable, understandable, and useful again across time,
places, people, and threads. Life can deliver value through factual organization,
retrieval, human expression, or synthesis; every opening need not contain a novel
AI insight.

Preserve:

- One corpus with four first-class Life lenses: Time, Places, Threads, People.
- The selected paper, typography, row, chip, and object language.
- The distinction between a personal place relationship in Life and the world-facing Places tab.
- Direct original access and search alongside organized records.
- Rich, ordinary, thin, and zero-record cases, without filing or reflection tasks.
- Conditional page sections, stable identities, and return to the entry context.
- Attributed social contributions within the same records people already use.
- Current / Review / Later labels outside the customer screen.
- Contextual Chat/Home excerpts, with their full designs remaining in their own lanes.

No new reflection questions, completion meters, review inboxes, relationship
profiles, or prompts to enrich the record should result from this pass. Reduce
the work needed to receive value without reducing the product to travel tickets
or one isolated loop.

## 6. Review bar and deliverables

Inspect the selected pages at their intended phone width and across the full
scroll. Check longer titles and larger text where practical. Use approved fixture
media or visibly identified placeholders; do not represent illustrations as the
person's actual photographs. State any remaining media or interaction gaps.

2. Does a friend's contribution show something from that person?

5. Can I recognize what changed after an import or correction?

### Remaining correction 1 — The later Return needs additional value

**Target:** `06 - Give once, get value back.dc.html`.

Chat already explains the starch-water emulsion. The question page repeats the
finishing mechanism. The later Return, “Why the pasta stayed creamy,” supplies
essentially the same explanation while the board describes it as something the
person did not know.

The revised sequence has more coherent evidence, but not a meaningful increment
in value. Reopening a saved explanation is useful retrieval. Presenting it later
as a new Return needs a material reason beyond its continued existence.

Choose one resolution within the existing board:

- Give the later Return a supported new distinction, comparison, or practical
  application, and show the additional evidence that enables it.
- If the intended benefit is refinding, depict deliberate reopening and name
  that benefit accurately instead of claiming novelty.
- If nothing new warrants a proactive Return, omit that Return from the sequence.

Do not weaken or postpone the immediate Chat answer to manufacture a later
payoff. Do not invent a new factual claim simply to fill this slot. Any new
content remains a labeled design fixture until its supporting sources are supplied.

**Acceptance:** a reviewer can point to precisely what the later moment adds,
or recognize it as useful retrieval without a claim of new understanding.

### Remaining correction 2 — Let the human contribution carry its own value

**Targets:** the shared-record composition in boards 03 and 07.

“Her answer was where … yours was when” forces a distinction the displayed note
does not support: Maya also describes timing, including abandoning afternoons and
staying by the river after eight. The formulation sounds more conclusive than
the material warrants.

Maya's note is already worthwhile. It can stand on its own. Retain a generated
comparison only if it reveals something supported beyond restating the two
accounts. Otherwise remove the comparison while preserving the note, its context,
and the optional human continuation. Update the withdrawal after-state accordingly
if no comparison remains.

**Acceptance:** the person receives something useful from Maya whether or not
Vesper has a further interpretation. No artificial contrast is needed to justify
the contribution's presence.

### Remaining correction 3 — Remove reviewer language from customer copy

**Targets:** selected detail, social, and search compositions and their repeats.

Still-visible examples include “NO FEELING INFERRED,” “NOTHING ELSE CLAIMED,”
“Nothing was invented to fill this,” and “what it means stays yours.” These
repeated assurances make the experience procedural and draw attention to the
system's restraint rather than the material the person came to see.

Keep useful attribution, actual action consequences, and uncertainty that affects
how someone should understand the result. Remove redundant declarations of design
principles. For example, “From Maya's note and your Rome record” provides relevant
context; “no feeling inferred” belongs in the design annotation.

**Acceptance:** with annotations hidden, the page speaks to the person's purpose.
It does not read as an explanation to reviewers of why the system is permitted
to show the page.

### Remaining correction 4 — Missing documentation should not become a task

**Targets:** the journey sequence in boards 03 and 03b; apply the same judgment
to contextual map treatments where relevant.

The new “No trace · Aug 18” row and empty placeholder risk presenting an
undocumented day as a hole to fill. A normal memory sequence can show the available
moments without an empty slot for every absent day.

Explain a gap when it changes the interpretation of the reconstruction—for
example, when the record cannot establish a route between two points. Do not
imply that an unevidenced leg happened, or draw a continuous route through an
unknown interval. For an ordinary selection of remembered moments, omit the
placeholder and use framing that does not claim exhaustive coverage.

**Acceptance:** the sequence remains honest about its scope while living without
documenting feels normal. There is no invitation, explicit or implied, to repair
an incomplete life record.
