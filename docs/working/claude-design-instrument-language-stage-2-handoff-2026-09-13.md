# Vesper — instrument language, Stage 2

## 1. Assignment: consolidate a language, not another app

**Work in the existing shared design-language workbench. Add a clearly separated
Stage 2 canvas group. Preserve Stage 1 and the six selected product designs.**

Vesper should make relationships, possibilities and practical situations easier
to grasp through substantial, intuitive visual forms. We already have many
good examples. This assignment turns them into a coherent, composable language
and demonstrates how that language works in real product compositions.

The intended outcome is not a chart library, a cockpit, or a universal card.
It is a recognizable family of ways to **see something worthwhile, understand
its structure, and sometimes work with it**. A person should usually receive
value before touching anything. Interaction is an optional extension, not an
entrance fee to understanding.

The founder wants consolidation and obvious aesthetic cleanup, not repeated
optimization of already-good compositions. Preserve warmth, visual substance,
human originals and generous value. Do not reduce the product to thin lines,
monospace labels and operational status. Conversely, “full-bodied” does not mean
thickening every stroke, adding gauges, or simulating physical hardware.

**Authorized here:** source investigation, Stage 2 design canvases, shared
prototype components, selected interaction demonstrations, native handoff mapping
and explicit recommendations. **Not authorized:** production app/backend edits,
new runtime schemas, service expansion, six-project migration, replacement of
accepted designs, or publishing a new shared package as accepted.

This brief is a design assignment, not a new product or runtime authority.

## 3. What the second research pass changes

### We have complementary strengths, not a blank slate

**Home has breadth.** Its R3 catalog names 23 instruments, including time bands,
curves, quantities, comparisons and discrete records. It explicitly calls its
data fixture invention. This is a visual vocabulary, not evidence that 23
families are selected, implemented, or required. Home's Forms canvas also shows
an important counterexample: a human original, a useful method or a plain fact
can be the best form. A ticket can have an adjacent instrument without itself
being transformed into an instrument.

**Places has richer construction.** The component canvas combines time, daylight,
a planned visit and a constraint in a single legible day line. Its selected day
line replaced an older two-curve chart: do not revive the older chart because
it looks more technically elaborate. “Two ways in” compares access on the same
minute scale and separates movement from waiting. Cross-sections explain a
mechanism rather than decorating a recommendation.

**Plans has interaction semantics.** Provider times, personal plan times and loose
intentions look different. List/map selection preserves the same stop. A proposed
lighter day is not already applied, and changing a plan does not alter a provider
ticket. These distinctions belong in reusable behavior, not merely captions.

**Life and Social prevent dashboard drift.** Chronology can organize tickets,
photographs and incomplete evidence without inventing a continuous trace.
Another person's actual words or photograph can add more than another graph.
Human authorship and uneven contribution are features, not missing structured data.

**The shared package does not yet consolidate this whole layer.** Its reusable
readers, tickets, identities, actions and notices are valuable. Instrument
geometry and compound construction remain distributed among donors. Matching
tokens or copied files alone do not close that gap.

### Several older visual laws need scoped reconsideration

Do not silently rewrite the accepted kernel. Bring concrete before/after
counterexamples and recommend these limited amendments:

| Earlier constraint or temptation | Stage 2 recommendation to demonstrate |
| --- | --- |
| Always two labels / no legends | Keep labels economical; use enough direct labeling to understand a compound relationship without opening an explanation. A fixed count is not the goal. |
| An instrument never opens a destination | Separate inspecting a mark from an explicit object/source opening. Existing reconstruction and plan designs already need meaningful destinations. No hidden navigation on every mark. |
| Every neighboring instrument needs a different silhouette | Vary form when meaning changes. Repeated comparable values should often keep the same geometry and scale. |
| Curves imply physical truth | Use a curve only with an appropriate model/data basis; decorative smoothing must not imply observed dynamics. |
| Gold means the same data category everywhere | Preserve shared brand roles and local direct labels; do not assume gold universally means selected, safe, confirmed, busy and recommended. Resolve collisions within a composition. |
| Full-bodied means more geometry | Use area, grouping and physical structure when meaningful. Preserve the plain-fact and human-original controls. |
| Every instrument must be manipulated | Give a useful resting state. Add a control only when the user's intention and its effect are legible. |

These are proposals for selection, not retroactive permission to alter all donors.
Keep Entity 14 R2 and its “opened for Saturday” setup as an immutable reference.

## 4. Online research and its practical implications

The research supports a direction, not a proven Vesper outcome. The following
mixes primary studies, technical models and design practice; their evidence
strengths differ.

| Source | What we should borrow | Limit / what not to infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Beaudouin-Lafon, Instrumental Interaction, CHI 2000](https://www.lri.fr/~mbl/papers/CHI2000/) | Distinguish the thing a person acts on from the instrument mediating that action. Make effects and reusable operations understandable. | A conceptual interaction model, not an instruction to make all information interactive or skeuomorphic. |
| [Victor, Explorable Explanations](https://worrydream.com/ExplorableExplanations/) | Author a useful explanation, then let a person explore a meaningful variable or relationship in context. | Not a justification for widget-filled pages or controls backed by no real model. |
| [Vega-Lite composition](https://vega.github.io/vega-lite/docs/composition.html) | Shared scales, layering and repeated structures show how a small vocabulary can produce rich coherent forms. | Architectural inspiration, not a mandate to adopt this library or invent a server-driven layout language. |
| [Dynamic Prompt Middleware, CHIWORK 2025](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-dynamic-prompt-middleware.pdf) | Contextual controls can reduce the burden of inventing the next prompt and support refinement where needed. | The formative and controlled studies concern comprehension tasks, not Vesper retention; extra controls can also add reasoning complexity. |
| [Mobile visualization interaction, EuroVis 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11602) | Favor discoverable, in-context, recoverable mobile interactions; precision and gesture choice matter. | Small formative study, not validation of our particular controls. Do not make a hidden gesture the only path. |
| [Rich Screen Reader Experiences, EuroVis 2022](https://vis.csail.mit.edu/pubs/rich-screen-reader-vis-experiences/) | Accessible graphics need meaningful structure, description and navigation, not merely a generic image label. | Primarily web-focused work; a prototype is not native VoiceOver verification. |
| [Apple, Bring accessibility to charts](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10122/) | Support readable labels and non-color distinctions; convey the information through accessible alternatives. | Platform guidance does not automatically make custom SVGs accessible. |
| [Giorgia Lupi, Philosophy](https://giorgialupi.com/philosophy) | Keep qualitative context, human meaning and the incompleteness of data visible. | Design practice, not evidence that decorative complexity improves comprehension. |
| [NN/g, Direct Manipulation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/direct-manipulation/) | Visible objects, immediate feedback and reversibility can make interaction intuitive. | Direct manipulation can also become repetitive work; a slider is not automatically easier than an answer. |
| [Transit, product quick-start](https://help.transitapp.com/article/546-transit-6-0-quick-start-guide) | Study how overview, a selected journey and route detail remain connected. | An adjacent product example, not a reason to build a transit product or claim equivalent real-time capabilities. |

**Our synthesis:** quiet visual confidence, substantial information, optional
agency and meaningful composition belong together. Neither “everything is prose”
nor “everything is a manipulable diagram” serves this product.

## 5. The language to extract

Build the smallest reusable set supported by the examples. The layers below are
a reasoning model, not a required directory structure or new schema.

1. **Foundations:** typography roles, ink/ground/accent, spacing, stroke and fill,
   touch targets, motion and accessible contrast. Start from the selected family.
2. **Marks and layout primitives:** interval, point, region, path, threshold,
   boundary, source anchor, directly associated label, measured axis and
   deliberately unmeasured sequence. Similar-looking marks can have different
   semantics; share geometry without erasing those differences.
3. **Meaningful constructions:** time window, access comparison, physical
   section, qualitative juxtaposition, evidence sequence, process/method.
4. **Interaction recipes:** inspect, select, compare, explore a variable,
   preview a change, explicitly apply a supported change, return. Keep their
   different consequences visible.
5. **Product composition:** a useful title, instrument or other leading medium,
   essential interpretation, source/context and relevant optional continuation.
   A compound day line is one coherent leading medium, not automatically four
   competing media because it contains several marks.

Demonstrate reuse through at least three concrete decompositions: the same
interval/label machinery across an evening and an access comparison; source
anchors across evidence and explanation; selection/return across a map and a
plan list. Do not manufacture reuse where geometry or semantics differ.

### Design terminology is broader than runtime terminology

“Instrument language” here includes visual explanations that require no action.
The product's runtime **Instrument** family has a narrower operative meaning.
An article containing a section, a friend's original and a passive comparison
must not all be reclassified into that family. Preserve composition, source,
object, instrument and receipt distinctions from the owner contract.

AI should select supported meaning and provide adequate structured facts.
Client-owned components control layout, scales, accessible structure and gestures.
Do not ask a model to invent arbitrary pixels, CSS, physical telemetry or a new
control protocol for every user. Reuse authored structures and grounded data;
generate personalization where it changes value, not every decorative element.

## 6. Six complete compositions, not a specimen-only catalog

Use the same evidence when comparing treatments. Illustrative fixtures are fine
for design, but label them in the canvas notes and identify required production
inputs. Do not hide invented observations inside personalized-looking content.

### A. An ordinary New York evening

- **Value:** show a worthwhile possibility that actually fits the available time,
  not just “you have a free evening.” Use explicitly supplied time constraints.
- **Form:** time window with travel/visit/return relationships; enough ordinary
  language to understand why it fits. Include a compact Home and opened version.
- **Optional action:** inspect timing or explore a later departure. Show which
  derived timings change. If availability is not known, do not assert feasibility.
- **Boundary:** selecting or exploring is not scheduling, booking or authorizing
  monitoring. A supported plan update, if shown, has its own explicit action.
- **Control:** compare against a good concise text answer. The instrument must
  improve something material, not merely take more space.

### B. Two ways to reach the same place

- **Value:** make the tradeoff between movement and waiting apparent at rest.
- **Form:** use the Places donor's common minute scale, named origin and separate
  walk/wait/ride. Link the selected option to a map/list or destination detail.
- **Optional action:** select an option or inspect a leg; retain selection on return.
- **Boundary:** distinguish a schematic route from measured geographic geometry.
  The donor's “32 + 2” and “40 + 20” are fixture scenarios, not uncertainty ranges
  or current ETAs. Unknown wait time must not silently become zero.

### C. A mechanism worth understanding

- **Value:** explain something genuinely new through a section or causal structure,
  beyond repeating that the person noticed a cliff or visited a harbor.
- **Form:** preserve the strong Places/Entity cross-section construction. Offer a
  legible resting explanation and optional inspection of one meaningful feature.
- **Boundary:** name schematic geometry or different horizontal/vertical scales.
  A scale bar alone does not make the whole drawing physically accurate. Do not
  treat the donors' fixture geology, water curves or engineering claims as verified.
- **Control:** no slider unless changing it has a defensible modeled meaning.

### D. Something to try, not an interpretation of the person

- **Value:** a usable cooking method or substantive comparison connecting an
  observation to a technique the person can understand or try at home.
- **Form:** compare process, sequence or visible outcomes. Qualitative differences
  do not need invented numeric scores. A short method may outperform a chart.
- **Optional action:** inspect a step or choose a relevant alternative; no obligatory
  logging, personality inference or “tell us how this made you feel.”
- **Control:** include the good numbered-method form from Home, not only an
  intentionally weak paragraph used to make the graphic win.

### E. A journey reconstructed from what exists

- **Value:** a useful chronology of tickets, photographs and places, with direct
  access to the originals. A summary can itself be valuable.
- **Form:** compact Home return and organized Life detail, with source anchors and
  honest gaps. Keep the same object and evidence across densities.
- **Optional action:** open an original, inspect a date or correct a specific fact.
- **Boundary:** a ticket is not proof of boarding; no invented continuous route,
  personal productivity score or completeness pressure. Missing data is not a
  reason to make the entire record useless.

### F. Another person's perspective opens something

- **Value:** a friend's actual experience adds a new perspective or a low-pressure
  opportunity to reconnect. The recipient benefits without matching the effort.
- **Form:** human originals lead. A paired view can juxtapose two experiences with
  clear authorship; a small time/place structure may support, not replace, them.
- **Optional action:** a contextual reply or a gathering suggestion. If showing
  overlapping time windows, use explicitly shared availability, not inference.
- **Boundary:** no relationship scores, friend-location surveillance, compulsory
  profile completion or inferred consent. Social value is not another notification.

Across these six, show compact and opened states where meaningful, but not six
full root redesigns. Explore **two materially different constructions for two
genuinely unresolved cases**, not three arbitrary themes for every example.
Recommend winners and preserve donor compositions where they are already better.

## 7. Behavior, data and accessibility are part of the design

| Situation | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Display only | Value legible at rest; no fake handle or misleading tappable styling. |
| Inspection | Obvious target, explanation near context, predictable dismissal; no mutation. |
| Exploration | Immediate visible effects and a reset; distinguish a hypothetical result from an observed fact. |
| Proposed change | Preview the affected scope and retained facts. An explicit supported action applies it. |
| External continuation | Clear destination and return context; opening directions or a booking link does not imply Vesper executed the transaction. |
| Sparse/unknown data | Simplify, qualify or choose another useful medium. Never fabricate shape to fill a template. |
| Stale or failed practical input | Preserve what remains useful, distinguish unknown from unfavorable, and avoid a falsely current “fits” result. |
| Long names / narrow screen / larger text | Reflow labels and order; do not shrink the whole drawing or hide the comparison. |
| Accessibility | Non-color distinctions, meaningful reading order, summary and relevant values. Interactive marks have accessible names/actions. No drag-only essential operation. |
| Motion and touch | Use motion to explain a change, respect reduced motion, and provide comfortable touch targets independent of thin visual marks. |

For measured forms specify units, domain, baseline and scale-sharing. For
qualitative forms state that spacing does not encode quantity. Avoid accidental
precision, truncated comparisons and chart areas that imply unavailable evidence.

Draw at approximately 320 and 390 point widths and with a larger-text scenario.
These are design stress cases, not native accessibility certification. Test the
selected prototype controls; do not claim an illustration proves the gesture works.
Keep developer IDs and diagnostic authority rails out of ordinary user-facing UI.

- current native counterpart, adapt/new decision and reason;

Recommend incremental adoption through the actual receiving components. Do not
rebuild the whole app, create one giant configurable instrument, or duplicate wire
models. Consult mobile Task Intake and owner contracts before any later production

- Does the resting state give something worth receiving, without demanding input?
- Is the important relationship easier to understand than in the good text/original
  control—not merely more visually elaborate?

- Is it recognizably one visual family without making unlike things identical?
- Do shared primitives demonstrably reduce repeated bespoke construction?
- Does sparse evidence still yield useful, honest output?
- Are the ordinary evening and social examples as convincing as the travel examples?
- Can a native implementer reproduce the important geometry, hierarchy and states
  without inventing a second design system?

These are review criteria, not claims of completed newcomer research. Record
observed prototype behavior separately from hypotheses about comprehension.

### 11.9 Instrument polish audit — September 13

The four constructions were audited for polish and aesthetics, measured in a
browser rather than judged from a screenshot: label boxes that actually
intersect, intervals too short to read as intervals, mark geometry against the
track's centre line, contrast against the painted ground, and the vertical
rhythm between a track and its labels. Evidence is in
`/private/tmp/vesper-stage2-20260913/instrument-audit/`.

**Fixed.**

- **Labels could collide.** The first pass estimated label widths at render
  time, which is wrong as soon as the instrument is narrower than assumed or
  the reader's text is larger. `s2-labels.js` now measures the real widths in
  the width the instrument actually got and re-flows: a label sits at its mark,
  drops to the line below when its neighbour is in the way, and slides right
  only as a third choice. A `ResizeObserver` watches both the track and the
  labels, so a narrower column and a larger text setting both re-flow.
- **Last resort, stated.** When a narrow column at a large text size cannot
  name every mark, the trailing label is hidden rather than overlapped and
  marked `data-s2-dropped`. The marks, the scale and the sentence stay. At
  320pt and 1.3× text this happens once across all six compositions, on the
  method strip.

### 11.13 Change withdrawn — the library is eight, not nine

Founder note: *"there's gotta be better ways to represent change than a line
with no reference."* Correct, and the answer turned out to be subtraction
rather than a better drawing.

**What was wrong.** `S2Change` drew two dated values with a line between them.
Asked what the line was measured against, there is no answer: it floats in a
fixed 34px box with no baseline, so its vertical axis is invented outright.
Worse, the slope is clamped — `Math.min(18, Math.max(4, …))` — so a two per
cent move and a forty per cent move draw nearly the same picture. The code
defended this in its own comment as "the angle is capped so a tiny change never
looks like a collapse". That was written as a safeguard and should have been
read as a confession: **an angle that has to be clamped to stay honest was
never proportional to anything.** It broke honesty rule 1, drawn from values,
in the one construction whose entire subject was a quantity.

**What replaced it.** `S2Against` with a dated mark. Today's value is the bar,
the earlier reading is the mark, and the bar runs from a real zero — so the
length is proportional, the gap between bar end and mark is the change at
scale, and €36 off €248 finally looks like the modest move it is rather than a
descent. The second Change specimen ("18 minutes faster than usual") turned out
to be a plain Against already: "usually" is a published figure, which is to say
a mark, not an earlier reading of today. **Both** specimens redrew with no new
construction.

`S2Against`'s docblock and its refusal row were widened accordingly: the mark
may be a commitment you made, a published figure, or an earlier dated reading
of the same thing — never a target Vesper invented. It also gained a `reading`
prop (every other construction had one) and now seats a mark at the top of the
scale flush inside the right end instead of half outside it. A "no truncated
scale" refusal was added: the bar starts at zero, because starting it near the
mark turns every small difference into a cliff.

**Consequences across the group.**

- Library II is four constructions, not five. The library is **eight**.
- The mark alphabet on `S2-06` is **fifteen** marks; *slope* was struck out.
- A new honesty rule, promoted to number 2 because it is the most general:
  **a comparison needs a baseline, not a gesture.** Down-and-to-the-right is a
  feeling; a length from zero is a measurement.
- A new refusal: *no angle that is not proportional, and no truncated scale*.
  And a closing one: *no drawing at all where none is honest* — a quantity
  with no meaningful zero (a temperature, a date, a rating) gets two numbers
  and a sentence, which is a worse-looking answer and a truer one. That case
  is what the withdrawn slope was quietly papering over.
- No drawing library is needed by any of the four survivors. Change was the
  only one that wanted `react-native-svg`, so the native map no longer does.
- `S2Change.dc.html` is kept, marked WITHDRAWN in its own docblock, solely so
  the rejected specimen renders beside the argument in `S2-07` section 5. It
  is not part of the library and must not be ported.

`S2-07` section 5 is now the withdrawal itself: the rejected drawing in a
dashed oxblood frame, the corrected against-a-dated-mark beside it, the
misfiled second specimen beside that, and what went wrong stated precisely.
Showing the rejected form next to the argument is a better board than either
alone, and `S2-00` now lists "withdraw its own proposals, in public, with the
reason kept beside the rejected drawing" among what the group may do.

Verified by rendering and capture as before. `S2-06`, `S2-07`, `S2-08`, `S2-09`
and `S2-00` were all updated so the counts agree across the group.

### 11.14 The sentence, the correction, two refusals, and a linter

Founder direction: *do all* — the four brainstormed directions plus the two
fixes that fell out of the Change withdrawal. Everything below is delivered.

**The two fixes first, because they came from distrusting my own code.**

- *Silent floors.* `S2Track` padded any interval under 0.6% up to a visible
  width — about 2.3 minutes on a day line — so a thirty-second gap and a
  two-minute gap drew identically. `S2Level` did the same to a narrow band.
  These are the clamped slope's quieter siblings: a length that stops being
  proportional at the small end. Both now become a **point mark** below the
  floor, because at that scale the thing really is a moment rather than a
  duration. Not padded, not hidden.
- *The unchecked promise.* The style said a dropped label's content is carried
  by the sentence. Nothing verified it. Now something does.

**`s2-lint.js` — the structural rules, made checkable.** Not a runtime
dependency and no board loads it; the verification run injects it. It checks
provenance present, dropped labels carried by the sentence, at most two labels
per line, never more labels than marks, a 10px type floor, and nothing readable
set in ink80. It earned its place on the first run by finding two real things:

1. **The flagship composition broke my own label rule four times over.** The
   evening on S2-02 carried four labels where the style said "at most two on a
   drawing", three of them crowded onto the upper line — "LIGHT TILL 7:04" ran
   straight into "THE PIER", two facts reading as one phrase. A rule the best
   work violates four times is a slogan, not a rule; but the crowding was real.
   **The rule is now two labels per _line_**, the minimum gap went from 6px to
   14px, and the re-flow enforces it. "THE PIER" moved to the lower line,
   directly beneath the block it names, and the composition is better than it
   was before. The native `index.ts` docblock's "at most two labels" needs the
   same amendment; it is proposed on S2-09 and not made.
2. **A defect in itself.** It reported every S2-02 composition as having no
   source line. They all have one, at the *composition* level — a composition
   holding several instruments on a shared scale carries one source line for all
   of them. That is now written into the ladder on S2-06, where it belonged.

**`S2-10 The sentence` — the voice spec.** The reading line was the least
specified and most load-bearing part of the style: it is where the claim is
actually made, it is what survives a dropped label, and it is what a hurried
implementer gets wrong first. The board carries eight facts written twice —
once as a competent product would ("Price dropped 15% — book now!", "2 of 4
responded", "2 items overdue", "You haven't read in 10 weeks") and once as the
boards actually do — then twelve rules, a list of words that never appear, and
how numbers, tense and person work. Everything is mined from sentences already
standing on S2-02 to S2-08; nothing was invented for the board. The rules that
matter most: name the second term, fractions rather than percentages, no verbs
of judgement, no imperatives, dates rather than distance-from-now, state the
consequence especially when there is none, and "not yet" and "not at all" are
different sentences.

**`S2-11` — one construction kept, two proposals refused.**

- **Correction, kept.** What we said, when, what it is now, and why. No drawing,
  because a correction is four facts in order with no quantity to put on a
  scale. Two kinds that must never look alike, for the same reason unknown and
  unfavourable must not: **theirs** (the world changed — flat reportage, no
  oxblood) and **ours** (we misread — oxblood mark, first person, "we got this
  wrong"). Not dismissible: the only honest way for it to go away is for the
  person to have seen it. The refusals are the substance — no silent overwrite,
  no blaming the source for our error, no accuracy theatre, no strikethrough on
  a value that was true when we read it.
- **Absence, refused.** It already has three forms fitted to three shapes of
  missing thing: a stretch of time is a *named blank*, a hole in a record is a
  *gap row*, a countable thing is a *ghost stamp*. A fourth would be a second
  way to say one thing. Folded into honesty rule 4. One thing *was* genuinely
  missing — all three draw the same blank for "nobody has told us yet" and
  "nobody publishes this at all" — and no drawing can separate those, so it
  became rule 11 of the sentence spec instead.
- **Contingency, refused as drawn.** A branch is a claim about which future
  happens. Every version needed a probability or an implied likelihood to read.
  Drawn honestly it is not a prediction at all: the night bus is in the
  timetable whether or not you miss the last one, so the fallback goes on the
  **same scale as the plan, as another row**, and the reader does the branching.
  No new construction, no new mark. The one case that is not on a clock — "if
  the restaurant is full, the wine bar two doors down" — turns out to be the
  two-ways-in comparison from S2-02 B.

**Counts, now consistent across the group.** Nine constructions (four from
S2-05, four from Library II, one correction). Fifteen marks. Thirteen honesty
rules, seven earned by a correction, three checked by machine. Fifteen refusals.

**Verification.** Every board rendered fresh and captured at 2×. Lint run over

### 11.18 Ordinary life — seventeen compositions on S2-C Part II, September 14

The founder asked to brainstorm beyond travel into lifestyle, then "draw them
all out". The frame that survived: the lifestyle-app genre is the fitness
tracker in different clothes and runs on self-tracking, which the style refuses;
so the question became which lifestyle questions have values that are
*published or kept*. Seventeen compositions went onto `S2-C` as **Part II ·
ordinary life** (II.1–II.6), built from the nine constructions plus the bench's
almanac line. No new component.

**Compositions.** Kitchen: a recipe with hands-on time drawn apart from the
dough's (the waiting rule outside transport for the first time), a loaf across
a working day, tomatoes' season at a market. Garden/home: a seed packet (packet
line + year cycle), a tariff's off-peak window with the dishwasher inside it,
a meter reading against last quarter, bin days as a week. Documents/culture: a
passport against a consulate's six-month rule, papers with dates and a gap,
a library loan with its due ring and today, a museum's free Friday hours.
Sky/people: a field guide entry for swifts (packet line + season), swift/
swallow/martin as three authored rows (never a key — a key is a fork),
birthdays as a year cycle, "last saw Priya" as one dated row and a gap.
Health/study: a prescription course from the label (no adherence), two blood
pressure readings in the no-drawing form, a school year, an exam week in
hours.

**What it found.**
- **A mark has a side.** The passport (18 months left, 6 required) was the
  first against whose mark was a floor; `S2Against` painted the twelve months
  to spare as an overrun. It now takes `markKind` (ceiling | floor), oxblood
  covers exactly the wrong side, and the default keeps every earlier specimen
  unchanged. Amendment 10 on S2-D; noted on S2-A rule 5 and the Overrun mark.
- **The packet line.** A seed packet, a pharmacy label and a field guide entry
  all wanted the object's fixed facts printed before any drawing. That is the
  almanac line with a different source, used three times without a change —
  the strongest argument for graduating it as a pattern, not an instrument.
  The ladder on S2-A now says a published label may sit above the drawing as a
  source line.
- **The lifestyle refusal.** Sleep, steps, calories, mood, streaks, screen
  time, adherence, forecasts, anything the person must log to see: proposed as
  a sixteenth refusal on S2-A, *nothing that must be logged, scored or
  predicted*, and tabled on S2-C II.6.

**Verified.** S2-C: 44 instruments, `s2-lint.js` no findings, 17 lanes none

### 11.19 The register study — S2-C Part III, September 14

The founder's objection to Part II: "a lot of these use the same visual theme
as our previous designs, lines, square, etc." Diagnosis: half intended (a
language is few forms reused), half a failure of the research direction — the
boards borrowed the almanac/timetable/field-guide/survey-map *epistemics* but
not their *look*; every specimen was a rounded pill on a rounded track in a
shadowed card, and rounded caps are the fitness-tracker silhouette.

Two fixes kept separable, built as a four-way study on `S2-C` Part III
(III.1–III.5): the recipe, the swifts, the seed packet and the tariff, each
three ways — as drawn, in a **print register**, and in **the tradition's own
form**.

- `s2-print.css` (new, a study file): scoped under `.reg-print`, same
  components and values, only material — square ends, a hairline with domain
  end-ticks instead of a filled channel, hatching for waiting / part-month /
  overrun, tally strokes for counts, ruled abutting cells, serif reading line,
  double-rule page frame instead of a card. No component changed.
- Tradition forms, hand-set from the same values and stamped as such: a
  Bradshaw-style **timetable** for the recipe (six timed steps, totals under a
  rule); a **plate** for the swift (schematic silhouette, three numbered
  callouts, caption); the **seed packet's own two-row month table** (SOW /
  PICK across twelve ruled columns — needs no legend); the **tariff as a table**
  with the dishwasher as one gold row.

**What it showed.** The register does most of the work on the track and the
cycle and barely touches the sequence and packet line (already typographic).
It cannot make a strip carry six timed steps — the recipe wants the table —
and cannot supply an illustration, so the plate is a labelled schematic only.
The packet's month table beats the coloured cycle because it needs no legend.
**Lean:** adopt the register as the material of the language (fold into
s2.css / s2-lib.css; take the card-vs-rule frame question to Stage 1 as a
proposal, since the card is vdl.css); put the **table** on the bench as
candidate nine; graduate the two-row month table as the print form of a
two-state cycle; keep the plate as a named gap. Nothing adopted; Parts I–II
unchanged.

**Verified.** S2-C: 56 instruments, linter no findings, no lane over width,
no clipping, no new contrast finding; eight study cells captured at 2× and
read. Part II's one caption widow remains. The frame was then changed from
stacked inset shadows (inner rule did not render) to `border` + negative
`outline-offset`, re-captured, double rule visible. S2-D updated: the register
as amendment 11 (the material), the table as a proposed bench candidate, a
native-map row ("a decision, not a build"), and "decide the register" as the
first open item for the founder.

### 11.20 The bench, round two — eight forms the language did not have, September 14

The founder's objection after the seventeen ordinary-life compositions was
not about style: "a lot of these use the same visual theme … lines, squares"
meant *other types of instruments*, not another register (the register study
in 11.19 was a misreading of the same sentence and is kept as its own
finding). The nine constructions are a line, a row of cells, a bar and a
list; round two asked which relationships none of those can carry and built
a form for each, on `S2-B` Part III (§20–27), as bench candidates loading
