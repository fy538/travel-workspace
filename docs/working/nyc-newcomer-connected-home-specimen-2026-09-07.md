# A useful Home before Vesper knows much about you

## 1. Recommendation and scope

**Be world-rich before being history-rich. Become more personally useful through
what the person deliberately brings, asks and keeps—not by withholding value
until they document their life.**

The strongest next design object is one complete, source-backed encounter:
something worth doing, something worth understanding, practical preparation
inside those objects, and dependable access to something kept. A friend can
enrich this experience without becoming its prerequisite.

This is the grounded companion to [the existing newcomer and absence
manuscript](situated-value-decision-matrix-2026-09-06.md#14-three-independent-entrances-and-a-return-after-absence),
not a new product grammar, implementation program or launch-geography decision.
It replaces fictional world supply **for this specimen only**. Other fictional
venues in the matrix remain explicitly fictional.

[The fresh-eyes review in §9](#9-fresh-eyes-review-useful-but-not-yet-distinctive-enough)
qualifies the initial composition: this is a credible sparse-context supply
baseline, not an accepted final Home design. Cross-root continuity does not
mean a required four-tab journey.

The person, actions and friend message below are synthetic. The named venues,
program and factual explanations have primary-source support. This document is
not a live app result, participant study, visual mockup, seeded database or
certification of current availability. Research was conducted September 7,
2026. Later dates describe conditional behavior, not future observations.

Read alongside the [Product Model](../../travel-agent/docs/product/Product%20Model.md),
[Editorial and Content Canon](../../travel-agent/docs/product/Vesper%20Editorial%20and%20Content%20Canon.md),
[contribution contract](../systems/contribution-and-consequence.md), and
[Home implementation map](home-connected-experience-implementation-map-2026-09-04.md).
Detailed composition stays with the dedicated design tool. No application code
or effective retention policy changes in this pass.

## 2. Exact starting conditions

On August 31, our hypothetical person selected NYC as the area they wanted to
explore, asked two ordinary questions, and explicitly kept the Stavros Niarchos
Foundation Library (SNFL). They return on Monday, September 7, without adding
anything else. There are no friends on Vesper in the base case.

We know the selected area and exact saved entity. We do **not** know their
physical location, home address, free time, mobility, budget, emotional state,
weekend plans or interest in geology. A city selection is not live location.
Neither question is available as later personal context in this fixture:
Ask's current no-new-durable-state agreement applies. Do not invent the content
of those questions to make the page feel personalized.

Keeping SNFL is not evidence of visiting, liking libraries generally, intending
to go today or accepting a watch. It supports refinding and bounded relevant
context, not an inferred outing.

This return date has an actual consequence: NYPL's published 2026 holiday
schedule includes September 5–7. That is a source-supported closure, not an
invented disruption to make the engine look useful. Direct page retrieval was
blocked; the official page's search-indexed content supplied the schedule, so
it is a research-level source check, not a live operational verification.
[NYPL closing schedule](https://www.nypl.org/help/closings/).

## 3. The complete Home encounter

This is a reading order and content specification, not four required sections
or four identical cards. Four units are an illustrative finite composition,
not a production quota. No setup questionnaire, catch-up list, friend-invite
placeholder or recap of the two old questions appears.

### A. Lead with a worthwhile possibility

> **Twenty minutes on the water.**
>
> Pier 2, Brooklyn Bridge Park · Saturday, September 12 · free kayaking.
> Equipment and instruction included. September sessions run Saturdays.

Compact availability qualification: **Published session; reservation
availability not checked.** Weather-dependent operation belongs with the
relevant terms. Do not turn every limitation into another paragraph on Home.

One primary continuation: **See the session**, opening exact program/Place
depth, not a generic Brooklyn results page.

The person receives the shape of the possibility before tapping. “Twenty
minutes” describes the paddle, not the total outing. No journey duration,
fitness suitability, open reservation or personal fit is asserted.
The earlier “not an all-day outing” headline was removed because it implied
an assessed whole-journey burden that the fixture does not establish.

The dated park listing establishes September 12, 2026, 10 a.m.–3 p.m. at Pier 2.
It recommends advance registration, lists equipment and instruction, requires
waivers, and makes the event weather-dependent. Its recurring summer text is
broader than the operator's September-only-Saturdays notice. Use the specific
date and operator's seasonal rule, not the broad recurrence.
[Dated park listing](https://brooklynbridgepark.org/event/kayaking-saturdays-september-12/),
[Boathouse seasonal notice](https://www.bbpboathouse.org/).

**Vesper's contribution:** select a concrete opening and resolve a practical
source discrepancy. This is more than a label, but is not uniquely possible
with Vesper. It is a complete initial service whose continuation must earn the
stronger differentiation.

### B. Make a piece of the surrounding world newly legible

> **Those Hudson cliffs cooled underground.**
>
> The Palisades began as magma pushed between older rock layers—not lava
> pouring over the landscape. Erosion later exposed the hard rock's edge as
> the cliff face.

The explanation is complete on view. One optional continuation opens its
source-bound spatial reading. The Palisades are a regional subject around NYC,
not a claim that the user is nearby or should hike there today.

The park's geological account supports intrusion, cooling into diabase and
differential erosion. It does not establish current trail access or a route.
[Palisades geology](https://njpalisades.org/nature/).

**Vesper's contribution:** a small causal explanation that changes what a
familiar-looking landform means. It is a city-curated candidate, not a claim
of inferred personal interest or guaranteed novelty to this individual. If
there is eligible prior delivery evidence, avoid an identical repeat; do not
assume an impression means the person learned it.

### C. Give ordinary life practical depth, not just leisure suggestions

> **Choose the market day, not just the market.**
>
> Union Square's producer roster changes by day. GrowNYC lists Mushroom Queens
> on Monday and Bear Creek Farm's dahlias on Wednesday. The market runs Monday,
> Wednesday, Friday and Saturday—not every day.

One continuation: **See the day-by-day market**, with the published rosters
and their checked-at context. These are operator-listed participants, not
guaranteed stall attendance, stock or prices. The absence of a producer from
this short comparison does not mean it appears on no other day.
[GrowNYC market and producer lists](https://grownyc.org/locations/union-square-greenmarket/).

**Vesper's contribution:** expose a distinction hidden by one map pin. The
person can use the distinction without becoming an itinerary builder or
telling Vesper what they intend to cook. This diversifies the page beyond
events and cultural readings. Whether this specific unit earns its space is
still an editorial judgment, not a finding about consumer preference.

### D. Keep entrusted material useful, without turning it into work

> **Your saved library has a public rooftop.**
>
> SNFL · Fifth Avenue at 40th Street. Free public terrace.
> NYPL lists the library as closed September 5–7 for the holiday.

One continuation: open the exact kept Place. This combines a genuine saved
relationship with useful venue depth and a dated limitation. It does not say
“your visit is affected,” move the library into a Plan or ask for a new date.
No push is earned merely because a saved venue is closed.

NYPL identifies the rooftop as publicly accessible. This does not establish
today's terrace condition or promise a particular reopening time.
[NYPL description of the two Midtown libraries](https://www.nypl.org/about/locations/schwarzman).

If Vesper already delivered this rooftop fact, retain a compact named saved
Place door or a material update rather than repeating the whole explanation.
The current holiday qualifier expires; the save does not.

### Why this is one product, not four unrelated content cards

The page helps the person understand and use the world they selected. Each
unit is complete at first contact, can deepen against exact evidence, and can
become practical without restarting the task. Nothing forces a save, plan,
share or reflection to justify its existence.

The order is provisional. A declared immediate need changes it. The library
closure is not urgent in this base case because there is no intended visit.
The kayaking lead is an editorial bet, not something secretly deduced from two
expired questions. If the person says “I just want something interesting to
read,” deepen understanding and reduce outing prominence in the appropriate
scope; do not convert that moment into a permanent preference.

The honest risk remains: without good continuation, this could be a pleasant
NYC guide. Breadth alone does not establish Vesper's identity. The rest of the
journey must absorb reconstruction and make the useful object more capable.

## 4. Interest becomes depth and practical help without a restart

### Home → Places

Opening the paddle unit preserves **this session, this operator, this Place and
the source-backed terms**. Places supplies the exact location, program shape,
registration handoff and relevant limits. Program identity and session date
must not collapse into a venue bookmark. No current availability is inferred
from a listing.

Home's compact expression and Places' depth use the same underlying evidence.
Places is not a longer generic version of the Home caption. It gives spatial
and practical resolution, with optional contextual Ask. The provider remains
the owner of registration; Vesper does not revive booking infrastructure.

### Places → Chat

The person asks: **“Could I do this before lunch, without making a whole day of it?”**

A useful response first distinguishes the short activity from the uncertain
whole outing: travel, waiting and an actual slot determine whether it fits.
Then ask only for the missing anchor that changes the answer: **“Where would
you be starting, and what time is lunch?”** These are necessary constraints
for the volunteered practical question, not Home onboarding fields.

Once supplied, route and schedule evidence may support a bounded assessment.
Without a current route/slot check, do not invent a leave-by time or call the
outing feasible. The answer can still clarify the tradeoff and link to the
operator. “Ask Vesper” must carry the session and sources; the user should not
have to paste the event back into the conversation.

These headings describe available continuations, not mandatory navigation.
The same contextual question may begin directly from the Home object. Someone
who only wants to read can stop there; someone ready to inspect provider
sessions need not first visit both Places and Chat. Keep one primary
continuation appropriate to the displayed value, with other supported actions
in owned depth. Internal ownership must not become a user-visible relay race.

No Plan, booking or recurring preference is created by asking. If a person
explicitly wants a later reminder or shared arrangement, resolve that separately
through the supported owner and its actual responsibility. Do not offer a
button whose consequence has no executable contract.

### Optional retention → Life and Places

A concrete Place bookmark has an existing save owner. Retaining the dated
session or an explanation is a different request: it needs the exact supported
Source/reading/intent path, not a mislabeled Place save. A save receipt must say
what was kept, then make that exact thing recoverable.

Desired Life behavior: “Kept SNFL” remains findable through Places/time lenses
without becoming “Visited SNFL.” The canonical Place opens from that retained
relationship; any visit or authored experience requires its own evidence.
This integration is a requirement, **not certified by the current code pass**.
Today, the verified direct entity-save route points to Places' saved list.

An answer-only Chat result need not appear in Life. Retention must not become
the entry fee for immediate help. Equally, the product must not promise later
refinding of a result it did not retain.

### Return to Home

Back preserves orientation while the selected unit remains valid. Changed or
withdrawn content requires recomposition, not restoration of a stale promise.
The selection itself is not attendance, accepted intent or a reason to schedule
follow-up work.

## 5. Another week passes without participation

For the hypothetical September 14 return, do not fabricate a future researched
feed. Apply these explicit transformations to the same encounter:

| Earlier material | Later treatment | What must not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 12 program | Past-date offer leaves current prominence; any retained original remains historical | Attendance, disappointment, cancellation or unfinished work |
| Operator's remaining September program | A later session can become a new candidate only after rechecking that exact date and conditions | Rolling the old date forward or implying a slot is held |
| Palisades explanation | Suppress an unchanged repeat when eligible exposure history exists; otherwise no claim that it was read | Knowledge, enduring geological interest or negative preference from silence |
| Union Square roster | Preserve stable market identity; refresh roster-dependent detail before asserting current participants | That a recurring schedule guarantees attendance or inventory |
| Kept SNFL | Save remains findable; remove expired holiday wording and obtain new current facts when needed | A completed visit, ongoing monitoring or a fabricated personal memory |
| Two earlier Ask exchanges | No later personal reuse under this fixture's authority | Automatic durable continuity just because the UI once held a conversation |

Home owes no “you missed” digest. It should offer the best currently supported
value, not refill every old slot. An ordinary read may reuse prepared material;
it should not commission a new custom magazine because seven days elapsed.
When current supply is weaker, narrow the claim or choose a strong stable
explanation. Do not turn a failed supply check into a request that the user
upload more material.

## 6. Change one fact: a friend sends a recommendation

Hold the same city, date and private history constant. In this variation,
Maya is an authorized sender and deliberately sends the kayaking Place with
this **fictional human message**:

> “I liked that it was a short paddle, not a whole expedition. Thought of you.”

Keep Maya's words as Maya's words. Attach the current program facts separately;
do not manufacture a new AI message from her. Her enjoyment is a stated
perspective, not a promised outcome for the recipient. She has not disclosed
current location, invited the recipient to meet or committed to attend.

**Composition change:** the existing paddle unit can receive the note rather
than adding a duplicate activity card. Attribution and the message may be the
leading value. Preserve a discoverable authorized received-items path even if
the unit is not ranked prominently. The current addressed-note adapter can
serve original text; folding it coherently into an independently sourced
program remains a receiving/composition requirement.

The recipient can enjoy the message, inspect the Place, privately keep it,
ask Vesper a practical question, or do nothing. No reply requirement, view
counter, social score or symmetric contribution appears. An optional human
reply belongs to an actually supported message path; an external guest
experience is not certified by this authenticated scenario.

If the recipient chooses “Let's go together,” **that deliberate intention**
opens shared preparation. Minimum necessary constraints, real terms and human
consent can support an offline gathering. The recommendation itself does not
create an Occasion or authorize Vesper to contact Maya.

On withdrawal, remove permission-dependent note content. Independently sourced
public program facts can survive with their own basis. A private Place save
does not silently preserve withdrawn friend text.

## 8. What to carry into the existing integration plan

**First: agree the experience and its stopping points.** Review this entire
encounter—including the nonparticipating return and friend variation—without
explaining the four-move philosophy first. Does it offer enough substance? Does
the practical continuation feel like the same product? Can each encounter end
without maintenance? This is design review, not proof of demand.

**Second: reconcile the exact continuation seams.** Carry the dossier route,
place/session Chat grounding and save-to-Life mapping into the existing
Home/Places and integration inventories. Establish identities and receiving
contracts before polishing more card families. Route precision is bounded
implementation work; retention semantics must not be invented by navigation.

**Third: specify dependable supply for the whole composition.** Map each unit
to an existing producer/read, actual admitted evidence, smallest supported
renderer, refresh trigger and expiry. Shared geological explanations need not
be rewritten for each person. Operational facts require proportionate checks.
Private context changes selection or assessment only when available and
authorized. No new generator per tab and no production on every app open.

**Fourth: exercise the cross-root portfolio together.** Include a sourced
explanation, ordinary opportunity, kept object and human contribution, plus
expired conditions, missing supply, no new input, withdrawal and process
restart. Keep current architecture breadth; this specimen is one demanding
consumer of shared seams, not a mandate to rebuild around kayaking.

**Then hand content and interaction to design.** Choose visual hierarchy,
compression and native components in the dedicated design lane. Compare the
result against a capable assistant plus Maps, the operator sites and messaging.
Count re-explanation, checking, copying and repair—not merely screen count.

The strategic conclusion is encouraging but bounded: **the sparse-context
promise does not require magical personal knowledge. It requires good public
world material, real editorial judgment, exact continuity and useful practical
help.** The code has pieces of that chain. The next step-change is to make them
dependably serve one coherent, varied experience—not to invent more product
nouns or commission a larger volume of generated text.

## 9. Fresh-eyes review: useful, but not yet distinctive enough

**Design judgment, not consumer evidence:** the specimen is more credible than
the fictional feed, but it does not yet justify saying we have found Home's
finished shape. It offers four useful pieces of NYC material. Only one is
grounded in an explicit personal relationship, and all four still use a
headline-plus-explanation pattern. Source quality improved faster than the
distinctiveness of the encounter.

That is not a reason to invent more personal context. Nor must every first-use
result be impossible to get elsewhere. The first encounter must be worthwhile;
the connected experience must earn a reason to choose Vesper again.

### 9.1 What survives, and what needs sharpening

| Element | Keep | Sharpen before treating it as the design target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paddle possibility | A concrete activity shape, basic terms and reconciled schedule | Do not imply the whole outing is short. Name what was actually checked. A provider handoff is legitimate, but generic “check everything yourself” copy is not completed preparation. |
| Palisades explanation | Complete understanding without requiring an outing or response | It is legitimate city-curated material, not evidence of personal fit. Its distinctiveness is the explanation, not a compulsory “Go there” action or a geology-interest inference. |
| Market distinction | A practical difference that a single map pin hides | The day comparison should itself be usable; avoid sending the person to reconstruct two long rosters. Do not inflate this into a meal plan or an interest questionnaire. |
| Saved library | An exact object the person can trust the app to retain | Saving may mean the rooftop is already known. Do not sell that fact as a revelation. A closure without intended attendance is a small qualifier, not the page's urgent lead. |
| Friend variation | Human pleasure and an independently useful recommendation | Preserve the friend's perspective as visible value. Do not bury it under automated logistics, duplicate the activity, or require a shared plan to justify its presence. |

The recommendation is **not to remove all but one unit**. Keep a generous,
finite field of different value, but allow unequal expression and importance.
A concrete possibility, a short causal explanation, a compact day comparison
and a named saved object need not be four editorial essays. This is a
content-density brief for design, not a new renderer specification.

### 9.2 Reduce reconstruction, not human choice

There are three different kinds of effort in this encounter:

1. **Work Vesper can absorb:** resolving conflicting published schedules,
   distinguishing a session from its venue, assembling a useful comparison,
   preserving the selected question subject, and reopening the exact kept item.
2. **Choice that should remain human:** whether kayaking sounds enjoyable,
   whether to spend Saturday that way, and whether to involve a friend.
3. **Facts or actions with another owner:** reservation inventory and provider
   registration, or a genuinely unknown starting point for a route assessment.

Do not confuse the second category with friction to automate away. Do not use
the third as an excuse to return all of the first category to the person.
When Vesper cannot finish a check, return the useful work it did finish and
identify the remaining uncertainty. Do not imply a comprehensive service and
then deliver a directory of links.

Before routing the user elsewhere, ask: **Does the next interaction add value,
or is it making them reconstruct value that should already be here?**

The person may read a complete explanation on Home; expand a comparison
without starting a chat; ask about the selected paddle without copying it; or
follow the operator link when ready. These are independent stopping points.
An experience can be architecturally connected while the person uses only one
surface. Supported direct continuations are receiving requirements, not claims
that every corresponding affordance is already built.

### 9.3 Home and Places can overlap without being the same surface

In this deliberately thin fixture, most legitimate material is city-based.
Some overlap is appropriate. Do not fabricate a personal dashboard, add empty
social rows, or withhold good material from Places to force differentiation.

- **Home chooses what is worth this person's attention now**, using the
  context actually available. With little context, this selection is modest
  and largely curated. With more authorized context, a current question,
  practical consequence or human offering can change the selection.
- **Places lets the person explore the world on their own terms:** change
  area, inspect spatial relationships, compare options and deepen a particular
  place or reading, regardless of whether Home selected it.
- **Chat lets them redirect the work:** answer a question, challenge a claim,
  change constraints or bring something new. No need to consume Home first.
- **Life provides dependable continuity:** what was actually retained and how
  it relates across time, places and people. It does not need to be visited
  to complete every successful interaction.

The test is whether each root changes the person's orientation and available
work—not whether each displays an exclusive class of object. As context grows,
Home should become cross-life in its selection, not merely a narrower filter
on NYC attractions.

### 9.4 Two strategic limits remain visible

**The social example demonstrates receipt, not a complete social proposition.**
A friend's ordinary note can be enjoyable before any AI work. The next useful
social design comparison is receipt-only versus recipient-chosen joint action:
does the app make an actual gathering easier without making casual sharing
feel like an invitation? Keep this inside the existing social/arrangement
portfolio, not a new feed subsystem.

**The memory promise is still larger than the effective agreement.** The
accepted direction includes useful optional continuity from ordinary questions,
but this fixture correctly cannot use the earlier Ask exchanges. A delightful
city feed does not resolve that strategic gap. Continue the existing
[history and source-expiry decision packet](conversation-history-source-expiry-decision-proposal-2026-09-06.md)
and its understandable user agreement; do not quietly save the questions or
make every question require manual Keep. Until adoption, explain the current
product honestly and evaluate the no-history experience on its own merits.

The [September 7 packet extension](conversation-history-source-expiry-decision-proposal-2026-09-06.md#september-7-recommendation-remember-the-exploration-not-a-verdict-on-the-person)
now specifies the proposed agreement and day-1/day-2/day-5 comparisons. It
does not retroactively supply the two unavailable Ask exchanges in this
specimen or change its base evidence.

### 9.5 Decision and receiving handoff

Keep the specimen as a **whole-experience review case**, with corrected copy
and optional continuations. Do not adopt its four units as a fixed Home
template or its dated listings as production supply.

Carry four requirements into existing packages:

1. **Exact subject continuity:** reading → exact reading; question → the
   selected Place/session/source; return → still-valid originating context.
2. **Prepared value before another request:** inspect what current owners can
   resolve before displaying a unit; distinguish a published option from an
   assessed fit. Unknown information narrows the claim, not the person's right
   to receive other useful value.
3. **Retention that matches the gesture:** a Place, a dated offer and an
   explanation are not interchangeable saves. Life receives a projection of
   the proper retained owner; it must not create a second store.
4. **Complete, low-participation endings:** no action after reading; direct
   operator continuation; return after absence; receipt without reply; and
   an optional user-initiated shared arrangement.

The first and third belong with existing A–D/CV-4 consumer completion; the
second informs CV-2 practical judgment and CV-3 supply; the fourth is a
cross-package acceptance requirement. Integration retains sequencing authority.
Do not wait for final visual composition to correct a verified destination
defect, but do not implement a new retention meaning as a navigation fix.
