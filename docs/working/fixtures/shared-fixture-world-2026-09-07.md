## 7. Rules for using this ledger

- A board may not add a person, place, date or message without adding it here first.
- Fixture labels (A1 … C5) appear only outside the phone.
- Policy narration stays outside the phone (§14.5): no "nothing was saved" lines, no statements about what anyone felt; the few context labels that matter (who is asked, who sees this) may stay inside.
- A send to other people crosses an audience boundary (T2) and is carried by the one clear Send; it is never labelled a private T1 receipt.
- World facts render inside the phone only with the register the Places project uses for unverified facts ("listed hours, not checked today").
- Portraits and photographs are stand-ins and say so outside the phone.
