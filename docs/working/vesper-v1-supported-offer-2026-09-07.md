# Vesper v1 — a concrete supported-offer candidate

## 1. Recommendation

**Offer useful help with what catches someone's attention, worthwhile things to
discover, and less work turning a possibility into part of their life.** Questions,
places, things from friends and plans taking shape are recognizable entrances—not
four separate products or a mandatory sequence.

Candidate explanation for discussion, **not approved availability copy**:

> Vesper helps you make more of what catches your attention. Ask about something,
> explore worthwhile possibilities, or pick up something a friend shared—and get
> useful understanding and help making it work in real life. Keep what matters
> so you can return to it without starting over.

The intended added benefit of optional continuity is better judgment with less
re-explanation, not more references to the user's past. That belongs in the
public promise only to the extent the adopted agreement and delivered behavior
support it. A good first answer can stand on its own.

This is the working service specification requested after the
[four-area research](vesper-audience-offer-and-market-strategy-research-2026-09-06.md#19-four-consequential-strategy-questions--september-7).
It elaborates the [accepted strategy](../decisions/2026-09-06-reconcile-consumer-strategy.md),
not a new canon, product grammar, feature-flag decision or execution queue.
**Included below means a recommended commitment for the completed first offer,
not currently shipped.** Section 4 records the initial implementation snapshot;
section 8 adds a later review and fresh local test results. Sections 1–7 were
documentation/research work, without new application tests. No live model
generation or consumer study was conducted in either pass.

[Section 7](#7-commercial-offer-candidate) turns that service into a comparable
membership/access-pass proposal: ordinary and demanding value, free participation,
limits, exit behavior and whole-service economics. It does not adopt billing.

[Section 8](#8-complete-offer-review-against-current-outputs) applies that proposal
to existing design specimens, captured results and the current code. It sharpens
the reason to subscribe and identifies the delivery gaps without inventing a
second implementation roadmap.

[Section 9](#9-received-value-review-three-complete-situations) reviews the
no-history, one-fragment and shared/practical situations against current output
content. It distinguishes a successful delivery contract from a worthwhile
result, and makes the candidate paid expansion more concrete.

## 2. Make these choices for the candidate

1. **Audience:** people already asking ordinary questions, saving places and
   exchanging recommendations. They need an existing reason to try Vesper,
   not an extensive archive or a new journaling habit. Travel is a demanding
   specialization of everyday life, not required onboarding.
2. **Situations:** understanding something encountered; finding a worthwhile
   use of available time; food, places and small shared plans; receiving and
   revisiting useful material. This is the first supported situation range,
   not a claim to replace every general assistant or specialist service.
3. **World coverage:** use NYC as the recommended first local-supply candidate
   because the existing encounter portfolio is already concrete there. Select
   the actual supported area only after inspecting supply. General explanations
   and comparisons need relevant evidence, not residence inside that area;
   local availability and practical coverage must not be implied worldwide.
4. **Input:** start with typed questions/text and explicitly supported existing
   objects: a place, readable public piece, authorized note or retained source.
   Admit photo, ticket, PDF, recording and linked-page families separately when
   their actual contents can be used reliably. This stages material support;
   it does not abandon the artifact-rich vision or require universal ingestion
   before any benefit can ship.
5. **Responsibility:** include current help and bounded requested preparation.
   Continuing to watch a condition is a separate accepted service; external
   transactions stay outside the offer. A proposed plan is not an executed one.
6. **Social starting point:** original addressed material must be valuable to
   a lightly involved recipient. Begin with supported signed-in recipient
   relationships; extend media, audiences and guest participation through the
   existing Social plan. Receiving must not require an AI question, reply,
   contribution or an Occasion.

NYC selection and these exact material boundaries remain recommendations for
the founder and delivery owners. No geography, permission or entitlement is
adopted by writing this candidate. Do not let those decisions hold unrelated
immediate help or already-authorized continuity work hostage.

## 3. Included, conditional and excluded

### Included in the intended base service

| Commitment | What the person should actually receive | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful help with the current thing | A substantive explanation, comparison or practical adaptation; a finished answer can be enough | Supported subjects/materials; no required Keep, profile-building or follow-up |
| Worthwhile Home and Places without a rich history | Readable substance, concrete possibilities and exact depth—not an invitation to supply more data | Supported world coverage; a chosen area when needed; no friends required |
| Help making a possibility work | Relevant constraints, grounded current judgment and a usable next step or alternative | Assess only what the evidence supports; distinguish proposed arrangements from accepted changes |
| Original human value | The friend's actual offering and context, with an optional route into private help or doing something together | Supported recipient/audience and material; no synthetic reciprocity or pressure to post |
| Deliberate continuity | Reopen the selected retained source or result, including the right version where retention promises it | A real retaining owner and destination; correction, expiry and withdrawal remain effective |

These are commitments to complete benefits, **not five compulsory cards or
separate modes**. Useful factual summaries remain welcome, but personalization
must add substance rather than restate a connection the person already made.
An unfamiliar cliff comparison, an understandable food technique or a friend's
different perspective may finish the experience without a task attached.

Home should open the present and anticipated future, not become a retrospective
report. Places provides spatial depth and exploration, including casual shared
place material. Home can elevate addressed or shared-consequential material;
Life organizes retained life and people. Chat stays a relatively clean way to
ask, bring and direct. **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** Each move can appear across these roots, unevenly and when useful.

The intended base service has the same quality bar with sparse context, few
retained items, no friends on Vesper, or infrequent use. Missing history limits
personal claims, not substantive supported help. Include credible ordinary,
at-home and non-commercial value rather than treating these as a diminished
version of travel or city exploration. Do not infer those preferences from
sparse data. Useful enjoyment need not become learning, a Plan, or documentation.

Continuity should improve assistance fit, reduce reconstruction and repeated
correction, and make returning easy. It does not promise increasing intimacy,
content volume, or intervention. Already-authorized retention can help without
the optional conversation-history agreement; the latter remains conditional.

### Conditional promises and delivery-dependent variants

Some rows require a new product decision; others require finishing an existing
delivery path. Neither is made available simply by exposing a setting.

| Promise or variant | Condition for offering it | What remains useful without it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selective long-range conversational continuity | Adopt the history/material/duration/use agreement and connect correction, retrieval and source loss | Immediate answers and already-authorized retained material; no memory setup before first value |
| Commissioned comparisons or longer preparation | Exact requested subjects/purpose, executable ingress, allowance/deadline and a reachable result with truthful endings | Immediate help and already-prepared receiving; never substitute contextual warming for the requested job |
| Rich source/media adaptation | Read the actual supported object and obtain the necessary use permission; retain a derivative only through its proper owner | Original receiving where supported; do not pretend a visible attachment was understood |
| Broader friends sharing and no-account participation | Resolve audience/identity/delivery rules and complete the actual writer, reader and controls | Existing addressed receiving; neither friendship nor a public link implies unrestricted access |
| Ongoing following and notifications | Name the condition, coverage, duration, checking responsibility and effective stop; implement the accepted mandate | Current assessment, retained plans and requested preparation without an implied watch |
| Wider local supply and paid depth/capacity | Demonstrated coverage and whole-service cost; an explicit commercial decision | A credible bounded base service; no price, subscription interval or unlimited-use promise yet |

Calling an essential connection unfinished must not silently remove its benefit
from the offer. For example, exact Keep/refind is a base requirement; an
unsupported generated recipe version is a gap to close or a specific claim to
withhold, not permission to advertise “saved” and retain a different object.

### Excluded from this offer

- In-app booking checkout, payment settlement, provider rebooking or autonomous
  contacting/commitment. Useful booking links and navigation handoffs are allowed;
  Vesper must show where another service takes responsibility.
- Blanket flight, venue or life monitoring; guaranteed real-world availability;
  universal geographic or professional-advice competence.
- “Upload anything and we understand it,” ambient collection as an assumed
  prerequisite, or treating every conversation as permanent usable memory.
- A posting-performance system, daily participation requirement or social feed
  whose value depends on equal effort from every friend.
- A collection of heavyweight itinerary, booking and expense administration
  destinations as the product's identity. Simple planning and evidenced expense
  assistance can be useful without promising a full travel operations suite.

Exclusion is a service boundary, **not authorization to delete code or retained
records**. Existing compatibility and recovery obligations remain with their
owners.

### Where Vesper differentiates and where it hands off

This is an investment and service boundary, not three quality tiers:

| Responsibility | Intended standard |
| --- | --- |
| Distinctive Vesper contribution | Substantive interpretation, purpose-sensitive selection, authorized continuity, human perspectives, and practical adaptation; demonstrate actual benefit beyond a topic match or callback |
| Dependable foundations | Correct identity, factual grounding and calculations; exact retained results; understandable state, effective correction and reliable handoffs—even where these are not unique features |
| External operations | Provider inventory, checkout, payment settlement, cancellation/rebooking execution and turn-by-turn navigation; do not imply these happen inside Vesper |

Choose among supported possibilities on their merits for the current purpose,
not novelty, prestige or bookability. The live engine connects current
conditions to that purpose; a status feed alone is not the contribution.

Before a handoff, preserve the exact subject, selected occurrence and relevant
parameters where supported, plus unresolved questions. Name what the destination
actually enables. If a provider cannot accept the context, say what remains to
be entered; do not conceal that work. Keep the original useful result reachable
if the external link fails. A click is not a purchase, RSVP or attendance.
Only a supplied confirmation or authoritative supported return updates the
relevant owner, without requiring uploads for unrelated value.

Fewer screens must not remove correctness mechanisms. If expense assistance is
supported, source-backed extraction, deterministic arithmetic and inspectable
shared state remain necessary; conversational presentation does not replace
them. This is not authorization to expand the expense offer or retire records.

Research and rationale: [ordinary-relationship memo §10](vesper-ordinary-value-and-relationship-strategy-research-2026-09-06.md#10-taste-deepening-usefulness-ordinary-value-and-service-boundaries).
The [ordinary-life comparison extension](situated-value-decision-matrix-2026-09-06.md#16-ordinary-life-quality-and-continuity-comparisons)
tests these expectations alongside the existing portfolio, not as a new launch
wedge or an implementation-completion claim.

## 5. Practical help and continuity tie the entrances together

The live engine is not a separate fourth entrance or a dashboard that appears
only on a trip. It should influence whether a suggestion fits the available
time, what conditions matter and whether an alternative better preserves the
person's purpose. Keep **current assessment, requested preparation, accepted
following and authorized changes** distinct. A useful explanation can survive
an unavailable operational check; a consequential recommendation cannot pretend
the missing check passed.

Life completes deliberate retention across entrances; it need not receive every
question. Its [execution receipt](life-system-execution-receipt-2026-09-06.md)
establishes substantial shadow projection, repair and organization work, **not
an indexed-serving cutover or complete generated-result retention**. Existing
canonical readers remain relevant. Do not wait for every Life lens to finish
before connecting supported refinding, and do not create a second artifact store
to evade the remaining ownership decision.

## 7. Commercial-offer candidate

**Status: proposed, September 7.** This extends the same service candidate;
it is not a second product, new launch prerequisite or billing implementation
plan. [Monetization Strategy](../../travel-agent/docs/product/Monetization%20Strategy.md)
owns commercial principles. [Research §20](vesper-audience-offer-and-market-strategy-research-2026-09-06.md#20-commercial-offer-research--september-7)
owns the evidence and its limits. Prices, numerical allowances, billing interval,
renewal defaults and any paid experiment remain undecided. Section 4 is still a
dated implementation snapshot, not certification of this proposed offer.

### 7.1 The paid proposition

Investigate **one personal membership for repeated worthwhile assistance,
with useful free participation**. The reason to subscribe is not storage,
tokens, four premium modes or more prose. It is worthwhile understanding,
possibilities and practical help that the person chooses repeatedly, with less
reconstruction when eligible context helps.

Working explanation, not approved sales copy:

> Keep using Vesper for the questions, possibilities and plans that matter to
> you, with more room for substantial research and preparation when you want it.
> Friends can receive and participate without needing their own membership.

That sentence requires a concrete allowance and supported-service description
before sale. It does not promise unrestricted questions, unlimited production,
complete world coverage or an ambient watch. One person may chiefly value casual
understanding, another exploration, another practical relief. Adapt the received
value, not the price to inferred vulnerability or enthusiasm. Do not require
every person to use every capability to get their money's worth.

### 7.2 An ordinary month worth renewing

These are **authored comparison cases, not observed usage or promised monthly
deliverables**. They are alternatives within one month, not an assigned itinerary,
engagement quota or requirement that a person narrate their life. Start each
case from an actual declared purpose or eligible source; do not infer fatigue,
companionship, residence or appetite for novelty from absence or old travel.

| Situation | Finished benefit to inspect | Why this could earn repeated choice | Commercial implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A casual question about pasta, a building or a landscape | An explanation or comparison with substance beyond what the person already said; optional depth, no task attached | Asking here is worthwhile even without a later Plan or Keep | Free must demonstrate this; paid can expand substantial research capacity, not basic correctness |
| An open afternoon in the supported area | A few genuinely different possibilities with a concrete reward and the relevant time/access tradeoffs; staying local or familiar can win | Vesper makes available time easier to use without becoming another research assignment | Home/Places receiving is part of the service; do not sell it as a daily quota of generated cards |
| A request to have friends over | A usable proposal incorporating the supplied constraints and practical preparation, without committing other people | The person retains wanted choosing/cooking while Vesper absorbs unwanted checking | More demanding preparation is a paid candidate; recipients need not subscribe |
| A friend sends one place note | The original offering is enjoyable/useful as received, with optional private help | Another human contributes perspective without a posting or reply obligation | Basic receiving and responding remain free within the supported access model |
| The person wants something they deliberately kept | The exact retained item or version, with usable context and no required regeneration | Returning does not mean searching several conversations or recreating the work | Existing access is not held hostage; new substantive work on it may use an allowance |
| A week passes without opening the app | A useful present-facing return, with no missed-content backlog or memory-maintenance task | Availability can be worthwhile without daily use | Absence proves neither satisfaction nor churn; do not manufacture engagement to defend renewal |

The subscription case is **repeated preference for this help over available
alternatives**, not the sum of six hypothetical benefits. A delightful cliff
comparison can establish first value without establishing monthly willingness
to pay. Some people may genuinely receive enough from the free service; do not
degrade their experience to force a conversion.

### 7.3 A demanding period without a different product

Compare an upcoming visit, hosted weekend or trip against the ordinary cases:

1. The person supplies a supported question, material or constraint. Vesper
   returns immediate help before asking for more documentation.
2. They request a substantial comparison or preparation. Before accepting it,
   show its exact scope, included allowance, expected completion and destination.
3. Practical judgment checks the supported decisive conditions and makes the
   next step easier. Provider booking, payment, cancellation and rebooking remain
   external; uncertainty is not hidden behind a paid badge.
4. Friends can receive the selected proposal and contribute under current
   authority. A paying organizer does not acquire other people's assent or data.
5. A requested continuing watch is a **conditional extension**, only if its
   condition, duration, coverage, budget, stop and handoff are implemented and
   accepted. It is not required to make immediate preparation useful or to
   monetize supported current help.
6. When the period ends, retained results remain under their actual custody
   policy; current help can continue under the applicable allowance. No mandatory
   recap, diary or subscription-saving campaign is attached.

This stresses greater depth and coordination through the same product grammar.
It does not justify bringing back a booking suite or selling undefined concierge
responsibility. Do not advertise a conditional capability simply to make a paid
bundle look substantial.

### 7.4 Proposed free and paid boundary

| Area | Useful free participation | Candidate paid expansion | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current help | Finished answers and a representative sample of differentiated interpretation/practical adaptation | Greater capacity for substantial help; deeper supported comparisons | No intentionally misleading or broken free answers |
| Home and Places | Worthwhile readable material and grounded possibilities without rich history or friends | More extensive requested preparation and supported personal/world depth | No paywall per product move, paid ranking, or regeneration on every view |
| Authorized context | Relevant existing context may improve supported help | More substantial work over supported material within a declared allowance | Payment grants no new retention, inference or audience permission; no blanket premium-memory wall |
| Deliberately retained material | Meaningful access, correction and release under custody/source rules | Additional disclosed storage growth or production capacity, if offered | No silent deletion or forced payment to retrieve what was entrusted; no promise to retain revoked/expired sources |
| Human sharing and shared state | Basic sending, receiving, responding and participation within declared material/access limits | Extra preparation requested by a payer may benefit several participants | Core shared truth and participant welfare do not depend on tier |
| Continuing assistance | Current assessment remains useful without a watch | Explicitly supported bounded watches or longer assistance | A membership is not a mandate; cost capacity is not action authority |

Receiving can be free without being anonymous: this proposal does not adopt
guest links, new audiences or identity rules. Those remain in the Social owners.
Media/storage growth and abuse controls need disclosed limits, not an unlimited
free-participation promise.

**The commercial boundary is additional accepted work, not a category of value.**
A complete explanation, a relevant connection and a supported practical check
can each belong in free use. Neither article length, use of memory, number of
tabs nor the presence of a friend determines a paid request. Section 9.5 supplies
concrete expansion examples; their numerical allowances remain undecided.

**Social cost rule:** reading an already-delivered offering must not unexpectedly
use the sender's research allowance. A recipient's new private research uses
their own applicable allowance unless another funding arrangement was explicitly
chosen. Do not create paid seats or expose a recipient's private requests to a
sponsor. The payer may benefit because friends benefit; payment and authority
remain independent.

### 7.5 Predictable use, legible exceptional work

Investigate predictable membership pricing for ordinary use. Do not put a visible
credit countdown on every casual question. Expensive work can have a plain-language
boundary, for example: “This detailed comparison uses one included research
request.” That is an illustrative treatment, not an adopted unit or count.

Before pricing, define:

- the ordinary-use allowance, reset period and behavior at its boundary;
- what counts as a substantial request, its bounded scope and amendments;
- how much authorized continuing work can be accepted and for how long;
- storage/material limits and the usable free fallback; and
- whether any separately purchased expansion exists—none is adopted here.

Internal model calls, retries, correction and worker failures are not separate
customer-request units. The proposed treatment is to reserve allowance at job
acceptance and settle it against the declared service outcome; a failed job
must have a clear release/remedy rule. Backend retries must still be counted as
real company costs. Do not add a new ledger or implement these mechanics from
this proposal; map them to existing commercial/job owners after adoption.

At a limit, preserve already-available answers, original material, shared state
and effective correction. Offer the supported bounded alternative or a clear
next availability time. Do not accept an expensive request and then require an
upgrade halfway through. No automatic overage charges or silent downgrade of
consequential correctness. Paid help must retain the choosing or making that the
person wants to do.

### 7.6 The comparable episodic alternative

Compare a membership with **non-renewing access for a stated period**. Use the
same supported benefit examples, world/material coverage and quality bar. State
allowances for each period explicitly; if they differ, record that as a second
experimental variable rather than attributing the preference to renewal alone.
No price or duration is selected here.

| Question | Membership hypothesis | Pass hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reason to buy | Repeated useful help and wanted availability across ordinary situations | A current bounded period warrants more assistance |
| Between demanding periods | Ordinary use still earns preference and voluntary continuation | Free receiving/current help remains useful; the person can buy access again |
| End of access | Clear renewal/cancellation terms; exact policy still to be chosen | Ends without another charge; repurchase is explicit |
| Evidence against it | Payment persists without valued availability, ordinary choice or healthy costs | Repeated purchase decisions deter casual use, or most customers want continuing access |

Do not build both merely to avoid the decision. Distinguish a non-renewing pass
from a weekly subscription, and annual prepayment from observed renewal. A pass
need not be named after a Trip or require an Occasion object. Membership is the
leading hypothesis, not a condition that the evidence must validate.

### 7.7 Fair exit and accepted-work lifecycle

These are **proposed commercial treatments**. Existing source, action and legal
obligations still govern; this table does not extend permission or certify a
refund mechanism.

| Event | Proposed treatment to specify before selling |
| --- | --- |
| Cancel future renewal | Make cancellation easy and show the effective end. Preserve the paid service through its stated covered period; stopping a watch remains a separate effective control |
| Downgrade or pass expiry | New work follows the lower allowance. Preserve meaningful retained access, correction and original human/shared value within existing custody and access rules |
| Finite job crosses the access boundary | At acceptance, either cover a finite completion window/budget or disclose the exact stopping/handoff rule. Do not imply perpetual service or surprise payment for an already accepted result |
| Continuing watch reaches its end | Stop under the agreed mandate, show that coverage ended, and leave the exact status/result reachable where retained. No silent extension, abandonment or invitation to assume ongoing protection |
| Permission or source is revoked | Apply the existing authority/lifecycle stop immediately; payment cannot keep unauthorized work alive. State the job consequence and apply the declared allowance/refund treatment |
| Service fails or billing is interrupted | Surface the actual coverage/result state and a bounded recovery/remedy path. Do not claim success, retry indefinitely or silently erase shared commitments |

Avoid hostage memory without promising perpetual unlimited hosting. Storage
growth limits and source withdrawal must be understandable before reliance.
A read-only or export path may support a fair exit, but its availability must
be implemented and declared rather than invented by this document.

The existing E3 work must bring together supported inbound travel, meaningful
time there, entry/availability and onward travel when a next commitment matters.
Return an intelligible option and the constraint that changes it; do not return
the constituent facts as an assembly assignment. An alternative needs its own
basis. An unknown duration is neither zero nor proof the option is impossible.

**Leave human:** choosing the outing, whether to invite someone, personal words
and each participant's assent. Provider registration/payment remain external.
Ask only for missing constraints that change the practical answer; do not
require a new Occasion or Trip for browsing. Sharing a suggestion is not a
commitment or authorization for ongoing monitoring.

**Commercial consequence:** receiving, responding, shared truth and a
representative complete practical benefit remain accessible without every
friend paying. Substantial preparation can be requested and funded by one
person without making recipients unpaid organizers or giving the payer their
private context. Guest access, broader sharing and ongoing checks still require
their separately owned implementation and policy.

### 9.5 Make the paid expansion a legible increment of service

These are **scope examples, not entitlements, numerical allowances or newly
supported capabilities**:

| Complete benefit the base service must demonstrate | Additional work someone could deliberately request | What does not become an upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A sourced explanation of the thing in front of them, including a useful comparison when appropriate | Investigate a selected set of places or documents and return a researched comparison addressing their question | Completing the original explanation, showing its basis or correcting an error |
| Worthwhile local options with supported practical context | Compare several alternatives against supplied constraints and prepare a bounded option with a relevant fallback | Truthful feasibility, coherent navigation or a basic check withheld after promising a workable option |
| Enjoy a friend's offering and access the actual shared arrangement | Prepare alternatives for the gathering and handle explicitly requested changes within an agreed scope | Reading, replying, declining, accessing shared truth or receiving an already-prepared result |

Internally assess investigation breadth, unresolved dependencies, requested
production, amendments and any continuing duration. Externally describe the
finished work: what will come back, what is included, the expected completion
window and what remains outside Vesper. Do not make the user estimate tokens,
select backend agents or classify their question before receiving ordinary help.

More sources or complexity do not automatically mean more value. Existing
prepared material may already answer a demanding question; reuse it under its
actual authority. A simple-looking request may need expensive investigation;
state the bounded expansion **before accepting it**, without manufacturing an
upgrade because the system chose an inefficient implementation. Repairs and
retries of accepted work are company costs, not automatically new user requests.

The positive membership candidate remains repeated access to work worth using,
at greater declared capacity or scope. This review strengthens that formulation,
not the evidence for recurring demand. Ordinary-month willingness to pay,
membership versus a non-renewing pass, full-service cost and useful allowances
remain unresolved. The cost report is ready to observe model-call evidence;
it does not fill the commercial worksheet or establish usefulness.

### 9.6 Receiving handoff: E2 and E3 together, no new lane

Use the existing [E2/E3 packages](complete-system-integration-roadmap-2026-09-05.md#e2--finish-public-and-personal-receiving-including-exact-depth).
E2 should connect actual public/personal material to worthwhile received value
and its exact depth. E3 should make those same possibilities answerable against
real constraints. Their first outputs should be reviewed together; a working
`open_now` label must not stand in for the live engine, and an article must not
stand in for a useful whole experience. Neither package waits for a monetization
experiment to settle the broader architecture.

For the next combined handback, attach to existing cases:

1. **Received content and continuation:** exactly what appears and what its
   primary action opens; no substitute task inventory or aggregate test count.
2. **Context contribution:** what the present question, authorized fragment or
   human offering actually changes; name appropriate non-use of history too.
3. **Remaining human work:** distinguish wanted choice, a necessary missing
   fact or provider action from checking/copying/reconstruction Vesper still owes.
4. **Completion and cost boundary:** state what the accepted work finished,
   what remained unresolved, what was reused and which actual costs were recorded.
   An explanation can complete without action; unfinished preparation cannot
   be reclassified as a successful explanation after acceptance.

Mobile commissioning and receiving stay with their owning implementation lane.
The hooks exist, but a fresh search of `app/`, `components/` and `data/` found
no caller of `useSubmitSourceContributionRequest` or `useSourceContributionResult`.
Current contextual navigation work includes additional uncommitted changes;
do not requeue them as absent or claim them as landed. This review does not
activate workers/providers, change Chat or Life, dispatch another task or
introduce billing. It records quality and service requirements for the work
already underway.
