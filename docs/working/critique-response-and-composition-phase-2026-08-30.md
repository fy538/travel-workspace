# Response to the Composition Critique — and the Phase-2 Brief

## Verdict

The critique is accepted in its central claim: **the current Home and Places
project demonstrates a coherent interface grammar more convincingly than it
demonstrates the whole product thesis.** Its controlled evidence world—Nice,
Sorrento, Amalfi, and Rome returning into New York—was useful for settling
craft and continuity. Repetition turned that method into a product bias: the
flagship now reads more readily as post-trip and local editorial intelligence
than as personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world.

The fresh canon pass changes the earlier diagnosis in one important way. It is
premature to conclude that this is only a fixture artifact and not partly an
anatomy or grammar defect. Generic kind names do not prove a domain-neutral
ontology. The product canon also explicitly keeps exact Home and Places
composition, ranking, and density unfrozen, while the later consumer-anatomy
decision freezes five Home regions and a bounded kind union. Phase 2 must
reconcile that tension rather than assume the later anatomy is already proven.

The working hypothesis is therefore:

> The narrowness comes primarily from fixture-world and composition bias, but
> C1–C3 must be allowed to reveal an anatomy, taxonomy, medium, or sequencing
> defect. Begin with the existing kinds. Do not preserve them through semantic
> contortion.

"Zero new kinds" is an economical starting constraint, not a success metric.
If a complete experience repeatedly needs a genuinely different semantic
unit, that is a canon event and a useful finding. If an existing kind changes
meaning merely to keep the count stable, the test has failed.

### Scope of this document

This is the critique disposition and the falsifiable composition brief for the
next Home/Places-led phase. It is not a replacement for the whole-product
portfolio, the Chat operating-surface contract, the Life retrieval and
continuity program, the multiplayer strategy, or the real-world consequence
contract.

C1–C3 are the smallest integrated backbone that can pressure the thesis. They
do not prove the product merely because Chat, Life, repair, withdrawal, voice,
or a second occasion appears as a noun in a flow. The required variants and
overlays below must make those behaviors visible enough to inspect. Companion
native or interaction labs may own final geometry, map responsiveness, voice,
push, and provider execution, but their findings remain promotion gates for
the complete experience.

## 1. Fresh-pass canon reconciliation

The wider product canon separates durable product commitments from surface
variables more clearly than the first response did.

| Stable product commitment | Still a design or implementation variable |
| --- | --- |
| Home, Chat, Places, and Life as four orientations over one intelligence | Exact Home regions, their visibility, order, and density |
| Make sense · Open possibility · Help it work · Carry forward as recursive, non-mandatory value moves | Whether a two-tier world read leads a given posture or any posture by default |
| Immediate value before classification, reflection, organization, or social labor | Exact unit kinds, count, containment, and renderer mapping |
| Home and Places return value; Chat receives and operates; Life preserves and organizes | Exact posture taxonomy and the split between attention posture and lifecycle modifiers |
| One dominant job per unit; at most one page dominant; silence is valid | How many supporting units appear—there is no quota |
| Canonical state, instruments, human contributions, and Vesper editorial contributions are different value lanes | Which lane or medium leads a particular screen |
| Multiplayer is cross-cutting, attributed, viewer-relative, and outcome-plural | Whether people ever receive a visible chapter in a particular composition |
| Carry forward requires a later material difference with causal lineage | Whether `continuity_capability_field` survives as a kind |
| Maps must preserve world, route, current-condition, and interaction truth | The final spatial renderer and interaction choreography |
| The warm material system is the current control | Whether it survives ordinary, casual, social, and operational stress without making the whole product feel like a travel journal |

This phase may recommend an explicit amendment to the accepted Home/Places
anatomy and build manifest. It must not silently contradict them.

## 2. Adopted corrections, with refined mechanisms

| # | Correction | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | **Causal continuity replaces poetic continuity.** Carry forward appears only when named prior evidence changes a present judgment, capability, consequence, or appropriate silence. | Use the existing `Carried forward`/receipt lineage or explicitly extend the receipt family. Do not silently redefine a capability field as a receipt; a capability delta and a causal explanation are different semantics. |
| A2 | **The admission question hardens.** *If this disappeared, would today's Home be materially worse—or would the page merely demonstrate fewer capabilities?* | At most one dominant. Every additional unit must beat silence and add marginal value relative to what is already selected. No fixed two-to-three supporting-unit quota and no product-move coverage target. |
| A3 | **Ordinary Home may make Help it work legible without narrating Vesper.** | Show direct owned state or a real authorized monitor: `Friday dinner · settled`, `Restaurant reply · monitoring until noon`, or a current threshold. Do not generate `I'm watching this`, `nothing needs booking`, or other product self-description when no owner or monitor job exists. |
| A4 | **Practical judgment can be the whole contribution.** | Focus and Field may lead with reachability, timing, burden, access, availability, tradeoff, or prepared consequence. Practical value does not need an editorial preamble. |
| A5 | **Depth jobs remain distinct.** | Home = why now; Field = what else is available; Focus = what is true here; Path = an earned relation or distinction; Live = current guidance, threshold, action, and monitoring. A deeper state may not re-sell the originating proposition. |
| A6 | **Path is earned; Live is a projection, not level four.** | Path renders only when the relationship earns a diagram or structured relation. Live may open from Home, Chat, Plan, Occasion, or Places and compresses to the current operational job. |
| A7 | **Infrastructure becomes lightly legible when it changes trust or action.** | Render route source, freshness, provider state, reservation state, thresholds, and uncertainty through native state or instruments—not integration theater. |
| A8 | **Social warmth is a product property, not a copy layer.** | Preserve human authorship through face/name/media, timing, provenance, pass-along context, asymmetric contributions, and visible consequence. Copy can support warmth, but reactions, counts, faux intimacy, or a decorative friend card cannot create it. |

## 3. Ruling recommendations

These touch accepted surface doctrine. Adopt them through an explicit amendment
after the compositions verify them, rather than silently editing around the
current decision.

**R1 — Treat people as a cross-cutting reservoir by default. Recommend:
adopt.** Social material belongs inside the state, possibility, Place,
instrument, or continuity it materially changes. A visible people chapter may
still appear when the people or gathering itself is the dominant subject; it
is not a mandatory fifth region. This aligns the Home contract with the
multiplayer and HCI doctrine and requires an amendment to the five-region rule
and Home union organization.

**R2 — Separate attention posture from lifecycle/social modifiers. Recommend:
adopt.** Attention posture may include Cold, Quiet, Available, Planning, Live,
and Urgent. Returned, traveling, hosting, disrupted, settling, and with-others
modify the evidence and composition. They do not independently determine page
architecture. C1–C3 should test the vocabulary before its enum is frozen.

**R3 — Replace the default world-read requirement with an orientation
contract. Recommend: revise.** Home opens with the most valuable true
orientation. A two-tier serif read is one eligible expression when an
interpretive contribution wins. Direct state, an instrument, an attributed
human contribution, a spatial proposition, or intentional quiet may lead
without being framed as an exception. This follows the canon's operation-first
and native-medium laws and prevents an editorial tax on ordinary life.

**R4 — Quiet admits at most one immediately consumable return when it beats
silence. Recommend: adopt with this wording.** Quiet means low coercion, not low
contribution. It must not lead with Ask Vesper, reflection, setup, or open
Places as a substitute for value. It also must not manufacture one weak return
to satisfy a quota.

**R5 — Large type is earned by page dominance, not unit existence. Recommend:
adopt.** A unit may not carry more typographic authority than its rank. Quiet
state, counts, receipts, rows, and low-demand doors are allowed to remain
visually quiet.

**R6 — Separate doorway personalization, substantive contribution, and
self-interpretation. Recommend: adopt.** Home and Places may use evidence to
choose a relevant doorway and must then add a new mechanism, comparison,
reconstruction, direct state, human perspective, or consequence. Unsolicited
self-interpretation stays out of proactive surfaces; explicit Chat may explore
it with calibrated alternatives and no automatic durable identity claim.

**R7 — Add casual and human registers; retain the current palette as the
control, not an immutable conclusion. Recommend: adopt and test.** Food,
friends, errands, ordinary weekends, and small rituals should not inherit the
solemnity of disruption or major retrospective synthesis. Use real personal
photography when it is the evidence. Do not redesign the palette during this
phase, but require C1 and C2 to reveal whether the current material system can
carry casual and social life without reading as a luxury travel journal.

## 4. Deferred questions and guarded pushbacks

1. **Palette and nostalgia:** defer a redesign, but keep the issue empirical.
   Repeated founder selection establishes a strong control, not proof across
   ordinary and multiplayer states. Revisit only if the stress compositions
   fail under real photography, lighter voice, and operational forms.
2. **Map instrumentality:** do not attempt final visual composition in static
   boards, but do storyboard the behavioral contract before the renderer is
   built: preserve the carried question, recompose when the map moves, converge
   map mark and row, retain viewport/selection, and return the consequence.
   Implementation then proves responsiveness and performance.
3. **Already-canonical cures:** states-not-a-funnel, one-reason-one-burden,
   photography-as-evidence, ACT NOW, and Why this? should not be reinvented.
   Their existence in prose is not surface proof; C1–C3 must render them under
   competing attention and allow them to lose to silence.

## 5. Phase 2 — three falsifiable proof compositions

The next design work is three complete experiences, not another component
gallery. Each begins from the existing grammar and reports any semantic
distortion, missing kind, redundant kind, or region failure as a finding.

### C1 — An ordinary non-travel week, including genuine quiet

Use a broad evidence pool, not a page checklist: an incoming friend, a book or
film, a restaurant or cooking observation, an event ticket, a local ritual, a
reservation, a commute or weather change, and one permissioned human trace.
No Europe-derived lead and no assumption that editorial synthesis must lead.

Compose both Available and Quiet. Admit only the few pieces that materially
improve the actual screen; a direct practical state, a social
contribution inside the object it changes, or silence may beat a generated
read. No prompt, setup request, capability tour, or generic local browse shelf
may substitute for contribution.

Compose C1 under two evidence conditions:

1. **Thin-context ordinary life:** a first-week account with current world
   truth, one selected object or commitment, and little or no personal
   history. Vesper must contribute before asking the person to bring, classify,
   or explain anything.
2. **Mature ordinary life:** enough governed context to support genuinely
   personal selection without making travel evidence the center.

At least one turn must solve a direct practical question and correctly end
without durable residue, a Home unit, or a continuation. At least one Place
must already be familiar or routine; ordinary value cannot depend only on
novel destination discovery.

**Success:** someone who has never seen the travel fixtures describes Vesper as
lived-world intelligence rather than a travel recap, cultural magazine,
nearby feed, or personal dashboard. The screen feels useful on an ordinary
week without pretending an ordinary week is secretly profound.

### C2 — A complete multiplayer Occasion

Render the full social and practical arc:

```text
human address or invitation
  -> each recipient receives private orientation and immediate payoff
  -> accept, decline, ignore, or defer without public performance
  -> one person contributes a Place or perspective
  -> another supplies a private constraint or practical commitment
  -> Vesper prepares a minimum-safe shared shape
  -> the group makes one bounded decision under explicit authority
  -> Home shows only the material settled consequence
  -> Places adapts for the actual people and current world
  -> the Occasion occurs, changes, repairs, or ends
  -> Life preserves shared occurrence and plural viewer-relative Outcomes
  -> a later occasion improves—or continuity ends cleanly
```

The composition must show why thin participants would contribute, what each
gets before group labor, what happens under nonresponse, and how a human
contribution is felt without reactions or an activity feed.

The arc must also show withdrawal or correction after a contribution has
already affected a shared projection, plus the resulting recompilation. After
the Occasion, include one bounded social continuation outside the room—an
addressed handoff, authored featured Status, or provenance-preserving relay—so
multiplayer is not reduced to synchronous group coordination. The recipient
must receive value without owing a reply, profile, or app-maintenance task.

At least one thin participant should become capable of initiating or improving
a later Occasion. Repeat-group value is not proven if only the original host
becomes more efficient.

**Success:** removing another person's contribution materially changes the
understanding, possibility, coordination, or consequence. Multiplayer reads as
a co-produced experience with plural agency—not friend-enhanced
recommendations or collaborative-workspace overhead.

### C3 — A longitudinal contribution-to-compounding thread

Begin before Home rather than treating navigation as the loop:

```text
Ask, Point, Bring, or authorize in Chat
  -> useful immediate answer, explanation, comparison, or preparation
  -> minimum justified Source/Claim residue in Life, or no residue
  -> later Home or Places consequence with a visible causal basis
  -> optional Place, Plan, Occasion, Commitment, or real-world action
  -> owner readback and receipt
  -> Occurrence and personal/relational Outcome when known
  -> materially better second occasion, changed judgment, or earned silence
```

Show the same identity and unresolved job moving through the roots with the
projection envelope. Not every step must visit every root. At least one map
sequence must storyboard viewport movement, recomposition, selection
continuity, action, and return.

The Chat step must be composed as an operating surface, not represented only
by an arrow: selected context arrives without restatement; the response solves
or advances the immediate job; direct manipulation remains available when it
is clearer than another prompt; and the owner, action ceiling, and return path
stay legible.

The Life step must include later re-finding from an incomplete human cue, not
only successful persistence. Then correct, withdraw, or release one upstream
piece of evidence and show how the dependent Home, Places, social, and learned
projections change. A cached composition that survives invalidated evidence is
a failed C3.

**Success:** the person can see what Vesper added immediately, what was kept,
why it reappeared, what it changed, and how to correct or release it. The
second occasion is materially better; the final state may correctly be
silence.

### Required overlays — failure and situated use

The three compositions remain the backbone, but two cross-cutting overlays are
mandatory:

1. **Disruption and partial execution.** Preserve F5 as a live stress overlay
   across C2 or C3. Show `prepared → authorized → attempted → partial, failed,
   unknown, or completed → canonical readback → repair or recovery → receipt`.
   Provider truth, money, audience, and real-world action may not collapse into
   assistant prose. F5 is not merely an old fixture control; it is the strongest
   existing proof that Help it work is part of the product spine.
2. **Situated attention.** At least one live moment must work when eyes, hands,
   or social attention belong to the world. Storyboard the choice among screen,
   voice, one bounded push, deferral, and silence. This does not require final
   voice or notification visual design, but it must prove that the product does
   not assume every valuable interaction is a seated scroll.

## 6. Cross-composition acceptance gates

Every composition is reviewed against these gates:

1. **Category read:** after five seconds, can someone explain what value this
   product provides without calling it a travel planner, content feed,
   dashboard, journal, archive, or generic assistant?
2. **New contribution:** what can the person now see, understand,
   distinguish, decide, coordinate, or do that they did not already supply?
3. **Value before demand:** is the compact unit useful before any tap, reply,
   classification, reflection, save, or social response?
4. **Marginal composition:** does every additional unit make the page
   materially better than the smaller composition and silence?
5. **Native lane and medium:** is canonical state rendered as state, an
   instrument allowed to work, a human contribution kept human, and editorial
   synthesis expressed through the smallest suitable medium?
6. **Semantic fit:** does the existing kind describe the unit honestly? A kind
   that needs a new meaning, hidden subtype, or one-off exception is a grammar
   finding—not a successful zero-kind result.
7. **Root distinction:** does Home answer why now, Places answer what the world
   affords here, Chat preserve contribution and agency, and Life preserve
   identity and continuity without duplicating the same proposition?
8. **Social materiality:** if the person or contribution is removed, does the
   value actually change? Are author, audience, private reason, and plural
   Outcome still intact?
9. **Causal carry-forward:** can a later consequence identify the prior
   evidence that changed it without narrating memory, inventing causality, or
   converting activity into identity?
10. **Visual-range test:** can the chosen system carry casual, human,
    operational, spatial, and editorial registers without every unit becoming
    solemn, luxurious, carded, or text-led?
11. **Attention and presence:** can the person ignore or finish on view without
    debt, and does the product withdraw when more interface would compete with
    the world or people present?
12. **Longitudinal proof:** does a second occasion require less explanation,
    fit better, coordinate faster, delegate more safely, reveal something new,
    or correctly earn silence?

13. **Thin-context sufficiency:** does Vesper make a worthwhile first move
    before it has a rich personal record, without generic browse, setup, or
    input homework?
14. **Answer and stop:** can a direct answer or practical relief end without
    persistence, a Home projection, a CTA, or an interpretive sequel?
15. **Execution honesty:** do prepared, authorized, attempted, partial, failed,
    unknown, and completed remain distinct through owner readback and receipt?
16. **Causal repair:** does correction, revocation, or source withdrawal
    invalidate or revise every dependent projection, monitor, share, and
    learned signal rather than only changing the source view?
17. **Life re-finding:** can the person later recover the target from an
    incomplete cue involving time, Place, person, source, or thread without
    maintaining folders or reconstructing Vesper's ontology?
18. **Situated interaction:** can the product preserve presence through an
    appropriate choice of compact screen, voice, bounded push, deferral, or
    silence?
19. **Capability and agency delta:** is the person—or a thin participant—more
    able to perceive, decide, coordinate, repair, host, or act afterward,
    including without Vesper doing every future step?

## 7. Contract-coherence gate before composition

The current source set contains same-day authority drift that must be made
explicit before C1–C3 can produce trustworthy findings:

- the accepted Home/Places anatomy decision and mobile surface contracts still
  cite 22 Home kinds, 26 Places kinds, and ten shared instruments;
- the current build manifest and canon boards cite 29 Home kinds, 33 Places
  kinds, and twelve shared instruments;
- the accepted Home contract freezes five visible regions and the two-tier
  world read, while R1 and R3 deliberately put those decisions back under
  compositional test; and
- the Claude overview presents the rulings as closed while this document now
  treats the rendered system as the control under challenge.

Before composing, record one authoritative baseline revision and reconcile the
counts. Label the existing Claude boards **control under Phase-2 challenge**.
Do not let engineering implement an older contract while design tests a newer
one, and do not let a newer working mockup silently overwrite the accepted
decision. C1–C3 may recommend amendment; only the explicit canon-alignment pass
changes authority.

## 8. Sequencing

Phase 2 should precede architecture-bearing Home and Places implementation.
The fact that a compiler or renderer is nominally fixture-agnostic does not make
its input taxonomy, page anatomy, or output contract thesis-agnostic.

1. **Resolve the ruling batch as provisional composition assumptions.** R1,
   R2, R3, and R5 directly change page anatomy and hierarchy; C1–C3 then test
   them.
2. **Compose C1–C3 with fixed evidence and explicit smaller/silence
   counterfactuals.** Record union fits, semantic distortions, region failures,
   medium failures, and missing states.
3. **Amend canon explicitly.** Reconcile the Home/Places anatomy decision,
   build manifest, kernel, HCI composition contract, and root contracts in one
   pass. Do not let a working mockup silently override an accepted decision or
   let an accepted decision suppress contradictory evidence.
4. **Then freeze the architecture-bearing contract.** Approve visible regions,
   posture/modifier vocabulary, bounded unions, read/orientation behavior,
   social projection, admission/degradation rules, and map interaction
   contract.
5. **Then continue the bounded build.** Extract only fields and renderers used
   by the selected compositions, keep runtime behavior gated, and validate the
   complete portfolio in the native lab before exposing the shell.

Safe work may continue in parallel on genuinely reusable, semantics-light
primitives already selected—`MediaPlate`, `RouteStrip`, `RestClose`, basic map
rendering experiments, accessibility, and fixture harnesses. Hold expansion of
the admission/degradation compilers, world-read producer, taxonomy-sensitive
resolvers, permanent Home region layout, and social projector contract until
the compositions close. The existing Returned-Saturday fixture remains useful
as a control; it must not become the implementation template by having shipped
first.

## 9. What remains settled—and what Phase 2 may reopen

Phase 2 does **not** reopen:

- the lived-world thesis and group-travel wedge;
- the four roots and four recursive product moves;
- immediate and compounding value as the core loop;
- one durable owner per consequence and one identity across projections;
- the contribution authority, correction, revocation, and plural-Outcome
  contracts;
- no-homework, novelty-to-person, value-before-input, silence, and presence
  laws; or
- the distinction among Direct State, Composition, Instrument, Receipt,
  human contribution, owner link, short prose, and silence.

Phase 2 **may** recommend reopening:

- five fixed visible Home regions and `With people` as one of them;
- the world read as mandatory/default Home opening;
- the posture enum and lifecycle-state conflation;
- unit union membership, count, containment, naming, and kind semantics;
- `continuity_capability_field` and any other kind preserved only by changing
  its meaning;
- palette universality if casual and multiplayer stress fails after the
  lighter-register correction;
- build order for compilers, read production, social projection, and map
  choreography; and
- any physical rule that prevents the selected complete experience from
  expressing the stable product laws above.

A proof phase that cannot alter its assumptions is a demonstration. The point
of C1–C3 is to preserve the coherent product philosophy while discovering the
smallest surface and system grammar that can actually carry it.

## 11. Post-alignment audit of the reworked Claude boards

### 11.1 Updated verdict

The reworked project is a material advance. C1 makes ordinary and thin-context
value visible; C2 makes private payoff, nonresponse, governed contribution,
shared consequence, and participant-to-initiator compounding visible; C3 makes
the contribution-to-consequence loop, execution uncertainty, map continuity,
Life re-finding, and causal unwind visible. The Urgent board gives Help it work
the product weight the earlier project lacked.

The semantic backbone is now demonstrated strongly enough to continue. It is
not yet fully conformant. Several fixtures cross stable authority or
first-person-meaning boundaries while their findings boards mark the relevant
gate as passed. These are not copy-polish issues and cannot be waived by the
canon-alignment pass.

The correct status is:

> **Phase-2 semantic coverage achieved; promotion remains conditional on the
> authority correction docket, the complete nineteen-gate matrix, and the
> remaining native-medium proofs.**

### 11.2 Authority and epistemic correction docket

### 11.3 Completion-integrity corrections

1. **Run the complete matrix.** The C1, C2, and C3 findings boards currently
   select the gates most relevant to each composition. Produce one C1/C2/C3 ×
   nineteen-gate matrix with `pass`, `partial`, `fail`, or `not exercised`, plus
