# Response to the Composition Critique — and the Phase-2 Brief

## Verdict

The critique is accepted in its central claim: **the canonization project
proved the grammar but not the thesis.** Every board runs on one fixture
world (Nice–Sorrento–Rome → New York), chosen deliberately for coherence
while grammar was being settled — and that methodological choice silently
became a thesis problem: the flagship demonstrates *a post-trip
intelligence product*, not *a lived-world intelligence that happens to
hold rich travel evidence*.

The precise diagnosis matters because it bounds the cost: **this is a
fixture artifact, not a grammar defect.** Nothing in the 62-kind union
mentions travel; the resolvers, compilers, and instruments are
domain-neutral. The cure is new fixture worlds and stricter composition
laws — not new kinds, and not a redesign. Phase 2 is therefore a
*composition* phase run entirely on the existing grammar, and "zero new
kinds" is itself the test of whether the canon holds.

## 2. The ruling batch — founder decides

Each of these touches ruled ground or standing doctrine; recommendation
attached, but they are calls, not adoptions.

**R1 — "With people" becomes a reservoir, not a visible region.**
Critique §5. The evidence is ours: the flagship already renders Maya
inside Horizons — the mockup discovered a better model than the doctrine.
People participate inside every visible region (Now · In motion ·
Openings · Continuity); the people kinds survive in the union and render
where they matter. *Recommend: adopt.* Changes contract rule 1 (five
regions → four visible + people as cross-cutting reservoir).

**R2 — Posture vs. context modifier split.** Critique §6. Posture =
attention (Cold · Quiet · Available · Planning · Live · Urgent);
modifier = lifecycle (returned · traveling · hosting · disrupted ·
settling · with-others). Returned Saturday is Available + returned. This
matches the standing watch item and *simplifies* the matrix. *Recommend:
adopt.*

**R3 — World-read law refinement.** Critique §7. Keep the founder's
amendment (the two-line serif read stays the default opening) but restate
the law: *Home opens with its most valuable true orientation; the world
read is the default expression of it.* Live/Urgent dominance and the
existing merge rule already cover the exceptions; this names them as law
instead of exceptions. *Recommend: adopt as refinement — the read is not
revoked.*

**R4 — Quiet gains exactly one consumable return.** Critique §9. "Low
demand, not low contribution." The Ask Vesper / open-Places buttons are a
retreat — initiative returned to the user precisely where accumulated
value should show. One immediately consumable return (a comparison, a
prepared weekend shape, a causal receipt); everything else stays quiet.
Guard: this project has over-corrected on sparseness three times — the
ruling is *one* unit, not re-enrichment. *Recommend: adopt.*

**R5 — §11.7 amendment: the large type moment is earned by dominance,
not granted by existence.** Critique §20 is a direct, correct hit on our
own ruling: per-unit large moments flatten hierarchy into secondary
crowns. Merge with the structure gradient (§1.1 law 2): a unit may not
carry more typographic weight than its dominance rank. Quiet rows, small
states, subtle receipts, counts are legal and good. *Recommend: adopt.*

**R6 — The personalization copy law.** Critique §8. Three registers,
kept separate: (1) doorway personalization — "this appeared because of
something you noticed"; (2) substantive contribution — a new mechanism,
comparison, or consequence; (3) self-interpretation — what this may mean
about you. (1) and (2) belong on Home; (3) only in explicitly requested
Chat. *Recommend: adopt verbatim.*

**R7 — A casual voice register.** Critique §19, the half we accept. The
palette and material world are ruled and stay. But the register ladder
has no casual rung — food, friends, and ordinary weekends currently
receive the same solemnity as a disrupted return. Add a lighter voice
register (and use real photography wherever evidence is personal — that
half is already doctrine, starved only by fixtures having no photos).
*Recommend: adopt the register; do not reopen the palette.*

## 3. Pushbacks — not adopted, with reasons

1. **The palette/nostalgia critique (§19, first half).** Warm paper,
   Garamond, riso, gold are deliberate brand, ruled repeatedly, and the
   requested "living registers" are achievable inside them (R7 + evidence
   photography + the operational register Live already has). Re-opening
   the material world would churn ruled ground on taste.
2. **"The map is not instrumental" (§11) — deferred, not refuted.** The
   admission compiler, scope handle, and map-grammar renderer are exactly
   the machinery for map-responsive composition, and all are BUILD-graded
   in the ledger. Static boards cannot show interaction. The critique's
   list (move-the-map recomposes · search preserves the question · pin
   and row converge) enters the map renderer's build contract as
   acceptance criteria rather than triggering board work.
3. **Several cures already exist in canon** and are re-derived by the
   critique: states-are-not-a-funnel, one-reason-one-burden,
   photography-as-evidence, the ACT NOW inversion, the Why-this
   instrument. No double-build; they need *rendering*, which is what the
   compositions below are for.

## 4. Phase 2 — three proof compositions (zero new kinds)

The next design work is not more specimens. It is three complete
compositions on the existing grammar; if any of them *needs* a new kind,
that is a canon event to surface, not route around — and a finding in
itself.

**C1 — An ordinary non-travel week.** No Europe-derived lead anywhere on
the page. The intelligence comes from: a book changing how a neighborhood
reads · a friend's upcoming visit reshaping the week · a restaurant
observation becoming a cooking distinction · a recurring local ritual
made easier · a commute revealing a usable pattern. Postures: Available
and Quiet (with R4's one consumable return). Success test: a reader who
has never seen the Europe fixtures concludes this is a lived-world
product.

**C2 — A complete multiplayer Occasion.** The loop the boards have never
drawn, and the wedge itself: an Occasion exists → one person contributes
a place → another privately supplies a constraint → Vesper prepares a
group-safe shape → the group makes one bounded decision → Home shows the
settled consequence → Places adapts the route for the actual group → the
occurrence lands in Life. Warmth per A8; grants per the contribution
contract; nobody becomes a taste profile. Success test: multiplayer reads
as co-produced experience, not friend-enhanced recommendations.

**C3 — One continuous cross-root thread.** Home → pushed Places depth →
action → receipt → Life continuity, drawn as one storyboard across roots
using the projection envelope. Every hop carries a causal receipt (A1).
Success test: the four-root claim stops being referenced and becomes
perceptible.

Composition constraints for all three: the hardened admission question
(A2) governs every unit; possibility budget per §3 of the critique — more
weight on something Vesper is handling, a shared thing cohering, a
concrete consequence, a causal receipt; carry-forward only in causal
form.

## 5. Sequencing

Phase 2 composition runs **parallel to, not blocking, step-4 build**:
increment 1 (MediaPlate, RouteStrip, RestClose, the Saturday fixture
screen) stands regardless of fixture-world choice, and the compilers/read
producer/map renderer are fixture-agnostic. The ruling batch (R1–R7)
should land first — R1, R2, and R5 change what the compositions are
allowed to assume. Board-by-board dispositions from the critique's
summary (Quiet's weakness, Focus's crowded action seam, F3's
"assignment" risk, F5's owner-locus) fold into C1–C3 rather than
receiving separate patch passes.

## 6. What this does not reopen

The kernel (§2–§11 of the extraction doc), the unit unions and grades,
the physical grammar and rhythm ramp, the board kit, the pruned
implementation set, and step-4 increment 1. Phase 2 composes on top of
all of it — that is the point.
