# Canonical Product Journey Families

> Status: working registry under product and behavioral validation
>
> This document defines reusable product-design journey families. It does not
> claim that every journey is implemented or shipped. The existing
> [J01-J28 registry](../journeys/README.md) remains the implementation-assurance
> and regression registry until product journeys are deliberately mapped into
> evidence-bearing coverage.

## 1. Why this layer exists

The August thesis outgrew a journey system organized mainly around Trip screens.
The product now needs journeys that can express a menu, movie ticket, ordinary
local evening, Place question, relational opening, delegated action, or later
return without inventing a new mini-product for each artifact.

The documentation layers are:

```text
Product Thesis                    why the product exists
Product Model                     canonical nouns and relationships
Experience Constitution           interaction laws and grammar
Product journey families (CJ)     meaningful sentences made from the grammar
Scenario fixtures                 concrete artifacts and occasions
Assurance journeys (J01-J28)      implemented routes, branches, and evidence
Surface and system contracts      where behavior is projected and executed
```

A CJ number names a product-design family. A J number names an assurance
contract. Similar numbering does not imply implementation coverage.

## 2. Canonical journey sentence

Every product journey should be expressible as:

```text
An Initiator causes a Person to perform a Verb
on an Object for an Immediate Job,
within a Moment, Scope, and relationship context.

Vesper chooses a Treatment,
which may produce an authorized Consequence,
an inspectable Receipt,
and an optional future Continuation.
```

Every journey definition must name:

```text
human motivation
initiator and starting state
human verb and object
immediate job
Place, time, attention, and people context
audience, custody, and authority
Vesper treatment
optional consequence
receipt and return point
required branches
must-never-happen rules
success evidence
second-occasion possibility
```

## 3. Journey families

### CJ01 — Bring a fragment to immediate value

**Promise:** I can bring Vesper whatever already has my attention and receive
useful help before organizing, saving, or sharing it.

**Typical inputs:** photograph, screenshot, ticket, menu, receipt, link, voice
fragment, invitation, reservation, book, or message.

**Core path:** bring -> determine immediate job -> answer, decode, orient, or
act -> show any consequence -> expire or request bounded continuity.

**Must never happen:** artifact classification becomes preparatory homework;
upload implies occurrence, preference, memory, or sharing.

### CJ02 — Ask about the lived world

**Promise:** I can ask an ordinary question and receive an answer fitted to the
present Place, moment, people, and commitments when those contexts are
authorized and useful.

**Core path:** ask -> determine whether context changes the answer -> answer at
the right depth -> allow follow-up -> usually expire.

**Must never happen:** conversational context silently produces durable memory
or consequential action.

### CJ03 — Explore without a fully formed question

**Promise:** I can browse, wander, or follow a thread without already knowing
the correct query.

**Core path:** establish an exploration posture -> offer a small number of
grounded openings -> let the person follow, dismiss, or redirect -> withdraw.

**Must never happen:** exploration becomes an infinite recommendation feed or
optimizes consumption for its own sake.

### CJ04 — Orient, locate, and move

**Promise:** Vesper helps me understand where I am and move through the world
while increasing, rather than replacing, my spatial capability.

**Spatial subverbs:** locate, route, orient, wander, discover, coordinate,
reconcile, and recall.

**Core path:** identify the spatial job -> resolve Place and current truth ->
apply user-authorized costs such as heat, shade, slope, crowds, accessibility,
energy, or scenic value -> give an environmentally grounded treatment -> adapt.

**Must never happen:** location sharing becomes ambient surveillance; one
universal route cost is treated as the objective best route.

### CJ05 — Notice a distinction and gain situated capability

**Promise:** Something practical or perceptible can become a doorway into local
logic, leaving me better able to perceive, judge, communicate, or act.

**Core path:** notice or ask -> solve the practical job -> reveal at most one
useful hidden layer -> offer a comparison or action -> test later transfer.

**Must never happen:** the world becomes coursework; existing interests become
permanent identity labels; an explanation is given only because content exists.

### CJ06 — Keep or connect with bounded continuity

**Promise:** I can privately preserve or connect something without being forced
to share it or let it shape every future judgment.

**Core path:** propose a consequence -> choose retention, inference, and
connection scopes separately -> write to the durable owner -> leave a receipt ->
support dormancy, correction, and release.

**Must never happen:** keep implies sharing; audience implies inference;
retention of raw material implies retention of every derived interpretation.

### CJ07 — Receive, inspect, and react

**Promise:** When another person, Vesper, a provider, or a changing world starts
the interaction, I can understand why it reached me and retain control.

**Available reactions:** open, inspect why, accept, modify, respond, snooze,
dismiss, mute, correct, or release.

**Must never happen:** a proactive opening hides its initiator, evidence,
urgency, audience, or consequence.

### CJ08 — Address a person through a meaningful opening

**Promise:** Vesper can help me recognize a genuine reason to think of or reach
out to someone while leaving the relationship in human hands.

**Core path:** encounter something -> recognize relational connective tissue ->
privately offer the opening -> person decides what it means -> person speaks in
their own voice -> recipient freely responds or does not -> optional shared
attention, action, or Occasion.

**Must never happen:** guilt reminders, relationship-health scoring, synthetic
intimacy, automated emotional outreach, or pressure to respond.

### CJ09 — Share, publish, or contribute with governed custody

**Promise:** I can create a bounded projection for its intended audience without
silently broadening retention, inference, reuse, or authorship.

**Audience forms:** named person, named people, Occasion roster, recurring
circle, link viewers, followers, public Place, or provider/service.

**Core path:** select projection -> select recipients and purpose -> resolve
copying, resharing, attribution, expiry, and inference -> preview -> share ->
support correction and honest revocation limits.

**Must never happen:** send, address, share, publish, and contribute collapse
into one visibility control.

### CJ10 — Shape an Occasion with the lightest adequate structure

**Promise:** A vague possibility can become just enough structure for something
worthwhile to happen.

**Possible outcomes:** answer only, one Move, lightweight Occasion, local Plan,
or structured multi-day Trip.

**Must never happen:** every curiosity becomes a Plan; an itinerary becomes the
company-level definition of experience.

### CJ11 — Invite, join, leave, or rejoin

**Promise:** Participation is explicit, scoped, and proportional to the kind of
shared experience being proposed.

**Core path:** inspect people, purpose, time, commitment, and shared state ->
accept, decline, tentatively join, or modify -> optionally provide private
constraints -> participate -> leave, mute, or rejoin under clear rules.

**Must never happen:** invitation acceptance exposes private reasons or grants
broad group authority.

### CJ12 — Decide together without averaging people

**Promise:** A pair or group can reach one coherent action while preserving
private information, distinct perspectives, and appropriate decision rights.

**Core path:** collect plural private input -> distinguish safety, veto,
expertise, preference, and delegation -> produce group-safe synthesis -> invite
right-sized participation -> commit one shared action -> preserve plural outcomes.

**Must never happen:** one aggregation rule is used universally; a generated
group personality replaces the people; the thinnest participant disappears.

### CJ13 — Coordinate and delegate responsibility

**Promise:** Vesper reduces invisible organizer labor and makes ownership of
real responsibilities understandable.

**Actions:** request input, claim a task, assign or transfer ownership, nominate
an organizer, privately disclose a constraint, create a personal variation,
temporarily share location, or delegate a bounded task.

**Must never happen:** coordination authority becomes permanent; private
constraints leak into the group explanation; Vesper silently becomes organizer.

### CJ14 — Live and adapt as reality changes

**Promise:** Vesper protects the intended human experience when heat, fatigue,
weather, delay, closure, accessibility, energy, interest, or group state changes.

**Core path:** observe or receive changed truth -> compare intervention with
silence -> preserve commitments that still matter -> repair, reroute, defer, or
withdraw -> keep every shared projection coherent.

**Must never happen:** itinerary completion outranks the people currently living
the experience.

### CJ15 — Decide, authorize, execute, monitor, and reconcile

**Promise:** I can let Vesper or another actor help make something happen without
granting ambiguous or durable authority.

**Core path:** decide what should happen -> grant task-scoped authority ->
provider or responsible actor attempts execution -> monitor without assuming
success -> reconcile provider and lived truth -> revoke or recover if needed.

**Must never happen:** approval of an option implies permission to contact,
commit, pay, or retry; a timeout is treated as failure or permission to repeat.

### CJ16 — Mark and reconcile what actually happened

**Promise:** Planned, provider-reported, and lived reality can diverge without
creating false history.

**Core path:** inspect current claim -> confirm, correct, or leave unknown ->
attach source-bound evidence -> optionally state personal meaning -> create an
outcome receipt -> decide whether anything should continue.

**Must never happen:** provider confirmation proves attendance, enjoyment, or
meaning; one person's outcome becomes everyone else's memory.

### CJ17 — Return through earned continuity

**Promise:** A prior encounter can make a later situation more useful or legible
without turning memory into a resurfacing engine.

**Admission rule:** authorized prior encounter + current semantic relevance +
current situational relevance + a present job it can change = admissible
reactivation.

**Must never happen:** stored material resurfaces merely because it exists;
recognition without changed perception, conversation, action, or capability is
counted as compounding value.

### CJ18 — Correct, release, forget, or revoke

**Promise:** I remain able to contest the system's identity, interpretation,
memory, audience, and authority claims.

**Correctable dimensions:** source identity, Place identity, person identity,
interpretation, participation, attribution, outcome, visibility, inference, and
permission.

**Must never happen:** deletion is promised beyond the custody Vesper controls;
correction requires a settings scavenger hunt; release destroys unrelated work.

## 4. Multiplayer modes across the journeys

These are modes, not separate destinations:

| Mode | Human value | Common journey families |
|---|---|---|
| Co-present shared attention | Look at or experience something together | CJ04, CJ05, CJ14 |
| Borrowed perception | Temporarily see through another person's lens | CJ05, CJ08, CJ12 |
| Asynchronous addressed attention | “This made me think of you” | CJ07, CJ08, CJ09 |
| Host-mediated attention | Reveal one's city, practice, or life to a visitor | CJ03, CJ05, CJ10, CJ14 |
| Governed joint decision | Converge on one action without flattening people | CJ10-CJ15 |
| Living ritual | Repeat with variation while preserving human meaning | CJ10, CJ11, CJ17 |

The recurring social pipeline is:

```text
plural private perspectives
  -> group-safe synthesis
  -> appropriate joint participation
  -> one coherent shared consequence
  -> separate personal outcomes
```

## 5. Scenario fixture library

Fixtures test whether the grammar generalizes. They are not top-level product
categories.

| Fixture | Immediate job | Product path worth testing |
|---|---|---|
| Plane ticket | Time, terminal, route, disruption | Movement commitment -> monitoring -> adaptation -> reconciliation |
| Movie ticket | Identify work and evidence state | Cultural encounter -> Place reactivation -> relational opening |
| Menu | Translate, compare, order | Practical help -> sensory distinction -> optional capability continuity |
| Photo with friends | Understand, preserve, or send | Source evidence -> Occasion association -> addressed projection -> plural outcomes |
| Book or passage | Identify, explain, discuss | Cultural lens -> current Place connection -> question or shared Occasion |
| Show ticket | Attendance operations | Invite/join -> route and timing -> live experience -> later conversation |
| Invitation | Understand people, purpose, and commitment | Inspect -> private constraint -> join/decline -> participation |
| Receipt | Understand charge or document outcome | Reconcile provider and lived truth -> expense or Place evidence |
| Screenshot | Determine the current job | Answer, action, sharing, documentation, continuation, or expiry |
| Voice observation | Capture a thought cheaply | Private reflection, Place connection, addressed opening, or expiry |
| Recipe or grocery receipt | Cook or buy something now | Situated capability -> household Occasion -> later transfer |
| Accessibility need | Make the chain livable | Private constraint -> route/provider coordination -> live adaptation |
| Recurring family dinner | Coordinate a living ritual | Vary the instance while preserving its mutually wanted meaning |

Every fixture must include at least:

- immediate-value and no-retention path;
- solo and relevant multiplayer path;
- incorrect-inference and correction path;
- attention-cost or silence path;
- authorized consequence and receipt path;
- second-occasion or explicit expiry path.

## 6. Mapping into assurance

The CJ registry should not replace J01-J28. Promotion proceeds by mapping a
validated product family into one or more assurance journeys and branch IDs.

| Product layer asks | Assurance layer asks |
|---|---|
| Is this the right human interaction? | Can the implemented path complete reliably? |
| What is the minimum meaningful consequence? | Which route, API, state, and provider own it? |
| What must remain private or human-authored? | Which deterministic invariants prevent leakage or fake success? |
| When should Vesper withdraw? | Which UI states and tests prove abstention or dismissal? |
| What would demonstrate later value? | Which evidence receipt certifies the second occasion? |

No journey family becomes “implemented” merely because a similar J journey or
test exists.

## 7. Promotion criteria

Promote this working registry into a durable contract only when:

- the Experience Constitution's grammar has founder approval;
- Sorrento and Rome proofs can be represented without bespoke verbs or objects;
- at least one ordinary local and one non-travel fixture fit the grammar;
- pair studies validate addressed attention and plural synthesis boundaries;
- delegated-action testing validates the authority transitions;
- product, design, and engineering agree on the CJ-to-J mapping method;
- no family silently claims a currently dark or unimplemented capability.
