# Claude Design handoff — Vesper Life, the current product

## 1. Assignment

Create a **new companion Claude Design project**, recommended name:
**Vesper — Life / Current Product**.

Use `/Users/feihuyan/Downloads/vesper-life-anchors` as the source export. The
September 6, 12:32 export contains 121 canvases, including historical alternatives,

The existing project is valuable evidence and must remain intact. Do not delete,
overwrite, rename its boards, or reorganize it destructively. If project creation
or cross-project copying is unavailable, report that limitation and prepare the
transfer set; do not claim the companion project exists.

The new project should answer, in one short review:

> What is Life today? What does the person see and do? Which few decisions are
> still open? Where is the supporting detail if we need it?

This is a **curation and consolidation assignment**, with narrowly scoped
corrections. It is not a request for another visual direction, new product
ontology, additional root, or production implementation. Preserve the chosen
visual language and useful behavior. Reduce the work needed to understand them.

The previous [Life handoff](claude-design-integration-2026-09-04/02-life-anchors.md)
remains context. Its Life-over-time assignment has produced boards 35–39; do not
run that exploration again. Consolidating does not itself approve those proposals.

## 2. Product context to preserve

Vesper turns attention and intention into useful understanding, grounded
possibilities, practical help, and continuity. Its moves are **Make sense. Open
possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.** They are not one move per tab or a
required sequence.

- Home delivers value for current life and the anticipated future.
- Chat makes giving, asking, and expressing a change effortless.
- Places makes the world and its possibilities intelligible.
- Life makes the retained record recognizable, findable, and useful again.

Life has four lenses over one corpus: Time, Places, Threads, People. These are
inside Life; they are not four additional application roots. Life's Places lens
explores personal continuity with places; the app's Places tab supplies the
world-facing discovery and situated view. Everything kept is access to retained
material, not a fifth lens or a second archive.

Life must work for an ordinary NYC week, an undated recipe, a friend's note,
and a future possibility as well as a rich Europe journey. Value may be factual
organization, exact refinding, attributed human expression, or useful synthesis.
It need not always be an AI insight. Avoid flat poetic recaps, inferred personality,
filing work, reflection prompts, and invitations to complete the record.

## 5. Required corrections during curation

### Resolve inconsistent authority labels

- Old 00A calls the 03E family canonical while 03E Time itself points to 25 as
  its replacement. Select 25/26; link to their provenance rather than reimporting
  the competing Time boards.
- 28 still announces an adopted per-lens illustration treatment. The current
  contract restricts the illustration to a genuinely empty whole corpus.
  Preserve sparse content from 27 without importing that superseded behavior.
- 26's annotation mentions swiping between lenses; board 15 and the contract
  prohibit horizontal page swipe. Preserve the labeled selector. Annotation
  cleanup must not accidentally introduce a new gesture.
- AHEAD, kept-intention ownership, and full authored-composition custody are
  not settled by a convincing walkthrough. Keep their status explicit.

### Do not turn the section inventory into a checklist

06's twelve conditional sections are available capabilities, not a requirement
to show twelve headings. Select and order content for the specific object.
A place with one note should be able to show that note directly. Photos need
not appear again as separate artifact and source sections with no added value.
Complete access can remain behind a clear contextual door. Do not invent a
numeric section cap or remove access simply to make the page look quieter.

### Separate customer copy from explanations to us

Move internal phrases out of phone frames, including:

- “EARNED · SUPPRESSIBLE.”
- “IN NO EPISODE — AND COMPLETE.”
- “YOUR CAPTION · PROTECTED.”
- “Three untouchables,” candidate tables, and causal/owner boundary diagrams.

For an article, “Article · saved September 2” may be sufficient. Its successful
retrieval demonstrates that no episode was needed. Keep attribution, meaningful
uncertainty, and actual action consequences visible when they help the person;
do not remove useful disclosure under the banner of simplification.

### Do not canonize the release regression

39 and the Life-over-time report say releasing a source means Vesper no longer
holds its original. 10B instead says a source may remain held while released
**from use**. These are different consequences.

For this transfer, preserve the existing separation between source custody,
permitted use, and deletion. Mark the newer universal-release wording Review;
do not change source retention policy to make one verb appear simpler. Show
contextual plain-language actions and readbacks only when needed. Source release,
intention release, and deletion must not be declared equivalent by this design
assignment. Route the remaining semantic choice to the owning contract.

### Simplify navigation without erasing distinctions

Use 37's primary-destination proposal to show how context can choose the landing:
Life relationship row → your record; arrangement row → plan; thumbnail → original;
discovery result → place; saved piece → chosen version. Keep this proposal's
adoption status explicit. Secondary destinations use names such as “The place
itself,” “Her note,” or “Friday's plan,” not an ontology chooser.

Do not require a five-way result just because someone searches a place name.
Show alternatives where the target is genuinely ambiguous. Likewise, 36's
split-resolution chooser is a fallback when an old target cannot be resolved
uniquely, not a compulsory detour for every historical link.

## 6. Transfer procedure and provenance

1. Inventory the source export and read relevant selected boards fully. Do not
   choose the README-selected canvas as the whole product by default.
2. Create the companion project and its Current / Review / Reference organization.
   If folders/sections are unsupported, use the naming prefixes above.
3. Transfer selected HTML/components/assets using supported project mechanisms.
   Preserve real media and the chosen visual language. Check that imported images,
   fonts, and components resolve in the new project; do not rely on accidental
   local paths that the recipient cannot access.
4. Remove duplicate compositions and reviewer-only chrome from the new copies.
   Leave originals untouched. Correct the narrow conflicts above; unresolved
   substantive choices remain in Review.
5. Maintain a concise manifest in the new project or companion document:
   new board/frame → original filename/frame → status/authority → changes made.
   Use exact old board titles so the original is recoverable without a live link.
   Record retained, consolidated, reference-only, and archive-only material.
6. Check the default walkthrough with all annotations hidden. Inspect normal
   phone width, full-scroll continuity, long content, and large-text/narrow states.
   Clearly distinguish a scripted design prototype from an implemented feature.
7. Return the new project link, manifest, default review order, concise change log,
   and remaining decision list. Do not claim participant validation or deployment.

No production code, migration, repository cleanup, branch merge, or contract
rewrite is authorized by this handoff. Other lanes' work and the original design
project stay intact. Do not reopen research questions merely to populate a section.

## 7. Review and completion bar

For every selected consumer composition, the founder should be able to answer:

1. Why did I open this page?
2. What value can I get before doing more work?
3. What does tapping the main thing open, and can I return without losing my place?
4. Which visible sections actually help this job?
5. Can I leave without filing, reviewing, answering, or completing anything?

For a state transition: does an existing familiar page change appropriately,
or have we invented another screen to explain our machinery?

The new project is ready when:

- The default path has approximately eight boards, not 121 or eight oversized dumps.
- Each page family has one selected current composition; variants have explicit scope.
- The original archive remains available and unchanged.
- All four lenses, ordinary life, useful originals/refinding, social contribution,
  and delivered value remain represented. Simplification has not reduced Life
  to a travel wallet, gallery, or one isolated proof loop.
- Future material, automatic evolution, and saved-piece proposals are easy to
  review without being mistaken for adopted or shipped behavior.
- The chosen components feel consistent; no new visual system has been introduced.
- A coding agent can identify the implementation target and its source authority
  without reconstructing the history of the design sprint.

**The goal is a smaller, clearer view of a rich product—not another round of
design expansion.** After this handoff, new decisions should update the selected
boards in place, with history preserved outside the default review path.
