## 2. Product context to preserve

Vesper turns attention and intention into useful understanding, grounded
possibilities, practical help, and continuity. Its moves are **Make sense. Open
possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.** They are not one move per tab or a
required sequence.

- Home delivers value for current life and the anticipated future.
- Chat makes giving, asking, and expressing a change effortless.
- Places makes the world and its possibilities intelligible.
- Life makes the retained record recognizable, findable, and useful again.

Life has four lenses over one corpus: Time, Places, Threads, People. These are
inside Life; they are not four additional application roots. Life's Places lens
explores personal continuity with places; the app's Places tab supplies the
world-facing discovery and situated view. Everything kept is access to retained
material, not a fifth lens or a second archive.

Life must work for an ordinary NYC week, an undated recipe, a friend's note,
and a future possibility as well as a rich Europe journey. Value may be factual
organization, exact refinding, attributed human expression, or useful synthesis.
It need not always be an AI insight. Avoid flat poetic recaps, inferred personality,
filing work, reflection prompts, and invitations to complete the record.
