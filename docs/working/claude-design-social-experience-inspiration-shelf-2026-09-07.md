## 2. Starting positions for the six decisions

These are what the sibling projects already lean toward. The social project recommends; it does not inherit these as rulings. *Reframed after the founder's Checkpoint 1 review (handoff §10): D1 is about openness, not an audience pill; D3 is the value and scope of authored signals, with supply variants as an added condition; D6 is occasion-bounded exchange versus an optional ongoing connection, with the email / no-path branch as an edge case. The reference behaviors the review excluded (R5's automatic use report, R9's private-Keep reporting and link rebinding, R10's consumer-facing MAY-USE wording) are not starting positions.*

1. **D1 sharing into possibility.** Four projects agree that the only openness mechanism is *audience*: a WHO choice defaulting to "just me, then the last choice made" (aperture), a prepared-not-sent card with Send as the deliberate step (Places 06), rows that exist before any container (Plans 02), and "only an Occasion decides; a pin informs" (aperture 06). Nothing draws a separate "happy for company" state. Starting position: natural language plus the audience choice; no openness affordance; participation is established only by a host's Send or a guest's answer. The comparison on canvas 04 is audience-pill versus language-only, not four composer categories.
2. **D2 acknowledgment.** Split two to two. Entity 04 and aperture 02 draw one author-only USEFUL ("reaches her only, no read receipt"); Places 04 and Life 07 draw Reply only ("Not replying is a complete outcome"). Starting position: Reply. USEFUL is the one candidate to compare, on the strict terms already drawn: author-only, no count, never a read receipt. This is the decision the handoff marks for observation with a real friend.
3. **D3 friend-aware discovery.** The signals every project accepts are authored: her line, the precision she chose, the *when* she wrote or the hour she pinned ("never inferred from anyone's movement"), a role on a record ("You hosted"). Relevance is stated, never assumed (RA6 form 2). People never rank by activity; pins "rank by who and how recent, never by how many". With one friend or none, the same skeleton renders ("not a degraded version") and nobody is asked to post.
4. **D4 hospitality.** The pieces exist but the view does not: Plans 04 C2 has the guest page and C7 partial attendance; Entity 12 has the honest handoff line; Places 02 has the sub-line register for facts not checked today. No project draws "you're expected around seven-thirty", how to find the host, or the arrival itself. This is new work for the social project, and the cheapest to keep small: one frame, supplied facts only.
5. **D5 enrichment.** Unanimous: the original leads and is never rewritten; enrichment sits beside, cited, once (Entity's trust order and citation marks; Places' three registers; aperture's "Vesper adds only current truth"; Life P3 "never rewrites … may offer a distinct new version"; Home 12's provenance sheet on request). Starting position: original-only by default; the context layer earns its place with one fact the friend did not say; the generated-led treatment is a challenger and the brief already allows concluding that original-only wins.
6. **D6 continuity.** The grammar exists for an existing relationship (Entity 12's three effects; RA6's separate may-contact grant; Plans 91 F2's reactivation by reference with no membership carried). For a one-time encounter, Life 01 draws the row ("met once") and stops. No project draws a legitimate contact path to a stranger or a scoped-link grant that is not a friend circle. This is the second piece of new work, and the brief's own boundary applies: where no path exists, the private memory remains useful and nothing is invented.

## 3. What the social project must draw new

Everything else on the brief has a precedent somewhere above. These five do not, and they are where the 24-phone budget should go:

- The arrival view and the interface receding (B3), including finding the host with supplied facts only.
- Guest delivery without the app for the photo and the later access (C1 second half); Plans marks this as its own open Q9 and Apple Invites separates it into three capabilities.
- The sender's return after a share into a gathering, distinct from the pull-model return already drawn for a pin.
- The photo the host never took arriving on Home and the Occasion, as a view of the same source Life 07 already shows.
- The one-time encounter's contact path (C3, D6).

Status as one authored choice of audience and expiry is drawn only as the aperture Brainstorm's Rollcall shape ("CITY · 12 H"), flagged there as "closest shape to stories". Treat it as a candidate, not a precedent.

## 4. Anti-patterns the sibling projects already rejected

The social project must not reintroduce any of these; each is a recorded rejection, not an opinion.

- Viewer lists, counts, "+2" badges, read receipts, likes, streaks, "you haven't posted in a while", a posting cadence; a push or badge on a friend's arrival.
- A fifth root, a friend feed, a social inbox, per-person sections, popularity order, a follower graph, "Invite friends" as an empty-state payoff.
- Live location in any form: "friends near you", arrival push, presence inferred from movement or from a plan's date passing.
- Interpretation or paraphrase layered on a friend's words; a friend's photo promoted outside her mark; Vesper narrating its own restraint.
- Category pickers, new threads or filing on contribution; one "save" that keeps the place, her words and forwards at once; notifying an author of a private copy.
- Adjustment-first sheets, permissions matrices, proposal queues, vote counts, "2 of 3" consensus, task/assignee/checklist for "I'll bring dessert"; PR-1 no-owner authority (deferred) and PR-2 adoption-by-answers (withdrawn).
- Tombstones and strikethrough on release; "did you go?"; checkmarks by clock; an empty-dayparts skeleton for a sparse day.
- Drawing an unbuilt capability as a working button; "do not show an unavailable verb".
