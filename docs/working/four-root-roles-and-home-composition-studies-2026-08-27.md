# Four Root Roles and Fuller Home Composition Studies

## Decision

Home should be **feed-like in motion, but not feed-like in incentives**.

It may scroll, change with the person's life, mix several kinds of material,
and elevate one item above the rest. It must not optimize for freshness,
novelty, volume, or engagement. Its ranking target is lived relevance:

> **What deserves foreground attention now, what is already moving, and what
> grounded possibilities are beginning to open?**

Home is therefore not a dashboard and not a content feed. It is a **living
aperture onto the person's world**. One current thing comes into focus while
quieter commitments, places, people, and continuity remain visible around it.

The broader Home correction in
[Home Possibility Field and Generative Opportunity Portfolio](home-possibility-field-and-generative-opportunity-portfolio-2026-08-28.md)
separates dominance from abundance: Home has a strict demand budget and a
generous possibility budget. One thing may command attention without becoming
the only valuable thing on the page.

Home is also not an interpreter standing over the person. It may reconstruct,
compare, explain, compose, and open possibilities from authorized evidence. It
must not convert that evidence into unsolicited claims about personality,
motivation, emotion, or identity.

> **Interpret the experience, not the person. Show the pattern; leave its
> meaning to them.**

The user's phrase “a window from which you can blossom into possibilities” is
directionally right. Home does not own the full exploration or creation of
every possibility, but it may compose several substantially different
trajectories into one legible possible world. It delivers enough value to make
each trajectory intelligible, then hands the person into the root whose mode of
attention fits the next job.

## 1. The four roots are four postures over one lived world

The roots are not four databases, content types, or departments. The same
Place, Occasion, person, artifact, or thread can project into several roots,
but each root performs a different operation on it.

| Root | Native posture | Human question | Possibility takes the form of | Characteristic verbs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Attend and enter | What matters now, what is moving, and what is becoming possible? | One dominant consequence within a diverse, grounded field of action, exploration, relationship, capability, and return | notice, resume, respond, enter, defer |
| **Chat** | Understand and shape | What can I bring, ask, work through, or make happen? | A thought becoming clearer, an ambiguity becoming a proposal, or language becoming action | bring, ask, interpret, compare, imagine, decide, prepare |
| **Places** | Orient and inhabit | What is around me, what is this place, and how might I experience it? | A spatial, practical, cultural, or human way into the physical world | orient, notice, explore, compare, navigate, inhabit |
| **Life** | Accumulate and return | What is becoming true for me and between us over time? | A prior encounter, artifact, person, or pattern becoming newly meaningful or useful | remember, connect, revisit, correct, express, continue |

This creates a useful navigation shorthand, not a partition of product value:

```text
Home   = attention
Chat   = transformation
Places = situation
Life   = continuity
```

The canonical product moves—**Make sense, Open possibility, Help it work, and
Carry forward**—may all appear across the roots. Their weight is intentionally
uneven:

| Root posture | Make sense | Open possibility | Help it work | Carry forward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Home / attend** | strong | core | core when active | strong |
| **Chat / understand and shape** | core | strong | core | supporting |
| **Places / orient and inhabit** | core | core | strong | strong |
| **Life / accumulate and return** | strong | strong | supporting | core |

This prevents a seductive but damaging mapping such as “Home opens, Chat makes
sense, Places helps, Life remembers.” One Home unit can reconstruct the recent
journey, open a current New York possibility, protect a weekend commitment, or
carry forward a friend's perspective. One dominant job governs the unit; the
root governs the posture; the durable object still owns the consequence.

### 1.1 Home is the aperture, not the owner of everything

Home composes across domains. It can show an Occasion, Place, invitation,
movement cue, return, or quiet possibility because its job is cross-domain
attention. But it should expose only enough of each object to answer “why now?”
and make the next move obvious.

Home owns:

- present importance and relative elevation;
- a bounded view of what is already in motion;
- a diverse, bounded portfolio of grounded openings beyond obligation;
- strict limits on demands, decisions, and response debt rather than an
  artificial limit on complete-on-view value;
- the transition into the appropriate deeper root;
- intentional quiet when silence is the correct judgment.

Home does not own:

- full conversation or generative work;
- comprehensive Place exploration;
- a reverse-chronological life record;
- full Plan, Occasion, financial, permission, or history controls;
- an infinite inventory of recommendations.

### 1.2 Chat is where possibility becomes form

Chat is the most open-ended root. It accepts fragments before the person knows
what they are, answers immediate questions, interprets artifacts, negotiates
shared meaning, and prepares consequential actions.

Chat is where someone can say:

- “Why does the Colosseum make me think about Troy?”
- “Dinner at mine Friday. Invite Lena and Reza.”
- “This pasta tastes different. What am I noticing?”
- “We are exhausted—repair tonight without losing the part we cared about.”

Chat may create or revise durable objects, but it should not become their only
home. Once an Occasion, Place relationship, decision, or kept thread exists,
its current importance belongs on Home, its spatial truth in Places, and its
accumulated continuity in Life.

### 1.3 Places is where possibility becomes situated

Places should reveal ways into the world that are spatially and culturally
grounded. It can answer practical questions—distance, heat, closing time,
route, accessibility—as well as interpretive ones—what to notice, why a place
matters, how another person experienced it, or how the user's relationship to
it is changing.

Home may show “one way into Rome tonight.” Places should let the person inspect
the route, area, alternatives, source, atmosphere, and relationship to nearby
Places. Chat can explain or reshape the experience. Life can preserve what the
encounter later changes.

### 1.4 Life is where possibility compounds

Life is not merely the past. It contains active and accumulated personal and
shared truth: artifacts, encounters, patterns, relationships, occasions,
status, contributions, and returns. It shows why a later experience can be
different because of what has already happened.

Life's possibility is longitudinal:

- a movie makes Rome more legible;
- Rome changes how a movie is remembered;
- Sorrento changes what the person notices in pasta back in New York;
- one dinner changes how the next dinner is hosted;
- a friend's perspective becomes relevant at a Place months later.

Life should make this continuity inspectable and correctable. It must not turn
the person into a profile to be mined or a story they are asked to perform.

## 2. Home's feed-like qualities and hard limits

### Keep these feed-like qualities

- **Continuously recomposed:** the page changes as time, place, commitments,
  people, and authorized evidence change.
- **Heterogeneous:** an operational instrument, compact row, map field, social
  strip, or editorial passage may coexist.
- **Dynamically elevated:** one grounded object can rise above the others when
  its consequence or opportunity becomes material.
- **Browsable:** the person can look beyond the dominant item without first
  declaring a task.
- **Serendipitous:** an earned cultural, practical, social, or longitudinal
  connection can appear without an exact query.

### Reject these feed incentives

- reverse chronology as the organizing principle;
- infinite scroll;
- freshness or novelty for its own sake;
- engagement, taps, or time-on-surface as ranking targets;
- repeated recommendation cards;
- social performance, popularity, or broadcast metrics;
- manufacturing unfinished business to keep the page populated;
- pretending every interesting thing deserves action.

### Ranking doctrine

Importance should be reasoned from:

```text
material consequence
+ temporal immediacy
+ explicit user intent
+ situational relevance
+ responsibility to affected people
+ earned continuity
+ grounded opportunity
- attention cost
- privacy and social cost
- interruption cost
```

This is not a literal universal scoring formula. It names the reasons the
composition must be able to explain. “Because it is new” and “because similar
users clicked it” are not sufficient reasons.

The dominant item may be:

- something to do;
- something to understand;
- something to decide;
- something to notice;
- something to return to;
- or a truthful statement that nothing needs the person now.

## 3. Stable Home anatomy

The stable roles are not required to appear as five literal uppercase section
titles. They are learnable composition slots whose visible labels should name
the current human job.

### A. World read

A brief authored orientation to current context. It should sound like Vesper
has composed the person's situation, not queried a database.

Examples:

- “Thursday in Brooklyn. Dinner is taking shape.”
- “Rome, 8:12 PM. The heat changed the shape of tonight.”
- “Home in New York. Saturday still has room.”
- “Tuesday in New York. The evening is open.”

### B. Now — one dominant stage

The only region allowed to dominate the first viewport. Its geometry follows
the job:

- live instrument;
- voiced judgment plus typed receipt;
- bounded decision;
- spatial field;
- editorial return;
- calm quiet statement.

Its anatomy remains stable even when its visual form changes:

1. canonical object identity;
2. phase and people when relevant;
3. Vesper's authored read;
4. visible evidence or state;
5. one primary action or intentional silence;
6. explicit destination and owner.

### C. In motion — bounded durable state

Two or three compact rows show other Plans, Occasions, invitations, decisions,
or consequential threads. Rows express administration and continuity, not
seduction. They use concrete state such as now, next, settled, flexible, open,
changed, waiting, or returned.

More items live behind **All plans and occasions** or another honest count
door. “Everything in motion” means the person can trust that Home accounts for
the rest—not that every object must be rendered on the root.

### D. Possibility field — several ways into the world

A bounded portfolio may combine substantially different human modes:

- one spatial conviction, reachability field, route, or neighborhood aperture;
- one practical or creative possibility to make, try, host, or practice;
- one explanation, contrast, or horizon that changes what is legible;
- one relationship-mediated possibility; or
- one farther continuation that carries no urgency.

Not every mode appears, and no mode is a quota. The composer prefers diversity
over repeated examples: three venue cards are repetition, while one route, one
mechanism, one human opening, and one future horizon can reveal four different
powers. Visible titles name the present use: **Within 20 minutes**, **For after
dinner**, **Make this here**, **One way into Rome**, or **Further from here**.
Each item preserves scope and opens the root that owns its depth.

### E. With people — relational movement

Shown only when authorized social truth exists: a contribution, affected-person
decision, invitation, directed opening, or active shared Occasion. Facepiles,
friend strips, and addressed rows are preferable to a generic social feed.

There is no permanent CONNECT module and no generic invitation growth card.

### F. Continuity and recognition — earned returns without a quota

Quiet passages, artifacts, Place relationships, capability transfers,
second-occasion deltas, factual registers, or threads from before may appear
when each contributes a different kind of value. They open Life or the
canonical object. They do not ask for a recap merely because an event ended,
and several may coexist only when their contribution is non-redundant and their
demand is low.

### G. Stable doors

Small, predictable doors preserve capability even when evidence regions are
empty:

- All plans and occasions;
- Open Places in the current scope;
- Open the relevant Life thread;
- Talk about this in Chat.

Home should not contain a standing Chat composer. Object-level “Talk about
this” handoffs are enough; Chat remains a first-order root.

### 3.1 The Home contribution contract

Structural eligibility is not enough for an item to appear. Every rendered
unit must have a content contract:

```text
authorized evidence
  -> new contribution
  -> why now
  -> smallest expressive form
  -> optional consequence or complete ending
```

The five fields mean:

| Field | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Authorized evidence** | Which deliberately shared artifact, canonical object, current world fact, or audience-safe contribution grounds this? |
| **New contribution** | What has Vesper computed, related, researched, reconstructed, or prepared that the person did not already provide? |
| **Why now** | Why does this deserve attention in the current place, time, relationship, commitment, or opening? |
| **Expressive form** | Which native form makes the contribution immediately legible: instrument, route, comparison, sequence, receipt, juxtaposition, or passage? |
| **Ending** | Is the value complete on view? If a continuation exists, what single optional consequence is already prepared? |

A candidate satisfies **new contribution** only when it does at least one of
these jobs:

1. reconstructs something the person never assembled;
2. reveals a concrete relationship or recurrence across evidence;
3. adds outside knowledge that explains a lived observation;
4. juxtaposes authorized perspectives, people, places, or moments;
5. transforms fragments into a more useful or expressive object; or
6. prepares a grounded present or future possibility.

Counts, chronology, and recap can support these jobs, but a flat report does
not qualify merely because it is accurate. Restating something the person
already said does not become a connection because Vesper rephrased it.

### 3.2 Claim-scope boundary

The surface should make propositions about the **evidence, event, place, or
situation**, not about an inferred inner self.

| Safe default | Not safe as unsolicited Home copy |
| --- | --- |
| “The Sorrento–Amalfi ferry is the only travel leg photographed from departure to arrival.” | “You experience transitions as much as destinations.” |
| “The same railing composition appears in Nice, Amalfi, and Rome.” | “You return to thresholds when you want perspective.” |
| “Three photographed dishes use the same glossy emulsion; this technique explains part of the texture difference.” | “You are becoming a more discerning eater.” |
| “Two friends' authorized traces place Paris and Rome side by side on the same evening.” | “You seek connection through parallel experiences.” |

The first column may still be surprising and personal because the selection is
well fitted. It does not claim privileged access to subjective meaning.

Operationally:

- prefer “this trip,” “these photographs,” or “that evening” over stable
  second-person traits;
- never surface dwell time, passive attention, private query text, individual
  votes, or other keep-silent signals as evidence;
- keep provenance inspectable for surprising compositions without narrating
  surveillance in the default copy;
- make correction, dismissal, and source removal possible at the owning
  object; and
- reserve personal interpretation for a conversation in which the person
  explicitly asks Vesper to help interpret it.

This boundary extends the graph-legibility doctrine's rule that intelligence
should be felt as fit rather than announced as a label. It is also consistent
with contextual-integrity research: information use should remain appropriate
to the context in which it was provided, rather than treating artifact intake
as permission for unrestricted personal inference. Research on recommender
creepiness further identifies implicit profiling, causal ambiguity, and weak
control as trust risks.

Sources: [Nissenbaum, *Privacy as Contextual Integrity*](https://digitalcommons.law.uw.edu/wlr/vol79/iss1/10/),
[Torkamaan et al., *How Can They Know That?*](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid%3A052e0862-a7ec-46bc-ac33-f34523224725),
[Microsoft, *Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction*](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=564561),
and [Google PAIR, *Mental Models*](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapter/mental-models/).

### 3.3 Post-return balance

Recency does not grant a finished trip ownership of Home. After return:

1. the first viewport belongs to the person's current world and the best
   grounded opening or commitment in it;
2. the recent trip may rise only when it materially changes a present action;
3. one or two retrospective compositions may appear below current life when
   they pass the contribution contract;
4. a compact factual trip register remains available even when no interpretive
   return earns elevation; and
5. ignoring a return creates no unfinished task, recap debt, or future prompt.

## 4. Composition study A — planning a Brooklyn dinner

### Situation

The host created a Friday dinner through Chat, invited Lena and Reza, and
accepted two contributions. Rain now makes the balcony plan unreliable. One
choice affects everyone, but most of the Occasion is already settled.

### First viewport

**World read**

```text
THURSDAY IN BROOKLYN
Dinner is taking shape. One part changed with the rain.
```

**Now stage — Brooklyn dinner**

- Friday · 7:30 PM · 5 people
- Vesper read: “Food and people are settled. The balcony no longer is.”
- Evidence instrument:
  - SETTLED — date, guests, main dishes
  - FLEXIBLE — arrival window
  - OPEN — indoor table for five
- Primary action: **See the indoor shape**
- Identity tap: **Open occasion**

This is a prepared consequence, not a setup form. Chat already created the
Occasion and resolved the relevant people.

### Supporting composition

**In motion**

1. `Saturday afternoon · still open · with Maya`
2. `Rome photos · Giulia added 3 · nothing needed`
3. Door: `All 4 plans and occasions`

**For after dinner — two-way Places fork**

- quiet walk toward Brooklyn Heights;
- warm late bar within twelve minutes.

The fork opens Places with Friday, neighborhood, group, rain, and walking
scope preserved. It is not silently added to the dinner.

**With people — contribution strip**

`Lena is bringing wine · Reza added a photo of the dish he is making`

No reply is demanded. Tapping opens the contribution in the Occasion; “Talk
about it” opens group Chat.

**Continuity — quiet passage**

`Last time this group ate together, everyone stayed in the kitchen.`

This appears only if grounded in authorized prior evidence and if it materially
helps the indoor shape. Otherwise the region is absent.

### What this composition proves

- Local occasions are first-class; Home is not a renamed trip surface.
- Multiplayer state can be visible without an activity feed.
- The dominant issue is narrow even when the underlying Occasion is rich.
- Home opens spatial possibility without trying to finish the plan itself.

## 5. Composition study B — live in Rome

### Situation

The person and their girlfriend have left the Colosseum in August heat. Dinner
is booked, walking time has lengthened, and there is a shaded water stop on the
way. The Aeneas connection is meaningful but must not compete with immediate
physical reality.

### First viewport

**World read**

```text
ROME · 8:12 PM
The heat changed the route, not the evening.
```

**Now stage — live instrument**

- NOW — leave the Colosseum side entrance
- `18 min walking · shaded water stop after 6 min`
- Dinner remains reachable with eleven minutes of margin
- Primary action: **Open the shaded route**
- Secondary object door: **Tonight's plan**

If the deadline becomes material, the route instrument can suppress nearly all
other Home material. If the evening is comfortably on track, the supporting
field remains visible below.

### Supporting composition

**Still to come — compact temporal rows**

1. `8:45 · dinner · settled`
2. `After · open · no decision needed`

**Along the way — spatial field**

A compact map shows the committed destination, shaded route, water stop, and
one honest fallback. It opens Places in route context.

**Together — participant strip**

Two-person Occasion identity, shared route state, and a quiet indication that
both people are affected by a reroute. No chat transcript or inferred mood.

**While you are here — one cultural opening**

```text
AENEAS BECAME AN ARGUMENT FOR AUGUSTUS
Under Augustus, Virgil's Aeneid tied Rome's Trojan ancestry to a new imperial
order; Augustan monuments repeated the claim in public stone.
```

This earns the opening only if it extends the connection the person already
made at the Colosseum. Merely repeating “Troy appears in Rome's origin story”
does not qualify as synthesis. The treatment opens the relevant Rome Places and
sources or Chat for a deeper explanation. It stays visually quieter than route
and heat. Under pressure, it disappears rather than becoming homework.

### What this composition proves

- Practical and cultural intelligence can coexist under a clear hierarchy.
- Home says what to do now; Places owns the full situated world.
- The product can create serendipity without obstructing the lived moment.
- Live multiplayer means shared state and affected people, not constant chat.

## 6. Composition study C — the first week home in New York

### Situation and available evidence

The person has just returned from Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi, and Rome. It is
Thursday in New York and the weekend still has room. The person has deliberately
shared with Vesper plane, train, and ferry tickets; photographs; food images;
explicitly kept notes; shared trip artifacts with the girlfriend; and
audience-authorized traces from friends who were in Paris at the same time.

The trip is recent but no longer operationally dominant. Home should first
make New York feel available again, then let Europe contribute several
substantively different possibilities when they pass the contribution contract:
practical transfer, interpretation, social juxtaposition, creative practice,
factual reconstruction, recognition, or a future branch. The restraint applies
to demand, redundancy, and visual competition—not to an arbitrary return count.
Exact counts, times, and comparisons must be computed from the evidence; the
design fixture must not invent them.

### Full-scroll order

#### 1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · THURSDAY
Home again. Saturday still has room.
```

This is present-tense orientation, not an emotional diagnosis of returning
home. If calendar evidence does not support the second line, Vesper replaces it
with the strongest truthful current opening.

#### 2. Now stage — one prepared New York possibility

```text
ONE GOOD WAY BACK INTO NEW YORK

Wall Street ferry → Red Hook waterfront → dinner within a short walk
Saturday afternoon into evening
```

The rendered instrument includes current ferry service, weather fit, total
travel time, walking load, one available dinner candidate, and an honest return
window. This exact possibility appears only if current world data supports it;
otherwise the composer selects another grounded New York shape.

- New contribution: a feasible half-day has already been composed across
  transit, weather, Place quality, availability, and the person's open window.
- Why now: it turns the first available weekend at home into an immediately
  usable possibility.
- Primary action: **Open Saturday** → Places with route and time scope intact.
- Complete ending: seeing or ignoring it changes nothing and creates no task.

The ferry may fit partly because the recent journey makes water travel locally
relevant, but Home never says “you are a ferry person” or exposes an inferred
preference. The fit is carried by the proposal.

#### 3. In motion — bounded current life

At most two compact rows account for real commitments or shared openings, for
example:

1. `Friday · [current local commitment] · settled / changed / open`
2. `This weekend · [authorized opening with a person] · no decision yet`

If neither exists, the region disappears. Europe does not occupy this region
merely because it is recent.

#### 4. Continue here — practical continuity with present value

```text
WHY THE PASTA TASTED DIFFERENT WASN'T ONLY THE PASTA

Three photographed dishes share a glossy emulsion rather than pooled sauce.
Finishing the noodles with starch-rich water explains part of the texture.
```

The treatment is a compact comparison of the actual food photographs, one
outside explanation, and one New York Place where the distinction is currently
perceivable. It does not call the person a foodie or say their taste evolved.

- New contribution: outside culinary knowledge explains a lived observation.
- Why now: the insight can change a meal or cooking decision in New York.
- Primary action: **See it in New York** → one scoped Places result, not a
  generic list of Italian restaurants.
- Complete ending: the explanation itself is useful; no save or reflection is
  requested.

#### 5. With people — authorized juxtaposition

```text
TWO CITIES, THE SAME EVENING

ROME                         PARIS
[authorized artifact]        [authorized friend artifact]
[one grounded contrast or recurrence across time, setting, or choice]
```

This is eligible only when both sides were deliberately made available to the
viewer. The contribution is not “your friends were also traveling.” It is a
specific juxtaposition the people had not already assembled—for example, two
evenings changing direction within the same hour for different local reasons.

- New contribution: synchronized, audience-safe perspectives reveal a concrete
  contrast or parallel.
- Why now: the overlap is recent and can create recognition between people.
- Optional consequence: **Send this to [friend]** appears only after the viewer
  invokes sharing and approves their own words. There is no response debt.

#### 6. From the journey — one evidence-first reveal

```text
THE ONLY JOURNEY PHOTOGRAPHED ALL THE WAY THROUGH

Sorrento → Amalfi by ferry
[actual duration] · [actual photo count] · first frame to last frame
```

A horizontal sequence aligns the ferry ticket, departure, coastline, arrival,
and photograph timestamps. The pattern is the product. Vesper does not append
“you experience transitions as much as destinations.”

- New contribution: ticket and photo evidence become a sequence the person
  never manually assembled.
- Why now: recent artifacts are complete enough to reconstruct accurately.
- Destination: **Open the crossing** → Life.
- Complete ending: it can simply be seen; Keep, Share, and Reflect are absent.

#### 7. The trip register — factual utility without pretending it is insight

```text
NICE → SORRENTO → AMALFI → ROME

[verified flight, rail, ferry, road, and walking legs]
[matched tickets and reservations] · [unresolved gap, if any]
```

This region reconciles the actual movement record and makes source corrections
available. It is allowed to be factual because factual reconstruction has
utility; it is not promoted as a profound reading. **Open Europe** enters the
durable Life object.

#### 8. Stable bottom doors

- **All plans and occasions**
- **Open New York in Places**
- **Europe in Life**
- object-scoped **Talk about this** where relevant

### Deliberately withheld from this scroll

The Aeneas thread is substantive only when Vesper adds something beyond the
connection already made at the Colosseum—for example, how Virgil's *Aeneid*
and Augustan imagery turned Trojan ancestry into an argument for Rome's new
political order. Even then, it competes for attention with every other Home
candidate. It does not appear merely because it is interesting and recent; it
remains available in Life or Chat when it adds too little, repeats another
trajectory, or contributes less than the present world can offer today.

### What this composition proves

- Returning Home can project life forward without suppressing the value of a
  factual or interpretive retrospective.
- Personalization can be felt in selection, timing, and prepared form without
  being narrated as a theory of the person.
- Multiplayer creates novel juxtaposition, not an activity feed.
- A past artifact earns Home only by adding reconstruction, explanation,
  juxtaposition, transformation, or present consequence.
- The page delivers value on view; every continuation remains optional.

## 7. Composition study D — an ordinary open Tuesday

### Situation

Months later, the person is home in New York. Nothing urgent or recently
returned deserves the crown. The evening is open; a nearby Place is genuinely
strong tonight; a friend deliberately shared one useful observation about it;
Friday's dinner is already settled; and one Saturday opening remains shared
with Maya.

Quiet describes the consequence posture and aesthetic—not a shortage of
interesting content.

### Full-scroll order

#### 1. World read

```text
TUESDAY IN NEW YORK
The evening is open. Here is one good way into it.
```

#### 2. Now stage — immediately engageable, not urgent

```text
[PLACE NAME] + [ONE NATURAL CONTINUATION]

Start with the back room Lena noticed.
17 minutes on foot · both open late · no reservation
```

The native treatment is a small route field, not a recommendation card with a
paragraph. It combines one high-conviction Place, the authorized friend trace,
current opening hours and conditions, and one compatible continuation nearby.

- New contribution: Vesper turns several truths into one usable evening.
- Why now: the fit depends on tonight's hours, conditions, reachability, and
  the person's genuinely open time.
- Primary action: **Open the walk** → Places.
- Complete ending: no response, RSVP, save, or explanation is required.

#### 3. In motion — calm administration

1. `Friday dinner · settled enough`
2. `Saturday with Maya · open after 3`
3. Door: `All plans and occasions`

No large settled card competes with the open evening.

#### 4. Along the way — one world explanation

One Place or streetscape fact may appear when it materially deepens the route:

```text
THIS BLOCK HAS LIVED THREE LIVES
[former use] → [later use] → [what remains visible now]
```

The content must add researched local substance that is perceptible on the
walk. Generic neighborhood prose does not qualify. It opens the Place detail,
not a lesson flow.

#### 5. With people — one addressed opening

```text
SATURDAY AFTER 3
Maya has left this window open with you.
```

If existing shared state supports it, Vesper may prepare one concrete
possibility underneath. It does not ask both people to fill out preferences or
turn the opening into a group poll. The affected people authorize any actual
commitment.

#### 6. One earned return—or nothing

A prior artifact or Place relationship appears only if it makes tonight,
Friday's dinner, or Saturday materially richer. “The question you asked in
Sorrento has a local continuation” is insufficient by itself. A valid return
must state the actual continuation and deliver the useful distinction on view.

If none passes the contribution contract, the region is absent. Home still
feels full because the present world is rich, not because the archive was
mined to fill space.

#### 7. Stable bottom doors

- **All plans and occasions**
- **Open nearby Places**
- **Life**
- object-scoped **Talk about this**

### What this composition proves

- Quiet Home can remain aesthetically calm while being immediately
  engageable.
- Richness can come from present world knowledge, authorized social context,
  and composition—not only from retrospective memory.
- One conviction plus one continuation is more useful than a carousel of
  recommendations.
- Silence applies to pressure and obligation, not to imagination.

## 7.1 Cross-composition four-move admission audit

The four moves are an internal review lens, never four visible Home sections.
Each unit names one dominant job; additional moves count only when the same
evidence and form genuinely perform them.

| Situation | Dominant Home job | Strong supporting moves | Demand budget | Correctly weak or absent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Planning · Brooklyn dinner** | **Help it work:** resolve the rain-dependent room without reopening the whole Occasion | Make sense of settled/flexible/open state; Open an optional after-dinner fork; Carry authorized group evidence into the decision | One prepared action | No setup, poll, recap, or social feed |
| **Live · Rome** | **Help it work:** preserve dinner through heat, route, water, and margin | Make sense of current feasibility; Open one cultural layer only when attention permits | One immediate action | Carry forward stays backgrounded; cultural content disappears under pressure |
| **Returned · New York** | **Open possibility:** make the current weekend inhabitable rather than letting Europe own Home | Make sense through culinary explanation, social juxtaposition, and reconstruction; Help the local route work; Carry selected evidence into Life and present capability | Zero required responses; one optional route action | No reflection prompt, personality reading, or trip-wide hero |
| **Quiet · open Tuesday** | **Open possibility:** offer one immediately usable way into the present city | Make sense through one situated Place explanation; Help through verified route fit; Carry forward only an earned relational or capability return | Zero required responses | No archive filler, generic recommendations, or manufactured urgency |

Admission rules:

1. Do not add a unit merely to cover a missing move. Absence is healthier than
   weak evidence or duplicated value.
2. **Make sense** requires a representational gain beyond retrieval or
   paraphrase.
3. **Open possibility** requires a grounded way in that is available now and
   optional by design.
4. **Help it work** must absorb real practical or coordinative burden and land
   any consequence with its durable owner.
5. **Carry forward** requires a named later difference; storage, recap, and
   recency do not qualify.
6. Social material must add authorized perspective, participation, or
   coordination—not activity for its own sake.
7. The screen still obeys one attention hierarchy: one dominant unit, very few
   demands, several possible complete endings, and stable root-native doors.

## 8. Cross-root transition rules

The roots remain coherent when an opening changes mode without losing context.

| From | Trigger | Destination | Preserved context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | “Why this?” / “Help me decide” / “Change the plan” | Chat | source object, current evidence, people, unresolved job |
| Home | map, route, nearby fork, Place conviction | Places | scope, time, route, selected object, occasion constraints |
| Home | artifact, return, pattern, shared moment | Life | thread, audience, provenance, relevant Occasion or Place |
| Chat | prepared Occasion, commitment, or invitation | Home / object | canonical object and receipt; current importance becomes eligible for Home |
| Chat | Place interpretation or spatial question | Places | query, evidence, selected Place, current situation |
| Chat | kept artifact or thread | Life | source, authorship, audience, correction controls |
| Places | “Work this through” | Chat | selected Place, scope, candidates, practical conditions |
| Places | saved relationship or encountered Place | Life | Place identity, evidence, privacy, occurrence |
| Life | “Return here” | Places | original Place plus current context, not a frozen historical view |
| Life | “Continue this” | Chat | thread history, source, people, audience, current question |

Global object details should open over the current root and return to the exact
prior scroll, selection, and scope. A person should not manually reconstruct
context after following one of Home's openings.

## 9. Product and design laws exposed by the studies

1. **Home is composed, not populated.** More eligible objects do not imply more
   rendered modules.
2. **Dominance is explainable.** A person should be able to infer why the lead
   item is elevated from time, consequence, intent, people, or continuity.
3. **Possibility is bounded and grounded.** Home offers apertures, not an
   endless recommendation supply.
4. **Demand and possibility have different budgets.** One consequence may
   dominate while several complete-on-view, low-pressure possibilities coexist.
   Limiting response debt must not become a limit on delivered value.
5. **Different jobs require different visual registers.** Stage, row, map,
   strip, receipt, and passage should not collapse into one universal card.
6. **One object, several projections.** Root-native views preserve canonical
   identity, revision, audience, authority, and provenance.
7. **Value precedes extraction.** Returned Home synthesizes before asking the
   person to keep, share, classify, or reflect.
8. **Multiplayer appears as shared state.** People, contributions, affected
   decisions, and authority are visible without turning Home into activity.
9. **Quiet governs pressure, not imagination.** An open day can avoid urgency,
   obligation, and fabricated importance while still offering a substantial,
   immediately engageable way into the world.
10. **Home accounts for the rest without rendering all of it.** Count doors and
   stable object indexes create trust without feed density.
11. **The next root is determined by the next mode of attention.** Understand
    and shape in Chat; orient and inhabit in Places; accumulate and return in
    Life.
12. **Contribution precedes elevation.** A candidate must reconstruct, relate,
    explain, juxtapose, transform, or prepare—not merely report or paraphrase.
13. **Interpret the experience, not the person.** Surface propositions about
    evidence, events, places, and situations; leave subjective meaning and
    identity to the person unless they explicitly invite interpretation.
14. **The present is not displaced by recency.** A completed trip, meeting, or
    occasion may enrich Home, but it does not own the crown simply because it
    produced many artifacts.

## 10. Design questions to resolve in visual composition

The product roles are now clear enough that these questions should be answered
in screen design rather than another high-altitude taxonomy:

1. How visually different can the Now stage become across quiet, live,
   decision, spatial, and returned treatments while retaining one learned
   location and anatomy?
2. How much of the first viewport belongs to the dominant stage versus the
   first glimpse of In motion?
3. Which supporting register should peek into the first viewport so Home feels
   expansive without weakening focus?
4. Should a person be able to say **Keep this here**, **Not now**, or **Less
   like this** directly on Home, and how can those controls avoid turning
   importance into feed tuning?
5. How do the map field, editorial passage, and social strip break the vertical
   card rhythm while remaining accessible at large text sizes?
6. What is the calm visual treatment for an open day that offers several
   substantively different possibilities without becoming a recommendation
   shelf, manufacturing urgency, or feeling empty?

These four situations should now be rendered as complete mobile pages using a
shared visual foundation. The purpose is not to select one static template. It
is to establish how one stable Home can change emphasis and instrument while
remaining recognizable, coherent, and alive.
