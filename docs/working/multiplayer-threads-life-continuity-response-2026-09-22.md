## 2. Against the handoff's acceptance questions

- **Understandable without the word "thread":** yes. Nothing in the drawings uses it; the label is "Kept", and each page is named by what it holds.
- **A list can stay a list; a personal thread can stay private indefinitely:** drawn (17.2, 17.1.2, 17.3.1). Setup is a name; collaborators are optional and never arrive by default.
- **Life without manual curation or blanket retention:** the visit evidence in 17.2.2 is a labelled fixture (a card payment and the person's own photographs). The kept question in 17.3.1 is an explicitly kept item, not remembered conversation. No retention agreement is portrayed as shipped.
- **Saved, suggested, planned, visited and liked distinguishable:** drawn in 17.2.1–17.2.3. Being there did not remove the place from the list, mark an intention complete, infer liking, or say anything about who else was there.
- **Building without voting, matched effort or management:** 17.4.1–17.4.3. No totals, no ranking, no prompt for the quiet member, no revival prompt.
- **Opening, invitation, hard coordination:** 16.B1 (opening, no attendance machinery), 16.B4 (invitation, answerable), 16.C1 (optional instrument, asked for). None is a mode the user selects before posting.
- **Availability, reactions and silence never become attendance:** 16.B3 keeps each person's words as words; 16.B4 asks Maya anyway; 16.B6 shows Dana's row unchanged with no badge or reminder. The single exception is 16.B5's explicit conditional commitment, honoured only while the details stay inside what she said.
- **Joining without exposing private history:** 17.3.2 names exactly what Maya sees and exactly what stays Nora's.
- **Later use beats scrolling a transcript:** 17.4.3 answers a real question from the collection, in the friends' own words, with two current facts.
- **Quiet and unacted-on possibilities are satisfying:** 17.1.2, 17.4.2, and the pier entry nobody has been to.
- **Fits board 15 and the other roots:** board 16's Life frame and board 17 use Life's own grammar (board 15, lifted from Life 07). No collection dashboard, no fifth root, no new destination.
- **Sharing, kept subjects and conversations distinguishable but connected:** a share (10), a kept thing (17), and replies on a share or on an item (16.B2, 17.4.1) are visibly different; the person page (15) connects them.
- **Current context expires; agreed details stay reliable:** 16.D1 and 16.B6.

## 3. Conflicts, named rather than resolved

1. **Board 16, September 21 version.** It made anyone who picked an option automatically "in". Superseded by this pass. This is a design correction, not a founder ruling on the underlying question.
2. **Group/Social charter.** Still carries voting-first and trip-room rulings. Board 16 now contradicts voting-first. Recorded for reconciliation by that charter's owner; nothing is implemented here.
3. **Group chat.** Ruled out by the founder on September 21. The handoff asks for thread-level conversation. Drawn as replies attached to a share or to an item, with no room for the group. If a persistent group conversation is wanted, that ruling has to be revisited explicitly.
4. **Group history on joining.** Board 12 ruled "forward-only"; the handoff says historical access is still open. Board 17 sidesteps it by inviting into *selected material*. The general case remains unresolved.
5. **Board 15's four decisions.** Ownership, quote-or-post, one composer, the ledger. Still unruled, and this pass does not promote any "RULED — not yet —" caption to a decision.
6. **"With" versus tagging policy.** Board 16 draws a minimal split (co-presence, visible association, entry into the shared record). Whether the other person's keeping needs the author's agreement is undrawn and open.

## 5. Recommended treatments, not decisions

- Keep the poll as an instrument a host asks for, never inserted because a conversation contains alternatives.
- Keep "availability" and "count me in" as different statements, with the second honoured only inside its stated condition.
- Keep "Keep" (personal) and "add to something shared" as separately named actions.
- Keep additive participation: anyone adds their own material and replies; nobody edits or removes another person's.
- Name collections by what they hold. Avoid a type picker, a purpose field, and the word "thread" in the interface.
