# Decision proposal: one connection, deliberate sharing audiences

## 1. Status and revision

**Proposed, not adopted or implemented.** This September 7 revision replaces
the first recommendation for a separately invited, asymmetric friends circle.
It does not change the runtime audience vocabulary.

Recommend one understandable optional connection, with a clear audience choice
when sharing. A person should not have to accept a connection and then accept
membership in another person's friends roster to understand ordinary sharing.
A bounded guest or addressed recipient does not have to become a connection.

## 2. Experience and semantic proposal

- **Connection is not all-access.** Mutual connection establishes the named
  contact capability in the companion proposal. It does not itself publish
  private material or expose history, location, availability or plans.
- **Friends is a sharing choice, not a second invitation workflow.** Resolve it
  from the author's selected eligible connections. Named recipients and an
  Occasion audience remain available for different jobs. An author may exclude
  people without creating a visible friendship rank or notifying them.
- **Resolve audience explicitly.** Bind the chosen recipients and relevant
  policy revision at the authorized send boundary. Recheck restrictions at
  delivery/read; queued delivery cannot pick up newly added connections.
  Later additions do not gain old shares. Ending a connection stops new
  delivery; access to old material follows its declared grant, withdrawal and
  block policy, which the implementation contract must specify.
- **No automatic audience default adopted here.** Compare a clear reusable
  audience choice against per-send selection. Reduce repeated setup only where
  the person knowingly chose that default; always make the actual audience
  legible and editable. Private Ask never becomes a post.
- **Media remain broad.** Photos, words, places, maps, recipes, articles and
  other eligible objects use the same sharing semantics. Rights and source
  custody still constrain what may be sent.
- **Location/time are meaningful controls, not homework.** Reuse declared
  precision and applicable expiry defaults where valid; surface choices when
  they materially change disclosure. Do not require a custom expiry form for
  every ordinary object. Place precision is irrelevant for non-spatial material.

## 3. Root behavior

Places supports generous browsing of deliberately shared spatial material,
including sparse supply and a return to independent world value. It is not an
exhaust-derived activity feed, but “never a feed” must not prohibit the accepted
browsing experience.

Home owns addressed value and shared consequences; preserve the accepted
featured non-spatial Status exception. Life provides eligible records and
retrieval. Root placement never adds audience or AI-use rights. Private Ask
about received material uses only sources admitted for that job.

## 4. Existing owners and implementation boundary

The initial audit's claim that no audience persistence is possible was too
broad. Named-person/Occasion audience vocabulary, relationship grants and pair
circles already exist. Trace their actual command, custody and delivery paths.

Evaluate resolving the user-facing Friends choice to existing named recipients
before adding a circle kind, enum or table. Persist effective scope, author,
source references and policy revision through the appropriate owner; no raw
internal grant payload must be exposed in a card just to make authorship clear.
The [corrected map](social-experience-capability-map-2026-09-07.md) owns the
evidence limits and cross-lane reconciliation.

## 5. Acceptance cases

1. Connected Maya shares to her chosen audience; excluded Priya sees nothing.
   Maya did not first administer a second membership invitation.
2. Theo connects later and does not receive or discover an older share.
3. Maya sends a map or recipe as easily as a photo; no new social mode appears.
4. Nora enjoys it without replying; Maya receives no viewing/Keep report.
5. Withdrawal revalidates in-flight and retained source-dependent projections;
   independently sourced world facts remain. Do not promise erasure of external
   copies or certify all-consumer repair without tests.
6. A casual non-spatial Status retains its accepted Home doorway; ordinary
   place sharing does not duplicate the Places field on Home.

## 6. Still to decide

Exact future-sharing defaults; connection-end effects on existing grants;
reusable audience selection; guest eligibility; and any required owner/schema
extension. These need contract review and comprehension checks before adoption.
The user-facing simplification does not collapse the five authority axes.
