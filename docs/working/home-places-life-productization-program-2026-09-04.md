# Home, Places, and Life Productization Program

## 0. Decision

The next product-bearing program is an integrated **Home + Places + Life**
productization pass.

It is not:

- three independent page redesigns;
- another general entity-system program;
- a Home-only proof loop;
- a reason to redesign Chat in this lane;
- a repository-wide noun migration; or
- authorization to promote default-off behavior publicly.

The program must make one governed lived-world model feel coherent through
three distinct orientations:

| Root | Orientation | Product job | Failure mode to prevent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Now | Return the most valuable current composition across the person's world | Feed, task list, trip dashboard, or reflective homework |
| **Places** | World | Make spatial truth, current affordance, personal relationship, human perspective, and possible movement intelligible | Generic map, ratings directory, or recommendation shelf |
| **Life** | Continuity | Make what Vesper has held findable, understandable, correctable, and useful again | Archive dump, second Home, or filing workflow |

The organizing test is:

> Can the same governed evidence become timely value in Home, spatial value in
> Places, and durable continuity in Life without duplicating truth, asking the
> person to do synthesis, or presenting the roots as unrelated products?

## 1. Scope and lane boundaries

### 1.1 In scope

- Home and Places real-owner composition, editorial quality, native behavior,
  and internal-cohort readiness;
- Life's finite corpus, four lenses, exact refinding, bounded Returns,
  Together, and repair;
- shared active-seat arbitration across Home, Places, and Life;
- shared identity, Source, audience, grant, revision, expiry, correction,
  withdrawal, consequence, receipt, and exact-return behavior;
- social contribution where it materially changes a current possibility,
  Place understanding, or durable shared record;
- real-device and degraded-state acceptance for all three roots; and
- rollout and rollback mechanics for one coherent internal rehearsal.

### 1.2 Chat boundary

Chat remains the contribution and agency root, but its surface behavior is out
of scope for this program. This lane may:

- consume already-governed Sources, owners, and contributions that entered
  through Chat;
- navigate to the existing Chat route with explicit origin context;
- preserve exact return from a Chat-owned continuation; and
- test that correction or withdrawal initiated through existing owners
  recompiles the three roots.

This lane must not:

- redesign the Chat home or composer;
- add a Chat discovery feed;
- silently create or select a conversation;
- introduce a second Chat writer; or
- make a root action look committed merely because Chat opened.

### 1.3 Entity boundary

Entity implementation proceeds in a separate task. Home, Places, and Life
consume its stable contract rather than block on its internal design.

This lane may depend on:

- canonical `ResourceRef` / `EntityRef` identity;
- viewer-safe presentation reads;
- typed destination resolution;
- canonical object-page opening;
- revision and expiry;
- correction, removal, or relationship invalidation events; and
- exact return to the originating root unit.

This lane does not own entity presentation DTOs, research generation,
relationship storage, redirect resolution, or object-page visual composition.
If an entity destination is unavailable, the originating root degrades
honestly; it does not synthesize an object page or copy entity truth locally.

## 2. Current implementation level set

### 2.1 Home

Already present:

- the four-root shell routes the existing Trips tab to `HomeRootExperience`;
- v2 Home has bounded owner reads, root-specific selection, value-over-silence
  judgment, posture arbitration, semantic composition, delivery/treatment
  evidence, consequence actions, Source inspection, proof expiry, and exact
  return;
- the native v2 renderer covers the currently admitted kinds and all seven
  postures; and
- the compatibility Home read remains available after v2 transport or
  contract failure.

Still open:

- two real accounts do not yet receive a consistently populated,
  editorially accepted portfolio;
- real model output has been operationally admitted but has not cleared the
  non-paraphrase and new-substance bar;
- low-risk Moment and protected-attention truth remain incomplete;
- the seven postures have mock/internal evidence but not a complete real-data
  and physical-device matrix; and
- public rollout remains ineligible.

### 2.2 Places

Already present:

- the mature Places workspace retains map, search, scope, save, provider,
  relationship, movement, and degraded-state capabilities;
- the joined governed runtime uses one Places feed plus one admitted semantic
  projection rather than two competing feed reads;
- server admission can change which mature cards survive and binds qualifying
  cards back to semantic delivery and causal treatment;
- canonical destinations, exact return, location lifetime, account lifetime,
  Source inspection, consequence confirmation, and owner readback exist; and
- a thin v2-only screen remains inspection infrastructure rather than the
  production cutover.

Still open:

- World Field, Place Focus, Place Path, and Live Reduction are not yet proven
  as one continuous production encounter;
- map and composition need stronger one-result-set behavior across scope and
  search transformations;
- current condition, reachability, timing, fallback, and freshness coverage is
  incomplete;
- practical, relationship, social, and editorial depth must remain distinct
  without becoming separate shelves; and
- native private-provider, location-permission, offline, process-death, and
  cross-account evidence remains incomplete.

### 2.3 Life

Already present:

- a default-off additive native Life v1 route;
- a typed read-only Life projection over viewer-relative Experience Graph
  records;
- rich, ordinary, thin, and yielded delivery states;
- a bounded Time digest, full-record door, and optional Return shape;
- a separate truth-aware refinding contract and deterministic prototype; and
- a canonical Life v1 experience contract covering Time, Places, Threads,
  People, dossiers, Everything kept, Returns, Together, and repair.

Still open:

- the production projection assigns every current candidate to Time;
- Places, Threads, and People are labels rather than functioning lenses;
- entries route through generic history until their dossier or object owner is
  available;
- root counts do not yet reconcile a complete one-corpus inventory;
- refinding reads legacy trip and itinerary substrates rather than the same
  canonical Life corpus;
- the root has no production Return generator or cross-root active-seat
  arbitration;
- Together, correction, withdrawal, and dependent recompilation are not a
  complete consumer experience; and
- the current native Return uses a card treatment despite the accepted
  uncarded Life law.

### 2.4 Overall diagnosis

Home and Places have substantial system and consumer substrate but still need
real value and release evidence. Life has a strong experience contract but a
much thinner consumer implementation.

The next bottleneck is therefore not another universal service. It is the
productization seam that makes real owner evidence produce differentiated,
complete value across all three roots.

## 3. Target milestone: three-root internal coherence candidate

The target is not a public release. It is one internal posture in which two
signed-in accounts can use Home, Places, and Life as one product.

The milestone is met only when:

1. **Home is valuable now.** It returns a true orientation, at most one
   unresolved demand, and substantive complete-on-view value across the
   relevant regions.
2. **Places is spatially useful.** Scope, map, composition, Focus, earned Path,
   and Live Reduction operate as one encounter over the same result set.
3. **Life is useful later.** The same governed world is finite, searchable,
   lensable, inspectable, and correctable without filing work.
4. **The roots are differentiated.** A contribution is not copied into three
   cards. Home explains why now; Places owns spatial depth; Life owns deliberate
   return and lineage.
5. **The loop closes.** A prior Source can improve a current opening, a current
   consequence can become an Occurrence or Outcome, and the result can later be
   recovered in Life.
6. **Social meaning is lawful.** Attributed material appears only for the
   authorized viewer and only where it changes value.
7. **Repair is causal.** Correction, withdrawal, expiry, account change, or
   owner revision removes or repairs every dependent projection.
8. **Native behavior is credible.** iOS and Android cover loading, stale,
   offline, process death, accessibility, exact return, and safe rollback.

## 4. One shared acceptance world

The three roots must be evaluated from the same governed corpus. Fixtures are
representative conformance cases, not custom product implementations.

### 4.1 Required situations

| ID | Situation | Evidence roles | Home obligation | Places obligation | Life obligation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HPL-01 Ordinary New York** | No trip urgency; an ordinary week and open local time | Current Moment, saved Places, local world truth, prior Sources | Useful orientation and earned possibilities without demand or filler | Scoped local field with practical and substantive branches | Stable recent record; no adaptive novelty theater |
| **HPL-02 Returned from Europe** | Recent Nice–Sorrento–Amalfi–Rome journey while current life has resumed in New York | Tickets, movements, photographs, itinerary reality, world context, current openings | Current New York remains foreground; only materially relevant transfer from Europe may appear | Current Place relations and earned spatial continuations; no memory museum | Journey reconstruction, refinding, evidence lineage, and one earned Return |
| **HPL-03 Live or disrupted** | A current Plan, Occasion, route, provider state, or commitment changes | Current condition, owner revision, affected people, alternatives | Stable next step or recovery dominant; no success claim before readback | Live Reduction with reachability, alternatives, and freshness | Current Return seat yields; later receipt and Outcome become recoverable |
| **HPL-04 Addressed social perspective** | A friend contributes an authorized Paris experience or Place perspective | Author, audience, grant, membership epoch, contribution, Place identity | Appears only if it changes a current possibility | Adds attributed Place understanding or an earned path; never “friends nearby” | Preserves authorized shared record and supports withdrawal |
| **HPL-05 Sparse or unavailable** | New account, thin record, missing provider, offline state, expired proof, or revoked grant | Explicit absences and degradations | Honest quiet or cold value; no intake assignment | Stable identity and available depth; dependents disappear | Finite thin/zero/yielded state with no completion chore |
| **HPL-06 Correction and later refinding** | A person corrects time, identity, audience, occurrence, or interpretation and later searches vaguely | Canonical correction, dependency manifest, query, containment chain | Stale contribution is absent or repaired | Place relationship and dependent explanation are repaired | Search and dossier return the corrected object with lineage intact |

### 4.2 Required fixture forms

For each situation maintain:

- owner-state setup, including revisions and represented clock;
- Source and Claim inventory;
- audience and grant matrix for both accounts;
- expected Home, Places, and Life envelopes;
- explicit expected silence and degradation;
- cross-root active-seat expectation;
- allowable actions and required confirmation/readback;
- expected correction, expiry, withdrawal, and account-switch result; and
- native scenario identifier for the complete journey.

### 4.3 Evaluation dimensions

Every surfaced contribution is judged independently on:

- genuinely new substance;
- evidence support and inspectability;
- non-paraphrase of material already known to the person;
- present relevance or durable return value;
- non-creepiness and restraint from personality inference;
- completeness before a tap;
- root-native expression;
- attention and demand cost;
- authorized social use; and
- truthful silence when the bar is not met.

## 5. Workstream A — shared three-root composition and acceptance

This workstream begins first and then supports the three surface workstreams in
parallel.

### A1. Freeze the projection boundary

- Retain canonical domain owners and writes.
- Retain `root_projection/v2`; do not create `v3` to avoid completing it.
- Treat Opening, Home contribution, Place path, and transfer candidate as
  expiring assessments unless a canonical action creates a durable owner.
- Keep semantic payloads free of client component, geometry, and route names.
- Use canonical resources and typed destinations for all cross-root movement.
- Record the entity lane as a destination dependency, not a source of root
  ranking or duplicate relationship truth.

**Exit:** every admitted fixture contribution names its owner, Sources,
audience, grants, revision, expiry, root role, destination, consequence, and
repair story.

### A2. Implement the shared fixture and assertion harness

- Compile all three root projections from one scenario clock and owner world.
- Snapshot the complete envelopes, not page screenshots alone.
- Assert identity and revision continuity across roots.
- Assert one active delivery seat for overlapping contributions.
- Assert root-specific substance rather than copy variation.
- Assert correction, withdrawal, expiry, and account teardown.
- Assert no hidden write on view, exposure, search, or ignored possibility.

**Exit:** HPL-01 through HPL-06 execute deterministically in backend and mobile
contract tests, with native fixtures derived from the same scenario records.

### A3. Make active-seat arbitration executable

Use the existing Life Return arbitration policy as the starting point:

- current temporal consequence -> Home;
- spatial explanation or alternatives -> Places;
- deliberate reconstruction, lineage, or refinding -> Life;
- substantial overlap -> one foreground seat, with owner/search continuity
  preserved elsewhere;
- root change or expiry -> recompute from owner truth, never local reshuffling.

**Exit:** the same semantic identity cannot occupy overlapping foreground
positions in Home, Places, and Life at the same represented time.

### A4. Close shared repair and telemetry

- Bind every served contribution to its dependency manifest.
- Revalidate final writes against Source and owner revisions.
- Propagate correction and withdrawal to retained generation, current
  projection, search, and Life lineage.
- Keep exposure and delivery evidence separate from knowledge, belief, and
  real-world outcome.
- Record bounded reason codes, never private content, in operational telemetry.

**Exit:** HPL-06 proves one correction and one withdrawal across all dependent
reads without resurrecting stale material.

## 6. Workstream H — Home productization

### H1. Populate Home from real owner data

- Use the existing bounded portfolio; do not introduce a new feed store.
- Populate ordinary, open, returned, live, shared, quiet, degraded, and cached
  cases for two internal accounts.
- Add only owner adapters that satisfy an existing admitted Home role.
- Apply the reviewed Place-content policy batch only through its guarded
  preview/apply workflow and only after a fresh operator review.
- Populate explicit-subject personal evidence through an authorized real
  gesture; do not fabricate artifacts or ask the user to classify them for QA.

**Exit:** both accounts receive real, non-fixture Home units from several owner
families, with independent degradation and no authority widening.

### H2. Clear the editorial value bar

- Calibrate the producer using richer evidence roles, not itinerary-only pairs.
- Require a supported distinction, mechanism, comparison, or transfer that the
  person did not already state.
- Preserve source-local claims and limits.
- Keep weak or repetitive material silent.
- Compare Home and Places expressions for root distinctness before admission.
- Keep the model-authored serving kill switch independent from owner-direct
  state and practical instruments.

**Exit:** the human-reviewed HPL corpus accepts substantive results for both
accounts and rejects recap, paraphrase, creepy interpretation, cloned roots,
and homework.

### H3. Make Moment and attention truthful

- Preserve canonical commitment timing through the owner read.
- Distinguish a settled commitment from a possibility or broad Plan horizon.
- Represent explicit deferral/unavailability without inferring mood.
- Bind current conditions and Openings to exact Sources, subjects, or Place
  ancestry.
- Separate in-app pull relevance from interruption permission.
- Expire treatments when the underlying window or owner revision changes.

**Exit:** every Home contribution can explain why it appears now, why its
attention posture is safe, and when it will disappear.

### H4. Complete the native posture matrix with real data

- Available: prepared possibility with complete value.
- Planning: one unresolved decision maximum.
- Live: current commitment plus useful supporting field when allowed.
- Returned: present life remains primary; earned continuity may transfer.
- Quiet: zero demand and useful complete-on-view returns.
- Cold: one useful invitation, never setup homework.
- Urgent: recovery dominant with conservative suppression.

Test medium anatomy, exact return, Source inspection, confirmation lifetime,
readback, dismissal, expiry, fallback, and scroll restoration in each relevant
posture.

**Exit:** seven real-data native cases pass hierarchy and behavioral review;
mock flows remain development support rather than promotion evidence.

## 7. Workstream P — Places productization

### P1. Establish one scoped result set

- Make scope, search, map, and composition operate on one server-governed
  result set and one context handle.
- Preserve the current scope and question across map/list transformation.
- Converge pin, row, trace, and Home continuation onto the same canonical
  Focus identity.
- Restore exact originating state on back.
- Prevent compatibility and governed reads from racing or duplicating
  authority.

**Exit:** changing representation does not silently change inventory, scope,
identity, or ranking inputs.

### P2. Complete World Field

- Admit one lead and a bounded number of useful branches.
- Mix practical, editorial, relationship, and human perspective only when each
  independently beats silence.
- Demote ordinary mast treatment; preserve a truthful cold/new-scope entrance.
- Keep generic saves, popularity, ratings, and renderer availability from
  becoming rank authority.
- Make the page end honestly rather than append a generic recommendation feed.

**Exit:** ordinary, open, sparse, degraded, and shared World Fields are useful
without requiring a selected Place.

### P3. Complete Place Focus through the entity contract

- Consume canonical entity identity and viewer-safe presentation from the
  separate entity lane.
- Add current affordance, personal relationship, and source-grounded
  interpretation as projections, not duplicate object truth.
- Make provider truth, freshness, hours, access, reachability, and fallback
  explicit when present and honestly absent when unavailable.
- Ensure Home-origin value is not repeated as Focus's selling proposition.
- Preserve Bring-a-photo and other deliberate gestures through the canonical
  Intake and entity subject contract.

**Exit:** every supported Place kind opens one canonical Focus with no
client-guessed route, owner, or relationship state.

### P4. Earn Place Path

- Render Path only for a supported spatial or structural relationship.
- Keep the path sparse and typed: another Place, person, mechanism, event,
  ingredient, route, or future possibility.
- Use a diagram only when topology or sequence carries meaning.
- State analogy limits and distinguish fact, interpretation, and proposal.
- Do not expose the raw graph or use generic similarity as fill.

**Exit:** each admitted Path teaches or enables something Focus alone cannot,
and weak paths disappear without residue.

### P5. Complete Live Reduction

- Compile it from current Moment, owner state, provider/world truth, affected
  people, authority, and alternatives.
- Treat it as compression of an earned practical moment, not a fourth screen
  in a funnel.
- Distinguish pending, partial, failed, stale, unknown, reversed, and
  superseded states.
- Claim success only after canonical owner/provider readback.
- Preserve useful world depth unless an actual urgent/protected window
  warrants suppression.

**Exit:** HPL-03 completes one practical change or recovery with an inspectable
receipt and a later Life consequence.

## 8. Workstream L — Life productization

Life begins alongside Home and Places rather than waiting for all P1-P3 work to
finish. Start with deterministic read composition; model-authored Returns remain
dark until the shared value bar is met.

### L1. Make one corpus real

- Define the canonical Life corpus inventory over existing owners and Sources.
- Reconcile total counts, lens counts, digest counts, and Everything kept.
- Preserve the same object identity across lenses.
- Distinguish custody from occurrence, plan from what happened, and missing
  evidence from did-not-happen.
- Keep unsupported owner kinds recoverable through a truthful owner/source
  page rather than dropping or promoting them.

**Exit:** every governed Life entry belongs to one inventory and has a stable
containment chain, even when it appears through more than one lens.

### L2. Implement the four lenses

- Time: weighted periods, journeys, occasions, and episodes.
- Places: held Place clusters and relationship history, not current discovery.
- Threads: supported cross-moment pursuits or questions, never completion
  projects.
- People: viewer-relative shared record with attribution and the ability to
  object, withdraw, leave, mute, or block where authorized.

Each lens uses a finite digest, one honest full-record door, conditional
Returns/reflections, and at most two fixed windows. Lens selection changes the
exploration, not the underlying record.

**Exit:** all four lenses are selectable and backed by canonical data for the
supported fixture world; empty individual lenses use honest absence rather
than setup prompts.

### L3. Unify refinding with the corpus

- Preserve the current `life-refind-result.v1` contract where sound.
- Move its read substrate toward the same canonical corpus used by the root.
- Support vague human cues without persisting raw query text.
- Return target first, then bounded context around it.
- Carry containment chain, truth state, audience, author, Source roles,
  correction, and Continue destination.
- Include cancelled, skipped, unused, disputed, and no-evidence truth where
  relevant rather than erasing negative history.

**Exit:** HPL-06 finds the same corrected canonical identity shown by a lens or
dossier, with honest capability limits and no query-derived memory write.

### L4. Complete dossier and destination behavior

- Open a canonical dossier or entity/object destination from every supported
  door.
- Keep the dossier bounded: what happened, what was planned, Sources and
  Claims, people and grants, supported shape, and evidence doors.
- Reuse one chip grammar for nested artifacts.
- Carry exact context into existing Chat when the user deliberately continues.
- Restore the originating Life lens, position, and query state on return.

**Exit:** root, search, lens, and cross-root doors converge on the same identity
and do not fork object representations.

### L5. Add one production Return family

A Return qualifies only if it reconstructs, explains, differentiates,
transfers, or re-enters with new supported value. Begin with the recent-return
Journey family because its factual sequence, Source lineage, and current-root
handoff can exercise the complete contract without turning Life into a recap.

- Use deterministic eligibility and active-seat arbitration.
- Require the new distinction in the title and useful substance before tap.
- Keep the Return fixed until the record, authority, freshness, or time changes.
- Remove the current card treatment and follow Life's uncarded hierarchy.
- Keep the entire Return absent when it loses to a current Home/Places seat.

**Exit:** HPL-02 produces one human-approved Life Return that adds substance,
does not recite the trip, and can lawfully transfer a current consequence to
Home or Places.

### L6. Add Together and causal repair

- Project one attributed shared contribution into the authorized shared
  record.
- Preserve author, subject, custodian, viewer, actor, audience, use, retention,
  inference, and action authority separately.
- Recompile the shared record when membership, grant, withdrawal, mute, block,
  or correction changes.
- Preserve each person's independent history where authority still permits.
- Never create a people leaderboard, inferred intimacy score, or social feed.

**Exit:** HPL-04 and HPL-06 pass for both accounts before and after withdrawal
or relationship-state change.

## 9. Workstream X — cross-root closure and native acceptance

### X1. Cross-root invariants

The integrated suite must assert:

- one canonical identity, not three copied objects;
- one active foreground seat for overlapping semantic value;
- Home and Places expressions are substantively root-distinct;
- Life remains stable across visits unless record, authority, time, or
  freshness changes;
- a Place door opened from Home returns to the exact Home unit;
- a Life door opened from Places or Home returns to the exact originating
  state;
- correction, withdrawal, expiry, and account switch repair all dependents;
- a view, scroll, search, or ignored suggestion performs no hidden write;
- proposal and execution remain distinct and success requires readback; and
- weak content, unsupported routes, and unavailable providers yield rather
  than generate filler.

### X2. Native matrix

Run the relevant situations on iOS and Android across:

- fresh launch and warm return;
- foreground/background and proof expiry;
- process death and persisted exact return;
- online, offline, partial, stale, and failed refresh;
- location denied, approximate, delayed, granted, and revoked;
- account switch during read, confirmation, action, and return;
- large type, VoiceOver/TalkBack, reduced motion, contrast, and touch target;
- map/list transformation and camera restoration; and
- low-end performance and bounded layout.

Screenshot capture is evidence, not the verdict. Each scenario also needs
machine-readable contract, state, and outcome assertions.

### X3. Rollout posture

During implementation:

- keep Home/Places governed composition and Life v1 default-off;
- retain independent internal diagnostics and fallbacks;
- retain the model-authored serving kill switch; and
- do not call a compile-time flag release evidence.

Before the internal cohort:

- bring Life into the unified product-system rehearsal posture;
- require Home, Places, and Life gates together for the selected cohort;
- preserve compatibility rollback to Trips, mature Places, and Atlas;
- record exact backend, schema, app, fixture, and native evidence revisions;
  and
- keep `releaseEligible` false until the complete cohort gate passes.

Compatibility deletion is a later operation. It requires replacement parity,
readback, correction, rollback, and observed cohort stability.

### HPL-H calibration substrate — 2026-09-04

The read-only `canonical-owner-evidence-audit` already answers whether a
named account has authoritative Intake, graph, activation, owner projection,
and Source-discovery material. It does not—and must not—call a model or turn
candidate presence into product value. The new `hpl_home_value_gate.py` keeps
that boundary explicit. Its manifest accepts only two named accounts and
stable candidate/source receipt references; a reviewer must score each
candidate on evidence grounding, new substance, non-paraphrase, present
relevance, root distinctness, complete-on-view value, non-creepiness, and
homework-free attention cost. A rejected row reports its exact failed
dimensions. `status: complete` is possible only when both accounts have an
accepted candidate and all side-effect counters remain zero.

The checked-in `hpl-home-value-review.yaml` is intentionally `not_started`
with no rows. Running `make hpl-home-value-gate` therefore exits blocked with
`no_human_reviews`, `missing_account_coverage`, and
`no_accepted_home_value`. This is the correct current result: the audit found
no live Intake population for Dao or Lena, and no synthetic candidate should
be added merely to make Home look populated. The next operator step is an
authorized real capture through the existing object-page/Intake path, a fresh
owner audit, then human review of the actual Home output.

## 13. Stop lines

- No new root, feed, universal context service, or duplicate owner.
- No new Home or Places semantic kind merely to make a design easier.
- No adaptive or engagement-ranked Life root.
- No reflection prompt, classification chore, or setup task on an output root.
- No generic recommendation filler to make sparse states look rich.
- No model-generated value before evidence, novelty, root, and human editorial
  gates pass.
- No social material without author, audience, grant, precision, expiry,
  correction, and withdrawal.
- No passive location, mood, personality, or relationship-strength inference.
- No entity implementation in this lane beyond typed consumption and
  integration tests.
- No Chat redesign or silent Chat write.
- No claim of success before canonical owner/provider readback.
- No public promotion from mock fixtures, screenshots, or compile-time flags.
- No compatibility deletion before replacement and rollback are proven.

## 14. Definition of done

This program is complete when:

> Two real people can open Vesper and experience one coherent lived-world
> product: Home makes the present more valuable, Places makes the world around
> them intelligible and workable, and Life makes what Vesper has held useful
> again. The same evidence and identities survive every handoff, social truth
> remains viewer-relative, weak material yields, real consequences are
> verified, and correction repairs the complete loop.

At that point the next decision is whether the internal evidence supports a
public four-root promotion—not whether another page or card can be added.
