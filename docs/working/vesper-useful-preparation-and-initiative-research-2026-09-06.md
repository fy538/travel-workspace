# Useful preparation, not a busier assistant

## 1. Answer and scope

**Recommendation: Vesper should absorb more of the work between attention and
a worthwhile experience—not merely answer more questions or send more content.**
That work includes finding the relevant material, identifying a consequential
distinction, researching a promising possibility, checking how it would work,
and carrying the result into its next useful context. Sometimes it is practical
relief; sometimes it is understanding or enjoyment. Not every contribution
needs a decision, a Plan, a notification, or measurable time saved.

This strengthens the four moves rather than adding a fifth one. The key
distinction is between **doing useful work**, **making its result available**,
**interrupting someone**, and **changing something on their behalf**. None
automatically authorizes the next.

The founder requested research and deep reasoning across:

1. What worthwhile work should Vesper undertake before another request?
2. When should it happen: during conversation, on a later visit, or between visits?
3. What makes its result worth receiving?
4. How do those decisions behave in three complete experiences: an ordinary
   question, a developing weekend intention, and a friend's recommendation?

This September 6 desk-research pass combines primary research, official product
documentation, existing Vesper contracts, and bounded code inspection. It is
not a systematic literature review, user study, full implementation audit,
launch decision, or new permission agreement. Proposed experiences are synthetic;
the existing week manuscript remains their shared reference, not a competing
fictional product. No external service, scheduler, watch or runtime was activated.

[Section 11](#11-follow-up-research-assistance-that-has-a-scope-not-a-permanent-mode)
adds the subsequent research into instruction lifetime, contextual application,
cross-root repair and the effect of changed instructions on preparation.

## 3. Research findings and their limits

### 3.1 The work people need help with starts before execution

Daminger's 2019 study used 70 interviews with members of 35 couples to describe
household cognitive labor: anticipating needs, identifying options, deciding
and monitoring. The publisher abstract was consulted; this is qualitative
household research, not a study of AI or a representative consumer sample.
[The Cognitive Dimension of Household Labor](https://doi.org/10.1177/0003122419859007)

**Vesper inference:** a person can delegate the final task yet still bear its
mental management. If they must notice every dependency, locate all evidence
and specify every next step, an assistant may save execution while leaving
much of the real burden intact. Preserve wanted choice and human expression;
do not use this framework to automate every aspect of domestic or social life.

Gilbert's four online experiments, totaling 1,196 participants, found that
external reminders improved performance on a short-delay intention task. The
repository abstract reports only weak predictive association with a later
naturalistic intention. Online publication was 2014; the journal volume is
2015. Full-text retrieval failed, so this pass relies on the institutional
abstract. [Strategic offloading of delayed intentions](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1457331/)

**Vesper inference:** remembering at the right moment is legitimate value. But
remembering is only one burden. “Choose a venue” leaves different work behind
than a prepared option with its access conditions checked. Neither result
establishes that the person should act or wants repeated reminders.

### 3.2 Urgency is not a universal proxy for desired help

Lai and colleagues' January 2026 preprint studied 50 people making 750 pairwise
choices between email-drafting vignettes. Compositional effort better explained
those choices than urgency, despite participants ranking urgency highly in a
separate reflection. These are hypothetical scenario preferences, not observed
workplace adoption, and not evidence that urgency is irrelevant to practical
warnings. [Users Mispredict Their Own Preferences for AI Writing Assistance](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04461v1)

**Vesper inference:** worthwhile preparation can be welcome before a crisis.
Compare actual experiences, not just an onboarding question about whether
someone likes proactivity. The paper's suggestion to favor implicit feedback
does not override Vesper's explicit controls or authorize durable personal
inference. A person's current refusal remains binding regardless of what a
prediction model expects them to prefer.

### 3.3 A confirmation button does not by itself make unsolicited help welcome

A September 2025 preprint reports two workplace vignette experiments with
761 and 571 analyzed participants. Unsolicited help produced greater reported
self-threat than requested help; merely offering rather than immediately
providing help did not significantly reduce that threat in the second study.
The outcomes were self-reports in imagined presentation work, not live Home
use or measured retention. [Proactive AI Adoption can be Threatening](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.09309v1)

**Vesper inference:** “Want me to help?” is not a universal psychological or
interaction fix. Avoid framing the contribution as diagnosing incompetence or
an unfulfilled duty. An intelligible result on a deliberately opened Home
surface is a different treatment from an unsolicited pop-up in someone's work;
whether users welcome it still needs experience evidence.

Horvitz's CHI 1999 mixed-initiative framework considers uncertain goals,
attention costs, direct invocation, termination and refinement of automated
results. It is a design framework with a scheduling prototype, not proof of
modern consumer adoption. [Principles of Mixed-Initiative User Interfaces](https://erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf)

**Vesper inference:** evaluate useful preparation and its delivery separately.
Someone should be able to request more directly, stop accepted work, or ignore
an optional result without negotiating with the assistant.

### 3.4 Recent proactivity research does not establish an always-learning mandate

The February 2026 *After Talking with 1,000 Personas* preprint combines simulated
training/evaluation with 30 human participants reviewing storyboards and a
short interactive adaptation study. It reports favorable preference and
perception results. The 1,000 personas are not 1,000 real users; the human
study does not establish longitudinal natural-use retention.
[Preference-aligned proactive assistants](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04000v1)

The May 2026 ATRBench preprint tests whether agents ask for hidden preferences
needed in later tasks. It uses 20 synthetic personas and 284 rule–task pairs;
future tasks are constructed to require those rules. This is useful diagnostic
evidence, not evidence that consumers want more anticipatory questions.
[Ask Now, Use Later](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.28108v2)

**Vesper inference:** do not optimize for acquiring facts simply because a
benchmark rewards them. The practical question is whether a missing fact
changes useful work sufficiently to justify asking now. “We might use it one
day” is a weak reason to make the person maintain a profile. These research
results also cannot turn simulated preference into consent or product demand.

## 5. What work should Vesper undertake?

The useful unit is a **bounded contribution that leaves someone better placed**,
not a new durable object or an agent assigned to every life topic.

| Work family | Valuable completion | Thin substitute to reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recover | The right evidence or prior explanation, usable in this context | A memory announcement or link to a large unfiltered archive |
| Clarify | A new distinction, supported comparison or mechanism | Restating the person's observation with a better headline |
| Discover | A grounded possibility with enough substance and practical shape to assess it | A teaser or list that requires the user to research every item |
| Prepare | Relevant facts and dependencies assembled into a usable option or assessment | “You should check the entrance hours” when supported checking is available |
| Coordinate | A shared consequence becomes clearer and easier through existing authority | More messages, inferred consensus or hidden organizer work |

These are work descriptions inside the existing four moves, not five new
capabilities, tabs or content quotas. A friend's original image needs no AI
operation. An exact ticket can be complete value. Curiosity can be pleasurable
without improving productivity.

Before spending on optional work, ask internally:

1. Is there a credible payoff from the existing situation or a grounded world
   possibility, not merely a large amount of available data?
2. Can we complete a useful part with eligible sources and supported services?
3. Has that value already been delivered, or will this genuinely change its use?
4. Is preparing it now better than reusing existing work or doing it later?
5. What review, coordination or correction burden will the result create?
6. Does its expected useful life justify the full production and refresh cost?

These are research questions and proposed heuristics, not calibrated scores.
Do not use unknown taste to disqualify all discovery, or an explicit interest
as permission to run indefinite research. Work can be inexpensive and
speculative while its presentation remains optional and its claims grounded.

**Completion should usually be more than advice to do the work, but less than
an unchosen commitment.** An access-checked candidate leaves venue choice to
the person. An explanation makes the distinction available without assigning
a lesson. A source-grounded gathering handoff leaves human expression intact.

## 6. When the work happens—and when its result appears

The previous discussion's three timings remain useful, but “between visits”
needs a distinction between finite work and continuing responsibility.

| Timing | Recommended treatment | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| During the current conversation | Complete the answer and useful preparation already justified by the request; stream or report progress only for actual work | Never delay a ready result to manufacture a later Home payoff |
| On a later visit | Fast composition from current owner reads and eligible prepared material; receive work initiated through a separately authorized trigger | Ordinary Home/Places GETs, tab focus and app launch do not initiate or enqueue optional production |
| Between visits: finite preparation | Finish already accepted work, or a narrowly policy-authorized contribution; return its result where useful | Continuing computation is not automatically monitoring. Use must remain eligible through execution and delivery; a transient Ask cannot outlive its policy just because a worker was queued |
| Between visits: continuing follow-up | Observe the named condition for the supported period, under its actual mandate and failure/stop semantics | Requires its own accepted owner/service contract; memory, Keep, a payment tier or metadata change does not create a watch |

The [accepted bounded production decision](../decisions/2026-09-06-bound-source-production-worker.md)
names the existing worker execution owner and permits only an authorized signal,
source correction/recomposition request or explicit user-requested warm action
within its gates. This research does not expand that list. A source change is
not sufficient authority for arbitrary work; a later visit is a receiving
moment, not a trigger. Finite preparation and continuing watches remain distinct.

### Fast Home and substantial work can coexist

If Friday's Riverside assessment already exists and its inputs remain valid,
Home can show it immediately. If only the recommendation exists, Home can show
Maya's actual contribution while a separately supported preparation path does
the checking. It must not initially claim “fits Saturday” and silently verify
that claim afterward.

When a result completes during the visit, integrate it without moving the item
under the person's finger or resetting their scroll. If it completes later,
make it available on a subsequent appropriate read; a push is not automatically
earned. These are receiving design requirements, not a shipped update policy.
Do not fill Home with speculative-job spinners. If the person explicitly asked
for a time-sensitive assessment, however, report actual progress or failure
rather than hiding accepted work behind a generic feed.

### Freshness should follow the claim, not the whole artifact

An explanation of image geometry can remain valid while a studio's event offer
expires. A friend's remark can remain their authentic past judgment while
current access hours need another source. A venue's general hours do not prove
an entrance is usable throughout them. Recheck consequential volatile facts
near use; do not regenerate durable explanation merely because the clock moved.

Production trigger, result eligibility and interruption timing are separate.
Many source changes require invalidation but no replacement work. An accepted
owner revision can invalidate a candidate immediately; recomputation still
needs a useful reason, supported path and bounded budget. Retire unselected
possibilities without making them pending tasks.

## 7. Three complete experience investigations

### E1 — An ordinary question becomes an applied understanding

Use the existing building-photograph strand. At first, the person asks why
verticals lean and receives the useful explanation immediately. Asking alone
does not retain the images or establish a photography preference.

For the durable comparison branch, the originals and authored exploration have
been deliberately retained as in §10 of the manuscript. Their actual geometry
supports a distinction: correcting the tightly framed image cuts into the roof;
the wider image has enough margin. A later permitted result can say:

> **The wider frame leaves room to straighten.**
> In the first shot, the correction cuts into the roof. The second gives you
> enough margin to keep it. · Compare the originals

The work is applying a technique to evidence, not sending another explanation
of camera tilt. If this was already possible and useful in the initial answer,
do it there. A later treatment earns its place through a genuinely later
application or newly available result—not a scheduled “insight tomorrow.”

**What is better:** the person can choose a starting image and recognize the
tradeoff without assembling an editing comparison themselves. Choosing,
looking and optionally editing remain theirs. A native comparison is sufficient;
we are not promising a photo editor, practice course or image export.

**Dependencies:** eligible originals and derivative custody, actual image
analysis, prior-delivery distinction, supported comparison production/renderer
and exact continuation. Current ordinary Ask expiry still applies. The optional
conversation-history branch waits for its separate contract; do not solve this
by adding a Keep question to every useful answer.

**Disconfirming comparison:** equal-quality immediate answer versus that answer
plus the later applied comparison. If the latter adds no understanding or
usable distinction, omit it; more remembered context is not automatically value.

### E2 — A loose weekend wish becomes an approachable possibility

Independent synthetic variant: the person says, “I'd like to make something
Saturday afternoon—something I can finish there. Keep that idea; I don't want
to plan the whole day.” This is authored intent, not an empty calendar inference.
The existing fixture supplies Canal Print Room's 45-minute drop-in session in
the 1–4 p.m. window, $12 including materials, with no reservation required.
The venue and offer are fictional and cannot be presented as a real listing.

**Immediate help:** return that concrete possibility if supported now, without
requiring a formal Plan or a permanent creative-interest profile. The intended
Keep behavior depends on the unadopted retained-intention owner; current product
must not claim that retention succeeded through a save or a placeholder Plan.

**Later work, when the needed policy and producer exist:** check whether that
same offer still runs and whether its practical terms still support a finished
piece. The returned result can be compact:

> **Make one print and take it home.**
> Canal Print Room · Saturday 1–4 · 45-minute session
> $12 includes materials. No booking needed. · See the session

This is useful refreshed preparation, not a new discovery if already shown.
If no meaningful condition or use has changed, a fresh check can keep the
existing offer trustworthy without earning another prominent card. The person
should not receive a notification merely because a check completed.
Do not claim no queue, guaranteed admission, a last-start time or travel fit
unless the relevant source supports it. If the actual provider requires extra
steps, expose them before presenting the opportunity as easy.

The possibility remains optional: no itinerary, status meter or unfinished
creative project. If the person wants “something near me in the next hour,”
that new request supplies a different timing/origin problem. The live engine
can then assess the actual journey within supported scope; it should not
collect current location merely to offer the first possibility.

**What is better:** locating a doable experience and resolving its basic
practical terms; not just learning that a studio exists. The person retains
whether to go and the pleasure of making. This case does not require a friend.

**Dependencies:** authored intent lifecycle for later use, real offer supply,
provider/source freshness, bounded preparation, Places detail and optional
external continuation. If continuing checks were not accepted, the result
must not imply Vesper will report subsequent changes.

**Disconfirming comparison:** listing, substantive offer, and the same offer
with meaningful preparation. If the extra research leaves no less work or more
confidence, reuse the simpler result. If no supported offer exists, record
the supply gap; a fictional perfect workshop cannot validate the proposition.

### E3 — A friend's recommendation becomes useful shared preparation

Use Maya's Riverside Reading Room note and Tuesday's existing family-meetup
requirement, 6:30–8:30 with step-free entry and exit. The friend already supplied
“We sat by the river windows. I'd go back.” The note is useful as received.

Later, without asking Maya to research logistics, a supported preparation path
checks Riverside's own published entrance/exit availability through 9. It
returns the §11 Friday candidate: a human recommendation whose specific access
conditions fit the prospective visit. It does not guarantee a window seat or
capacity. Any material unknown remains in the assessment, not hidden behind
the friend's endorsement.

**What is better:** one person's lived perspective supplies a promising place;
Vesper carries out the contextual checking the friend did not volunteer to do.
The organizer need not reconstruct the note or restate the meetup requirement.
No novelty test is imposed on Maya's words.

**The important boundary:** fit is not selection; selection is not invitation;
invitation delivery is not acceptance. On explicit venue adoption, use the
arrangement owner's current command and readback. If the organizer then shares
the plan with a thin participant, carry only the recipient-authorized material.
Maya's original note is not freely forwardable merely because it shaped a choice.

**Dependencies:** current arrangement requirement, eligible addressed note,
separate fresh place facts, preparation and exact root continuation, plus
supported audience/participation/delivery for any subsequent shared action.
These are existing owner responsibilities, not a Home-owned social planner.

**Disconfirming comparison:** the existing messenger plus a capable assistant
versus Vesper's linked experience. Count finding the note, providing context,
checking facts, updating the arrangement, informing people and later lookup.
If the guest avoids effort only because the organizer copies and reconciles
everything manually, the intended coordination benefit has not been delivered.

**Recommended join:** identify the bounded job and authority, select eligible
evidence, reuse or prepare through the existing production path, then read back
and admit a current result to the appropriate surface. Revalidate changed
source/owner revisions before consequential publication or action. Use the
existing owner and production contracts before proposing additional storage.

Home owns which available results deserve space, not all the underlying work.
The live-engine families own situated judgment within their actual scope, not
a universal feed ranker or continuous biography analysis. Life supplies exact
authorized continuity; it should not become a speculative-work inbox. Chat
remains the direct way to ask, steer or challenge, not a mandatory unlock for
every result. Places carries spatial depth and original place/human context.

### The unsolved execution choices

1. **Which permitted trigger supports each proposed result?** The execution
   owner and trigger boundary are already decided; bind each case to a concrete
   authorized signal, correction/recomposition request or explicit warm action,
   its executor and budget. Missing bindings remain gaps, not permission to
   produce on visit. A source update does not authorize every possible job.
2. **How does valid work survive until delivery?** Preserve use/retention scope,
   source and owner revisions, deadline and cancellation. A queued job cannot
   use released material or publish an obsolete plan assessment.
3. **How does Home receive later completion?** Reuse existing root delivery
   and invalidation paths; settle stable in-session updates without a second
   generator, duplicate card or forced refresh loop.
4. **What responsibility was actually accepted?** Finite answer/preparation,
   retention, ongoing follow-up and state change need distinct receipts where
   consequential. Do not create a generic autonomy toggle to cover them all.

These are receiving questions for the current integration roadmap, not new
work assigned to the active engineering lanes in this research turn.

## 9. How to judge whether the result earns its place

Review the whole experience, not just a polished output:

- **Substance:** new understanding, enjoyment, access to evidence, a feasible
  possibility or a practical dependency resolved.
- **Completeness:** enough useful work is finished to avoid sending the person
  back to assemble the same sources. An identified consequential unknown can
  be honest partial value, but persistent incompleteness is a coverage problem.
- **Wanted participation:** choosing, making, expressing or wandering remain
  available; optional learning never becomes a requirement.
- **Total effort:** initiation, context assembly, comprehension, verification,
  coordination and repair across every affected person—not just prompt count.
- **Appropriate timing:** ready when useful, without manufactured urgency,
  attention capture or delayed first answers.
- **Continuity:** exact refinding and correction; a later result does not rewrite
  what the person or friend originally said.
- **Economics:** acquisition and rights, review, retrieval, volatile refresh,
  production, delivery, free-recipient cost and discarded work. Model price per
  generated paragraph is not the service's full cost.

Use equal-information comparisons to examine answer quality, then equal-starting-
source comparisons to examine total effort. Include an ignored result, an
already-delivered result, a canceled intention, a withdrawn social source,
unsupported access data and a recipient who does not install the app.

Do not optimize only for chosen options or daily returns. A person can enjoy
understanding, decline a poor fit, retrieve a ticket and leave, or meet their
friend without recording the outcome. Conversely, no reply or no return cannot
by itself prove that value was received.

## 11. Follow-up research: assistance that has a scope, not a permanent mode

### 11.1 Question, method and recommendation

The founder requested this pass after [the competing-purposes week](situated-value-decision-matrix-2026-09-06.md#13-a-week-of-competing-purposes-changing-receptivity-and-repair).
It asks how “for this answer,” “today,” “for Saturday” and a lasting preference
should affect later assistance and finite preparation. This September 6 pass
examined primary HCI/behavioral research, an NLP benchmark paper, official
product documentation and the current local contracts. It is not a systematic
review, user study, exhaustive competitor comparison or new code audit.

**Recommendation:** adapt the current help from relevant context; preserve an
instruction's actual applicability and lifetime when those are established;
make repair easy without asking people to configure a personal operating system.
Do not mistake absence of a user-written instruction for absence of a useful
job. Contextual judgment remains necessary; it must not silently become a
durable claim about the person or permission to do more work.

The most important correction to our manuscript is that **duration and reach
are independent**. “Today” specifies a time boundary, not necessarily all tabs,
all subjects or every kind of assistance. “For Saturday” can identify a particular
occasion, not everything that happens on that date. Resolve the intended subject
and effect from the conversation/object before propagating an instruction.

### 11.2 What the evidence supports—and what it does not

**Local correction and global customization are different design tools.**
Microsoft's HAX guidance distinguishes global customization, scoped service
when intent is uncertain, and limiting disruptive adaptation. This is practitioner
guidance, not evidence that one exact scope UI improves Vesper retention.
Our inference is to avoid treating “too long” as a global setting while keeping
real global controls available when wanted. Uncertainty can justify a bounded
response rather than another clarification question.
[Global controls](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/provide-global-controls/),
[scope when uncertain](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/scope-services-when-in-doubt/),
[cautious adaptation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/update-and-adapt-cautiously/).

**Moment-specific objectives can improve help, but control can still be costly.**
Lam and colleagues' Just-In-Time Objectives work studies contextual objective
induction. The inspected October 2025 preprint reports experiments with 14 and
205 participants and an in-person study with 17; a CHI 2026 proceedings record
was located, but the final full text was inaccessible. Participants preferred
specialized outputs in the studied tasks, yet some hesitated to correct tools
despite available controls. The authors discuss multi-click editing and generation
latency as possible barriers. These task studies do not validate passive
whole-life monitoring, cross-root scope inference or long-term consumer benefit.
**Vesper inference:** infer a helpful current approach, but make redirection work
at the point of use. Do not require everyone to approve an objective first, or
copy a generate-many-and-rank pipeline without checking costs.
[Inspected preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14591v1),
[CHI 2026 record](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790713).

**People's desired memory changes with the activity.** Jones and colleagues'
CHI EA 2025 paper combines six regular users' interviews with qualitative Reddit
analysis. Participants described separating contexts through chats/personas;
the paper distinguishes task, project, domain and person-related needs. This
small exploratory study supports the importance of relevant transfer, not a
universal taxonomy or proof that people want to manage memory folders.
**Vesper inference:** a different chat should not require needless re-explanation,
but neither should a shared chat erase boundaries between unrelated purposes.
Transfer needs a reason, not just semantic similarity.
[Users' Expectations and Practices with Agent Memory](https://brennanjones.com/media/documents/publications/chiea25-666.pdf).

**Memory can be accurate yet inappropriate in context.** Zhang and colleagues'
2025 preprint reports 18 semi-structured interviews. Participants described old
requirements affecting later tasks, unrelated-topic reuse, and wanting control
over memory use and timing. The sample is centered on Chinese users and skewed
toward young, highly educated participants; reported expectations do not prove
which interface people would use. **Vesper inference:** improving extraction
accuracy is insufficient. We must distinguish correcting a fact, restricting
its influence, changing present help and deleting the source. A request for
more control is not automatically a request for a dashboard full of controls.
[Understanding Users' Privacy Perceptions Towards LLM's RAG-based Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.07664v1).

**Conditional application is a technical problem, not just better prose.**
Yuan and colleagues' COLING 2025 paper proposes conditional memory construction
and retrieval, evaluated on three GPT-4-generated datasets covering continuity,
personalized knowledge and feedback. It explicitly notes that remembered
information can apply only in particular situations. This is benchmark evidence,
not an evaluation of user-authorized scope, lifecycle enforcement or lived-world
benefit. Its feedback examples also assume some earlier corrections should
generalize; Vesper must independently establish that scope rather than adopting
the benchmark's preference assumptions.
[Personalized LLM Assistant with Evolving Conditional Memory](https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-main.254.pdf).

**A little effective control can matter; this is not a license for fake control.**
Dietvorst, Simmons and Massey's Management Science paper (2018 volume, online
2016) reports three studies using incentivized forecasting tasks. Participants
were more willing to use imperfect algorithms when allowed to modify forecasts,
even within narrow bounds. Forecasting is not a personal assistant, so the
finding cannot establish the right Vesper interaction. **Vesper inference:**
“Let me choose; compare the access” should produce a real, immediate difference
in assistance. We should not optimize perceived control while ignoring the
instruction, or make users adjust everything before receiving competent help.
[Overcoming Algorithm Aversion](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Dietvorst-Simmons-Massey-2018.pdf).

**Adjacent products already offer memory and instruction controls.** Google's
current help describes saved instructions applied across chats, editing/deleting
them and switching them off. It also warns that instructions to avoid topics
may not work perfectly. Its separate memory help permits conversational correction
and describes distinct source-deletion/disconnection steps. These are vendor
claims read September 6, not tested behavior or a complete product audit.
**Vesper inference:** “you can correct the assistant” is not a unique proposition.
The opportunity is reliable, low-effort correction of actual downstream use,
without requiring deletion of useful originals. These pages do not establish
whether Gemini supports every temporary cross-surface case we propose.
[Gemini instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625),
[Gemini memory](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469).

### 11.3 What our current contracts already answer

The [memory charter](../systems/memory-preference.md#assistance-preferences-and-scoped-correction)
already separates topic, desired benefit, depth and wanted participation. It
distinguishes “not this weekend,” “for my parents,” and restricting recommendation
use while keeping material. The [contribution contract](../systems/contribution-and-consequence.md)
provides current-instruction precedence, L1 session versus L2 situational versus
L3 governed evidence, scoped expiry and dependency repair. It expressly says
adaptive behavior does not widen authority. We do not need a second memory model.

What remains unsettled is the exact cross-root delivery and lifetime of an
assistance instruction outside a single response, not whether scope matters.
The 24-hour inactivity backstop for session state is not the meaning of “today.”
Likewise, a recorded instruction's storage lifetime is not the period during
which it should influence suggestions. A source can remain kept while its
Saturday cue is no longer applicable. Conversely, a desired duration cannot
override retention/use authority that ends sooner.

### 11.4 Proposed scope behavior, in ordinary language

This is a decision table for review, not a new user-facing grammar or accepted
write contract. Use existing owners where available; missing persistent or
cross-root paths must not be simulated with prompt summaries.

| What the person says | Recommended effect | What remains unchanged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “For this answer, keep it short” | Shorten this response; no later default is established | Topic interest, Home mix, saved material |
| “Too much planning—give me something to read” while discussing one card | Give the reading now; treat broader reach as unspecified | Other commitments and future plans; no automatic day-long Home mode |
| “On Home today, fewer things to decide” | Where supported, reduce optional decision pressure on Home for the resolved local period; retain useful reading/human material | Chat's ability to answer a planning request, relevant practical consequences, saved intentions |
| “For Saturday's family dinner, let me choose; just compare” | Change assistance for that identified episode, including its private continuations across roots | Shared arrangements until an authorized command changes them; unrelated Saturday activities |
| “Usually start with a recommendation, but show alternatives” | A correctable assistance default within the clearly established domain, only through admitted authority | A current request for comparison overrides the default without erasing it |
| “Keep this, but don't use it for recommendations” | Preserve custody; restrict the named source's dependent recommendation use | Exact retrieval and other uses that remain authorized; no deletion by implication |
| “Stop checking this” | Resolve and stop the actual named ongoing/finite work within authority; acknowledge the real result | Source custody and unrelated jobs; no claim of a stopped watch if none existed |

**Ambiguity rule:** act on the clearly supported immediate intent; do not expand
duration or reach just to make personalization feel powerful. Ask only when the
ambiguity changes a material consequence, important future reliance or a needed
owner effect. An explicit broad request should not be artificially narrowed to
one answer merely to avoid implementing its real scope.

Resolve dated wording against the known timezone and the referenced episode.
For a “today” treatment, the proposed behavior is to retain the resolved end
boundary rather than silently lengthen it if timezone context later changes.
Time ambiguity that matters requires clarification; unrelated content does not
need a location request. This is a proposed lifecycle choice, not an adopted
timezone policy. No expiry notification is needed unless the person would rely
on knowing the responsibility ended.

### 11.5 Carry context across roots, not a global mood

The appropriate unit of continuity is the relevant purpose/object plus actual
scope—not the tab where a sentence happened to be typed. “Compare for this
dinner” can influence the same dinner in Chat, Home or Places where authorized.
“Keep this answer short” does not make every Home artifact short. Life should
not lose entrusted evidence because an item is currently a poor suggestion.

A local exception need not overwrite a durable default. When the exception ends,
reconsider current evidence and applicable defaults; do not revive a superseded
Saturday cue or automatically re-promote something the person postponed.
More recent text wins only where it actually changes the same subject/effect
within authority. Quoted instructions, a friend's words and an imported document
do not become the viewer's assistance preferences.

Architecturally, preserve the authored basis, subject, intended effect,
applicability, resolved end/stop condition and revision where the owning contract
requires them. These are semantic requirements for existing context/owner
readers, not a proposed new universal table. Models may interpret ambiguous
language; expiry, audience, cancellation and revision checks must not depend
solely on whether a later prompt happens to remember that language.

### 11.6 What a changed instruction means for preparation

An instruction about presentation does not automatically start or cancel work.
Reassess separately:

- **Shorter output:** the same valid assessment may be reused in a shorter
  expression; there is no reason to repeat research.
- **Different present benefit:** an already useful workshop result can lose
  prominence while retaining valid authorized depth. Do not deliver it just
  because generation cost has already been incurred.
- **Changed substantive goal:** stop obsolete work where cancellable, or suppress
  its late result for the replaced purpose. A prepared artifact cannot become
  evidence that the old goal is still active.
- **Keep without influence:** preserve the original while invalidating the
  disallowed dependent use; do not hide it by deleting everything.
- **New work would help:** select a warranted result, then bind a separately
  permitted trigger and bounded executor under the accepted worker decision.
  A preference for ideas, correction, or root visit is not universal production
  authority. A current explicit request can justify useful finite help.

This permits proactive assistance without making the user author every job:
supported signals and context can identify work within already accepted scope.
The unresolved product task is naming those concrete signal-to-benefit cases,
not adding a generic autonomy slider or pretending Keep creates a watch.

### 11.7 Experience checks and decisions to carry forward

Compare the same situation under three treatments: response-only adaptation,
scoped cross-root adaptation and indiscriminate global adaptation. The last is
a negative control in a specimen, not a behavior to deploy. Hold evidence and
output quality constant so preference is not confounded by one version having
better content. Include a return after the instruction expires and a result
that finishes after the user changes their mind.

Ask what the person expects to change elsewhere and tomorrow before explaining
our intended rule. Observe actual repair effort, repeated correction, unexpected
suppression, unintended propagation and completion of the original job. Do not
evaluate only “felt personalized,” number of retained preferences or apparent
control. Test unfamiliar users, not only the founder's richly documented week.

Recommended next decision: resolve a small set of instruction-scope cases through
the existing contribution/context contracts, then specify consumer behavior for
Home/Places and their Chat continuations. Start with this response, an explicit
root/time period and a named episode; durable defaults retain their separate
governed-evidence path. Review before adding a new domain object or memory store.
Immediate useful answers and independently supported preparation can progress
without waiting for every scope policy to settle.

The evidence supports context-sensitive help, meaningful correction and clear
boundaries; it does **not** settle a universal lifetime, recurrence threshold,
ranking formula, retention effect or competitive advantage. Our recommendation
is to make Vesper easy to redirect and dependable afterward—not to make the
person a diligent manager of its memory.

## Research trail and remaining uncertainty

The later §11 pass inspected selected methods, results and limitations in the
linked memory, objective-induction and forecasting papers, plus official HAX
and Gemini guidance. The JIT final proceedings page was inaccessible; its dated
preprint is identified rather than treated as the final version. These sources
were used for scoped-adaptation design reasoning, not for a claim that Vesper
has implemented or outperforms them. No new participant research was conducted.

Discovery covered cognitive/mental labor, intention offloading, mixed initiative,
2025–2026 proactive-assistance studies, Google contextual/background assistance
and Granola's preparation patterns. Follow-up examined original methods and
limits, checked newer Google announcements against the earlier January framing,
and inspected the local serving/production separation. The strongest corrective
finding was that several apparently behavioral results were actually vignette,
storyboard or synthetic-agent results; they cannot validate natural-use demand.

The sources above identify dates and access limits next to their claims. The
UCL paper's PDF was inaccessible; Daminger's publisher abstract was the accessible
evidence. Google's release-updates page yielded no usable release history, so
the May announcement is reported as an announcement rather than September
availability. Vendor functionality was not exercised. Search snippets and
secondary commentary were discovery leads, not proof of comparative quality.

Research stopped after every question had primary support or an explicit
evidence gap and concrete receiving implications. More desk research is unlikely
to settle actual willingness to delegate, offline relationship benefit, sustained
return or costs. Those remain unmeasured Vesper questions, not missing citations.
