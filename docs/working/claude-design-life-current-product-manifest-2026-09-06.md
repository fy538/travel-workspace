# Vesper — Life / Current Product — manifest

**Companion project:** "Life" — `https://claude.ai/design/p/e72a2fd2-799f-4c3d-861a-d5acaac1cdaf`
(type `PROJECT_TYPE_PROJECT`, founder-created). **Source:** the Sep 6 12:32
export of "Vesper — Life & Anchors" (`f524c7f0`, 121 canvases), which stays
intact — nothing there was deleted, renamed, or rewritten by this transfer.

Method: every panel in the new project is a **verbatim region** of a source
board (its root phone element, a study section, or a frame block), extracted
programmatically and re-wrapped in one compact shell with the three labels
(Current / Review / Later) outside the phone frames. No board was redrawn.
Kernel styles were unioned per board; the only definitional differences found
were a default `color` on `.kicker` and `white-space`/`flex` on `.meta`, where
the stricter first definition was kept.

**Register rule (second pass, same day):** the project reads in one register.
Every Life moment is a 393px screen in the root/dossier grammar; Chat and Home
touchpoints are plain *context strips* (a card with a sentence and a receipt
line — visibly not a phone), because their designs belong to their lanes. The
330px storyboard frames of the 21–24 and 31–38 sequences are no longer used;
their content was re-derived as screens from the roots and dossiers, with the
sequence boards remaining evidence in the original project. No step numbers,
no delta washes; one caption per moment; every canvas 1760px wide; headers at
most three lines.

**Consistency audit (Sep 6, third pass):** all fourteen boards checked
programmatically and by render — every canvas 1760px; one header shape
(eyebrow + status pill, serif title, italic question, ≤300-character
paragraph); the board's pill carries its status and panel pills appear only
where a panel's status differs (P1's Current Time root, P3's Later card, R0's
Later swipe); no 330px storyboard frames, delta washes, or step captions
remain; panel captions carry no source-board numbers (the manifest keeps the
mapping); the phrases moved out of phone frames in the first pass are absent
everywhere (one "EARNED · NOW" residue on 03b's 06A copy fixed); no internal
document shorthand (§, C&C, docket) in board copy. Remaining deliberate
register exceptions: 04's two 470px object studies (passes at true size, the
non-pass objects) and R0's reference sections.

**Value-and-continuity refinement (Sep 6, fourth pass — handoff
`claude-design-life-value-and-continuity-refinement-handoff-2026-09-06.md`):**
the project is now generated by one builder (`pages.py` + `build_all.py`)
so every board inherits the same laws: 32pt chips everywhere (card, serif,
and dark variants; 26px residue from the details resolved deliberately toward
the adopted V1), the customer-copy corrections ("Workflows" → "With this
pass"; "Custody" → "Everything kept · all of it, yours" / "Your originals";
"two projections revised" → "gone from the dinner and from here"; "nothing
here needs you" → "Kept, and not yet part of any episode"; "Complete here;
nothing to do" removed with the announcement it belonged to), no generic
Map-only utility rows (contextual Map·Share rows on journey and place stay;
the full-history scroll's Map removed per contract), and the labeled lens
selector present on every root including thin and zero-record.
