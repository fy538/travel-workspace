## Project-wide laws (applied at build)

One register: every Life moment is a 393px screen in the root/dossier grammar;
Chat and Home touchpoints are context strips (a card with a sentence and a
receipt line, visibly not a phone). No storyboard frames, delta washes, or step
captions. Every canvas 1760px; headers at most three lines. Chips are 32pt
everywhere (card, serif, dark). Customer copy carries no reviewer assurances
("Workflows" → "With this pass"; "Custody" → "Everything kept · all of it,
yours" / "Your originals"; "two projections revised" → "gone from the dinner
and from here"; "nothing here needs you" → "Kept, and not yet part of any
episode"; "what it means stays yours" → "the record noticed"; "no feeling
inferred", "nothing else claimed", "nothing was invented" removed). Generic
Map-only utility rows are removed (the full-history scroll; the pasta
question); contextual Map·Share rows on journey and place stay. The labeled
lens selector is on every root, including thin and zero-record.
