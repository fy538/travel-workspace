# Claude Design Brief — Multiplayer Prominence in Home and Places

## 0. Assignment

Extend the existing **Vesper — Home & Places** Claude Design project at:

```text
/Users/feihuyan/Downloads/vesper-home-places
```

Explore how multiplayer becomes visibly important across ordinary Home and
Places experiences without creating a social feed, a People tab, a profile
ritual, ambient location surveillance, or a collection of decorative friend
cards.

The project already contains accepted Home and Places semantics and several
strong multiplayer fixtures. The assignment is not to replace that work. It is
to pressure-test and extend it around one unresolved product question:

> How can another person visibly change the understanding, possibility,
> action, or continuity Vesper returns, while preserving authorship, private
> plurality, low social pressure, and exact consequence authority?

The intended result is a **controlled multiplayer-prominence exploration**:

- complete Home and Places compositions rather than isolated components;
- several materially different multiplayer situations;
- direct comparisons among decorative, supporting, and causal social
  treatments;
- viewer-relative projections over shared canonical truth;
- visible contribution consequence and withdrawal behavior; and
- decisions that can later constrain production composition and producer work.

This is a visual and interaction investigation. It does not authorize
production implementation, schema creation, a new product root, or changes to
the accepted four-root product model.

## 1. Required posture

Treat the existing Claude Design project as an accepted semantic north star,
not a blank canvas.

### Preserve

- Home as one editorial field with **Now · In motion · Horizons ·
  Continuity** in a stable order;
- Places as one navigable encounter: **World Field → Place Focus → Place
  Path**, with **Live Reduction** as cross-cutting compression;
- one dominant job per unit;
- one coherent attention hierarchy per screen;
- the existing physical visual grammar, typography, color, spacing, card,
  map, Door, receipt, and instrument families;
- root-native hierarchy rather than one universal card feed;
- current source, authority, evidence, grant, expiry, degradation, and
  consequence distinctions; and
- silence as an acceptable result.

### Challenge

- the assumption that cross-cutting multiplayer should usually remain
  visually subordinate;
- the tendency to represent people mainly through avatars, attribution lines,
  friend strips, or audience metadata;
- social treatment that remains isolated in dedicated fixtures rather than
  changing ordinary Home and Places compositions;
- Places scopes that remain personal even when an authorized relationship or
  Occasion materially changes the world field;
- live routes that are operationally single-player despite shared Plans and
  affected people; and
- social continuity described in doctrine but not made perceptible through
  the roots.

### Do not reinterpret “prominent” as

- more social cards;
- larger facepiles;
- a permanent With people chapter;
- a friend map;
- a visible activity stream;
- a generic invitation-growth module;
- forced group setup;
- a new notification class;
- public reaction or popularity metrics; or
- equal social density on every screen.

Prominence means that when a person is causally necessary to the value, the
person and their contribution survive into the unit's primary semantic and
visual hierarchy.

## 2. Executive product thesis

Vesper is not multiplayer because several people can see an itinerary, edit a
list, or vote on a restaurant. It is multiplayer because independent people
can maintain private relationships with one intelligence, enter a bounded
shared world, contribute different kinds of value, govern common consequences,
and carry forward what remains personally, relationally, and collectively
true.

The canonical strategic claim is:

> Vesper helps people give one another better access to places,
> possibilities, and shared experiences without requiring everyone to become
> a planner or participate in a social feed.

Multiplayer changes the product in three basic ways:

1. **People as sources.** Another person's situated attention, judgment, or
   evidence changes what Vesper can help the viewer understand.
2. **People as participants.** Who is involved changes what is feasible, what
   is protected, what is open, and who must authorize a consequence.
3. **People as relationships.** What people deliberately shared, did, or
   carried together may create a later opening without becoming a merged
   identity or permanent permission.

The differentiated reasoning unit is:

```text
person × relationship × Place × situation × Outcome
```

The shared-life law remains:

> Shared place, plural experience, governed common action.

## 3. How the multiplayer thinking evolved

The exploration must reflect the whole two-week evolution, not only the latest
surface vocabulary.

### 3.1 August 18 — from group planning to shared perception

The attention-trace work reframed multiplayer as **distributed attention**.
Several people may inhabit the same or related situations and notice different
layers. With permission, one person's trace can change another person's
encounter without requiring co-presence, simultaneous use, or a shared Plan.

Important concepts:

- borrowed lenses;
- asynchronous shared attention;
- one person routing curiosity to another;
- divergence without consensus;
- group decisions grounded in ordinary lived evidence;
- shared open loops;
- recurring relationship patterns without averaged taste profiles; and
- multiplayer as a consequence path, not a visibility default.

Design implication: a friend can be integral to an explanatory or spatial
path, not merely a social object adjacent to it.

### 3.2 August 21 — the social object is a trace, not a post

The differentiated-social work established that the Place can be a meaningful
third thing between people. A trace may be:

- kept private;
- addressed to a person;
- used as one bounded lens;
- contributed to an Occasion;
- carried into a governed consequence; or
- explicitly elevated into a public Place contribution.

Those are separate transitions. A share is not a universal widening toggle.

The AI's role is to mediate **relevance**, not intimacy. It may reduce recall,
search, timing, and coordination cost. It must preserve the person's choice to
initiate, the person's interpretation of why the relationship matters, the
recipient's freedom not to respond, and the human work of care and repair.

Design implication: Vesper may produce a truthful reason for contact, but the
interface must not impersonate warmth or auto-author an intimate message.

### 3.3 August 23 — the whole Occasion lifecycle

The gathering and behavioral-prototype work expanded multiplayer across:

- **Before:** invitation, social accessibility, participation flexibility,
  private orientation, and a truthful way in;
- **During:** presence, contribution, shared agency, bounded facilitation, and
  the interface receding;
- **After:** shared occurrence, plural Outcomes, afterglow, correction, and a
  clean ending; and
- **Later:** relay, relationship continuity, ritual, second Occasion, or
  earned silence.

The key distinction is:

> Invitation is not incorporation.

A recipient may need private value and a legible route into the temporary
social world before being asked to do group work. The product should not solve
that by forcing an Occasion-specific profile.

The later prototype ruling rejected automatic contextual identity. The target
is human-authored Status or no identity treatment, with actual contributions
and mutually visible context providing social footholds.

### 3.4 August 27–31 — object-native circulation

The four-root and Plan/Occasion work established:

> Social material enters through a person but lands in the Place, Plan,
> Occasion, Source, or possibility it changes.

For example:

```text
Maya sends a Place
  → the handoff belongs to the Place

it enables a new comparison
  → the comparison may appear on Home

it changes Saturday dinner
  → the contribution belongs to the Occasion

it produces retained consequence
  → the authorized result belongs in Life

the person wants to respond or shape it
  → the action begins in Chat
```

This is not one social surface. It is one social cause projecting through the
root that owns the current human job.

## 4. The complete multiplayer value model

The exploration should cover at least four of these five value families and
must not reduce multiplayer to group coordination.

### 4.1 Shared perception

People help one another perceive more through:

- attributed observation;
- borrowed lenses;
- local or situated judgment;
- plural comparison;
- a question that becomes useful to someone else;
- a distant person's perspective becoming relevant on arrival; or
- Vesper connecting separately authored evidence on one bounded axis.

The interface must preserve who supplied which lane and what connective claim
belongs to Vesper.

### 4.2 Social accessibility

Vesper may make a human situation easier to enter by providing:

- host-authored intent;
- separately labeled recipient relevance;
- one permissioned foothold;
- one optional contribution the recipient could bring;
- flexible ways to participate; and
- a way to decline, defer, ignore, or leave without public residue.

It must not predict compatibility, rank people, assign a social role, or turn
the Occasion into a people browser.

### 4.3 Shared agency

People govern common consequences through:

- invitations;
- contributions;
- private caucus;
- Decisions;
- Commitments;
- mandates and delegation;
- responsibility;
- live repair;
- reconvergence; or
- an intentional subgroup split.

The visible group result may be shared while private rationales remain private.

### 4.4 Relationship continuity

An encounter or Occasion may create a later reason for contact without
automatically creating friendship, Follow, recurring-group identity, or
notification permission.

The continuity gradient is:

```text
addressed
  → Occasion-bounded recognizability
  → optional private remembrance
  → explicitly mutual or directional reachability
  → confirmed recurring relationship context
  → dormant, expired, narrowed, revoked, or cleanly ended
```

“Remember privately,” “keep in orbit,” “make me reachable,” “invite again,”
and “share contact” authorize different consequences.

### 4.5 The Place commons — exploratory and deferred

A person may eventually leave a bounded, attributed contribution with a Place:

- stable perceptual lens;
- time-scoped access observation;
- correction to Place identity;
- resident or expert interpretation;
- situated warning; or
- question inviting plural perspectives.

The reward is useful downstream consequence and refinement, not likes or
creator reach. Do not make this a primary production recommendation in this
design pass. It may appear as one clearly labeled horizon specimen only.

## 5. Social units and their different behavior

Do not flatten all multiplayer into a generic group.

| Unit | Core opportunity | Key risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pair | Frequent handoff, micro-coordination, private negotiation, shared ritual | Merged taste or couple assumptions |
| Recurring circle | Lower-friction recurrence, remembered ritual, fairer participation | Permanent group personality |
| Ephemeral party | Bounded Occasion with conservative defaults | Treating roster as durable friendship |
| Host and guest | Situated knowledge and care without full membership | Host authority becoming universal |
| Family or household | Frequent asymmetric coordination | Ignoring care, age, representation, or account asymmetry |
| Solo | Complete personal product value | Social density becoming a requirement |

This exploration should prioritize **pair**, **host and guest**, and one
**ephemeral Occasion**. Pair-first value makes multiplayer visible in ordinary
life without requiring several people to adopt the app simultaneously.

## 6. Contribution is broader than artifact sharing

People must appear through the effect they had, not only through media they
uploaded.

Eligible contributions include:

| Contribution | What it may change |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | Why a shared world exists and what it protects |
| Presence | The fact and form of participation |
| Perspective | A question, story, interpretation, or way of seeing |
| Initiative | A suggestion, introduction, detour, or branch |
| Situated judgment | Timing, access, conditions, local knowledge, or warning |
| Practical labor | Bringing, booking, navigating, paying, preparing, coordinating |
| Care and boundaries | Access, rest, privacy, pace, safety, or right to decline |
| Commitment and evidence | Promise, responsibility, photo, ticket, correction, observed change |
| Meaning and continuation | What remains shared or may open later |

Examples of stronger multiplayer state:

- “Maya is bringing dessert,” not merely “Maya added an artifact.”
- “Daniel's ferry knowledge changed the return branch,” not “Daniel likes Red
  Hook.”
- “A private constraint removed the late option,” not an attribution that
  exposes who supplied it.
- “Jess accepted the reservation responsibility,” not a generic group avatar.

## 7. Authority and projection model

The visual work must distinguish four lanes even when the consumer interface
uses lighter language.

| Lane | Contents | Visibility consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shared_core` | Accepted facts, Decisions, Commitments, revisions, unresolved shared state | Eligible viewers must receive the same current truth at an owner revision |
| `audience_lane` | Attributed contribution visible to a defined audience | Viewer-specific according to current grant and purpose |
| `personal_overlay` | Private constraint, preparation, relevance, note, or possibility | Never becomes shared merely because it shaped private judgment |
| `system_assessment` | Vesper's ranking, feasibility, treatment, suppression, or Opening | Expiring read judgment; never common truth or owner authority |

Different participants may therefore see:

- different elevation and ordering;
- different explanations and density;
- different private preparation;
- different audience-scoped contributions;
- different available actions; and
- the same canonical dinner time, Place decision, or Plan revision.

This is a relaxed shared-view model, not inconsistency.

### 7.1 Five authority axes remain distinct

- **Use:** what immediate job, purpose, and consumer this contribution may
  power;
- **Retention:** what Source, claim, or projection may persist;
- **Inference:** what semantic distance and learning target are permitted;
- **Audience:** who may see what, including contributor, subject, recipient,
  custodian, and affected principals; and
- **Action:** what may be advised, prepared, proposed, committed, or externally
  executed.

Audience alone does not authorize reuse. Attribution alone does not authorize
naming. Membership alone does not authorize future continuity.

### 7.2 Consumer interaction posture

Keep the underlying authority exact while keeping the visible interaction
human:

- low-risk private work may happen with a quiet receipt;
- a social or material boundary should be previewed once at the point of
  consequence;
- one human choice may resolve several internally distinct axes if the exact
  effect is legible;
- do not expose a permission worksheet;
- declining and silence are valid completion states; and
- no response becomes inferred rejection or relationship state.

## 8. Current Claude Design project assessment

### 8.1 Existing strengths to preserve

The current bundle already provides valuable multiplayer work:

- `Fixture F4 - Attributed Friends.dc.html` makes a two-person comparison a
  dominant Home contribution and keeps each evidence lane separate from
  Vesper's synthesis.
- `Canon - Places Social Anatomy.dc.html` defines four admissible Places
  forms: attributed evidence, relational relevance, plural comparison, and
  participation consequence.
- `C2 - The Occasion.dc.html` models host, link guest, contributor, private
  constraint, bounded decision, and settled shared consequence.
- `C2 - The Grant Moment.dc.html` expresses a material social boundary as one
  understandable human choice rather than consent ceremony.
- `C2 - The Evening and After.dc.html` carries correction through Occurrence,
  plural personal views, relay, and later initiation.
- `Home - Returned Saturday.dc.html` includes Maya's Place note as one
  supporting contribution.
- `Places - 2 Place Focus.dc.html` includes Maya's attributed Red Hook
  judgment.
- `people_status_aperture` exists as an accepted Home specimen.

These are not to be discarded or cosmetically redrawn.

### 8.2 The unresolved gap

The strongest social work lives in explicitly social fixtures. In ordinary
root compositions, multiplayer commonly becomes:

- a supporting row;
- an avatar;
- a friend strip;
- an attribution line;
- an audience label; or
- a note that does not alter the root's hierarchy.

The project has not yet shown:

1. when a relationship can reorganize an otherwise ordinary Home;
2. pair-first multiplayer outside an Occasion;
3. the World Field changing under a relationship or Occasion scope;
4. affected people changing a Place Path or Live Reduction;
5. the contributor and recipient seeing different truthful consequences;
6. withdrawal recompiling both roots; or
7. the same multiplayer cause moving coherently through Home and Places
   without becoming a social shelf.

## 9. Central visual hypothesis

Test this law:

> When another person materially changes the understanding, possibility,
> action, or continuity, that person and their contribution must be visible in
> the unit's primary semantic hierarchy rather than relegated to metadata.

Use this counterfactual:

> If replacing the named person with “someone online” loses nothing, the
> social attribution is decorative. If removing the person destroys a
> material part of the value, the relationship should be immediately legible.

Every relevant board must compare three possible treatments:

1. **Metadata social:** person appears as attribution, avatar, count, or
   supporting fact.
2. **Supporting social:** person receives a secondary row, strip, or module.
3. **Causal social:** their contribution changes the dominant understanding,
   possibility, route, decision, or consequence.

The goal is not to promote treatment 3 universally. It is to discover the
evidence and composition conditions under which it is the honest treatment.

## 10. Home composition requirements

Home should be able to answer five social questions when authorized evidence
earns them:

1. What has someone given me?
2. What are we currently doing together?
3. What changes because of who is involved?
4. What could I naturally open with someone?
5. What did we create or carry forward together?

### 10.1 Now

Multiplayer may own the dominant stage when shared consequence is the most
important current truth:

- active invitation decision;
- changed Plan affecting several people;
- Occasion with one open consequence;
- live meeting or repair state;
- one addressed handoff that materially answers the current situation; or
- one prepared relational opening.

The crown must show what is settled, flexible, open, affected, and actionable.
It must not become a generic group recap.

### 10.2 In motion

Eligible material includes:

- Occasion state;
- accepted or pending participation;
- contribution received;
- responsibility accepted;
- one bounded Decision;
- a repair currently moving; or
- a contribution whose consequence is still unfolding.

Show people through what changed, not as a standing roster.

### 10.3 Horizons

This is the primary home of low-pressure social possibility:

- a Status creates a natural opening;
- two independently kept Place threads become timely;
- a friend's handoff makes one present possibility richer;
- mutual availability supports a prepared opening; or
- a Place change makes an old promise relevant.

The product may prepare the possibility. A human initiates contact.

### 10.4 Continuity

Eligible material includes:

- Our Place or shared ritual becoming useful again;
- a second Occasion using confirmed evidence from the first;
- a friend's contribution remaining useful in another context;
- shared occurrence with plural personal Outcomes;
- a retained relationship-place thread; or
- an ended social world that remains findable but no longer returns
  automatically.

Do not infer a group feeling or relationship identity.

### 10.5 Home prominence amendment under test

The accepted reservoir law remains directionally correct, but this pass should
test a stronger version:

> People are a cross-cutting source of evidence, agency, consequence, and
> continuity. When a relationship or shared world is the dominant subject,
> multiplayer may own any Home region, including the crown.

## 11. Places composition requirements

Places should become relationship-aware without becoming a friend map.

### 11.1 World Field

Test temporary explicit scopes such as:

- `For me`;
- `With Maya`;
- `For Friday dinner`; and
- `From people I trust`.

These are not permanent Places tabs. They are bounded scope handles over the
same World Field architecture.

The scope may change:

- what is admitted;
- why a branch matters;
- which authored traces appear;
- which constraints shape feasibility;
- which actions are available; and
- how the field narrows.

The map must not reveal live presence or infer availability.

### 11.2 Place Focus

One Place may need to show:

- who brought it into the viewer's world;
- what that person actually contributed;
- why the contribution matters here;
- what Vesper independently verified;
- what remains personal perspective;
- whether shared history or prospective intent exists; and
- what consequence the contribution enabled.

“Maya saved this” is insufficient. A person must change the reading.

### 11.3 Place Path

Explore paths such as:

- one Place through two people’s separate evidence;
- one friend's situated handoff adapted to present conditions;
- a Place relationship connecting two episodes;
- one Occasion route shaped by several contributions;
- a shared ritual across Places; or
- a pair's different perspectives preserved rather than averaged.

The person may be part of the explanatory path. They should not become a
separate social shelf.

### 11.4 Live Reduction

Make multiplayer operational:

- who is affected;
- what changed;
- what remains protected;
- which contribution or Commitment shaped the route;
- who owns the next decision;
- whether Vesper may prepare, propose, or act;
- which fallback preserves the shared purpose; and
- whether people stay together, split, or reconverge.

Private rationale may shape the safe result without appearing in the shared
explanation.

### 11.5 Four admissible social forms

Retain the accepted forms:

1. attributed evidence;
2. relational relevance;
3. plural comparison; and
4. participation consequence.

Each complete Places scenario should use at least two. The Occasion/Live
scenario should use participation consequence.

### MP1 — Pair handoff: someone gives me a better way into a Place

**Situation:** Maya deliberately sends Feihu a Red Hook judgment. She is not
present and no Occasion exists. Her useful distinction is: use the ferry to
arrive; do not depend on it to return. Current service and route facts are
verified independently by Vesper.

**Required views:**

1. recipient Home full scroll;
2. recipient Place Focus;
3. recipient Place Path or bounded route consequence;
4. contributor receipt showing what her note changed; and
5. compact grant/provenance inspection state.

**Compare:**

- `Maya shared Red Hook` metadata;
- a supporting Maya note row; and
- a causal treatment in which her distinction changes Saturday's route.

**Questions:**

- When does the handoff earn Home prominence?
- Can value be complete before Respond or Thank?
- Can Vesper verification and Maya's judgment remain visibly separate?
- Does the contributor see efficacy without public engagement metrics?
- Can withdrawal be understandable from both sides?

**Hard failures:**

- friend recommendation feed;
- inferred live presence;
- Vesper paraphrase presented as Maya's claim;
- response debt;
- generic social proof; or
- Maya becoming a stable taste profile.

### MP2 — Relational opening: when may another person reorganize Home?

**Situation:** Maya has a human-authored, audience-scoped `just back from
Paris` Status. Feihu and Maya separately retained waterfront traces. Current
New York conditions make one Saturday possibility newly useful. No one has
started a Plan or Occasion.

**Required compositions:**

1. social detail as metadata only;
2. social detail as a supporting Horizon;
3. social possibility as the dominant Home unit;
4. the selected bounded possibility underneath each treatment; and
5. the state after the user ignores it.

**Required decision annotations:**

- evidence required for each elevation;
- why now;
- why this person;
- what Vesper added;
- what remains for the person to initiate;
- expiry;
- why ignore creates no debt; and
- what would make silence better.

**Primary question:** what exact threshold moves a person from supporting
context into Home's primary hierarchy?

**Hard failures:**

- “you two would love this”;
- inferred mutual availability;
- AI-written intimate outreach;
- match scores;
- automatic Occasion creation; or
- a reminder after ignore.

### MP3 — One World Field, three social scopes

**Situation:** use the same Saturday New York evidence and render:

1. `For me`;
2. `With Maya`; and
3. `For Friday dinner`.

**Keep stable:**

- the Places information architecture;
- map grammar;
- basic geographic truth;
- selected Place identity;
- route/source honesty; and
- World Field → Focus → Path behavior.

**Allow to change:**

- admitted branches;
- ordering and dominance;
- explanation;
- attributed evidence;
- feasible route shape;
- current constraints;
- actions; and
- visible contribution consequence.

**Required views:**

- three adjacent full World Fields;
- one shared Place Focus opened from each;
- a difference ledger explaining what changed and what did not; and
- a scope-control specimen showing how the user enters and leaves the scope.

**Questions:**

- Can relationship scope feel powerful without becoming a mode switch?
- Is the World Field still recognizably one product?
- Which private overlay may alter ranking without appearing publicly?
- How does the interface explain why a Place differs across scopes?
- Does scope exit restore private Places without losing navigation context?

**Hard failures:**

- separate friend maps;
- divergent accepted facts;
- hidden person inference;
- unexplained disappearance;
- location disclosure; or
- a scope that behaves like a permanent relationship profile.

### MP4 — Complete Occasion through Places

**Situation:** extend the existing C2 dinner into the Places root. Dana is a
guest using a link, Sam contributes situated Place judgment, a private budget
constraint shapes feasibility, the host resolves one bounded decision, and a
later Place correction forces a safe recomposition.

**Required views:**

1. Occasion-scoped World Field;
2. candidate Place Focus showing authored contribution and independent facts;
3. Place Path for the selected evening;
4. Live Reduction before correction;
5. Live Reduction after correction and fallback promotion;
6. host projection;
7. guest projection; and
8. contributor projection/receipt.

**Show clearly:**

- shared core;
- audience-scoped contribution;
- private overlay's group-safe effect;
- Vesper's assessment;
- host decision authority;
- affected people;
- what was never booked;
- what the correction invalidated;
- what remained settled; and
- why only one bounded notification was justified.

**Primary question:** can Places express shared agency and repair without
duplicating the Occasion decision UI or becoming a group workspace?

**Hard failures:**

- exposing the private budget source;
- showing nonresponse publicly;
- poll or preference questionnaire;
- treating unknown as compatible;
- correction silently following the replacement Place;
- generic group recap; or
- assistant prose standing in for owner/provider readback.

### MP5 — Withdrawal, narrowing, block, and clean ending

**Situation A:** Maya withdraws the Paris trace that supported the Rome–Paris
comparison.

**Situation B:** Maya narrows exact Place precision to city-level Paris.

**Situation C:** Feihu blocks Maya after independent personal evidence already
exists.

**Required before/after views:**

- Home;
- Place Path;
- Source/provenance inspection;
- any retained composition;
- explicit retrieval state where still permitted; and
- quiet state after automatic social return is suppressed.

**Demonstrate:**

- dependent social projection disappears, degrades, or recompiles;
- independent Feihu evidence remains;
- precision reduces everywhere, including map, snippet, title, and route;
- generated paraphrase does not preserve withdrawn content;
- block suppresses relationship rendering and future openings without
  deleting Maya's independent record;
- unblock restores no old grant; and
- the root remains useful and visually complete after social removal.

**Primary question:** can the design make withdrawal real without turning
privacy into maintenance or making absence look like a broken surface?

### MP6 — Multiplayer component and composition disposition

Only after MP1–MP5, extract the smallest reusable grammar.

For every proposed component or treatment, record:

- name;
- semantic job;
- owning root/state;
- accepted evidence;
- viewer/audience requirements;
- source and authorship display;
- consequence behavior;
- degradation behavior;
- withdrawal behavior;
- whether an existing component can express it;
- whether it is a root kind, shared instrument, or composition recipe; and
- whether it should be built, adapted, retained as fixture-only, or rejected.

Expected reuse candidates include:

- attributed evidence lane;
- social cause line;
- contribution consequence receipt;
- affected-people row;
- relationship scope handle;
- author/system distinction;
- viewer-relative shared-state instrument;
- relay/handoff Door; and
- withdrawal/degradation treatment.

Do not add a component merely because it appears in one board.

It is a strategic horizon, not a V1 commitment.

## 14. Board construction requirements

### 14.1 Complete experiences, not fragments

MP1–MP5 must show enough of the full root scroll or state to judge:

- hierarchy;
- competition with non-social value;
- demand;
- density taper;
- root identity;
- stable doors;
- silence;
- and where the social material ends.

Do not crop away the non-social parts that make prominence a real tradeoff.

### 14.2 Same evidence, controlled comparisons

When comparing treatments, hold evidence constant. Change only:

- elevation;
- form;
- medium;
- root placement;
- or available consequence.

Do not make the causal treatment appear better by secretly giving it richer
evidence.

### 14.3 Viewer-relative truth

For Occasion and withdrawal boards, show multiple viewers side by side.

Every shared item should have:

- stable semantic identity;
- owner revision;
- lane;
- source or actor;
- audience basis;
- human-refindable label; and
- current consequence state.

“The third card” or identical screen cloning is not sufficient.

### 14.4 Output before input

Home and Places return value. They must not become places where Vesper asks
people to:

- write reflections;
- rank preferences;
- explain the relationship;
- maintain profiles;
- approve every low-risk inference;
- respond to a friend;
- or perform gratitude.

Any required decision should occur only at a real social, information-flow, or
shared-consequence boundary.

### 14.5 Content bar

Social content must provide substance beyond reporting that another person was
present, saved something, or traveled somewhere.

Valid Vesper contribution includes:

- a concrete comparison;
- a hidden spatial or practical relation;
- a reconstructed consequence;
- a materially better route or decision;
- a source-bounded explanation;
- an earned connection between separate evidence; or
- a new usable possibility.

Avoid personality interpretation, relationship narration, and empty lines such
as “Maya's experience connects to yours.”

## 15. Visual-language constraints

Use the current project's visual kernel. This is not a request for a new social
brand language.

### Preserve

- parchment and ink palette;
- oxblood for protected or consequential state;
- gold for action and selected consequence;
- blue or other existing person/source distinction where already established;
- serif/mono hierarchy;
- physical card rhythm;
- map honesty;
- Door grammar;
- compact receipts;
- density taper; and
- the ban on decorative left-edge accents.

### Explore carefully

- authored human lanes versus Vesper synthesis;
- multiple people without facepile dominance;
- cause-and-effect rather than avatar-first composition;
- relationship scope as contextual chrome;
- affected-person state without roster bureaucracy;
- plural views over one shared owner;
- social withdrawal without red-warning theater; and
- contribution consequence as efficacy rather than engagement.

### Avoid

- social-media gradients;
- presence dots;
- online status unless explicitly authored and scoped;
- follower or friend counts;
- heart, like, reaction, or streak metaphors;
- large profile cards;
- personality labels;
- celebratory animation for ordinary consent; and
- visual pressure to reply.

## 16. Interaction and copy laws

1. Name the human source before Vesper's transformation.
2. Label Vesper synthesis as synthesis, not as the person's voice.
3. Make current consequence more prominent than historical activity.
4. Distinguish prepared, proposed, authorized, committed, unknown, read back,
   corrected, withdrawn, and superseded.
5. Use human verbs such as Send, Address, Share, Invite, Keep, Pull, and Open
   together; keep authority-axis vocabulary in inspection or annotation.
6. Let ignore and decline complete silently.
7. Do not show one person's private response state to another unless the
   shared contract requires it.
8. Do not ask someone to restate information Vesper already has authority to
   use.
9. Do not draft intimacy as fact.
10. Do not infer relationship health from contact frequency or silence.
11. State important limits: who sees it, what travels, what does not, expiry,
    and what cannot be unread after external delivery.
12. Every state-changing action must imply a canonical owner, revision,
    confirmation boundary where required, receipt, and readback.

- friends-nearby surveillance;

- social attribution that adds no value;
- a social module that remains empty when evidence disappears; or
- a fifth social root hidden inside Home or Places.

## 18. Evaluation gates

Use the following gates for every required scenario.

### Product value

- **G1 — Materiality:** removing the named person removes or materially weakens
  the value.
- **G2 — Additive contribution:** Vesper adds a comparison, explanation,
  preparation, route, or consequence rather than repeating the human trace.
- **G3 — Immediate payoff:** the viewer receives value before being asked to
  reply, join, classify, or maintain anything.
- **G4 — Root fit:** Home answers why now; Places answers why here, there, or
  along this path.

### Social integrity

- **G5 — Authorship:** each human lane and Vesper operation are distinguishable.
- **G6 — Plurality:** no private Outcome or perspective becomes group meaning.
- **G7 — No debt:** ignore, decline, leave, and silence create no public shame
  or relationship inference.
- **G8 — Purpose:** the displayed use is permitted for this consumer and
  purpose, not merely visible somewhere.
- **G9 — Affected people:** contributors, subjects, recipients, custodians, and
  affected principals are not silently collapsed.

### Consequence integrity

- **G10 — State legibility:** prepared, proposed, authorized, committed,
  corrected, and read-back states do not blur.
- **G11 — Owner boundary:** the design never implies that a card, transcript,
  or Composition owns shared truth.
- **G12 — Repair:** correction recomputes dependent projections without asking
  everyone to restart.
- **G13 — Withdrawal:** withdrawn content disappears or degrades causally,
  including generated derivatives.

### Attention and composition

- **G14 — Earned prominence:** social hierarchy follows material value rather
  than a quota.
- **G15 — Stable root:** the screen remains recognizably Home or Places.
- **G16 — Density:** the screen ends when the next unit does not beat silence.
- **G17 — Present world:** multiplayer does not crowd out current practical or
  spatial truth merely because social material exists.
- **G18 — Fullness after removal:** the root remains useful and dignified when
  social evidence is absent or revoked.

### Comprehension

- **G19 — Why this person:** the viewer can explain why this person's material
  matters here.
- **G20 — What changed:** the viewer and contributor can identify the bounded
  consequence without reading a policy document.
- **G21 — What is private:** each viewer can predict what others do not see.
- **G22 — What happens next:** available actions and the consequence of doing
  nothing are clear.

No scenario passes from visual polish alone.

## 25. Final standard

prominent because people are more consequential—not because social material is
more numerous.

> Vesper should make it perceptible what another person helped me see, what
> became possible because of our relationship, what their contribution
> changed, what we are causing together, and what remains useful between us
> afterward—while preserving each person's authorship, private life, freedom
> not to respond, and ability to leave.
