# Life & Anchors — Fresh Design Audit and Correction Brief

> Audience: the Claude Design agent continuing the project
> `~/Downloads/vesper-life-anchors`.
>
> This is a correction phase, not a request to restart the project, produce
> production code, or polish more variants. Preserve the strong design system.
> Resolve the semantic and experiential gaps below through a small number of
> complete compositions.

## Executive verdict

The project is directionally right and unusually coherent. It establishes a
credible fourth root, a strong object language, one viewer-relative corpus
across four lenses, dignified thin states, target-first retrieval, and a
separate custody surface. It is the strongest expression of Life produced so
far.

The project nevertheless canonized the **record anatomy** before fully drawing
the **return anatomy**. As rendered, most Life elements culminate in more
record: a row opens a dossier, a dossier opens an organ, an organ opens a full
list, and a Source opens an object. That is excellent continuity and retrieval,
but it is not yet the complete post-pivot product.

The corrected model is:

> **Life is Vesper's governed continuity index: every eligible Journey, Place,
> Thread, Person/relationship, Occasion, and held object is both a durable
> record and a persistent foundation from which Vesper can open the world
> again.**

The archive is necessary. It makes the opening trustworthy, inspectable,
repairable, and cumulative. It is not the ultimate value. The ultimate value
is that accumulated life becomes useful again through reconstruction, new
understanding, capability transfer, relational continuity, practical relief,
or a grounded future possibility.

Do not discard the current system. Add the missing return grammar, correct the
two lenses that currently violate product psychology, and pressure-test the
whole thing outside the Europe-rich fixture world before treating the anatomy
as build-ready.

## 1. What the design actually accomplished

The design is not one screen. It is a coherent system with six layers:

1. **A material kernel** — paper, serif/mono hierarchy, quiet rules, semantic
   color, doors, truth treatments, and source-aware media.
2. **An anchor/object language** — passes at object scale, chips and marks at
   smaller scales, stable identity, containment, occurrence truth, and source
   provenance.
3. **A Life index** — Time, Places, Threads, and People as four readings of one
   corpus rather than four databases.
4. **A fractal held-thing grammar** — dossiers at Journey, chapter, Place,
   Thread, Person, and Occasion grain.
5. **A retrieval and custody system** — full scrolls, semantic search, object
   pages, Vault, grants, withdrawal, correction, and deletion.
6. **A cross-root loop hypothesis** — Life supplies governed continuity;
   current consequences appear through Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion;
   receipts return to Life.

That is a substantial product-design achievement. The correction phase should
not reduce it to a conventional photo timeline, journal, memory feed, contact
graph, or travel wallet.

## 2. Preserve these decisions

The following decisions survive the fresh audit and should remain the control
unless a pressure composition disproves them.

### 2.1 One corpus, four explorations

Time, Places, Threads, and People are useful human orientations over the same
authorized record. Correction, deletion, withdrawal, and authority change
must recompile every lens rather than create copies.

### 2.2 Stable orientation instead of an engagement feed

Life should remain recognizably stable. It must not rotate material per visit,
shuffle memories for novelty, or optimize for consumption. Changes may follow
time, evidence, authority, explicit user direction, or a material change in
the referenced world.

### 2.3 The pass family and miniaturization ladder

The pass family is the project's clearest object-design success. Full anatomy
at L1/L2 and `chip -> mark` below it prevent dollhouse UI while preserving
object identity. Planned, occurred, unused, cancelled, revised, and disputed
truth remain distinct.

### 2.4 Thin is short, not needy

The minimum dossier is one of the strongest compositions. Conditional organs,
no empty slots, no completion score, and no request to feed the archive are
canonical. The user has already done work by living and contributing.

### 2.5 Target-first retrieval and visible containment

Search should return the sought thing before reconstructing context. The
containment chain is valuable orientation. Raw query language must not become
durable interest or relationship evidence.

### 2.6 Custody is separate from the living record

The Vault is correctly a back-of-house surface rather than a fifth lens.
Arrivals, unplaced artifacts, grants, withdrawal, provenance, export, release,
and deletion belong here or in contextual stewardship—not in the main Life
composition.

### 2.7 No input homework on Life

Life is an output and return surface. Correction and authorship remain
available, but the default page must never ask the person to classify,
complete, journal, reflect, close a Thread, or maintain the archive.

## 3. The central correction: record plus opening

The current project uses “opening” in three inconsistent ways:

- opening a row means navigating to a dossier;
- an unresolved Thread is called open;
- Home/Places receive a product Opening generated from prior evidence.

These are different concepts and must not share one vague interaction promise.

### 3.1 Adopt a three-layer anatomy for eligible Life objects

Every eligible Life object should support three separable layers:

```text
THE RECORD
  what this is, what happened, what is supported, and where it came from

WHAT IT HAS CONNECTED TO
  accumulated episodes, people, Places, authored observations, Outcomes,
  compositions, and causal receipts

WHAT IT CAN OPEN NOW
  zero or a bounded number of earned current returns, compiled for this
  viewer and moment, with reason, owner, freshness, and destination
```

The first two are durable and governed. The third is a current projection,
not another permanent truth about the person.

### 3.2 Keep the dossier as the stable destination

Do not replace every index tap with a dynamic recommendation. The accepted
“tap lands on dossier” rule can remain because it preserves orientation and
trust. Correct it as follows:

- the dossier is the durable home of the object;
- an optional `WHAT THIS OPENS` or `AVAILABLE NOW` organ may appear near the
  top when an earned return exists;
- the return must deliver value before asking for action;
- it names why it is newly useful and which current condition makes it so;
- it hands off to the consequence-owning surface with a projection envelope;
- no eligible return means the organ is absent, not empty;
- the historical dossier remains available even when the return expires.

This makes every element a **foundation for an opening** without turning every
element into an unsolicited recommendation.

### 3.3 Restore one bounded root Returns region

Adopt Option B from `life-returns-layer-ruling-2026-08-30.md`, with tighter
language:

- one `RETURNS` region between the scroll door and reflective material;
- zero to three rows;
- only the already accepted classes: `Continue where you left off`, `Newly
  useful`, `Recently completed`, `From someone you know`, and `Worth returning
  to`;
- every row states its material reason inline;
- the region changes only when time, evidence, authority, explicit direction,
  or the referenced world materially changes;
- the entire layer can be suppressed without hiding search or stable access;
- it is not filled to quota and never rotates for freshness.

The root Returns region is a selective front door. The dossier return organ is
the complete object-relative home. Home and Places still own timely and
spatial consequence.

### 3.4 Correct the loop language

Retire “Life generates; Home and Places deliver.” A surface does not generate
intelligence. Use:

> **Life holds governed evidence, lineage, and durable handles. Vesper
> recompiles them against the present. Home and Places deliver timely or
> spatial consequence; Chat, Plan, and Occasion help it work. Life preserves
> the resulting receipt.**

Redraw board 04 around that model. Keep the causal loop and correction path;
remove the implication that Life is an engine or owner of every generated
Opening.

## 4. Lens-by-lens correction

The four lenses are conceptually valid. Their current digest rules are not
equally safe or mature.

### 4.1 Time — strongest lens, but too much automatic nostalgia

What works:

- current period, Journey, Occasion, and chapter containment are legible;
- chronology survives without becoming a flat log;
- upcoming, occurred, hosted, and returned states can coexist;
- the full scroll provides dependable historical access.

Correct:

- `A YEAR AGO TODAY` does not earn a seat merely because the date matches. It
  must deliver experiential return or useful continuity; otherwise omit it.
- `FROM THE FULL RECORD` cannot exist solely as proof that the archive is
  deep. A specimen needs a retrieval, experiential, social, or factual reason
  to appear.
- generated reads such as “this summer still settling” may imply an inner
  state. Prefer supported descriptive orientation or let the statline lead.
- upcoming Occasions belong in Time as governed lifecycle continuity, but
  present coordination belongs to Home/Chat/Occasion rather than expanding
  inside Life.

### 4.2 Places — useful relationship lens, but must not duplicate Places root

What works:

- the lens shows where the person's life has actually accumulated;
- region grouping and Place clusters provide strong retrieval structure;
- mixed evidence can reveal a relationship with a Place without claiming
  taste or identity.

Correct:

- Life/Places owns **your held relationship and episodes**, not current hours,
  generic discovery, or the whole world model.
- Generic `Map` is ambiguous. Distinguish `Map this record` from a handoff to
  current Places depth.
- “Keeps opening” is currently a promise without a visible opening. Either
  show an earned return or use factual relationship copy.
- Place frequency alone cannot generate intimacy, preference, or a top-level
  cluster.
- A Life Place dossier may show `WHAT THIS OPENS NOW`; its spatial depth
  hands off to Places with historical relation separated from refreshed world
  truth.

### 4.3 Threads — strategically important, currently the largest product error

Threads can make evolving attention and capability visible. That is highly
differentiated. The current Thread composition instead behaves like a project
manager.

The pasta fixture currently says:

- `Three pursuits open`;
- `One pan, at home — not yet attempted`;
- `One pan from closing`;
- `The next step`;
- `Cook it`.

This turns curiosity into unfinished work. It also contradicts the fixture
admission rule: the current pasta evidence is `admitted_nested_not_thread` /
`ineligible_single_observation` until a second supported cluster or authored
attempt exists.

Correct:

- remove `OPEN / RESTING / CLOSED` as the dominant user-facing lifecycle;
- remove completion language and progress-to-closure copy;
- do not promote pasta to a first-class Thread merely because it makes an
  attractive screen;
- keep it nested under the Italy Journey until the seven Thread entrance gates
  and operation-specific evidence threshold pass;
- when admitted, frame a Thread as a line of attention that has reappeared,
  deepened, or become useful—not an obligation;
- a Thread may end in better seeing, a satisfying explanation, a comparison,
  or silence. It does not owe an action;
- an optional present continuation must be complete-on-view before offering a
  Place, article, recipe, conversation, or practical action.

Design a qualifying and non-qualifying Thread side by side. Show merge, split,
rename, detach, demote, and correction paths.

### 4.4 People — essential multiplayer lens, currently too close to profiling

The People lens is strategically justified: lived continuity includes people,
and multiplayer should enrich the same record rather than create a social
feed. The current composition nevertheless implies relationship scoring:

- `CLOSE / FURTHER`;
- `4 HELD CLOSE`;
- `12 SHARED NIGHTS`;
- `Mostly the two of you`;
- `2K MSGS`;
- a universal dossier named for another person.

Frequency is not intimacy. A private system can still feel invasive when its
composition claims to rank closeness or summarize a relationship.

Correct:

- organize around **viewer-relative shared episodes and explicitly retained
  relationship context**, not an inferred contact ranking;
- prefer `Shared with Maya`, `Held together`, `Occasions with Maya`, or an
  authored relationship label over a dossier *about* Maya;
- remove `CLOSE / FURTHER` unless the user explicitly authors that grouping;
- remove message volume as a crown metric;
- show Conversations only when the thread was explicitly retained or linked;
  chat history is not durable evidence by default;
- do not imply that an Occasion created friendship, recurrence, reachability,
  location access, or future-contact permission;
- the other person's private account stays absent; plural Outcomes remain
  plural;
- generic `Share` on a person dossier is unsafe. Share a named artifact,
  episode, or composition under an audience preview—not the person-view as a
  unit;
- a generic Map on a person dossier is also unsafe unless it represents an
  explicitly shared, correctly scoped episode.

The current board is a happy-path static record. It does not yet design the
multiplayer product. Add viewer-relative variants for active sharing,
contribution withdrawal, participant departure, block, audience narrowing,
later reconciliation under a new epoch, and a safe no-output state.

## 5. Dossier correction

The dossier's self-similar grammar is coherent, but “every organ shows a taste,
count, and door” risks optimizing for inventory completeness instead of the
person's job.

### Preserve

- one durable destination per held identity;
- conditional organs and honest counts;
- visible evidence, containment, provenance, and correction;
- job-specific doors into Places, people, Chat, and objects;
- a full record beneath a compact first reading.

### Correct

1. Add the optional `WHAT THIS OPENS` organ near the top.
2. Rename `WHAT IT LEFT` to distinguish:
   - durable outcomes and saved compositions;
   - carried-forward receipts;
   - currently available returns.
3. Do not force every object type into the same visible order. Share a common
   capability model but let the first scroll match the object's job.
4. Move high-value re-entry and accumulated understanding ahead of drawers,
   contact sheets, message counts, and ledgers when they exist.
5. Keep the full inventory reachable without making it the lead experience.
6. Use counts for retrieval and reconciliation, not as a substitute for value.
7. A dossier can be complete while remaining short.

Recommended first-scroll priorities:

| Dossier | First-scroll priority |
| --- | --- |
| Journey / Occasion | what happened → what Vesper reconstructed → what it opens now → record |
| Place relationship | held episodes → accumulated distinction → current Place handoff |
| Thread | evidence progression → new connection/capability → optional present return |
| Shared-with-person | shared episodes → attributed contributions → governance → optional relational opening |
| Artifact / object | the object → occurrence truth → where it lives → source and correction |

## 6. Object and medium correction

The pass family is excellent, but it is currently carrying too much of the
project's object proof. If generalized carelessly, Life will feel like a
travel-and-events wallet.

### 6.1 Passes remain one object family

Do not ticketify photographs, books, films, menus, notes, recordings, messages,
or saved Vesper compositions. Each should preserve the material identity that
makes it recognizable.

Create object-page studies for at least:

- a photograph;
- a menu or receipt;
- a book/film/ticket relationship where the film is the work and the ticket is
  evidence of attendance/purchase;
- a human-authored note or contribution;
- a saved Vesper article, comparison, map, or audio composition with manifest,
  Sources, version, audience, and invalidation behavior.

### 6.2 Do not let the archival aesthetic prohibit evidence-led return

The blanket `NO HERO PHOTOGRAPHY ANYWHERE IN LIFE` ruling needs a stress test.
The valid intent is to prevent Instagram-like staging and decorative imagery.
It must not prevent a photograph from leading when the photograph is the best
evidence or medium for re-entry.

Compose one Journey return using real personal photography as evidence—not as
a lifestyle hero—and compare it with the current contact-sheet/riso treatment.
If the evidence-led version is materially more faithful and useful, amend the
blanket ban into a narrower law:

> Photography may lead only when the photograph itself carries the return;
> generated or decorative imagery never impersonates lived evidence.

### 6.3 Use the full expression canon

Current Life boards are dominated by text rows, chips, counts, and riso
keepsakes. The product supports six core media: evidence, sequence,
comparison, spatial, concise prose, and interactive instrument.

Life does not need all six on one page. Each substantive return should choose
one lead medium and at most one supporting medium. Test:

- sequence for a reconstructed Journey;
- comparison for Rome/Paris or two coastal geographies;
- spatial for a personal Place relationship;
- evidence for a photo, ticket, menu, or note;
- concise prose for a genuinely new mechanism;
- an instrument only when current truth or direct manipulation matters.

## 7. Retrieval correction

The search composition is visually clear but semantically too literal.
`Search finds what can be named` conflicts with the human-memory benchmark.

Life search must support incomplete cues such as:

- “the ticket I never used”;
- “the bar after Teodora”;
- “the creamy pasta in Rome”;
- “Maya's note near Capri”;
- “the flight that got cancelled”;
- “what did Alex bring to dinner?”

Correct:

- return a governed target or answer first;
- attach at most three `Around this` landmarks when helpful;
- allow direct factual resolution when safe rather than forcing every result
  through another navigation step;
- keep containment visible;
- expose uncertainty, planned-versus-occurred truth, and attribution;
- hand reasoning or open-ended transformation to Chat with the resolved object
  already attached;
- show zero-result, ambiguity, permission-limited, deleted-source, and
  socially suppressed variants.

## 8. Social and multiplayer correction

The project contains good governance copy and a useful Vault ledger, but the
visible Life experience represents almost exclusively the happy path. That is
not sufficient for a product whose social differentiation depends on
viewer-relative shared continuity.

The design phase must visibly prove:

1. one authorized contribution enriches a later return;
2. the contributor remains attributable;
3. private Outcomes do not become group meaning;
4. withdrawal recompiles the composition without leaking a paraphrase;
5. leaving preserves only the correct historical core;
6. blocking suppresses unsafe relationship and social-periphery paths without
   erasing independent personal history;
7. unblocking does not restore old grants or proactive return;
8. reconciliation starts a new epoch;
9. relationship continuity can end cleanly;
10. the recipient gets value without owing a response.

Use the existing Brooklyn dinner and Rome/Paris fixture graph. Do not invent a
generic activity feed, relationship score, friend-location view, or group
profile.

## 9. Language and information-architecture corrections

### 9.1 Make the lens system discoverable

The four-pane facet is visually elegant but hides a first-order mental model.
Tap-to-cycle and long-press selection are insufficient for early use,
accessibility, and direct switching.

Show Time, Places, Threads, and People as labeled choices until the user has
learned the vocabulary. The facet may remain as a compact or scrolled state,
not the sole initial explanation.

### 9.2 Use user language for custody

The Product Model distinguishes **Artifact** as user language from **Source**
as an evidentiary role. `All sources` and `312 sources` currently expose
internal language as the primary utility.

Test:

- `Vault`;
- `All artifacts`;
- `Everything kept`;
- `Originals and sources` only inside provenance views;
- `Where this came from` inside a dossier.

Do not change terminology by taste alone; run a comprehension comparison.

### 9.3 Reduce poetic ambiguity

Generated reads must add orientation rather than atmosphere. Avoid phrases
that imply emotional state, relationship status, or identity, including
“still settling” and “mostly the two of you,” unless the person authored them.

Silence or factual orientation is preferable to a mandatory voice moment.

### 9.4 Preserve casual and ordinary registers

The visual system is warm and beautiful, but almost every object receives the
same archival seriousness. A Brooklyn dinner, a recurring night out, an
ordinary errand, a family visit, a book, and a major Journey should not all
feel like museum catalog entries.

Keep the kernel. Pressure-test lighter density, ordinary photography, human
authorship, and more casual copy without introducing social-media chrome.

## 10. Truth, control, and accessibility corrections

### 10.1 Separate object truth from editorial annotation

Object print such as `FLOWN`, `UNUSED`, or `CANCELLED` may express supported
occurrence state. Phrases such as `THE HEAT WON`, `ONE LONG DAY`, or `NINE
CAME` mix factual truth with authored or generated interpretation. Place the
latter in a separately attributed note or composition.

### 10.2 Clarify deletion

`Delete permanently` overpromises if online invalidation, backups, exports,
and external screenshots have different lifecycles. State what leaves product
paths immediately, what expires later, which derivatives recompile, and what
cannot be remotely revoked.

### 10.3 Resolve the oxblood conflict

The kernel reserves oxblood for a live threshold; the Vault uses it for
deletion. Amend the semantic palette explicitly or choose another destructive
treatment. Do not let one color mean both “act now in the world” and “destroy
data.”

### 10.4 Treat swipe as an optional accelerator

Swipe-to-Ask and swipe-to-steward are promising shortcuts, not foundational
navigation. They are hidden, compete with platform gestures, and require
accessible visible equivalents. Do not build them before the visible object,
return, correction, and cross-root handoff grammar works.

### 10.5 Stress accessibility and real content

The boards rely on small mono type, low-contrast metadata, subtle opacity,
tiny state dots, dense rows, and hidden gestures. Produce one accessibility
board covering:

- dynamic type and long localized names;
- VoiceOver order and object/door semantics;
- non-color state distinctions;
- 44pt touch targets;
- reduced motion for the facet;
- keyboard/search behavior where applicable;
- photo descriptions and nonvisual alternatives for diagrams and maps.

Create `00A · Canon and Open Arcs` containing:

## 14. Acceptance gates

The correction phase passes only if all are true:

1. A new viewer can explain Life without calling it a diary, photo recap,
   archive, wallet, contact tracker, or task list.
2. The stable record remains useful even when no return is eligible.
3. At least one Life object produces a substantive current opening without
   asking the person to do interpretive labor.
4. Home, Places, Chat, Plan, and Occasion retain their depth and action
   responsibilities after the handoff.
5. Threads make attention more useful without creating completion pressure.
6. People makes shared life richer without ranking intimacy or exposing the
   other person's private account.
7. An ordinary non-travel corpus feels native to the system.
8. A vague query retrieves the intended governed target with correct context.
9. A withdrawn or corrected Source removes unsupported derivatives and
   returns without erasing independent history.
10. Real photographs and non-pass artifacts retain their identity.
11. The page delivers value before any request to correct, share, continue,
   or act.
12. Every adaptive return has an inspectable reason and can be suppressed
   without harming stable access.
13. The design remains stable, calm, and non-feed-like after the Returns layer
   is added.
14. A coding agent can distinguish canonical, historical, tabled, and open
   boards without guessing.

## 15. Do not regress

The correction phase must not introduce:

- a memory feed or shuffled nostalgia shelf;
- a fifth root;
- a universal “Opening” database object;
- one product move per lens;
- a prompt or reflection tax;
- inferred personality, taste, intimacy, grief, or relationship health;
- friend activity, ambient location, reactions, or follower mechanics;
- a universal card stream;
- generic recommendations detached from evidence;
- recurrence-as-preference;
- an action merely to close a Thread;
- decorative generated imagery posing as evidence;
- silent audience expansion or group consensus;
- hidden-only correction, sharing, or return controls;
- more object variants before the complete experiences above work.

## 16. Compact direction to the design agent

Preserve the material system, pass family, one-corpus lens model, thin-record
grammar, provenance, search orientation, and Vault. Do not redesign from
scratch.

Correct the product around this sentence:

> **Life keeps the governed record and gives every eligible part of it a
> durable address. Vesper can use that address to make something newly
> intelligible, possible, workable, or worth returning to—without making the
> person maintain the archive or complete their life.**

Start with C0–C2. Do not extend Threads, People, swipe, or production component
architecture until those boards demonstrate the corrected relationship among
record, return, and cross-root consequence.

## 17. Return anatomy execution brief

Do not begin by drawing the `RETURNS` section. Design the **return itself**
first. Otherwise the project may add another attractive shelf whose rows still
terminate in archive pages.

### 17.1 Distinguish record, return, and Opening

- A **record** says what exists, what happened, what remains supported, and
  where it came from.
- A **return** uses part of that governed record to provide value again.
- An **Opening** is one possible output of a return: a grounded, optional
  possibility made available now.

Not every return is an Opening. A faithful reconstruction, new connection,
attributed juxtaposition, useful comparison, sensory re-entry, or factual
pattern may be complete on view and correctly stop there.

### 17.2 Semantic anatomy of a return

Every return contains six semantic parts. They need not render as six visible
modules.

1. **Foundation** — the governed Journey, Place, Thread, shared-with-person
   view, Occasion, Artifact, Source, Outcome, or saved Composition from Life.
2. **Material reason for returning** — new evidence, current location, a
   changed world condition, another person's authorized contribution, an
   unfinished inquiry becoming relevant, an explicit request, or another
   inspectable trigger. Engagement probability and calendar coincidence alone
   are insufficient.
3. **Vesper's contribution** — reconstruction, mechanism, comparison,
   juxtaposition, transfer, practical judgment, relational route, or another
   real delta beyond replaying the Source.
4. **Complete value** — the useful contribution is perceptible without a tap,
   reply, reflection, classification, or action.
5. **Optional consequence** — at most one natural continuation when earned:
   inspect, listen, view a map, open the relevant Place, ask a person, use a
   method, prepare an Occasion, or continue in Chat.
6. **Lineage and control** — why this appeared, supporting evidence, freshness,
   audience, correction, `Not now`, `Keep searchable`, and `Never surface
   this` as appropriate.

The compact equation is:

```text
durable foundation
  + material return reason
  + substantive new contribution
  = complete value now
  + optional continuation
  + inspectable lineage and control
```

### 17.3 Four return families to prove first

These are evaluation families, not permanent navigation categories or backend
objects.

#### A. Experiential return

**Job:** let the person meaningfully re-enter something.

Candidate fixture: the Nice-to-Rome transport and lived sequence composed from
tickets, changes, photographs, Places, conversations, and occurrence truth.
The contribution must be structure, juxtaposition, reconstruction, or sensory
return—not “you used four forms of transportation.” It may end on view.

Likely lead medium: sequence or evidence.

#### B. Epistemic return

**Job:** reveal a supported connection or mechanism the person had not already
made.

Candidate fixture: the pasta texture observed in southern Italy connected to
a specific starch, fat, heat, and finishing mechanism. The mechanism should be
understandable in the compact treatment. A deeper article, comparison, or
source inspection remains optional. No action is required to complete the
Thread.

Likely lead medium: comparison or concise explanation.

#### C. Capability or possibility return

**Job:** allow prior experience to improve something available now.

Candidate fixture: an Italy-derived distinction materially changes how Vesper
judges or explains a present New York possibility. The treatment states the
shared mechanism, the material difference, and the bounded current use. It
hands current Place truth to Places or preparation to Chat without reducing
the person to “likes pasta.”

Likely lead medium: comparison with a spatial or operational handoff.

#### D. Relational return

**Job:** make shared history or another person's authorized contribution
useful again.

Candidate fixture: the Rome record and a friend's simultaneous Paris record
form an attributed comparison. The two human lanes remain separate; no
consensus, intimacy claim, ambient status, or response obligation is created.
The composition may open one human-authored reason to reconnect, or end on
view.

Likely lead medium: attributed comparison or parallel sequence.

### 17.4 One return, three densities

The same semantic return should project at three densities without creating
three owners.

#### Compact — Life root

The bounded `RETURNS` region exposes:

- what is returning;
- the substance Vesper added;
- why it is relevant now; and
- at most one door.

Do not use a generic label as the entire value. For example:

```text
Weak
Pasta, pursued · newly useful

Stronger
Why the pasta stayed creamier without more cream
The dishes you kept point to the same finishing mechanism.
Compared across Sorrento and Rome →
```

The stronger specimen is still only a placeholder until evidence and novelty
are verified. Its important property is that value begins before the tap.

#### Standard — dossier return organ

`WHAT THIS OPENS` contains:

- the relevant evidence;
- the new connection, reconstruction, or transferable mechanism;
- material differences and limits;
- why it is useful now; and
- one optional continuation.

The dossier remains the stable home after this return expires.

#### Destination — consequence-owning surface

If the return matters specifically now or here:

- Home owns why it merits present attention;
- Places owns current spatial truth and alternatives;
- Chat owns interrogation, shaping, authorship, and operation;
- Plan or Occasion owns commitment and shared consequence.

The destination receives a projection envelope. Life preserves the foundation,
lineage, and later causal receipt rather than duplicating the destination's
full experience.

### 17.5 Return lifecycle

Design the state model as well as the static rendering:

```text
dormant foundation
  -> material trigger appears
  -> evidence, novelty, authority, and current truth rechecked
  -> return composed
  -> admitted or rejected
  -> compact projection surfaces or remains available on demand
  -> viewed, ignored, suppressed, corrected, or continued
  -> optional consequence occurs under its durable owner
  -> causal receipt returns to Life when something materially changed
  -> return expires, evolves, invalidates, or becomes durable only through an
     authorized saved Composition, Outcome, correction, or receipt
```

Lifecycle laws:

- ignoring is not failure and creates no unfinished task;
- opening does not automatically save a new Composition;
- acting creates a receipt only when something actually changes;
- every destination rechecks current authority and truth;
- removing upstream evidence invalidates or recompiles dependent returns;
- a return may become ineligible while its historical foundation remains;
- successful experiential return may end on view with no residue;
- repeated viewing or dwell never turns a return into identity or preference.

### 17.6 Return Anatomy Lab

Before revising the full Life root, create one board family named
`Return Anatomy Lab` containing:

1. the four complete return families above;
2. compact, standard, and destination projections of the same identity;
3. one candidate rejected for insufficient evidence or no novelty;
4. one experiential return that correctly ends on view;
5. one changed-world return with its reason inline;
6. one social return that recompiles or disappears after contribution
   withdrawal;
7. one valuable return with no action;
8. one return whose consequence produces a `Carried forward` receipt;
9. one expired return whose durable record remains intact; and
10. one correct silence state.

The lab should use real fixture evidence and current authority state. Do not
fill it with abstract wireframes, generic labels, or more component variants.

### 17.7 Evaluation questions

For every specimen ask:

1. Is the new value perceptible within five seconds?
2. Did Vesper add something the person did not already provide or explicitly
   state?
3. Does the treatment provide value before requesting effort?
4. Is the reason for returning real, material, and inspectable?
5. Is this experiential return, Make sense, an Opening, Help it work, or a
   combination with one dominant job?
6. Does the projection belong at this density in Life, Home, Places, Chat,
   Plan, or Occasion?
7. Can the return disappear without damaging the underlying record?
8. Can the person inspect, correct, suppress, or release it without learning
   an ontology lesson?
9. Would Google Photos, Maps, a calendar, or a stateless chat already provide
   equal value?
10. Would silence be better?

Only after several specimens pass should the design compose the root
`RETURNS` region and dossier `WHAT THIS OPENS` organ around proven material.
This ordering prevents the return anatomy from becoming another archive shelf
or another feed-shaped promise.

## 18. Post-regeneration review — 2026-08-31

This section reviews the regenerated `vesper-life-anchors` export containing
boards `00A`, `04B`, `13`–`15`, and `C1`–`C8`. It does not supersede the
earlier diagnosis. It records which corrections now work, which remain
provisional, and what must happen before the correction phase can be called
complete.

### 18.1 Overall ruling

The regenerated project is a substantial conceptual advance. It now expresses
the correct underlying relationship:

> **Life preserves the governed record. Vesper recompiles that record into new
> value. Home, Places, Chat, Plan, and Occasion own the relevant present-tense
> or operational consequences.**

`04B The Loop Corrected` should replace the old loop as the authoritative
product model. The project also now understands that:

- a Return must deliver value before requesting effort;
- a Return may end on view without producing residue;
- silence, expiration, withdrawal, and invalidation are legitimate states;
- not every recurring detail becomes a Thread;
- shared material must retain attribution, audience, and withdrawal effects;
- objects should preserve their native medium rather than becoming passes; and
- causal repair must propagate through every dependent projection.

The correction phase is therefore **architecture-resolved in several areas,
but not consumer-complete**. `00A` must not say `ALL COMPLETE` or `ALL RULINGS
CLOSED` yet.

### 18.2 What is accepted

The following advances should be retained:

1. **`04B The Loop Corrected`** — correct system and surface ownership.
2. **`13 Return Anatomy Lab`** — correct semantic anatomy and evaluation
   structure.
3. **`13D Relational — Rome and Paris`** — strongest current proof of an
   attributed, no-debt, socially compounded Return.
4. **`13E The Honest States`** — rejection, expiration, and correct silence.
5. **`C4 Thread Admission`** — pasta remains nested; Aeneas demonstrates a
   qualifying line only when recurrence, evidence, return value, and user-safe
   repair all hold.
6. **`C6 Human Refinding`** — incomplete cues, occurrence correction,
   uncertainty, direct answers where safe, and bounded context trails.
7. **`C7 Non-Pass Objects`** — native object treatments and generated
   Composition lineage.
8. **`C8 Causal Repair`** — upstream release visibly recompiles dependents
   while independent evidence survives.
9. **The revised People root** — shared records instead of relationship
   rankings or inferred intimacy.
10. **`Everything kept`** — preferred user-facing language for the custody
    surface; `Vault` may remain internal shorthand only.

These are not invitations to proliferate more variants. They are the stable
parts around which the remaining consumer compositions should be corrected.

### 18.3 Return specimen rulings

#### A. Experiential — promising, not yet proven

`13A The Way South` is honest and useful, but its present contribution is
primarily record repair:

> All 47 photographs found their days.

That does not yet prove meaningful experiential re-entry. Approximate temporal
organization is already available in photo libraries. Vesper must demonstrate
the additional value created by combining occurrence truth, transport changes,
photographs, conversations, gaps, and lived sequence.

Before this specimen passes:

- render it with real fixture photographs rather than an illustration or
  placeholder;
- test portrait, landscape, low-light, visually noisy, and face-containing
  evidence;
- show how a meaningful photograph can outweigh several logistical objects;
- preserve the honest missing day without turning the gap into a gimmick; and
- make the re-entry perceptible in the composition, not merely promised by the
  sentence “plays as one line.”

This specimen may remain complete on view. Its failure is not lack of an
action; it is insufficient demonstrated value beyond organization.

#### B. Epistemic — strong form, excessive factual confidence

`13B The Pasta Mechanism` demonstrates the right compact-to-standard anatomy,
but the sentence “No cream involved” exceeds what photographs, menus, and a
saved method ordinarily establish about three restaurant kitchens.

The composition must distinguish:

- what the person directly noticed;
- what a cited source explains;
- what Vesper infers is consistent with the evidence; and
- what remains unknown.

Use language such as “the texture you noticed is consistent with…” unless a
source actually establishes the kitchens' methods. Epistemic value comes from
making the mechanism understandable, not from sounding certain.

#### C. Capability — does not pass

`13C The Stacked Shore` should be replaced or substantially reconceived. One
Sorrento observation has been promoted too quickly into an “instinct,” a
capability, a Red Hook sequence, and an outcome that supposedly “held.” This
violates the transfer ladder's evidence threshold and risks covert identity
inference.

The board also admits that Red Hook is flat and materially unlike Sorrento
while still prescribing the Sorrento arrival order. Writing a negative-transfer
guard beside the recommendation does not repair a recommendation whose stated
difference undermines its mechanism.

A passing capability specimen must:

1. identify a supported distinction learned from prior evidence;
2. show why that distinction is relevant to a present decision;
3. state the material similarities and differences;
4. change the current judgment in a bounded, reversible way; and
5. avoid naming a stable preference, instinct, or capability before repeated
   cross-context evidence warrants it.

Until then, call the transfer a hypothesis worth testing, not a capability.

#### D. Relational — closest to passing

`13D Rome and Paris` is the strongest specimen because it creates a comparison
the person did not explicitly provide, keeps the two human lanes separate,
attributes the friend's material, requires no response, and disappears cleanly
after withdrawal.

Its remaining correction is evidential precision. “Two answers” and “inverted
solutions” are interpretations. The standard view should expose the routes,
notes, photographs, timings, or other evidence that support “shade between
monuments” and “days around water.” Prefer an observable juxtaposition over a
psychological characterization.

### 18.4 Surface-ownership correction

The revised Time root currently places two unlike things inside one `RETURNS`
region:

- `The way south` is record-native re-entry and may belong in Life.
- `Red Hook, the Sorrento way` is current, spatial, and Saturday-specific; it
  belongs primarily in Places or Home.

Adopt this narrower law:

> **Life may deliver record-native understanding, reconstruction, and
> experiential re-entry. Present-tense possibilities belong primarily to Home
> or Places. Life preserves their foundation, lineage, and any later causal
> receipt.**

Life may contain a bounded projection or door when it helps explain what the
record now enables. It must not become a second present-tense recommendation
feed. The full product proof must show the same Return identity across:

1. its durable foundation in Life;
2. its present delivery in Home or Places when applicable;
3. an optional Chat, Plan, or Occasion continuation; and
4. the `Carried forward` receipt after something materially changes.

### 18.5 Canonical contradictions to resolve

`00A Canon and Open Arcs` currently overstates completion and contains mutually
incompatible statuses. Correct it before any implementation handoff:

- Dossier `WHAT THIS OPENS` is marked pending while `C2` is marked done.
- Search incomplete-cue correction is marked pending while `C6` is marked
  done.
- Lens discoverability is both open and described as shipped.
- Photography testing is listed as undesigned although board `14` exists and
  records a ruling.
- Roots are variously described as v8, v9, and v10.
- `ALL RULINGS CLOSED` conflicts with the open and provisional items on the
  same board.

The canonical screens also disagree about pasta:

- `C4` says the pasta material does not qualify as a Thread.
- `11A Search — Pasta` calls `Pasta, pursued` an `Open thread`.
- `06A Journey — Nice to Rome` says the question is still open.
- `06C The Pasta Question` says it is answered as far as it asked to be.

Resolve this as one governed state and update every canonical projection. Old
exploration boards may retain the superseded concept only when unmistakably
marked historical or rejected.

### 18.6 Architecture proof is not consumer completion

Several C-series boards successfully define requirements but do not yet prove
the corresponding consumer experience:

- `C2` is a useful object/opening matrix, not four complete dossiers.
- `C5` is a governance-state catalogue, not the full Together experience.
- `C6` is a strong search test, but canonical Search still contradicts it.
- `C7` resolves ontology, but its object studies remain schematic.
- `C8` resolves causal semantics, but not the complete consumer repair flow.

Use the following status vocabulary in `00A`:

- **Architecture resolved** — semantics, owner, authority, and state transitions
  are settled.
- **Consumer composition proven** — the complete 1:1 experience works with
  realistic evidence and surrounding page density.
- **Canonical integration complete** — every authoritative root, dossier,
  search result, and projection agrees.
- **Build-ready** — state, loading, absence, accessibility, and interaction
  behavior are specified sufficiently for implementation.

Do not collapse these into `DONE`.

### 18.7 Two rulings to narrow

#### Photography

Board `14` currently says a photograph may lead a Return “and only a Return.”
That is unnecessarily tied to a surface category. A photograph could
legitimately lead a dossier organ, Occasion reconstruction, or shared episode
when it performs the primary semantic job.

Replace it with:

> **A photograph may lead where it is the strongest evidence for the
> composition's job. It must remain real, attributed, permission-safe,
> occurrence-grounded, and visibly distinct from generated or decorative
> imagery.**

Typographic roots and mastheads may remain the visual default without turning
that default into an ontological prohibition.

#### Return reasons

`REASON · NEW EVIDENCE` and `REASON · THE WORLD CHANGED` expose internal
categories, not inspectable reasons. Prefer the actual material fact:

- “Nine newly imported photographs completed the sequence.”
- “Weekend ferry service resumed yesterday.”
- “Maya addressed this Paris record to you.”

The category may remain in metadata. The person-facing treatment should explain
why this specific thing is here now.
