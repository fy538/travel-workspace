## 2. One product, not five feature areas

| Part of the product | Primary orientation | What must remain connected |

The four moves remain a repertoire across these surfaces. “Make sense” is not
confined to Chat, “Open possibility” to Places, “Help it work” to a Plan, or “Carry

duplicating the underlying owner or requiring identical layout.

The strongest product interpretation is therefore not a super-app of operations.
It is one intelligence helping attention become worthwhile understanding,
possibility, practical assistance or shared experience, with continuity when
useful. Booking execution, a heavyweight expense product, a social performance
feed and compulsory life documentation are not needed to complete that identity.

## 4. Project findings

### 4.1 Home

Home's best work is the variety of things worth receiving: an original photograph,
a practical update, a usable method, a substantive comparison and an optional
afternoon. Preserve this breadth and the difference between urgency and optional
depth. The detailed 38-kind audit remains in the
[Home review](vesper-home-design-review-2026-09-08.md#component-by-component-product-fit-audit).

The next revision should import existing capabilities from Social, Plans and Life,
not add a section for each missing relationship. In particular:

- Use Social's original-photo arrival and ordinary shared-care states.
- Use Plans' contextual assistance and scoped result instead of another Home-only
  adjustment or coordination mechanism.
- Use Life's exact-result and cross-day patterns without repeating its archive.
- Generalize the trip strip/work receipt when ordinary current-life help warrants it.
- Replace the capability-field specimen's product instruction with its actual
  useful consequence; do not reserve an italic voice-horizon slot to prove learning.

Social's copied Home views also need reconciliation. Dana's 03.3 still places one
request under an Addressed-to-you heading, despite Social 07 preserving the
two-contribution grouping rule. Reuse the single-item treatment; do not silently
create a new Home rule through a copied frame.

### 4.2 Places

Places' substantive field, access comparisons and physical sections are valuable.
Its main weakness is the default accumulation of individually sensible components:
verdict, basis, register, notes, trace, route, burden, evidence, consequence, posture
and fallback. This makes the same encounter feel operational. Selection by purpose
should replace the expectation that every place page renders every component.
The full catalog assessment is in the
[Places review](vesper-places-design-review-2026-09-08.md#component-by-component-product-fit-audit).

Three existing donors should influence its revision:

1. Social's Elsewhere and original-first receiving, including contextual human
   Reply versus private Ask.
2. Plans' value-first place inspection and existing owner-backed way to compare,
   shape or change an arrangement.
3. Life's “place as part of my history” and contextual map exit, without turning
   Places into another archive or banning useful history links.

The exclusion of old candidate/day-edit screens must not exclude the person's
intent to fit a place into a day. Likewise, unexpected discovery and meaningful
relational relevance are not exclusively Home's responsibility.

### 4.3 Social

Social is the best existing reference for *connected ordinary multiplayer
experience*. Its three situations are independent benefits, not a funnel:
receive a little of a friend's world; get together more easily; let something
useful remain afterward. Original receiving works without an AI derivative,
invitation or future contact.

Preserve these specific gains:

- 02.5/02.6 keep human Reply separate from private Ask and preserve the source.
- 03.1 turns a host's sentence into recipient-specific previews and one Send.
- 03.3 gives a nonattending contributor a legitimate narrow way to participate.
- 03.5 explains the human tradeoff between seven and eight, not just the time edit.
- 03.7 gives a guest concrete arrival help and then lets the evening happen offline.
- 04.8 starts another occasion with useful prior material, fresh invitations and
  no inherited old availability.

The highest-priority defect is inside the adopted-eight branch. 03.6 says Sam may
come at eight or at his previously planned seven-thirty, with no acceptance
assumed if he does not answer. The 03.7 variant then says he is expected around
eight while its annotation still says his answer is pending. A canonical dinner
start and an individual's accepted arrival must remain distinct.

The connected map also overstates its completeness. Social 06 says the adopted
change reaches only Sam, while its current branch table and 03.6 say all affected
people receive tailored updates. Maya's route points at a Nora-only view and at
Nora's post-withdrawal result rather than Maya's withdrawal action. These are
route-map defects to fix, not evidence that the product intends to expose private
context.

The accountless path is well conceived but incomplete. The verified guest later
receives the photograph; optional connection is shown from both sides. What Sam
uses after acceptance, where he returns next, and how he changes future contact
remain underdrawn. Private guest Ask exists in the Plans reference but is not
clearly retained in Social's selected guest route. Complete that bounded experience
under a chosen guest agreement; do not imply that the prototype has settled identity,
delivery, Friends audiences or source-use rights.[^social]

### 4.4 Life

Life is the strongest continuity donor. Four lenses organize one corpus without
requiring filing. Original photos, notes, documents, passes and deliberately saved
generated results have appropriate distinctions. Search targets the thing remembered
rather than forcing a traversal through every container. Ordinary life includes
pancakes, a book in progress, dumplings, a call and running shoes—not only a trip.

03b's “two doors, one page” and direct-or-explore retrieval should anchor shared
object identity and Back behavior. P2's late-material and split/old-link examples
are valuable design work; P3 distinguishes a moving record from a saved version;
P1 supplies the prospective AHEAD proposal; P4 supplies record-relative maps.
Those proposals are not adopted merely because they complete the story.

The important missing connection is from a live arrangement to a remembered
experience and back. Fixture v0.1 L02 already requires one stable Occasion identity
across prospective/live/recent/historical readings. The selected screens do not
fully demonstrate access to the original Plan and the distinction between what
was arranged, what is evidenced to have occurred and each person's account.
“Remembered” must not transfer the Plan's authority into a new Life object.

Life 06 already models an answer on August 17, added evidence/readings on August
24–25 and later value. Its concluding assertion that the Rome plates were finished
the same way is stronger than its own earlier “what each kitchen actually did is
unknown.” Preserve the explanation and actual evidence increment; do not manufacture
certainty to demonstrate compounding. Fixture v0.2 C05 supplies a better donor:
a supported mechanism, a possible one-variable cooking comparison and a rule
against replaying the same explanation as new.

“Everything kept · all of it, yours” also needs care because the collection
contains revocable human originals. A dependable path back does not imply ownership
of someone else's material. This can be corrected through ordinary copy and consistent
reader behavior, not another custody-management surface.[^life]

### 4.5 Plans in real life

Plans provides the strongest destination for practical consequence. A loose
Saturday, an unordered collection, a detailed trip and a shared evening use a

opens first to be understood. Questions, corrections and adaptations use the

Its provider-return and failure examples are particularly useful donors: a changed
arrangement is not a changed reservation; returning from a provider does not prove
booking; an unknown outcome does not mean nothing happened; scoped Undo preserves

- **Whole-option judgment:** 08 P10 offers a Nara hotel for Monday without reconciling

- **Initiative:** the accepted seven-sentences reduction deliberately constrains
  recommendation and removes buttons. Useful recovery I1–I1c remains proposed,
  while Social shows an action-bearing response. This calls for one targeted
  decision, not an assumption that the newer general vision automatically
  supersedes the specific accepted restriction.

The old appendices must not be promoted wholesale. Some contain stronger coverage
than the selected route, but also stale consensus copy, an optimistic beach-time
calculation without the return journey, and later reuse of historical participant
timing. Use those as comparison material. The selected F1 treatment—an attributed
account of going to the river with the original plan still available—is the better

## 5. Genuine gaps and consequential seams

### A. Ordinary non-spatial sharing outside an Occasion

The product model permits artifacts beyond places and trips. The shared fixture
ledger even proposes A5: Maya's short recipe object shared directly with connected
friends and opened as a non-spatial object. The selected Social receiving sequence
instead uses a place share, a spatial map and an occasion-bound cooking contribution.
Social 05 D1 does contain a useful non-spatial donor: Nora's pasta photograph and
line to friends, with no guest count, beside the recipient's original and Reply.
It is a leading alternative under comparison, not a selected cross-root receiving
and refinding route. D6 also names recipes and lines as eligible exchange.

The remaining gap is the selected end-to-end route and discoverable destination,
not absence of a non-spatial sharing design, missing product intent or noncompliance
with the later narrowed design-round closure. Reuse D1 and the existing original-material
and object-reader families for a recipe, excerpt or small collection. Do not force
a location, create a social tab, or assume that every friend contribution belongs
on Home. The contextual destination and the pull route for non-spatial shared
material need an explicit composition answer.[^fixture]

### B. One contextual assistant across surface boundaries

Plans already designs the most complete resting/active/resolved pattern. Social
has a strong Reply/private-Ask separation. Home/Places have different entry and
prominence rules. Life mostly shows Chat/Home context strips rather than complete
transitions.

Consolidate target identity, current purpose, the relevant exchange, the distinction
between a private question and a human message, and the return to the exact result.
A person should not have to discover different assistance grammars in each root.
The standalone Chat design was not supplied for this pass, so full Chat history,
voice and resolved-exchange behavior remain unassessed here. Record that dependency;
do not redesign Chat indirectly from these five projects.

### C. Practical help that completes the supported job

The live engine is not just an alarm renderer or a source of timestamps. It joins
meaning, timing, reachability, current commitments and the people involved into
an option that makes sense. This contribution begins before any ongoing watch is
accepted and does not require provider execution.

Use Places' performance/dinner and Plans' Nara overnight as complementary cases.
Show the important unresolved dependency and a useful continuation without
unnecessary restructuring of existing commitments. A nearby café open late is
not automatically a dinner fallback; available lodging is not automatically
compatible with the rest of the trip.

The same principle applies when a person wants to choose or make something
themselves. Reduce unwanted logistical work without replacing wanted participation.
Use existing “make it less rushed” and “keep the dinner” interactions to show
different desired assistance, rather than inventing lifestyle profiles.

### E. Future, ongoing and remembered Life

P1 is a strong starting point for arranged things and softly held intentions;
the ordinary *Middlemarch* entry shows an ongoing activity. The complete return
to such an ongoing thing is less clear. Show one arrangement, one ongoing interest
and one time-bound intention whose cue passed, without making them a work queue.

The retention, expiry, future eligibility and original-owner distinctions need
to be resolved at their current contracts. Keeping an option is not scheduling
it, saving a source is not saving an adaptation, and a date passing is not evidence
of an occurrence. No new Plan archive or Life planning root is needed.

### F. Learning that changes later help

Life 06 and Social 04.8 demonstrate more than passive recall. The remaining
question is whether the prior experience changes the next help beyond restoring
the same place, photograph or start time.

A useful next specimen can reuse Nora's own supported cooking account to change
preparation for the next evening, or preserve a prior correction in a later
answer. Contrast a current situation where the old circumstance should not be
used. Keep allowed-use boundaries explicit in the fixture; do not turn every
Ask into durable history. No reflection ritual or mandatory “How did it go?”
should be introduced to manufacture an outcome.

### G. The lived encounter, not just the decision before it

Social's hospitality and offline evening are good. Plans' Tōdai-ji inspection
and appendix J1c/J1d already offer recognition, shade and what to skip. Places'
cliff section makes physical access legible. These are substantial ingredients,
not an absent on-site capability.

Connect them into one place encounter: expressed current need, useful approach,
something newly noticeable, and an appropriate end. Optional depth should remain
available, but the long browsing feed should not be mandatory when the person
is already navigating a hot, crowded or confusing place. This is not a request
for a new automatic tour or continuous narration service.

### H. The same object through different readings

Life's historical Bushwick and Places' current Bushwick can legitimately be
different pages. Likewise, Home's compact guide and Life's retained guide may
have different density. Consolidation means stable subject, version, ownership
and return context—not one universal page layout.

Complete the bidirectional route among the relevant record, current world facts,
arrangement and exact source/result. A saved guide must not reopen only its
bakery. A remembered dinner must not lose the original arrangement. A friend’s
photo must not turn into an unowned generated summary.

## 7. Component consolidation

Use a shared crosswalk separating **semantic role, visual primitive, composition
policy, canonical owner and action consequence**. Home's 38 names, Places' kinds,
Life's record organs and Plans' row variants are not additive product modules.
Some are different projections of the same underlying responsibility.

| Reusable family | Existing donors | Preserve the distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Object identity, pointer and reader | Life 03b/04/05; Home 09; Plans 06 | Compact pointer versus actual source, saved result, record or arrangement. |
| Attributed original and plural evidence | Social 02/04; Life 07; Home 08b; Places 07 | Authorship, viewer scope and source identity; original material versus AI synthesis. |
| Contextual assistant and human reply | Plans 08; Social 02.5/02.6; Life context exits | Private Ask versus human message; retained exchange versus transient help. |

| Outgoing preview and result | Social invitation/contribution; Plans D3/I1b; Home receipts | Send proposal versus update-and-send, and actual result versus unknown/pending. |
| Temporal and spatial instruments | Places sections/maps; Plans sequence; Home live pass; Life P4 | Explanatory geometry versus live operational state; planned versus evidenced movement. |
| Record, exact saved version and repair | Life P2/P3/07; Home saved guide; Social withdrawal | Changing record versus deliberately saved version; dependent versus independent material. |

These are reuse groups, not a proposal for seven universal React components or
a new schema. Shared rendering must not erase authority or turn the backend into
an arbitrary screen-tree generator. Root-specific information ordering is legitimate.

Resolve small visual inconsistencies after behavior: chip size, time typography,

Content supply also needs one cross-project account. Human-original units do not

private synthesis should earn its incremental cost. “Rendering only,” a fixed
number of modeled turns or a BUILD badge is not measured production economics.

## 8. Consolidation sequence and orchestration

### First: establish the selected-design map

Keep the five exports intact for provenance. For each selected behavior, name
one primary design source, its dependencies, its current/proposed/historical
status and the exact receiving result. Update existing project indexes/handbacks
with accepted deltas instead of maintaining multiple supposedly final copies.

Recommended design leads:

- Home and Places retain their root composition ownership.
- Plans leads the readable arrangement and contextual practical-help destination.
- Life leads record/original/result retrieval and stable continuity.
- Social leads multi-person receiving/participation examples across those surfaces.
- The orchestrator resolves cross-project contradictions and consequential choices;
  it does not approve every ordinary component change.

### Second: resolve conflicting behavior before visual convergence

Resolve the Sam pending-arrival branch, proposed versus applied shared changes,
the block-versus-attendance mistake, the narrow initiative decision, and the
record/Plan/source/result relationships. In parallel, design the missing service
and entry conditions in section 9: these are not settled by repairing a fixture
or agreeing on shared components.
Reconcile the fixture-world variants used for each chosen walkthrough. Do not
resolve all this by choosing the newest file or copying a reference board's rules.

### Third: assemble connected encounters from existing work

Use a small portfolio spanning the system—not one loop as the architecture and
not a new scenario for every component:

| Connected encounter | Reuse | What still needs to become explicit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One ordinary question or capture → useful answer → optional later use | Home sparse material; Life 06/v0.2 C05; Plans private Ask | Useful answer first; optional retention; changed later help; no forced callback. |
| A friend shares a non-spatial thing → receive → optional reply/refind | Fixture A5; Social 05 D1 alternative and 02; Life 04/05 | The non-spatial destination and pull route; no Occasion or geographic marker required. |
| A worthwhile local possibility → fit into an existing day → owner return | Home/Places field; Plans A4/B5/08 | Current constraints checked together; existing commitments respected; exact result preserved. |
| Shared evening → change → each person's arrival → offline | Social 03; Plans 04/05/11 | One shared revision with distinct participation and provider facts; no host courier work. |
| After the evening → original photo/record → another occasion | Social 04; Life 07/P2/P3; Plans F1 | Stable owner linkage, plural supported accounts, current participation, useful selective continuity. |
| Already at a place → brief practical/perceptual help → optional depth | Places cliff/access; Plans Tōdai-ji; Social arrival | A complete on-site receiving experience that does not require a long feed. |

The second pass adds three necessary pressures to this portfolio: bounded world
supply across repeated ordinary opens; bringing an arrangement made elsewhere;
and the lifecycle of explicitly accepted continuing help. Apply changed-purpose,
ambiguous-retrieval and disconnected-return variants to existing encounters
rather than commissioning a separate product or full project for every variant.

Each encounter needs a real initial benefit and a legitimate ending without
further action. Some can finish entirely on receipt. This is a design integration
exercise, not a claim of consumer validation or a prerequisite to designing the
system architecture broadly.

### Fourth: return bounded changes to the design projects

Each owner should receive one outcome-sized assignment: the source pattern to
reuse, the exact conflict to resolve, the adjacent owner's contract, and the
finished receiving state. Owners can coordinate shared components and fixture
changes directly. Escalate here for changed product promise, conflicting authority
or a new root-wide rule—not every ordinary layout adjustment.

Checkpoints should be the first coherent connected revision, a consequential
decision, and the final selected handback. Avoid cycles where a lane only documents
the same gap and waits for another micro-instruction.

### Fifth: distinguish design closure from engineering readiness

After the selected behaviors agree, update the appropriate owner contracts or
accepted decision records through their normal process. Only then translate the
agreed deltas into an engineering assignment with current-code evidence. The
existing code is not presumed absent or complete based on these exports.

This review does not restart the paused Integration lane or dispatch an
implementation round. No physical export merge, archive removal, schema migration,
new guest service or policy activation is authorized by the consolidation itself.

## 9. Adversarial second pass: omissions, corrections and priorities

The initial review correctly recognized substantial reusable designs, but its
connection-first diagnosis was incomplete. The following findings distinguish
missing design evidence from source-supported contradictions. A missing selected
experience is not proof that the capability is absent from code or every earlier
design project. Proposed boards remain proposed; no recommendation here amends
accepted policy.

### 9.1 Coverage is not the same as a complete receiving experience

| Product pressure | Existing evidence | What the five-project portfolio still does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful with little personal context | Home 04; Places first-open field; Social 05 D3 | Useful repeated receiving with realistically limited **world supply**, not simply the rich field with personal material removed. |
| Life without documentation homework | Life 02's ordinary week; Life 06's deliberate photo; Home 04's ticket | How an ordinary useful interaction forms a legitimate record without a second journaling job; a week with no retained material is also valid. |
| Help with life already underway | Plans 03's populated Trip; Home ticket; Plans 90 D6's provider confirmation | Bringing a largely completed external arrangement and receiving help without rebuilding it inside Vesper. |
| Dependable continuing help | Home checked boat/claim; Home 12's retries; Plans 91 W2 proposal | What continuing work was accepted, through when, what silence means, how coverage ends, and what the person receives after failure or return. |
| Adaptation to wanted help | Plans lighter day and parent-aware help; Places 09's stages | Same person and evidence, different current purpose or wanted participation, producing a materially different kind of assistance. |
| Casual and asynchronous social value | Social 02; 05 D1 alternative; 03 remote contribution | Selected non-spatial receive/open/refind; an occasional single contributor; a busy collection; worthwhile remote-participant receiving. |
| Effortless retrieval | Life 03b/05; Social 04.5 | Similar candidates, imperfect cues and incomplete evidence without turning a miss into a claim that the event never happened. |
| Shared continuity | Social photo delivery; Life records; Plans F1 | A guest-specific later record, current arrangement versus captured day, and truthful return without a connection. |

These are pressures on the existing system, not eight new feature areas. The
relevant authorities are Product Thesis, Product Model §§4.1–8, accepted consumer
strategy §§1–5, and the contribution contract.[^vision][^second-service]

### 9.2 Independent receiving with bounded world supply

**Finding:** sparse personal context is much better designed than sparse usable
world supply. Home 04 can offer value with only New York chosen, but its cold
page depends on two weather stations, a forecast, a local microclimate explanation,
transit disruption and event deadlines. Places 03's sparse page still contains
the film, market, two-pier comparison and a detailed flood mechanism. Its own
open-items note says a genuinely thin city needs a separate fixture. Social's
empty friends scope returns to the same world supply.[^second-receiving]

This does not prove the product is uneconomic. Home 07 already distinguishes
reusable material, live facts and new production, and rejects writing every slot
on open. The unresolved design question is what remains worthwhile when the best
material was already seen, expired, is irrelevant to the current purpose, or
cannot be supported here. No-history, no-friends, no chosen city and unsupported
geography are different conditions.

**Recommended design:** use one supported locality with an explicitly bounded
supply set across three ordinary opens with no new contribution. Change one
source or remove one offering. A useful current answer, another relevant item,
an original, a legitimate refind or a complete quiet ending may win. Do not
manufacture novelty, require contributions, or turn this into an endless-feed
test. Separately make the unsupported-coverage boundary understandable without
promising the same field everywhere.

### 9.3 Ordinary Life needs a believable formation path

**Finding:** Life 02 provides excellent ordinary subjects: a partly read book,
dumplings, a call, a bakery visit and retired running shoes. But its statement
that the record grows through living rather than feeding the app is not established
by showing the finished record. The call duration, book progress and shoe mileage
need actual authorized evidence. Life 06's supplied photograph and Home's ticket
are useful formation donors; neither establishes effortless knowledge of the
whole ordinary week.[^second-life]

**Recommended design:** show one mundane interaction that was worthwhile for its
immediate job, the governed residue it leaves, and a later Life view. Show a
parallel ordinary week in which nothing was retained, without treating the person
as deficient. Do not solve this with a capture campaign, automatic Ask retention,
ambient surveillance, or unsupported statements about what happened. The design
should explain the benefit causally, not ask people to understand storage rules.

This is separate from the cross-day learning question. A record can form properly
without proving that later personal judgment improved; an answer can improve
without creating a Life record.

### 9.4 Continuing assistance is a service experience, not a live-looking row

**Finding:** Home 08c displays the boat checked at 6:12 and a claim whose change
will be reported once. Home 12 explicitly describes rechecking a failed provider
feed every few minutes. Plans 07 excludes a monitoring promise, while 91 W2
explores a new scoped standing instruction. The accepted strategy already
distinguishes immediate help, retention, bounded following and an authorized
change; the selected designs do not show the full continuing-service agreement
and ending.[^second-service]

The boat's timestamp alone need not imply a watch: a current read can legitimately
check a fact. The stronger unresolved promise is *future work* on which the person
may rely. A row saying nothing needs them is meaningful only if they can understand
what Vesper has and has not taken responsibility for.

**Recommended design:** one ordinary request to follow a supported condition,
a compact accepted scope and ending, a useful result or meaningful silence,
loss of coverage, and stop/expiry. On return, the person can tell whether help is
still active without reconstructing prior conversation. Preserve the immediate
help-only alternative. No watch dashboard, general scheduler, billing screen,
external booking execution or newly activated mandate follows.

Commercial design affects this boundary even before a price is chosen: basic
recipient access must remain complete, shared truth must agree across tiers,
and costly or continuing work cannot exceed an undisclosed allowance or period.
This requires a legible service limit, not a token meter on ordinary questions or
a paywall dividing the four product moves.[^second-economics]

### 9.5 Enter with an external arrangement; adapt the kind of assistance

**Finding:** Plans shows an already populated Kyoto trip and an invitation made
inside Vesper. Its provider-confirmation import is a useful later handoff, not a
complete first encounter with an itinerary or gathering arranged elsewhere.
Directed import is already part of the product strategy; the receiving experience
must preserve it as evidence without making Vesper the original booking or
planning authority.[^second-service]

**Recommended design:** bring an existing itinerary, invitation or calendar item
and ask for help. Show what Vesper understands, what remains external, and the
immediate benefit without compulsory reconstruction. Add a competing commitment
owned by another arrangement. Plans 06 E1/E2 already solves partial overlap and
different personal travel horizons; the narrower unresolved case is one person's
incompatible obligations, not overlap in general.

Then compare the same current facts under two requests: **help me choose**, and
**I have chosen; help with the practical checks**. A third brief interpretation
can be useful, but is not a quota. The parent-aware Tōdai-ji response and lighter
Monday are strong donors. Current purpose, available energy, access and wanted
effort should change the help—not merely add more personal references or make a
longer-term user's page denser. Preserve capability access and revisable scope.

### 9.6 Practical visual instruments need complete, interpretable scope

Places 07 calls its approximately forty-minute burden strip “door to door.” The
shown components include a twenty-five-minute ferry, service every forty minutes,
a nine-minute walk and an approach to Pier 11. The cold comparisons do not name
the origin or departure that makes this a usable estimate. This does not prove
no origin exists elsewhere; it means the visible comparison cannot be interpreted
reliably as drawn. Waiting can dominate the apparent difference between the
thirty-two- and forty-minute options.[^second-places]

The same chain extends inside the destination. Places 08 describes a level walk
from the landing but an exhibition in upstairs rooms. Sorrento's section honestly
exposes a forty-metre cliff and unlisted connections. These are useful partial
facts, not a complete arrival assessment for someone whose access needs matter.
Plans' parent-aware response is an existing donor, so the requirement is not
another demographic persona or a universal accessibility badge.

**Recommended design:** identify the estimate's origin/time or separate travel
from waiting; trace one route through the final entrance and relevant interior
access; preserve uncertainty and a useful alternative that still serves the
purpose. This improves the recommendation itself, not just its disclaimer.

### 9.7 Social continuity must work for each actual recipient

Three findings sharpen the social review.[^second-social]

1. **Blocking versus attendance:** Home 10's proposed block frame changes Alex's
   gathering from four going to three, with no attendance-change event. Hiding a
   participant from a view does not establish that they stopped attending. If a
   count is viewer-relative it must not masquerade as the actual gathering size;
   preserving safe shared truth may require a different treatment.
2. **Guest-specific record:** Social 04.4 labels Nora's account private to her,
   while Sam's 04.6 record promises the photo and what Nora wrote. No separately
   shared account is drawn. Nora seeing Sam's privately supplied departure time
   in her own record is not itself a leak. The unresolved route is what Sam
   actually receives later. Name the shared original, not an implicitly merged
   account of the evening. His post-connection destination also remains undrawn.
3. **Remote contributor benefit:** Dana's narrow, nonattending contribution is
   genuinely designed, but the selected return mainly benefits Nora. Dana may
   find contributing worthwhile on its own; neither a thank-you nor a photo is
   owed. Still, a selected variation should show her initiating or receiving
   something worthwhile through these people without becoming a guest, supplying
   equal effort or gaining automatic access to their dinner material.

Social supply needs intermediate and busy states, not only a rich group versus
no friends. Reuse Places with one occasional contributor and with many eligible
contributions, repeated places and asynchronous material. This is a selection
and retrieval problem, not justification for a new social feed or mandatory
engagement signals.

### 9.8 Retrieval, current truth and disconnected return

Life has a strong direct-access grammar, but its selected targets are relatively
recognizable. Nine pasta results and a photograph named by sender and occasion
do not demonstrate handling several similar dinners, uncertain dates, or a thing
remembered under the wrong medium. Life 05's no-result language includes “no
Portugal travel,” which should remain a statement about held evidence rather
than a claim about the person's life.[^second-life]

Home 08c also sends “Today, as it stands” to a Life page that says its strip is
what Home showed at 8:40. A Life projection of current structure is legitimate,
but the design must distinguish that captured reading from the current owning
arrangement. Show an update after 8:40 and a clear continuation to the Plan;
contextual Ask must not accidentally operate on a stale representation.

Home 12's “record is local; it is never stale” is too broad. It correctly labels
last-known commitments and withholds unsupported live values elsewhere. Retain
that good behavior while separating an available original, last-known shared
state, current eligibility and an unavailable fresh assessment. Social's
phone-away ending is not evidence of technical offline behavior.

**Recommended design:** extend an existing arrival and refind, including one
ambiguous target, an incomplete-record miss, an intervening arrangement change
and a disconnected return. Preserve useful originals and the actual question;
do not hide all cached material or promise every shared state is current. A
Back annotation and four-tab footer do not establish shell restoration after
unrelated browsing. Full standalone Chat/shell behavior remains outside this
five-project evidence set.

### 9.9 Corrections to the prior review and rules not to invent

- **Free evening:** Home's strip ending at six does not establish suppression of
  all later value. Its morning page already has worthwhile material below. Home
  H2 now asks for an evening comparison rather than diagnosing a stopping-rule
  defect or demanding an extra offering.
- **Non-spatial sharing:** Social 05 D1 is a real alternative donor. Section 5A
  now names it; the selected destination/refind gap remains.
- **Ask versus Bring:** Home 04's connected sample explicitly explains Try with
  yours as bringing a ticket into Life with Undo. Do not classify that entire
  route as an automatic Ask-retention violation. A generic question with an
  attachment needs a separate contrasting case.
- **Later social value:** Social 04.8 saves retyping and recovers useful material.
  That is real preparation continuity, not yet proof of relationship-based
  judgment. Stronger Life v0.2 donors should be reused, not claimed as absent.
- **Withdrawal notices:** quiet omission during current discovery and an honest
  explanation when reopening a missing target can both be appropriate. Home
  08b's Life reference and Life 07/Social 04.9 need their exact receiving contexts
  reconciled, not a universal rule to always name or never name withdrawal.
- **Lifestyle breadth:** low-cost, at-home, familiar, solitary and restful value
  already exists. The missing comparison is changed current purpose and burden,
  not a larger catalog of lifestyle types.
- **Components:** retain the reviewed 38 Home kinds and Places' component/canon
  drawings as an inventory of meanings and variants, not a requirement to build
  that many permanent widgets or show them all. Do not make additional kinds the
  answer to missing service behavior.

### 9.10 Revised consolidation priority

The selected-design map remains useful, but should be lightweight enough that
it does not postpone the unresolved experiences. Organize the next design round
around the following outcomes rather than five independent redesigns:

| Priority | Outcome | Existing design leads and donors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Independent receiving with bounded supply, and a credible ordinary input-to-value-to-Life path | Home/Places composition; Life 02/06; Home 04/07; Social sparse alternatives. |
| 2 | Complete bounded assistance for an external arrangement, including changed purpose, accepted continuing help and its ending | Plans 08/09/11 plus labeled appendix donors; Home 08c/12; existing service decision. |
| 3 | Same current arrangement and safe recipient-specific material through change, return and retrieval | Plans 06; Home trip-day/block states; Social 03/04; Life 03b/05/P2/P3. |
| 4 | Non-spatial and asymmetric social receiving across sparse and busy conditions | Social 05 D1 and 03/04; Places friends scope; Life original readers. |
| 5 | Component convergence and visual polish against the selected connected encounters | Existing root and object families; no new universal layout or component quota. |

Priorities 1–4 can share examples and proceed with direct owner coordination;
they are not a requirement to finish one loop before thinking about the system.
The orchestrator owns consequential cross-project choices and synthesis. Owners
can resolve ordinary composition details without repeated micro-handoffs.
Agreement on drawings is still not evidence of demand, delight, affordability,
native usability or complete implementation. Integration stays paused.
