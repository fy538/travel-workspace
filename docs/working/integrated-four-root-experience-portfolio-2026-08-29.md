# Integrated Four-Root Experience Portfolio

## Ruling

This is the selected semantic portfolio for the next visual and native labs.
It does not freeze geometry, component choice, ranking weights, copy polish, or
production API shape. It does freeze the product jobs, root postures, evidence
boundaries, owner relationships, result families, and transitions that those
later decisions must preserve.

The portfolio tests one product rather than one grammar per tab:

> **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.**

Home and Places return value. Chat accepts attention and provides agency. Life
supports finding, returning, seeing anew, and continuing. Multiplayer changes
the meaning, possibility, participation, or consequence already present; it
does not create a social feed.

## Controlled evidence world

### Founder-grounded material

- return to New York after Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi, and Rome;
- flight, rail, and ferry tickets brought to Vesper;
- photographs, menus, restaurants, hotels, transport, and founder notes;
- Rome heat, cultural density, and the Colosseum/Aeneas attention thread;
- Sorrento cliffs and the practical role of elevators in a layered street
  network;
- the wish to project life forward into the coming New York weekend;
- interest in how travel becomes practical capability rather than personality
  interpretation.

### Governed synthetic fixture material

- Maya's addressed Paris trace is synthetic and exists only to test attributed
  social juxtaposition and withdrawal;
- the Red Hook Saturday opening is a designed fixture grounded in the founder's
  post-return New York situation;
- the four-person dinner, availability, rain, reservation, fatigue, and live
  change states are synthetic operational fixtures;
- all provider state is fixture truth, never a claim about the founder's actual
  reservations or friends.

## Shared expression grammar

Every visible unit is one of:

| Family | Product use |
| --- | --- |
| **Direct State** | Complete owner truth is already the value |
| **Composition** | Vesper contributes reconstruction, explanation, contrast, orientation, or editorial synthesis |
| **Instrument** | Current state, manipulation, authorization, or consequence must remain live |
| **Receipt** | A completed, failed, or partial attempt must be reconciled to owners |
| **Owner link** | The owner is already the best continuation |
| **Short prose** | A direct conversational answer needs no structured expression |
| **Silence** | No intervention earns its attention cost |

Every Composition begins with substance, not a prompt. Every Instrument exposes
freshness, authority, fallback, and recovery. Every social unit preserves
author, audience, and withdrawal. Every root can stop.

## Situation 1 — Ordinary New York, nothing pressing

### Human job

Re-enter ordinary life without feeling that the trip is either forgotten or
still occupying the whole product. Find something immediately worthwhile in
the coming weekend without being asked to reflect.

| Root | Selected semantic composition |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Foreground Composition: a compact Red Hook opening that begins with one concrete, current way into Saturday and one non-obvious reason it fits now. Supporting Direct State: the coming weekend window and current weather/availability, if verified. Supporting Composition: one Europe-to-New-York transfer with a practical mechanism, such as how layered waterfront access changes the day—not “you are a transition person.” At most one continuation per unit. |
| **Chat** | Clean composer with concrete invitations: bring something, ask about this weekend, continue the Red Hook opening, or ask about the Europe journey. No scroll of generated value and no required onboarding classification. A contribution returns immediate value plus a compact receipt only if something persisted. |
| **Places** | World Field centered on current New York: a small spatial set of areas whose differences matter this weekend, not a generic nearby list. Red Hook appears because the ferry/waterfront/food/pace relation is useful now. Europe remains a faint relationship layer, not the center. |
| **Life** | Stable doors into the Europe journey, August 2026 timeline, map, people, and Artifacts. The entrance leads with useful return identity and re-finding, not a retrospective assignment. |

Suppressed: “What do you not want to lose?”, generic trip totals as the crown,
personality interpretations, and a feed of every possible connection.

## Situation 2 — Saturday begins to take shape

### Human job

Turn an appealing opening into something four people could actually join
without forcing the organizer through a form or forcing invitees into profiles.

| Root | Selected semantic composition |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Foreground Instrument: the material shape of Saturday—window, Red Hook anchor, people not yet contacted, one live constraint, and current decision boundary. Supporting Composition: why this version of the day works better than the obvious alternative. Human contributions appear inside the Occasion consequence, not in a social module. |
| **Chat** | The host says “Make this work for Maya, Daniel, and Jess.” Vesper resolves people, asks only for authority it lacks, prepares an Occasion, and shows the proposed consequence before invitations. The host can alter the idea conversationally or manipulate the visible Occasion directly. |
| **Places** | Place Focus for Red Hook shows reachability, current conditions, spatial rhythm, and two ordered alternatives. Place Path expresses how ferry, walking, food, and weather fit together. It does not duplicate the Occasion decision UI. |
| **Life** | The accepted Occasion appears as a prospective bounded world with purpose, participants, contributions, commitments, and later lineage. It is not promoted to a Trip or Journey prematurely. |

Required authority: naming people does not authorize contact. Preparing is not
sending. Each invitee may contribute an Artifact, availability, judgment,
boundary, or action without creating an Occasion-specific identity profile.

## Situation 3 — Reality changes while something is live

### Human job

Absorb a meaningful disruption, preserve the worthwhile core, and make the
smallest safe consequence happen without turning the evening into workflow.

| Root | Selected semantic composition |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Live Reduction: one foreground Instrument replaces all ordinary editorial units. It states what changed, what remains protected, the recommended bounded response, authority status, and fallback. Nothing else competes until the state resolves. |
| **Chat** | The person can inspect why, change the protected constraint, authorize a provider or group consequence, or ask Vesper to repair. Chat retains the originating Instrument and returns to it after work. |
| **Places** | Live Reduction removes discovery abundance. The map keeps only current location, viable route/venue alternatives, relevant timing margin, and inaccessible or stale paths. |
| **Life** | No live workflow feed. After resolution, only verified commitment changes, receipts, Occurrence, and separately authored Outcomes become eligible for continuity. |

Failure behavior: pending, partial, failed, stale, and unknown remain distinct.
Assistant prose never asserts that a reservation, route, or message changed;
canonical readback and receipts do.

## Situation 4 — Return to the Europe journey

### Human job

Find what happened, recover a detail, see a supported relation that was not
already obvious, and optionally carry something useful into present life.

| Root | Selected semantic composition |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Europe appears only when timely: a compact Composition with a new supported distinction, a relevant source resurfacing, or a present New York consequence. A factual recap may appear if it solves a current retrieval job; it is not the default crown. |
| **Chat** | Natural retrieval and transformation: “Which restaurant had that pasta?”, “Show the ferry parts”, “Compare the menus”, “What is still uncertain?”, or “Turn this into something I can send.” Chat can inspect Sources and correct relations without making the user classify an ontology. |
| **Places** | A Journey Path binds Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi, Rome, routes, thresholds, and gaps to geography. Place Focus can expose a non-obvious researched mechanism—such as Sorrento's elevator as street infrastructure—while preserving what the founder already knew versus what Vesper added. |
| **Life** | Journey entrance with map, sequence, people, Artifacts, saved Compositions, and Returns as lenses over one identity system. The lead Composition may show the menu's three meanings of “local” only if the exact menu evidence supports it. Unknown transfers remain gaps, not invented continuity. |

No interpretation of personality is admitted. Claims concern the journey,
sources, places, routes, food, or supported relations unless the person authors
a claim about self.

## Situation 5 — A friend's trace enriches, then leaves

### Human job

Receive a meaningful, bounded perspective from another person without a public
feed, synthetic intimacy, itinerary exposure, or permanent possession.

| Root | Selected semantic composition |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A social Composition juxtaposes the founder's Rome heat route with Maya's addressed Paris relief pattern on one substantive axis. Both authors and the boundary are visible. It appears only while timely and authorized. |
| **Chat** | The viewer may ask what evidence supports the juxtaposition, save a private version only where permitted, or address a human-authored response. Vesper does not draft intimacy as fact or contact Maya without authority. |
| **Places** | The social trace appears only where it changes spatial understanding—such as how relief intervals shaped a route. Exact private locations remain absent. |
| **Life** | If deliberately saved, Life preserves the viewer's exact authorized version and dependency manifest. Maya's Source remains independently governed. |

On withdrawal, the affected claim and every projection are invalidated or
revised. A frozen version may retain only what its grant permits and must show
the withdrawal boundary; it never turns Maya's contribution into founder-owned
evidence.

## Cross-root transition contract

Every transition carries:

- selected canonical `ResourceRef`s and revisions;
- viewer and audience context;
- current Moment and immediate job;
- result family and active Instrument when present;
- optional continuation with its authority boundary; and
- origin plus exact return target.

Opening another root is optional. Back returns to the originating semantic unit
when it still exists; if stale, the app explains the change and returns to the
owner rather than a visually similar replacement.

## Page-level demand budgets

| State | Home | Chat | Places | Life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary | 1 foreground + up to 2 substantive units | clean entry | bounded World Field | stable doors + one return cue |
| Emerging | 1 material Instrument + up to 1 supporting Composition | preparation and authority | Focus + Path | prospective Occasion lineage |
| Urgent | 1 Instrument only | inspect/authorize/repair | Live Reduction | no live workflow |
| Return | 1 timely compact return at most | retrieve/transform/correct | Journey Path or Place Focus | index + selected episode |
| Social | integrated into affected unit | inspect/address/withdraw | only if spatially material | governed continuity |

## Promotion gates

The portfolio advances only if:

1. every first viewport delivers value without input;
2. every field has one owner or an explicit unavailable state;
3. the same identity survives cross-root movement;
4. generated Compositions remain distinct from Sources and canonical state;
5. social authorship and withdrawal recompile every dependent projection;
6. Chat can finish without retention or navigation;
7. Home and Places are substantial but not attention feeds;
8. Life supports re-finding without curation homework; and
9. each situation includes a graceful stop or silence path.

This portfolio is now the upstream requirement for visual-system exploration,
projection extraction, bounded owner reads, and the native lab.
