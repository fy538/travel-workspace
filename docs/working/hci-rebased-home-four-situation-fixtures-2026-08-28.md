# HCI-Rebased Home — Four-Situation Fixture Pack

## Decision

Test one recognizable Home across four radically different situations:

1. an ordinary New York week with no urgency;
2. the first days back from Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi, and Rome;
3. live Rome under heat, walking, time, and attention pressure; and
4. a Brooklyn dinner with private and shared state.

Each fixture uses the same four composition regions:

```text
situational frame
  -> zero or one foreground
  -> bounded composed field
  -> continuity and stable doors
```

The fixtures are complete content-and-interaction compositions, not final
visual designs. “Full scroll” means the selection, order, value, demand,
ownership, and handoff are specified from top to bottom. Exact geometry,
typography, visual theme, animation, and illustration belong in the dedicated
visual-design environment after this semantic comparison is accepted.

All personal, provider, weather, schedule, availability, social, historical,
and scientific details below are fixture evidence. They must be replaced by
authorized, current, source-bound production evidence. The pack does not claim
that any named friend said something, any provider state is live, or any
fictional Plan exists for the founder.

## 1. Common comparison method

Every situation compares five treatments.

| Treatment | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **A — infrastructure** | Maps, calendar, booking, messages, weather, or canonical objects without Vesper's cross-source contribution |
| **B — underreach** | Factual recap, feature inventory, generic recommendation, or prompt that transfers synthesis back to the person |
| **C — overreach** | Identity diagnosis, opaque ranking, inferred consensus, unmandated action, or intimacy beyond authority |
| **D — recommended** | Completed, bounded value with evidence, appropriate medium, low demand, owner, and stopping condition |
| **E — silence/smaller** | Nothing, or a deliberately smaller composition, when marginal value is insufficient |

The recommended treatment must beat A, B, C, and E. More Vesper is not
automatically better than infrastructure or silence.

## 2. Common fixture schema

Each visible unit records:

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Class** | Canonical state, operational instrument, human contribution, or Vesper contribution? |
| **Dominant job** | What one human job owns the unit? |
| **Delta** | What becomes newly understandable, possible, workable, or durable? |
| **Truth and owner** | Which object or Source owns each claim and revision? |
| **Why now** | What current fact makes this worth attention? |
| **Demand** | Is value complete on view? What response, if any, is constitutively necessary? |
| **Medium** | What is the smallest native form that makes the contribution perceptible? |
| **Social authority** | Who authored what, who may see it, and what is withheld? |
| **Consequence** | What happens next, under whose authority, and where does it land? |
| **Expiry/degradation** | When does the unit change, simplify, or disappear? |

The copy examples expose the proposition and hierarchy. They are not a license
for final production voice.

## 3. Shared page laws

All four fixtures obey these constraints:

- the first viewport gives value before asking for input;
- no page opens with a reflection question, profile setup, poll, or empty
  composer;
- at most one unresolved primary decision dominates;
- complete-on-view units may coexist when their marginal value is real;
- people appear inside the object or contribution they change;
- behavior does not become an identity or emotional reading;
- the recent trip never owns Home by recency alone;
- one lead medium governs each unit;
- action authority and affected people remain visible;
- a human-authored contribution remains human-authored;
- depth routes to a durable owner with context intact; and
- silence and a smaller composition remain live alternatives.

## S1 — Ordinary New York, no urgency

### 4. Situation and evidence

It is an ordinary Tuesday in New York several weeks after the Europe trip is no
longer the current temporal boundary. Nothing is broken or urgent.

The authorized fixture contains:

- current New York location at city-level precision;
- a verified open Saturday window from 2–8 PM;
- current dry-weather, daylight, ferry-service, walking, and venue-hour facts;
- an existing Place relationship with Red Hook, without an inferred “water
  person” trait;
- one permissioned note from Maya attached to the route, visible to this viewer;
- a Brooklyn dinner Occasion next week whose indoor room is settled and whose
  roof fork does not require action yet; and
- no explicit current request for recommendations.

The page must prove that ordinary life can be rich without urgency, a trip mode,
or manufactured psychological need.

### 5. Comparative treatments

| Treatment | Example | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Calendar shows the open block; Maps can route to Red Hook; messages contain Maya's note | Useful infrastructure, but the person must assemble the day and reconcile conditions |
| B | “What do you want to do this weekend?” or twelve nearby recommendations | Homework or inventory; Vesper withholds the contribution |
| C | “You miss movement and transitions after Europe” | Unsupported emotional and identity interpretation |
| D | One feasible harbor afternoon, one city mechanism, one compact Occasion state, and no required response | Recommended |
| E | Only the Occasion row and stable doors | Correct fallback if route evidence or marginal editorial value is weak |


The line appears only if the calendar window and Place scope are current. No


The note modifies the route by making the ferry segment part of the experience,

## S2 — Returned to New York, life already moving forward

### 8. Situation and evidence

It is Thursday in the first days after returning from Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi,
and Rome. The trip produced unusually dense evidence, but there is no unresolved
travel emergency.

The authorized fixture contains:

- flight, train, and ferry documents with scheduled states;
- photographs and available time/Place metadata;
- hotel and restaurant evidence;
- menu and dish photographs plus the person's explicit observation that pasta
  tasted different;
- the person's explicit Sorrento-cliff observation;
- a movie ticket or Source related to Greek epic;
- a Colosseum visit and the person's already-made Troy/Aeneas/Rome connection;
- Maya's separately permissioned Paris evidence from an overlapping period;
- current New York location, an open weekend, and the current Brooklyn dinner
  Occasion; and
- current local provider facts only when verified.

The page must return more value than a trip report while refusing to trap the
person inside Europe.

| --- | --- | --- |

| C | “You experience travel through transitions” or “mythology defines how you see the world” | Identity overreach from sparse evidence |
| D | A present New York opening, compact current state, one evidence-led Place transfer, and one permissioned social contrast | Recommended |
| E | Present opening plus current Occasion only | Better when the retrospective contributions are weak, repetitive, or insufficiently sourced |

#### S2-X1 — Strong cliff comparison deliberately not selected today

```text
SORRENTO AND THE PALISADES LOOK LIKE THE SAME IDEA.
GEOLOGICALLY, THEY ARE OPPOSITES.

[paired cliff sections]

Sorrento
Volcanic tuff deposits over carbonate bedrock; marine erosion and fractured
tuff help produce the steep inhabited coastal edge.

Hudson Palisades
A Jurassic diabase sill exposed as surrounding rock eroded; the cliff is the
resistant intrusion itself.

What looks like one “cliff city” relation comes from different rock histories.
```

This is a structural comparison, not visual resemblance or a bucket-list
recommendation. It supplies a new axis—deposit over bedrock versus resistant
intrusion—with which to perceive both Places.

Research basis for the fixture claim:

- a CNR-hosted Sorrento study describes carbonate bedrock covered by Campanian
  Ignimbrite tuff, marine erosion, fracturing, cavities, and rock-fall
  instability;
- Campanian Ignimbrite research documents pyroclastic material reaching and
  depositing on the Sorrento Peninsula; and
- the U.S. Geological Survey describes the Palisades as a magma intrusion that
  cooled into a sill and became exposed as surrounding rock eroded.

The production contribution must preserve the exact source, claim scope, and
analogy limit. “Opposites” is an editorial headline for the contrast, not a
claim that every process differs in every respect.

It is withheld because the selected page already has one trip-derived mechanism
and one trip-derived social contrast. It may replace either on a later day or
surface inside Places when the Palisades are situationally relevant.

#### S2-X2 — Strong Aeneas contribution deliberately not selected today

```text
AENEAS BECAME MORE THAN ROME'S SURVIVOR STORY.
CAESAR AND AUGUSTUS MADE HIM PART OF A FAMILY CLAIM TO POWER.
```

A source-bound version can show:

```text
Venus -> Aeneas -> Iulus -> the Julian clan
                     -> Caesar's Venus Genetrix
                     -> Augustus's Aeneas among the Julian ancestors
```

This is materially stronger than repeating “Aeneas escaped Troy and connects
the film to Rome.” Museum and classical scholarship support the Julian claim of
descent through Venus/Aeneas/Iulus and the placement of Aeneas with Julian
ancestors in the Forum of Augustus.

It is not selected in this composition because the page already contains three
Europe-derived contributions. It remains eligible for Places or Life and may
replace, rather than join, another retrospective unit on a later day. This is a
test of marginal value, not a judgment that the content is weak.

## S4 — Brooklyn dinner as temporary social constitution

### 16. Situation and evidence

The host created a Saturday Brooklyn dinner in Chat through ordinary language.
The fixture contains:

- host, partner, close friend, and weaker-tie invitees;
- date, time, address, indoor table, roof option, and current rain forecast;
- invitation states including nonresponse;
- Maya's attributed salad contribution;
- Luis's attributed later-arrival contribution;
- one private shellfish constraint whose owner has not authorized attribution;
- a shared menu consequence that can satisfy the constraint without revealing
  its source;
- no Occasion-specific profile; and
- no mandate for Vesper to send, publish, purchase, or settle a material choice
  without the appropriate person.

The page must help the dinner work while preserving private relationships with
Vesper and plural participant Outcomes.

### 17. Temporary constitution

| Element | Fixture rule |
| --- | --- |
| Convenor | Host may create and end the Occasion and settle ordinary host-owned logistics |
| Participants | Each person owns join, decline, contribution, correction, and departure |
| Shared decision | Material changes affecting attendees require the declared decision rule; nonresponse is not assent |
| Private constraints | Vesper may use them only for the permitted purpose and expose the minimum common-ground consequence |
| Human authorship | Dish, timing, photograph, note, and preference remain attributed to the contributor |
| Vesper role | Prepare state, options, tradeoffs, and receipts; never fabricate consensus or impersonate a participant |

| E | Canonical invitation and room state only | Correct until enough authorized contributions or current weather evidence exists |

## 23. What these fixtures correct

Relative to earlier Home work, this pack makes eight corrections:

1. **Home returns value before requesting input.** No standing ask, trip prompt,
   Occasion profile, or “pick a feeling” unit survives.
2. **The four moves are not sections.** One move can dominate and another can
   be absent without compensation.
3. **The seven-region anatomy becomes four stable contracts.** Social and
   continuity appear only where earned.
4. **A recent trip supplies material, not a page mode.** Current New York and
   current people remain visible.
5. **Substance precedes visual novelty.** The Sorrento and Aeneas examples name
   a new mechanism and source requirement before selecting a diagram.
6. **Social presence must transform the object.** Maya changes a route or a
   comparison; attendee contributions change the dinner.
7. **Current physical reality can suppress ambition.** Rome is allowed to
   become a route, reservation, and one thirty-second cue.
8. **The composer edits.** A strong Aeneas contribution can be withheld because
   another page composition already spends too much attention on Europe.

## 24. Visual-design handoff

The next visual exploration should render all four situations from this exact
content contract before inventing additional modules.

The dedicated design tool may vary:

- hierarchy, spacing, density, and rhythm;
- whether the foreground feels like a stage, instrument, route, evidence field,
  or Occasion object;
- the visual grammar for provenance, authorship, freshness, and uncertainty;
- compact comparison, sequence, map, and state forms;
- how stable doors remain recognizable across radically different page
  densities; and
- how quiet aesthetics coexist with high delivered value.

It must not vary:

- unit selection or order without recording the semantic reason;
- truth, author, audience, owner, or consequence;
- the one-foreground hierarchy;
- the demand budget;
- the complete-on-view proposition;
- accessibility equivalence; or
- the boundary between Home projection and durable owner.

Produce at least three visual treatments of each same composition rather than
one treatment of twelve different content inventories. The comparison should
answer whether one visual language can support spatial, evidentiary,
operational, relational, and editorial value without collapsing everything
into uniform cards.

## 25. Evaluation checklist

For each rendered page, ask:

1. Can a person state the foreground value after five seconds?
2. Does the first viewport provide value without a response?
3. Is every additional unit materially different from what came before it?
4. Can the person distinguish world fact, human contribution, Vesper
   contribution, and proposed action?
5. Is social authorship visible without producing an activity feed?
6. Does the page interpret evidence and environment rather than the person's
   identity?
7. Are tradeoffs visible where physical or social burden matters?
8. Does opening a unit deepen value rather than reveal a withheld answer?
9. Does every consequence have an owner and authority boundary?
10. Would a smaller composition or silence be better?
11. Does Home return the person's attention to the world?
12. Could the resulting understanding or capability help on a second occasion
    without requiring Vesper?

Promotion should require blind comparison against the infrastructure,
underreach, overreach, and silence treatments—not founder enthusiasm for the
recommended treatment alone.
