# Medium Selection and Home Projection Fixtures

## 1. Purpose

This portfolio tests whether one evidence-rich life situation can generate
several distinct kinds of value without becoming a trip recap, prompt queue,
renderer gallery, or generic feed.

Every fixture starts from the same bounded post-return context, then records:

```text
evidence
  -> contribution
  -> product move and immediate job
  -> lead medium and modifiers
  -> compact Home projection
  -> durable owner and deeper projection
  -> trust, accessibility, lifecycle, and optional consequence
```

The portfolio is a product and design oracle. It does not authorize factual
claims, live-provider reads, user-data ingestion, production ranking, new
schema, or automatic social use. Exact historical, cultural, environmental,
price, schedule, Place, and friend claims require current authoritative
evidence and appropriate audience permission before rendering.

## 2. Shared evidence world

The fixtures assume an authorized test corpus representing:

- departure from New York on or after August 14;
- Nice, Sorrento, Capri/Amalfi, Rome, and return to New York;
- flight, train, and ferry documents;
- hotels or temporary bases;
- restaurant and menu evidence;
- photographs with timestamps and available Place metadata;
- an explicitly noted Sorrento-cliff observation;
- a movie ticket or other source related to Greek epic before Rome;
- a Colosseum visit and the person's already-made Troy/Aeneas connection;
- an authorized friend's Paris evidence from an overlapping period;
- present New York location and a future open weekend; and
- current Plans, commitments, weather, hours, transit, and availability only
  when an authoritative fixture or live source supplies them.

The corpus does **not** imply:

- that every ticketed movement occurred;
- that every photographed dish was eaten or liked;
- that a photographed person attended or consented to sharing;
- that the user has a stable personality, taste, or identity trait;
- that the friend permits cross-audience comparison;
- that an unverified historical connection is true; or
- that a generated future is desired, predicted, or committed.

## 3. Portfolio-wide oracles

Every fixture must pass all of these:

1. **New contribution:** Vesper adds a representation, mechanism, distinction,
   current state, or prepared possibility the person did not already supply.
2. **Complete on Home:** the compact projection contains useful substance
   before a tap or response.
3. **One lead medium:** supporting media carry proof or navigation, not visual
   variety for its own sake.
4. **Truthful role:** state, human contribution, editorial contribution,
   instrument, and receipt remain visibly distinct.
5. **Owner continuity:** opening the unit preserves identity and context while
   routing depth to Place, Life, Plan, Occasion, or Chat.
6. **Visible trust:** provenance, attribution, freshness, uncertainty, and
   authority are proportionate to the claim.
7. **No homework:** no reflection, caption, ranking, quiz, or reply is required
   to obtain the initial value.
8. **No psychographic leap:** Vesper interprets evidence, situation, or
   mechanism before interpreting the person.
9. **Accessible equivalence:** the nonvisual or reduced-motion alternative
   preserves the contribution and consequence.
10. **Lifecycle:** ignore is complete; the unit can recede, evolve, expire,
    invalidate, or remain with its owner without creating debt.

Priority means portfolio order, not production authorization:

- **P0:** validate first because the medium is high-fit, broadly usable, and
  already close to existing app capabilities.
- **P1:** validate after P0 because trust, research, social authority, or
  sustained-attention cost is higher.
- **P2:** experimental because the medium has high end-to-end complexity or
  greater risk of implying desire, certainty, or agency.

## 5. Detailed fixtures

### M01 — The journey as evidence-led sequence

**Evidence:** tickets, provider records, timestamps, photographs, hotel dates,
and explicit corrections.

**Required contribution:** assemble the best-supported actual sequence and make
one useful relation perceptible—for example, which crossing has complete
departure-to-arrival evidence or where intended and actual movement diverge.
A route that merely redraws uploaded tickets fails.

**Home projection:** a compact origin-to-return spine, one bounded finding, one
visible gap or uncertainty if present, and `Open journey`. No “places that
changed you,” trip score, or question.

**Owner projection:** Life owns the standard or immersive reconstruction with
source inspection, corrections, selected media, and Place/Occasion links.

**Accessible alternative:** ordered place/time list with scheduled, observed,
corrected, and unresolved states.

**Lifecycle:** Home recedes after meaningful exposure unless new evidence,
correction, or present consequence changes the contribution. Life retains the
authorized reconstruction.

### M02 — Pasta mechanism on the evidence

**Evidence:** one or more menu photographs, dish photographs, restaurant and
regional context, the person's explicit observation, and authoritative culinary
sources.

**Required contribution:** identify and explain a mechanism the person did not
already supply, such as ingredient, extrusion, starch management, emulsification,
service timing, or regional preparation. Exact mechanism depends on evidence.

**Home projection:** the photograph remains primary; one or two annotations
make the difference visible; a short claim explains why it matters. `See the
sources` or `Try this in New York` may appear only after the explanation is
complete.

**Owner projection:** the relevant Place or Encounter artifact owns the sourced
explanation. A prepared cooking or restaurant possibility may route into Places
or Chat without rewriting the original evidence.

**Negative oracle:** “Italian pasta tastes different” or “you notice texture”
fails because it repeats the observation or interprets the person.

### M03 — Sorrento cliff comparison

**Evidence:** the user's explicit observation, geolocated photographs, verified
topography and settlement evidence for Sorrento, and equivalent sources for one
comparison Place.

**Required contribution:** choose a comparison dimension that changes
understanding—geology, marina access, settlement edge, transport threshold,
tourism infrastructure, or another evidenced relation. Similar-looking cliffs
alone are insufficient.

**Home projection:** a paired cross-section, small map, or two annotated images
state the important difference directly. The title must contain the new
relationship rather than “Cliffs around the world.”

**Owner projection:** Places owns the fuller comparison, maps, sources, and any
future Place horizon. An unvisited comparison Place is context, not a bucket-list
recommendation.

### M04 — Rome and Paris, without collapsing two people

**Evidence:** separately authorized time, Place, weather, route, photograph,
or authored observations from each person.

**Required contribution:** reveal a real contrast neither person's evidence
could show alone—for example, how heat, water, public space, dining, transit,
or nighttime use shaped the two evenings.

**Home projection:** two attributed lanes, one consistent axis, one new
contrast, and explicit audience. No synthetic group sentiment and no message
written as either person.

**Owner projection:** Life may preserve the authorized social mosaic; a named
Occasion owns shared consequence if one is created. Each original Source keeps
its own author, custody, and removal path.

**Lifecycle:** expiry or revocation of either audience grant withdraws the
social projection. It may degrade to the current viewer's private evidence only
if that remaining evidence still supports an independently useful contribution.

### M05 — New York weekend possibility field

**Evidence:** current Place, time, weather, hours, transit, commitments, stated
constraints, prior authorized Place relationships, and current provider truth.

**Required contribution:** shape an open interval into two or three materially
different possibilities rather than list venues. The route, transition,
conditions, and ending near existing life should be part of the value.

**Home projection:** an interactive or compact map with one lead possibility,
one complete ordered alternative, current constraints, and `Open in Places`.
No Plan is created and no preference is inferred from travel history.

**Owner projection:** Places owns spatial exploration and current world truth.
Plan or Occasion begins only when the person chooses commitment or shared
coordination.

### M06 — Return movement as live instrument

**Evidence:** provider-backed flight or transit state, terminal/gate when
current, connection timing, current location when authorized, and existing
commitments.

**Required contribution:** reduce uncertainty through the present state, one
material margin, explanation of change when known, and the next authorized
consequence. A generic countdown fails.

**Home projection:** current state leads. One action appears only when it can
change the outcome; otherwise the instrument explicitly reassures or recedes.

**Owner projection:** the Movement or Plan authority owns live state and
mutation. Home and Push are projections; receipts return to the same owner.

### M07 — Observable photographic recurrence

**Evidence:** authorized photographs with sufficient visual and temporal
coverage.

**Required contribution:** expose an observable repeated form or anomaly—pose,
framing, color, object, material, meal stage, viewpoint, or contributor—without
claiming why it mattered or what it says about the person.

**Home projection:** a small contact sheet and one factual recurrence. The
person may inspect or correct it; they are not asked to explain it.

**Owner projection:** Life owns source inspection, collection boundaries,
removal, and any human-authored meaning added later.

### M08 — Movement mix as a consequential comparison

**Evidence:** verified modes, legs, timestamps, distances, elevation, cost, and
waiting time when available.

**Required contribution:** reveal a relevant distribution or tradeoff, such as
time in motion versus time at a base, planned versus unplanned transfer cost,
or how ferry and rail changed reachability. A vanity total fails.

**Home projection:** one compact chart or small multiple with units, window,
and one bounded takeaway. The chart does not imply a travel personality.

**Owner projection:** Life owns the full reconstruction and values; Places may
reuse a bounded movement distinction when it changes a future spatial choice.

### M09 — Short audio brief

**Evidence:** one admitted editorial contribution, its sources, current
listening context when explicitly available, and an owner artifact.

**Required contribution:** make a connection or mechanism useful in an
eyes-free moment. Audio is a rendering of a worthwhile contribution, not the
contribution's justification.

**Home projection:** first substantive sentence, `2–4 min`, source cue,
playback control, and transcript access. Never autoplay.

**Owner projection:** the source-backed Place or Life artifact owns the
transcript, evidence, and correction. Cached audio invalidates when a dependent
claim or Source changes.

### M10 — Long-form article or guide

**Evidence:** the person's already-known connection, primary and authoritative
secondary sources, current Place relation, and known-to-person exposure.

**Required contribution:** go beyond the person's Rome/Troy observation by
adding a verified mechanism, historical transformation, contested perspective,
or later consequence. Rephrasing “the film made Rome familiar” fails.

**Home projection:** headline contains the bounded new claim; one proof cue and
reading time are visible. The article is optional depth, not the location of
the first useful proposition.

**Owner projection:** the relevant Place or Life thread owns the full article,
sources, uncertainty, and later cross-medium renderings.

### M11 — Human contribution inside the object it changes

**Evidence:** an attributed, permissioned human Source and the Place, route,
comparison, or Occasion to which it applies.

**Required contribution:** the person's contribution must change understanding,
possibility, coordination, or consequence. Their name, avatar, or activity
alone does not qualify.

**Home projection:** attribution and audience sit inside the transformed map,
comparison, sequence, or instrument. Any reply or share remains human-authored.

**Owner projection:** the original Source retains authorship; Place, Occasion,
or Life owns only its governed projection and consequence.

### M12 — Constraint-responsive weekend simulation

**Evidence:** current options, deterministic route/time/cost/weather facts, and
explicit constraints. Prior behavior may narrow search only within authorized,
revisable scope.

**Required contribution:** show how changing one meaningful constraint changes
the prepared shape. The simulation must not predict desire or auto-commit.

**Home projection:** one prepared shape and the single changed constraint are
visible; deeper manipulation opens the instrument. Future state is labeled as
simulation.

**Owner projection:** the simulation is normally ephemeral. A Plan or Occasion
owns only a person-authorized commitment produced from it.

## 7. Composition tests

Build several complete Home arrangements from the register rather than
evaluating twelve isolated units.

### 7.1 Returned, ordinary Thursday

- M05 leads because current New York life is prospective.
- M06 appears only if return movement still has a material state.
- M02 or M03 provides one complete interpretive contribution.
- M04 appears only with current permission and genuine contrast.
- M01 or M07 sits lower as continuity.
- no prompt asks the person to summarize the trip.

### 7.2 Urgent movement disruption

- M06 leads and suppresses most possibility.
- M05 may remain only if it solves the changed arrival or weekend constraint.
- M01 becomes a receipt or later reconstruction, not current editorial content.
- M02–M04, M07–M10 recede.

### 7.3 Quiet open weekend

- M05 leads or the page presents no dominant demand.
- M02, M03, M04, or M11 may open materially different powers.
- M07–M10 appear only when they add non-redundant value.
- quiet is visually calm, not empty or reflective homework.

### 7.4 Protected shared Occasion

- current Occasion state and affected people lead.
- M11 may appear within the Occasion object.
- M04 appears only if its source and audience belong in the present context.
- private anchors may alter pacing or subtraction without appearing.
- no social feed or synthesized group story is generated.

## 8. Comparison protocol

For each P0 fixture, compare five treatments over identical evidence:

1. flat factual report;
2. prose-only synthesis;
3. selected native medium;
4. deliberately richer medium with greater interaction or production cost;
5. silence.

Reviewers answer:

- What did you understand within five seconds?
- What was new rather than repeated?
- Could you tell why Vesper presented it?
- Was any claim invasive, inflated, or more precise than its evidence?
- Did the medium reduce or add work?
- Was the value complete without opening?
- Did deeper inspection preserve the same object and context?
- Would a different medium be simpler without losing the contribution?
- Did the full Home feel coherent rather than like a format gallery?

Record qualitative results plus:

- comprehension accuracy;
- source and uncertainty recognition;
- successful correction or consequence;
- time to grasp the primary contribution;
- complete-on-view value;
- demand count and cumulative burden;
- accessible-alternative parity; and
- whether another unit on the same page made this one redundant.

## 9. Engineering implications to discover, not assume

The fixture portfolio should reveal whether implementation needs:

- an explicit semantic-role and medium-selection seam;
- separate density and surface-context fields;
- one supporting-medium allowance;
- social, relational, and quantitative modifiers;
- compact-to-owner projection identity;
- medium-specific trust and accessibility contracts;
- cached audio or generated-artifact invalidation; and
- composition-level repetition and burden signals.

It must not be used to justify:

- a universal Artifact or Opportunity table;
- a persisted medium decision for every object;
- arbitrary server-driven component trees;
- one Home section per medium or product move;
- one new backend endpoint per fixture;
- automatic social audience expansion;
- generated media as a retention tactic; or
- a production Home migration before complete-composition review.

## 10. Exit criteria

The portfolio is ready to inform implementation when:

- all P0 fixtures have factual, prose-only, native-medium, richer-medium, and
  silence comparisons;
- each P0 fixture has compact Home, durable-owner, correction, expiry, and
  accessible-alternative states;
- at least three full Home compositions pass five-second and cumulative-burden
  review;
- social fixtures preserve author, audience, revocation, and plural Outcomes;
- maps and charts expose complete ordered or value-based alternatives;
- audio has transcript, duration, control, and invalidation behavior;
- the native medium beats prose-only or silence on the intended human job;
- no P0 case requires a seventh core medium; and
- any proposed schema or resolver field can be traced to a repeated need across
  several fixtures rather than one attractive specimen.

## Closing principle

The same life evidence can return as a route, explanation, comparison, map,
instrument, human contribution, article, or audio brief. That breadth is useful
only when each expression contributes a different power.

> **The portfolio succeeds when Home feels like one intelligence choosing the
> right form—not many features competing to display the same data.**
