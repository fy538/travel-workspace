## 1. What we are designing toward

Vesper's broader product is personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world. It helps people **make sense, open possibility, help it work, and carry forward**. These are ways of delivering value, not a four-step funnel or one move per tab.

Its social expression is:

> **See a little more of your friends' worlds, find something worthwhile through them, and make it easier to do something together.**

The founder's core objections to earlier work are material design requirements:

- Home must deliver value from the attention and material already entrusted to Vesper—not ask for another reflection, classification or contribution before the payoff.
- A recap, count or obvious connection is insufficient when presented as new insight. A friend's original photograph, however, can be valuable without an AI interpretation.
- Life after a trip points forward. France and Italy can enrich an ordinary NYC weekend without becoming the whole identity or content supply of the person.
- Sharing should not require polished performance, but neither should it require performative informality. A beautiful photograph and an ordinary dish photo are both welcome.
- Human connection is the purpose. AI should remove search, recall and coordination work, not simulate affection, replace the friend, or assign social homework.
- The live engine must be felt in ordinary life, not confined to a rare travel emergency. Its practical competence belongs inside these same experiences.

**Design one social world, not one mandatory social journey.** Seeing a friend's photo, joining a meal and reconnecting later must each be independently worthwhile. Some can connect; none must graduate into the next.

### The three independent experiences

| Experience | What makes it worth opening | What can happen next | An equally valid ending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A · A little of your world** | Ordinary human presence, a particular perspective, useful access to a place | Ask privately, reply, keep where permitted, or explore | Enjoy it and leave |
| **B · An easier way to get together** | A concrete, appealing possibility and clear participation | Invite, join, contribute, adapt, meet offline | Decline, remain undecided, or join only one part |
| **C · Something stays with us** | A photo I missed, an identifiable shared occasion, a promised continuation | Refinding, a bounded exchange, or another occasion | Keep a legitimate record without more contact |

The project needs emotional warmth, ordinary humor and visual substance—not only correct permissions. A design that is safe but uninteresting has not completed the assignment.

### Holistic direction after the checkpoint review

**Reduce the work around human intention; preserve the human intention.** Finding the right photo, attaching relevant context, explaining arrival and carrying an agreed change are good work for Vesper. Inventing affection, narrating closeness, or prompting a person to maintain friendships is not the default product.

Low pressure is not the absence of meaning or clarity. A casual photo can ask nothing; an invitation can clearly need an answer; an accepted arrangement can carry real responsibilities. Make declining easy without disguising uncertainty as acceptance. Unequal app contribution is normal, but the design must not make the host do everyone else's administration.

The social distinction is the combination of **human perspective, relevant world understanding, practical assistance and legitimate continuity**. It is not merely private photos plus RSVP plus an album. Demonstrate that combination once with real substance, while preserving ordinary receiving that needs no AI layer, plan, save or response.

### Working social-structure recommendation — design proposal, not a new graph contract

Use an optional, explicitly accepted ongoing connection as the recommended relationship experience to compare with remaining occasion-bound. Do not require this to receive a photo or attend a dinner. Do not build public following or a visible friendship ladder in this pass; published taste may be a separate later use, not a synonym for friendship.

Keep three effects legible without three setup workflows: **this material is shared with you; this occasion includes you; we choose to remain connected.** Accepting a connection enables only the named future contact/sharing capability, not all past material, location, availability, private memory or automatic inclusion in future plans. A friends-visible audience is still an author choice, not a promise that every connection sees every share. The exact default capability is a recommendation the designer must state and the implementation lane must map—not a hidden schema decision.

Allow someone to reduce what they receive without publicly rejecting the relationship. Contextual mute/hide, disconnect and block/report serve different jobs; recommend proportionate access, not a new settings dashboard. Do not notify another person of private muting or memory. Blocking must be assessed across links, messages and invitations, not only a feed card. These controls are coverage requirements and proposed experience, not assertions of implemented support.

## 2. Research that changes how to build this project

This is a targeted follow-up to the two existing social research rounds, not a systematic review or evidence of product-market fit. The sources below were inspected on September 7, 2026. Historical HCI research informs mechanisms; official app documentation establishes described capabilities, not measured consumer outcomes. No adjacent app was hands-on tested in this pass.

### R1 · Prototype circumstances and roles, not only screens

Buchenau and Suri's *Experience Prototyping* describes active engagement with an integrated experience in its context, using commercial design examples rather than a controlled comparison proving one method superior. **Design implication:** give the host and recipient separate roles; include waiting, arriving and putting the phone away. A linked tour narrated by an omniscient designer is not sufficient. [Original paper, author-uploaded full text, DIS 2000](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221441600_Experience_Prototyping).

### R2 · Visibility can create obligation as well as understanding

Erickson and Kellogg distinguish visibility, awareness and accountability, including the tension between privacy and visibility. Their work concerns social computing and knowledge communities, not Vesper's friendship outcomes. **Our inference:** make authored intent and actual agreements legible, while avoiding passive-view evidence that makes a casual recipient accountable for noticing. The two sides must understand what the other can see without requiring a policy lesson. [Original paper, author-uploaded full text, TOCHI 2000](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220286360_Social_translucence_an_approach_to_designing_systems_that_support_social_processes).

### R3 · Casual sharing alone is not the differentiation

Locket describes close-friend photo sharing, reactions without like counts, memories and chat. Its March 2026 help article also describes a weekly Rollcall prompt. **Design implication:** borrow the immediacy of receiving human material, not a blanket claim that the whole app is pressure-free or a requirement to post on cadence. “Private photos without popularity counts” is adjacent territory, not a unique Vesper thesis. [Locket's current product explanation](https://help.locket.com/en/articles/14225418-my-teen-asked-me-to-get-locket-what-is-it).

### R4 · Invitations plus memories already form a coherent adjacent product

Apple Invites combines invitations, directions/weather, shared albums and playlists. **Design implication:** an attractive dinner invitation followed by a photo album does not by itself show Vesper's distinction. Show where ordinary attention, place knowledge, private assistance or existing continuity changes the actual experience. Do not force every photo to demonstrate that distinction. [Apple's product introduction, February 2025](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/02/introducing-apple-invites-a-new-app-that-brings-people-together/).

### R5 · Guest access is several capabilities, not one checkbox

Partiful documents browser access without an app download, with possible login requirements. Apple's privacy notice separately requires an Apple Account for joined guests to access shared albums, and an Apple Music subscription for shared playlists. **Design implication:** draw viewing, replying, contributing and later access separately. “No app needed” must not silently imply every later action is account-free or implemented in Vesper. [Partiful guest access, June 2026](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525328-do-my-guests-need-to-download-the-app); [Apple Invites privacy notice, March 2025](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/rsvp/).

### R6 · The previous research remains the social rationale

The [six-topic brief](vesper-social-experience-design-brief-2026-09-07.md) covers indirect invitations, unequal participation, authored awareness, gathering hospitality, artifact receiving and contextual continuity. Its [research report](../../output/pdf/vesper-social-six-topics-research-2026-09-07.pdf) includes study methods and limits. Apply those recommendations without turning their internal distinctions into six visible workflows.

**Synthesis:** prototype the person-to-person exchange and the additional value Vesper supplies, not just the movement of a record between tabs. Make actual consequences understandable; let ordinary receiving stay ordinary.

## 5. Experience A — A little of your world

**Design question:** can giving and receiving feel casual while the material opens real value, without a post-production ritual or a demand to act?

### A0 · The private input is useful before any social action

Nora asks Vesper about a cooking photo. Show an actual compact, useful answer, not “Tell me more” or “Would you like to remember this?” A source accompanying Ask does not automatically become a retained artifact or a friends post.

Nora then deliberately chooses to share the selected photo—possibly with no caption. Show the smallest audience/contents treatment that makes the effect clear. The default draft must not include her private question, AI analysis, inferred identity, exact home location or an Occasion invitation. A deliberate human Share gesture is required; the app does not append a sharing prompt to every answer or Save.

This is a private-to-social path to compare, not a requirement that all sharing start in Chat. Also provide a simple direct share from an existing eligible photo or record. Do not force a private AI conversation before an ordinary human gesture.

### A1 · Places is worth opening even if nobody needs anything

Compose a real full-scroll Places friends scope using A1 and A3 plus one or two relevant existing shares. Maya's original photo/words have enough visual presence to be enjoyable; a short note stays short; Dana's city-level Status is an Elsewhere doorway, not a live marker.

Show the relationship to the wider world: a readily understandable return to the general Places field, a useful place detail, and exploration when social supply is sparse. Enough surrounding content must be visible to judge density and hierarchy. A rich field can widen; do not imply four contributions are the permanent limit of Vesper.

One version ends after Nora enjoys Maya's photograph. No compulsory reply, save or AI summary. Another opens the Print Room and reveals original human material, current/dated world information and optional independent enrichment as distinct things.

### A2 · Two actions, two recipients

From the same photo/detail, compare:

- **Reply to Maya:** Nora's human-directed words and the recipient are clear. Sending a message does not change participation or edit the place.
- **Ask Vesper about this:** a private question uses the attached object context. Maya receives nothing, and returning restores the Places question, form and scroll position.

Do not route both through a generic Chat screen whose recipient only appears in tiny metadata. They may reuse components, but their social meaning must be immediately distinguishable. Do not require extra confirmation screens for every ordinary reply; the compose/send boundary itself can carry the necessary clarity.

### A3 · What Vesper adds—and when it adds nothing

Use one controlled comparison of the same material:

1. Original only.
2. Original plus one compact useful context layer.
3. Generated-led treatment, as a challenger rather than the assumed premium form.

The Print Room's independently sourced before/after spatial explanation can add something Maya did not say. The Paris/Rome pair can instead supply a grounded contrast about the spaces or conditions—not “you were both in Europe” or a personality interpretation. Do not manufacture an enrichment just to fill the third version; explicitly conclude that original-only wins where appropriate.

**Content acceptance requirement:** write the actual compact cooking answer and one complete Vesper-added social payoff before polishing their frames. ## 6. Experience B — An easier way to get together

**Design question:** can interest become a real gathering without turning leisure into planning work or making the host an unpaid operator of the app?

This branch shows a possible bridge from A, but **B also starts directly**: Nora can want dinner with friends without first publishing a photo or consuming a social feed.

### B1 · Host once, not a wizard

Suggested fixture instruction:

Resolve existing people through an available search/selection path; do not imply private address-book scanning or unknown identity matching. Produce a preview of what each relevant recipient will receive, then one deliberate Send. The preview should be a usable invitation, not a permissions worksheet. Keep Home a useful whole page, with other worthwhile personal/world material beyond this gathering. It is not an operations dashboard or an inbox of six decisions. Preserve the accepted one-social-ask budget; informational and enjoyable human contributions are not limited to one artifact.

**Required successful endings:** a chosen gathering, a decline without explanation, dinner-only participation, and a practical alternative left unadopted. The interface must not require everyone to use Vesper equally.

### C3 · A legitimate future continuation

Show another ending with no new contact. No “keep the connection alive,” elapsed-time guilt, recap completion request or inferred closeness score.

**Required successful endings:** receive without contributing; find the evening without recuration; continue a specific exchange; remember privately without contacting; narrow access without rewriting all history.

## 10. Decisions the designer must recommend, not merely illustrate

1. **Sharing into possibility:** natural language alone or one optional author-controlled openness affordance? Which action establishes participation, and under what offered terms?
2. **Acknowledgment:** is private Reply sufficient, or does one lightweight voluntary reaction earn its place? This cannot be settled on paper: recommend by principle (the six social principles and the no-proof-of-consumption rule in A4), state the recommendation's cost to the contributor plainly, and mark it as the first decision to observe with a real friend once a build exists.
3. **Friend-aware discovery:** what specific authored signals make a useful opening possible, and what stays private? How does this feel with one friend, sparse supply, and no friend content?
4. **Hospitality:** what is the minimum arrival experience that makes Sam feel practically included, without a profile or extra host work?
5. **Enrichment:** which original-only treatment wins, and where does added context materially improve receiving? How is the human voice preserved?
6. **Continuity:** which contact/reachability path is understandable after one encounter? What can happen without full app adoption, and what legitimately requires another step?

Keep these questions intact in canvas 05. D1 is **openness**, not audience pill versus no pill; D3 is **the value and scope of authored social signals**, with supply variants as an additional condition, not its substitute; D6 is **occasion-bounded exchange versus optional ongoing connection**, with email/no-path as an edge case. D5 may reuse original-only from canvas 02 plus two comparison frames to stay within the overall 12-frame budget. Recommend one direction for each decision rather than presenting a gallery of equivalent choices.

The current defaults in the research brief are starting recommendations, not a ban on well-supported alternatives. If a choice conflicts with an accepted contract, present the exact conflict and proposed amendment separately; do not quietly change what sharing or acceptance means.
