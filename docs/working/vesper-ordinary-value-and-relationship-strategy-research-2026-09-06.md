# The ordinary relationship with Vesper: six strategic investigations

## 1. Status, scope and conclusion

The founder requested parallel research into six questions after the strategy
task's September 5–6 synthesis. Three independent research agents covered
dependable value and disconfirmation, capability and initiative, and social
recipients. The coordinating pass investigated world-value supply, read the
relevant product contracts, spot-checked primary sources, and synthesized this
document. Research was conducted September 6, 2026.

This is desk research and proposed product reasoning, not a systematic literature
review, participant study, implementation audit, new canon, or release approval.
Research findings, vendor descriptions, existing Vesper decisions and our
inferences are distinguished. Older foundational work is included where useful;
search-engine crawl dates are not publication dates. Some sources were available
only as publisher abstracts/excerpts, identified below. No Vesper adoption,
retention, willingness to pay or offline relationship benefit was measured.

**Conclusion: the product should earn a dependable ordinary relationship, not
depend on an extraordinary connection every time.** Its range can stay broad:
useful answers, enjoyment, practical relief, human presence, exploration and
continuity. What needs to become reliable is the quality of contribution and
continuation, not a fixed content category or one mandatory loop.

| Question | Recommendation from this research | Still unproven |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is reliably good? | Establish an affirmative value floor below occasional discovery: useful payoff, bounded completeness, low assembly burden and sound continuation | Which supported encounters consistently earn preference over existing alternatives |
| What if the person contributes nothing new? | Compose from valid existing work, world changes, developing intentions and eligible human material; do not manufacture novelty | Coverage, repeated-visit quality and the full cost of dependable supply |
| Can the user become more capable? | Preserve wanted participation and offer useful distinctions when relevant; accomplishment need not become a lesson | When explanations create transferable understanding rather than more work |
| How much initiative is appropriate? | Match the contribution, moment and channel; familiarity, reliability and mandate are separate | Actual receptivity and dependable supported follow-up services |
| What does a thin participant receive? | Complete the recipient's benefit without equivalent contribution, unnecessary setup or organizer burden transfer | Sender preference, recipient enjoyment and external-channel continuity |
| What changes our minds? | Separate implementation, experience and strategic failures, with credible competing explanations | Real-use and commercial evidence, not another round of internal agreement |

These are refinements of **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry
forward.** They do not add product moves, roots, permanent user types, a new
ranking service or a universal life-state owner.

## 3. Dependable value: competence is necessary, but not sufficient

### Evidence

Bhattacherjee's 2001 study of 122 online-banking users associated satisfaction
and perceived continued usefulness with continuance intention. Initial
acceptance and continued use were different questions. This was a cross-sectional
survey with a low response rate, not a causal experiment or observed Vesper
retention. Its relevance is the need to examine experienced usefulness after
the promise, not to make our product exclusively utilitarian.
[Publisher record](https://aisel.aisnet.org/misq/vol25/iss3/2/).

The 2026 peer-reviewed publication of the jagged-frontier experiment reports a
preregistered study with 758 consultants. AI helped within tested capabilities,
but could produce more persuasive output while reducing correctness on the
outside-frontier task. It used GPT-4 and a specific professional population;
one outside-frontier task cannot estimate contemporary Vesper reliability.
The implication is to review actual correctness and usefulness independently
from polish. [Dell'Acqua et al.](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/dell-acqua-et-al-2026-navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier_5c589c8c-fbb5-458f-b285-c944746cd717.pdf).

Microsoft's research-backed human–AI guidelines emphasize understandable
capabilities, expected performance, efficient correction and cautious adaptation.
They are design guidance evaluated with practitioners, not proof of retention.
They support setting intelligible expectations, not importing a feedback prompt
after every contribution. [Amershi et al., 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-eighteen-best-practices-for-human-centered-ai-design/).

### Recommendation

A supported encounter should meet five affirmative standards:

- **Recognizable payoff:** something understood, found, made feasible, enjoyed
  or received from another person.
- **Bounded completeness:** the useful part is actually delivered; a teaser or
  promise of future personalization does not count.
- **Low assembly burden:** eligible material is retrieved and combined instead
  of making the person reconstruct the answer packet.
- **Calibrated judgment:** consequential knowns and unknowns are clear enough
  for the person to rely appropriately on the result.
- **Sound continuation:** opening, keeping or acting preserves the exact
  object and context; a successful first paragraph is not the whole experience.

This is an internal review standard, not a consumer checklist. Apply it to the
job: a friend's original photograph can satisfy it without a new AI insight,
and exact retrieval can be complete value without teaching anything.

The revised Friday example is stronger because Vesper checks the departure
requirement, retrieves an alternative and reduces reconstruction. It is not
strong because it mentions Sorrento. A capable assistant with the same evidence
may reason equally well; Vesper must earn its advantage in how little context
the person must find, repeat, update and repair.

**Important counterweight:** honest abstention can be the correct response to
an unsupported claim. Repeated abstention may still reveal a weak product.
Do not lower the truth bar; investigate coverage, retrieval or the promise.
Where possible, complete the supported part, identify the exact remaining
unknown and provide an appropriate continuation rather than generic refusal.

### Comparison to run

Use two complementary comparisons already anticipated by the matrix:

1. Equal authorized information, comparable tools and a clear request: assess
   output quality without claiming unique reasoning from an information advantage.
2. Equivalent initial sources distributed across ordinary interactions:
   assess total work to retrieve, combine, correct and continue. Include the
   burden of establishing Vesper's context, not only the final effortless turn.

Neither comparison alone establishes sustained choice or commercial viability.

## 4. Value without new input: supply changes, not manufactured freshness

### Evidence

Lathia et al.'s SIGIR 2010 study examined recommendation sequences rather than
single lists. Across 104 anonymous survey responses, repeated identical popular
movie lists lost appeal; changing popular recommendations performed better than
random lists. The five simulated weeks were presented within a survey, not
observed over five weeks, and respondents could complete multiple variants.
This supports examining repetition across visits, not a universal refresh quota
or random novelty policy. [Author-hosted paper](https://amatria.in/pubs/lathia_sigir10.pdf).

Chen et al.'s 2019 mobile-commerce study surveyed more than 3,000 users about
serendipity, relevance, unexpectedness, timeliness and satisfaction. Its results
support distinguishing useful surprise from novelty alone. The accessible
institutional abstract describes causal relationships, but does not by itself
establish a general causal effect on offline wellbeing or Vesper retention.
Do not infer a permanent curiosity trait or prescribed surprise ratio.
[Institutional record and abstract](https://scholars.ln.edu.hk/en/publications/how-serendipity-improves-user-satisfaction-with-recommendations-a/).

Pinterest's 2025 industry preprint describes linking specific saves to later
cross-surface revisits and reports a two-month experiment involving 24 million
users. Its contribution here is treating reuse of existing material as meaningful
behavior and confronting attribution, rather than equating value with fresh
content. Its impressions, revisits and active-user outcomes do not prove
understanding, satisfaction or offline benefit. Nor does its low incremental
model cost establish Vesper's economics.
[Jiang et al., Save, Revisit, Retain](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.18013v1).

### Recommendation: separate five reasons for value to appear

| Basis | What can make it worthwhile now | Necessary distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing entrusted material | The person needs it, or a real decision makes it useful | Re-finding is valuable without claiming new insight |
| A developing situation | Timing, participants, conditions or an expressed intention change | A real change is not an invented activity or inferred availability |
| World supply | A newly available event, new evidence or a useful unfamiliar perspective | New to the world, new to Vesper and new to this person differ |
| Human material | Someone chooses to share or address something | Original expression can be complete; sharing is not an unpaid content obligation |
| New application of known material | A comparison, practical adaptation or synthesis contributes something different | Rewriting the same lesson does not create new value |

No new user input is different from no new world evidence, and both are different
from no remaining value. Conversely, a new source does not automatically deserve
a place on Home. Selection still needs purpose, timeliness and substance.

The strongest supply design already exists in our docs: research durable
questions ahead of demand; retrieve volatile facts when they matter; assemble
current owner state; render human contributions in their own voice; generate
selectively when a new synthesis earns it. This is not a command to build a
larger generic research pipeline.

**Narrow initial coverage more deliberately than the underlying grammar.**
Coverage should mean support for particular uses and conditions, not a single
city-ready badge. A place can be known well enough to explain but not to make
an access-sensitive recommendation tonight. World Foundry's separate catalog,
operational and narrative readiness supports this distinction. Broad inquiry
can remain available outside the initial locally deep area with honest limits;
geographic depth does not mean curiosity must stop at a city boundary.

### Economics and a concrete constraint

Track research preparation and review, provider retrieval, freshness maintenance,
ranking, media/storage/delivery, selective generation, free recipients and
support. Reusable does not mean free. Prompt caching is not serving an already
finished result without another generation call.

Provider reuse rights also matter. Google's Places API documentation restricts
prefetching/caching/storage beyond exceptions, exempts place IDs, and specifies
attribution/display requirements. Applicable terms and regional differences
need provider-specific review; this memo is not a legal clearance or a blanket
cache policy. The strategic point is that public accessibility does not make a
provider's entire response an indefinitely reusable Vesper asset.
[Official Places API policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies).

### No-new-input comparison using the existing week

Hold the person's contributions fixed after Tuesday. This is a synthetic
comparison variant, not a claim about live supply or a required three-card feed.

| Visit | What can change without another contribution | What must remain honest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wednesday | The eligible applied photo comparison, existing Maya note and a genuinely sourced weekend possibility can offer distinct value | Do not delay the original answer to create this payoff; no known current origin or free afternoon |
| Friday | A supported owner/world update or newly relevant decision window can change practical treatment; other eligible world material can broaden the field | If nothing changed, do not invent a recommendation update or rewrite old advice as new |
| Later return | Current possibilities can lead; useful prior work remains accessible, and obsolete operational treatments recede | No attendance inference, optional-content backlog or demand to document the missing days |

Run a weaker variant with no new friend contribution and no fresh event supply.
Preserve useful retrieval and genuinely unspent material, but do not define
permanent thinness as a virtue. If repeated ordinary visits are unrewarding,
investigate the supply proposition rather than adding input prompts or filler.

## 5. Capability: preserve the worthwhile part that remains human

### Evidence

de Bellis, Johar and Poletti's 2023 research links meaning in manual work with
resistance to autonomous products. It includes household-product settings and
a representative survey of 1,004 Swiss consumers. Transferring this to cooking,
hosting or exploring with Vesper is an inference, not a tested Vesper finding.
It argues against equating all effort with waste, not for persuading people out
of activities they enjoy.
[Full paper](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/citation_file_upload/de-bellis-et-al-2023-meaning-of-manual-labor-impedes-consumer-adoption-of-autonomous-products.pdf).

Shen and Tamkin's 2026 randomized study of 52 developers learning Trio found
lower immediate mastery scores with AI assistance and no statistically
significant overall speed improvement. Patterns of AI use differed, but those
patterns were not independently randomized. This short coding-learning preprint
does not establish general cognitive decline or long-term skill loss. It shows
why completing a task and acquiring understanding must be evaluated separately.
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20245v1).

Xu et al.'s CHI 2025 experiment assigned 47 participants to different AI-support
conditions in structured design activities. Assistance changed the distribution
of cognitive work; diversity and process confidence did not move uniformly
together. The publisher abstract/excerpts were accessible; direct DOI retrieval
was unavailable during the research pass. Treat this as supporting the need to
inspect how work is redistributed, not as a prescription for one interaction
mode. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713649).

### Recommendation

Distinguish accomplishment, agency and transferable understanding internally.
An encounter may provide any combination, and enjoyment need not produce a
competence gain at all. These are evaluation distinctions, not new app modes.

| Situation | Work Vesper can absorb | Worthwhile participation to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person wants to cook for friends | Scale quantities, prepare timing, identify practical conflicts | Choosing, cooking, presenting and expressing care |
| Person wants better building photographs | Compare framing consequences and make the relevant distinction visible | Noticing and choosing the image; optional learning |
| Person enjoys choosing an afternoon | Research real differences and practical feasibility | The choice itself, without a research assignment |
| Same person is tired and asks for relief | Give one supported recommendation with alternatives available | Ability to revise or decline; no compulsory comparison exercise |

The useful review question is: **What does the person no longer have to do,
and what worthwhile part remains theirs?** Ask separately whether learning is
wanted or useful. Do not withhold answers, add quizzes, create skill goals or
turn every useful distinction into an educational program.

An explanation is not automatically more empowering than a completed result.
Too much explanation can move work into reading and verifying the assistant.
Conversely, a decisive recommendation need not remove agency if its decisive
reason and alternatives remain easy to inspect.

### Comparison to run

Apply paired treatments to the current photography and hosting cases: one
completed recommendation versus meaningful alternatives; compact applied
explanation versus optional depth; wanted craft preserved versus unwanted work
removed. Observe understanding only where relevant. Do not make people perform
a learning task merely to demonstrate that Vesper is empowering.

## 6. Initiative: familiarity improves fit, not permission

### Evidence

Pu et al.'s CHI 2025 within-subject study with 18 participants compared reactive
and proactive programming support. Proactivity could reduce comprehension effort
while disrupting workflow and reducing awareness of AI activity. Preferences
varied by task phase. Small single-file Python tasks and variable model output
limit generalization; the study does not identify a universal Vesper trigger.
[Author-hosted paper](https://kevinpjk.github.io/assets/pdf/codellaborator_chi_2025.pdf).

Fitz et al.'s 2019 two-week randomized field study with 237 participants compared
notification delivery patterns. Three daily batches improved several reported
experiences, while complete suppression could increase anxiety/FoMO. Publisher
abstract and methods excerpts were consulted. The Android/MTurk context does not
justify three Vesper digests per day or batching time-sensitive commitments;
the lesson is that dependable availability and fewer disruptions can coexist.
[Publisher article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747563219302596).

Horvitz's CHI 1999 mixed-initiative paper considers uncertain goals, attention
cost, refinement and choices among acting, dialogue and inaction. It is a design
framework with a scheduling prototype, not a consumer trial or authorization
policy. Its relevance is choosing a useful form of help rather than maximizing
autonomous action. [Paper](https://erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf).

### Recommendation

Keep five questions separate:

- Does Vesper have relevant familiarity with this situation?
- Is the proposed contribution actually useful?
- Can the supported service deliver it reliably?
- Is this an appropriate moment and channel?
- Does the applicable instruction or agreement authorize this use and effect?

A first-day user may explicitly authorize a supported narrow service. Years of
history do not implicitly authorize monitoring, contact, publication or a new
class of interruption. Nor does user permission make an unsupported capability
reliable. Familiarity should improve timing and fit, sometimes resulting in less
intervention, not a steadily increasing autonomy level.

| Same underlying information | Appropriate treatment to compare |
| --- | --- |
| User asks whether Gallery House works for the meetup | Lead with the sourced departure-access mismatch and a useful alternative |
| User opens Home while the choice matters | Consider appropriate practical prominence alongside other worthwhile value |
| User is discussing photography in Chat | Do not hijack the conversation for ordinary relevance; a genuinely urgent material consequence needs separate justification |
| User is outside the app | Only an authorized, supported, appropriately time-sensitive delivery path can interrupt; saving the link did not start a watch |
| A new cliff comparison is ready | It may be excellent on-visit content without deserving a push |

Opening Home is a deliberate request to receive value. It does not require every
contribution to pass a push-level urgency test. Conversely, a longer generous
Home is not authority to interrupt the person elsewhere.

### Comparison to run

Hold content constant and change delivery moment/channel. Evaluate total work:
prompting, comprehension, interruption, checking, correction and supervision.
Do not equate fewer prompts with less burden or a dismissed contribution with
enduring disinterest. Existing service and notification owners retain control;
no new universal initiative score is proposed.

## 7. Recipient-complete social value

### Evidence

Nonnecke et al.'s 2004 study received 1,188 valid responses from MSN communities,
including 219 nonposters. Many nonposters said reading was sufficient; others
reported uncertainty, poor fit or participation difficulty. The response rate
was 2.3%, and bulletin boards are not contemporary adult friend-sharing.
The durable distinction is that nonposting can be worthwhile receiving or a
symptom of friction; silence alone cannot distinguish them.
[Author-hosted paper](https://www.cis.uoguelph.ca/~nonnecke/research/OnlineLurkersTellWhy.pdf).

Kim et al.'s CSCW 2024 interviews with 29 BeReal users aged 13–18 found value in
ordinary glimpses of friends' lives, but also pressure from timing, reciprocal
posting and constrained authenticity. Qualitative adolescent findings do not
establish adult acceptance or long-term relationship benefits. They challenge
replacing polished-performance pressure with compulsory spontaneity.
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.02883v1).

Castaneto and Aknin's 2025 work analyzed 863 outreach notes and conducted a
further study with 312 participants. Message features did not consistently
explain whether people sent a note. It does not test AI-generated messages or
Vesper's contextual handoffs. A shared object as a natural reason to reconnect
remains a hypothesis, not an established solution to hesitation.
[Paper](https://online.ucpress.edu/collabra/article/11/1/143319/212890/Who-Reaches-Out-to-Old-Friends-and-What-Do-They).

Official adjacent-product descriptions provide useful mechanisms, not outcome
evidence. Locket describes close-friend photos, optional reactions without like
counts, history and chat; therefore casual awareness is not vacant territory.
Partiful says guests can use the web without installing the app, but may need
to log in, and its privacy documentation describes authenticated guest access.
No-install is not synonymous with anonymous or account-free access.
[Locket explanation](https://help.locket.com/en/articles/14225418-my-teen-asked-me-to-get-locket-what-is-it),
[Partiful guest access](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525328-do-my-guests-need-to-download-the-app),
[Partiful privacy](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525648-how-does-partiful-protect-my-data).

### Recommendation

The social product should work for a recipient who never develops a rich Vesper
history. This is already compatible with the
[Multiplayer Strategy](../../travel-agent/docs/product/Multiplayer%20Product%20Strategy.md)
and the [social handoff](claude-design-social-aperture-and-shared-world-exploration-handoff-2026-09-02.md).
The missing work is delivering that benefit consistently, not adding a social mode.

| Material received | Complete first benefit | Optional, not owed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photograph | The actual image, chosen words and intelligible authorship | Reaction, reply or equivalent photograph |
| Recommendation | Exact place and the friend's reason; factual checks distinguished | Keep, directions, private assistance |
| Juxtaposition | Authorized perspectives and a substantive comparison if warranted | Further exploration or a reciprocal story |
| Invitation | Understandable proposal and what accepting means | Profile building or contribution work |
| Material arrangement change | The current details and relevant consequence | Becoming an organizer or habitual user |

Remove unnecessary installation, profile completion, contact upload and
personalization setup. Preserve necessary recipient verification for private
material. A broadly shareable link and a named-recipient projection have
different audience semantics; convenience must not silently substitute one for
the other. These are access-design recommendations, not authorization to publish
private material or build a new external sharing service now.

Preserve considered expression. A prepared caption, polished image or saved song
can be as legitimate as an imperfect cake. The goal is freedom from performance
requirements, not requiring everyone to look unpolished. AI should not rewrite
a modest human contribution into something grander than the sender intended.

**Sender value matters too:** preserve expression without packaging work; let
the recipient inspect relevant facts without repeatedly asking the sender;
reduce coordination after people actually choose to gather. Do not transfer
guest convenience into manual host work copying responses across channels.
Some photographs will still be easier to send through an existing messenger;
Vesper need not host every human conversation to be useful.

### Comparison to run

Compare an existing messenger, a minimal source-preserving Vesper handoff and
that handoff with optional useful assistance. Include a recipient who declines
installation, personalization and reciprocal contribution. Follow a later lookup
and one changed gathering detail. Evaluate both people's burden, enjoyment,
understanding and freedom to stop. No reply can be successful; it is not evidence
of success. A delivered invitation is not an improved friendship.

## 8. Disconfirmation: learn without explaining every failure away

### Evidence

Camuffo et al.'s 2024 replication combined four randomized training trials with
759 firms. Scientific decision-making training increased idea termination and
produced a more focused pattern of pivoting. Proposed mechanisms such as
methodic doubt are interpretations, not a formula for product-market fit. The
relevant lesson is to specify which observations change which beliefs, not to
continually rewrite strategy or delay system work until one loop is proven.
[Open-access paper](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smj.3580).

METR's February 2026 update describes selection and measurement problems in its
later AI-productivity study and declines to treat its newer central estimate as
a reliable current productivity measurement. This is a methods report about
software work, not evidence about Vesper consumers. It supports checking who
participates, what tasks were selected and what work a comparison actually
counts, rather than generalizing from enthusiastic self-reports.
[Research update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/).

Meta's 2025 Retentive Relevance preprint distinguishes interest matching,
worthwhile consumption and intention to return for similar content. It reports
separate survey samples across 18 countries and production experiments. The
target includes next-day platform retention, not durable offline benefit, and
the authors acknowledge missing session/sequence context. It is a reason to
separate these constructs in research, not to insert surveys throughout Vesper
or treat a stated intention as observed return.
[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07621v1).

### Recommendation: diagnose three different failures

1. **Implementation failure:** the intended experience was not delivered.
2. **Experience failure:** the intended experience was delivered but was
   unhelpful, demanding, confusing or poorly timed.
3. **Strategic failure:** competent and understandable experiences still fail
   to earn relevant repeat choice or support a sustainable offer.

Do not use the first category to shield the other two indefinitely. Equally,
do not treat a broken reader or unsupported source as evidence that continuity
or the whole four-root product is unwanted.

| Claim to examine | Concerning pattern | Competing explanation to separate | Decision affected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Casual questions earn entry and return | Good low-burden answers do not earn voluntary choice when the need recurs | Habit/distribution, inadequate competence, unclear benefit | Entry message, supported jobs and acquisition |
| Continuity adds value | History produces callbacks or more repair, not better help or less assembly | Bad retrieval/integration versus irrelevant history | Continuity promise, scope and mechanism |
| Home works without new input | Ordinary visits remain repetitive, thin or unrelated | Supply, selection, presentation and missing source rights | Coverage and production investment before more cards |
| Social supports asymmetric participation | Recipients need equivalent effort or hosts absorb hidden administration | Access/UI failure versus unwanted exchange | Recipient handoff and cross-channel coordination |
| Practical help fits the product | People repeatedly misread checked versus followed, or the useful job exceeds support | Promise, service capability and authority boundary | Service offer and escalation/handoff |
| Consumer funding is viable | Actual purchases/renewals and full serving cost do not support the offer | Wrong cohort, price, recurring benefit or cost structure | Commercial hypothesis and exposure scope |

Specify observations and review conditions before interpreting results, but do
not invent numeric success thresholds from desk research. Account for quality,
assembly/repair burden, context-acquisition burden, perceived usefulness and
actual behavior separately. Paid research participation is not organic adoption.
Returning may reflect confusion; not returning may reflect a finished job.

## 9. Whole-product synthesis and next decisions

The six topics reinforce one another:

- A dependable first payoff makes voluntary contribution credible.
- World supply prevents personal history and friend activity from becoming
  prerequisites for a worthwhile Home.
- Useful continuity lowers later reconstruction without requiring a biography.
- Wanted participation lets assistance feel helpful rather than controlling.
- Recipient-complete sharing lets human material enrich the system without
  requiring equal adoption or labor.
- Appropriate initiative makes those benefits available without an engagement
  obligation; clear disconfirmation keeps the strategy accountable.

This is an explanatory relationship, not a forced funnel or six new services.
Understanding and enjoyment may be complete. Social awareness need not become a
gathering. Practical work need not become learning. The live engine serves
feasibility and adaptation across these experiences; it is neither an optional
travel add-on nor the ranking model for everything on Home.

The most important refinement to the previous recommendation is: **a dependable
floor must be generous enough to be worthwhile, not merely safe enough to ship.**
Correctness, authority and repair are necessary foundations. We still owe people
substance, appropriate initiative, and a reason to choose this particular app.

### Recommended order, without stopping independent engineering

1. Review the current week with two variants: no new contributions after Tuesday,
   and a least-invested recipient. Include weak supply and an unsupported request.
2. Name the actual payoff and avoided work at each encounter; compare equal
   information and end-to-end effort rather than celebrating a fluent answer.
3. Specify initial world/service coverage by use and condition, with freshness,
   rights, fallback and total-cost dependencies. Do not launch a seeding batch
   or choose a public geography on this memo's authority.
4. Resolve the existing continuity and intention packets only where their
   decisions are required. Keep present contracts in force meanwhile.
5. Carry the content and interaction requirements into the existing Home,
   Places, Life, Chat, social and integration owners. Detailed visual composition
   remains with design. No new shell or universal autonomy model is needed.
6. Use newcomer comprehension and repeated real-use research to distinguish
   delight, dependable value, voluntary return and payment. Study participation
   should not turn the production experience into a questionnaire.

Potential receiving amendments, after review, are bounded: a value-floor section
in the Editorial/Surfacing guidance; an ordinary-week supply comparison in the
existing manuscript; wanted-participation and channel-specific initiative pairs;
recipient access/continuation requirements in the social handoff; and explicit
