# Life Behavior Research Round 3

## Entrance, retrieval, return, social lifecycle, source identity, and Continue

## Research question

The jobs-first Life fixture pack now defines **Find, Return, See anew, and
Continue** across six evidence worlds. This round asks what behavior and
authority contracts are required for those jobs to remain useful, effortless,
socially safe, and autobiographically honest:

1. What earns a stable Life entrance rather than a nested, resurfaced, or
   search-only posture?
2. How should a person re-find lived material when they remember context but
   not an exact date, title, or object?
3. When may otherwise valid material return automatically, and when should it
   remain dormant?
4. How should shared memory behave when membership, audience, relationships,
   custody, or a person's life status changes?
5. How can a person later distinguish what they authored, what another person
   contributed, what the record establishes, what Vesper connected, and what
   external sources report?
6. How should accumulated Life material improve a present or future experience
   without becoming a task queue, reminder engine, or invasive nudge system?

Place-relationship semantics remains an adjacent seventh research seam. L06
pressures every topic in this round, but this document does not attempt a full
environmental-psychology taxonomy of Place attachment, familiarity, or
identity.

## Executive finding

The six questions do not collapse into one personalization or ranking model.
Life needs several independent judgments over the same governed record:

```text
governed record
  -> entrance posture: stable | nested | contextual | search-only
  -> retrieval response: target | answer | episode | reconstruction trail
  -> return eligibility: allowed | suppressed | user-requested only | silent
  -> composition eligibility: substance-bearing | thin / rejected
  -> Continue eligibility: use silently | show possibility | offer to carry
                           | remind | prepare | act
```

The compact architecture law is:

> A stable door exists because there is a trustworthy whole to return to. A
> result appears because it helps with this retrieval now. A memory returns
> automatically only when its current use and delivery are authorized. A
> composition exists only when it adds source-grounded substance. A
> continuation becomes consequential only through a separate authority gate.

Evidence may inform all five judgments. No score may allow one judgment to
authorize another.

The parallel research also exposes four orthogonal control planes:

| Control plane | Owns | Must remain independent from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Record authority** | Whether a Source, Plan, Occurrence, Outcome, or relation exists; its author, truth state, and custody | Whether it should return now |
| **Audience authority** | Who may read or reuse which field, at what precision, for what purpose and epoch | Relationship status or engagement |
| **Relationship-safety boundary** | Whether Vesper may connect, address, notify, suggest contact, or traverse a person's social periphery | Source deletion and ordinary ranking |
| **Return policy** | Whether authorized material may be foregrounded automatically, on which surface, for which purpose and duration | Searchability and record truth |

A block is not deletion. Leaving is not Source revocation. Keeping is not
permission to resurface. Unblocking is not reconciliation. Reconciliation is
not restoration of old grants.

Finally, provenance must become a user-facing memory aid rather than inspector
metadata. The minimum claim-local vocabulary is:

- **You said / noticed**;
- **[Name] contributed**;
- **The record shows**;
- **Vesper connected**;
- **Vesper suggests**;
- **External sources report**; and
- **Unresolved / disputed**.

“AI-generated” alone is insufficient because it identifies surface production,
not the origin, author, authority, transformation, or repair owner of a claim.

## Evidence discipline and method

Three parallel research lanes examined:

1. entrance eligibility plus personal re-finding;
2. resurfacing receptivity plus changing social-memory lifecycles; and
3. AI source identity plus prospective continuation.

Each lane read the jobs-first Life fixtures and relevant repository doctrine,
then reviewed primary HCI, CSCW, cognitive-science, privacy, and official
product sources. Recent 2024–2026 work was prioritized where available and
combined with durable foundational research.

This document distinguishes:

- **research evidence** — a cited study or deployed product behavior;
- **repository doctrine** — an accepted or active Vesper position;
- **product recommendation** — a decision inferred from the two; and
- **open decision** — a question requiring fixture execution, prototyping,
  human research, legal review, or policy choice.

The research does not approve screen geometry, a new durable owner, a model
architecture, notification permission, posthumous policy, or implementation
schema names.

## 1. Entrance eligibility

### 1.1 Research synthesis

Event-segmentation research finds that people spontaneously organize activity
into hierarchically nested parts using both physical change and conceptual cues
such as actors' goals. Segmentation affects later memory. See [Zacks and
Swallow](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8721.2007.00480.x).

Autobiographical-memory models similarly distinguish lifetime periods, general
events, and event-specific knowledge. Retrieval often moves through these
levels rather than directly to one detail. See [Conway and
Pleydell-Pearce](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.107.2.261). Personal events
may cluster through causal relation, temporal proximity, and content
similarity, but no one cue is sufficient. See [Brown and
Schopflocher](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9280.00087).

Event boundaries can also be reframed during later retrieval. More distant
events were more open to boundary redefinition in one experimental series,
supporting reversible organization rather than permanent machine segmentation.
See [Flexibility of Event Boundaries in Autobiographical
Memory](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3914309/).

Digital-trace reconstruction can combine heterogeneous material into coherent
episodes and improve recall, but candidate ranking does not make an inferred
episode canonical truth. See [Supporting Human Memory by Reconstructing
Personal Episodic Narratives](https://doi.org/10.1609/icwsm.v16i1.19306).

### 1.2 Decision: categorical posture, not importance score

Entrance eligibility must be implemented as hard semantic gates followed by a
posture decision. Trace count, recent relevance, editorial quality, or
engagement cannot override a failed gate.

#### Gate A — governed existence

The candidate resolves to authorized durable state or a reversible read
projection over authorized state.

Failures include:

- an unadmitted chat mention;
- a model-only thematic similarity;
- a ticket presented as attendance; or
- a revoked contribution.

#### Gate B — return identity

The candidate forms a coherent unit a person could plausibly address: Journey,
Occasion, recurring Place relationship, authored collection, or saved
composition.

Inferred units should use converging cues such as:

- goal or purpose;
- lifecycle;
- causal structure;
- people;
- Place transition;
- supported time boundary;
- authored naming; and
- governed occurrence.

At least two independent cues should support an inferred episode candidate. No
cue is individually decisive, and the resulting boundary remains reversible.

#### Gate C — truth adequacy

The entrance can be titled and previewed without converting uncertainty into
fact.

- A Journey may contain honest gaps.
- An upcoming Occasion may be stable before occurrence because its social
  lifecycle is explicit.
- A Plan-only museum must not resemble a lived episode.
- A recurring Place relationship requires supported occurrences, not saves.

#### Gate D — nonredundant return value

A stable door supports **Return plus at least one additional job**—Find,
Continue, or eligible See anew—without inventing a story. If all value belongs
inside a parent episode, the unit stays nested.

#### Gate E — stability

The candidate remains intelligible when:

- editorial rank changes;
- a generated cover changes;
- current-world relevance disappears; or
- one nonessential Source is deleted.

If value exists only because the object matters today, use contextual
resurfacing rather than a stable Life entrance.

#### Gate F — viewer-safe plurality

The entrance can compile a valid viewer-specific projection. Temporal overlap
or social relevance cannot manufacture a stable Together object.

#### Gate G — repairability

The system can explain membership and support merge, split, detach, rename,
correct, demote, pin, or hide without rewriting constituent authorities.

### 1.3 Posture-routing contract

| Posture | Required semantics |
| --- | --- |
| **Stable Life door** | Passes every gate; has durable or strongly supported return identity; remains useful across time |
| **Nested doorway** | Truthful and useful, but depends on a parent episode, relationship, or authored thread |
| **Contextual resurfacing** | Currently useful because of Place, person, time, availability, condition change, or new composition, but lacks stable independent return identity |
| **Search-only** | Valid atomic Source, Plan, trace, or weak/provisional relation whose complete current job is Find |
| **Pinned shortcut** | Explicit stable access without semantic promotion; retains the object's actual type and truth state |

Explicit user action may override access posture but never truth. “Pin this
ticket” creates a shortcut, not a museum visit. “Keep this Rome thread” may
create an authored collection, not occurrence evidence for every linked item.

### 1.4 Fixture consequences

| Fixture | Recommended posture |
| --- | --- |
| **L01 Europe** | Stable Journey after bounded period, movement, and bases are supported; honest gaps remain |
| **L02 dinner** | Stable Occasion from explicit purpose, time, Place, membership, and lifecycle even when sparse |
| **L03 museum** | Search-only or nested Plan/Source; contextual return only when present value reopens |
| **L04 film/book** | Nested cultural bridge; stable only through authored thread, saved composition, or repeated cross-medium return |
| **L05 Rome–Paris** | Each Journey stays stable; comparison belongs in an addressed Together return region |
| **L06 Red Hook** | Stable Place relationship only after supported, temporally distinct recurrence; repeated saves fail |

### 1.5 Evaluation

Execute each fixture through an evidence ladder:

```text
one trace
  -> three related traces
  -> one supported occurrence
  -> heterogeneous sources
  -> explicit correction
  -> second episode
  -> temporally separated recurrence
  -> authored name or pin
  -> audience revocation
```

Measure stable-door overpromotion and underpromotion, false occurrence,
audience violation, duplicate doors, posture agreement, index churn, successful
return, and repair propagation. False occurrence or audience leakage is a hard
failure regardless of usability.

## 2. Contextual re-finding and mental-model restoration

### 2.1 Research synthesis

Personal-information research repeatedly finds that people search using
partial contextual cues. Stuff I've Seen identified time and people as
important retrieval dimensions across heterogeneous personal material. See
[Stuff I've Seen](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/stuff-ive-seen-a-system-for-personal-information-retrieval-and-re-use/).
Adding personal and public landmarks to a timeline improved search time over
dates alone in a small study. See [Milestones in
Time](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/milestones-in-time-the-value-of-landmarks-in-retrieving-information-from-personal-stores/).

Re-finding is often iterative. People “orienteer” through small contextual
steps instead of formulating one perfect query, reducing query burden and
preserving context. See [The Perfect Search Engine Is Not
Enough](https://doi.org/10.1145/985692.985745). Re-finding can also proceed by
locating the likely information source and then browsing inside it. See [Capra
and Pérez-Quiñones](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0310011).

IRCHIVER's 2025 study found a useful distinction: direct re-finding benefits
from top-matching terminal pages, while restoring prior sensemaking benefits
from intermediary chronological context and the original trail. See
[IRCHIVER](https://doi.org/10.1145/3698204.3716459).

OmniQuery's CHI 2025 work collected 299 realistic questions; 74.4% were
contextual-filter or hybrid rather than direct-content queries. Its context-
augmented approach received higher perceived accuracy than a conventional RAG
baseline in a small evaluation. See
[OmniQuery](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713448).

### 2.2 Decision: target first, context attached

Life search should optimize for both target retrieval and context restoration:

```text
vague cue
  -> likely object, answer, episode, or relationship
  -> recognizable landmarks and truth state
  -> exact original evidence
  -> optional reconstruction of why, what changed, or how it connected
```

A generated answer must not hide the original Source, parent episode, social
scope, or distinction among Plan, Occurrence, Outcome, and current provider
truth.

### 2.3 Query contract

Search accepts combinations of:

- episode or approximate period;
- relative time such as before, after, or during another event;
- person, contributor, or audience;
- Place, route, or spatial relation;
- object, visual feature, or media type;
- exact or approximate authored wording;
- Plan, Occurrence, Outcome, or correction state;
- decision, revision, or adjacent event; and
- intended present or future use.

Representative fixture queries include:

- “The pasta photo after Amalfi.”
- “The ferry we booked but didn't take.”
- “Who was bringing dessert?”
- “What time did we move dinner to?”
- “The museum ticket I never used.”
- “What did the Colosseum make me connect to Troy?”
- “Maya's Paris photo about the heat.”
- “That Red Hook ferry route I saved but never used.”

The system may quietly decompose the query into an inspectable interpretation:

```text
scope: Mine
episode: Europe Journey
relative time: after Amalfi
target: photograph
content cue: pasta
truth constraint: occurred context only
```

Ask only when ambiguity changes audience, occurrence, action, or materially
different high-salience results.

### 2.4 Response selection

| Retrieval job | Lead response |
| --- | --- |
| Known Source or object | Source-first candidate |
| Governed factual question | Direct answer with owner and evidence |
| Vague lived episode | Episode-first result with landmarks |
| “Why did we?” or “What changed?” | Mental-model reconstruction trail |
| Recurring Place or person | Relationship projection through constituent episodes |
| Present-use query | Historical result first, then clearly separated current-world handoff |

Simple deterministic matches should appear immediately. Slower semantic or
generative interpretation may augment them without delaying or replacing them,
similar to Google Photos' current combination of fast classic search with
background Ask Photos processing for complex questions. See [Ask Photos search
update](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/photos/updates-ask-photos-search/).

### 2.5 Result contract

Every primary result needs:

```text
result identity and type
viewer scope
why it matched
relevant time role(s)
truth state
parent episode or relationship
author / contributor
original Source links
before / after / adjacent landmarks
uncertainty or conflict
correction / detach controls
valid Continue actions
```

For direct retrieval, show the best target plus a compact **Around this** trail.
For decision or mental-model restoration, show factors, alternatives, changes,
and Sources in episode order while preserving intended, occurred, and later-
concluded state.

Follow-up queries inherit current episode and viewer scope unless visibly
changed. Search refinements remain ephemeral unless the person explicitly
corrects, keeps, attaches, names, or authors a relation.

### 2.6 Evaluation

Build at least fifty queries balanced across exact retrieval, vague context,
relative time, Plan-versus-Occurrence, attributed social evidence, decision
reconstruction, repeated Place relations, source/composition distinction, and
current-use handoff.

Compare:

1. ranked Source list;
2. direct answer;
3. episode-first result; and
4. target-first result plus context trail.

Measure target success at ranks one and three, time to first recognizable
waypoint, time to verified target, reformulations, wrong-state errors, source
verification, decision-sequence restoration, confidence calibration,
perceived completeness, and correction effort. Delayed tests should use actual
participant material after forty-eight hours, two weeks, and six weeks.

## 3. Resurfacing receptivity and sensitive return

### 3.1 Research synthesis

Retention and return are different human choices. Breakup research found that
people may preserve digital artifacts to respect their past while not wanting
algorithmic reminders, including indirect exposure through mutual friends and
relatives. See [Pinter et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/3359172).

Recent work on digital severance similarly found that participants often
wanted to expunge artifacts immediately after severance, while some became
willing to revisit them later. Severance involved power, control, ambiguous
loss, and mismatched wishes. This does not authorize a model to infer a recovery
stage. See [Dissolving a Digital
Relationship](https://doi.org/10.1145/3788050).

Longitudinal reminiscence research finds enjoyment, embarrassment, privacy
concern, and positive and negative feelings coexisting around old material;
current disclosure wishes may differ from those at creation. See [Jungselius
and Weilenmann](https://doi.org/10.1177/20563051231207850).

Forgetting is therefore a legitimate product operation, not archive failure.
Recent HCI synthesis distinguishes several practices of forgetting rather than
reducing them all to deletion. See [Ankenbauer and
Brewer](https://doi.org/10.1145/3701211) and [Design for
Forgetting](https://doi.org/10.1145/2470654.2466241).

### 3.2 Decision: explicit gates, not inferred emotional readiness

An automatic return requires every gate:

```text
current read authority
  AND valid, non-revoked dependencies
  AND no applicable relationship-safety boundary
  AND no scoped resurfacing exclusion
  AND legitimate present job or substantive value
  AND authorized delivery tier
  AND viewer-safe rendering
```

Failure at any gate yields a quieter posture or silence. The system must not
persuade the person to revisit material or diagnose why they declined.

### 3.3 Return tiers

| Tier | Default rule |
| --- | --- |
| **User-requested retrieval** | Available whenever current read authority survives, including after exclusion from automatic return |
| **Stable or nested Life entrance** | May remain as record navigation unless the person also hides that entrance |
| **Passive Life return** | Requires every gate; low-interruption and immediately dismissible |
| **Contextual continuation** | Requires direct relevance to an active job plus compatible purpose and audience |
| **Home foregrounding** | Higher threshold than Life; avoid ambiguous social or emotionally exposed material |
| **Push / lock screen** | Off by default for retrospective memory; allow only explicit subscription or distinct time-sensitive prospective consequence |

### 3.4 Permitted and forbidden signals

Permitted signals include:

- explicit `show more`, `show less`, `not now`, `never surface`, or saved
  return;
- direct search and user-initiated episode opening;
- current surface and job;
- current authority, membership, dependency, and precision state;
- an explicitly supplied transition or boundary;
- prior rejection scoped to the same Source, person, episode, Place, period,
  treatment, or purpose; and
- whether the return adds present utility rather than recognition alone.

The following must not become resurfacing evidence:

- facial-expression, voice-tone, or emotion recognition;
- inferred grief, trauma, breakup stage, attachment style, or recovery status;
- personality or psychographic labels;
- engagement time as proof that the return was welcome;
- inactivity as evidence of death, estrangement, or readiness;
- a remaining friend or membership edge as permission to interrupt; or
- high historical interaction count as permission to foreground a person.

### 3.5 Scoped suppression grammar

Suppression can apply to:

```text
Source
episode / Occasion / Journey
person
relationship
social periphery
Place
date or period
composition type
delivery surface
purpose
duration
```

The product must distinguish:

- “Don't bring this up.”
- “Don't show me memories with Maya.”
- “Don't use this dinner in recaps.”
- “Hide this period until next year.”
- “Keep it searchable, but not on Home.”
- “Don't suggest contacting anyone from this Occasion.”
- “Remove my contribution from Together.”
- “Delete the original.”

Recommended precedence is:

```text
hard block or safety boundary
  > Source or audience revocation
  > person / relationship / social-periphery shield
  > episode / date / Place / Source resurfacing exclusion
  > composition-class exclusion
  > temporary snooze
  > editorial rank
```

A relationship shield must traverse known dependency paths: direct
contributions, attributed lanes, generated comparisons, covers, Place
suggestions, contact actions, and mutual-event cues. If identity is uncertain,
omit the ambiguous material from automatic returns while preserving authorized
direct retrieval.

### 3.6 Response states

| Response | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Useful** | May modestly affect eligibility for the same treatment and context; creates no preference or recovery claim |
| **Not now** | Temporary suppression with no inferred reason |
| **Less like this** | Viewer chooses a Source, person, episode, Place, period, or composition class to reduce |
| **Never surface this** | Indefinite automatic-return exclusion while direct retrieval remains available unless separately deleted |

Do not ask “Are you ready to revisit this?” or “Have you moved on?” Neutral
control is less invasive and does not demand emotional disclosure.

### 3.7 Fixture consequences

| Fixture | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **L01 Europe** | A person or period shield removes affected covers, social comparisons, route annotations, and contact cues without erasing independent transport or Place history |
| **L02 dinner** | Membership, contribution grant, shared core, personal overlay, safety boundary, and return policy remain separately testable |
| **L03 museum** | Default silence; current utility never becomes regret or completion pressure; “don't bring this up” preserves retrieval |
| **L04 film/book** | Private observation may be excluded from future compositions while cultural Source remains; regenerate when a premise disappears |
| **L05 Rome–Paris** | Revocation removes a lane from search, title, cover, map, comparison, and Continue; block also removes address actions and peripheral suggestions |
| **L06 Red Hook** | Suppressing one person or Occasion preserves independently supported Place history and removes relationship-dependent returns |

### 3.8 Evaluation

Measure low-regret usefulness rather than clicks. Test identical evidence under
direct retrieval, stable navigation, passive Life return, Home foregrounding,
and push.

Primary measures:

- useful, irrelevant, badly timed, and upsetting return rates;
- interruption regret;
- surprise exposure after a boundary was set;
- suppression leakage across dependency paths;
- successful explicit retrieval after “keep, but do not surface”;
- time and steps to set the intended boundary; and
- ability to predict what Vesper will show next.

Unauthorized exposure and known-path suppression leakage must be zero in
deterministic fixtures.

## 4. Social-memory lifecycle

### 4.1 Research synthesis

Shared memory is not pooled memory. Collaborative recall can add detail, but it
also changes vividness, emotionality, language, and later narration. See
[Collaborative Facilitation in Older
Couples](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6288253/) and [Effects of
Collaboration on Autobiographical
Recall](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32072853/). Vesper's attributed lanes
and plural Outcomes are therefore an authority requirement rather than visual
polish.

Deletion and revocation must follow the dependency graph. Longitudinal privacy
research found that deleted posts and accounts could continue to expose
information through replies and residual activity. See [Longitudinal Privacy
Management in Social Media](https://www.usenix.org/conference/soups2016/technical-sessions/presentation/mondal).

Digital legacy is a multi-user lifecycle involving encoding, access, and
dispossession; access does not automatically establish stewardship. See [Doyle
and Brubaker](https://doi.org/10.1145/3610059). Bereavement practices are
heterogeneous, and persistent artifacts may be treasured or painful. See
[Passing On and Putting to
Rest](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/passing-on-and-putting-to-rest-understanding-bereavement-in-the-context-of-interactive-technologies/).

Contextual integrity provides the correct privacy frame: appropriateness
depends on actors, information type, context, purpose, and transmission
conditions, not merely whether something was once shared. See [Nissenbaum,
2004](https://digitalcommons.law.uw.edu/wlr/vol79/iss1/10/).

### 4.2 Decision: four independent state systems

The four control planes in the executive finding must compile at read and use
time. Do not create a universal copied Together archive whose permissions were
true only at creation.

Each contributed field or Source needs at least:

```text
author
data subject(s)
custodian
audience / named recipients
purpose / eligible projections
precision
membership epoch
valid from / until
reshare and export right
post-membership behavior
posthumous behavior, if supplied
revocation owner and scope
dependency lineage
```

### 4.3 Lifecycle matrix

| Transition | Record | Current Together access | Return and Continue | Restoration rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Active member and grant** | Shared core plus attributed contributions and plural Outcomes | Current viewer receives authorized fields | Eligible under return policy | Not applicable |
| **Invitation declined or deferred** | Invitation may remain with owner; no attendance | No participant lane or private context | No “you missed” treatment | Later acceptance creates a new explicit state |
| **Participant leaves** | Historical membership fact may remain | End future writes immediately; historical access follows explicit Occasion policy | Remove active Together and contact cues by default | Rejoin creates a new epoch |
| **Contribution withdrawn** | Author may retain private Source | Remove Source and authored field from former audience | Invalidate dependent returns and compositions | Re-share creates a new grant |
| **Original Source deleted** | Remove custody; retain only independently supported state | Remove Source everywhere | Withdraw unsupported claims, covers, embeddings, and handoffs | Re-import is a new Source unless explicit recovery applies |
| **Audience or precision narrowed** | Exact value may remain for author | Recompile every viewer projection | Precise map/comparison becomes ineligible | New grant creates a new revision |
| **Block / no contact** | Each person retains independently owned record | Stop unauthorized cross-person reads | No address, invite, notification, comparison, contact, or social-periphery traversal | Unblock removes only safety overlay; restores nothing else |
| **Estrangement without block** | Do not infer or rewrite record | Grants remain technically distinct from return wishes | Explicit relationship shield suppresses return | User-led reopening changes return policy, not audience |
| **Reconciliation** | Old history remains accurate | New relationship and audience epoch | No retrospective dump | Old revoked inputs remain absent until re-shared |
| **Account deletion** | Remove account Sources/private Outcomes; preserve other authors' material and independently supported facts | Remove grants originating only from deleted account | Remove deleted inputs and unsupported social cues | Recreation is a new principal absent verified recovery |
| **Verified death with directive** | Follow Source- or account-specific directive | Preserve only stated audience and use | Stop contact prompts, active pushes, and generated speech in the person's voice | Steward executes directive but cannot impersonate |
| **Verified death without directive** | Minimum-disclosure preservation pending policy | Do not unlock private material based on relationship alone | User-requested remembrance only; no automatic push | Requires legal and product policy |
| **Managed export** | Ownership unchanged | Projection reflects export-time authority | Managed link may recompile or close | Control remains only while managed |
| **Screenshot or download** | No new internal owner | Outside enforcement boundary | Never claim successful remote revocation | Fresh internal sharing requires new admission |

### 4.4 Actor rights

| Actor | May do | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source author** | Correct, narrow audience, withdraw projection, delete original subject to policy | Rewrite another person's Outcome or independently supported occurrence |
| **Depicted or named person** | Suppress their representation from Vesper-managed generated/social projections and invoke safety boundary | Delete another person's private original merely by being depicted |
| **Occasion host** | Correct purpose, time, Place, membership, and governed shared state | Claim ownership of participant Sources or private Outcomes |
| **Viewer** | Hide, snooze, exclude from resurfacing, delete own saved composition | Mutate another person's Source or grant |
| **Legacy steward** | Execute explicit legacy directives and limited disposition | Read private material by default, impersonate, or invent consent |
| **Vesper** | Recompile, degrade, or withdraw derivatives under current authority | Preserve a comparison by silently copying revoked material |

Multi-party privacy preferences can conflict. See [Such and
Criado](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.04642). For outward sharing and generated
social treatment, Vesper should apply the most restrictive relevant managed-
projection boundary while preserving each participant's independent private
record. Majority vote does not authorize disclosure.

### 4.5 Causal revocation

When a Source, grant, precision, membership, or safety boundary changes:

1. resolve dependent claims, relations, covers, compositions, search snippets,
   cached views, semantic indexes, and pending handoffs;
2. remove unsupported outputs;
3. regenerate outputs still supported by independent Sources;
4. reduce precision where a coarser authorized claim survives;
5. preserve a minimal internal revocation receipt; and
6. avoid exposing the private reason for withdrawal, block, death, or boundary
   through a tombstone.

### 4.6 Historical access after leaving

The research does not resolve one product choice: whether a former Occasion
member retains a compact historical shared core or loses historical read access
when they leave.

Recommended first contract:

- preserve the historical fact of invitation or membership;
- stop all future updates immediately;
- require an Occasion policy of `historical_shared_core_retained` or
  `current_members_only`;
- never retain access to a withdrawn Source because it was once seen; and
- default ordinary local social Occasions to a compact historical shared core
  while every contribution remains independently revocable.

This recommendation still requires human and policy testing.

### 4.7 Evaluation

Deterministic tests must cover leave, withdrawal, deletion, precision reduction,
block, unblock, reconciliation, account deletion, death with and without a
directive, managed export, and unmanaged copy.

Hard metrics:

| Metric | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| Unauthorized field exposure | **0** |
| Suppression leakage across known dependency paths | **0** |
| Stale derivative survival after revocation | **0** |
| Old-grant restoration after unblock or reconciliation | **0** |
| Attribution loss or reassignment | **0** |
| Synthetic consensus from plural Outcomes | **0** |
| Unverified death transition | **0** |
| Material-input dependency coverage in generated compositions | **100%** |

Human evaluation should test whether people distinguish hide, detach,
withdraw from Together, leave, block, and delete; can predict what former
participants still see; can set one cross-surface relationship boundary; and
understand the limits of export revocation.

## 5. Human, social, record, AI, and external source identity

### 5.1 Research synthesis

Source identity is reconstructed rather than retrieved from a perfect mental
tag. The Source Monitoring Framework describes how people infer origin from
perceptual, contextual, semantic, affective, and cognitive-operation cues;
those judgments are flexible and error-prone. See [Johnson, Hashtroudi, and
Lindsay](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.114.1.3).

AI collaboration makes the problem materially worse. The preregistered 2026
CHI *AI Memory Gap* study tested 184 participants one week after unaided and
AI-assisted ideation. Mixed human/AI workflows produced substantial source-
attribution errors and overconfidence. See [The AI Memory
Gap](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791494).

Interactive provenance can improve perceived ownership and control. HaLLMark
preserved prompts, responses, human text, AI text, AI-influenced text, and a
process timeline in a small creative-writing evaluation. Life should borrow
progressive source history without importing a dense authoring visualization
into ordinary reading. See [HaLLMark](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3641895).

AI interpretation can also manufacture autobiographical meaning. A recent
study found ChatGPT interpretations overemphasized emotion, complexity,
insight, and positive transformation compared with human-authored self-
defining memories. See [AI and autobiographical
narrative](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-06426-y).

Generated treatments may influence later recollection. AI-generated videos of
AI-edited images produced more false recollections than unedited controls in a
preregistered experiment. See [Synthetic Human
Memories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08895).

### 5.2 Decision: provenance is claim-local and persistent

Four questions must remain distinct:

1. Who originated the material or observation?
2. Who authored the visible wording?
3. Who has authority to correct or withdraw the claim?
4. What transformation did Vesper perform?

The minimum visible source-role vocabulary is:

| Visible cue | Meaning | Authority and repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You said / noticed** | Explicit authored observation or meaning | Person may edit, supersede, detach, or release |
| **You captured** | Person-originated Source without implied interpretation | Correct metadata or delete/revoke Source |
| **Maya contributed** | Another person's attributed material | Maya edits/revokes under the granted scope; viewer may hide |
| **The record shows** | Provider, occurrence evidence, or canonical owner state | Correct evidence binding or owner state |
| **Vesper connected** | Grounded comparison, computation, reconstruction, or relation | Challenge relation, exclude input, regenerate |
| **Vesper suggests** | Calibrated hypothesis or present possibility | Reject, narrow, or suppress; no authority over meaning or occurrence |
| **External sources report** | Time-bounded world claim with citations | Refresh, replace citation, mark stale or disputed |
| **Unresolved / disputed** | Evidence cannot establish one answer | Add evidence or preserve uncertainty |

For L04, a compact composition might say:

> **Vesper connected your Rome observation with three external sources.**

Within the composition:

- “Aeneas connects Troy to Rome” — **You noticed**;
- a later historical mechanism — **Vesper connected**;
- supporting scholarship — **External sources report**; and
- a contested transition — **Vesper suggests** or **Unresolved**.

### 5.3 Composition-manifest requirements

Borrow the action-and-ingredient structure of provenance standards such as
[C2PA](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/) without claiming that media
provenance alone solves autobiographical authority.

Material claims need:

```text
claim id
surface-text author
origin role and named human author
epistemic operation
claim-authority tier
supporting ResourceRefs
support relation: quotes | supports | computed_from | inferred_from | refreshes
source span or region
represented time
generated time
freshness or expiry
uncertainty
audience and custody
correction owner
generated revision
exclusions
superseded-by relation
invalidation state
```

The composition header names Vesper authorship, represented period, generated
time, and viewer scope. Material claims expose local source roles. Quotations
retain named authors.

Human editing of generated wording does not silently convert the underlying
inference into authored personal meaning. Saving freezes a composition revision
for return but not current world truth. Sharing may omit private ingredients
only when the remaining projection is still truthful and attribution survives.

Correction targets the smallest authority:

- correct my statement;
- remove another person's contribution from this view;
- challenge Vesper's connection;
- replace an outdated citation;
- retract the occurrence;
- exclude an input;
- forget the interpretation; or
- delete the original.

### 5.4 Fixture consequences

| Fixture | Source-identity requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **L01 Europe** | Separate tickets, supported movements, authored observations, Vesper reconstruction, and refreshed transport truth |
| **L02 dinner** | Preserve named contributions, governed shared core, private Outcomes, and Vesper synthesis |
| **L03 museum** | Show ticket possession, intention, explicit non-attendance, and current provider facts as four different layers |
| **L04 film/book** | Keep original cultural Source, person's observation, Vesper contribution, and external citations visibly distinct |
| **L05 Rome–Paris** | Two attributed human lanes plus one bounded Vesper comparison; never a synthetic group voice |
| **L06 Red Hook** | Distinguish occurrence evidence, explicit evaluation, Vesper longitudinal reading, and refreshed world truth |

### 5.5 Evaluation

Compare:

1. generic AI badge;
2. composition-level label;
3. claim-level source roles; and
4. claim-level roles plus inspectable history.

Test immediately and after one week. Measure a self/other/Vesper/external
confusion matrix, confidence calibration, authorship bleed, generated
interpretation mistaken for occurrence or meaning, correction-target success,
time to decisive Source, and attribution survival after share or export.

A material increase in people later attributing Vesper's inference to
themselves or another participant is a hard failure.

## 6. Continue and prospective use

### 6.1 Research synthesis

Everyday future remembering is broader than dated reminders. A survey and
diary study found time- and event-based intentions, vague upcoming intentions,
persistent goals, conditional intentions, and information meant for future
conversations. See [How to Remember What to
Remember](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/remember-remember-exploring-possibilities-digital-reminder-systems/).

Reminder use is metacognitive. A 2024 study of real-world plans found people
more likely to set reminders for important intentions and when they lacked
confidence they would remember; offloading predicted greater fulfillment. See
[Using Reminders to Support Prospective
Memory](https://doi.org/10.1037/xap0000515). A broader review similarly finds
intention offloading effective but not perfectly calibrated. See [Intention
Offloading](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13423-022-02139-4).

Relevance and interruption cost are separate. Microsoft's Jogger modeled
forgetting probability, situational relevance, and interruption cost
independently. See [Jogger](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/jogger-models-for-context-sensitive-reminding/).

Research on AI-powered work reminders found people wanted immediate response,
scheduled work, saving without a time, acknowledgement without action, and
support for interdependent tasks—not one universal `Do / Snooze / Done` state.
See [AI-powered reminders in Microsoft
Viva](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/AI-powered-reminders-CSCW-2024.pdf).

Memory-grounded future suggestions may feel personal and novel, but recent
studies such as Resonance required substantial daily journaling and imagination
work. Vesper should borrow grounded novelty and actionability while rejecting
compulsory reflection. See [Resonance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24145).

Model prospective memory remains fragile. TriggerBench reports degraded recall
under long context and overloaded triggers plus an “always remind” precision-
recall failure mode. Production Continue therefore needs deterministic state
and contrastive negative tests, not a model rereading an undifferentiated
memory context. See [TriggerBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23459).

### 6.2 Decision: Continue is an eligibility-gated handoff

Past material may quietly improve Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion. It
becomes a reminder, message, commitment, booking, or delegated action only
through explicit intent or the relevant existing authority.

Silence is a first-class Continue outcome.

### 6.3 Trigger taxonomy

| Trigger | Example | Default posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Explicit carried intention** | “Keep this for my parents' visit” | May become Plan or reminder after trigger confirmation |
| **Time or deadline** | Ticket release or reservation window | Notify only under explicit intent or commitment authority |
| **Place or situation** | Near Red Hook with open afternoon | Quiet Home/Places possibility; push only when requested |
| **Person or conversation** | Seeing Maya after she asked about a recipe | Bounded cue in Chat or Occasion under current relationship scope |
| **Occasion or activity boundary** | Planning Rome return or beginning grocery trip | Show relevant material inside active workflow |
| **Changed-world condition** | Missed exhibition opens nearby; ferry resumes | Reopen only after freshness verification |
| **Capability or analogy transfer** | Coastal mobility insight helps a New York weekend | Compact possibility, never automatic task |
| **Recurring relationship or practice** | Several supported Red Hook episodes | Contextual return without streak or identity judgment |
| **Unresolved prior Plan** | Ticket retained after non-attendance | Dormant/searchable until present relevance; no guilt |

### 6.4 Delivery ladder

```text
1. use silently inside an already-open destination
2. show a complete-value possibility on Home or Places
3. offer to carry into a Plan, reminder, Occasion, or explicit intention
4. remind under explicit trigger and authority
5. prepare a route, message, options, or change preview
6. act only under separately delegated authority
```

An inferred possibility cannot jump directly to reminder, commitment, message,
booking, or historical Outcome. Ignoring a possibility does not mean it was
known, unwanted, completed, or revoked.

### 6.5 Cross-root Continue envelope

```text
origin root
origin ResourceRef and revision
selected episode or object
origin lens and viewer scope
represented time range
authorized context refs
source role and author per material input
authored intent, if any
trigger type and basis
eligibility reason
destination root
freshness requirements
audience and disclosure boundary
allowed authority: inspect | propose | prepare | act
completion semantics
suppression and resurfacing history
return-receipt destination
```

The destination must:

1. re-resolve current world truth;
2. preserve historical truth without back-writing it;
3. recheck audience and relationship permissions;
4. distinguish suggestion, intention, Plan, and commitment;
5. preview any new consequence and name its durable owner;
6. return a receipt only when governed state changed; and
7. leave Life unchanged when the possibility is ignored or dismissed.

### 6.6 Fixture consequences

| Fixture | Continue requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **L01 Europe** | “Use this in New York” stays a possibility until the person creates a Plan or Occasion |
| **L02 dinner** | “Ask Maya” is a bounded, user-authorized cue rather than generated relational labor |
| **L03 museum** | Reopen only under current goal or refreshed provider relevance; never turn the miss into homework |
| **L04 film/book** | Carry original Source, authored observation, Vesper claims, and citations rather than inferred identity |
| **L05 Rome–Paris** | Bounded share retains attribution and no underlying private histories; block removes social actions |
| **L06 Red Hook** | Places refreshes service, weather, hours, and opportunity; new Plan does not rewrite earlier record |

### 6.7 Evaluation

Use deterministic fixtures followed by a four-to-six-week diary or micro-
randomized field study. Compare silence, silent destination use, compact in-app
possibility, explicit carry-forward offer, requested reminder, and unsolicited
push.

Measure:

- opportunity precision and recall;
- false-alarm and intrusiveness rate;
- “felt like homework” rate;
- possibility mistaken for commitment;
- useful-without-action rating;
- conversion into authorized Plan or Occasion;
- suppression fidelity;
- stale-world error;
- context and source-role survival across handoff;
- historical-mutation error;
- social boundary violations;
- repeated-dismissal burden; and
- appropriate silence.

Do not optimize click-through, reminder completion, or notification engagement
as proxies for human value.

## 8. Architecture requirements exposed by the research

These are behavioral and read-model requirements, not authorization for
specific tables or services:

1. **Entrance-posture evaluator** with semantic hard gates, viewer scope,
   explanation, and reversible posture history.
2. **Contextual Life search** over multiple time roles, people, Places,
   episodes, media, truth states, authorship, decisions, and relative landmarks.
3. **Target-plus-context result projection** with parent episode, why matched,
   Source trail, uncertainty, and correction.
4. **Return-policy compiler** across Source, episode, person, relationship,
   social periphery, Place, period, composition, surface, purpose, and duration.
5. **Relationship-safety overlay** independent of audience, membership, record
   custody, and ordinary return ranking.
6. **Audience and membership epochs** evaluated at read and use time.
7. **Causal dependency and invalidation graph** reaching compositions, covers,
   snippets, semantic indexes, caches, exports, and pending handoffs.
8. **Claim-local source roles** and a composition manifest with typed
   ingredients, transformations, revisions, and correction ownership.
9. **Continue envelope** that carries source identity, scope, trigger,
   freshness, allowed authority, completion semantics, and return receipt.
10. **Deterministic prospective state** with positive and contrastive negative
    trigger tests rather than model-only long-context recall.

### Product and interaction

- What minimum converging evidence makes an inferred Journey independently
  nameable without explicit authorship?

- How much **Around this** context restores a mental model without recreating

- Which Continue categories may enter Home without explicit carry-forward?

- Does leaving preserve a compact historical shared core or end historical
  read access?
- Does leaving also withdraw prior contributions, or must that remain separate?
- How far must social-periphery suppression traverse before false suppression
  becomes disproportionate?
- What veto should a depicted person have over another participant's private
  episode view versus managed generated/social projections?
- What is the posthumous default without an explicit directive?
- How should minors' material be revalidated when they reach adulthood?
- How should legally retained but product-inaccessible data be represented?
- Should managed exports close entirely after revocation or degrade to
  remaining authorized inputs?

## Compact product standard

> Life should make the person's accumulated world easy to enter, find, return
> to, understand anew, and carry forward. It must do so without promoting every
> trace, guessing emotional readiness, reviving expired relationships,
> obscuring who contributed what, or converting the past into a task queue.
