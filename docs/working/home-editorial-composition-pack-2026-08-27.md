# Home Editorial Composition Pack

## Decision

This pack defines one recognizable Home across four radically different lived
situations:

1. the first week back in New York;
2. live in Rome under heat and movement pressure;
3. the private and shared choreography of a consequential Occasion; and
4. flight disruption and recovery.

The comparison is the product work. No one scenario is the product, and no
scenario is a request to prove one isolated behavior loop. Together they force
Home to handle possibility, live reality, multiplayer authority, continuity,
and recovery while preserving the roles of Chat, Places, Life, Plan, and
Occasion.

This is a content-and-interaction composition specification. It is not a final
visual-language decision, backend schema authorization, route migration, or
production rollout plan. Exact schedules, availability, weather, prices,
historical claims, and provider state are fixture requirements until verified
from authoritative data.

The broader generative model now lives in
[Home Possibility Field and Generative Opportunity Portfolio](home-possibility-field-and-generative-opportunity-portfolio-2026-08-28.md).
That model corrects an over-application of restraint in this pack: one item may
dominate attention, but the full page may deliver several substantively
different, low-pressure possibilities.

## 1. The shared Home contract

Home is a stable editorial shell whose emphasis and native instrument change
with the current job. It does not continually rearrange its navigation or
invent a new interface. The learned reading order is:

```text
world read
  -> one dominant stage
  -> bounded things in motion
  -> a diversified possibility field
  -> attributed relational movement when authorized
  -> earned continuity and recognition
  -> stable doors into owned depth
```

Every region is existence-gated. Home has a **strict demand budget and a
generous possibility budget**. During live pressure, Home may be only the world
read, stage, one affected-people or receipt row, and stable doors. During a
quiet Saturday opening, several distinct registers may coexist because they
deliver value on view and none demands urgent action. This is not a quota by
domain: a route, mechanism, social mosaic, practice, and future horizon may be
less repetitive than five recommendations from the same family.

### 1.1 First-viewport rule

The first viewport should contain:

- the complete world read;
- the complete dominant claim or operational state;
- the primary action, if one is authorized; and
- enough of the next register to imply that Home contains a wider life.

The user should never need to open the dominant unit to learn why it matters.
Opening gives depth, alternatives, evidence, or action—not the withheld answer.

### 1.2 The unit brief

Every rendered Home unit must resolve this receipt before copy or geometry:

| Field | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Which canonical Place, Plan, Occasion, Commitment, Source, Occurrence, Outcome, or relationship projection owns the truth? |
| **Viewer scope** | Why may this person see it, and which details remain private or audience-bounded? |
| **Evidence** | Which authorized Sources, canonical state, attributed contribution, and current-world facts support it? |
| **Already known** | What did the person explicitly say, import, see, receive, or previously learn? |
| **Vesper contribution** | What new reconstruction, explanation, distinction, social mosaic, or prepared consequence did Vesper add? |
| **Why now** | What current condition, commitment, person, Place, or opening makes it relevant? |
| **Native form** | Which instrument makes the relationship perceptible with the least work? |
| **Ending** | Is value complete on view? If not, what one optional or authority-required consequence follows? |
| **Destination** | Which root or owner receives depth without losing context? |
| **Degradation** | What remains if freshness, media, confidence, or authority is missing? When does the unit disappear? |

### 1.3 Ranking order

Home resolves hard obligations before editorial opportunity:

```text
safety or stranded-state recovery
  > expiring provider or movement consequence
  > affected-person decision or protected commitment
  > current physical guidance
  > grounded present possibility
  > earned social opening
  > retrospective editorial contribution
  > factual register
```

This is a precedence model, not a universal score. A low-confidence recovery
claim may lose to a verified current route. A retrospective contribution may
rise when it directly changes tonight. No content rises merely because it is
new, recent, beautiful, or generated.

## 2. Composition A — home in New York, life already moving forward

### Situation

It is Thursday, August 27. The person has returned from Nice, Sorrento, Capri,
Amalfi, and Rome. The trip produced tickets, reservations, photographs, menu
questions, explicit observations, shared artifacts, and parallel friend
traces. There is no unresolved travel emergency. The weekend in New York is
the present; Europe may contribute without owning the screen.

This composition is one prospective baseline. The
[Post-Return New York Home Possible Worlds Gallery](post-return-new-york-home-possible-worlds-gallery-2026-08-28.md)
holds this evidence constant across six different editorial theses so A2's
route-led solution does not silently become the universal returned Home.

### Full scroll

#### A1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · THURSDAY
Home again. Saturday still has room.
```

This line is admissible only if calendar and commitment evidence support the
open window. It describes the present situation without diagnosing post-trip
emotion.

#### A2. Dominant stage — prepared New York possibility

```text
ONE GOOD WAY BACK INTO NEW YORK

Wall Street ferry -> Red Hook waterfront -> dinner nearby
Saturday afternoon into evening
```

Native form: a compact route field with departure window, current service,
weather fit, walking load, one grounded dinner continuation, and an honest
return window. The ferry and dinner are not recommendation cards stacked
together; they are one feasible experience shape.

| Brief field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Prospective local Plan shape; Places owns spatial truth until the person commits |
| Evidence | Actual open window, current ferry service, weather, route, availability, authorized place history |
| Already known | The person is home and has an open Saturday |
| Vesper contribution | Composed a feasible half-day across transit, Place quality, pace, and dinner rather than returning a venue list |
| Why now | It gives the first weekend home a concrete aperture without creating an obligation |
| Form | Route field with one continuation |
| Ending | Complete on view; ignore creates no task |
| Action | **Open Saturday** |
| Destination | Places, preserving route, time, and candidate context |
| Degradation | If live service or availability is weak, render a bounded neighborhood aperture without operational claims; otherwise remain silent |

The surface never explains this as “you like ferries” or “you experience
travel through transitions.” Fit is expressed by the quality of the prepared
possibility.

#### A3. In motion — current life only

Render zero to two compact rows for real local commitments, people, or recovery
items. Examples must come from current state, not be invented to make the page
look inhabited.

```text
[Friday commitment] · settled / changed / open
[Weekend opening with a person] · no decision yet
```

If there are no honest rows, this region disappears. The trip does not enter
In motion merely because it was recent.

#### A4. Continue here — culinary explanation with present transfer

```text
THE SAUCE DIFFERENCE WAS PARTLY A TIMING DIFFERENCE

Three photographed dishes use a glossy emulsion rather than sauce poured over
finished pasta. Starch-rich water and finishing the noodles in the pan explain
part of the texture you noticed.
```

Native form: three-image evidence strip, one annotated mechanism, and one
bounded New York continuation. The mechanism must be source-grounded and the
photographs must actually support the visual claim.

| Brief field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Source set plus personal Place/food continuity; no identity claim |
| Already known | The person explicitly noticed that pasta tasted different |
| Vesper contribution | Explains a mechanism and shows where it recurs in the person's evidence |
| Why now | The distinction can change a meal, cooking attempt, or what becomes visible in New York |
| Form | Annotated comparison, not prose card |
| Ending | Explanation is complete on view |
| Optional action | **See the technique here** |
| Destination | One scoped Places result or Chat for “show me how to cook it” |

This unit competes with every other return. It is not guaranteed a slot because
it passes the editorial bar in isolation.

#### A5. With people — authorized Rome and Paris mosaic

```text
TWO CITIES, THE SAME EVENING

ROME                         PARIS
[authorized trace]           [authorized friend trace]
[one grounded contrast neither person had assembled]
```

The contribution must be a real comparison—river use, heat adaptation, street
occupation, changed plans, food, or movement—not the observation that two
friends were in Europe.

| Brief field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Two separately authored Sources plus a viewer-safe social projection |
| Viewer scope | Both contributors authorized the material for this viewer and this use |
| Vesper contribution | Aligns two partial perspectives on a dimension neither person could observe alone |
| Form | Attributed parallel lanes |
| Ending | Complete on view; no reply debt |
| Optional action | **Send this to [friend]**, followed by human-authored message approval |
| Destination | Life for provenance; Chat only when the person chooses to compose a message |

#### A6. From the journey — one evidence-first reconstruction

```text
THE ONLY CROSSING RECORDED FROM DEPARTURE TO ARRIVAL

Sorrento -> Amalfi by ferry
[verified ticket] · [timestamped sequence] · [known gap, if any]
```

Native form: a horizontal sequence of ticket, departure, coastline, and
arrival. Vesper contributes the reconstruction, not a psychographic verdict.
The action **Open the crossing** enters Life.

Only one of A4, A5, or A6 should normally compete with the dominant stage or
make a high-demand request. Multiple returns may coexist in distinct supporting
registers when each is complete on view, non-redundant, and the portfolio
remains legible. Production composition still applies admission, diversity,
attention, and depth budgets; it does not impose a one-return ceiling.

#### A7. Factual Europe register

```text
NICE -> SORRENTO -> AMALFI -> ROME
[verified movement ledger] · [matched Sources] · [unresolved gap]
```

This is intentionally quiet and factual. It does not pretend that counts or
chronology are insight. **Europe in Life** opens the source-backed register,
correction, and provenance controls.

#### A8. Stable doors

- **All plans and occasions**
- **Open New York in Places**
- **Europe in Life**
- object-scoped **Talk about this** only where discussion adds something

### Suppressed in this state

- “What do you want to remember?” and every other reflection prompt;
- a trip-wide hero merely because the trip is recent;
- the Aeneas thread when another return contributes more today;
- raw photo counts or “places that changed you” copy;
- private relationship interpretation; and
- any New York event, route, or opening that has not been freshly verified.

## 3. Composition B — live Rome, practical reality and culture in one hierarchy

### Situation

The couple has left the Colosseum in August heat. A later commitment exists,
walking conditions are worse than the static plan assumed, and both people are
affected. The person has already connected *The Odyssey*, Troy, Aeneas, and
Rome; repeating that association would add nothing.

### Full scroll

#### B1. World read

```text
ROME · 8:12 PM
The heat changed the route, not the evening.
```

The second line is justified only when the later commitment remains feasible.
If the plan is no longer feasible, Home says so directly.

#### B2. Dominant stage — live movement instrument

```text
DINNER STILL WORKS

18 min walking · water and shade after 6 min · 11 min margin
```

Native form: current route with the committed destination, one relief stop,
remaining margin, and one fallback. It is not a paragraph and not a generic
“nearby” card.

| Brief field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Current Plan commitment plus Places-owned route truth |
| Evidence | Location, time, heat, walking route, opening/access, booking time, affected people |
| Vesper contribution | Recomputed a lower-burden route while preserving the evening's commitment |
| Why now | Delay or omission changes the lived outcome |
| Form | Live route and margin instrument |
| Ending | Value begins on view; navigation requires invocation |
| Action | **Open the shaded route** |
| Destination | Places/navigation with Plan destination and participant context preserved |
| Degradation | If live location or route freshness is weak, show the confirmed commitment and state that route conditions need refresh; never fabricate margin |

#### B3. Still to come — temporal rows

```text
[time] · dinner · settled
After · open · no decision needed
```

The rows reassure without inviting extra planning. Tapping dinner opens its
Plan/Occasion owner. The open period enters Places only when the person invokes
it.

#### B4. Together — affected people, not inferred mood

Show two-person Occasion identity and the shared route consequence. The strip
may say that both people receive the reroute or that one participant is meeting
later. It may not say either person is tired, annoyed, excited, or grateful
unless they authored that state for this audience.

#### B5. While here — one cultural contribution

```text
AUGUSTUS TURNED A TROJAN EXILE INTO ROMAN STATECRAFT

The Aeneas story was older than Augustus. His regime joined it to Julian
ancestry, Virgil's epic, and public monuments that made a family origin story
part of imperial order.
```

This is eligible only after authoritative historical verification and only
when it extends rather than repeats what the person already noticed.

Native form: a compact bridge among Aeneas, the *Aeneid*, lineage, and one
visible Roman monument or object. The full sources and Place depth belong in
Places; alternative interpretation belongs in Chat.

Under movement pressure, B5 disappears. Its quality does not outweigh the
attention cost of obstructing the moment.

#### B6. Stable doors

- **Tonight's plan**
- **Rome around you**
- object-scoped **Why this route?** or **Ask about Aeneas**

### Suppressed in this state

- broad “things to do in Rome” inventory;
- a tour or cultural lesson requiring setup, headphones, completion, or quiz;
- retrospective Italy content;
- a shared poll about an obvious low-burden reroute; and
- any statement that the system knows how either person feels.

## 4. Composition C — a protected Occasion without setup or leakage

### Situation

The founder has arranged a private proposal evening around a Capri day. A
boat, captain, photographer, flowers, light, dinner, transfers, and surprise
must cohere. The partner participates in the shared day but must not see the
protected anchor. The Occasion emerged through Chat and imported commitments;
neither person created an Occasion-specific profile or filled out a workflow.

This composition requires two viewer-safe projections of the same underlying
day.

### Owner's private Home

#### C1. World read

```text
CAPRI · TUESDAY
The evening is protected. Keep the day light.
```

“Protected” is private owner copy. It never enters the partner's Home, shared
Plan, notification, or group explanation.

#### C2. Dominant stage — guardrail, not checklist

```text
PROTECT TONIGHT, NOT THE CHECKLIST

Morning and lunch still fit.
Leave Anacapri by [verified time] to preserve rest, changing, and transfer
margin before the private commitment.
```

Native form: a day ribbon with **settled**, **flexible**, and **protected**
segments. The private commitment appears only as a protected block in the
owner view. The system subtracts the Blue Grotto or another optional excursion
when it would threaten the evening; it does not celebrate a fuller itinerary.

| Brief field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Private Plan/Commitment nested within a shared day and consequential Occasion |
| Evidence | Reservations, provider confirmations, transfer times, light, weather/sea state, privacy, current pace |
| Already known | The founder created the proposal and supplied its dependencies |
| Vesper contribution | Reconciles dependencies, identifies the last safe transition, and prepares a lighter day |
| Why now | One optional daytime choice can materially weaken the Occasion |
| Form | Protected day ribbon and margin receipt |
| Ending | The read is complete; changing the shared day requires appropriate authority |
| Action | **Open today's shape** |
| Destination | Private Plan/Occasion owner; shared changes preview minimum-safe wording before mutation |
| Degradation | If provider or sea-state truth is stale, show which dependency needs reconfirmation; do not claim the evening is safe |

#### C3. In motion — private coordination receipt

```text
EVENING TEAM
Captain · photographer · concierge
[confirmed states only] · [one unresolved handoff, if any]
```

This is a coordination receipt, not a social feed. Provider identities and
private notes appear only to the owner and only as needed. A change enters Chat
or the private Occasion with the current receipt attached.

#### C4. With the partner — minimum-safe shared state

The owner sees what the partner can see:

```text
SHARED DAY
Morning · lunch · return window
No claim about time outside the shared plan
```

This projection is valuable because it lets the owner inspect the privacy
boundary. It does not reveal the protected reason for ending the shared day at
that return window.

#### C5. World opening — intentionally absent

No cultural story or “one more beautiful place” is promoted merely because
Capri contains one. Home's contribution in this state is protecting the
experience and returning attention to the partner and the place.

### Partner's Home

```text
CAPRI · TUESDAY
Morning and lunch are settled. The route back stays easy.
```

The partner may see the shared day, current route, weather, and mutually known
commitments. They do not see the private boat, photographer, flowers,
proposal-specific pacing reason, or provider coordination. If the owner changes
the shared day, the partner sees the resulting ordinary consequence and the
minimum-safe explanation—not the private cause.

Minimum-safe projection permits omission, not deception. Vesper must never
label time “open,” imply that no commitment exists, or give operational advice
that conflicts with protected truth merely to preserve a surprise.

### After the proposal

Home does not immediately ask what the moment meant or request a recap. It may
show a practical after-state when useful:

- dinner timing and route;
- confirmed photographer delivery;
- a thank-you or gratuity task the founder explicitly intended;
- a factual receipt of what changed; or
- silence.

Plural private Outcomes remain in each person's Life only when each person
authors or accepts them. A beautiful shared occurrence does not authorize one
synthetic romantic voice.

### Suppressed in this state

- any private anchor in group-safe copy;
- an Occasion setup form or partner profile;
- repeated confirmations that create more coordination labor;
- cultural content competing with the protected transition;
- inferred partner emotion; and
- a mandatory afterglow, caption, reflection, or share.

## 5. Composition D — flight disruption and recovery

### Situation

The Rome-to-New York flight is cancelled. A reroute may exist through Chicago,
but seats, baggage, lodging, meals, passenger rights, and a work interview do
not all share the same authority or state. The product must restore control
before offering interpretation or memory.

### Full scroll

#### D1. World read

If no route is confirmed:

```text
ROME FIUMICINO · DEPARTURE DAY
AA235 is cancelled. The fastest credible route home is not confirmed yet.
```

If a route is held but not ticketed:

```text
ROME FIUMICINO · DEPARTURE DAY
A route home is held. Seats and baggage are still unconfirmed.
```

The wording mirrors provider truth. “Held,” “confirmed,” “ticketed,” and
“suggested” are never interchangeable.

#### D2. Dominant stage — recovery sequence

```text
GET HOME FIRST

1  Route      [held / ticketed / unresolved]
2  Bags       [confirmed transfer / collect / unknown]
3  Tonight    [hotel and meal state]
```

Native form: a dependency sequence with source and freshness for each row. The
instrument may prepare one best alternative, but confirmation remains explicit
when it changes tickets, money, another person's travel, or a provider
commitment.

| Brief field | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Flight commitment, recovery Plan, provider records, and affected travelers |
| Evidence | Airline status, ticket stock, seat state, baggage instruction, lodging/meal policy, current time and location |
| Already known | The person knows the flight was cancelled |
| Vesper contribution | Reconciles fragmented provider and policy truth into the order of operations and one prepared path home |
| Why now | Delay, mistaken certainty, or wrong ordering has immediate cost |
| Form | Dependency sequence with typed state |
| Ending | Current state is useful on view; consequential choice requires explicit confirmation |
| Action | **Review the route home** or **Confirm held route**, depending on authority |
| Destination | Recovery Plan owner; Chat remains available for negotiation or provider interaction |
| Degradation | Show confirmed cancellation and the next authoritative contact; never fill missing seat, bag, hotel, or rights state with model inference |

#### D3. Affected people — shared consequence

Show who is traveling together, whether the recovery path is common, and which
decision requires both people. Do not reveal one person's private work or
financial constraints in the shared explanation. One rich owner may manage the
recovery while the other receives thin, accurate state and the decision that
affects them.

#### D4. Outside the trip — one prepared work consequence

```text
INTERVIEW
The original time no longer works with the held arrival.
[concise human-voiced reschedule draft]
```

The first line is a deterministic conflict if the calendar and arrival state
support it. Vesper may draft the message, but the person remains the author and
sender. If the arrival is not confirmed, the draft says so rather than
inventing a new time.

#### D5. Receipts and rights — bounded utility

```text
KEEP FOR RECOVERY
Airline notice · hotel · meals · ground transport
```

This is a source checklist with captured/needed state, not an expense story.
Passenger-rights or compensation guidance must use current authoritative
sources and jurisdiction; it opens in Chat or a receipt owner rather than
becoming confident Home prose.

#### D6. World opening and retrospective content — absent

Rome history, the proposal, food, trip reconstruction, and social comparison
do not appear while the recovery sequence is materially unresolved. Once home,
Life may reconstruct the actual route and preserve claim evidence. Home does
not convert disruption into a dramatic travel story while the person is still
living it.

#### D7. Stable doors

- **Recovery plan**
- **Travel documents and receipts**
- **Ask Vesper**
- **Trip and people**

### Suppressed in this state

- cultural or memory content;
- generic airline-rights prose without current jurisdictional sourcing;
- a route labeled confirmed before ticketing;
- several competing alternatives after one credible path has been prepared;
- silent action on tickets, money, employer communication, or another traveler;
  and
- “silver lining” or emotional coaching language.

## 6. What stays stable and what changes

| Situation | Dominant human job | Stage form | Supporting density | Editorial posture | Primary handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New York return | Re-enter present life through one grounded possibility | Prepared route/experience shape | Rich but bounded | Present first; one or two earned returns | Places; Life for provenance |
| Live Rome | Move and preserve the evening under current conditions | Live route and margin instrument | Narrow | Culture may appear only below practical reality | Places/navigation; Plan |
| Protected Occasion | Preserve choreography, privacy, and shared ease | Viewer-scoped day ribbon and coordination receipt | Narrow/private | Subtraction and silence are contributions | Private Occasion/Plan; Chat for change |
| Disruption | Restore control across dependencies and affected people | Recovery sequence | Minimal | Utility and authoritative truth only | Recovery Plan; Chat/provider action |

Stable across all four:

- Home begins with current-world orientation;
- one stage dominates;
- the claim and value are visible before a tap;
- rows account for other durable state without competing;
- people appear through affected state and attribution, not activity metrics;
- every action names the owner it will enter or change;
- one object may project into several roots without duplicating its truth;
- privacy, authorship, provenance, correction, and freshness survive every
  projection; and
- silence is allowed at the unit, region, and whole-contribution level.

Changes by situation:

- the native instrument;
- how much of the wider life remains visible;
- whether interpretation is admissible;
- whether the next step is read-only, user-invoked, confirmation-bound, or
  automatically applied under prior authority; and
- how aggressively freshness failure collapses the composition.

## 7. Component grammar extracted from the four compositions

The target system needs a bounded vocabulary of semantic instruments. “Card”
is presentation geometry, not the grammar.

### 7.1 Stable shell

1. **World read:** context, time, and one bounded authored orientation.
2. **Stage frame:** stable learned location for the dominant instrument.
3. **Section transition:** labels the next human job without repeating feed
   marker-rule-card cadence.
4. **Stable doors:** Plans/Occasions, Places, Life, and contextual Chat.

### 7.2 Stage instruments

| Instrument | Jobs | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prepared possibility** | make an open period immediately inhabitable | New York half-day route |
| **Live movement** | preserve a commitment under current physical conditions | Rome shaded route and margin |
| **Occasion guardrail** | protect pacing, privacy, and dependencies | proposal-day ribbon |
| **Recovery sequence** | resolve a breakdown in the correct order | cancelled-flight path home |
| **Bounded decision** | expose one material tradeoff among affected people | route or shared commitment choice |
| **Editorial return** | reconstruct or explain when it genuinely outranks other work | evidence-first journey or mechanism |

### 7.3 Supporting instruments

- **State row:** owner identity, concrete state, optional count/depth door.
- **Condition receipt:** only conditions that change the current judgment.
- **Spatial field:** route, reachability, area, stop, or bounded fork.
- **People strip:** affected participants, contribution, authority, or shared
  state without feed metrics.
- **Evidence comparison:** two or more Sources plus the mechanism or difference
  Vesper contributes.
- **Social mosaic:** separately attributed perspectives in parallel.
- **Sequence/register:** factual reconstruction, dependencies, or source-backed
  chronology.
- **Quiet passage:** short prose only when prose is the smallest adequate form.

### 7.4 Current implementation leverage

The current app already contains useful primitives, but their semantic owners
must change before they can constitute the new Home.

| Target role | Nearest implemented source | Reuse judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World read and root rails | `TripsNowBand`, Trips/Places mast and floating-root chrome | Reuse layout and motion grammar; remove Trip/Plans semantic ownership |
| Dominant stage and receipts | `TripsStackCrown`, `TripsCrownReceiptBody`, `TripsConditionsBand` | Reuse typed receipt families; replace trip-branded universal crown and generic ranked-card assumption |
| In-motion rows | `AlsoInPlayCard`, `LocalPlansCard`, `TripsOpenLoopsCard`, stack rows | Reuse compact row anatomy and owner-specific doors |
| Spatial field | `TripDayMapCard`, `PlacesMapCanvas`, Places route/card primitives | Reuse map and context handoff; Places remains spatial authority |
| People | `TripsGroupSection`, `TripsFacepile`, Places `FriendFeedCard` | Reuse identity primitives; add viewer-safe affected-state and attributed social mosaic |
| Editorial depth | `TripCompanionCard`, Places editorial renderers | Reuse reading hierarchy only after contribution and known-to-person admission |
| Continuity and register | `TripsHomeTrail`, Places memory/register renderers | Reuse low-elevation geometry; Life ownership and correction path are new |

New work implied by the pack:

- a Home root shell distinct from the Plans index;
- viewer-safe Home composition over canonical objects;
- contribution-brief admission before editorial rendering;
- native social-mosaic and evidence-comparison treatments;
- a Life destination for Sources, reconstruction, provenance, correction, and
  plural Outcomes;
- recovery and protected-Occasion stage treatments; and
- lossless cross-root context handles.

Do not carry forward:

- one Trip-branded crown as the universal Home object;
- repeated equal-weight card sections;
- prompt cards whose value appears only after user labor;
- `TripFeel` or other unsolicited character reads;
- generic recommendation carousels; or
- client-side reranking that disagrees with the composition authority.

## 8. Conceptual composition contract

Implementation should begin from the canonical object, not from a universal
job resolver or arbitrary component tree:

```text
canonical owner + revision
  -> lifecycle and current truth
  -> viewer, audience, and authority
  -> admitted current job
  -> editorial/consequence receipt
  -> root-native treatment key
  -> native client rendering
```

A conceptual Home unit needs:

```yaml
id: stable_projection_id
owner_ref: canonical_object_reference
owner_revision: revision_or_content_hash
viewer_scope: personal | relationship | occasion | group | public
lifecycle_state: bounded_domain_state
job: bounded_semantic_job
evidence_refs: []
known_to_person_claims: []
new_contribution: ""
claim_authority: occurrence | evidence_world_relation |
  operational_consequence | human_authored_meaning
why_now: ""
treatment_key: bounded_native_instrument
destination: typed_owner_or_root_handoff
action_authority: read | offer | apply | confirm
freshness: {}
uncertainty: []
complete_on_view: true
fallback_treatment: host_native | quiet_row | silence
```

This is a design receipt, not a committed API schema. The server/application
layer may select semantic treatment and order; the client owns native geometry,
accessibility, interaction, fallback, and platform behavior. It must never
receive arbitrary code, coordinates, styling, gestures, or navigation.

## 9. Acceptance tests for all four pages

Each composition fails if any of these are true:

1. The first viewport withholds the useful answer behind a tap.
2. Home opens with a question, prompt, or setup flow.
3. The dominant item is elevated only because it is recent or generated.
4. Two different jobs are forced into the same generic card anatomy.
5. A known-to-person observation is restated as Vesper's insight.
6. Copy infers personality, emotion, relationship meaning, or group consensus
   without human authorship.
7. A private anchor or constraint becomes visible in a shared explanation.
8. Another person's Source is reused outside its authorized audience or scope.
9. Operational state is presented without source, freshness, or degradation.
10. A privacy-safe projection becomes false or misleading instead of simply
    omitting protected detail.
11. A Home action mutates a Place, Plan, Occasion, or relationship without the
    owning authority and receipt.
12. Following an opening loses the current time, Place, people, object, or
    unresolved job.
13. The page looks full only because sections or recommendations were repeated.
14. Ignoring a contribution creates debt, guilt, an unfinished recap, or a
    future engagement prompt.
15. Editorial content survives when a live physical or recovery consequence
    needs the same attention.
16. Removing media causes the underlying meaning or action to become dishonest.

## 10. Product sequence after this pack

The next design and implementation work should preserve both within-state
divergence and cross-state whole-product comparison:

1. **Freeze one rich post-return fixture.** Encode the shared evidence, owner,
   lifecycle, viewer, authority, current truth, editorial receipt, live-source
   boundaries, and negative oracles used by all six New York possible worlds.
2. **Render the six possible worlds.** Build complete static full-scroll pages
   for prospective, interpretive, capability, multiplayer, reconstructive, and
   adaptive composition. Hold evidence constant so the editorial differences
   are real rather than fixture theater.
3. **Review full pages, not isolated cards.** Judge first-viewport hierarchy,
   scroll cadence, value on view, visual register diversity, privacy, and
   whether each page feels like one authored possible world.
4. **Choose the default and composition laws.** Select which New York world is
   the baseline, when another thesis displaces it, and which instruments are
   genuinely reusable. Do not pick one static template for every Moment.
5. **Re-test the chosen shell across Rome, Occasion, and recovery.** The live,
   protected multiplayer, and disrupted states remain necessary falsifiers;
   they ensure the returned/open-day design did not become the whole product.
6. **Extract the minimum projection contract.** Promote only fields required
   across the selected New York worlds and the three pressure states. Do not
   generalize from one hero or one behavior loop.
7. **Implement composition behind a founder-only flag.** Reuse current receipt,
   row, map, people, and editorial primitives where their semantics remain
   honest; keep existing Plan, Occasion, and Places authorities intact.
8. **Connect one real owner at a time.** Current commitment/recovery truth
   first, Places spatial handoff second, Occasion viewer safety third,
   editorial known-to-person admission fourth, Life continuity fifth.
9. **Compare against silence and the current roots.** The new Home earns
   rollout only when the complete composition is more understandable, useful,
   and trustworthy than the current surface or no intervention.

The immediate artifact is therefore a six-page, same-evidence native
composition gallery. It should settle the page-level thesis before the product
generalizes a dynamic composer.
