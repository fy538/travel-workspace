# Life Composition Visual-Tool Brief

> Round-one disposition: comparative evidence only. No strategy was selected.
> The next visual pass follows the cluster, timeline, thread, local-Map, and
> editorial-content correction in the execution plan. Do not polish or extend
> Strategies A, B, or C directly.

## Purpose

Create the first **complete, comparable Life compositions** from one frozen
semantic dataset. This is visual product exploration, not production app code,
an API design, or an isolated card gallery.

Use:

- fixture version `2026-08-29.1`;
- [renderer-neutral fixture
  data](fixtures/life-composition/life-composition-fixtures-v0.1.json); and
- the [execution
  plan](life-composition-and-continuity-lab-execution-plan-2026-08-29.md).

Name the external design file:

```text
Vesper — Life Composition Lab v0.1
```

Every visual frame must display its frame ID, fixture reference, scope,
lifecycle, and evidence posture outside the phone frame. These annotations are
review metadata, not product UI.

## Product premise

Life is Vesper's governed continuity and return layer. It helps a person:

1. **Find** something they know or vaguely remember;
2. **Return** to a bounded Journey, Occasion, period, or Place relationship;
3. **See anew** through a source-grounded operation that adds substance; and
4. **Continue** into present life without turning the past into homework.

Life is not:

- a photo archive;
- a journal requiring entries;
- a social feed;
- an AI autobiography;
- a task list made from unresolved memories;
- an itinerary after the trip;
- a stack of every captured artifact; or
- a second Home or Places screen.

The emotional/product feel should be:

- quiet in aesthetic pressure, not quiet in value;
- rich without feeling crowded;
- attentive without feeling creepy;
- editorial without becoming a magazine pasted onto a database;
- alive without using feed mechanics;
- personal without diagnosing the person; and
- continuous across time without romanticizing everything.

## What must be designed together

The first round is a family of four whole experiences:

| Frame | Fixture | Complete experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I-01** | `IDX-RICH-COMBINED` | Rich Combined Life index containing all six worlds |
| **E-01** | `EP-L01-RICH` | Nice-to-Rome Journey with nested L03 and L04 |
| **E-05** | `EP-L02-LIFECYCLE` | Brooklyn dinner shortly after it happened |
| **E-07** | `EP-L06-LONGITUDINAL` | Supported Red Hook relationship across time |

Do not design one frame to completion and then force its component system onto
the others. Compose all four at low fidelity first, compare them as one
product, and then deepen the family.

## Required first-round output

Produce **three genuinely different composition strategies**, each containing
I-01, E-01, E-05, and E-07.

That yields twelve full-scroll compositions:

```text
Strategy A: I-01A · E-01A · E-05A · E-07A
Strategy B: I-01B · E-01B · E-05B · E-07B
Strategy C: I-01C · E-01C · E-05C · E-07C
```

The strategies must differ in information architecture and reading behavior,
not merely color, typography, card radius, or illustration style.

### Strategy A — Period-led living folio

Explore:

- periods and episodes as the unmistakable stable spine;
- strong full-width episode doors rather than a grid of cards;
- rich detail folding into continuous chapters;
- generated Returns interleaved only where an episode earns them;
- restrained but expressive editorial pacing; and
- a clear sense that the person can blossom from record into possibility.

Pressure test whether the editorial treatment obscures re-finding, whether
Returns begin to rewrite the record, and whether L02 and L06 feel like awkward
variants of a travel story.

### Strategy B — Temporal-spatial continuity field

Explore:

- one legible field where time, Place, episodes, and people become navigable;
- episode entrances anchored in periods but connected by meaningful spatial or
  longitudinal relations;
- Map and Timeline as contextual continuations rather than separate tabs;
- a strong ordered/list equivalent beside or beneath expressive spatial
  treatment; and
- transitions that preserve the selected semantic object.

Pressure test whether the map becomes the product, whether nonspatial Sources
and social authority become secondary, whether recurrence is mistaken for
importance, and whether the index becomes hard to scan.

### Strategy C — Stable index with expressive episode rooms

Explore:

- a highly legible, library-like index with a small number of stable doors;
- large contrast between the efficient index renderer and expressive episode
  renderer;
- bounded episode-specific visual grammar inside a common anatomy;
- Returns as a separate, earned region; and
- progressive disclosure for Sources, repair, and unresolved truth.

Pressure test whether the index feels like a database or archive, whether the
contrast between index and detail feels like two products, whether value is
hidden too deeply, and whether the stable structure feels static after the
person returns home.

The visual tool may propose a stronger fourth strategy only if it changes the
composition model materially and still renders the same controlled fixtures.

## I-01 — rich Combined Life index

The complete index must contain:

```text
Life · Combined

Find and scope

In motion
  Dinner in Brooklyn, only if the selected state is prospective/live

2026 · Summer
  Nice to Rome · Aug 14–27
    returned Journey; supported chapters; some route gaps

  Dinner in Brooklyn · Aug 29
    one Occasion identity; selected recent lifecycle

Across time
  Red Hook
    earned from supported recurrence, not one save

Returns
  Why Rome needed Troy to lose
    eligible sourced operation; not yet final copy

  Same heat, different escape valves
    addressed Rome–Paris comparison under current grants

Timeline · Map · Sources
```

L03, L04, and L05 must remain useful without equal promotion:

- the unused museum ticket nests under the Rome Journey as planned/not-lived;
- the film-to-Rome relation nests under the Journey and may support a Return;
- the friend comparison is an addressed, revocable Return—not ambient status.

Ask whether the stable organization is understandable within seconds, whether
value arrives before action, whether the main regions have distinct jobs,
whether the episode kinds share a family without one universal silhouette,
and whether the index remains alive when `In motion` is absent.

Reject equal cards for every Source, dashboards of counts, identity buckets,
reflection prompts, generic friend activity, a giant map as the only entrance,
generated stories that look canonical, and static archive behavior.

## E-01 — rich Europe Journey

Render all eight eligible episode regions:

1. identity and truth;
2. episode skeleton;
3. occurred story spine;
4. Plans, changes, and unresolved state;
5. people and contributions;
6. Sources in place;
7. See anew; and
8. Continue and repair.

The occurred spine is:

```text
Nice
  supported stay and encounters

Movement south
  supported rail
  ferry remained planned-only

Sorrento and Amalfi
  supported Places, photographs, and authored attention

Rome
  supported Colosseum encounter
  museum ticket explicitly not lived
  film Source and owner's authored connection

Return to New York
  supported return
  open present continuity
```

The frame must show how many Sources become a more coherent episode rather
than a longer card stack.

`Why Rome needed Troy to lose` is a working composition title, not an accepted
fact claim. Reserve a source-grounded treatment that begins after the owner's
existing observation, explains one reviewed mechanism or consequence,
separates the human note from Vesper's contribution, shows claim-local Sources,
and can disappear without damaging the Journey record. Use clearly marked
placeholder copy rather than invented historical substance.

Possible continuations are `Use this in New York`, `Continue the cliffs
question`, and `Open this Place now`. Show only what the composition can support
gracefully. Continue is an opening, not a reminder or documentation request.

## E-05 — Brooklyn dinner, recent state

The same Occasion existed before, during, and after the dinner. Preserve:

- host-authored purpose;
- governed Place and revised time;
- supported occurrence;
- shared core;
- the viewer's private overlay;
- Maya's attributed commitment and photograph under current grant;
- Alex's attributed receipt;
- separately governed Outcomes; and
- optional afterglow.

`What everyone brought to the table` may combine supported dishes or objects,
explicitly contributed stories or decisions, credited human lanes,
commitment-versus-fulfillment truth, and an optional sourced food-tradition
relation. It may not become a generic photo montage, universal positive
sentiment, contribution ranking, required recap, comment/like feed, or Occasion
profile.

Make attribution and plurality easy to perceive without turning permissions
into the main aesthetic. Show how one lane can be withdrawn and the composition
can reflow without a leaked thumbnail or synthetic paraphrase.

## E-07 — Red Hook across time

This is an earned longitudinal Place projection across:

- saves and routes that remained planned-only;
- supported visits;
- a dinner Occasion;
- relevant people under current authority;
- explicit evaluations; and
- a future open New York weekend.

Distinguish:

```text
one save -> search-only
one supported visit -> nested in its period or Occasion
supported recurrence -> stable Across time door
```

`Three ways Red Hook becomes reachable` may compare supported ferry, bus,
walking, weather, companions, and time-of-day conditions. It may end with one
current possibility only after Places refreshes current truth.

Reject `you love Red Hook`, personality statements about waterfronts or
transitions, frequency badges, heatmap-as-importance, and stale provider truth
copied into Life.

The root division is:

- **Life:** the governed history through which Red Hook exists in the person's
  life.
- **Places:** given current conditions and selected history, what Red Hook
  makes possible now.

Show the handoff into Places and the semantic-anchor return to Red Hook.

## Shared visual constraints

### Hierarchy and medium

- One clear reading spine per screen.
- A stable door is more substantial than a nested Source but need not be a
  generic card.
- Generated Returns look derived and valuable, not canonical or disposable.
- Repair and provenance stay reachable through progressive disclosure.
- Richness creates meaningful folds, clusters, sequences, or relations—not
  proportional surface count.
- Use evidence for proof, sequence for change, comparison for consistent axes,
  spatial treatment for reachability, and prose only when explanation leads.
- Social treatment modifies authorship; it is not a feed medium.
- Each present composition has one lead medium and at most one necessary
  supporting medium.

### Copy and accessibility

- Write descriptive truth before interpretation.
- Add substance rather than paraphrasing user attention.
- Prefer concrete mechanisms, differences, and consequences.
- Never diagnose character, taste, emotion, or relationship quality.
- Never make missing data sound like homework.
- Avoid artifact counts as headlines.
- Use placeholders instead of inventing unsupported claims.
- Annotate reading order, heading hierarchy, map/list equivalence, comparison
  axes, attribution language, action labels, and Reduce Motion behavior.

The tool need not implement accessibility, but meaning cannot depend entirely
on visual position.

## Review sheet

For each strategy, answer:

1. What is the stable reading spine?
2. Where does Vesper deliver new value?
3. What happens when no Return is eligible?
4. How does the family handle Journey, Occasion, and Place relationship?
5. How do Mine and Together alter the composition?
6. How do Map, Timeline, and Sources preserve identity?
7. Where does the present world enter without duplicating Home or Places?
8. What is intentionally absent?
9. Which region risks feeling like homework, a feed, or an archive?
10. Which parts require native interaction testing?

Verdicts are `adopt`, `revise`, `defer`, or `reject`.

## Round-one exit gate

The round is ready for founder convergence only when all three strategies
include I-01, E-01, E-05, and E-07; all six worlds use their correct entrance
posture; the three full episode kinds feel related but are not one universal
card; L03–L05 remain subordinate; value precedes input; social material remains
attributed and revocable; generated value is derived and substantive; Continue
is optional; every rich module has a linear fallback; and no strategy requires
a new truth owner or server-authored component tree.
