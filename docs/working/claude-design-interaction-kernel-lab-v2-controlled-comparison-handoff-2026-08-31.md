# Claude Design Handoff — Interaction Kernel Lab V2 Controlled Comparison

## 0. Read this as a corrective continuation, not a new product brief

Continue the existing Claude Design project:

- **Project:** `Vesper — Interaction Kernel Lab`
- **Project ID:** `6dd8b450-9686-4814-8db9-667f0e99db2c`
- **Durable link:** <https://claude.ai/design/p/6dd8b450-9686-4814-8db9-667f0e99db2c>
- **Current local export for inspection only:**
  `~/Downloads/vesper-interaction-kernel-lab/project/`

Do **not** create another Claude Design project. Do **not** overwrite or delete
the current A/B/D treatments. They are useful as a V1 record of the product
hypothesis, but they are not a participant-valid comparison.

The job is to add a controlled V2 research layer to the same project while
preserving V1 provenance.

This handoff does not authorize production React Native work, backend work,
schema changes, canonical product decisions, or a repository-wide noun
migration. It also does not narrow Vesper to three behavior loops. A2/B2/D2 are
a diagnostic portfolio for architecture-bearing interaction seams across solo,
social, and live operational situations.

Nor is V2 only a planning-and-operations lab. Vesper must return generated
value—not merely help a person manipulate Plans, Occasions, and disruptions. A
bounded artifact/composition compatibility gate therefore sits beside
A2/B2/D2. It verifies that the same interaction kernel can inspect, challenge,
reshape, save, withdraw, expire, and refind the compositions and instruments
through which Vesper makes sense, opens possibility, helps life work, and
carries meaning forward. It is not a fourth treatment comparison.

Every new or materially updated page must retain this status:

> **EXPLORATION · INTERACTION RESEARCH · NOT CANON · NOT PRODUCTION**

## 1. Why V2 is required

V1 successfully clarified a compelling product hypothesis:

> Keep the thing being shaped visible. Let language supply the judgment that
> direct controls cannot efficiently express. Resolve touch and language through
> one semantic command layer. Show current truth on its owner. Pause once at a
> real human or world boundary. Make repair local.

That is valuable design work. V1 also produced a useful architectural
distinction:

- the **shared command layer** appears to provide much of the safety,
  authorization, correction, and repair behavior; while
- the **persistent owner projection** appears to provide glanceability,
  manipulation, common ground, and refinding.

But V1 often demonstrated the preferred answer by construction. It bundled
several independent differences into each treatment:

- interaction modality;
- access to Vesper's intelligence;
- integration across services;
- whether owner state persists outside a transcript;
- social policy and privacy capability;
- recovery capability; and
- the quality and completeness of the underlying interface.

Examples that V2 must correct:

1. D-form is a journey through airline, hotel, email, transport, and human
   working memory rather than the strongest integrated direct-manipulation
   Vesper disruption center required by the original handoff.
2. B-form exposes Dana's constraint, sends Sam's contribution out of band, and
   defaults to a public poll. Those change privacy, social capability, and
   coordination policy—not merely modality.
3. A-form loses the contextual comparison and pacing intelligence available to
   Chat and hybrid.
4. Chat sometimes has to request a state card that an excellent Chat treatment
   could provide proactively.
5. Transcript burden is described with labels such as “38 messages” instead of
   being experienced through a real transcript and real refinding task.
6. Many inputs are prefilled or rendered as static `div`s. The participant
   clicks through work that the annotations claim they performed.
7. Several visible actions are `__noop`, including meaningful alternatives,
   Edit, Undo, decline, and hold-off paths.
8. Trace counts and accessibility QA are not yet reliable enough for a study.

Therefore V1 may support design discussion, but its trace totals and apparent
treatment winner must not be treated as participant evidence.

## 2. The V2 research question

Do not ask one overloaded question—“Are forms, Chat, or hybrid best?”

V2 asks three controlled questions:

### Q1 — Expression and local editing

When the semantic owner, visible state, contextual intelligence, evidence, and
available consequences remain constant, when do direct controls, contextual
language, or a mixture minimize total collaboration burden?

### Q2 — State and refinding

When language, reasoning, provider capability, and boundary handling remain
constant, what changes when current state rests in the transcript versus a
persistent owner projection or instrument?

### Q3 — Social contribution and authorship

When the same Occasion, audience rules, private constraints, and contributor
rights remain constant, which expression path makes contribution, decision,
withdrawal, and common ground feel most ordinary and least administrative?

### Compatibility question — Generated value across the four roots

Without adding another full treatment matrix, can one generated Composition
and one live Instrument retain a single semantic identity while each root gives
it a native job?

- Home returns complete, timely value without demanding a response.
- Chat supports inspection, challenge, correction, reshaping, and deliberate
  saving.
- Places adds spatially relevant depth or nearby possibility.
- Life preserves admitted Sources, deliberately saved expression versions,
  lineage, and outcomes—not every transient rendering.

This is a contract and circulation check, not participant evidence that one
visual treatment is superior.

Boundary safety is **not** a treatment advantage. The same command must receive
the same consequence classification and the same proportional review whether
it originated in touch, language, voice, or a contribution.

## 3. The controlled experimental model

### 3.1 Hold these constant inside each comparison

Every treatment in a fixture receives the same:

- authorized Sources and fixture facts;
- fact freshness and uncertainty;
- model and contextual reasoning capability;
- Place, Plan, Occasion, Commitment, provider, and receipt readers;
- provider and social action capability;
- privacy and five-axis authority rules;
- owner truth and revision semantics;
- exact desired human outcome;
- exact failure script;
- recovery, Undo, correction, stop, and takeover capability;
- output quality; and
- visual design quality.

No treatment may win because another treatment has been deprived of Vesper.

### 3.2 Vary only the named factor

The V2 treatments are not three complete product identities. They are bounded
ways of addressing the same semantic system.

#### `direct`

- The same owner projection remains visible.
- The same AI-backed context and recommendations are available.
- The person uses excellent native controls, generated contextual choices,
  selection, sheets, menus, dragging, buttons, and structured review.
- Free-form language is not the primary edit path.
- Do not reduce `direct` to legacy forms or incumbent service fragmentation.

#### `language`

- The same owner truth and contextual intelligence are available.
- The person uses a contextual composer or conversation to express the change.
- Readback quality is equal to the other treatments.
- For an expression comparison, the changed owner may still be rendered as the
  result; “language” does not require hiding state in a transcript.
- Do not create gratuitous clarification or withhold proactive summaries.

#### `hybrid`

- The same owner and intelligence remain available.
- Selection or direct manipulation supplies the referent where useful.
- Short language supplies motive, relation, ambiguity, or tradeoff.
- It receives no extra facts, integrations, or recovery capability.
- It must earn its value through lower total collaboration cost, not by having
  both complete interfaces visible simultaneously.

#### `transcript-state`

- Used only for the D2 state/refinding comparison.
- Language and action capability are identical to `instrument-state`.
- Vesper proactively emits excellent structured cards; the person is not forced
  to ask “where do things stand?” immediately after execution.
- Current state remains primarily in the conversation and must later be
  re-found there.

#### `instrument-state`

- Used only for the D2 state/refinding comparison.
- Language, action capability, and exact review are identical to
  `transcript-state`.
- Current state rests in a persistent owner-local instrument that can be
  re-entered directly.

### 3.3 Do not force the same treatment matrix onto every fixture

Uniformity created part of V1's confounding. Use the smallest comparison that
answers each fixture's primary question:

| Fixture | Primary question | Treatments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A2 — Solo NYC | expression, referents, semantic delta, local repair | `direct`, `language`, `hybrid` |
| B2 — Brooklyn Dinner | low-friction social contribution, privacy, authorship, withdrawal | `direct`, `language`, `hybrid` |
| D2 — Flight Disruption | transcript state vs persistent instrument, boundary comprehension, partial recovery | `transcript-state`, `instrument-state` |

## 5. A2 — Solo NYC controlled comparison

### 5.1 Fixed semantic fixture

Reuse V1 A exactly:

- ordinary week in New York;
- Saturday 2–8 PM is verified open;
- the person begins with `Maybe jazz Saturday`;
- Blue Aster and Corner Note remain fixture candidates;
- set times, transit, weather, and the 8 PM edge remain identical;
- nothing is booked, contacted, or retained without the appropriate act;
- the final retained owner and final clean removal must be identical.

All three treatments use the same context engine. Every treatment knows how
weather, transit, dinner timing, and the 8 PM edge affect the whole Saturday.

### 5.2 Human jobs

1. Open a useful Saturday possibility from vague intent.
2. Make the evening less rushed.
3. Compare the two rooms inside the whole-Saturday context.
4. Keep one privately.
5. Correct or Undo one local change.
6. Remove the jazz and leave with no owner residue or cleanup task.
7. Re-find what is currently true after leaving and returning.

### 5.3 A2-direct

Build the strongest intelligent direct-manipulation Vesper:

- The first useful Saturday Shape appears before a durable Plan is required.
- Direct selection, comparison, and Keep are available on the visible Shape.
- “Less rushed” is represented by a context-generated pacing control, not by
  manually typing three database fields. A credible example is an `Adjust
  pace` sheet whose choices are generated from the current shape—such as
  `More breathing room`, `Keep balanced`, and `Fit more in`—with an inspectable
  preview of what each means here.
- The contextual engine computes the same dinner, buffer, weather, and transit
  implications as language and hybrid.
- The participant actually chooses the pacing treatment and candidate; do not
  pre-render the result and credit them with the work.
- Keep, remove, and Undo mutate the same owner commands as other treatments.

Do not use category entry, exact-time requirements, an empty Plan shell, or a
context-blind comparison as the essence of direct manipulation. Those may be
incumbent patterns, but they do not represent the strongest direct Vesper.

### 5.4 A2-language

- The Saturday Shape and owner truth are equivalent to A2-direct.
- The person begins and reshapes through a contextual composer.
- Do not require them to restate Saturday, selected Place identities, weather,
  or other visible context.
- Resolve `Less rushed` sensibly without inventing a clarification.
- If the person says `Keep the first one`, use strong visible-reference
  resolution. Clarify only if the rendered ordering or scope is actually
  unstable or the consequence would otherwise target the wrong candidate.
- Show semantic deltas and current owner readback at equal quality.
- The participant uses language for removal and Undo; these must be real.

### 5.5 A2-hybrid

- Let the person select the evening or candidate directly.
- Let selection carry the referent into a short expression such as `Less
  rushed`.
- Render the smallest semantic delta on the visible Shape.
- Use direct controls for compare, Keep, remove, and Undo where they are
  already visible and exact.
- Do not leave a permanent composer or assistant panel on screen when no
  expression is needed.

### 5.6 A2 evaluation

Observe:

- time and burden before the first useful Shape;
- whether the participant discovers how to change pacing;
- number of representational decisions made only for the system;
- whether selection scope is predictable;
- visible-context restatement;
- whether one correction stays local;
- whether the current version is understood without a truth-check question;
- ownership and authorship; and
- whether removal leaves real or perceived residue.

The hybrid hypothesis is weakened if participants cannot predict selection
scope, hesitate between touch and language, or prefer the generated direct
pacing control with equal or lower burden.

## 6. B2 — Brooklyn Dinner controlled comparison

### 6.1 Fixed semantic fixture and invariants

Reuse Nora, Dana, Sam, and Alex and the same C2 facts. Every treatment must
provide the same social system:

- Nora is the host and owns the bounded final Place decision.
- Naming people does not contact them.
- Dana receives a guest link with value before any ask; no install or profile.
- Dana's approximately $40 constraint remains private to Vesper, shapes the
  minimum-safe set in aggregate, and is never attributed to Nora or the group.
- Sam opens late without punishment.
- Sam can contribute a Place and rule in his own voice.
- Contributing is not attending.
- Sam's attendance remains unknown until he answers.
- Nora sees group-safe candidates and makes the bounded decision.
- Sam owns Edit, Undo, and withdrawal of his contribution.
- Withdrawal recompiles dependent projections but does not rewrite occurred
  history or unmake Nora's independent decision.
- The settled result creates no public activity feed, poll residue, reaction
  counts, or reciprocity pressure.

These are invariants, not hybrid-only features.

### 6.2 Shared Occasion state

All three B2 treatments use the same compact Occasion owner at rest. The study
does not compare whether an Occasion exists. It compares how people contribute
and coordinate around the same Occasion.

The Occasion must remain message-weight:

- settled purpose, time, and Place;
- one or two genuinely open questions;
- viewer-relative response state;
- attributed contributions under their audience grants;
- private overlays never exposed as shared reasons; and
- bounded owner actions.

It must not become a project board, participant administration dashboard,
social feed, or permission matrix.

### 6.3 B2-direct

Build the strongest native social interaction:

- Nora creates the initial Occasion through a minimal direct composition with
  excellent defaults and a single audience preview.
- Dana's guest link offers a real response control and an optional private
  constraint affordance whose effect and privacy are clear.
- Sam adds his recommendation through a lightweight contribution control on
  the Occasion—no out-of-band text and no host re-entry.
- Contribution fields are minimal and context-aware. Do not ask Sam to classify
  the Place, create a profile, or complete an event form.
- Nora receives the same group-safe comparison and makes the same host decision
  directly. Do not default to a poll.
- Sam can actually Edit, Undo, or withdraw his contribution.

### 6.4 B2-language

- Nora may begin with the ordinary sentence.
- Dana may respond and state her constraint in ordinary language on the guest
  link; the UI must explain the private effect without surfacing authority
  jargon.
- Sam may contribute the Place and rule through ordinary language.
- Each contribution lands on the same Occasion owner with the same attribution
  and correction rights.
- Nora decides in language; the consequence and audience result are identical
  to B2-direct.
- Withdrawal by language must target only Sam's contribution and dependent
  projections.

### 6.5 B2-hybrid

- Let each actor use the lighter modality for the immediate job.
- Nora may initiate in language, inspect the prepared Occasion, and send from
  one exact audience preview.
- Dana may respond directly and optionally contribute her private limit in a
  contextual composer.
- Sam may contribute in language, then Edit or Undo directly on the attributed
  contribution.
- Nora chooses between visible safe candidates directly.
- The Occasion remains the resting common ground; conversation appears only
  for contribution or qualitative intent.

### 6.6 B2 evaluation

Observe separately for Nora, Dana, and Sam:

- time to first value before an ask;
- whether private versus shared effect is understood;
- whether contribution is mistaken for attendance;
- whether Sam can return late without visible social penalty;
- whether unique human knowledge survives with attribution;
- whether the safe set exposes Dana's reason;
- whether Nora understands what decision is hers;
- whether correction and withdrawal feel like the author's right;
- whether anyone experiences contribution as event administration; and
- whether the settled state feels like people making an evening together
  rather than approving Vesper's work.

The hybrid hypothesis is weakened if a minimal direct contribution path is
more predictable and equally effortless, or if mixing modalities makes the
Occasion feel heavier.

## 7. D2 — Flight disruption state comparison

### 7.1 Fixed capability contract

D2 must compare two integrated Vesper experiences. Neither treatment is an
airline app plus four external apps.

Both receive:

- the same verified cancellation;
- the same provider alternatives and exact fixture facts;
- the same affected interview, hotel, pickup, transfer, and receipt context;
- the same qualitative objective: protect the important commitment and reduce
  walking;
- the same route and burden reasoning;
- the same private preparation;
- the same provider, money, and audience operations;
- the same exact one-time bundle review;
- the same scripted hotel success and transport `unknown` result;
- the same per-owner readback;
- the same reconcile-before-retry behavior;
- the same Stop and one-action takeover capabilities; and
- the same final settled truth.

The only primary variable is where operational state rests and how it is
re-found.

### 7.2 Shared D2 journey

1. Vesper announces the verified cancellation and explicitly says nothing has
   been accepted, submitted, charged, or sent.
2. The person inspects one coherent adaptation.
3. The person expresses the priority in language; both treatments receive the
   same contextual language capability.
4. Vesper shows the same smallest coherent alternative and tradeoff.
5. The person sees the same exact provider/money/audience bundle once.
6. One confirmation commits it.
7. Per-owner execution produces the same verified, pending, and unknown states.
8. Vesper proactively renders those states; neither treatment makes the person
   ask immediately for a summary that the system already knows they need.
9. The participant leaves the immediate surface.
10. After the controlled delay, the participant re-finds current truth.
11. They reconcile and retry only the unresolved transfer, Stop, or take over
    that one action.
12. Successful sub-actions are never replayed.

### 7.3 D2-transcript-state

- The operational summary is an excellent structured card in Chat.
- It appears proactively after execution.
- The complete transcript is rendered and scrollable. Do not substitute a
  label saying “38 messages.”
- Add enough realistic, fixture-controlled conversation to create genuine
  refinding without turning the transcript into a caricature.
- The returning participant may scroll, use transcript search if that is part
  of the treatment, or ask Vesper.
- Asking remains a valid success, not automatically counted as failure.
- Repair, Stop, and takeover work through language and use the same command
  layer as the instrument treatment.

### 7.4 D2-instrument-state

- The operational state rests in a persistent disruption Instrument owned by
  the affected Plan/Commitment/provider relations.
- Chat or contextual language may initiate and reshape it.
- Current per-owner truth, freshness, and recovery actions remain visible at
  rest.
- The participant re-enters the Instrument directly after the controlled
  delay.
- The instrument must dissolve after stable return; it must not become a
  permanent disruption dashboard or a personal trait.

### 7.5 D2 boundary comprehension

The external review is identical in both treatments. It must name:

- the exact reroute and provider effect;
- refund-path consequence without unsupported legal claims;
- hotel hold and money effect;
- exact outgoing message and audience;
- transfer request;
- recovery limit for each action; and
- which actions cannot be reversed.

Measure comprehension, not treatment victory:

- What is merely prepared?
- What will happen after confirmation?
- What cannot be undone?
- What actually succeeded?
- What remains unknown?
- What can Stop or takeover affect?

### 7.6 D2 evaluation

Observe:

- comprehension immediately after partial execution;
- time and actions required to re-find current truth;
- whether asking feels easier than glancing;
- whether transcript search or assistant summary is sufficient;
- whether the persistent instrument feels helpful or like project management;
- whether current truth is confused with the request transcript;
- whether repair targets only the unresolved owner;
- whether the participant can Stop or take over without losing supervision of
  the rest; and
- whether the urgency treatment recedes appropriately after resolution.

The persistent-object hypothesis is weakened if participants refind the
transcript card or ask naturally with equal comprehension and lower perceived
weight.

## 8. Real interaction requirements

V2 participant mode must measure actions people actually perform.

### 8.1 Inputs

- Participant inputs begin empty unless the task itself supplies literal text.
- The person types or speaks the expression; do not count seeded capture text
  as participant words.
- Direct controls must be real controls with real selection and state change.
- Static display fields may show defaults, but any field or choice claimed as
  user work must actually be manipulable.
- Enter must submit a composer where expected.
- Back must restore state, selection, scroll, and unsent input accurately.

### 8.2 Branches

- No visible participant target may use `data-go="__noop"`.
- A visible alternative must either work or not be shown in participant mode.
- Implement the meaningful branches: alternative candidate, decline, Edit,
  Undo, hold off, Stop, safe retry, and one-action takeover.
- Capture mode may address a deterministic branch directly by query parameter;
  deterministic capture is not permission to make participant choices inert.

### 8.3 State

- Owner state changes must survive the transitions relevant to the treatment.
- Refinding must operate on real persisted session state, not explanatory copy.
- A refresh may return to start only where persistence is intentionally absent.
- V2 transcript and instrument treatments must use the same underlying fixture
  state and command results.

### 8.4 Receipts and repair

- Every applied mutation reads back from its canonical owner.
- Receipts distinguish proposed, applied, pending, verified, failed, partial,
  unknown, and not attempted where relevant.
- Correct, Undo, Release, withdrawal, Stop, and takeover identify the smallest
  affected relation.
- Repair must not regenerate or replay unrelated successful state.

## 9. Research protocol

### 9.1 Add three runtime modes

Support:

```text
?mode=research|participant|capture
```

#### Research mode

- research rail, stable step IDs, annotations, oracles, trace, Reset, and copy;
- may expose treatment identity to the researcher;
- never used as the participant-facing surface during a moderated task.

#### Participant mode

- no annotations, failure oracles, hypotheses, findings, or treatment labels;
- no explanatory text that tells the participant what burden they are meant to
  experience;
- realistic scenario copy;
- empty live inputs;
- complete interactive branches;
- local tracing continues unobtrusively.

#### Capture mode

- exactly one deterministic mobile specimen;
- research chrome removed and motion frozen;
- seeded content allowed solely for deterministic visual capture;
- seeded text must never enter participant trace totals.

### 9.2 Study design

The protocol must state whether a session is within-subjects or between-
subjects. For a small founder/moderated study, default to within-subjects with
counterbalanced order.

- Counterbalance treatment order; do not always show hybrid last or first.
- Counterbalance candidate ordering where reference resolution is under test.
- Do not reuse an identical task in immediate succession without accounting for
  learning and memory.
- Record prior familiarity with Chat-first and planning tools as context, not a
  score adjustment.
- Use the same moderator wording for matched treatments.
- Declare a blocked threshold before the session.
- Do not coach a participant toward the modality the hypothesis prefers.
- Ask comprehension and authorship questions after the action, not during it.
- Use a real controlled delay for the refinding task.

If the sample is too small for inference, report individual patterns and
counterexamples. Do not compute a composite winner.

### 9.3 Participant task wording

Task scripts must state human goals, not interface instructions. Examples:

- A2: “You might want jazz on Saturday. See what could work, make the evening
  feel less rushed, keep what you want, then change your mind.”
- B2 Nora: “Dana is visiting. Put together Saturday dinner with Sam and Alex
  without making this a project.”
- B2 Dana: “See what Nora is proposing. Respond however you want, and privately
  mention the spending limit if it matters.”
- B2 Sam: “You know a place that could make this evening better. Add what you
  know, then later remove the food advice.”
- D2: “Your return was cancelled. Protect tomorrow's important commitment and
  reduce unnecessary walking. Make sure you know what has and has not actually
  happened.”

Do not tell participants to use Chat, select an object, open a sheet, or find a
particular button.

## 10. Trace and measurement contract

### 10.1 Session identity and timing

- Generate a fixture-local random session ID.
- Record session start, treatment start, and monotonic event time.
- Preserve monotonic timing across in-treatment navigation and refresh where
  the treatment persists.
- Render elapsed time and time to first useful state.

### 10.2 Count physical actions accurately

Every pointer or keyboard activation counts once as an action regardless of
semantic event type. `confirm`, `undo`, `correct`, `stop`, and `takeover` are
still physical actions.

Keep semantic event type separate from physical action count.

Record at minimum:

```yaml
fixture:

session_id:

step_id:
event_type:

owner_after:
owner_revision_before:
owner_revision_after:
consequence_class:
truth_before:
truth_after:
result:
```

### 10.3 Automatic metrics

Compute without qualitative interpretation:

- elapsed time;
- time to first useful state;
- physical actions;
- words and characters actually entered;
- screens or distinct states;
- scroll distance;
- clarification turns;
- backtracks;
- confirmations;
- corrections and Undos;
- owner/surface changes;
- regeneration versus local delta events;
- re-entry/refinding path and time;
- Stop and takeover; and
- final machine-detectable owner residue.

### 10.4 Moderator-coded observations

Keep these separate from automatic metrics:

- visible-context restatement;
- confusion about current truth;
- hesitation between modalities;
- perceived administrative burden;
- perceived prompting burden;
- perceived control;
- authorship and ownership;
- privacy or audience misunderstanding;
- social pressure;
- whether asking felt easier than glancing; and
- whether an object felt helpful or like project management.

Provide a compact score sheet and comment field. Do not convert these into a
single numeric winner.

### 10.5 Trace correctness

## 11. Accessibility and layout acceptance

### 11.1 Text scaling

The current `body.text135` rule does not scale most text because descendants
use fixed pixel sizes. V2 must use a scaling mechanism that changes computed
text size for the actual participant content.

Verify computed size at 100% and 135%; a nominal class without a changed
computed size is a failure.

### 11.2 Targets and input semantics

- Every participant action target is at least 44×44 CSS points.
- Do not rely on `min-height: 34px` chips for participant actions.
- Use native buttons, inputs, textareas, links, radios, and controls where their
  semantics apply.
- When a custom element is unavoidable, provide correct role, name, state,
  focus order, Enter/Space behavior, and touch target.
- Fake form fields rendered as non-interactive `div`s may not stand in for
  participant input.

### 11.3 Required checks

For the densest state in every V2 fixture, verify:

- 393-point width, standard text;
- 320-point width, standard text;
- 393-point width, 135% text;
- 320-point width, 135% text;
- keyboard-only completion;
- visible focus;
- sensible accessible names and ordering;
- reduced motion;

## 12. Content and visual treatment

Preserve the current Vesper design kernel. This is not a visual-language round.

- sans remains for interaction and operational work;

- one dominant job per state;

Participant mode must not show:

- failure oracles;

- setup checklists framed as value; or

Generated value must be complete enough to reward viewing before asking for
classification, reflection, continuation, or additional input. A Composition

## 13. Product and architecture guardrails

### 13.1 Preserve owner truth

Do not invent a new universal `Artifact`, `OccasionItem`, workflow, UI tree, or
generic persisted interaction object.

Use the existing distinctions:

- **Artifact / Source** — admitted human or external material;
- **Composition** — generated explanatory, editorial, comparative, or
  reconstructive value;
- **Instrument** — current state plus manipulation or consequence;
- **Projection** — viewer-, root-, scope-, and Moment-relative expression;
- **PlanItem** — lightweight child of a personal Plan where that accepted
  prospective identity is independently earned;
- **Occasion** — bounded shared operational ground; and
- **Commitment / provider state** — consequential and external truth.

The UI need not expose these nouns.

### 13.2 One command layer

Touch, language, voice, and contribution must resolve to the same typed owner
command and the same authority path. The prototype may use fixture-local
commands, but their semantics must be visible in research mode:

```yaml
resolved_interaction:
  origin_root:
  visible_owner:
  selected_resource_refs: []
  viewer:
  moment:
  immediate_job:
  expression:
  semantic_intent:
  affected_owner_commands: []
  authority_basis:
  boundary_class:
  return_envelope:
```

### 13.3 Truth planes

Never collapse:

1. canonical owner truth;
2. proposal;
3. human expression or generated composition; and
4. provider/execution truth.

An assistant message is not owner truth. A request callback is not provider
success. A saved shape is not a booking. A contribution is not attendance. A
question is not a durable preference.

### 13.4 Contribution and consequence

Follow `docs/systems/contribution-and-consequence.md`:

- value precedes classification or continuation work;
- use, retention, inference, audience, and action are independent;
- private constraints may shape the safe set without exposure;
- attributed contributions preserve author and audience;
- T0 answers or prepares privately;
- T1 applies a private reversible consequence with quiet receipt and Undo;
- T2 previews one material human/world boundary exactly once; and
- correction, deletion, expiry, and withdrawal propagate through causal
  dependencies without rewriting unrelated history.

### 13.5 Four-root continuity

V2 is not a root redesign, but every fixture must preserve a valid return:

- Chat is the lowest-friction expression and contribution entry;
- Home may elevate current-life value or an urgent Instrument;
- Places owns place-relative exploration and live spatial reduction;
- Life provides durable human refinding across Sources, episodes, Plans,
  Occasions, and outcomes; and
- owner surfaces hold their own current truth.

Cross-root transitions carry semantic context and return; they do not make
Chat the owner of everything.

### 13.6 Artifact and composition compatibility gate

Build a bounded compatibility page after the shared runtime is corrected. Reuse
the existing A01–A06 semantic fixtures; do not redesign all six as six more
participant experiments.

Render two contrast cases:

1. **Generated Composition** — use either A02, the attributed Rome/Paris social
   juxtaposition, or A04, the Sorrento cliff connection that adds substantive
   geology and urban-infrastructure value beyond repeating the user's own
   observation.
2. **Live Instrument** — use A06 or the equivalent D2 disruption instrument,
   where operational UI may expire while owner truth and a receipt remain.

For the generated Composition, show the same semantic identity through four
root-native projections:

- **Home:** the compact return is valuable on view, contains its lead evidence,
  and requires no response;
- **Chat:** the person can ask why it appeared, challenge a claim, correct a
  source, reshape the expression, or deliberately save that exact version;
- **Places:** the return gains spatial context, comparison, or a nearby opening
  without becoming a generic destination card; and
- **Life:** only admitted Sources and a deliberately saved, versioned expression
  with dependency lineage persist. The transient Home/Places rendering does not
  become a durable object merely because it was shown.

For the live Instrument, show that:

- current state and available consequences remain glanceable while live;
- completed external actions produce provider or owner truth plus receipts;
- expiry removes stale operational controls;
- expiry does not erase the underlying episode, outcome, or receipt; and
- the expired UI is not preserved in Life as though it were a saved
  Composition.

Use the existing circulation contract or its fixture-local equivalent:

```yaml
circulation:
  semantic_identity:
  source_refs: []
  expression_version:
  origin_root:
  current_projection:
  viewer:
  moment:
  immediate_job:
  allowed_actions: []
  persistence_policy:
  dependency_manifest: []
  return_envelope:
```

The field names are research scaffolding, not a proposed production schema.

### 13.7 Home admission and suppression

Do not treat every valid or beautiful Composition as a Home item. The gate must
show that Home selects among competing valid returns according to present
value, timing, consequence, novelty, and what has already been understood.

Use A05 as the admission example: a current Red Hook or New York opening can
win the crown position while several valid Italy compositions remain available
but suppressed. An urgent state may collapse Home to one operational
instruction. A prior-trip insight may support the current opening, but it may
not take over the page merely because it is recent or rich.

Record at least one explicit suppression reason in research mode. Never expose
ranking machinery, scarcity theater, or “engagement” language to participants.

### 13.8 Lifecycle and causal repair

Exercise these contract cases on the compatibility page:

- a contributor withdraws a Source used by an attributed social Composition;
- new evidence revises a reconstruction without silently rewriting its saved
  prior version;
- a saved Composition retains its exact expression version and dependency
  manifest;
- a corrected or withdrawn dependency invalidates only dependent claims, not
  unrelated history;
- stale or offline state is visible without fabricated freshness; and
- a live Instrument expires while durable owner truth and receipts remain.

The same semantic identity and return envelope must survive valid revisions.
When a prior expression can no longer be supported, show a local lifecycle
notice and a repair path rather than replacing history invisibly.

### 13.9 Renderer boundary

Claude Design may explore root-native compositions, but this lab must not
canonize a universal `ArtifactCard`, a model-authored component tree, or a
general-purpose UI DSL. The agent composes meaning; the app renders bounded
semantic anatomy.

The compatibility page may use compact, standard, and immersive arrangements
to test density and refinding. Those are renderer explorations, not new object
types. Root changes may alter medium, density, available action, and persistence
affordance; they may not duplicate or mutate the underlying truth.

## 14. Findings and falsification

`93 V2 Findings and Falsification.dc.html` begins without conclusions.

For every comparison record:

- what became easier;
- what work moved onto the person;
- where current state became unclear;
- where correction became local or broad;
- whether a material confirmation was missing or duplicated;
- whether the participant could leave cleanly;
- what felt authored;
- which modality the participant tried naturally;
- actual trace totals;
- comprehension answers;
- strongest counterexample; and
- whether the result is design-time, founder walkthrough, or participant
  evidence.

The preferred hypothesis loses or weakens when:

1. selection scope is not predictable;
2. modality choice costs more than it saves;
3. intelligent direct control matches hybrid with lower burden;
4. language-only with visible owner readback matches hybrid;
5. transcript refinding matches the persistent Instrument with lower perceived
   weight;
6. the persistent object feels like project management;
7. local repair still regenerates unrelated state;
8. direct and language paths produce different authority or truth;
9. the shared Occasion introduces social pressure or privacy confusion; or
10. the result feels primarily authored by Vesper.

Do not average these into one product score. A treatment may win one moment and
lose another. The desired result is a modality and state allocation rule, not a
champion interface.

Record the compatibility gate separately. It must report whether generated
value is complete on view, whether one identity survives root-native
projection, whether Home suppresses as well as admits, whether saved expression
and transient rendering remain distinct, and whether revision, withdrawal, and
expiry repair only their causal dependents. These are contract findings, not
votes for a visual treatment.
