# Brand candidates: Hermene / Hermi and Oivaro / Ovi

## Status

**Hermene / Hermi** and **Oivaro / Ovi** are the two live candidates. This is
an exploration record, not an adoption decision. A replacement must beat
Hermene / Hermi on philosophical fit, pronunciation and recall, and the
quality of a human-facing intelligence handle.

The relevant product promise is not an AI travel planner or an AI character.
It is a product that helps people make more of the world personally legible,
inhabitable, and shareable, then helps something worthwhile happen there. The
AI should be useful and accountable in the moment while remaining subordinate
to the person, the people with them, and the place.

## Candidate 1: Hermene / Hermi

**Hermene** names the interpretive intelligence layer. **Hermi** is the
human-facing handle for that intelligence.

### Strengths

- It is a coherent two-level pair: the formal name is distinctive and the
  short form is easy to say, remember, and address.
- It gives people a practical way to invoke and correct the intelligence:
  "Ask Hermi," "Why did Hermi suggest this?", or "Hermi, keep this for us."
- Interpretation remains a real differentiator: the product helps people see
  historical, cultural, sensory, practical, relational, and personal layers
  of a place rather than merely retrieve or rank information.
- The name can feel thoughtful, serious, literary, and differentiated from a
  generic travel or map product.

### Risks

- `Hermene` has first-hearing pronunciation and spelling friction.
- It names one important capability--interpretation--rather than the larger
  outcome of lived capability, situated action, relationship, and continuity.
- A prominent Hermi persona could make the AI the protagonist. The product
  model must preserve the better relationship:

  ```text
  people <-> place / occasion / encounter <-> people
                    ^
          Hermi improves relevance, judgment, and coordination
  ```

### Product rule

Hermi is the product's voice and bounded intelligence, not a fictional friend,
tour-guide character, or member of a group. Hermi may surface an opening,
explain its reasoning, coordinate a decision, or help retain a consequence;
it should then return attention to the world and the people involved.

## Candidate 2: Oivaro / Ovi

**Oivaro** is a coined name, not a direct Finnish, Greek, Latin, or Japanese
word. Its intended association is the Finnish family around `oiva` (excellent,
splendid) and `oivallus` (insight, realization, discovery). Do not claim that
Oivaro literally translates to any of those terms.

**Ovi** is the possible short, human-facing form.

### Strengths

- It is a broader umbrella for the evolved philosophy: an encounter becoming
  newly intelligible, practical, meaningful, and capable of changing a later
  one.
- It does not make the AI or interpretation the product's center. Oivaro can
  encompass place, people, artifacts, action, memory, culture, and return.
- The sound is warm, curious, and worldly. It feels like a brand that can earn
  its own meaning rather than a term that arrives with a narrow definition.
- `Ovi` is compact and usable in interaction, though less naturally expressive
  than Hermi.

### Risks

- It is more foreign-seeming and semantically opaque. People may need to hear
  it once before confidently saying it; the initial `Oi` is not entirely
  pronunciation-safe across languages.
- Its invented form can read as travel, lifestyle, or luxury before the product
  demonstrates its deeper point of view.
- There is a small Maldives travel operator called **Oivaro Travels**. It is a
  category-adjacent collision to investigate in a proper trademark search, but
  not a scaled consumer-product conflict sufficient to reject the candidate.

### Product rule

Oivaro should name the product and the world it helps a person build. If Ovi is
used, it should remain a light, situated interface voice rather than a mascot.

## Direct comparison

| Dimension | Hermene / Hermi | Oivaro / Ovi |
|---|---|---|
| Primary association | interpretation and meaning-making | discovery and lived realization |
| Strongest role | the intelligence layer | the whole product/world layer |
| Emotional tone | thoughtful, literary, serious | warm, curious, expansive |
| Master-brand flexibility | somewhat constrained by interpretation | broad; must earn its meaning through product |
| AI-handle quality | excellent: Hermi feels natural | good: Ovi is concise but lighter |
| Main usability risk | pronunciation and spelling of Hermene | foreignness and pronunciation of Oivaro |
| Main philosophy risk | centering the AI | becoming aesthetically pleasant but semantically thin |

The useful sentence test is:

> **Hermi**, help us decide what to do next.

This strongly favors Hermene / Hermi as an intelligence system.

> **Oivaro** helped us make more of Rome ours.

This strongly favors Oivaro as the master product brand.

## Architecture options to test

1. **Hermene / Hermi as the complete system.**
   The product is Hermene; Hermi is its conversational intelligence. Best if
   interpretation is the most memorable front door.

2. **Oivaro / Ovi as the complete system.**
   The product is Oivaro; Ovi is a light voice when a name is useful. Best if
   the lived world, rather than the intelligence, is the memorable front door.

3. **Oivaro as product; Hermi as intelligence.**
   Potentially elegant: Oivaro owns the human world and Hermi performs bounded
   interpretive, planning, and coordination work inside it. Do not adopt this
   extra naming layer unless Hermi is genuinely visible enough to deserve a
   distinct identity.

## Validation before deciding

Run the same short test for both pairs with people who have not seen the
etymology:

1. Ask them to pronounce **Hermene**, **Hermi**, **Oivaro**, and **Ovi**.
2. Say each name once and ask them to spell it.
3. Test ten-minute unaided recall.
4. Show each in a notification, a chat header, an app-store listing, and a
   spoken referral.
5. Ask which name feels like a map, travel planner, AI assistant, social app,
   personal-world product, or something else.
6. Ask which sentence feels more natural: "Ask Hermi" versus "Ask Ovi," and
   "I saved this in Hermene" versus "I saved this in Oivaro."
7. Before public use, commission professional trademark screening in intended
   jurisdictions and native-speaker review of the final pronunciation.

## Screened derivative: Legari

**Legari** is a coined, Italian-sounding derivative of `legare` (to bind,
connect, or, figuratively, form rapport). The root is relevant to the
multiplayer thesis, but the candidate is **not a master-brand lead**.

- It is pleasant to say (`leh-GAH-ree`), people-shaped rather than overtly
  technical, and makes the relational dimension immediately legible.
- It should not be presented as standard Italian for "to connect." `legare` is
  the standard Italian infinitive; `legari` is attested as a Sicilian form and
  is also an Italian surname. Latin `legari` has a separate passive sense
  connected to being appointed or sent as an envoy.
- More decisively, **Legora** is an established, fast-growing collaborative AI
  product for legal teams. `Legari`, `Legara`, and `Legaro` share its highly
  distinctive consonant frame and software/AI naming pattern, creating a
  material spoken, search, and brand-confusion risk. `Ligari` also has existing
  app usage. Do not try to solve the collision with a one-vowel variation.
- The root remains useful in product language: a `legami`/"ties" metaphor can
  describe the voluntary, privacy-respecting relationships that let a place or
  occasion carry meaning forward. It must not imply that the product binds
  people to one another or makes the relationship itself the thing being
  optimized.
