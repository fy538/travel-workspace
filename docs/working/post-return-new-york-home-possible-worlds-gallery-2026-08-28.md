# Post-Return New York Home Possible Worlds Gallery

## Decision

The same truthful evidence can produce several coherent Homes because Home is
not a database view. It is an editorial composition of the **possible world
that matters at this moment**.

This gallery holds the person, evidence, date, roots, privacy rules, and
product laws constant while changing the organizing proposition of the full
scroll. The alternatives are not themes, user-selected modes, or five feeds:

1. **New York is already waiting** — prospective and spatial;
2. **the city becomes newly legible** — situated and interpretive;
3. **the trip becomes capability** — practical and creative;
4. **other people enlarge the world** — relational and multiplayer;
5. **the journey becomes usable truth** — reconstructive and consequential;
6. **one life, several horizons** — the recommended adaptive synthesis.

The exercise reveals a stronger rule than “choose the best card”:

> **The composer first chooses the page's editorial thesis, then chooses a
> dominant treatment and a diverse supporting portfolio that make that thesis
> perceptible.**

No world is a permanent personality assignment. The evidence and current job
determine which world is admissible today; another may be correct tomorrow.

## 1. Shared fixture

### 1.1 Evidence held constant

The fixture includes only evidence already described in the founder fieldwork:

- the person has returned to New York after Nice, Sorrento, Capri, Amalfi, and
  Rome;
- an upcoming weekend has room, subject to live calendar confirmation;
- the trip produced authorized flight, rail, ferry, hotel, reservation, menu,
  photograph, food, and movement Sources;
- the person explicitly noticed the pasta-texture difference, Rome's heat and
  abundance, accumulated reuse, arrival and ferry friction, and the
  *Odyssey*–Troy–Aeneas connection;
- the Capri proposal Occasion involved the partner, movement, timing, light,
  flowers, photography, dinner, service contributors, and protected private
  material;
- the return involved cancellation, rerouting, lodging or meal questions,
  possible compensation, and work rescheduling;
- the person proposed a Rome–Paris juxtaposition with friends and a temporary
  “just returned” Status, but neither becomes usable without actual
  audience-authorized Sources; and
- New York—not the completed trip—owns the present viewport.

### 1.2 Fresh world truth used in this design fixture

The fixture demonstrates how current infrastructure can join personal evidence
without becoming Vesper's product center:

- Friday, August 28 is humid with possible thunderstorms; Saturday is forecast
  to be less humid with clouds and sun; Sunday is warmer and more humid with a
  possible shower. Production must recheck at render and action time.
- The official South Brooklyn NYC Ferry route currently connects Wall
  Street/Pier 11, Atlantic Avenue, Red Hook, Sunset Park/Brooklyn Army
  Terminal, and other landings. Exact departure and disruption truth remains
  live provider authority.
- NYC Parks lists Saturday evening Vinyl Nights at Pier I and a Sunday Sketch
  Jam in Riverside Park. These are world candidates, not presumed interests.
- Brooklyn Army Terminal began as a military supply base and now houses more
  than 100 businesses; BATWorks and current site-specific public art make its
  industrial, ecological, labor, and reuse layers unusually legible.

Sources: [New York forecast](https://www.accuweather.com/en/us/new-york/10021/weather-forecast/349727),
[NYC Ferry South Brooklyn](https://www.ferry.nyc/routes-and-schedules/south-brooklyn/),
[NYC Parks Summer on the Hudson](https://www.nycgovparks.org/events/summer_on_the_hudson/ajax/aggregate/p2),
[Brooklyn Army Terminal](https://edc.nyc/about),
[BATWorks](https://edc.nyc/press-release/nycedc-opens-batworks-temporary-space-advance-climate-innovation-green-jobs-bat),
and [In Plain Sight](https://edc.nyc/press-release/nycedc-announces-in-plain-sight-public-art-exhibition-sunset-park).

The sources establish candidate truth, not recommendation quality. A
production proposal must still resolve the person's location, calendar,
mobility, current service, opening hours, accessibility, availability,
companions, acceptable effort, and intent.

### 1.3 Conditional fixture fields

The gallery uses brackets rather than inventing these facts:

- `[open Saturday window]`;
- `[starting neighborhood and experienced travel time]`;
- `[verified ferry departure and return]`;
- `[available restaurant, market, or ingredient]`;
- `[partner's independently expressed Saturday intention]`;
- `[friend-authorized Paris trace]`;
- `[unresolved recovery item]`; and
- `[photograph or Source that actually supports the treatment]`.

If a conditional field does not resolve, the unit degrades or disappears. It
does not become plausible lifestyle copy.

### 1.4 Invariant product laws

Every composition must:

- make one thing dominant without making it the only valuable thing;
- keep demands, decisions, and response debt much scarcer than possibilities;
- deliver the useful contribution on view before offering depth or action;
- preserve current life above retrospective recency;
- interpret evidence, events, places, and mechanisms—not the person;
- add something beyond what the person already noticed;
- preserve source, authorship, viewer scope, and affected-person authority;
- use Chat, Places, Life, Plan, and Occasion as owned continuations;
- allow ignore to be a complete outcome; and
- collapse toward practical truth during disruption, urgency, or protected
  presence.

## 2. Stable shell across all six worlds

The worlds should feel like one learned Home, not six mini-apps. They share six
semantic regions even when geometry and prominence change:

```text
WORLD READ
  present Place, time, conditions, and posture

DOMINANT STAGE
  one current claim, instrument, or consequence

IN MOTION
  bounded Plans, Occasions, affected people, and material loose ends

POSSIBILITY FIELD
  several distinct ways to enter, notice, understand, make, relate, or continue

CONTINUITY AND RECOGNITION
  source-backed return, reconstruction, capability, or farther horizon

STABLE DOORS
  Plans and Occasions · Places · Life · contextual Chat
```

The composition changes the **thesis, native instruments, order, and density**
inside that shell. The bottom navigation remains Home / Chat / Places / Life.

## 3. Possible World A — New York is already waiting

### Organizing proposition

The person has not returned to an empty life. The current city already contains
several usable possibilities, and Vesper can prepare one without asking the
person to restart planning.

This is the strongest default immediately after return because it restores
forward momentum and lets Europe influence selection without becoming the
subject of the page.

### Full scroll

#### A1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · FRIDAY
Storms may interrupt this afternoon. Saturday opens clearer.
```

This is current physical truth. It does not say the person feels empty,
restless, nostalgic, or ready for adventure.

#### A2. Dominant stage — one prepared Saturday

```text
SATURDAY HAS A WATER ROUTE

Pier 11 -> Red Hook -> Sunset Park waterfront
[verified departure] · [experienced travel time] · [walking load]
[one available dinner continuation] · home by [honest return window]
```

Native form: a route field, not a destination card. It shows the ferry leg,
weather fit, meaningful stops, walking burden, dining continuation, and return
path. The trip evidence silently improves the judgment about arrival and
movement; the copy never says “because you like ferries.”

Primary action: **Open Saturday** → Places with route, time, evidence, and
alternatives intact. Seeing the shape is already useful; ignoring it creates no
Plan.

#### A3. In motion — the life that resumed

```text
[current local commitment]      [settled / changed / open]
[recovery item, if unresolved]  [owner · next material boundary]
[weekend opening with a person] [no decision yet]
```

Zero to three compact rows. Europe appears only when a recovery consequence is
still live.

#### A4. More than one way into the weekend

Three different trajectories, rendered as different instruments rather than a
carousel of venues:

```text
AFTER DARK
Vinyl Nights · Pier I · Saturday 6:00–9:30
An outdoor evening with a natural drop-in and exit.

MAKE
Sunday Sketch Jam · basic supplies available
A two-hour public practice, not an all-day plan.

AT HOME
The pasta technique in your photographs, reduced to one usable dinner.
```

Each treatment must expose enough time, effort, current source, and exit shape
to be evaluated on view. “At home” opens the capability treatment from World C;
the other two open scoped Places. No save prompt appears.

#### A5. A technique that survived the flight

```text
THE GLOSS COMES FROM WHAT HAPPENS BEFORE THE PLATE

[three supporting food photographs]
starch-rich water -> pan finishing -> emulsion held at serving
```

The annotation explains a mechanism already researched from the person's
question and identifies what the photographs do and do not prove. Optional
continuations are **Try it here** or **See it here**, never “Reflect on what
food means to you.”

#### A6. With people — only if there is real relational truth

```text
[person] is free after [time]
Your Saturday route can meet their evening without changing either afternoon.
```

This is a viewer-safe availability and reunion projection, not a social update.
If no authorized opening exists, this region disappears.

#### A7. One honest return

```text
SORRENTO -> AMALFI
The only crossing captured from ticket to arrival.
[source-backed horizontal sequence]
```

It is complete on view and opens the crossing in Life. It sits below present
possibility because recency is not importance.

#### A8. Stable doors

```text
All plans and occasions · New York in Places · Europe in Life
```

Object-level **Talk about this** appears only on treatments where conversation
can actually deepen or reshape the same opportunity.

### What this world proves

- Home can make the current city feel abundant without becoming a generic
  discovery feed.
- A recent trip can improve route, pacing, and capability judgment without
  being narrated as preference.
- Several possibilities can coexist when they differ in human mode and effort.

### Primary risk

If every candidate becomes an event or venue tile, the world collapses into a
personalized weekend guide. The route, public event, practice, home capability,
relationship, and reconstruction need visibly different jobs and forms.

## 4. Possible World B — the city becomes newly legible

### Organizing proposition

Vesper should sometimes lead with a substantial new lens that makes an
immediately reachable part of the world more intelligible. The contribution is
not “this reminds you of Rome.” It is the newly researched local mechanism the
person did not already supply.

### Full scroll

#### B1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · SATURDAY
The harbor is usable until evening. [Live wind, rain, and service state]
```

#### B2. Dominant stage — an interpretive route with operational substance

```text
A MILITARY SUPPLY BASE IS NOW TRAINING NEW YORK'S CLIMATE WORKFORCE

Brooklyn Army Terminal began as a military logistics complex. Today more than
100 businesses work there, BATWorks is building a climate-workforce program,
and site-specific art makes the waterfront's industry, labor, and ecology
visible in the same place.

Pier 11 -> Brooklyn Army Terminal
[verified ferry] · [public-access boundary] · [weather fit]
```

Native form: a three-layer Place passage joined to a live route field:

```text
1918–1966     military movement and supply
today         manufacturing, food, upcycling, and climate work
on site now   public art interpreting industry, labor, ecology, and community
```

The contribution adds a current New York case to the person's Rome question
about accumulated reuse. It names the important difference: Rome's layers
often involve imperial, religious, civic, and archaeological reuse; this
waterfront complex remains active industrial infrastructure. The cities are
connected along a dimension, not declared equivalent.

Primary action: **Open the waterfront** → Places. The route, access limits, and
sources travel with the handoff.

#### B3. The spatial field — one waterfront, several functions

```text
RED HOOK             former shipping center -> public pier + neighborhood
SUNSET PARK          military terminal -> working industrial campus
HARBOR CONNECTION    infrastructure that still moves people between them
```

A map shows present access, not historical pins. The person can enter at Red
Hook, continue to Sunset Park, or shorten the route without solving a quiz.

#### B4. In motion

Compact current commitments and any affected-person state remain visible.
Interpretive richness never displaces a real deadline or recovery boundary.

#### B5. A different kind of public evening

```text
THE CITY AFTER DARK DOESN'T HAVE TO BE A TOUR

Vinyl Nights at Pier I · Saturday 6:00–9:30
Drop in, leave freely, and let the public space carry the structure.
```

This is not recommended because Rome at night was hot or overwhelming. It is a
current, low-commitment counterform whose practical shape is made explicit.

#### B6. A farther connection — media can re-enter through place

```text
THE AENEAS QUESTION IS STILL OPEN, BUT IT DOESN'T BELONG TO NEW YORK YET

The next useful return needs a verified local object, exhibition, book,
screening, or conversation that adds a new mechanism. Until then, the thread
stays available in Life rather than occupying Home through thematic similarity.
```

This negative editorial judgment is itself important: the system can preserve
a promising thread without manufacturing a current connection.

#### B7. Evidence return

The actual Rome reuse observation, its user-authored source, researched
explanation, and the new BAT relation appear as an inspectable bridge in Life.
Home shows the contribution; Life shows why Vesper is entitled to make it.

#### B8. Stable doors

```text
The harbor in Places · Open Rome's layers in Life · Ask about this
```

### What this world proves

- Place intelligence can lead Home when it joins serious interpretation to a
  currently usable physical experience.
- Cross-place personalization can broaden the world rather than enclose the
  person in an old interest.
- A withheld connection can be more trustworthy than a weak one.

### Primary risk

This world can become an unsolicited magazine or museum lesson. The lead earns
Home only because the Place is reachable now, the route is practical, the new
mechanism is substantial, and the person can receive the value without reading
an essay or taking a tour.

## 5. Possible World C — the trip becomes capability

### Organizing proposition

An experience compounds when it changes what the person can do—not when Vesper
labels what the experience says about them. Home can lead with a fully prepared
act of cooking, hosting, seeing, moving, or making when the current window and
effort fit.

### Full scroll

#### C1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · SATURDAY
[open evening window] · home is a viable Place tonight
```

#### C2. Dominant stage — a complete act, not a prompt

```text
THE PASTA QUESTION HAS A NEW YORK ENDING

Dinner for four · [market or pantry path] -> cook -> table
[verified ingredient set] · 35 minutes active · one pan-finishing technique
```

The stage already contains:

- a short menu based on the actual technique, not a themed Italy reenactment;
- the ingredient and equipment gaps;
- the mechanism that produces emulsion;
- a realistic preparation and serving sequence;
- a graceful two-person version and a four-person version; and
- one optional invitation aperture when actual people and availability exist.

Primary action: **Use this tonight** → a lightweight Plan or Occasion preview.
No object is created until the person accepts the consequence.

#### C3. The mechanism, visible in the evidence

```text
[dish 1]              [dish 2]              [dish 3]
sauce clings          little pooling         finish still glossy

What the images support: a recurring surface result.
What outside knowledge adds: starch, agitation, fat, and finishing can produce it.
What remains unknown: exact ingredients and kitchen process in each restaurant.
```

This is a compact evidence comparison, not “you became a more discerning
eater.” It is useful even if the dinner never happens.

#### C4. If cooking is the wrong effort

```text
SEE THE DIFFERENCE HERE
[one source-verified New York kitchen or dish where the technique is legible]
[availability] · [distance] · [why this exact example, not generic Italian food]
```

Only one substitute appears. This is a lower-effort branch of the same
capability, not a restaurant carousel.

#### C5. Other capabilities that came home

A compact three-part field, each complete on view:

```text
ARRIVE   why verticality and luggage change a transfer judgment
PACE     why subtracting one stop can protect the part of an Occasion that matters
NOTICE   how reused material changes what becomes visible in an ordinary building
```

These are procedural distinctions supported by the trip. They do not become
claims that the person “travels through transitions” or now possesses a stable
trait.

#### C6. With people

If a real friend is available, the product may show:

```text
[friend] can join after [time]. The four-person version still fits.
```

The app may search known people or prepare an invitation only after invocation.
It does not infer who should be invited from affinity scores.

#### C7. One reconstructive return

The Sorrento menu corridor shows how four immediate translations became one
mechanism and one present capability. Life retains every Source and correction;
Home shows only the useful transformation.

#### C8. Farther horizon

```text
FROM TECHNIQUE TO LINEAGE
One future market, producer, regional distinction, book, or Place may deepen
the same question later. No reminder is created.
```

#### C9. Stable doors

```text
Cook in Chat · Ingredients and kitchens in Places · Food thread in Life
```

### What this world proves

- Continuity can become practical ability rather than memory content.
- Home can prepare a whole low-stakes Occasion without requiring setup work.
- Evidence, outside knowledge, current logistics, and people can form one
  coherent consequence.

### Primary risk

Capability can easily feel like assigned self-improvement. This world should
lead only when the effort matches the current window or an expressed intention.
The complete-on-view mechanism must remain valuable even when the person does
nothing.

## 6. Possible World D — other people enlarge the world

### Organizing proposition

Multiplayer is not a social feed and not merely consensus. Other people can
change what is visible, make separate intentions coexist, provide human
judgment, and turn private possibility into an effortless shared Occasion.

This world is intentionally strict: it may render richly only when actual
authorization and live relational truth exist. The fixture's mere mention of a
partner or Paris friends is not enough.

### Full scroll

#### D1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · SATURDAY
Two afternoons are open. Dinner is the shared edge.
```

This sentence requires two viewer-safe calendar or conversational facts. It
does not expose private reasons or declare how either person feels.

#### D2. Dominant stage — divergence without rupture

```text
TWO SATURDAYS, ONE DINNER

YOU                          [PARTNER]
Pier 11 -> waterfront        [their independently chosen afternoon]
[time and return path]       [viewer-safe time and return path]

TOGETHER
[verified dinner] · [meeting time] · [honest travel margins]
```

Native form: two private-authority lanes converging on one shared commitment.
Each person owns their lane; the Occasion owns only the meeting edge. Vesper
can adjust public logistics without revealing why someone chose rest, work,
shopping, solitude, or another plan.

Primary action: **Open Saturday together** → Occasion preview. The proposal is
read-only until both affected people have the appropriate authority.

#### D3. In motion — people as affected principals

```text
[person]  invited / joined / unaffected by change
[person]  contribution waiting / no response required
[decision] organizer may prepare / both must confirm
```

This is shared state, not activity. Counts, likes, and “friends are exploring”
do not appear.

#### D4. A social mosaic only Vesper can assemble

```text
ROME · [time]                         PARIS · [same time]
[authorized source]                  [friend-authorized source]
[specific changed plan or condition] [specific changed plan or condition]

[one grounded contrast neither side could observe alone]
```

Eligible dimensions include river use, heat adaptation, evening street life,
movement, food, or abandonment of plans. “You were both in Europe” is not a
contribution. Each source remains attributed, and the friend can correct or
withdraw their side.

Optional consequence: **Send this to [friend]** opens human-authored message
approval. There is no automatic publication or reply debt.

#### D5. A temporary social aperture

```text
JUST BACK · 10 DAYS
Nice -> Sorrento -> Amalfi -> Rome
[one chosen photograph or factual line]
Visible to [audience]
```

This is a private preview of a Status, not an active call to publish. It is
useful only when the person has already expressed the intention to become
socially legible. Exact words and media remain theirs.

#### D6. Human judgment where AI should defer

```text
[friend] knows Sorrento beyond the visitor layer.
The next fitting question can go to them instead of becoming generated advice.
```

The unit appears only when the relationship and person's stated knowledge make
the handoff appropriate. It does not repeatedly recommend contacting people.

#### D7. Shared occurrence, plural continuity

The Capri Occasion can show verified shared commitments, contributors, and
delivered artifacts while private Outcomes remain in each person's Life. No
“your perfect proposal” montage or inferred shared emotion appears.

#### D8. Stable doors

```text
People and Together in Life · Saturday Occasion · Talk privately in Chat
```

### Honest degraded state

If partner intent, friend Sources, or audience permission is absent, D2–D6 do
not fill with hypothetical people. Home falls back to World A or B and shows at
most a factual shared Occasion row. This is evidence that adaptive composition
needs admissibility, not a requirement to make every root equally full.

### What this world proves

- Multiplayer can increase perception and experiential range, not only reduce
  coordination cost.
- Private divergence and shared consequence can coexist in one instrument.
- Social legibility can be low-pressure, temporary, and audience-bounded.

### Primary risk

The relational world is the easiest to fake and the most damaging when wrong.
Availability, expertise, audience, authorship, and private intent cannot be
inferred from proximity, old messages, photographs, or graph strength.

## 7. Possible World E — the journey becomes usable truth

### Organizing proposition

Sometimes the most valuable post-return act is neither inspiration nor a new
outing. It is closing material loose ends and reconstructing what actually
happened well enough that later continuity rests on truth.

This world has two legitimate postures: **recovery still active** and
**recognition after closure**.

### Full scroll

#### E1. World read

Active recovery:

```text
NEW YORK · HOME
The travel ended. Two consequences have not.
```

Closed recovery:

```text
NEW YORK · FRIDAY
The return is settled. The weekend belongs to the present.
```

The first is allowed only when actual material obligations remain.

#### E2. Dominant stage — restore control or step aside

Active version:

```text
THE RETURN IS NOT CLOSED YET

[airline claim]     [required evidence] · [real deadline] · [prepared next step]
[work consequence] [confirmed reschedule or unresolved owner]

One action: Review and submit the material consequence.
```

The stage names owner, authority, sources, deadline, uncertainty, and what
Vesper has already prepared. It suppresses editorial material until the person
has control.

Closed version:

```text
NOTHING FROM THE RETURN NEEDS YOUR ATTENTION

[settled receipts] · [resolved work change] · [sources retained until date]
```

This receipt does not ask the person to archive, confirm, or reflect. In the
closed posture it should not necessarily dominate; World A's current opening
can replace it after one glance.

#### E3. The journey did not follow the bookings

```text
INTENDED                         ACTUAL
Rome -> New York                Rome -> [reroute] -> Chicago -> New York
[ticket sources]                [changes, lodging, meals, and known gaps]
```

Native form: paired route timelines with correction controls. The pattern is
factual; Vesper does not turn resilience or disruption into a personality
story.

#### E4. One crossing, fully reconstructed

```text
SORRENTO -> AMALFI
ticket -> departure -> coastline -> arrival
[timestamps] · [Place identities] · [known gap]
```

This is the strongest recognition unit because the sources become a sequence
the person never manually assembled.

#### E5. Four menus became one question

```text
TRANSLATE -> NOTICE -> EXPLAIN -> USE HERE
```

A compact causal thread shows which interactions ended immediately and which
earned continuity. The unit leads into the capability world only when the
current Moment supports it.

#### E6. The present re-enters

```text
SATURDAY STILL HAS ROOM
[one prepared New York opening from World A or B]
```

Even a continuity-led page must give the future a door before rendering a long
retrospective register.

#### E7. The factual trip register

```text
NICE -> SORRENTO -> CAPRI -> AMALFI -> ROME -> NEW YORK
[verified movement modes] · [temporary bases] · [unresolved source gaps]
```

Life owns correction, provenance, retention, and private Outcomes. Home shows
only the useful overview.

#### E8. Stable doors

```text
Recovery owner · Europe in Life · New York in Places
```

### What this world proves

- Factual reconstruction can be a substantive product contribution without
  pretending to be insight.
- Recovery can span movement, money, work, and relationship coordination after
  the nominal trip ends.
- A page can acknowledge closure and hand attention back to the present.

### Primary risk

Once material consequences are settled, this world quickly becomes a trip
report. It should then lose the dominant stage and contract into one or two
recognition instruments lower in World A, B, or F.

## 8. Possible World F — one life, several horizons

### Organizing proposition

This is the recommended default composition model. It does not pick one domain
as the identity of Home. It makes one current consequence dominant, then
composes a few different powers of the product across now, this weekend,
emerging continuity, people, and a farther horizon.

The unity comes from a point of view: **what this person's world makes possible
now**. It does not come from making every unit about Europe or forcing every
unit into the same card.

### Full scroll

#### F1. World read

```text
NEW YORK · FRIDAY
Storms this afternoon. Saturday opens clearer.
```

#### F2. Dominant stage

```text
SATURDAY HAS A WATER ROUTE
Pier 11 -> Red Hook -> Sunset Park waterfront
[live route, effort, availability, companion, and return truth]
```

One clear primary action. No other unit competes at the same elevation.

#### F3. In motion

Two or three compact rows account for current commitments, affected people,
and any material return loose end. The person can trust that Home has not hidden
obligation beneath inspiration.

#### F4. Four ways the world can open

```text
ENTER
The working waterfront as a usable half-day.

NOTICE
A 1918 military logistics complex now housing manufacturing,
climate work, and site-specific public art.

MAKE
The pasta-emulsion mechanism, already reduced to one dinner.

WITH
One viewer-safe person or social aperture—only when authorized.
```

These are not four equal cards. **Enter** may be a route strip, **Notice** a
three-layer Place passage, **Make** an annotated mechanism, and **With** a
face-addressed row or split/reunion lane. Their difference is part of the
product's legibility.

#### F5. One relationship across places

Preferred order:

1. an actual attributed Rome–Paris mosaic, if it adds a specific new contrast;
2. a local friend's situated lens where human authority is stronger;
3. a person-specific Saturday opening; or
4. no relational region.

The product does not substitute generic “friends nearby” activity.

#### F6. What the journey now lets you do

```text
ARRIVE with better margin
PACE an Occasion by subtraction
MAKE the texture you noticed
SEE reuse rather than merely age
```

This is a capability field grounded in actual episodes. It remains careful
about scope: each is a reusable distinction, not a stable theory of the person.

#### F7. What actually happened

The paired intended/actual return route or Sorrento–Amalfi crossing appears as
one evidence-first instrument. A quiet **Europe in Life** door exposes the full
register and corrections.

#### F8. Farther from here

```text
ONE THREAD WITH NO DEADLINE

[a source-bound future Place, season, book, exhibition, person, or Occasion]
Why it genuinely continues the question · what important difference remains
```

The horizon can be understood and ignored. It never becomes an automatic save,
reminder, or bucket-list entry.

#### F9. Stable doors

```text
All plans and occasions · New York in Places · Life · Chat
```

### Why this is the recommended default

- It keeps the current city and current obligations legible.
- It demonstrates Vesper's range without making the trip or one feature the
  organizing ontology.
- It lets practical, interpretive, creative, relational, and reconstructive
  value coexist without equal elevation.
- It can become much sparser under urgency and richer on an open day without
  changing the shell.

### Primary risk

Without an editorial thesis and diversity-aware composer, this becomes a
buffet of good cards. The page must read as one authored possible world: the
dominant stage establishes the present, supporting units open different powers,
and continuity widens rather than interrupts that present.

## 9. Comparison

| World | What dominates | Strongest product proof | When it should win | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. New York is waiting** | prepared local possibility | present momentum from rich context | ordinary return, open day, open weekend | personalized city guide |
| **B. City becomes legible** | current Place plus new mechanism | differentiated situated intelligence | unusually strong reachable connection | unsolicited magazine or tour |
| **C. Trip becomes capability** | prepared act of making or practicing | experience compounds into ability | expressed intention plus fitting effort | homework or self-improvement program |
| **D. People enlarge the world** | viewer-safe shared possibility | multiplayer increases perception and coherence | sufficient authorization and live relational truth | fabricated intimacy or social feed |
| **E. Journey becomes truth** | recovery or reconstruction | factual and operational continuity | unresolved consequence or rare recognition value | retrospective trip report |
| **F. Several horizons** | one current consequence inside diverse field | whole product coherence | default non-urgent Home | cafeteria without editorial composition |

### 9.1 These are not mutually exclusive content buckets

A BAT route can appear in A as a usable Saturday, in B as layered reuse, in D
as one lane of a shared day, and in F as both route and interpretive aperture.
The canonical Place remains the same. The current job determines the projection
and how much of the relation Home reveals.

The system should never show all four projections simultaneously. That would
be duplication disguised as richness.

### 9.2 The default selection rule

```text
if material recovery or safety is unresolved:
    foreground E and suppress most possibility
else if an affected-person consequence is current:
    foreground D's shared instrument
else if one prepared local possibility is unusually complete:
    foreground A
else if one reachable interpretation is unusually substantive:
    foreground B
else if one capability matches expressed intent and effort:
    foreground C
else:
    use F with truthful quiet and several low-pressure apertures
```

This is a design rule, not executable scoring logic. It distinguishes page
thesis from candidate rank and leaves room for silence.

## 10. Surface and component requirements generated by the gallery

The six worlds imply a bounded instrument family rather than one universal
card:

| Instrument | Needed for | Existing inspiration | New requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **World read** | current Place, time, weather, posture | Trips now band | neutral Home ownership and freshness |
| **Dominant stage frame** | one lead across route, recovery, shared state, capability, interpretation | Trips crown and receipts | content-shaped geometry without Trip semantics |
| **Route field** | A, B, D, F | Trip maps and Places canvas | experienced effort, return, and owner-preserving handoff |
| **Layered Place passage** | B, F | Places editorial renderers | source-bound timeline plus live access in one unit |
| **Possibility small multiple** | A, F | pre-pivot mixed Home sections | diversity across modes without equal-card shelves |
| **Annotated evidence comparison** | C, E, F | artifact renderers | visible claim scope, uncertainty, and mechanism |
| **Split/reunion lanes** | D | group rows and facepiles | personal authority lanes converging on shared edge |
| **Attributed social mosaic** | D, F | friend cards | dual provenance, audience, correction, withdrawal |
| **Paired actual/intended timeline** | E | receipts and movement rows | source gaps and correction without psychographic copy |
| **Capability field** | C, F | none as a coherent family | procedure, effort, transfer, and optional consequence |
| **Farther horizon** | B, C, F | editorial passages | future possibility with expiry and no save pressure |
| **Stable doors** | all | existing root chrome | exact context restoration after return |

The client still owns native geometry, accessibility, animation, and fallback.
The server or application layer may choose bounded treatment keys and ordering;
it must not emit arbitrary layout or styling.

## 11. Fixture contract for the native gallery

Each world should be encoded as a static fixture with the same source corpus:

```yaml
possible_world:
  id: post_return_nyc_a_through_f
  editorial_thesis: string
  world_read:
    place: source_bound
    time: source_bound
    conditions: source_bound
    freshness: timestamp
  dominant_stage:
    candidate_id: stable
    treatment_key: bounded
    action_authority: read | offer | confirm
  in_motion: []
  supporting_candidates:
    - human_mode: enter | notice | understand | make | relate | recognize
      temporal_horizon: now | weekend | emerging | farther
      complete_on_view: boolean
      demand_level: none | optional | decision
      owner_ref: canonical
      evidence_refs: []
      known_to_person_claims: []
      new_contribution: string
      viewer_scope: bounded
      treatment_key: bounded
      destination: typed
      degradation: quiet_row | alternate | silence
  suppressed_candidates:
    - candidate_id: stable
      reason: stale | unauthorized | redundant | already_known |
        excessive_demand | wrong_moment | weaker_contribution
```

Every fixture must include negative oracles:

- no personality explanation;
- no opening question;
- no generic recommendation shelf;
- no trip-wide hero by recency;
- no social content without authorization;
- no historical, event, weather, transport, or availability claim after its
  freshness boundary;
- no more than one unresolved consequential ask in the first viewport; and
- no duplicate projection of the same opportunity merely to fill regions.

## 12. Review questions for the rendered gallery

The full pages should be reviewed side by side at normal and large text sizes:

1. Can a new viewer say what each Home is offering within five seconds?
2. Does the first viewport contain the useful answer rather than a teaser?
3. Is one thing dominant without making the rest of the person's world look
   empty?
4. Do supporting units open different human powers, or merely different
   content categories?
5. Does the page feel current even when recent evidence supplies much of its
   intelligence?
6. Does every past-facing unit add reconstruction, mechanism, capability,
   relationship, or future consequence?
7. Can relational units disappear without leaving a broken or barren layout?
8. Does each visual register communicate its job before the copy is read?
9. Can the page become sparse during recovery without changing navigation or
   teaching a new shell?
10. Does following any opening preserve its causal context in Chat, Places,
    Life, Plan, or Occasion?
11. Does ignoring everything leave no residue, task, guilt, or response debt?
12. Which world feels most like Vesper rather than an excellent version of an
    adjacent product?

## 13. Recommendation

Render all six worlds from the same fixture before choosing components or
implementing dynamic composition.

Use **F — one life, several horizons** as the default whole-product hypothesis,
but require the gallery to prove the sharper extremes. A is the strongest
post-return baseline; B tests differentiated Place intelligence; C tests
capability rather than memory; D tests multiplayer as perception and
coordination; E tests whether factual continuity can be useful without taking
over.

The product should not expose these names or ask the person to choose a mode.
The eventual composer selects a thesis from current consequence and evidence,
then renders one stable Home with native instruments.

> **The person opens Home once. Vesper decides whether this moment is best
> served by a ready city, a newly legible world, a capability, another person,
> an honest reconstruction, or a carefully composed mixture of them.**
