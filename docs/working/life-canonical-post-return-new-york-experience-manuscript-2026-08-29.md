# Canonical Life Experience Manuscript — Post-Return New York

> Status: active founder-review artifact
>
> This is an editorial and behavioral manuscript, not a final screen design.
> It specifies what the person receives, in what order, what evidence is
> visible, what remains optional, and how objects move across roots. A
> dedicated visual tool owns geometry, typography, imagery, motion, and final
> component treatment after this manuscript passes value review.

## 0. Product decision

The canonical post-return Life experience is:

> **One strong return into a stable life record, with precise retrieval and
> optional continuations—not a retrospective feed.**

The first Life visit after Europe does not try to demonstrate every capability.
It contains:

1. a first-class search aperture;
2. one earned editorial Return;
3. the stable current-year and period spine;
4. episode-level experiential reconstruction;
5. Together only where an authorized contribution changes value;
6. cross-root Continue without current-world duplication; and
7. `What carried` only after a later consequence is supported.

The product hierarchy is:

```text
Life
  search
  one earned Return
  stable periods and episode doors
  compact global lenses

Europe Journey
  identity and supported spine
  experiential reconstruction
  Plans, changes, people, and Sources in place
  one substantive See anew contribution
  optional Together return
  Continue and repair

Home / Places
  current application

Life later
  Carried forward receipt, if earned
```

## 1. Evidence and fiction boundary

This manuscript uses three explicit evidence classes.

### 1.1 Founder-supplied or fixture-grounded

- Nice, Sorrento, the Amalfi coast, Rome, and return to New York;
- the August 14–27 represented period;
- travel Sources, photographs, stays, movement questions, route changes, a
  raw Nice hotel bill, and a raw Sorrento-to-Capri ferry ticket;
- six raw-inspected menu photos from two resolved venues, with exact named
  ingredients bound to their Source images;
- authored attention to Sorrento's verticality, ferry/road tradeoffs, heat,
  pasta texture, and the Colosseum/Aeneas connection;
- the pre-trip film/book relationship; and
- the desire for the current New York weekend to own Home.

### 1.2 Externally grounded editorial contribution

The Aeneas/Augustus treatment is admitted from the reviewed editorial brief:

- Virgil links Iulus to the future Julian line;
- Book 6 places Augustus among Iulus's descendants;
- the useful mechanism is political-literary lineage, not the broad claim that
  Rome adopted one side of the Trojan War; and
- the product must expose the literary and interpretive limits.

### 1.3 Explicitly synthetic prototype material

`Maya` and her Paris contribution are synthetic. So is the completed New York
waterfront Outcome used to demonstrate a later receipt. These exist only to
make authorization, social routing, reconciliation, and causal lineage
concrete. They must never be presented as facts about a real friend or the
founder's actual weekend.

Where exact transport Sources remain unresolved, the manuscript specifies the
truthful copy pattern rather than inventing a route, provider, or date.

The booked-but-not-taken ferry remains useful only in the controlled outcome
storyboard. It is not part of the adjudicated founder corpus.

## 2. State at opening

The person has returned to New York. The Europe Journey is historical and
eligible for a stable door. No New York weekend Plan or Occasion has yet been
created. There is no supported `Carried forward` receipt. The synthetic Maya
contribution is authorized only inside the controlled Together branch.

Therefore:

- `In motion` is absent;
- the New York weekend does not appear in Life;
- `What carried` is absent;
- no generic recent-trip recap appears;
- no reflection prompt appears;
- no global Map appears; and
- no Together region appears on the default entrance merely to advertise
  social capability.

Absence is part of the composition, not a missing-content error.

## 3. Scene 1 — The default Life entrance

### 3.1 Reading order

```text

    Nice → Rome · Aug 14–27

```

The page ends. It does not continue into archive filler or recommendations.

### 3.2 Search aperture

Visible copy:

> **Find a ticket, place, day, person…**

Search is prominent but not presented as an empty-state assignment. It accepts
natural language and direct filters. Raw wording and reformulations remain
session-local unless the person explicitly saves or corrects something.

### 3.3 The one earned Return

Visible copy:

```text
ACROSS TWO DINNERS

The menus made “local” visible at three scales.

GARDEN
Villa Zagara lemon · flowers and vegetables around the table

PENINSULA
Sorrento walnut · Monti Lattari beef · Provolone del Monaco

COAST
Amalfi Sfusato lemon · Re Fiascone, recovered in Tramonti

Six menu photos · two venues · Vesper compared and researched
```

Compact claim limit:

> **Menus and producers make these origin claims. They do not prove what you
> ordered or where every ingredient in these servings was grown.**

Optional apertures:

- `See the six menu photos`
- `How Re Fiascone came back`
- `Find one of these in New York`

There is no `What do you think?`, `Save this memory`, quiz, museum assignment,
or generic `Explore more` action.

Why it earns the entrance:

- it compares multiple Sources accumulated for other immediate jobs;
- the three-scale structure was not supplied by the person;
- it preserves the difference between menu language, provenance, and eating;
- it can open current New York value without creating work; and
- it demonstrates Life's corpus dividend rather than a generic trip recap.

The Aeneas/Iulus/Augustus contribution remains nested in Rome. It is strong
editorial value, but it should not be the first explanation of why Life exists.

### 3.4 Stable 2026 spine

Visible copy:

```text
2026

SUMMER
Nice → Rome · Aug 14–27
Returned · Nice, Sorrento, Amalfi coast, Rome
```

If the source graph contains a material unresolved relation, append:

> `Some movement remains unresolved`

Do not show artifact counts, completion percentages, `places that changed`,
`threads still open`, or an AI-written period name.

### 3.5 Global lenses

The default compact utility row is:

> `Timeline · Sources · People`

`Map` is absent globally in this composition. It appears inside a city,
neighborhood, route, or episode only when spatial form explains something.
People opens governed attributed relationships and contributions, not a social
activity feed.

### 3.6 Default-entrance stopping rule

The entrance has already delivered one substantive contribution and one
stable way into the record. It stops. It does not populate sections for empty
product moves, media types, social supply, or available data.

## 5. Branch B — Open the Europe Journey

### 5.1 Episode identity

Visible copy:

```text
Nice → Rome
Aug 14–27 · Returned

Nice · Movement south · Sorrento and Amalfi · Rome · Return to New York
```

Secondary utilities:

> `Timeline · Sources · People · Episode map`

The episode map is local to this Journey and must distinguish supported,
planned-only, and unresolved movement.

### 5.2 The occurred spine

The manuscript presents chapters, not one card per object:

```text
NICE
Supported stay and encounters
Menus, hotel bill, departure Source

MOVEMENT SOUTH
Nice-to-Naples ticket Source referenced; exact fields require re-inspection

SORRENTO AND AMALFI
Sorrento-to-Capri ticket · Wed Aug 19 · Marina Piccola
Supported Places, menu Sources, photographs, authored observations
Port/town verticality and ferry/road questions remain attached here

ROME
Supported Colosseum encounter
Unused admission or Plan evidence remains not-lived where applicable
Film/book Source, your Aeneas observation, and Vesper's sourced contribution

RETURN TO NEW YORK
Two AA235 FCO–JFK cancellations
Rerouting through Chicago; airline-provided hotel, transport, and meal
Cancellation cause and exact final arrival remain unresolved
```

These labels are truth-oriented scaffolding. The rendered experience should
let original Sources, images, movement states, and Place relations carry the
texture rather than narrating every line.

### 5.3 Experiential reconstruction

One bounded reconstruction is available inside the coast chapter:

```text
THE COAST, THROUGH ARRIVAL

On this coast, “nearby” depended on how you arrived.

Your questions joined what a map separated: ferry check-in, luggage, the port,
the climb into Sorrento, road alternatives, and the margin left in the day.

Captured here
  [authorized coast photographs in supported order]

Moved here
  [supported movements; planned-only states visibly separate]

Not resolved
  [only material remaining gaps]
```

This is representational and experiential value, not a claim that Vesper
discovered the person's identity or preference. If supported movement cannot
make the reconstruction materially better than the chapter record, this
region is absent.

No final sentence interprets the person. No reflection question follows.

### 5.4 Plans and changes in place

Changed Plans appear beside the movement they affected only when the actual
Source graph supports the divergence:

```text
PLANNED
[booking]

SUPPORTED
[actual movement, if known]

UNRESOLVED
[only remaining relation]
```

The product does not label the divergence a mistake, regret, or travel style.

### 5.5 Sources in place

Source group labels remain ordinary:

- `Tickets and movement`
- `Stays and receipts`
- `Photographs`
- `Notes and questions`
- `Films, books, and generated readings`

`All sources` opens episode-scoped retrieval. Manual filing is never required.

## 6. Branch C — Open the Aeneas contribution

### 6.1 Exact content

```text
Aeneas gave Augustus a family tree inside Rome's origin story.

You had already connected the Colosseum, Troy, and the film you watched before
the trip. The additional mechanism is Iulus.

Aeneas
  ↓ father of
Ascanius / Iulus
  ↓ ancestral name used for
The Julian line
  ↓ claimed by
Julius Caesar and Augustus

Virgil makes the linkage legible twice: Book 1 connects Iulus's name to the
Julian line; Book 6 places Augustus among Iulus's descendants.

LIMIT
This is a literary and political lineage. It does not show that every Roman
accepted one version of the story, and it does not reduce Virgil to propaganda.
```

### 6.2 Claim-local source roles

```text
YOU NOTICED
The Colosseum encounter connected Rome back to Troy and the earlier film.

THE TEXT SUPPORTS
Virgil, Aeneid 1 and 6

VESPER CONNECTED
Iulus made Aeneas's family line usable as Julian and Augustan lineage.
```

The person may inspect sources, correct their original note, keep the reading,
or hide the Return without damaging the Journey.

### 6.3 Optional continuation

- `Open the evidence`
- `Follow this thread in New York`
- `Discuss in Chat`

`Follow this thread in New York` creates no Plan or reminder. It hands the
source relation and current Moment to Home or Places, which must find a current
substantive opening or remain silent.

## 7. Branch D — Together inside the Journey

This branch exists only in the synthetic controlled social state. It is absent

```text
Maya found a different way through the same kind of hot evening.
```

### 7.2 Two attributed lanes

Synthetic fixture copy:

```text
ROME · YOU
“There is so much everywhere, but in this heat even a good tour feels like
homework.”

PARIS · MAYA
“We stopped trying to fit in another neighborhood and stayed by the water
until it cooled down.”

VESPER CONNECTED
The useful common mechanism is not the two cities. In both accounts, reducing
```

Context strip:

> `Your authored Rome observation · Maya's direct contribution · city-level
> context only`

The composition does not state where Maya stayed, reveal a route, infer an
emotion, or claim a shared feeling.

### 7.3 Human knowledge route

If the viewer wants the missing human detail:

> **Maya may know more about how she found that place. This is everything she
> chose to share here.**

Actions:

- `Open Maya's note`
- `Ask Maya` — opens an editable human-authored request in Chat
- `Hide this comparison`

No automatic message is sent. No response is expected. Removing Maya's grant
recompiles the Journey without this branch and without deleting independent
Rome history.

## 8. Branch E — Carry into the current New York weekend

Life does not foreground the upcoming weekend. The person chooses `Use this in
New York`, `Follow this thread in New York`, or encounters a separately earned
current opening in Home.

### 8.1 Home projection

Controlled manuscript copy:

```text
THIS WEEKEND

Set the return edge first; keep the waterfront day loose.

The coast trip is relevant for one bounded reason: transport conditions changed
the usable shape of the day. New York's terrain and network are different, but
one crossing or final return can still become the hard edge.

See current waterfront options
```

This is complete on view. It does not require the person to create a Plan or
explain what they want.

### 8.2 Places projection

Places owns:

- current waterfront candidates;
- origin-dependent reachability;
- live ferry, subway, bus, walking, and operating truth;
- weather and return constraints;
- differences from the coast mechanism; and
- current alternatives.

Places explains:

```text
WHY THIS MAY TRANSFER
One hard return edge can determine the usable day.

WHAT DIFFERS HERE
New York is flatter and has different transport redundancy. Current service,
origin, weather, and companions decide whether the analogy holds.

USEFUL NOW
Choose one timed anchor only if live truth makes it the real constraint.
```

The person may use the structure, create a Plan or Occasion, continue in Chat,
reject the analogy, or ignore it.

## 9. Later state — `What carried`

This region is absent in the initial state. It appears only after a supported
later consequence.

### 9.1 Synthetic reconciled Outcome

For prototype purposes only, assume the person created a waterfront Plan,
fixed the return crossing, left the remaining day open, and later supplied or
governed evidence supports that structure.

### 9.2 Receipt at the consequence

```text
CARRIED FORWARD

The return crossing stayed fixed; the rest of Saturday stayed open.

FROM
The coast movement thread

CHANGED HERE
One return became the anchor instead of fixing the entire day

RESULT
[supported Occurrence or the person's authored Outcome]

Inspect · Correct · Stop using this connection
```

The receipt first appears in Home, Plan, or Occasion where the consequence
happened. It later becomes available in Life.

### 9.3 Later Life placement

Life now contains:

```text
WHAT CARRIED

Coast movement → New York waterfront day
One prior mechanism · one later consequence
```

Opening it shows both episodes, their shared mechanism, material difference,
known result, and correction path. It does not merge Europe and New York into
one Journey or state that Vesper caused the meaning of Saturday.

If the suggestion was merely viewed, ignored, or not supported by later
evidence, this region remains absent.

## 10. Return visit and adaptive behavior

The stable 2026 spine remains in the same place on later visits. The dynamic
Return may change only when another candidate has higher complete value and
passes authority and resurfacing gates.

Allowed changes:

- the Aeneas contribution disappears from the entrance after `Not now` or
  `Never surface this`, while remaining retrievable if permitted;
- an addressed Together return appears when a real authorized contribution
  becomes newly useful;
- a `Carried forward` receipt becomes available after reconciliation; or
- the Returns region becomes empty.

Disallowed changes:

- Journey doors reorder around click probability;
- a searched Source becomes an inferred interest;
- a recent photo becomes a Return without new value;
- Maya's contribution becomes ambient friend activity;
- the New York weekend appears in Life before a governed object exists; or
- dismissed content reappears under rewritten copy with no new evidence.

## 13. Demand and interaction budget

The default Life entrance has:

- **one search aperture**;
- **one earned cross-artifact contribution**;
- **one stable period spine**;
- **three compact lenses**; and
- **zero required actions**.

The opened Journey may contain many Sources but only one dominant editorial
operation. Together and Continue are contextual. Trust controls remain
available without competing with reading.

The composition fails if the person must choose among several equal generated
stories, interpret a system diagram, answer a question, manage an album, or
understand internal nouns before receiving value.

## 14. Manuscript evaluation

| Dimension | Score | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Completed value | 2 | Menu comparison, Aeneas contribution, retrieval result, and reconstruction can each end on view. |
| Novel delta | 2 | The product performs sourced explanation, truthful reconstruction, transfer, and human routing. |
| Truth | 2 | Plan, Source, Occurrence, generated contribution, and Outcome remain distinct. |
| Authority | 2 | Social and cross-root behavior is purpose-, viewer-, and action-bounded. |
| Identity restraint | 2 | Mechanisms stay contextual; no behavior becomes personality. |
| Burden | 2 | There are no required reflection, filing, sharing, or repair actions. |
| Root coherence | 2 | Life preserves; Home and Places deliver current consequence; Chat handles interrogation and authorship. |
| Compounding proof | 2 | `Carried forward` appears only after supported later consequence. |
| Repair | 2 | Source-, episode-, person-, composition-, and resurfacing-level repair are available. |
| Specialist delta | 2 | The integrated continuity, provenance, transfer, and human routing exceed any one adjacent specialist. |
| **Total** | **20/20 contract score** | Requires founder content judgment and later real-fixture validation; this is not a usability score. |

The perfect contract score means the manuscript contains the required
behavioral safeguards. It does not prove that the experience is compelling,
beautiful, understandable, or technically feasible.

## 15. Founder-review questions

Founder review should answer only these composition-bearing questions:

1. Does the menu comparison make Vesper's accumulated-evidence dividend clear
   within five seconds, or does it still read as food editorial?
2. Does `Worth returning to` sound like the correct human label, or should the
   Return appear without a named region?
3. Is the stable 2026 spine substantial enough after one contribution, or does
   it still feel like an archive index?
4. Does the Journey reconstruction restore experience, or merely explain the
   record more elegantly?
5. Does the Maya branch demonstrate human value, or does it still feel like
   social content with permission machinery?
6. Is `What carried` understandable without teaching causal architecture?
7. Is the default page appropriately finite, or does it feel underpowered
   given Vesper's corpus?

## 16. Visual handoff boundary

After founder approval, a dedicated visual tool should render at least three
complete strategies over this exact manuscript:

1. stable index with one expressive Return;
2. period-led living folio with the Return embedded near its episode; and
3. expressive episode-forward entrance with the stable spine immediately
   recoverable.

Every strategy must preserve:

- the same exact copy and evidence roles;
- the same initial absences;
- target-first search;
- one dominant Return;
- stable episode identity;
- contextual rather than global Map;
- optional Together and Continue;
- the causal receipt's later arrival; and
- complete value without input.

Visual exploration may change hierarchy if it demonstrates a materially better
composition, but it may not invent new content, weaken source roles, expose
unauthorized social material, or convert Life into an infinite feed.
