# Audience-Relative Trace Fixture Pack

## Decision

Treat audience as an architecture-bearing input to value admission, not as a
redaction setting applied after one universal card has been generated.

This pack holds four evidence worlds relatively stable while changing the
viewer:

1. Sorrento arrival, verticality, luggage, and the port-to-town threshold;
2. *The Odyssey*, Aeneas, Rome, and Augustan political use;
3. permissioned Rome and Paris evening traces; and
4. a lightweight shared dinner whose human contributions change what happens.

For each world, the pack separates:

- canonical state or factual record;
- attributed human contribution;
- Vesper editorial contribution;
- operational consequence or instrument; and
- rejection or silence.

It then asks what, if anything, should be shown to:

- the person themselves;
- a close friend or direct recipient;
- an acquaintance or weak tie; and
- a stranger or public viewer.

The result is a semantic and authority fixture, not final visual design, a
runtime schema, a claim that the examples are implemented, or permission to
publish founder material.

## 1. The proposition under test

A trace does not have one intrinsic social value. Its possible value is a
function of its job, viewer, relationship, moment, authority, and the operation
performed over it:

```text
governed source evidence
  × viewer and relationship
  × purpose and moment
  × authority intersection
  × real epistemic or practical operation
  -> state | instrument | human contribution | editorial contribution | silence
```

This means the same underlying evidence should not become four increasingly
redacted versions of one post. It may instead produce four different outcomes:

- a private reconstruction for the person;
- an attributed handoff adapted to a friend's situation;
- a deliberately featured human expression for an acquaintance;
- a separately authorized, transferable Place contribution for the public; or
- no projection at all.

The burden rises as shared relational context falls. A close friend may receive
value from continuity itself. A public viewer needs the contribution to carry
its own explanation, utility, provenance, and limits.

## 2. Evidence and fixture discipline

### 2.1 Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **FOUNDER-PROVENANCE** | A product-generating observation preserved in the founder fieldwork ledger; not automatically publishable user content |
| **FIXTURE** | Controlled data invented for the test; not a claim about an actual person, trip, provider, or condition |
| **RESEARCH-REQUIRED** | A historical, geographic, operational, or scientific production claim that needs current authoritative support |
| **HUMAN-AUTHORED** | Words or media supplied or explicitly approved by a person for the stated audience and purpose |
| **VESPER-AUTHORED** | A bounded system contribution whose sources, claim scope, and uncertainty remain inspectable |
| **OWNER-READBACK** | Canonical state returned by Place, Plan, Occasion, Commitment, provider, or another domain owner |

The exact Sorrento route values, lift hours, Rome and Paris conditions, Aeneas
history, and dinner participants below are fixtures unless a cited production
source establishes them. The friend named Maya is a controlled fixture actor.
No audience permission is inferred from the founder stories.

### 2.2 Lane resolution precedes writing

Every candidate receives one primary disposition before headline, copy, card,
or medium is considered:

| Disposition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **STATE** | Render canonical truth directly; generated narration would add ceremony |
| **INSTRUMENT** | Integrate the evidence into a route, option set, preparation, repair, or other working consequence |
| **HUMAN** | Preserve the person's authored perspective with author, audience, purpose, and expiry |
| **EDITORIAL** | Vesper adds restructure, explanation, differentiation, or transfer beyond what the viewer already knows |
| **BOUNDARY** | Prepare an exact audience or action effect and ask once before crossing it |
| **REJECT** | The candidate is false, invasive, derivative, unauthorized, or lower value than a smaller treatment |
| **SILENCE** | Even a truthful candidate does not earn attention in this viewer's present composition |

More than one lane may compose an experience, but they remain visibly
distinct. A human note can become an input to an instrument. An editorial
explanation can sit beside canonical state. Neither permits Vesper to absorb
the human voice or manufacture a new audience.

### 2.3 Contribution brief

Before admitting an EDITORIAL candidate, record:

```yaml
contribution_brief:
  viewer: null
  present_job: null
  known_to_viewer: []
  source_refs: []
  human_authors: []
  vesper_operation: restructure | explain | differentiate | transfer
  new_claim_or_power: null
  why_this_viewer: null
  why_now: null
  authority_basis: null
  forbidden_inferences: []
  lead_medium: evidence | sequence | comparison | spatial | prose | instrument
  durable_owner: null
  correction_and_revocation: []
  silence_counterfactual: null
```

If `new_claim_or_power`, `authority_basis`, or `silence_counterfactual` cannot
be completed, the candidate does not proceed to copy.

### 2.4 Audience matrix

| Viewer | Default source eligibility | What can earn projection | Default failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Self** | Private governed evidence plus sources authorized for private use | reconstruction, correction, continuity, changed capability, or a timely consequence | repeating the person's observation or exposing storage as value |
| **Close friend / direct recipient** | Only material directly addressed, mutually visible, or deliberately shared to that relationship | attributed perspective, shared-history continuation, recipient-specific handoff, Occasion improvement | attaching the author's name to generic activity or drafting intimacy for them |
| **Acquaintance / weak tie** | Deliberately featured, bounded expression whose audience includes this viewer | a legitimate overlap, useful distinction, or low-pressure opening that carries its own context | inferred status, ambient location, or private context used to simulate familiarity |
| **Public / stranger** | Explicit public contribution or independently sourced public truth | transferable explanation, operational utility, or positional Place knowledge with provenance and limits | laundering private evidence through paraphrase or anonymity |

### 2.5 Admission gates

A projection is admitted only when all applicable gates pass:

1. **Truth:** the evidence supports the claim at the stated level.
2. **Lane:** state, instrument, human expression, and Vesper editorial work are
   not conflated.
3. **Novelty:** Vesper does not present the person's own connection as a new
   discovery.
4. **Social transformation:** removing the other person would materially reduce
   understanding, possibility, coordination, consequence, or continuity.
5. **Authority:** the viewer may receive both the source and the derived use.
6. **Authorship:** human meaning and relational expression remain human.
7. **Present value:** the unit beats silence inside the whole composition now.
8. **Completion:** value is available on view and creates no reply or reflection
   debt.
9. **Repair:** correction, expiry, or revocation can invalidate every dependent
   projection.

## 3. Fixture A — Sorrento arrival is a vertical transfer

### 3.1 Controlled evidence packet

**FOUNDER-PROVENANCE:** the founder asked about Marina Piccola versus Marina
Grande, ferry check-in, luggage, the walk into town, and the way Sorrento's
verticality changes arrival. The person already knows that the marina and town
are vertically separated. That fact cannot be returned as editorial novelty.

**FIXTURE:**

- a ferry ticket establishes a scheduled arrival at Marina Piccola, not that
  the ferry was taken;
- a later hotel check-in independently supports arrival in Sorrento;
- verified Place geometry distinguishes port level, town level, lift, stairs,
  and road access;
- a route provider supplies current lift state, walking burden, and taxi
  fallback; and
- the person deliberately authors this scoped note after the trip:

> The hotel looked close to the ferry, but with luggage the lift hours decided
> whether it was actually close.

The note is initially private. Its authorship does not establish permission to
send, feature, or publish it.

### 3.2 Lane ledger

| Candidate | Disposition | Ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled port, verified arrival, lift state, route burden | STATE | Useful truth; keep schedule, occurrence, and current world state distinct |
| Port-to-hotel route selected by bags, heat, lift state, and margin | INSTRUMENT | This is the strongest live value; it should work rather than become an essay |
| “The lift hours decided whether it was actually close” | HUMAN | Situated judgment belongs to the person and retains scope |
| Why map distance misprices a threshold settlement; how to transfer that distinction to another arrival | EDITORIAL | Earned only when Vesper adds mechanism or a consequential comparison |
| “Sorrento has cliffs” or “the marina and town are vertically separated” as a crown card | REJECT | Specific state, but no new epistemic operation |
| “You experience travel through transitions” | REJECT | Unsupported identity interpretation |

### 3.3 Self projection

**Current arrival or return:** INSTRUMENT, not a reflection card.

> **The lift is the easiest arrival with bags.** It is operating now; the
> stair route is shorter on the map but adds the full port-to-town climb. Use
> the road fallback if the lift closes before you arrive.
>
> Port state + route burden · checked now

The exact route, condition, and fallback must come from current authorities.
The compact projection completes the judgment; opening it reveals the route,
evidence, and fallback rather than the withheld answer.

**Post-return in New York:** normally SILENCE on Home. Life may preserve the
ticket, supported arrival, route reconstruction, and authored note. Places may
reuse the distinction only when a current Place or question makes it useful.
The abundance of trip data does not give Sorrento permanent Home priority.

### 3.4 Close-friend projection

Assume a friend tells the person they are arriving in Sorrento tomorrow with a
suitcase. The person explicitly chooses **Address**, selects the authored note,
and lets Vesper attach a current route check.

**HUMAN + INSTRUMENT:**

> **From Fei:** “The hotel looked close to the ferry, but with luggage the
> lift hours decided whether it was actually close.”
>
> For your arrival tomorrow, the lift is scheduled to be operating. The road
> route is the fallback if the ferry is late. Check again on arrival.

Why this works:

- the human supplied the judgment;
- Vesper adapts it to the recipient's actual arrival rather than repackaging a
  generic tip;
- the friend receives complete practical value without needing to reply; and
- removing the relationship removes the situated handoff and leaves only a
  public route check.

This is a BOUNDARY before send. Vesper may prepare it; the person initiates the
relational act.

### 3.5 Acquaintance projection

Default: SILENCE. A weak tie should not receive the private note because both
people recently mentioned Italy, because their locations overlap, or because
Vesper predicts a conversation opener.

If the person deliberately features a friends-visible Status containing the
note, an acquaintance inside that audience may see the human-authored
expression with its expiry. Vesper can supply a non-invasive affordance such as
`Open Sorrento`, but not a synthetic greeting, inferred invitation, or ambient
arrival status.

### 3.6 Public projection

The private trace does not travel. Two separate public paths are possible:

1. the person explicitly **Publishes to Place** a reviewed, attributed version
   of the note; or
2. Vesper independently researches a public Place explanation and uses no
   private human evidence in the public claim.

The second path may say:

> **In Sorrento, walking distance omits the main arrival cost.** Port-to-center
> routes differ less by horizontal distance than by how they cross the coastal
> rise: lift, stairs, or road. With luggage, compare the transfer, operating
> state, and fallback—not only the pin-to-pin distance.

This is a VESPER-AUTHORED public explanation only if the geometry and operations
are independently sourced. It is not an anonymized version of the person's
private note.

### 3.7 Surface and owner ruling

| Surface | Legitimate role | Non-conforming treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Current arrival consequence or a truly timely transfer; otherwise silence | post-trip “what the cliffs taught you” card |
| World Field | One returned distinction only when it changes a present spatial possibility | Europe recap module inside New York browse |
| Place Focus | Verified geometry, relationship trace, and attributed note within scope | flattening human note into Vesper recommendation |
| Place Path | Explain one consequential edge such as distance versus vertical transfer | analogy without a meaningful difference or current use |
| Live Reduction | Route, burden, margin, fallback, and freshness | prose pretending to be a working route |
| Life | Ticket, supported occurrence, reconstruction, note, custody, and correction | treating the ticket as visit proof or identity evidence |

### 3.8 Pass and failure oracles

Pass if the person receives a working arrival judgment, the friend receives an
attributed and adapted handoff only after Address, and the public receives
either a separately authorized human contribution or independently sourced
Place utility.

Fail if the system repeats “Sorrento has cliffs,” widens the private note,
claims the ferry ticket proves travel, or turns a route problem into a poetic
statement about the person.

## 4. Fixture B — Aeneas becomes Augustan political machinery

### 4.1 Controlled evidence packet

**FOUNDER-PROVENANCE:** the person had already connected *The Odyssey*, the
Colosseum, Troy, Aeneas, and Rome. The person explicitly rejected a response
that merely restated that connection.

**FIXTURE / RESEARCH-REQUIRED:**

- a privately imported movie ticket supports that the ticket existed, not that
  the film was watched;
- a person-authored statement establishes that they watched the film and made
  the Troy-to-Rome connection;
- independently verified sources establish the Julian descent claim, Virgil's
  Augustan context, and relevant monumental programs; and
- disputed monument identifications remain disputed rather than being used as
  the hinge of the explanation.

Known-to-person blocklist:

```yaml
known_to_person:
  - Aeneas escaped Troy
  - Aeneas belongs to Rome's origin tradition
  - the film and the Colosseum created a cross-medium connection
do_not_pitch_as_novel:
  - Rome chose the survivor's side of the Trojan War
  - your movie became relevant in Rome
```

### 4.2 Lane ledger

| Candidate | Disposition | Ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Movie ticket, authored viewing claim, Colosseum encounter | STATE | Separate evidence, occurrence, and authored connection |
| “The film reminded you of Aeneas” | REJECT | Parrots the person's own connection |
| Augustan use of Trojan ancestry to connect dynasty, piety, destiny, and political order | EDITORIAL | A grounded mechanism beyond the known claim |
| “Rome adopted the survivor's side” | REJECT | Attention-grabbing but historically imprecise and under-explanatory |
| “You are drawn to exile and survival” | REJECT | Unsolicited identity reading |
| A source-linked Rome reading path | INSTRUMENT only if the person elects depth | Optional access, not the price of receiving the core claim |

### 4.3 Self projection

**EDITORIAL, lead concise prose with supporting evidence:**

> **Augustus turned Trojan ancestry into an argument for who should rule
> Rome.** The Julian family claimed descent through Aeneas's line; Virgil's
> *Aeneid* joined that family story to Rome's destiny, while the Augustan public
> program made dynasty, piety, and order visible together. The myth did not
> merely supply an origin—it helped make a new political order look inherited.
>
> Your stated connection + verified literary and material sources

This is admitted because it explains a mechanism the person did not supply. It
must expose sources and scope, and it creates no new belief, preference, or
identity record by default.

### 4.4 Close-friend projection

The private movie and Colosseum traces remain private. If the person explicitly
shares the sourced Vesper artifact and authors a message, the friend may receive
both within the named relationship:

> **From Fei:** “I knew Aeneas linked Troy to Rome; I hadn't realized how much
> Augustus made that lineage do politically.”
>
> **Attached Vesper explanation:** Augustus turned Trojan ancestry into an
> argument for who should rule Rome. · 2 min · Sources

The person writes or approves the first sentence. Vesper may not generate it as
an unlabeled intimate message. The friend can read the complete claim without
replying.

Without explicit Address or Share, the close friend receives SILENCE even if
Vesper predicts shared interest in mythology.

### 4.5 Acquaintance projection

The only normal path is a deliberately featured, time-limited HUMAN Status or
an explicitly shared editorial artifact. For example, the person may author:

> Watched *The Odyssey* before Rome; the Colosseum sent me down the
> Aeneas-and-Augustus rabbit hole.

The Status creates a legitimate aperture because the person chose the
expression and audience. Vesper may attach the complete sourced claim, but it
must not infer that the acquaintance should contact the person, disclose the
private ticket, or convert a view into relationship evidence.

### 4.6 Public projection

A public Place explanation can exist without the person's personal trace:

> **Augustus made Rome's Trojan past point toward his own dynasty.**
>
> [bounded, source-grounded explanation]

Its evidence is public scholarship; its owner is a sourced editorial artifact
or Place projection. The person's private ticket and authored connection are
not hidden inputs. If the person separately publishes their account, that is a
HUMAN contribution with explicit attribution and public authority.

### 4.7 Surface and owner ruling

| Surface | Legitimate role | Non-conforming treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Compact first real claim when Rome, the film, or a current question makes it newly relevant | generic trip-memory card weeks later |
| Place Focus | Rome identity plus one source-bound lens if it outranks other current value | magazine stack of disconnected history |
| Place Path | Film → authored connection → Augustan mechanism, with disputed claims marked | graph showing similarity without explaining the edge |
| Life | Movie Source, authored viewing/connection, Rome episode, and sourced artifact as separate authorities | final “what Rome means to you” biography |
| Chat | Inspect, challenge, compare sources, or ask for depth | forcing the person to narrate more before receiving substance |

### 4.8 Pass and failure oracles

Pass if the first sentence advances beyond the known connection, the personal
trace stays private until deliberately shared, and the public artifact remains
valid without personal data.

Fail if the output could be produced by paraphrasing the person's words, makes
an identity claim, relies on a disputed image without disclosure, or treats
interest prediction as social permission.

## 5. Fixture C — Rome and Paris become one permissioned comparison

### 5.1 Controlled evidence packet

**FOUNDER-PROVENANCE:** a friend in Paris during the Rome trip was proposed as
a second lens. The value is not that two friends were in Europe. It is a
dimension neither person could observe alone.

**FIXTURE:**

- the person has a private Rome evening route, timestamped photographs, and a
  private authored note about heat and movement;
- Maya has a Paris evening route, timestamped photographs, and the authored
  caption “We kept following the river because the air finally moved”;
- Maya features that trace to `friends`, including the person, for seven days;
- the person has not shared their Rome trace with Maya;
- independently sourced conditions establish comparable time, temperature,
  route, and public-space axes; and
- neither person has authorized public reuse, quotation outside the stated
  audience, or inferred emotional meaning.

### 5.2 Lane ledger

| Candidate | Disposition | Ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routes, timestamps, temperature, audience, expiry | STATE | Necessary comparative axes and authority |
| Maya's caption | HUMAN | Keep her words, scope, and expiry |
| Rome-versus-Paris comparison on heat, river, movement, and public life | EDITORIAL | Differentiation becomes possible only because both evidence sets exist |
| “Your friend was also in Europe” | REJECT | Social fact without transformation |
| “You both seek water when overwhelmed” | REJECT | Unsupported shared psychology |
| “Send Maya this comparison” | BOUNDARY | A new audience consequence; never automatic |

### 5.3 Self projection

The person's private projection may use Maya's friends-visible trace while that
grant remains active:

> **The same hot evening produced two different public lives.** Your Rome route
> stayed near fountain edges and late tables while Maya's Paris walk kept moving
> along the Seine after the temperature fell. The useful difference is not
> Rome versus Paris in general; it is how heat and the cities' water edges
> changed what the evening could hold.
>
> Your private Rome trace + Maya's friends-visible Paris trace · expires with
> Maya's share

Lead medium: comparison. Supporting medium: attributed evidence or spatial
route. The exact claim must stay within the shared axes; it cannot infer why
either person felt or chose what they did.

### 5.4 Close-friend projection

Even though Maya is the close friend and authored half the comparison, she
cannot receive the person's private Rome evidence by symmetry. Until the person
chooses Share or Address, Maya receives SILENCE.

If the person shares the Rome trace back to Maya, a relationship-scoped social
mosaic becomes admissible:

> **Two evening routes, one heat threshold**
>
> Rome — Fei's shared route and note
>
> Paris — Maya's shared route and note
>
> Vesper: the river and fountain edges did different work as the cities cooled.

Each person remains author of their observation. Vesper owns only the bounded
comparison. Either revocation removes the affected half and invalidates the
mosaic rather than leaving a fluent orphan summary.

### 5.5 Acquaintance projection

An acquaintance included in Maya's friends audience may see Maya's original
Status. That does not grant access to the person's Rome trace or to the private
comparison created for the person.

If both people deliberately feature compatible traces to an audience that
includes the acquaintance, Vesper may compute a viewer-safe comparison only
when the allowed purposes include that derived use. Two visible posts do not
automatically authorize synthesis. Default: show the authored traces separately
or remain silent.

### 5.6 Public projection

Default: SILENCE. Removing names, faces, or exact routes does not turn the two
friends-visible traces into public data.

A public Rome–Paris comparison may be created from independent public weather,
urban-form, and observation sources, but that is a different VESPER-AUTHORED
artifact. It may not imply that these two people enacted the public pattern.
Alternatively, both people may separately authorize a public social mosaic,
with attribution and revocation terms preserved.

### 5.7 Surface and owner ruling

| Surface | Legitimate role | Non-conforming treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | One timely private comparison complete on view | standalone “Maya is in Paris” social card |
| World Field | Optional attributed social layer only for the current viewer | ambient friend-location map |
| Place Focus | One person's authorized Place note and relationship scope | collapsing the note into Place consensus |
| Place Path | The strongest home for the actual Rome–Paris comparison and its analogy limit | decorative dotted line between two cities |
| Life | The person's private Rome episode; Maya's trace only as a governed reference while authorized | copying Maya's artifact into permanent personal memory |
| Relationship | Mutually shared mosaic after both sides authorize | inferred closeness or compatibility score |

### 5.8 Pass and failure oracles

Pass if removing Maya destroys the comparison, Maya's audience expiry can
invalidate the self projection, and reciprocal friendship does not create
reciprocal data access.

Fail if the social value is merely co-location in Europe, two visible traces
are treated as synthesis permission, or a generated message performs either
person's relational expression.

## 6. Fixture D — A dinner contribution changes the dinner

### 6.1 Controlled evidence packet

This case tests a different proposition: some social traces are valuable
because they change canonical shared consequence, not because they deserve
content treatment.

**FIXTURE:**

- the host says in private Chat: “Dinner at mine Saturday. Invite Maya and
  Theo. Relaxed, no elaborate plan.”;
- Vesper resolves the two contacts and prepares a lightweight Occasion;
- Maya accepts in the Occasion and writes: “I'll bring tiramisu.”;
- Theo accepts and contributes: “I can get there at 8.”;
- Theo separately supplies a private minimum-safe constraint that the main
  course must happen before 9:15; the shared projection may change timing but
  may not reveal the reason;
- the host's draft shopping list still contains dessert; and
- no photograph, attendance, enjoyment, or shared meaning exists yet.

### 6.2 Canonical consequence

The contributions should change the owned Occasion and preparation state:

```text
Maya: accepted + dessert commitment
  -> dessert covered
  -> remove dessert from unresolved shopping

Theo: accepted + arrives at 20:00
  + minimum-safe private timing constraint
  -> main course target 20:30
  -> shared reason: "works for the group"
  -> private rationale remains private
```

The useful output is not “Maya contributed tiramisu” as a social-feed fact. It
is that dessert is covered, the shopping list changed, the meal sequence still
works, and everyone can see only what they need.

### 6.3 Lane ledger

| Candidate | Disposition | Ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invite state, acceptance, arrival, dessert commitment, meal target | STATE | Occasion and Commitment truth |
| “I'll bring tiramisu” and “I can get there at 8” | HUMAN | Attributed contributions inside their authorized Occasion |
| Updated shopping list and serving sequence | INSTRUMENT | The social traces earn value through consequence |
| Private timing constraint | INSTRUMENT input | May shape the result through a minimum-safe projection; never explain the person |
| “Your group values relaxed generosity” | REJECT | Unsupported group-mind interpretation |
| Post-dinner story before supported occurrence or authored Outcome | REJECT | Planning and contribution do not prove what happened or mattered |

### 6.4 Self / host projection

Home may show one compact current consequence when preparation is timely:

> **Dinner is covered except drinks.** Maya is bringing dessert; Theo arrives
> at 8; serve the main around 8:30. Your shopping list now has pasta,
> vegetables, and drinks.
>
> Occasion state · updated from 2 participant contributions

This is STATE + INSTRUMENT. It does not need an editorial headline, a prompt to
“reflect on the gathering,” or a card asking who is choosing. The host can
open the Occasion to inspect commitments or correct the plan.

### 6.5 Close-friend / participant projection

Each accepted participant receives a role-appropriate projection:

> **Saturday dinner · 8:00 works.** Main around 8:30; Maya has dessert covered.
> Bring only what you committed to.

Maya sees her own tiramisu commitment and Undo while it remains reversible.
Theo sees the group-safe timing but not an explanation of his private
constraint. Neither person receives the host's private Chat or the other
person's hidden context.

The value is effortless orientation and a coherent shared consequence, not a
request to rank options or complete a profile.

### 6.6 Acquaintance projection

An acquaintance who is not invited receives SILENCE. The Occasion does not
become ambient availability, a social ranking signal, or evidence that they
were excluded.

If the host explicitly invites the acquaintance, they receive a separate
pending invitation with immediate orientation and a clean decline path. They
do not inherit the existing participants' private state merely by being
addressed.

### 6.7 Public projection

Default: SILENCE. The dinner, guest list, commitments, location, and any later
photographs remain private or Occasion-scoped.

After the occurrence, a participant may deliberately feature a HUMAN Status or
publish a dish contribution. That later boundary requires:

- supported occurrence;
- the contributor's authorship;
- affected-person treatment for identifiable people, location, and shared
  experience;
- a chosen audience and expiry; and
- no inference that all participants liked the meal or share one meaning.

A public recipe or Place note can be useful, but it is a separately authorized
contribution—not the inevitable afterlife of the dinner.

### 6.8 Surface and owner ruling

| Surface | Legitimate role | Non-conforming treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat | Host intent, contact resolution, private preparation, one send boundary | Occasion setup questionnaire |
| Home | The one material preparation state when timely | participant activity feed |
| Occasion | Canonical participation, contributions, commitments, sequence, and correction | group profile or public scrapbook |
| Places | Route/access truth only when a Place consequence exists | showing the dinner merely because it has a location |
| Life | Durable Occasion, supported occurrence, authorized Sources, plural Outcomes, and later correction | automatic “best night with friends” story |
| Status / public Place layer | Only deliberately authored later projection | automatic sharing or AI-authored social caption |

### 6.9 Pass and failure oracles

Pass if a one-line contribution removes a real planning burden, private context
shapes a safe result without leaking, and nonparticipants receive nothing.

Fail if the system creates a poll, asks for Occasion-specific profiles,
announces every contribution as content, treats acceptance as attendance, or
writes a retrospective group feeling without authored Outcomes.

## 7. Cross-fixture result

### 7.1 What remains private substrate

The following normally remain private or narrowly scoped:

- raw tickets, receipts, routes, photographs, and imported media;
- inferred links among those Sources before admission;
- a person's private Sorrento note before Address, Feature, or Publish;
- the movie ticket and private known-to-person record;
- the person's Rome route and Maya's friend-visible trace outside its active
  audience and purpose;
- minimum-safe participant constraints;
- Vesper's ranking, rejected candidates, and internal contribution brief; and
- exposure, dwell, silence, and dismissal signals beyond product- or
  situation-level learning.

Private substrate may improve a private projection or a group-safe consequence.
It is not latent social content waiting for copy.

### 7.2 What can become socially valuable

| Social value | Necessary transformation | Normal audience | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Situated handoff | Human judgment adapted to the recipient's current situation | named friend or group | human contribution + recipient projection |
| Shared-history continuation | A prior shared episode changes a present understanding or possibility | exact relationship | Relationship / authorized artifact |
| Featured aperture | A person deliberately makes one bounded expression available | chosen friends or public audience | human-authored Status |
| Social mosaic | Separately authored evidence creates a comparison neither side possessed | viewers inside the authority intersection | governed editorial artifact |
| Occasion contribution | A small human act changes preparation, coordination, or shared truth | current Occasion participants | Occasion / Commitment |
| Public Place contribution | Positional knowledge carries transferable utility, provenance, limits, and public authority | Place audience | human contribution or sourced Place artifact |

The author's presence must do work. If replacing a friend with “someone online”
does not change the value, the social label is decorative. If the relationship
matters only because Vesper inferred intimacy, the treatment is invasive.

### 7.3 Four non-equivalent routes to wider visibility

```text
private trace --Address--> named recipient
private trace --Contribute--> bounded Occasion
private trace --Feature--> chosen Status audience + expiry
private trace --Publish--> public Place audience + review/authority
```

These are not stages in a funnel. Sending does not imply contributing;
contributing does not imply featuring; featuring does not imply publication.
Every route keeps its own purpose, audience, expiry, receipt, and revocation.

## 8. Surface placement matrix

| Value | Home | World Field | Place Focus | Place Path | Live Reduction | Life | Occasion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sorrento arrival mechanism | only when current or transferable now | sparse returned distinction | geometry + scoped note | distance-versus-threshold explanation | route, burden, fallback | evidence and reconstruction | only if shared trip state needs it |
| Aeneas / Augustan mechanism | compact claim when timely | normally absent | one Rome lens | full source-linked mechanism | absent | durable artifact + source relations | only if deliberately contributed |
| Rome–Paris mosaic | one private timely comparison | optional permissioned social layer | each authorized Place note | primary comparison home | only if it changes an immediate route | own episode + governed reference | shared only after mutual authorization |
| Dinner contributions | one material preparation consequence | absent | only actual Place truth | absent | route/access if decisive | Occasion and later plural Outcomes | canonical home |

Surface selection follows the effect:

- Place truth and relationship belong in Places.
- Current-life consequence may circulate through Home.
- Durable evidence, episode structure, and correction belong in Life.
- Shared commitments and participation belong in the Occasion.
- Conversation begins, challenges, authors, and crosses boundaries, but it does
  not own the resulting world state.

No surface receives a quota for social content. Social authorship lives inside
the object whose understanding or consequence it changes.

## 9. Product and component implications

The fixtures require a small semantic repertoire, not one polymorphic “trace
card”:

| Semantic component | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Canonical state row / register** | Reads from the owner; distinguishes schedule, supported occurrence, current condition, and unknown |
| **Working instrument** | Accepts current state, burden, authority, fallback, and receipt; does not merely look interactive |
| **Attributed human note** | Preserves author, exact audience, purpose, source, expiry, and revocation |
| **Editorial contribution** | Exposes the new operation, bounded claim, known-to-person check, sources, and correction dependencies |
| **Social mosaic** | Holds plural authors and the intersection of allowed uses without inventing a group voice |
| **Boundary preview** | Shows one exact recipient, publication, or affected-person consequence before T2 confirmation |
| **Trust footprint** | Makes source, freshness, authorship, uncertainty, and owner inspectable without dominating the unit |
| **Silence / recede behavior** | Allows expiry, scroll-past, revocation, or loss of relevance to remove the projection without a completion prompt |

These are semantic contracts. A dedicated design tool should determine their
visual composition after the portfolio passes. The fixture pack does not
authorize uniform cards, fixed Home sections, a social feed, or server-driven
layout instructions.

## 10. Architecture implications

### 10.1 Required projection inputs

Every non-trivial audience-relative projection needs at least:

```yaml
projection_input:
  resource_id: null
  resource_revision: null
  semantic_role: state | instrument | human_contribution | editorial_contribution
  source_and_claim_refs: []
  human_authors: []
  vesper_operation: null
  viewer: null
  relationship_scope: null
  purpose: null
  surface_context: null
  moment_and_freshness: null
  authority_intersection:
    use: []
    retention: null
    inference: null
    audience: []
    action: null
  known_to_viewer: []
  durable_owner: null
  expiry_and_revocation: []
  correction_dependencies: []
```

This does not require one new universal `trace` table or one generalized social
post model. Existing Sources, claims, Places, Relationships, Occasions,
Commitments, Moments, Outcomes, and projections may supply the fields through a
shared compiler and policy decision.

### 10.2 Required decisions

The engine must be able to answer, in order:

1. What immediate job exists for this viewer now?
2. Is the source best used as state, an instrument input, human expression, or
   material for Vesper editorial work?
3. What does this viewer already know?
4. What exactly does the other person add?
5. What is the authority intersection for this derived purpose?
6. Which canonical owner holds truth and repair?
7. What is the smallest complete medium and projection density?
8. Does this beat direct state, a smaller instrument, another selected unit,
   and silence?

The renderer should receive the admitted semantic role, contribution, medium,
trust footprint, actions, owner handoff, and lifecycle. It should not decide
audience authority from visual proximity or invent editorial novelty from raw
data.

### 10.3 Correction and revocation pressure

The pack fails structurally unless these repairs work:

- “I bought the ferry ticket but did not take it” preserves the ticket and
  invalidates occurrence-dependent reconstructions;
- “I already knew the Augustan point” updates only the governed
  known-to-person boundary permitted by the person's instruction;
- Maya revoking the friends-visible Paris trace invalidates the dependent
  private mosaic;
- removing Maya's dessert commitment reopens dessert in the shopping
  instrument; and
- an affected person narrowing a dinner photograph removes wider projections
  without requiring deletion of every participant's private original.

## 11. Evaluation protocol

### 11.1 Controlled comparison

For each fixture, render at least these treatments:

1. flat factual report;
2. generic social activity;
3. Vesper overinterpretation;
4. correct state or instrument;
5. admitted human or editorial contribution;
6. wrong-audience projection; and
7. silence.

Do not change the underlying evidence when changing the viewer. This isolates
whether audience, relationship, and purpose actually alter admission.

### 11.2 Questions

Ask participants:

- What did you learn or become able to do?
- What was already obvious or already known?
- Whose words and perspective were these?
- Why were you allowed to see this?
- Did the other person's presence materially improve the value?
- Did this make contact easier without making contact feel required?
- Did any projection feel like surveillance, synthetic intimacy, or a hidden
  personality judgment?
- Was the value complete without opening, replying, or explaining yourself?
- Would a route, state row, source, or silence have been better?
- What should happen if the source is corrected or withdrawn?

### 11.3 Hard failures

A treatment fails regardless of qualitative score when it:

- repeats the person's own observation as Vesper's discovery;
- changes audience by paraphrasing, anonymizing, or removing attribution;
- shows a friend without the friend changing understanding or consequence;
- treats mutual friendship as mutual source access;
- asks the person to supply the missing interpretation before value appears;
- turns an operational contribution into engagement content;
- exposes a private rationale in a shared explanation;
- infers attendance, enjoyment, identity, group feeling, or relationship health
  without the required authority;
- cannot be invalidated after correction, expiry, or revocation; or
- occupies Home merely because the underlying trip generated abundant data.

### 11.4 Success measures

Measure the portfolio, not only individual specimens:

- five-second comprehension of the actual value;
- source and authorship recognition;
- correct understanding of audience and consequence;
- reduced planning or interpretation burden;
- meaningful use of a human trace versus decorative social presence;
- correction and revocation comprehension;
- no reply debt;
- whole-page marginal value after other Home or Places units are present; and
- willingness to keep using Chat as a low-friction input because visible output
  later proves worthwhile.

Views, dwell, replies, and shares are product telemetry, not proof of personal
meaning or relationship quality.

## 12. Settled rulings from the fixtures

1. **Trace is not a content noun.** It is a governed source role that may feed
   state, an instrument, human expression, editorial work, or nothing.
2. **Audience changes the product, not only the copy.** Different viewers may
   receive different lanes, owners, media, consequences, or silence.
3. **The strongest social value often lands inside another object.** A friend's
   note improves a route; a dish commitment changes a shopping list; a private
   constraint changes timing. Do not extract these into a social feed.
4. **Relationship is value only when it transforms the result.** Attribution
   alone is insufficient.
5. **Public utility requires its own authority and context.** A private trace
   cannot be laundered into a public insight; independently sourced public
   editorial is a different contribution.
6. **Human expression remains human.** Vesper may find the opening, prepare the
   object, and reduce logistics. The person chooses whether and how to speak.
7. **Reciprocity does not imply symmetry of access.** A friend sharing to the
   person does not expose the person's private evidence back to the friend.
8. **Value before input applies to multiplayer.** Participants receive
   orientation or consequence before any request for labor, and silence or
   decline is a complete ending.
9. **Home should circulate consequences and unusually strong contributions,
   not announce stored traces.** Places, Life, Relationships, and Occasions
   keep depth and authority.
10. **Silence is a first-class successful projection.** It prevents social
    possibility from becoming ambient surveillance, obligation, or feed noise.

## 13. Next use

Use these four fixtures as the semantic input to a dedicated composition pass:

1. render each admitted unit using the smallest native medium;
2. place it inside complete Home, Places, Life, and Occasion compositions;
3. include the wrong-lane and wrong-audience negative controls beside it;
4. test correction, expiry, and revocation as visible state changes; and
5. promote only the component and architecture requirements that recur across
   multiple fixtures.

Do not begin by designing a universal trace card, a social feed section, or an
audience toggle on one canonical post. The pack's central result is that those
abstractions erase the very differences Vesper needs to preserve.
