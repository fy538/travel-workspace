# Life Outcome Storyboard Pack

> Status: active value-first design artifact
>
> This pack composes behavior and content, not final screens. It uses one
> controlled Europe-to-New-York world to compare four complete Life outcomes:
> **Recover, Re-enter, Carry, and Together**. Layout, type, illustration,
> motion, and final component choices remain work for a dedicated visual tool.

## 0. Decision this pack is testing

Life is not valuable because it contains a large amount of personal data. It
is valuable when governed history becomes easier to recover, richer to return
to, safer to remember together, or capable of improving something later.

The cross-root law is:

> **Chat receives and shapes contribution. Life preserves evidence and
> lineage. Home and Places deliver present consequence.**

The four storyboards test whether this law produces experiences a person can
understand and value without learning Vesper's ontology or completing
reflection work.

## 1. Controlled evidence world

The shared fixture may contain:

- a supported Journey from New York through Nice, Sorrento, the Amalfi coast,
  and Rome, followed by a disrupted return to New York;
- plane, train, ferry, transfer, stay, and admission Sources, including at
  least one booked movement that did not occur;
- photographs with partial time and Place metadata;
- authored observations about Sorrento's verticality, ferry and road tradeoffs,
  August heat, walking, pasta texture, and the Colosseum/Aeneas encounter;
- a pre-trip film or book Source connected by the person to the Rome encounter;
- current New York context and an open waterfront-weekend possibility;
- one separately authorized, explicitly synthetic Paris contribution used only
  for the Together fixture; and
- corrections, audience grants, and uncertainty attached to the relevant
  evidence.

This is a controlled product fixture, not a claim that every booked movement
occurred, every photograph proves a visit, every observation became a durable
interest, or any real friend's private Paris history is available. Exact
provider schedules and present conditions must be refreshed by their current
owners during implementation or live dogfood.

## 2. Shared storyboard grammar

Every storyboard must complete this movement:

```text
entry with a human job
  -> useful result before input
  -> optional evidence or depth
  -> optional cross-root consequence
  -> governed durable result or explicit no-write
  -> correction, suppression, or silence path
```

Each must make legible:

1. what the person already knew;
2. what Vesper did that was new;
3. the evidence and authority that permit it;
4. why Life owns this part of the experience;
5. whether the value ends on view;
6. what another root owns if the experience continues; and
7. what must remain absent.

## 3. Storyboard A — Recover the ferry that was booked but not taken

### Outcome hypothesis

> **When memory is partial, Life restores the exact target and just enough
> surrounding truth to make it usable—without turning retrieval into a recap.**

### Human job and known baseline

The person remembers that a ferry was booked somewhere on the coast but also
remembers that the route changed. They do not remember the exact segment, date,
or replacement movement.

Vesper adds value by resolving the correct Source, separating intention from
Occurrence, and restoring the minimum surrounding episode. It does not add
value by narrating the trip.

### Scene A1 — Enter Life

The stable Life entrance contains:

- a prominent search aperture;
- the stable Journey door `Nice → Rome · Aug 14–27`;
- bounded Returns only if independently eligible; and
- no prompt asking the person to organize or reflect.

The person searches:

> **The ferry we booked but didn't take.**

Search language remains ephemeral. It does not become an interest or
resurfacing signal.

### Scene A2 — Target-first result

Life returns the original booking first:

```text
Ferry booking
[supported route] · [represented date]

Booked · not recorded as taken
Replaced by [supported movement], if evidenced
```

The exact Source image or document is available immediately. The state line is
not decorative copy; it is the core value. If the replacement movement is
unresolved, Life says so instead of inferring it from timing.

### Scene A3 — Around this

Below the target, Life provides no more than three contextual landmarks:

1. the relevant coast chapter;
2. the preceding supported stay or movement; and
3. the replacement or next supported Occurrence.

The person may expand the Journey sequence, but the result does not begin with
a route animation, city summary, artifact count, or generated story.

### Scene A4 — Inspect or repair

Available actions are:

- open the original Source;
- inspect why the item is marked booked-not-taken;
- correct the actual movement;
- detach it from the wrong chapter; or
- keep it searchable but exclude it from automatic Returns.

No new durable state is created unless the person corrects, scopes, saves, or
otherwise authors a consequence.

### Completed payoff

The person has the exact ferry, its truthful state, and enough episode context
to understand it. No further response is necessary.

### Admission and failure tests

| Requirement | Pass condition | Hard failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | Original booking plus governed non-occurrence or explicit correction | Ticket possession reported as travel |
| Orientation | Target first plus at most three landmarks | Full-trip recap before the Source |
| Authority | Viewer may inspect the Source and episode context | Shared/private material leaks into the result |
| Burden | Complete on view | Required correction or filing before retrieval |
| Persistence | Search itself writes nothing | Query becomes a durable preference or reminder |

## 4. Storyboard B — Re-enter the coast as it actually unfolded

### Outcome hypothesis

> **Life can restore the texture and structure of an experience without
> declaring what it meant or requiring reflection.**

### Human job and known baseline

The person deliberately opens the Europe Journey after returning home. They
already know the cities and broad route. Another city list, photograph collage,
or statement that the coast was beautiful would add nothing.

Vesper's new operation is a supported reconstruction of how movement changed
what was reachable and how the coast day unfolded through evidence—not a
personality interpretation.

### Scene B1 — Open the stable Journey

The Journey opens to a quiet ordered read:

```text
Nice → Sorrento → Amalfi → Rome
Aug 14–27 · Returned

[supported chapters]
[one compact unresolved-evidence signal, if needed]
```

The page does not lead with totals. Occurrences lead; planned-only, changed,
and unresolved items remain visible in quieter truth states.

### Scene B2 — Select an earned reconstruction

One substantive Return is available inside the coast chapter:

> **Where the coast changed what was reachable**

This is admitted only when movement Sources, supported Occurrences, terrain or
transport context, and corrections can support a real delta. Otherwise the
Journey remains a factual, searchable record.

### Scene B3 — Reconstruct through evidence

The reconstruction uses a short sequence rather than an essay:

```text
intended movement
  -> supported change or constraint
  -> what became reachable or impractical
  -> one evidence-bearing photograph, note, or Place encounter
```

At each step, the representation distinguishes:

- ticketed versus taken;
- planned versus occurred;
- captured time versus represented time;
- the person's observation versus Vesper's explanation; and
- historical evidence versus present provider truth.

The person can open photographs, tickets, Places, and the original Sorrento
note without leaving the Journey identity.

### Scene B4 — Let the return end

The final state is a complete reconstruction, not a prompt. Optional apertures
may offer:

- inspect the evidence;
- open one coast Place;
- hear or read a longer version;
- continue a supplied cliffs or pasta question in Chat; or
- keep the composition.

There is no `What did this trip teach you?`, completion state, streak, or
unanswered reflection debt.

### Completed payoff

The person can re-enter one meaningful dimension of the coast and understand
the best-supported relationship among route, constraint, and experience. They
may simply close Life afterward.

### Admission and failure tests

| Requirement | Pass condition | Hard failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New value | Reconstruction reveals supported structure beyond the known route | City count, highlight reel, or paraphrase |
| Truth | Intended and occurred movement remain distinct | Fabricated continuous route |
| Meaning | Human meaning stays authored | `This changed you` or travel-style claim |
| Burden | Return is complete without interaction | Reflection prompt as the payoff |
| Medium | Sequence and evidence carry most of the explanation | Long AI essay replacing the experience |

## 5. Storyboard C — Carry a coast mechanism into New York

### Outcome hypothesis

> **Prior experience becomes differentiated product value when it improves a
> present situation and shows why the transfer holds and where it does not.**

### Human job and known baseline

The person is back in New York and wants an open weekend that feels alive after
vacation. They are considering a waterfront day. They did not open Life to
reminisce, and the recent trip should not dominate Home merely because it
produced dense evidence.

The relevant coast evidence suggests one bounded mechanism: access and return
conditions can determine the usable shape of a waterfront day. Vesper must not
translate that into `you experience travel through transitions`.

### Scene C1 — Home delivers the present value

Home, not Life, may show one complete-value opening:

> **Set the return edge first; keep the waterfront day loose.**

The compact explanation is:

```text
This may transfer because:
  on the Amalfi coast, movement conditions changed the usable day.

What differs here:
  New York is not the same terrain or transport system; current crossings,
  weather, origin, and final return still need a live check.

Useful now:
  treat one crossing or return as the timed anchor, then preserve optionality
  around it.
```

This is a planning principle plus a prepared possibility, not a retrospective
story or automatic itinerary.

### Scene C2 — Places owns spatial truth

Opening the unit hands the current Moment and prior mechanism to Places.
Places refreshes:

- actual origin and permitted location precision;
- live ferry, subway, bus, walking, and return constraints;
- weather and operating status;
- affected companions or Occasion, if any; and
- alternatives that preserve the same purpose.

Places may compare Red Hook, Governors Island, or another supported waterfront
candidate only when current data makes that comparison truthful. Life does not
cache a current route recommendation as historical truth.

### Scene C3 — Optional consequence

The person may:

- use the prepared structure without creating a Plan;
- add one anchor to a Plan or Occasion;
- ask Chat to change the pace or companions;
- reject the analogy; or
- do nothing.

No reminder, booking, invitation, or durable preference follows without the
corresponding authority.

### Scene C4 — Reconcile and issue a receipt

If supported later evidence shows that the prior contribution materially
changed the plan or experience, the consequence-owning surface may show:

```text
Carried forward

From
  the supported coast movement episode

Changed here
  the return crossing became the only fixed anchor

Result
  [supported occurrence or authored outcome]

Inspect · Correct · Stop using this connection
```

If the person never used the suggestion, no success receipt is generated. If
the analogy failed, Life may preserve the bounded difference as evidence that
the mechanism did not carry.

### Scene C5 — Life preserves causal lineage

Life later shows the receipt under the relevant Journey, New York episode, or
`What carried` return. It links both sides without merging them into one
episode or claiming that Vesper caused the weekend's meaning.

### Completed payoff

The person's earlier contribution reduces assembly work in a current
situation. The later receipt makes the compounding benefit inspectable.

### Admission and failure tests

| Requirement | Pass condition | Hard failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer | Shared mechanism, material difference, and present use are explicit | Surface resemblance presented as proof |
| Placement | Home opens; Places refreshes; Life preserves | Retrospective Life card pretending to be current truth |
| Authority | Suggestion remains below Plan/action authority | Automatic itinerary, reminder, or booking |
| Receipt | Supported causal contribution and later result | Success receipt from exposure alone |
| Identity restraint | Context-specific mechanism | Personality or stable travel-style inference |

## 6. Storyboard D — Route to a friend's relevant knowledge

### Outcome hypothesis

> **The smallest differentiated social value is not seeing what friends did.
> It is recognizing when a person we know holds relevant experience and making
> that human route easier.**

### Controlled social fixture

This storyboard introduces an explicitly synthetic friend, `Maya`, solely to
test product behavior. It is not evidence about a real person's Paris trip.

The fixture grants the viewer access to:

- one attributed Paris evening contribution;
- city-level Place and bounded date only;
- one authored practical observation about adapting a hot evening; and
- permission to use that contribution for a direct comparison or direct cue
  to this viewer.

It does not grant Maya's full route, live location, private Outcome, camera
roll, trip history, or permission for public projection.

### Human job and known baseline

The viewer knows they were in Rome during a hot period and knows Maya was in
Paris. Coincidence itself is not useful. Vesper earns attention only if Maya's
authorized observation becomes relevant to a present question or reveals one
bounded contrast neither person could see alone.

### Scene D1 — An addressed Together return

Inside the Europe period, Life may show:

> **Maya found a different way through the same kind of hot evening.**

This is not a stable social door and does not appear merely because the dates
overlap. The treatment is admitted because the contribution is authorized,
attributed, and relevant to one common axis.

### Scene D2 — Preserve two lanes

The comparison shows:

```text
Rome · your lane
  [your authorized observation and supporting public context]

Paris · Maya's lane
  [Maya's exact authorized contribution]

Vesper connected
  one bounded difference in how the evening became usable
```

The copy never says `you both felt`, `your shared rhythm`, or `Maya loves`.
Missing evidence remains missing. Public weather, transit, shade, or opening
hours may support context but cannot replace either person's account.

### Scene D3 — Human knowledge route

If the viewer's current question exceeds the shared contribution, Vesper may
say:

> **Maya may know more about this. This is the note she chose to share with
> you.**

The person may:

- open the contributed Source;
- ask Chat to prepare a concise human-authored question;
- address the bounded comparison back to Maya; or
- leave it alone.

Vesper does not answer in Maya's voice, expose additional material, or send a
message without confirmation and authority.

### Scene D4 — Durable social consequence

If the two people later exchange a message or contribute to a bounded Occasion,
Life preserves attribution and the applicable audience epoch. It may show the
shared continuation inside Together while keeping each person's private
Journey and Outcome separate.

Revoking Maya's contribution removes it from future Together projections and
invalidates dependent compositions. It does not delete the viewer's independent
Rome history.

### Completed payoff

The viewer gains either a substantive attributed contrast or a lower-friction
route to the right person. No posting, profile browsing, public feed, or reply
obligation is required.

### Admission and failure tests

| Requirement | Pass condition | Hard failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social value | The human contribution changes understanding or routes knowledge | Name/avatar added to generic content |
| Authority | Exact contribution, viewer, purpose, and precision are valid | Full friend history or inferred location exposed |
| Attribution | Two authors and Vesper's operation remain distinct | Synthetic shared voice or consensus |
| Agency | Human chooses whether to contact or share | Auto-message or AI relational impersonation |
| Degradation | Revocation safely recompiles or closes the return | Paraphrase leakage after Source removal |

## 7. Comparison matrix

| Storyboard | Human memory job | Primary dividend | Complete value | Strongest product proof | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Recover** | Retrieve and orient | Cognitive relief | Exact target, truthful state, minimum context | Vesper remembers more usefully than file systems | Archive dump or false occurrence |
| **Re-enter** | Recollect and revisit | Experiential and representational return | Supported reconstruction without reflection | Mixed evidence becomes a legible experience | Flat recap or AI autobiography |
| **Carry** | Transfer and imagine | Capability and possibility | Prior mechanism improves a present situation | Context compounds across occasions | Creepy identity claim or false analogy |
| **Together** | Remember with and route through people | Relational gain | Attributed contrast or useful human route | Relationship becomes an intelligent index | Friend feed, surveillance, or impersonation |

These are not four permanent Life sections. They are four value modes that
must share stable truth, evidence, authority, and handoff behavior.

## 8. Invariant Life anatomy derived from the four outcomes

The storyboards produce five semantic regions. They do not yet prescribe five
visual modules.

### 8.1 Entrance and orientation

- Search is a first-class Life action.
- Stable doors organize eligible periods, Journeys, Occasions, and earned
  longitudinal relationships.
- A small finite Returns region may offer one or two strong entrances in the
  first experiment; the exact budget remains a composition hypothesis.
- Sparse state remains honest and useful through retrieval, custody, and
  correction rather than generated filler.

### 8.2 Episode viewer

- A Journey or Occasion keeps one stable identity across Timeline, local Map,
  Sources, people, and composition lenses.
- Occurrences lead; Plans, unresolved evidence, and generated compositions keep
  distinct truth roles.
- Evidence nests beneath the relation or episode it supports.

### 8.3 Contribution or composition viewer

- Original human material, external fact, and Vesper operation remain visibly
  distinct.
- The viewer exposes the known baseline, new operation, evidence, limits, and
  optional consequence without requiring a prose essay.
- Experiential return may end on view.

### 8.4 Continue and causal receipt

- Life can carry selected context into Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion.
- The destination refreshes its own truth and authority.
- Material later improvement may produce a `Carried forward` receipt that Life
  retains as causal lineage.

### 8.5 Trust and control

- Source, audience, uncertainty, and correction remain inspectable.
- Unit-level controls begin with `Useful`, `Not now`, `Keep searchable`, `Less
  like this`, and `Never surface this`.
- Deeper scope controls appear only when requested or after repeated explicit
  dismissal.

## 9. Cross-root handoff map

```text
Chat contribution
  -> immediate answer or operation
  -> authorized Source / owner readback
  -> Life placement

Life recovery or reconstruction
  -> complete value in Life
  -> optional Chat interrogation or correction

Life-supported transfer
  -> Home opening because it matters now
  -> Places refresh because it matters where
  -> optional Plan / Occasion consequence
  -> Occurrence / authored Outcome
  -> Carried forward receipt in Life

Authorized human contribution
  -> Together return or knowledge route
  -> optional human-authored request through Chat
  -> bounded shared continuation
  -> plural private and shared records in Life
```

Every handoff must carry origin object, immediate job, selected evidence,
viewer and audience, authority ceiling, current Moment, freshness, destination,
return path, and correction lineage.

## 10. Minimum renderer-neutral projection requirements

These are read-model requirements, not authorization for new universal tables
or final API names.

### 10.1 `LifeIndexProjectionV0`

- viewer and scope;
- stable-door identities, labels, represented periods, and truth summaries;
- active/in-motion owners when applicable;
- finite eligible Returns with reason, value mode, demand, and control scope;
- search capability and sparse-state contract; and
- correction, suppression, and custody entry points.

### 10.2 `LifeEpisodeProjectionV0`

- canonical episode identity and owner;
- represented period and chapter/landmark order;
- planned, occurred, changed, unresolved, and generated roles;
- nested Sources and claim-local provenance;
- Mine/Together projection and audience epoch;
- available lenses without duplicating identity; and
- Continue envelopes and invalidation dependencies.

### 10.3 `LifeReturnProjectionV0`

- primary human job and dividend;
- known-to-person baseline;
- Vesper operation and completed payoff;
- evidence manifest, uncertainty, and freshness;
- resurfacing tier, why-here, why-now if applicable, and expiry;
- authorship and audience roles;
- native-medium hints without component prescription; and
- silence, correction, and suppression behavior.

### 10.4 `CarriedForwardReceiptV0`

- prior Source, episode, authored observation, or Outcome references;
- current consequence owner and operation;
- shared mechanism and material difference;
- known Occurrence or authored Outcome, if any;
- group-safe and private explanation projections;
- causal confidence and non-causality caveat where needed; and
- inspect, correct, release, and stop-using paths.

### 10.5 `HumanKnowledgeRouteV0`

- contributor identity at permitted disclosure;
- exact authorized contribution references;
- viewer, purpose, audience epoch, and precision;
- reason the person may be relevant without an expertise trait claim;
- allowed actions: inspect, prepare request, address, or silence;
- no-impersonation and no-auto-send boundary; and
- revocation and safe-degradation behavior.

## 11. Content and behavior evaluation

Score each complete storyboard from 0–2 on the following dimensions:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Completed value** | Requires work before payoff | Partial value | Useful before any response |
| **Novel delta** | Repeats known material | Adds weak context | Performs new retrieval, reconstruction, transfer, or routing |
| **Truth** | Collapses Plan, Source, Occurrence, or Outcome | Correct but opaque | Truth roles and uncertainty are clear |
| **Authority** | Uses or exposes without permission | Scope exists but is unclear | Viewer, author, purpose, and action authority are legible |
| **Identity restraint** | Converts behavior into self-claim | Implicit overreach | Experience and mechanism remain contextual |
| **Burden** | Reflection, filing, or reply is required | Optional action feels expected | Ignore or completion-on-view is fully valid |
| **Root coherence** | Duplicates owners across roots | Handoff loses some context | Each root owns the right depth and consequence |
| **Compounding proof** | No future value or fabricated causality | Future value is implied | Later consequence and lineage are inspectable |
| **Repair** | No narrow correction or suppression | Broad control only | Source-, episode-, person-, and return-level repair are available |
| **Specialist delta** | Photos, Maps, Journal, or chat already does it | Combination helps | Governed continuity creates distinct value |

Maximum score is 20. A storyboard advances to visual composition only when:

- it scores at least 17;
- Completed value, Truth, Authority, and Root coherence each score 2;
- no hard failure from the storyboard triggers; and
- silence would not be better for the person.

## 12. Product conclusions and next gate

The four outcomes imply that Life's defining interaction is **return with
lineage**, not browsing for its own sake. Search and stable doors make the past
reliable. Bounded Returns make it newly useful. Cross-root handoffs let the
past affect the present without turning Life into Home. Causal receipts make
that compounding visible. Authorized human routing makes social memory useful
without creating a feed.

The four outcomes are now integrated in the [Canonical Life Experience
Manuscript — Post-Return New
York](life-canonical-post-return-new-york-experience-manuscript-2026-08-29.md).
Founder review now applies to that complete composition and the relative
importance of its four payoffs. After that review:

1. tighten any storyboard whose payoff still depends on explanation;
2. confirm or revise the manuscript's shared Life entrance and episode anatomy;
3. hand the renderer-neutral pack to a dedicated visual composition tool;
4. compare complete compositions rather than isolated cards; and
5. only then extract native projection contracts and an engineering slice.

No production schema, navigation cutover, or generalized renderer is approved
by this document.
