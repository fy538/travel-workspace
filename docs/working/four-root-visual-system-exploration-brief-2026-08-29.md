# Four-Root Visual-System Exploration Brief

## Assignment

Design one coherent mobile visual system for the selected [Integrated Four-Root
Experience Portfolio](integrated-four-root-experience-portfolio-2026-08-29.md).
The output is a comparative visual study, not a product brainstorm. Hold the
content, root jobs, result families, owners, audience, and actions fixed.

The existing `vesper-home-surfaces 2` warm paper / Roman serif / restrained
gold language is **Direction A: control**. Produce two genuinely different but
equally mature directions, B and C. A direction is not different if it merely
changes color, corner radius, or font while retaining the same hierarchy.

## Non-negotiable product posture

- Home and Places give value; they are not engagement feeds.
- Chat is clean, contribution- and agency-oriented, and not a value feed.
- Life is stable organization for finding, returning, seeing anew, and
  continuing—not an archive dump or ontology manager.
- Value precedes input.
- Social material changes an existing object or consequence; there is no
  social section.
- The world and people remain primary. Vesper's expression recedes after the
  useful contribution.

## Frozen frame set

Create these twelve phone frames at one consistent viewport, plus one compact
flow strip showing cross-root continuity:

1. Home — ordinary post-return New York;
2. Home — emerging Red Hook Saturday;
3. Home — urgent/live reduction;
4. Chat — clean default with contextual entry points;
5. Chat — prepared Occasion consequence awaiting authority;
6. Places — New York World Field;
7. Places — Red Hook Place Focus;
8. Places — Red Hook Place Path;
9. Places — Live Reduction;
10. Life — stable entrance;
11. Life — Europe Journey episode;
12. Social — Rome/Paris juxtaposition with attribution and withdrawal state.

Do not add a fifth root, discovery feed, reflection prompt, profile insight,
daily streak, generic AI suggestion carousel, or separate social destination.

## Content lock

Use only claims, actions, labels, and evidence described in the integrated
portfolio. Copy may be shortened to fit after preserving its actual substance;
it may not be replaced with vague lifestyle language. In particular:

- do not use “you may experience travel through transitions” or comparable
  personality interpretation;
- do not repeat “Rome adopted the survivor's side” without adding the supported
  mechanism or distinction;
- do not ask “what do you not want to lose?” before returning value;
- do not make trip counts or “threads still open” the Home crown;
- do not invent current provider state, friend activity, or audience grants.

## Visual semantics the system must distinguish

The distinctions should be perceptible before explanatory labels:

| Semantic family | Required visual behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Direct State** | stable, concise, owner-linked; no editorial ceremony |
| **Composition** | authored but evidence-bound; contribution and proof have hierarchy |
| **Instrument** | current, manipulable, freshness-aware, with clear authority and fallback |
| **Receipt** | verified per-owner consequence and recovery; not celebratory chrome |
| **Human contribution** | person, audience, and authorship remain legible without social-feed styling |
| **Artifact / Source evidence** | inspectable custody and provenance without dominating the main value |
| **Unknown / stale / revoked** | visually honest and usable, not merely gray or hidden |

Maps, routes, timelines, evidence strips, comparisons, receipts, quantitative
marks, controls, and prose should use the form that best carries the job. Avoid
turning all of them into interchangeable rounded cards.

## Direction requirements

### Direction A — control

Use the current warm paper, Roman serif, restrained gold, spacious hierarchy,
and quiet materiality. Improve it only where the fixed frames expose a real
semantic limitation. Test whether it can support live state, maps, attributed
human material, dense evidence, and operational controls without losing calm.

### Direction B — spatial field

Explore a system in which relationship, time, place, and consequence are
organized through fields, bands, paths, and anchored layers. It may be more
diagrammatic and spatial than A, but must remain mobile, legible, and warm—not
a data dashboard or sci-fi HUD.

### Direction C — editorial instrument

Explore a system that joins editorial clarity with operational precision:
strong evidence rhythm, typographic hierarchy, purposeful data marks, and
native instruments. It may feel closer to a beautifully edited field guide or
living publication, but must not make every state an article.

## Required state variants

For each direction, show:

- complete and partial evidence;
- current, stale, and unknown world state;
- private and named-person audience;
- human, Vesper, provider, and shared-state authorship;
- quiet, emerging, and urgent temporal posture;
- large Dynamic Type or equivalent accessibility stress state for at least one
  Home and one Instrument frame; and
- reduced-motion/static equivalent for any motion-dependent meaning.

## Comparison rubric

Score each direction from 1–5 and provide concrete evidence:

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| First-five-second comprehension | 15 |
| Dominant value and attention hierarchy | 15 |
| Difference among state, Composition, human material, and Instrument | 12 |
| Coherence across all four roots | 12 |
| Density without information overload | 10 |
| Native map, evidence, receipt, and control expression | 8 |
| Social authorship and audience legibility | 8 |
| Provenance, uncertainty, and freshness | 6 |
| Accessibility and degraded states | 6 |
| Distinctiveness and emotional fit | 5 |
| Implementation tractability | 3 |

Automatic rejection conditions:

- the first value is hidden behind a tap or response;
- most units share the same card anatomy;
- visual styling implies false certainty or broader audience;
- Chat becomes another scroll;
- Life becomes a feed;
- Places becomes only map/search;
- Home becomes only recap, recommendation, or task list;
- the direction requires new product sections or owner types; or
- the system cannot survive the urgent Instrument state.

## Handoff package

Return:

1. the twelve isolated frames for A, B, and C;
2. one cross-root flow strip per direction;
3. tokens and component principles, not a full component library;
4. the scored rubric with rejection evidence;
5. the recommended direction and any one justified hybrid move; and
6. stable frame IDs suitable for later native comparison capture.

The founder's selection is the gate for native visual implementation. Semantic
fixtures and owner contracts may continue to mature, but no production visual
system should be inferred from an unselected exploration.
