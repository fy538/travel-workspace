# Life Object-and-Lens Fixture Pack

## Question or outcome

Once a person has asked, pointed, brought, kept, shared, planned, acted,
contributed, or corrected something through Vesper, what should that governed
state become in **Life**?

This pack tests whether Vesper can turn heterogeneous accumulated material into
a coherent, useful, and governable life record without making the person
maintain an archive. It compares the same six evidence worlds through:

1. a Journey or Occasion composition;
2. Timeline;
3. Map;
4. Mine;
5. Together;
6. Library; and
7. one generated composition.

The pack specifies semantic organization, truth, management, continuity, and
required read models. It does **not** choose final visual composition,
typography, card geometry, animation, or illustration. Those belong in a
dedicated visual-design environment after the object-and-lens behavior is
accepted.

## Executive decision

Life should be a **governed record with an episode spine, several retrieval
lenses, and derived compositions**:

```text
durable authorities
  -> coherent episode containers
  -> several viewer-relative retrieval lenses
  -> optional generated compositions
  -> a later understanding, possibility, consequence, or return
```

The recommended structure is **container-first, lens-supported, and
composition-bearing**:

- Journeys and Occasions make bounded periods or episodes coherent.
- Timeline, Map, People, and Library retrieve the same corpus by different
  dimensions; they are not competing stores.
- Mine and Together are authority-bearing viewer scopes, not cosmetic filters.
- Articles, maps, reconstructions, comparisons, audio, and recaps are
  regenerable compositions over the record, not canonical truth.
- Home may project what matters now; Places may project spatial possibility;
  Chat may change or interrogate an object. Life owns the standard viewer
  experience for accumulated continuity, longitudinal retrieval, correction,
  and forgetting while durable owners retain truth.

Life should be designed around four human jobs rather than seven equally
weighted views:

1. **Find** — retrieve something known or vaguely remembered through a person,
   Place, period, episode, medium, or contextual cue;
2. **Return** — re-enter a Journey, Occasion, Place relationship, recurring
   practice, or other meaningful episode;
3. **See anew** — receive a source-grounded comparison, reconstruction, map,
   article, audio piece, juxtaposition, or other composition that adds value
   beyond restating the record; and
4. **Continue** — carry an object or episode into present action through Home,
   Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion.

Timeline, Map, People, Mine, Together, and source retrieval support these jobs.
They must not each become an equally prominent destination merely because they
are useful read dimensions.

Life must also separate the **stable record** from the **return layer**. Episode
membership, Sources, Plans, Occurrences, Outcomes, authorship, and audience
remain governed and relatively stable. Resurfacing rank, generated
compositions, and editorial entrances may change, but they cannot rewrite or
masquerade as the record.

The current canonical-artifact Life lab remains useful as a projection test,
but its stack of similarly shaped cards must not become the Life architecture.
Plans, Occasions, Sources, Outcomes, and compositions are different units and
must remain different on purpose.

## 1. Boundary with the contribution-contract fork

The [Contribution Contract Fixture
Pack](contribution-contract-fixture-pack-2026-08-29.md) owns:

- what a person's gesture authorizes;
- whether an Ask, Point, Bring, Keep, Share, Invite, Decide, Act, Correct, or
  Release creates durable state;
- immediate value in the contributing turn;
- admission, receipt, correction, Undo, and causal release; and
- the distinction between current implemented behavior and target behavior.

This Life pack begins **after** that contract has resolved a contribution into
one or more governed authorities or has intentionally retained nothing.

Life does not reinterpret an answer-only turn into durable memory. It does not
turn a provisional source into a visited Place. It does not manufacture an
Occasion because several artifacts happen to be nearby. It consumes authorized
state and exposes a viewer-safe projection of it.

The seam is:

```text
Chat contribution contract
  -> authorized Source / anchor / Plan / Occasion / occurrence / Outcome state
  -> Life aggregation and projection
  -> later Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion consequence
```

## Research basis

The fixture architecture draws on several patterns without importing any
product's surface wholesale:

1. **Design for a memory job, not total capture.** Sellen and Whittaker
   distinguish recollecting, reminiscing, retrieving, reflecting, and
   remembering intentions. Those jobs require different interfaces; a single
   exhaustive archive does not deliver them automatically. See [Beyond Total
   Capture](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/beyond-total-capture-a-constructive-critique-of-lifelogging-2/).
2. **Expect lapses and returns.** The Lived Informatics model finds that people
   track intermittently, change goals, lapse, resume, and may seek curiosity or
   a record rather than continuous behavior change. See the [University of
   Washington summary](https://www.hcde.washington.edu/news/article/2025-10-15/professor-sean-munson-and-collaborators-recognized-10-year-impact-award).
3. **One corpus can support several curated entrances.** Apple Photos organizes
   one library into Memories, People & Pets, Trips, Recent Days, Albums, and
   user-pinned collections; the person may reorder the collection surface. See
   [Apple Photos Collections](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph4f36c4148/26/ios/26).
4. **Document time and lived time differ.** Apple Journal supports sorting by
   Entry Date or Moment Date, reinforcing that when something was recorded is
   not always when it belongs in a life record. See [Apple Journal search and
   retrieval](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph6257be047/ios).
5. **Preserve the record; regenerate the rendering.** Polarsteps keeps a Trip
   and its Steps as the underlying record while generating an editable Travel
   Book from the route, stories, statistics, and media. See [Polarsteps Travel
   Books](https://support.polarsteps.com/hc/en-us/articles/24005113311634-What-is-a-Travel-Book)
   and [Steps](https://support.polarsteps.com/hc/en-us/articles/23760478173586-What-are-steps-and-how-do-I-add-or-edit-them).
6. **A structured event can anchor several analytic views.** Strava's activity
   page keeps map, route, photos, analysis, and privacy attached to one activity,
   while Personal Heatmaps provide a separate longitudinal spatial lens. See
   [Strava activity pages](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401886-ride-activity-pages)
   and [Personal Heatmaps](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15402028-personal-heatmaps).
7. **Editorial synthesis needs eligibility and control.** Spotify Wrapped uses
   participation thresholds, sequenced stories, revisit controls, and
   shareable excerpts rather than treating every listening history as equally
   story-ready. See [Spotify 2025
   Wrapped](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-12-03/2025-wrapped-user-experience/).
8. **A social episode can span invitation through memory.** Partiful keeps
   invitation, RSVP, discussion, coordination, and shared photos around one
   event rather than creating an unrelated archive afterward. See
   [Partiful](https://partiful.com/).
9. **Several views need not imply several copies.** Notion demonstrates the
   information principle that one object corpus can support list, board,
   timeline, calendar, gallery, and other views. Vesper should retain that
   principle without exposing database configuration as a user obligation. See
   [Notion database views](https://www.notion.com/help/views-filters-and-sorts).
10. **People segment life into nested, revisable events.** Event-segmentation
    research finds that people spontaneously organize activity into parts and
    subparts using physical changes and conceptual cues such as actors' goals;
    autobiographical-memory research distinguishes lifetime periods, general
    events, and event-specific knowledge. Life should therefore infer episode
    candidates from changes in Place, people, activity, goal, and causal
    structure rather than date proximity alone. See [Event
    Segmentation](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-8721.2007.00480.x)
    and [autobiographical memory and hierarchical
    search](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12888-014-0310-z).
11. **Retrieval is contextual and landmark-bearing.** Personal-information
    retrieval research shows the value of combining time with personal
    landmarks such as photos and calendar events. Life search should accept
    partial contextual cues rather than requiring an exact date, title, or
    folder. See [Milestones in
    Time](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/milestones-in-time-the-value-of-landmarks-in-retrieving-information-from-personal-stores/).
12. **Prospective, occurred, and logged state must remain distinct.** Letterboxd
    separates a film marked as watched at some unknown past time, a dated diary
    log, and a prospective watchlist item; completing the intended action
    changes the watchlist state without erasing the historical distinction.
    Life should preserve the analogous differences between Source, Plan,
    Occurrence, and Outcome. See the [Letterboxd
    FAQ](https://letterboxd.com/about/faq/?s=marking).
13. **The record and resurfacing controls are separate systems.** Day One keeps
    entries stable while providing Today, Calendar, and On This Day entrances;
    Readwise independently tunes how often durable highlights return; Google
    Photos lets an archived item remain searchable while excluding it from
    highlight generation. See [Day One On This
    Day](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/on-this-day-view/),
    [Readwise review
    controls](https://docs.readwise.io/readwise/docs/faqs/reviewing-highlights),
    and [Google Photos
    Archive](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/7362432?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en).
14. **Shared memory benefits from attribution and distributed control.** Apple
    Photos exposes Personal, Shared, and Both scopes and retains who contributed
    an item. Research on social photo displays found that distributed control
    changed interaction and improved enjoyment. Together should expose a
    governed shared core, attributed contributions, and plural Outcomes rather
    than synthesize one unqualified group memory. See [iCloud Shared Photo
    Library](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/use-icloud-shared-photo-library-mm544e1bd039/icloud)
    and [Social enjoyment with electronic photo
    displays](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/social-enjoyment-with-electronic-photo-displays-awareness-and-control/).
15. **Generated memory treatments require stronger controls than ordinary
    recommendations.** Google Photos and Apple Photos support hiding or
    featuring people, Places, dates, or memories less without necessarily
    deleting the underlying material. Emerging research also shows that
    AI-altered media can distort later recollection. A Vesper composition must
    remain visibly generated, source-linked, correctable, and separable from
    Occurrence truth. See [Google Photos featured-memory
    controls](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/9454489?hl=en-GB_ALL),
    [Apple Photos feature-less
    controls](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/feature-a-person-a-place-or-memory-less-often-ipsa4c9edc62/web),
    and [Synthetic Human Memories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08895).
16. **AI may reduce expression work without creating a reflection chore.**
    Remento preserves an original recording while turning speech into editable
    prose, but its recurring prompts remain an explicit storytelling ritual.
    Vesper should borrow source retention and editable transformation while
    generating value from ordinary contributions already admitted through
    Chat, sharing, planning, and lived activity. See [Remento: How it
    works](https://home.remento.co/how-it-works).

These precedents support the architecture, not the product thesis. Vesper's
distinct burden is to unify prospective Plan truth, lived Occurrence, plural
social Outcomes, heterogeneous Sources, Place relationships, and generated
compositions while preserving ownership and correction.

### Life pattern canon: borrow the primitives, invent the assembly

Vesper does not need to invent timelines, maps, episode grouping, personal
search, shared collections, resurfacing, or generated recaps. It must invent
the governed assembly through which those familiar primitives participate in
one coherent loop:

```text
ordinary contribution
  -> governed Source / Plan / Occasion / Occurrence / Outcome
  -> revisable episode organization
  -> Find / Return / See anew
  -> Continue into present possibility or consequence
```

The product differentiation lives in five combinations:

1. prospective and lived state coexist without collapsing;
2. private and shared experience coexist without manufacturing consensus;
3. heterogeneous media become episode evidence without flattening into cards;
4. generated compositions add substance while remaining distinct from truth;
   and
5. accumulated life becomes an input to present and future possibility rather
   than ending as nostalgia or storage.

The following imports are explicitly rejected:

- streaks, blank-day backfilling, or required journaling maintenance;
- a raw location-history or media-grid default;
- automatic inclusion of shared material in private generated memory;
- a chronologically mixed stream of lived events and AI compositions;
- an engagement-ranked social or nostalgia feed;
- a yearly recap ceremony as the primary return mechanism; and
- a universal generated biography or identity interpretation.

### Round 3 behavior adoption

The [Round 3 Life behavior
research](life-behavior-research-round-3-2026-08-29.md) is adopted here as an
execution contract rather than left as background inspiration. Every fixture
must preserve the following decisions:

| ID | Adopted fixture rule |
| --- | --- |
| **R3-D1** | Stable entrances use semantic hard gates and posture routing, never one importance score |
| **R3-D2** | Pinning changes access, not truth, ownership, occurrence, or semantic type |
| **R3-D3** | Search leads with the target or governed answer and attaches only useful contextual landmarks; reconstruction is optional |
| **R3-D4** | Search queries, reformulations, and refinements remain ephemeral unless the person explicitly authors or corrects state |
| **R3-D5** | Keeping and searchability do not authorize automatic return |
| **R3-D6** | Retrospective push is off by default; interruption requires explicit prospective authority |
| **R3-D7** | Record, audience, relationship safety, and return policy remain four orthogonal control planes |
| **R3-D8** | Block, leave, unblock, reconciliation, revocation, and deletion remain distinct lifecycle operations |
| **R3-D9** | Social treatments retain attributed lanes and plural Outcomes; they never infer consensus from a majority or merge private interpretations |
| **R3-D10** | Source identity is persistent and claim-local; a generic “AI-generated” label is insufficient |
| **R3-D11** | Continue follows an authority ladder and cannot jump from an inferred possibility to reminder, commitment, message, booking, or action |
| **R3-D12** | Appropriate silence and explicit retrieval after automatic suppression are successful outcomes |

These decisions keep five judgments separate:

```text
may exist in the governed record
  != may be found on request
  != deserves a stable Life entrance
  != may return automatically
  != may interrupt or act
```

Collapsing those judgments into one relevance score is a fixture failure even
when the resulting content appears personally relevant.

### What does not yet exist as a complete product contract

- an aggregate Life index projection;
- a Journey read projection across current durable owners;
- Life-specific Timeline, Map, Library, and search contracts over the
  heterogeneous corpus;
- accepted viewer-safe Occasion composition in the Life root;
- a persisted or ephemeral composition manifest with source dependencies and
  invalidation behavior;
- complete cross-lens correction, deletion, and audience propagation; or
- production evidence that the proposed Life organization is useful and
  effortless.

Nothing in this pack should be cited as a shipped Life capability.

## 2. The Life vocabulary

### 2.1 Durable authority is not presentation identity

| Unit | What it owns | What Life may do with it | What Life must not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | Evidence, authored material, custody, provenance, scope, and reuse rights | Make it findable, inspectable, grouped, and available to authorized compositions | Treat possession as occurrence, preference, or meaning |
| **Place** | Canonical physical identity and governed facts/perspectives | Show autobiographical relationships and route into current Places context | Duplicate the Place or turn Life into a directory |
| **Plan** | Prospective structure, revisions, participants, and intended consequence | Show what was intended, changed, settled, completed, or left open | Rewrite old intent to match what later happened |
| **Occasion** | A bounded shared world, lifecycle, members, purpose, and common consequences | Compose before, during, and after as one episode | Merge private context or plural subjective Outcomes |
| **Commitment** | Consequential promise, dependency, provider, actor, and state | Preserve what mattered to the episode and link the current owner | Present every task or operational receipt as a memory |
| **Occurrence** | The governed claim about what happened, changed, was missed, or remains unknown | Order and distinguish actual from intended history | Infer attendance from a ticket or media alone |
| **Outcome** | Person- or relationship-scoped consequence or authored interpretation | Carry forward explicit meaning, evaluation, capability, or correction | Synthesize a group consensus from several private Outcomes |

An **Experience Anchor** is a bounded projection anchor over these authorities.
An **artifact** is a viewer-safe carrier. Neither should become a universal
owner merely because it is convenient to render.

### 2.2 Human-facing containers and outputs

| Unit | Product role | Candidate implementation posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Journey** | Makes an ordered movement or extended period coherent across Sources, Plans, Places, Occurrences, and Outcomes | Initially a viewer-relative composition over Trip, Plan, Occasion, and anchor owners; do not approve a new durable owner in this pack |
| **Occasion** | Makes one bounded social episode coherent across before, live, and after | Existing durable owner and lifecycle |
| **Library bundle** | Groups Sources and artifacts by meaningful retrieval role | Read projection; never a required folder hierarchy |
| **Composition** | Adds a reconstruction, distinction, comparison, explanation, or playable return | Derived projection with lineage; persistence is an open decision |

The UI may use friendlier labels than these architecture nouns. The distinction
must survive even if the visible language becomes “Trips,” “Dinners,” “Things
you brought,” “Together,” or “Stories.”

## 3. Time, membership, and truth

### 3.1 Time roles

Life must be able to distinguish:

| Time role | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Source time** | When a ticket, photograph, note, or message was created |
| **Admission time** | When Vesper received or retained it |
| **Plan time** | When something was intended or scheduled to happen |
| **Occurrence time** | When it actually happened, changed, or was missed |
| **Outcome time** | When someone evaluated, corrected, or authored meaning |
| **Composition time** | When a recap, article, comparison, or reconstruction was generated |

Each lens chooses the appropriate role. Timeline normally leads with occurrence
time, preserves Plan time when the distinction matters, and does not move an
August event into October because its photograph was imported then. Library may
sort by source or admission time. A generated recap retains its own creation
date while clearly naming the period it represents.

### 3.2 Membership basis

An object may appear inside a Journey or Occasion only through a legible basis:

- **explicit** — a person or owning workflow attached it;
- **observed** — governed occurrence evidence establishes the relation;
- **authored** — a person explicitly states the relation or meaning; or
- **provisional** — a private, source-bound candidate that has not become
  shared or authoritative.

Model-inferred thematic similarity may suggest a composition but must not
silently rewrite container membership or shared truth.

### 3.3 Planned is not lived

Every lens must preserve these distinctions:

```text
considered != intended != planned != booked != occurred != liked != meaningful
```

A missed museum ticket can remain a valid Source and Plan history without
becoming a visit. A photographed dish may be valid evidence without proving who
ate it or how they felt. A shared Occasion may have one governed Occurrence and
several different personal Outcomes.

## 4. Lens contracts

### 4.1 Journey / Occasion

**Human question:** What was this coherent period or episode?

The projection may compose movement, bases, Places, people, decisions,
selected traces, changes, and plural Outcomes around one episode. It should
hide low-value operational debris while preserving inspection and correction.

It must not become a pile of equal artifacts or an automatically written
biography.

### 4.2 Timeline

**Human question:** What happened, what was intended, and what changed over
time?

Timeline uses meaningful episodes and occurrences as its main marks. Supporting
Sources can nest underneath them. It should preserve gaps and uncertain time,
show missed or changed intent only when useful, and avoid implying that every
captured item is an event.

### 4.3 Map

**Human question:** Where has my life touched the world, under what truth state,
and with whom?

Life Map is autobiographical and longitudinal. Places is situational and
world-facing. The Life Map may distinguish:

- visited or occurred;
- planned but not established as visited;
- Source-only or provisional;
- Mine or Together;
- time range; and
- Journey or Occasion membership.

It must not show a single undifferentiated heatmap that converts Plans, tickets,
photographs, saves, and occurrences into the same “been” signal.

### 4.4 Mine

**Human question:** What is accumulating for me?

Mine may include private Sources, personal Plans, attention, Outcomes,
corrections, saved compositions, and authorized shared material visible to this
person. Private meanings remain private even when their Occasion is shared.

### 4.5 Together

**Human question:** What exists between us under current permission?

Together composes a shared core with attributed contributions and plural
personal lanes. It follows membership epochs, audience grants, source removal,
and current viewing authority. It is not Mine with several fields removed, a
friend-activity feed, or a merged group scrapbook.

### 4.6 Library

**Human question:** Where is the thing I gave Vesper, and what has it become?

Library provides efficient retrieval by Source, type, owner, Place, person,
time, and container. It may offer system-generated bundles and optional pins,
but should not require manual filing, tags, captions, ratings, or inbox zero.

Useful bundles include:

- tickets and movement documents;
- photographs and media;
- books, films, articles, and cultural Sources;
- reservations and provider records;
- authored notes and observations;
- shared contributions; and
- generated compositions.

### 4.7 Generated composition

**Human question:** What can Vesper now help me see, understand, retrieve, or
continue that the raw record did not provide?

A composition must:

- add a reconstruction, mechanism, contrast, relation, explanation, or usable
  continuation;
- meet an evidence threshold;
- distinguish fact, human authorship, and generated synthesis;
- preserve source lineage and viewer scope;
- support exclusion or correction of a material input;
- avoid personality diagnosis and synthetic group meaning; and
- hand off any consequence to its durable owner.

A title plus summary of already-known facts is not a composition.

## 5. Shared evaluation method

### 5.1 Scored dimensions

Score every fixture and lens from 1–5:

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Entrance discipline** | Does the evidence earn the correct stable, nested, resurfaced, or search-only Life posture without promoting every object? |
| **Jobs coherence** | Are Find, Return, See anew or justified silence, and Continue each concrete without becoming equal navigation tabs? |
| **Episode coherence** | Does related material become one comprehensible episode instead of a card pile? |
| **Retrieval** | Can the person find a known object through time, Place, person, type, or search? |
| **Truth discipline** | Are Source, Plan, Occurrence, Outcome, and composition visibly distinct? |
| **Cross-lens consistency** | Does correction or audience change propagate without duplicate truth? |
| **New value** | Does Vesper add something beyond indexing and paraphrase? |
| **Effort** | Can the person benefit without filing, captioning, reviewing, or completing a profile? |
| **Social plurality** | Are author, audience, shared core, and subjective Outcomes preserved? |
| **Continuity** | Can the record appropriately improve a later Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion experience? |
| **Repairability** | Can the exact wrong date, Place, person, occurrence, membership, meaning, or audience relation be corrected? |
| **Restraint** | Does Life avoid identity judgment, completion pressure, scorekeeping, and archive theater? |

### 5.2 Jobs-first walkthrough template

Every evidence world must be written through the same product sequence before
its architectural lenses are evaluated:

| Walkthrough field | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Default Life entrance** | Whether the world deserves a stable door, where it sits, its viewer scope, and what honest state is visible before opening |
| **Find** | At least three realistic partial-context queries and the governed object or episode each should retrieve |
| **Return** | What becomes coherent on reopening, what remains nested, and which uncertainty or social boundary stays visible |
| **See anew** | One substance-bearing composition that adds an evidenced distinction, explanation, comparison, reconstruction, or playable return, plus one thin treatment the system must reject |
| **Continue** | One concrete handoff to Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion, including the context that must travel |
| **Sparse state** | The smallest evidence state that remains immediately useful without a generated story or setup request |
| **Rich state** | How abundant evidence increases resolution and possibility without becoming a card pile, capture score, or biography |

The default Life entrance is not guaranteed. A valid Source or Plan may remain
searchable, nested under another episode, or absent from the stable index when
it does not yet justify its own door. “See anew” is also eligibility-gated:
silence is correct when the available composition would only paraphrase,
decorate, congratulate, diagnose, or ask the person to provide more material.

The walkthrough specifies semantics and transitions, not card geometry. Lens
verification follows it to ensure the behavior can be supported without
duplicating owners or weakening truth.

### 5.3 Hard failures

A fixture fails regardless of score if it:

- treats a ticket, reservation, save, photograph, or imported route as proof of
  occurrence;
- makes Timeline, Map, and Library separate copies of the object;
- places a private Source or Outcome in Together without authority;
- collapses several people's experience into one interpretation;
- promotes every valid Source, Plan, or relation into a stable Life entrance;
- asks the person to finish organizing before providing value;
- turns generated prose into canonical fact;
- leaves deleted or corrected evidence active in another lens;
- generates a recap too thin to contribute new substance when silence would be
  more truthful;
- awards countries, streaks, completeness, or capture volume; or
- implies that Life must be continuously maintained to remain useful.

## 6. Fixture register

| **Sparse state** | One saved Place or route is immediately findable and may influence Places, but Life does not create a relationship door or longitudinal composition. One supported visit may appear inside its Occasion or period without claiming recurrence. |
| **Rich state** | Several supported visits, routes, Occasions, contributions, and explicit evaluations make longitudinal comparison possible. Richness creates better retrieval and current handoffs, not a stronger identity label or more public profile content. |

### 12.3 Lens verification

| Projection | Required treatment | Must remain absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Journey / Occasions** | Individual visits, dinners, or routes retain episode coherence; no fake Journey is needed merely because the Place recurs | One giant Red Hook album |
| **Timeline** | Supported visits, Plans, and Occasions appear in their periods; a longitudinal Place doorway can summarize coverage honestly | Repeated saves counted as repeated visits |
| **Map** | The autobiographical layer shows occurred, planned, and source-only relations distinctly; opening current context hands off to Places | Heatmap intensity presented as importance |
| **Mine** | Private Place history, explicit evaluations, Sources, and saved compositions | Identity diagnosis or unexplained affinity score |
| **Together** | Shared Occasions and attributed notes involving Red Hook | Friend presence outside authorized episode scopes |
| **Source retrieval** | Place-scoped Sources and contributions are retrievable without moving them from their original containers | Duplicate “Red Hook copies” of every artifact |
| **Composition** | A longitudinal Place reading that explains an evidenced relation—such as how harbor geometry and network access repeatedly shaped the route—and may open one current possibility | “You love Red Hook” or a frequency leaderboard |

### 12.4 Life–Places handoff

The distinction must be visible in behavior:

- **Life:** “Here is your governed history with Red Hook and what remains
  explicit, uncertain, or shared.”
- **Places:** “Given where you are, current conditions, and that history, here
  is what Red Hook makes possible now.”

`Return here` carries the selected time range, Place relation, relevant people,
and authorized prior Outcomes into Places. Places returns current provider and
world truth; it does not mutate the historical Life record unless an
authorized consequence occurs.

### 12.5 Management and repair

The person can:

- correct a Place binding or merge duplicate Place identities;
- change an explicit Place evaluation;
- detach a Source from the Place while retaining the Source;
- hide a Place reading without deleting visits;
- exclude one Occasion from a generated longitudinal composition; and
- share one Place perspective without exposing the complete personal history.

### 12.6 Derived requirements

- Place relationship projection across several object owners.
- Separate Plan, occurred, saved, noted, and explicit-evaluation states.
- Life Map to Places context handoff with identity preserved.
- Place-scoped source and Timeline queries.
- Composition thresholds that require more than frequency.

### 12.7 Pass condition

The accumulated record makes a Place more legible and useful on a later return
without converting recurrence into a trait, badge, recommendation cage, or
public identity.

## 13. Cross-fixture management grammar

Life should optimize for **Find, Return, See anew, Continue, scope, and repair**,
not for filing. Find, Return, and See anew are reasons to enter Life. Continue
is the governed handoff through which accumulated life affects the present or
future.

### 13.1 Four independent control planes

Every projection and lifecycle test must evaluate four control planes
separately:

| Plane | Question it answers | It must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Record authority** | What governed state may continue to exist, and under whose custody? | Who may currently see it, whether it may resurface, or whether a relationship is safe |
| **Audience authority** | Who may read or use which contribution, at what precision, purpose, and membership epoch? | Ownership, automatic return, or continued access after a boundary change |
| **Relationship safety** | Which people, relationships, or social peripheries must be excluded from current rendering and continuation? | Erasure of independent personal history or restoration of old grants after unblocking |
| **Return policy** | Where, when, why, and at what delivery tier may valid state reappear? | Permission to notify, create a Plan, message someone, book, or act |

The system must not model these as one visibility flag. A block is a safety
overlay; leaving changes membership; withdrawal or revocation changes a grant;
deletion changes custody or existence. Unblocking removes only the safety
overlay. Reconciliation begins a new relationship epoch and restores no prior
grant by implication.

### 13.2 Primary operations

| Human intent | Life treatment | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the original | Search or Library bundle with source inspection | Source custody |
| See the episode | Journey or Occasion composition | Existing Trip / Plan / Occasion / anchor authorities |
| Return through a person, Place, or landmark | Contextual search or a lens opened around the same episode graph | Existing owners plus viewer-safe read projection |
| See something new in the record | A source-grounded distinction, comparison, reconstruction, map, article, audio piece, or social juxtaposition | Composition manifest over ResourceRefs; never a replacement owner |
| See when it happened | Timeline using the relevant time role | Occurrence and owner revisions |
| See where it happened | Life Map with truth-state layer | Canonical Place plus occurrence evidence |
| See what exists between us | Together viewer projection | Membership, audience, Source author, Occasion |
| Correct one relation | Plain-language repair targeted to date, Place, person, occurrence, container, meaning, or audience | Owning authority plus causal invalidation |
| Hide from resurfacing | Remove from generated/foreground treatments without deleting underlying truth or requested retrieval | Viewer return policy / composition state |
| Detach from a container | Remove the membership relation, not the Source | Relation owner |
| Forget an interpretation | Release the interpretation and dependent state while preserving authorized raw Source if requested | Outcome / authored meaning / derived state |
| Delete the original | Delete or revoke Source custody and invalidate unsupported derivatives | Source owner |
| Leave or change membership | End or revise current shared access without silently withdrawing another person's contribution or deleting the historical record | Membership epoch and audience grants |
| Block or establish no-contact | Apply a relationship-safety boundary across direct and derived social treatments | Relationship-safety overlay |
| Unblock | Remove the safety overlay only; do not restore grants, membership, return rank, or prior relationship state | Relationship-safety overlay |
| Reconcile | Start a new relationship epoch with newly granted access and return policy | New membership/audience epoch |
| Continue in the world | Hand off to Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion with object, source, authority, and lens context | Destination owner |

The system must treat the following as separate controls even when the visual
surface later groups them under plain language:

1. **Correct** a fact, relationship, date, Place, person, occurrence, or
   audience;
2. **Detach** an item from one episode without deleting it;
3. **Exclude from resurfacing** while preserving retrieval and truth;
4. **Forget an interpretation** while preserving an authorized underlying
   Source when requested; and
5. **Delete or revoke the original**, with causal invalidation of unsupported
   derivatives;
6. **Leave or change membership**, without equating departure with contribution
   withdrawal; and
7. **Block, unblock, or reconcile**, without restoring historical authority by
   implication.

### 13.3 Source identity inside Life

Generated treatments must distinguish claim-local roles such as:

- **You said** or **You noticed**;
- **You captured**;
- **Maya contributed**;
- **The record shows**;
- **Vesper connected**;
- **Vesper suggests**;
- **External sources report**; and
- **Unresolved** or **disputed**.

One composition may legitimately contain several of these roles. Editing
Vesper's wording does not convert the supporting inference into human-authored
meaning. Every material claim must retain supporting ResourceRefs, source
spans where available, its epistemic operation, represented and generated
times, uncertainty, current audience/custody, correction owner, revision, and
invalidation state.

### 13.4 Optional curation, never required maintenance

Life may support:

- pinning a Journey, Occasion, Place, bundle, or composition;
- renaming a human-facing container;
- selecting a cover or representative trace;
- saving a generated composition;
- creating an explicit authored collection or thread; and
- excluding an item from one composition.

Life should not require:

- folder taxonomies;
- a review inbox for every provisional relation;
- tags before retrieval works;
- captions before media becomes useful;
- periodic cleanup to preserve accuracy;
- streaks, completeness, country counts, or annual quotas; or
- approval of every low-stakes private organization hypothesis.

If ambiguity does not change a material consequence, Life may preserve a
private provisional relation with a quiet correction path. If ambiguity changes
shared truth, occurrence, audience, action, or a significant composition, the
system must narrow, omit, or request the smallest necessary decision.

## 14. Candidate Life information architectures

The fixtures compare four hypotheses; they do not approve final geometry.

| Hypothesis | Strength | Structural weakness | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Timeline-first** | Familiar longitudinal retrieval | Fragments episodes, privileges capture frequency, weak for Plans and shared lifecycle | Secondary lens |
| **Library-first** | Excellent source retrieval and custody | Feels like files; artifacts crowd out Occasions, Plans, and Outcomes | Secondary lens |
| **Composition-first** | High delight and immediate meaning | Unpredictable, difficult to trust, risks generated-biography feed | Bounded region, not spine |
| **Container-first hybrid** | Preserves episode coherence while lenses and compositions add retrieval and meaning | Requires cross-owner aggregation and careful empty states | Recommended default hypothesis |

The provisional Life index should therefore test:

1. a contextual Life search that accepts incomplete cues involving people,
   Places, episodes, media, relative time, and adjacent landmarks;
2. active, recent, and meaningful Journey/Occasion doors organized as a stable
   episode index rather than a uniformly chronological list;
3. a legible Mine / Together scope control without requiring one permanent
   binary treatment inside every object;
4. stable contextual entrances to Timeline and Map, with People available
   inside search and episodes before becoming a global root lens;
5. source inspection and an optional all-sources utility without making
   “Library” the primary user-facing product metaphor;
6. a longitudinal doorway such as the current year or a landmark-bearing life
   period; and
7. a bounded return region for saved or newly valuable compositions, clearly
   separated from the stable record.

This is a semantic hierarchy, not a screen specification. Home retains
attention ranking and present consequentiality. Life ordering should be more
stable, legible, and user-steerable. Dynamic rank belongs primarily in the
bounded return region; it must not make the episode index feel like an
engagement feed.

### 14.1 Entrance posture is a routing decision

Each evidence world must resolve to one of five postures:

| Posture | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Stable Life door** | A durable, independently returnable episode or relationship that passes every entrance gate |
| **Nested doorway** | Valuable inside a parent Journey, Occasion, Place relationship, or period but not independently stable |
| **Contextual resurfacing** | Useful only in a relevant current context under current return authority |
| **Search-only** | Governed and retrievable on request, but not eligible for automatic return |
| **Pinned shortcut** | An explicit access choice layered over the object's existing semantic posture |

A stable door must pass all seven hard gates:

1. governed existence;
2. return identity;
3. truth adequacy;
4. nonredundant return value;
5. stability;
6. viewer-safe plurality; and
7. repairability.

Pinning cannot satisfy a failed truth or safety gate. It only creates a
shortcut to whatever object or query the person chose.

### 14.2 Search is target-first, context-attached

Life search should answer the request before explaining the archive. A result
may lead with the original Source, a governed direct answer, an episode, a
relationship projection, or a historical result with a separately refreshed
current-world handoff. It may then attach a compact **Around this** trail with
the minimum landmarks needed to restore orientation.

Each result contract carries identity and type, viewer scope, why it matched,
time roles, truth state, parent episode or relationship, author/contributor,
original Sources, adjacent landmarks, uncertainty/conflict, correction or
detach controls, and valid Continue paths. Search reformulations remain
ephemeral unless the person explicitly corrects, keeps, or names something.

## 15. Required read-model capabilities

These are target capabilities, not authorization for new tables or names:

### P0

1. **Life index projection** — viewer scope, entrance posture and hard-gate
   explanation, stable episode doors,
   landmark-bearing periods, bounded return region, honest
   counts/completeness, and contextual search affordance.
2. **Episode projection** — Journey or Occasion composition over references to
   existing owners, with nested supporting Sources and unresolved state.
3. **Timeline projection** — multiple time roles, episode folding, phase,
   occurrence state, gaps, and correction propagation.
4. **Life Map projection** — canonical Place references plus planned,
   occurred, source-only, Mine, Together, time-range, and container layers.
5. **Contextual search and Source retrieval projection** — target-first
   answers, heterogeneous Source search, bundles, contextual landmarks,
   multiple time roles, truth state, authorship, owner destinations,
   downstream-use inspection, and ephemeral query refinement; “Library” is an
   internal/read-model label until a user-facing need proves otherwise.
6. **Viewer-safe Together projection** — shared core, attributed lanes,
   membership and audience epochs, relationship-safety overlay, return policy,
   and plural Outcomes.
7. **Composition manifest** — input ResourceRefs, claim-local source roles and
   source spans, epistemic operation, claim/source lineage,
   represented period, generated time, audience, generated revision,
   exclusions, save/share state, and invalidation behavior.
8. **Return-policy compiler** — source-, episode-, person-, relationship-,
   social-periphery-, Place-, period-, composition-, surface-, purpose-, and
   duration-scoped eligibility with deterministic suppression precedence.
9. **Causal invalidation projection** — dependency traversal across claims,
   relations, covers, compositions, search snippets, cached projections,
   embeddings/indexes, exports, and pending handoffs.
10. **Cross-root Continue envelope** — origin root/reference/revision, selected
    object, lens, scope, represented time, authorized ResourceRefs, source
    roles, authored intention, trigger and eligibility basis, freshness,
    audience, allowed authority, completion semantics, suppression history,
    and receipt destination.

### P1 after P0 truth and retrieval are trusted

- People and circles as a full Life lens;
- explicit authored threads across Occasions;
- cross-Occasion comparisons;
- recurring practices or Place relationships that require more than frequency;
- periodic or event-triggered recap eligibility; and
- saved composition collections.

### Deferred

- global inferred personal knowledge graph;
- automatically inferred identity themes;
- relationship trajectories;
- public Life profile;
- algorithmic social feed;
- country, city, or activity achievements; and
- a universal generated biography.

## 16. Decisions exposed and adopted by the pack

The recommendations in O1–O16 are adopted for the semantic prototype through
the [Life continuity and return
decision](../decisions/2026-08-29-adopt-life-continuity-and-return-contract.md).
They remain numbered here so their fixture provenance stays inspectable.

### O1 — Is Journey a durable owner?

**Recommendation:** no new owner yet. First prove a Journey projection across
current Trip, Plan, Occasion, Source, anchor, occurrence, and Outcome
authorities. Promote only if correction, membership, or lifecycle cannot remain
coherent through references.

### O2 — What persists about a composition?

**Recommendation:** the underlying record always persists under its owners.
Generated output may be ephemeral, saved, shared, or regenerated. Persist a
composition identity only when the person saves or shares it, cites or acts
through it, or when stable revision and correction are necessary for later
use. Persist its lineage and invalidation requirements before persisting a
free-floating rendered body.

### O3 — What is Life's default landing mode?

**Recommendation:** test a stable, container-first Life index with contextual
search, meaningful episode doors, a landmark-bearing longitudinal entrance,
and one bounded return region. Do not default to raw Timeline, Map, source
library, or an algorithmic composition feed.

### O4 — Is Mine / Together a top-level switch or contextual scope?

**Recommendation:** retain an explicit scope control near the Life index and
search in the first semantic prototype because it makes privacy legible and
borrows a learned Personal / Shared / Both pattern. Do not force every episode
into a binary visual treatment. Permit object-native mixed previews only when
the governed shared core, attribution, audience, and plural Outcomes remain
obvious.

### O5 — Is People P0?

**Recommendation:** people are a P0 retrieval cue and must be queryable and
visible inside the episodes they change. A global People lens can remain P1
until Together, membership epochs, authored contributions, and audience-safe
cross-episode aggregation are trustworthy.

### O6 — How much system organization may happen without review?

**Recommendation:** private, reversible, source-bound bundling may happen with
quiet repair. Episode candidates may use changes in Place, people, activity,
goal, causal structure, and time, but no single cue is sufficient. Boundaries
must be mergeable, splittable, renameable, and detachable because retrieval can
reframe where an episode begins or ends. Ask only when classification changes
occurrence, audience, shared membership, external action, or a high-salience
composition.

### O7 — Should generated compositions appear chronologically?

**Recommendation:** compositions retain generated time and represented period,
but they should not masquerade as lived events. Timeline may show a saved or
shared composition in an authored/created layer, not among occurrences.

### O8 — Is Library a user-facing primary destination?

**Recommendation:** no. Preserve a complete heterogeneous source-retrieval read
model, but first expose contextual search, source inspection inside episodes,
and an optional “all sources” utility. Promote Library only if observed known-
item retrieval cannot remain legible through those entrances. The user should
not maintain folders or understand storage architecture to retrieve something.

### O9 — What is the stable unit and what may change dynamically?

**Recommendation:** governed authorities, episode membership, attribution,
audience, correction state, and user-authored names or curation are the stable
record. Editorial selection, resurfacing rank, media choice, and unsaved
compositions are dynamic projections. Dynamic changes must never silently
alter the record or the person's correction history.

### O10 — What are the minimum resurfacing controls?

**Recommendation:** support separate operations for correction, detachment from
an episode, exclusion from resurfacing, release of an interpretation, and
deletion or revocation of the original. Also support person-, Place-, date-,
episode-, and source-scoped exclusions where a single-item control would create
repeated work.

### O11 — How does a shared episode avoid manufacturing consensus?

**Recommendation:** compose a governed shared core alongside attributed
contributions and plural person- or relationship-scoped Outcomes. Permit
distributed steering and lightweight participant cues. Never convert several
private or authored interpretations into one unqualified group conclusion.

### O12 — What should Life do for a sparse or newly created account?

**Recommendation:** make admitted Sources, Plans, Places, and Occasions
retrievable immediately; form lightweight private episode candidates only when
evidence supports them; and continue into Home or Places when present value is
stronger than retrospective composition. Do not create blank-day prompts,
streak pressure, review inboxes, or generic reflections merely to make Life
look populated.

### O13 — How much contextual trail should search retain?

**Recommendation:** render the smallest useful **Around this** trail in the
current result and keep reformulations ephemeral by default. Persist only an
explicit correction, named query/collection, saved result, or authored
meaning. Sensitive search behavior must not quietly become a new personal
profile or resurfacing signal.

### O14 — Does leaving preserve historical shared-core access?

**Decision:** for an ordinary occurred social Occasion, leaving preserves a
frozen historical shared core from the participant's membership epoch. It
includes no later contributions or newly generated social return and remains
subject to Source withdrawal, audience narrowing, safety boundaries, legal
requirements, and an explicitly ephemeral/access-terminating Occasion policy.
Leaving does not silently withdraw prior contributions, erase another
participant's private history, or restore access on later reconciliation.

### O15 — Which Continue categories may enter Home automatically?

**Recommendation:** a historical object may enter Home only as a complete-value
possibility when current context and return policy support it. A carried
intention may advance to an offered carry-forward. Reminder, preparation,
message, booking, and action require increasingly explicit prospective
authority. Retrospective material never earns push merely by being meaningful.

### O16 — How compact can provenance become?

**Recommendation:** test compact claim-role language in reading and preserve
the full manifest behind inspection. Never collapse mixed authorship into a
single AI badge or imply that user editing endorses Vesper's inference. The
delayed source-identity test should determine the minimum visible treatment,
not whether lineage exists.

## 17. Fixture execution protocol

For each fixture:

1. instantiate the smallest governed object graph;
2. decide whether the evidence world deserves a stable Life entrance, a nested
   entrance, contextual resurfacing only, or search-only retrieval;
3. author its Find, Return, See anew, and Continue walkthrough, including the
   sparse and rich states;
4. verify the walkthrough through every relevant lens without designing cards
   or creating duplicate owners;
5. apply one boundary change, one correction, one resurfacing exclusion, one
   Source deletion, and one audience change;
6. verify every affected projection invalidates or degrades correctly;
7. generate one substance-bearing composition and one intentionally rejected
   thin composition;
8. hand one object to Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion and verify identity
   and context survive;
9. for shared evidence, verify governed common truth, attribution, and plural
   Outcomes remain distinguishable;
10. score the fixture using the shared rubric;
11. run every applicable social transition from the [deterministic lifecycle
    matrix](life-social-lifecycle-fixture-matrix-2026-08-29.md) and verify
    record, audience, safety, and return-policy effects independently;
12. run every assigned query from the [fifty-query re-finding
    benchmark](life-refinding-query-benchmark-2026-08-29.md) and verify
    target-first retrieval, useful contextual landmarks, and ephemeral
    refinement; and
13. record missing read capability separately from missing durable authority.

The first fixture pass should use deterministic data and authored expected
outputs. It is not a model-quality demonstration.

## 18. Acceptance gate

The Life direction is ready for visual composition only when all six fixtures
show that:

1. one object remains one governed object across every lens;
2. each evidence world defines a credible Find, Return, and Continue path plus
   either a substantive See anew treatment or an explicit, justified silence;
3. stable, nested, resurfaced, and search-only index postures remain honestly
   different rather than promoting every valid object into a Life door;
4. Journeys and Occasions are more coherent than fragment timelines;
5. inferred episode boundaries use several cues and remain reversible without
   rewriting their constituent authorities;
6. known Sources are easy to retrieve without manual filing or a prominent
   Library metaphor;
7. Plan, Occurrence, and Outcome never silently collapse;
8. Mine and Together remain meaningfully different and socially safe;
9. a shared episode preserves governed common truth, attribution, and plural
   Outcomes rather than manufacturing consensus;
10. correction, detachment, resurfacing exclusion, interpretation release, and
   Source deletion remain distinct and propagate appropriately;
11. the stable record remains legible when dynamic resurfacing rank and
    compositions change;
12. generated compositions add substance, expose lineage, and remain
    subordinate to evidence;
13. Life can improve a later Home, Places, Chat, Plan, or Occasion experience;
14. sparse accounts remain honest and useful without synthetic biography or
    input homework;
15. the person receives compounding value without accepting an archive job;
16. entrance postures are produced by all seven semantic gates rather than one
    importance or engagement score;
17. keeping, pinning, searchability, resurfacing, interruption, and action
    remain distinct grants;
18. record, audience, relationship safety, and return policy can change
    independently without leakage or accidental restoration;
19. search leads with the requested target or governed answer and retains no
    new durable state from mere reformulation;
20. claim-local source roles survive delayed reading, editing, sharing,
    correction, and invalidation;
21. Continue never skips an authority tier and returns a receipt only after a
    real state change; and
22. suppression and silence are accepted as correct outcomes while explicit
    authorized retrieval remains possible.

The compact product standard is:

> Life is not where the person proves they documented enough. Life is where
> Vesper proves that what they already gave it compounded.
