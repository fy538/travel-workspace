# Consequence Arbitration and Cross-Surface Experience Blueprints

> Status: working behavioral and product-design contract
>
> This document does not authorize a universal consequence table, a new root,
> a Status schema, automatic sharing, or production action. Canonical authority
> remains in Product Model, the Experience Constitution, Surfacing Strategy,
> Multiplayer Product Strategy, and each domain owner. This document makes
> their consequence boundary concrete enough to design and compare.

## 1. The decision

Vesper should absorb decision work without absorbing human authority.

> **Infer the consequence, prepare the work, and ask only at the boundary.**

The person should not choose a workflow, classify the input, reconstruct known
context, or rank a list of generic next steps. Vesper should solve the immediate
job, privately prepare at most one best next consequence, and then either:

- stop;
- expose one optional continuation;
- apply an already-authorized bounded action with an honest receipt; or
- ask for the smallest confirmation required by audience, shared expectation,
  external action, irreversibility, or uncertain authority.

The normal interaction is not a funnel. A complete answer may end. Preparation
does not imply persistence, presentation, notification, or execution.

## 2. Who chooses

| Decision | Responsible chooser |
|---|---|
| What the person probably needs now | Vesper infers from expressed intent and current context |
| Whether a continuation beats silence | Vesper arbitrates attention and cost |
| Which single consequence is worth preparing | Vesper selects; it does not present a workflow menu |
| What an experience means personally | The person |
| Whether private material crosses an audience boundary | Its authorized author or custodian |
| Whether shared truth or expectation changes | The principal(s) named by the Occasion constitution |
| Whether an external or irreversible action occurs | The authorized principal at the exact boundary |
| What provider-reported result occurred | The provider plus deterministic reconciliation |
| What a shared occurrence meant | Each person separately, with any shared Outcome explicitly authored |
| Whether a recurring pattern may be automated | The person through a narrow, revocable mandate |

Vesper chooses treatment and preparation. Humans authorize meaning, audience,
shared expectations, and external consequence. Domain owners persist truth.

## 3. Consequence transition

```text
understood input
  -> candidate consequences generated privately
  -> one candidate selected against silence
  -> prepared without crossing a boundary
  -> treatment gate: stop / quiet affordance / act / ask
  -> authorization resolved
  -> owner command applied or external attempt executed
  -> authoritative readback and receipt
  -> Vesper recedes
```

| State | Meaning | Product law |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate** | A possible continuation inferred by Vesper | Never visible or durable merely because it scored well |
| **Prepared** | Object, copy, recipients, owner, command, and preview are resolved enough to inspect | Preparation may not widen audience or change canonical truth |
| **Offered** | One optional continuation becomes visible | Ignore and dismiss are complete outcomes; do not repeat by default |
| **Authorized** | Explicit language, confirmation, Occasion authority, or an existing mandate covers the exact consequence | Authorization is scoped to actor, object, audience, consequence, and time |
| **Applied** | The owning system accepted the mutation or an external actor attempted it | Assistant prose is not proof |
| **Reconciled** | Readback establishes current truth, including unknown or failed outcomes | Never infer success from request completion or silence |
| **Receded** | Receipt remains available while the interface returns attention to the world | Do not append reflection homework or another generic CTA |

## 4. Authorization modes

### M0 — no boundary

Vesper answers, explains, or remains silent. No retention, audience, shared
expectation, or external state changes. No choice UI is required.

### M1 — optional opening

Vesper has found a useful but nonessential continuation. It renders one quiet
affordance without saving or sharing anything. Engagement authorizes only the
next view or explanation.

### M2 — choice already expressed in language

The person's natural language is the authorization. Vesper may apply the
consequence and return an Undo, correction, or revocation receipt only when all
of these are true:

1. actor and current channel are known;
2. object and intended owner are unambiguous;
3. audience is explicit or normally inherent in the current channel;
4. the consequence is the ordinary meaning of the utterance;
5. the actor has current authority;
6. the action is bounded and reversible or compensatable; and
7. it does not newly expose protected data, spend money, contact a provider,
   publish publicly, or create an irreversible commitment.

“I’ll bring tiramisu” inside an Occasion may create a visible contribution and
Commitment with Undo. A photograph uploaded privately does not authorize
sharing. A message or attachment deliberately sent into an Occasion inherits
the room audience; private derivations from it do not.

### M3 — one-tap material confirmation

Vesper prepares one specific consequence and asks once because the action
changes durable continuity, audience, another person's expectation, or a
meaningful shared object. The preview shows what, where, who can see it, and
the honest recovery path.

### M4 — affected-principal decision

The Occasion constitution routes a shared Decision to the people whose rights,
Commitments, money, safety, or experience are affected. Vesper narrows and
recommends rather than polling everyone by default.

### M5 — action under a bounded mandate

Vesper may execute a repeated pattern only within a previously granted scope.
Mandates name domain, actor, audience, consequence, expiry, material limits,
and recovery. They are earned from explicit use, not inferred from compliance.

### M6 — explicit external or public authorization

Provider contact, booking, payment, sensitive disclosure, public publication,
or an action with weak reversal requires explicit authorization at the exact
boundary. Unknown outcomes stop and reconcile before retry.

## 5. No-homework consequence laws

1. **Value before extraction.** Solve or illuminate the present job before
   asking for classification, reflection, retention, or contribution.
2. **One prepared consequence.** Do not show a generic menu of everything that
   could happen next.
3. **No trailing question by default.** A satisfactory answer may simply end.
4. **Clarify only material ambiguity.** Ask one bounded question only when its
   answer changes audience, authority, consequence, or external action.
5. **Do not reconfirm an explicit reversible choice.** Natural language can be
   the command when M2 conditions hold.
6. **Prepare before presenting.** Resolve object, owner, people, audience,
   materiality, and preview instead of sending the user into setup.
7. **Progressively disclose destinations.** Keep private, Send, Share, Status,
   Occasion, and Place appear only after relevant intent or an invoked share
   affordance—not after every Ask or Bring.
8. **Receipt instead of interrogation.** After a bounded write, show what
   changed and how to undo; do not ask the person to restate the decision.
9. **Silence completes the loop.** Ignore, dismiss, decline, expiry, and no
   durable Outcome are valid endings.
10. **Autonomy stays narrow.** Repetition may earn a mandate for one action
    pattern, never a global permission to “do things for me.”

### Portfolio-wide negative oracles

- A complete answer becomes a retention prompt.
- Vesper shows multiple generic next-step buttons after every interaction.
- A private Source becomes social because its content seems relevant.
- Status is generated or published without human authorship.
- Speaking in an Occasion grants authority outside the room or current object.
- Undo is promised where another person or provider may already possess the
  information.
- A shared Decision becomes a poll because Vesper did not narrow the problem.
- Afterglow asks every participant to journal or authors one group meaning.
- Public Place contribution becomes a feed, popularity system, or creator rank.

## 7. Composite storyboard A — Rome: understanding becomes expression

### A1 — Ask and immediate value

At the Colosseum, the person asks why Aeneas and Troy appear in Rome's origin
story. Vesper answers at walking depth: Aeneas is a Trojan survivor whose later
journey was used to connect Rome to the heroic world of Troy. The answer is
complete. No Keep or Share sheet appears.

### A2 — Quiet connection

Because the person's recent movie ticket or conversation is authorized and the
moment is receptive, Vesper may add one M1 affordance:

```text
This is the Roman continuation of the Trojan world you were already carrying.
[Connect it to The Odyssey]
```

Ignoring it retains nothing. Opening it deepens the explanation without
silently turning the ticket or interest into identity.

### A3 — Private continuity

If the person says, “Keep this connection—I want to return to it,” that language
is an M2 private command. Vesper writes through the existing Source/thread or
Outcome owner and responds: `Kept privately · View connection · Undo`.

### A4 — Featured expression

Later, after the person says they have just returned from Nice and Sorrento and
are still thinking about regional food and ancient stories, Vesper may prepare:

```text
Back from Nice and Sorrento.
Still noticing how food and old stories change from place to place.

Friends · until replaced
[Feature] [Edit] [Dismiss]
```

The human language remains primary. Feature is M3 because audience changes.
The result appears on the chosen social face, not as a Home feed or live
location. Eligible friends may use it as an opener; nobody receives a push
merely because Status changed.

### A5 — Addressed handoff

“Send the Aeneas connection to my girlfriend—this is what we were talking
about at the Colosseum” supplies sender, recipient, object, and human reason.
If the recipient resolves unambiguously, M2 applies. Vesper sends exactly that
authored framing, preserves source, and returns a receipt. It does not write a
more intimate message or expect a response.

### Surface trace

```text
Vesper: Ask, answer, optional connection, prepared consequence
Place: Colosseum identity and source-aware world truth
Life/You: private kept thread and chosen Status/control
Recipient view: authorized Status or directed handoff
Home: nothing unless a later return genuinely earns attention
```

## 8. Composite storyboard B — Brooklyn dinner: conversation becomes shared life

### B1 — Create through Chat

“Dinner at mine Friday. Invite Lena and Reza.” Vesper resolves the people and
known home Place. If time is materially missing, it asks only, “What time?” It
then shows one M3 preview with title, Place precision, time, invitees, and the
ordinary named-invitation/host-membership default. Confirmation creates the
Occasion and Invitations; readback proves the revision.

### B2 — Contribute without a form

Inside the Occasion:

```text
“I’ll bring tiramisu.”        -> M2 Commitment · Added · Undo
“I can get there after nine.” -> M2 participation window · Updated · Undo
“I need to leave by eleven.”  -> M2 boundary · Updated · Undo
photo deliberately sent here -> room-visible artifact; private derivations stay private
```

Nobody fills out an Occasion profile. Quiet presence counts. Vesper does not
turn every message into structured state; it extracts only when the ordinary
meaning changes coordination or shared expectations.

### B3 — Live repair and silence

An F-train delay threatens arrival and dinner timing. Vesper privately prepares
one repair. If no mandate exists, the affected shared change is M4: one proposal
with the people and consequence already resolved. If everyone is naturally
handling it, Vesper stays silent. The Occasion, any Plan projection, and Home
must read the same accepted result.

### B4 — Afterglow without extraction

Afterward, the Occasion shifts phase. It may show what occurred, accepted
photos or contributions, separate viewer-authorized Outcomes, and one unfinished
thread. It does not ask each person to recap the evening. A person may privately
remember someone, share a trace, correct occurrence, or let the boundary expire.

### Surface trace

```text
Vesper: create, clarify once, contributions, decisions, repair
Occasion: membership, participation, shared truth, before/live/after projection
Home: invitation, material live change, earned afterglow return—or quiet
Plan: only prospective structure and accepted consequence
Life/You: private Outcomes, relationship continuity, correction and controls
```

## 9. Composite storyboard C — practical Place capability becomes transferable judgment

### C1 — Solve the practical job

The person photographs a pasta menu and asks what to order. Vesper translates,
explains the material differences relevant to the meal, and recommends one
choice. No journaling or cultural lesson is owed.

### C2 — Make one distinction perceivable

If attention permits, Vesper offers one grounded cue—for example, what texture,
sauce relationship, or regional logic to notice. The person later recognizes
that distinction while cooking at home. Capability transfer is the Outcome;
durable artifact creation is optional.

### C3 — Keep or hand off

“Keep the difference between these two pasta styles” is an M2 private command.
“Send this to Maya before she goes” becomes an M2/M3 addressed handoff depending
on recipient and copy ambiguity. Vesper does not convert the insight into a
universal taste claim.

### C4 — Contribute to the Place

“Leave this tip for other people looking at this menu” prepares a public Place
contribution. M6 preview shows the exact bounded observation, Place, source,
attribution, uncertainty, audience, and withdrawal limit. The public projection
does not expose the private conversation, companions, or inferred preferences.

### Surface trace

```text
Vesper: practical answer, optional perceptual cue, preparation
Place: identity, operational truth, governed public perspective
Life/You: optional private capability/thread and authorship controls
Recipient view: directed handoff with provenance
Home: later transfer only when a real cooking or Place moment makes it useful
```

## 10. Surface consequence responsibilities

| Surface | Consequence responsibility | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| **Vesper** | Admission, understanding, private preparation, negotiation, bounded confirmation, and receipt | Workflow selector, universal owner, or endless CTA generator |
| **Home** | The one prepared, active, changed, or returned consequence that deserves attention now—and intentional quiet | Feed of every candidate, artifact, or social update |
| **Occasion** | Membership, participation, shared Decisions and Commitments, live truth, repair, and phase-relative afterglow | Profile system, generic event workspace, or group scrapbook |
| **Place** | Stable world truth, situated interpretation, spatial consequence, and governed public contribution | Review directory, creator leaderboard, or private-memory owner |
| **Life / You** | Private continuity, chosen Status, relationships, correction, audience, mandate, and forgetting controls | Archive dump, static dossier, or performance profile |
| **Plan** | Prospective structure and accepted consequence | Required container for curiosity or owner of lived meaning |
| **Push** | Material time-sensitive boundary where delayed awareness would create harm | Recommendation habit or reminder of ignored optional openings |

Home's product question becomes concrete:

> **What prepared, active, changed, or returned consequence—if any—deserves my
> attention now?**

## 11. Architecture implications, not authorization

The behavioral contract suggests a storage-neutral prepared-consequence
envelope before any generic persistence:

```yaml
prepared_consequence:
  source_turn_or_event: null
  immediate_job: null
  intent: null
  proposed_owner: null
  proposed_command: null
  actor: null
  affected_principals: []
  audience: []
  authorization_mode: m0 | m1 | m2 | m3 | m4 | m5 | m6
  materiality: null
  reversibility: null
  authority_basis: null
  confidence: null
  preview: null
  expires: null
```

This is an application decision object, not a durable universal authority. It
should normally disappear after stop, expiry, command resolution, or receipt.
Owner-specific commands remain typed: Keep, Address, Share, Feature, Occasion
contribution, Place contribution, Plan/Commitment mutation, and provider action.

The implementation sequence generated by these experiences is:

1. static E01-E08 fixtures with authorization-mode and negative-oracle checks;
2. pure consequence preparation and treatment arbitration;
3. exact owner-command preview and receipt contracts;
4. mobile prepared-action, inline receipt, and progressive-destination
   treatments;
5. Occasion artifact and non-artifact contribution adapters;
6. featured-share and directed-handoff authority;
7. before/live/after Occasion composition;
8. public Place contribution only after custody and moderation are explicit;
9. cross-surface readback, correction, expiry, revocation, and native visual QA.

No item authorizes a database migration until existing Source, Opening,
Occasion, Plan, Commitment, Outcome, permission, and receipt owners are mapped
and shown insufficient.

## 12. Decisions and remaining questions

### Recommended decisions

- Natural language counts as authorization under M2's complete condition set.
- The authored message or attachment inherits the current channel audience;
  derived interpretation and private context do not.
- A clear reversible contribution may apply immediately with readback and an
  honest Undo or correction path.
- The full destination chooser appears progressively, never after every input.
- First-time or changed Status audience requires preview; later defaults remain
  bounded and user-controlled.
- Shared changes route through affected principals rather than broad polling.
- Afterglow is passive composition first; reflection and sharing stay optional.
- Learned autonomy is mandate-specific and revocable.

### Questions for behavioral design

1. Which exact Occasion utterances feel like obvious M2 contribution commands,
   and which feel over-interpreted?
2. When does a quiet M1 affordance feel helpful versus like an unwanted CTA?
3. Which Status expiry language feels natural without turning expression into
   maintenance?
4. When does immediate application plus receipt feel safer than confirmation?
5. Which Home return treatments feel like recognition rather than resurfacing?
6. What honest revocation copy is needed when a person or provider may already
   possess the projection?

## Closing principle

> Vesper should make consequence feel like the natural completion of the
> person's intention: most of the work already carried, the real boundary
> unmistakable, and no additional labor after the moment is complete.
