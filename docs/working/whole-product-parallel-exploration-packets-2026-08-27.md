# Whole-Product Parallel Exploration Packets

> Status: bounded working package for divergent product investigation and later
> convergence. It changes no product canon, release scope, architecture, or
> production surface.

> **2026-08-28 composition correction:** the scenario packet's earlier
> one-opening language governs the dominant consequence or action-bearing ask,
> not the full Home possibility field. Use
> [Home Possibility Field and Generative Opportunity Portfolio](home-possibility-field-and-generative-opportunity-portfolio-2026-08-28.md)
> for quiet and returned Home composition.

## 1. Outcome and use

This package lets several investigators explore Vesper's unresolved product
shape in parallel without dividing the product into unrelated features.

All investigators work from the same four situations, the same product laws,
and the same output contract. They examine different dimensions of the same
experience:

1. **Adaptive experience composition** — how the product materially expresses
   the situation through Home, Chat, Places, Life, and temporary interfaces.
2. **Situated judgment and consequential continuity** — what Vesper understands,
   when it intervenes, how it joins practical and meaningful help, and how a
   later occasion becomes genuinely different.
3. **Multiplayer agency and smooth consequences** — how independent personal
   loops cross into bounded shared action, authorship, participation, authority,
   receipts, and plural outcomes.
4. **Visual language and affective identity** — what Vesper should feel like,
   which visual rules make that feeling recognizable across heterogeneous
   situations, and whether the current paper / ink / gold / editorial system is
   foundation, one candidate, or inherited constraint.

The founder and the originating synthesis thread own convergence. Individual
investigators may recommend product language and architecture implications;
they do not independently change canonical nouns, root navigation, shared
contracts, production code, or product canon.

## 2. Research verdict: are four directions justified?

### 2.1 Decision

**Four parallel directions are justified: composition, judgment across time,
shared agency, and visual language.**

The earlier split—dynamic interfaces, situational intelligence, and
multiplayer plus consequences—left continuity under-owned. Continuity is not a
decorative Life concern. **Carry forward** is one of the four product moves,
the proposed retention mechanism, part of the moat hypothesis, and the
canonical “second occasion” proof. It now belongs explicitly to Direction 2.

The directions are research lanes, not the product grammar. Every lane must
test **Make sense, Open possibility, Help it work, and Carry forward** where
relevant, and multiplayer must remain cross-cutting rather than owned only by
Direction 3.

The first three lanes remain correctly scoped. The fourth is warranted because
adaptive composition and visual language answer different questions:

- composition asks what form the interface takes, how it behaves, what owns
  truth, and how it changes across roots and situations;
- visual language asks what emotional promise, typography, color, material,
  imagery, iconography, density, and motion make those forms feel like one
  product.

If one investigator owns both, a review cannot tell whether a treatment failed
because its interaction structure was wrong or because its art direction was
wrong. The controlled method is to hold scenario content and interaction
geometry stable while varying visual language, then let the composition lane
hold visual styling provisional while it varies structure.

The other apparently missing concerns should not become separate feature lanes:

| Concern | How it is owned in this round |
|---|---|
| Universal admission and intake | Direction 2 owns classification, context assembly, and immediate-job inference; Direction 1 owns legible entry; Direction 3 owns audience changes after intake. |
| Consequence and action | Direction 3 owns choice, authority, execution boundary, and receipts across personal as well as multiplayer actions; Directions 1 and 2 must make the treatment and judgment legible. |
| Continuity, Life, and second occasion | Direction 2 is primary; Direction 1 renders it; Direction 3 owns relational and shared continuity. |
| Visual language and affective identity | Direction 4 owns emotional register, identity rules, type, color, material, image, icon, density, and motion. Direction 1 owns structural morphology and interaction behavior. |
| Trust, correction, release, and failure | Mandatory negative-oracle pass in all four directions; never a later settings project. |
| Accessibility and divided attention | Mandatory prototype condition in all four directions; especially bright sun, walking, heat, one hand, interruption, and poor connectivity. |
| Architecture | Derived independently by every direction, then reconciled after experience convergence. A detached architecture lane would repeat the premature-abstraction failure the repository already warns against. |
| Behavioral validation | The synthesis owner converts the resulting prototypes into testable rounds. Evaluation is a shared gate, not a fourth concept-design silo. |
| Wedge, growth, and investor narrative | Important but outside this composition round. They should be revisited after the integrated experience becomes concrete enough to explain and demonstrate. |

The fourth lane is not a general “whole product” critic and not a polish pass.
It is a controlled visual-system investigation. Convergence and adversarial
evaluation remain founder-owned shared functions rather than a fifth lane.

### 2.1.1 Why the existing visual system is reopened

The repository contains two incompatible levels of confidence:

- the canonical mobile Design Language declares a leather travel journal,
  warm parchment, ochre/gold, EB Garamond, tactile radii, and an editorial
  travel register as the aesthetic identity;
- the August visual-direction dossier calls the code a semantic scaffold,
  treats major visual claims as hypotheses, asks for three controlled visual
  directions, and says the exploration must permit a surprising fourth answer.

The current foundation packet then materializes one very specific answer:
large editorial serif, warm paper, gold mono labels, sparse institutional
boards, hairlines, and document-like compositions. It is coherent and
thoughtful, but it may also read as museum, publication, luxury-travel
editorial, or a beautifully systematized research dossier. Those are known
failure risks for a product that must also feel alive, native, practical,
social, ordinary, and useful in motion.

Therefore:

- product laws, trust semantics, accessibility, native behavior, and
  world-facing restraint remain constraints;
- current tokens, screenshots, and foundation boards are evidence of the
  present system, not the target;
- “living field atlas,” “Quiet Field Atlas,” “Living Ephemera,” and
  “Relational Correspondence” remain hypotheses, not required aesthetics; and
- Direction 4 must be free to keep, transform, or retire parchment, gold,
  Garamond, mono stamps, document metaphors, subdued earth tones, and the
  current density—provided it explains what replaces their functional jobs.

No claim is left with “everyone” as its only owner.

### 2.3 External method research

The operating method is intentionally **parallel but comparable**:

- Dow et al.'s parallel-prototyping research found that parallel concepts,
  compared directly, produced more divergence, more explicit comparison, less
  attachment to a single concept, and stronger design results than serial
  refinement. The useful unit is not four unrelated reports; it is several
  alternatives responding to a shared brief.
  [Stanford HCI paper](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/2010/parallel-prototyping/ParallelPrototyping2010-final.pdf)
- Scenario-based design makes envisioned episodes the shared representation
  through which understanding, interaction design, and system requirements can
  inform one another. That supports using the same lived stories across all
  four investigations instead of assigning one feature to each agent.
  [Rosson and Carroll](https://www.frankritter.com/papers/rossonC02.pdf)
- Set-based concurrent design explores feasible alternatives from different
  functional perspectives, establishes constraints before commitment, and
  converges through the intersection of compatible sets rather than selecting
  one early point solution. This is the right mental model for Vesper's
  experience, intelligence, social, and architecture seams.
  [Set-based design overview](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/design-science/article/adopt-an-augmented-setbased-design-framework-with-optimisation/C19D43F222371477EDBB1D924C534CFD)
- GOV.UK's prototyping guidance recommends using the lowest-cost prototype that
  can answer the present question, testing alternatives before production, and
  treating prototype code as disposable rather than silently promoting it into
  the product.
  [GOV.UK prototyping guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-prototypes)
- Human-AI guidance from Google PAIR and Microsoft HAX requires examining the
  experience initially, during interaction, when the AI is wrong, and over
  time; it also emphasizes understandable control, editability, graceful
  fallback, and context-sensitive automation.
  [Google PAIR](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapter/feedback-controls/),
  [Microsoft HAX](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/)

These findings produce four operating rules:

1. Parallelize **lenses over shared situations**, not features over separate
   stories.
2. Require concrete, comparable artifacts rather than unrestricted essays.
3. Keep several viable treatments alive until their cross-system constraints
   are visible.
4. Converge through intersection: retain only choices that work experientially,
   socially, epistemically, and architecturally.

## 3. Shared product brief

### 3.1 Product in one paragraph

Vesper is personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world. A person
can bring whatever already has attention—a question, Place, photograph,
ticket, invitation, Plan, changing situation, or unfinished human thread.
Vesper solves the immediate job, connects the fragment to the relevant people,
Place, Moment, Source, Occasion, and prior experience, then helps the smallest
worthwhile consequence happen or returns attention to the world. It should make
the world more legible, the day lighter, and shared experience more coherent
without becoming the experience.

### 3.2 Stable invariants for this round

- Accept before organizing; no workflow selection before value.
- Solve the immediate job before interpretation, retention, reflection, or
  social projection.
- Known interests are bridges, not identity borders.
- Practical relief, changed perception, increased capability, coordinated
  action, human opening, continuity, and silence are all valid consequences.
- Vesper chooses what is worth preparing; people retain authority over meaning,
  audience, shared expectations, and external or weakly reversible action.
- Screens own durable truth; conversation owns movement.
- Artifacts are viewer-safe carriers over canonical truth, not universal
  authorities or synonyms for vertical cards.
- Shared occurrence may become coherent while personal outcomes remain plural.
- Social assistance reduces discovery and coordination friction while keeping
  expression, care, vulnerability, and response human.
- Continuity is earned only when a later perception, decision, action,
  conversation, capability, or occasion becomes materially different.
- Proactivity competes with silence and uses the quietest sufficient surface.
- Infrastructure providers retain authority for maps, inventory, transit,
  weather, communication, and transactions. Vesper preserves experiential
  intent, context, permission, and outcome across them.

### 3.3 Root hypotheses under test

| Root | Human question | Product responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | What matters now, and what is becoming possible? | One dominant prepared, active, changed, or returned consequence within a bounded portfolio of grounded possibilities—or honest low-pressure quiet |
| **Chat / Vesper** | What can I ask, show, or work through? | Universal admission, interpretation, negotiation, preparation, and compact receipts |
| **Places** | What is around me, what is this Place, and how can I inhabit it? | Spatial and world truth, conditions, interpretation, relationship, and possibility |
| **Life** | What is accumulating for me and between us? | Mine/Together continuity, people, episodes, threads, artifacts, correction, and release |

The roots are attention postures, not four databases. The same canonical object
may appear differently in several roots while retaining one owner and revision.

### 3.4 Current implementation reality to respect

- The repository has meaningful canonical identity, viewer-safe projection,
  revision, audience, lifecycle, action-resolution, receipt, and fallback
  substrate.
- Chat currently supports a broad attachment registry plus a safe bounded
  `composed_card.v1`; its general composition remains Chat-specific and mostly
  a vertical stack.
- Places has rich but partly legacy card/render-plan machinery and several
  native spatial and editorial components.
- Hosted Occasion, invitation, decision, commitment, contribution, and outcome
  slices exist at differing levels of implementation and certification.
- The current root shell is not the proposed Home / Chat / Places / Life shell.
  Life remains a product and navigation hypothesis.
- The August product model is substantially broader than the currently polished
  release surfaces. Investigators must distinguish reusable substrate,
  adaptable code, missing product behavior, and speculative architecture.

### 3.5 Non-goals

- Do not redesign every existing screen.
- Do not produce a generic product vision or feature inventory.
- Do not authorize a universal UI DSL, universal artifact table, universal
  relationship graph, or universal consequence state machine.
- Do not make Life an archive, Places a feed, Home an inbox, Chat the owner of
  everything, or Occasion a collaboration workspace.
- Do not treat mockup beauty, response impressiveness, saved artifacts, screen
  time, invitations, or message volume as proof of human value.
- Do not modify canonical documents or production code during this divergent
  investigation.

## 4. Shared output contract

Each investigator must work through all four packets and return a comparable
report. High-level recommendations without the following evidence are
incomplete.

### 4.1 Required output for every direction

1. **Code-and-document diagnosis**
   - What the repository already supports.
   - What is merely documented.
   - What is absent or contradicted.
   - Cite exact files and relevant code symbols.
2. **Four complete experience traces**
   - Exact moment-by-moment user experience.
   - What appears in Home, Chat, Places, and Life.
   - What changes state and what remains temporary.
   - Include user-facing copy, controls, transition, receipt, and ending—not
     only a concept description.
3. **Alternatives at the hardest seam**
   - At least two materially different treatments for each packet's main
     unresolved question.
   - Compare them; do not immediately collapse to the first plausible answer.
4. **AI behavior across time**
   - Initially, during interaction, when wrong, and over time.
   - Include silence, dismiss, correction, undo/release, expiry, and degraded
     or offline behavior.
5. **Object and state implications**
   - Canonical owners, projections, temporary state, authority, audience,
     revisions, actions, receipts, and cross-root readback.
   - Recommend contracts only when multiple scenarios require the same seam.
6. **Negative oracles**
   - What must never happen, including seductive failure modes.
7. **Decisions and uncertainties**
   - What should remain stable.
   - What can be provisionally chosen.
   - What requires prototype or behavioral evidence.
8. **One next prototype**
   - The smallest whole-product prototype that can falsify the direction's
     most important assumption without becoming a narrow feature proof.
9. **Adjacency handoff**
   - Questions and constraints the three adjacent investigators must resolve.

### 4.2 Shared comparison rubric

Every proposed treatment is judged on:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Immediate value | Can the person proceed without first organizing, teaching, or reflecting? |
| Situational fit | Does the treatment fit Place, time, attention, physical burden, people, and current commitments? |
| World-facing quality | Does the interface return attention to the world or become the experience? |
| Capability delta | Might the person later perceive, navigate, judge, communicate, or act with less assistance? |
| Consequence fluency | Does intention become the smallest appropriate consequence without a workflow menu? |
| Authority | Are authorship, audience, affected principals, external action, and reversibility unmistakable? |
| Plurality | Can common action remain coherent without merging personal context or meaning? |
| Continuity | Is any retained consequence bounded, correctable, and able to change a later occasion? |
| Root coherence | Does each root have a distinct job while the same object retains identity? |
| Adaptive expression | Does interface shape follow the situation rather than force everything into cards or prose? |
| Affective fit | Does the visual language feel alive, personal, practical, and world-facing across ordinary and extraordinary experience—not merely tasteful? |
| Visual identity | Is the product recognizable through generative rules rather than one palette, font, card, or decorative metaphor? |
| Quiet and recovery | Can ignore, decline, failure, correction, expiry, and silence complete the interaction cleanly? |
| Architectural leverage | Does the proposal reveal a reusable seam without allowing the first case to define a universal abstraction? |

## 5. Scenario Packet S01 — Cold start in a hot New York evening

### 5.1 Human situation

It is Thursday evening in New York. The person has little or no durable Vesper
history. It remains hot, they may want to go out, but they do not want to plan a
night or browse an overwhelming nearby feed. They may open Home, Places, or Chat
without a precise query.

### 5.2 Immediate job

Help me decide whether there is one low-effort thing worth doing nearby—or let
me stop—without requiring preferences, onboarding, a Plan, or memory.

### 5.3 Known and unknown context

Known or safely obtainable:

- approximate current area with permission;
- current local time and public weather;
- public Place hours, route facts, and reachability where verified;
- the person's explicit request in this interaction.

Unknown unless supplied:

- whether the person wants company;
- whether heat is merely unpleasant or an accessibility constraint;
- stable taste, energy, budget, or willingness to travel;
- whether an offered Place will matter personally.

### 5.4 Baseline experience trace

```text
open Home or Places
  -> Vesper compares one quiet opening with silence
  -> “Nothing needs your attention” remains a valid dominant state
  -> optionally show one cooler, reversible possibility and one fallback
  -> person inspects, asks, follows a route, dismisses, or closes
  -> no Plan, profile, memory, or follow-up is required
```

If the person instead asks, “What can I do nearby that will not be exhausting
in this heat?”, Chat should answer immediately from current conditions and may
open a spatial comparison without asking them to choose a workflow.

### 5.5 Four-root responsibility

| Root | Required expression |
|---|---|
| Home | Honest low-pressure quiet with several substantively different, complete-on-view possibilities when grounded; no trip list, repeated recommendation shelf, or manufactured urgency |
| Chat | Open multimodal admission, immediate answer, bounded clarification only if material |
| Places | One or two condition-aware possibilities with experienced reachability and easy exit |
| Life | Explain by its emptiness that immediate help need not become collection, profile, or task |

### 5.6 Hard questions for parallel exploration

- **Adaptive composition:** What makes quiet feel intentional rather than empty?
  When should a spatial instrument replace prose? How does the same opening
  change across Home, Chat, and Places without duplication?
- **Judgment and continuity:** What evidence makes an opening beat silence?
  How should Vesper infer “not exhausting” without inventing identity? What,
  if anything, is allowed to affect a later evening?
- **Agency and multiplayer:** When is “send this to someone” relevant rather
  than another CTA? If the person invites a companion, what is the smallest
  safe crossing into shared context?
- **Visual language:** Can quiet feel warm and intentional without resembling
  an empty editorial spread? What visual identity survives cold start, bright
  sun, a mundane local opening, and the absence of travel imagery?

### 5.7 Required branches

- no location permission;
- Place hours or weather freshness is uncertain;
- the person dismisses the opening;
- the person says the route still sounds exhausting;
- the person asks for company or names someone;
- the person chooses a Place but does not create a Plan;
- poor connectivity after the route opens.

### 5.8 Negative oracles

- A generic nearby feed answers uncertainty with more abundance.
- Cold start becomes preference onboarding.
- “Hot” is stored as a durable personal limitation.
- A dismissed suggestion returns repeatedly.
- Home performs intelligence theater when nothing earns attention.

## 6. Scenario Packet S02 — Rome now: heat, movement, Troy, and a pair

### 6.1 Human situation

The founder and his girlfriend arrive in Rome during August after Nice,
Sorrento, and the Amalfi Coast. Rome feels overwhelmingly dense with things to
see and remains hot even at night. A conventional tour guide sounds helpful in
theory but risks becoming generic, tiring homework. At the Colosseum, the Troy
story appears. Two weeks earlier they watched a film adaptation of *The
Odyssey*. The connection is satisfying, but Homeric return, Aeneas's departure
from Troy, and Rome's later origin story must not be collapsed into one false
story.

### 6.2 Immediate jobs

There are several competing jobs:

1. Get to and through the Colosseum comfortably and on time.
2. Understand one culturally meaningful connection at walking depth.
3. Preserve conversation and shared presence with the partner.
4. Decide whether the connection should end, deepen, become private
   continuity, or become a human-to-human opening.

Operational needs outrank optional interpretation when they compete.

### 6.3 Evidence and authority

- Ticket: source evidence and prospective commitment; not proof of attendance.
- Current heat, route, entrance, water, hours: public or provider facts with
  freshness requirements.
- Movie ticket or explicit prior conversation: private source that may support
  relevance if authorized; it does not prove mythology expertise or preference.
- Pair/trip context: shared operational state where authorized; personal
  interpretation remains separate.
- Later occurrence: must be reconciled separately from what the visit meant.

### 6.4 Baseline experience trace

```text
Home foregrounds entry readiness, exposed walk, water, and leave time
  -> one line notes that Troy has returned through a prior authorized source
  -> Places supplies the lower-burden approach and one optional historical layer
  -> Chat explains Odysseus / Aeneas / Rome with source distinctions intact
  -> Vesper withdraws while the pair experiences the Place
  -> afterward, occurrence can be reconciled without demanding meaning
  -> either person may keep, correct, address, or release their own connection
```

### 6.5 Four-root responsibility

| Root | Required expression |
|---|---|
| Home | One dominant operational truth plus one optional meaningful connection; no stacked tour itinerary |
| Chat | Seeded private or shared explanation with exact source boundaries and an optional deeper path |
| Places | Spatial route, current conditions, Place identity, and one situated interpretive layer |
| Life | Europe episode, private “Troy and return” thread, separate personal outcomes, and evolving Place relationships |

### 6.6 Hard questions for parallel exploration

- **Adaptive composition:** When should the interface become a route, a causal
  sequence, an evidence relationship, shared audio, or a sparse reading? Can one
  artifact move among these treatments without becoming one universal card?
- **Judgment and continuity:** How does Vesper arbitrate heat, entry time,
  attention, cultural relevance, and partner conversation? What makes the movie
  bridge illuminating rather than contrived? What is the legitimate second
  occasion?
- **Agency and multiplayer:** What is shared between the pair by default? When
  does “this made me think of our movie” remain human conversation rather than
  an AI message? How can one partner borrow the other's perspective without
  creating a group interpretation?
- **Visual language:** Can practical urgency, monumental Place, source
  evidence, intimate recognition, and optional depth coexist without becoming
  a museum guide, luxury travel magazine, or dense operational dashboard?

### 6.7 Required branches

- too hot or fatigued for the original route;
- late arrival or changed entry conditions;
- one partner wants the interpretation and the other does not;
- no authorized movie-ticket source exists;
- Vesper makes an inaccurate or overconfident historical connection;
- either person keeps a private connection;
- the couple discusses it but retains nothing;
- a later Rome or non-Rome moment makes the thread relevant—or does not.

### 6.8 Negative oracles

- The practical answer is delayed by an unsolicited lesson.
- A dense narrated tour competes with the Colosseum or the couple.
- “Interested in Greek mythology” becomes a stable identity boundary.
- A shared visit becomes one merged memory.
- The artifact claims the movie told Aeneas's story or treats myth as settled
  historical fact.
- Every interesting connection becomes a keep/share prompt.

## 7. Scenario Packet S03 — Brooklyn dinner through ordinary language

### 7.1 Human situation

The host says in private Chat: “Dinner at mine Friday. Invite Lena and Reza.”
Vesper resolves the home Place and people, asks only for materially missing
time, previews the boundary once, and creates the bounded Occasion. Invitees
should understand why they were invited and contribute through normal
conversation rather than profiles, forms, or project-management mechanics.

Lena says, “I'll bring tiramisu.” Reza says, “I can get there after nine.” A
train delay may later affect the shared evening. During dinner people may add a
photo, dish, observation, or nothing at all. Afterward the Occasion may have an
afterglow without becoming a recap assignment or group scrapbook.

### 7.2 Immediate jobs

- Host: create a coherent dinner and reduce invisible coordination work.
- Invitee: understand the invitation, receive personal orientation, and join,
  decline, or contribute with minimal friction.
- Group: maintain one truthful shared consequence without exposing private
  reasons or forcing universal participation.
- Everyone: let the interface recede when people can handle the gathering.

### 7.3 Baseline experience trace

```text
host speaks ordinary intent in Chat
  -> Vesper resolves Place, people, time, audience, and authority
  -> one creation/invitation preview crosses the social boundary
  -> each recipient enters through a personal loop and may decline silently
  -> clear in-room utterances become bounded contributions with readback + Undo
  -> ambiguous chatter remains chatter
  -> material disruption produces one prepared repair or silence
  -> Occasion shifts from before to live to afterglow
  -> personal, relational, shared, or no continuity remain separate choices
```

### 7.4 Four-root responsibility

| Root | Required expression |
|---|---|
| Home | Current invitation or Occasion truth, one material change, or “nothing needs a decision” |
| Chat | Natural creation, invitation, contribution, affected-principal decision, receipt, and human conversation |
| Places | Private home arrival, route, nearby operational need, and relationship-to-Place without publishing the home |
| Life | Mine/Together episode, bounded shared facts, plural private outcomes, relationship continuity, and afterglow |

### 7.5 Hard questions for parallel exploration

- **Adaptive composition:** What temporary shared instrument makes the dinner
  legible without resembling an event dashboard? How should people,
  contributions, arrival, Place, and phase compose across roots?
- **Judgment and continuity:** Which chat utterances should become state? When
  should Vesper stay silent during coordination? What evidence makes a third
  dinner a useful ritual rather than an inferred recurring identity?
- **Agency and multiplayer:** Who may create, invite, contribute, decide,
  change, undo, leave, or retain? How does each invitee receive personal value
  before group labor? What is the exact boundary from personal loops to the
  Occasion and back?
- **Visual language:** How can a dinner feel social, alive, ordinary, and
  co-present without avatar piles, stationery mannerism, event branding, or
  administrative chrome? How does Vesper recede during the gathering?

### 7.6 Required branches

- missing time or ambiguous person resolution;
- invitee declines, ignores, tentatively joins, or leaves;
- one invitee has a private constraint;
- an utterance looks like a contribution but is joking or ambiguous;
- contribution changes or is withdrawn;
- train delay is already being handled by people;
- repair affects only one person versus the entire dinner;
- a photo is posted to the room but private interpretation is not;
- no participant wants reflection or continuity afterward;
- the group later chooses to repeat the dinner.

### 7.7 Negative oracles

- Occasion-specific profiles or preference questionnaires.
- Every message becomes extracted structured state.
- The host or loudest participant silently receives greater welfare weight.
- Vesper speaks because it can, not because coordination needs it.
- Private constraints appear in the group explanation.
- Attendance creates friendship, recurring-circle, or future reachability by
  default.
- Afterglow becomes a collage workflow, survey, or single group meaning.

## 8. Scenario Packet S04 — Return to New York: Status, Life, and transfer

### 8.1 Human situation

The traveler returns to New York after Nice, Sorrento, the Amalfi Coast, and
Rome. The trip should be allowed to settle without sorting, posting, or
journaling. A friend, Lena, asks, “How was Italy?” The traveler also says to
Vesper, “Put out that I'm back from Nice and Sorrento.” A Sorrento pasta photo
and the observation that pasta tasted different can support several distinct
consequences: a named reply, a friends-visible Status, private continuity, a
local cooking opening, an Occasion afterglow, or nothing.

These consequences may reuse the same source but must not collapse custody,
audience, authorship, inference, or ownership.

### 8.2 Immediate jobs

- Reply to one person authentically without composing a generic trip summary.
- Optionally feature one current human-authored expression for a bounded
  audience and time.
- Let the Europe episode remain coherent without merging the partner's meaning.
- Carry forward practical capability only when it changes a real later moment.
- Avoid turning return into administrative or reflective homework.

### 8.3 Baseline experience trace

```text
Home acknowledges return and remains mostly quiet
  -> because Lena already asked, one directed opening may be prepared
  -> Chat distinguishes the named reply from the friends-visible Status
  -> one publication preview resolves exact words, media, audience, and expiry
  -> Life renders the Europe episode, Place relationships, sources, and private threads
  -> Places may later connect the Sorrento observation to a real Brooklyn cooking moment
  -> recognition alone is not continuity proof; the later judgment or capability must change
```

### 8.4 Four-root responsibility

| Root | Required expression |
|---|---|
| Home | Current life first, with a bounded portfolio of earned human, practical, interpretive, reconstructive, and future openings—not a post-trip task list |
| Chat | Natural-language preparation of named reply or Status, exact audience boundary, and receipt |
| Places | Sorrento relationship and an earned local continuation based on practical relevance, not mimicry |

- pre-pivot brand lock;
- later design doctrine;

- accepted current-revision device evidence;
- historical or compatibility evidence;

## 14. Synthesis session after the four reports

The next session should not average the reports or select one “winning agent.”
It should produce:

1. one integrated experience trace for each scenario;
2. one four-root composition for each scenario;
3. one shared list of product primitives and negative oracles;
4. a conflict ledger showing unresolved composition, visual-language,
   judgment, social, and authority seams;
5. an architecture-demand map labeled existing, adaptable, missing, or
   intentionally deferred;
6. a prototype portfolio and behavioral research plan;
7. a founder decision docket separating stable, provisional, and unproven
   choices.

Only after that synthesis should the team decide whether to update product
canon, expand the adaptive-artifact contract, redesign root surfaces, or begin
a production implementation program.
