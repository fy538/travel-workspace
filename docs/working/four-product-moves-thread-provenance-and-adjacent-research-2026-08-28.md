# Four Product Moves — Thread Provenance and Adjacent Research

## Purpose and method

This memo reconstructs the Strategy task from August 21–28, including turns
that had left the active context window, and compares the resulting synthesis
with current canonical and working documents across the workspace, backend,
and mobile repositories. It records why the four moves emerged and what they
must not erase. It is provenance and research, not a second product authority;
the accepted decision and canonical product documents win.

The task record is Codex task `01a030af-13a8-74e1-81be-7d526bec3045`. The
review used the task's paginated turn history rather than relying on the
compacted conversation summary.

## 1. What the thread actually changed

The four moves did not appear as an abstract naming exercise. Each one resolves
a distinct failure discovered through the founder's trip stories, product
compositions, and repository audits.

### August 21–23: from feature breadth to a human experience

The early discussion established that Vesper should make the world more
legible while making the person more present and capable. Rome's heat, Yungang,
the Sorrento menu, a work lounge, and the Odyssey–Aeneas connection showed that
practical and cultural value cannot be separate modes. Known interests should
open adjacent curiosity rather than constrain it. A correct interaction can
end after relief or remain silent.

This work supplied the foundations for **Make sense**, **Open possibility**,
and **Help it work**, but the documents still described them through several
overlapping promises and object names.

### August 24–25: responsibility and social circulation

The pitch work identified experiential responsibility: maps, marketplaces,
assistants, and chats own fragments, while Vesper is organized around whether
the experience works for the people living it. Multiplayer then expanded from
group coordination into separately governed personal loops, bounded shared
worlds, attributed perspective, plural Outcomes, and clean endings.

The status and artifact work simplified social behavior further. A Place-linked
artifact can remain private, be addressed to a friend, contribute to an
Occasion, or become a featured share. Social value comes from lived experience
circulating with human authorship and bounded audiences—not from adding a feed
or an Occasion-specific identity profile.

This is why social cannot remain the old fourth promise, `Experience it
together`. It changes all four moves.

### August 26: the consequence boundary

The no-homework work clarified the division of labor after understanding:

```text
Vesper privately generates and prepares
  -> stop / offer / apply under authority / ask once
  -> owner readback and receipt
  -> recede
```

The user supplies irreducible judgment, consent, authorship, correction, and
protected decisions. Vesper should absorb search, reconstruction, comparison,
coordination, preparation, and repair. This became the operational content of
**Help it work**.

### August 27: four roots, one intelligence

Home / Chat / Places / Life became stable postures rather than feature
containers:

- Home attends and offers entry.
- Chat understands and shapes through conversation.
- Places orients and situates in the physical world.
- Life makes accumulated personal and shared continuity inspectable.

The complete Rome, dinner, return, and ordinary-day compositions demonstrated
that every root can contain several product behaviors. The strongest law was
not one grammar per tab; it was one dominant job per unit and one attention
hierarchy per screen.

### August 27–28: contribution, not reflection labor

The Home screenshots exposed a decisive product failure: counts and trip
summaries were records, while “What do you not want to lose?” asked the user to
generate the value. The founder's correction raised the standard again: even a
personalized connection fails if it merely repeats what the person already
noticed.

The Granola analogy changed the division of labor. The person lives and
directs sparse attention; Vesper reconstructs, researches, compares, explains,
and prepares something more useful. The Editorial Canon expressed this as
restructure, explain, differentiate, and transfer. Home became the output of
that work, not another intake surface.

The post-return Home work added the final correction: **Carry forward** is not
retrospection. Europe should become one input into a life already moving
forward in New York. Past evidence earns attention only when it changes a
present understanding, weekend, conversation, capability, relationship, or
future horizon.

## 2. Why the previous four promises no longer suffice

| Previous promise | What it got right | Why the new model is sharper |
|---|---|---|
| **See more** | Perception and interpretation matter | It conflates understanding what is already present with making a new possibility available. |
| **Live it well** | Practical, situated responsibility matters | “Help it work” better covers preparation, group authority, execution, adaptation, repair, and withdrawal without claiming Vesper owns living. |
| **Carry it forward** | Continuity must improve a later occasion | Retained, but explicitly prospective and selective rather than archival. |
| **Experience it together** | Multiplayer is structural | Social participation affects every move and should not be isolated as one phase or feature pillar. |

The resulting compression is:

```text
MAKE SENSE
What is going on, how does it fit, and what did Vesper add?

OPEN POSSIBILITY
What grounded way of noticing, entering, doing, making, continuing, or sharing
is newly available?

HELP IT WORK
What practical, social, or operational burden should Vesper absorb so the
chosen experience can remain coherent in reality?

CARRY FORWARD
What authorized consequence can make a later situation materially different?
```

## 3. Research fit

The research does not prove demand for Vesper or validate this exact product
grammar. It does support the distinctions and guardrails.

### Make sense must produce representational gain

Distributed-cognition research argues that useful cognition spans people,
artifacts, and whole environments rather than living entirely inside one user
or one computer. That fits Vesper's role as a coordinator of Sources, Places,
people, current conditions, and external systems rather than a chatbot that
merely produces prose. [Hollan, Hutchins, and Kirsh](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/8sb9s5rm)

Analogy research is even more directly applicable. Structure mapping aligns
relational systems and can project candidate inferences into the less complete
case. Surface overlap alone is not enough. Therefore “you watched a film about
Troy and then saw Aeneas in Rome” is retrieval; explaining why Rome needed both
Romulus and Aeneas can add structure and inference. [Gentner](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1551-6709.2010.01114.x)

**Product consequence:** Make sense requires a delta in representation,
explanation, distinction, or judgment. Personalization without new epistemic
work does not qualify.

### Open possibility is relational, not inventory

Ecological accounts of affordance treat possibilities for action as arising
from the relationship between an environment's conditions and an agent's
abilities or constraints. The same stair, route, evening, or cultural object
does not afford the same thing to every person in every condition. [Greeno](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8022965/)

**Product consequence:** an opening is not “something nearby” or “content the
person may like.” It must state or embody why this possibility is available to
these people in this Place and Moment. It may expand perception without
requiring action.

### Help it work should be mixed-initiative and uncertainty-aware

Horvitz's mixed-initiative principles remain unusually current: automated help
should add genuine value, account for uncertainty in goals and attention,
consider the cost and timing of intervention, permit efficient invocation and
termination, minimize the cost of poor guesses, and do less when uncertainty is
high. [Horvitz](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/chi99horvitz.pdf)

Microsoft's later human–AI guidelines reinforce contextual timing and
relevance, scoping service when uncertain, easy correction, and support over
time. [Amershi and colleagues](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-design/)

**Product consequence:** Help it work is not maximum automation. It is the
smallest useful interleaving of system preparation and human authority, with
clear stopping, correction, and recovery.

### Carry forward should be prospective, not commemorative by default

Autobiographical-memory research distinguishes directive, social, and self
functions. Vesper has a legitimate role in helping prior experience guide
action and support relationships, but should be conservative about claiming
what an experience means for someone's identity. [Bluck, Alea, Habermas, and Rubin](https://dukespace.lib.duke.edu/bitstreams/78d0f43e-c4eb-4aff-8b8c-f6adceb1a4d1/download)

Research on constructive episodic simulation links flexible use of past detail
to imagining future events, planning, problem solving, and reappraisal. The
same flexibility can also contribute to memory distortion, strengthening the
need for provenance and visible separation between record, reconstruction,
outside knowledge, and authored meaning. [Jing, Mador, and Schacter](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/xge-xge0000142.pdf)

**Product consequence:** continuity earns its cost when it changes a later
experience. A recap or archive may still be useful as a record, but it is not
evidence of Carry forward until it produces prospective value.

### Multiplayer amplifies experience without guaranteeing harmony

Experiments found that merely sharing attention can intensify both pleasant
and unpleasant experiences. Social participation therefore matters even
without explicit messaging, but “shared” does not mean positive, consensual,
or experienced identically. [Boothby, Clark, and Bargh](https://acmelab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2014_shared_experiences_are_amplified.pdf)

**Product consequence:** Vesper can create social value by making shared
attention and attributed perspective easier, but must preserve plural Outcomes,
exit, private context, and the possibility that one Occasion worked
differently for different people.

## 4. The layered model

```text
whatever already has attention
        ↓
four product moves
  make sense / open possibility / help it work / carry forward
        ↓
formal interaction grammar
  initiator + person + verb + object + job + context
        ↓
treatment and human boundary
  silence / answer / opening / proposal / action / receipt
        ↓
canonical owner
  Person / Place / Source / Occasion / Plan / Commitment / Moment / Outcome
        ↓
root-native projection
  Home / Chat / Places / Life / focused owner / push
```

This is a layered explanation, not a mandatory sequence. The product moves
classify value; the interaction grammar resolves the present event; the
canonical owner holds durable truth; the root composes an appropriate view.

## 5. Root and multiplayer crosswalk

| Root | Make sense | Open possibility | Help it work | Carry forward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Strong: Vesper's current read and finished contributions | Core: a diverse bounded field beyond obligation | Core when something is active or ready | Strong: prior evidence may alter the present composition |
| **Chat** | Core: question, interpretation, comparison, correction | Strong: alternatives, imagination, addressed openings | Core: shape, prepare, decide, authorize, repair | Supporting: keep, seed, or hand off into an owner |
| **Places** | Core: identity, conditions, mechanisms, perspectives | Core: spatial and experiential affordances | Strong: route, access, timing, feasibility, navigation | Strong: Place relationship, return, and transferred capability |
| **Life** | Strong: reconstruct and relate accumulated evidence | Strong: prior threads become future horizons | Supporting: prepare a return or relational continuation | Core: provenance, plural Outcomes, correction, and earned recurrence |

Multiplayer crosses the same matrix:

- **Make sense:** compose attributed perspectives and group-safe context.
- **Open possibility:** address a friend, invite, contribute, or reveal a
  shared possibility without manufacturing response debt.
- **Help it work:** govern decisions, commitments, coordination, adaptation,
  facilitation, and repair.
- **Carry forward:** preserve shared occurrence, plural personal Outcomes, and
  separately authorized relational continuity.

## 6. Tests for future work

For any unit, experience, or proposed feature:

1. Which one move is dominant here?
2. What did Vesper add beyond retrieval, formatting, or paraphrase?
3. If it opens a possibility, why is that possibility available now?
4. If it asks for input, is the answer irreducible human judgment, authorship,
   consent, correction, or authority?
5. If it helps something work, what burden did it absorb and where does truth
   land?
6. If it carries something forward, what later difference justifies retention?
7. How does another person's participation change the move without merging
   privacy, agency, or meaning?
8. Which root best composes this now, and which durable owner survives after
   the temporary treatment disappears?
9. Can the interaction end here with complete value or silence?

The strongest concise law remains:

> **One dominant job per unit. Several product moves per root. One coherent
> attention hierarchy per screen. One durable owner per consequence.**
