# Europe Journey Evidence Manifest and Life Opening Comparison

> Status: active evidence and editorial adjudication
>
> This document is not a final screen. It establishes what the real founder
> corpus can support, what remains a controlled fixture, and which opening most
> clearly demonstrates Life's accumulated-evidence dividend.

## 0. Decision

Use a **cross-artifact menu synthesis** as the first canonical post-return Life
opening. Keep the Aeneas/Iulus/Augustus contribution nested inside Rome as a
second strong Return.

Why:

- it requires the person's accumulated corpus rather than one note plus public
  knowledge;
- it adds something the person did not already state;
- it demonstrates that menus can become evidence, not disposable translation
  requests;
- it opens back into places, ingredients, dishes, producers, and future New
  York possibilities without asking for reflection; and
- it is legible as a concrete product operation: **Vesper compared artifacts
  from different moments and found a supported pattern.**

The prototype-only `ferry booked but not taken` must not be presented as the
founder's history. The real corpus contains a Sorrento-to-Capri ferry ticket;
retrieval should use that ticket unless separate non-occurrence evidence is
later supplied.

## 1. Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning | Admissible product language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RAW_INSPECTED` | Original image or document was visually inspected during this pass | Exact visible fields, with private identifiers redacted |
| `ATTACHMENT_EXTRACTED` | A source attachment exists and its contents were transcribed in the originating conversation, but every field was not independently re-read here | Bounded claims attributed to the source; no stronger occurrence claim |
| `USER_AUTHORED` | The founder directly described an event, attention, question, or outcome | `You noted`, `you asked`, or event language where the statement itself supports occurrence |
| `EXTERNAL_GROUNDED` | A public source supports Vesper's added explanation | Sourced explanation with a claim-local limit |
| `SYNTHETIC` | Deliberately invented to test behavior | Prototype only; never presented as founder history |
| `UNRESOLVED` | Available evidence does not establish the relation | Explicit uncertainty or omission |

An artifact supports its own contents. It does not automatically prove that a
trip segment occurred, a dish was ordered, a hotel was occupied, or an event
was meaningful.

## 4. Opening A — mythology-led

```text
WORTH RETURNING TO

Aeneas gave Augustus a family tree inside Rome's origin story.

The useful link is Iulus: Virgil connects Aeneas's son to the Julian line,
then places Augustus among his descendants.

Aeneas → Ascanius / Iulus → Julian line → Caesar and Augustus

Your Colosseum note · Virgil, Aeneid 1 and 6 · Vesper connected
```

### What is genuinely new

The Iulus-to-Julian mechanism, not the film-to-Colosseum connection.

### Strength

Substantive, memorable, sourced, and bounded.

### Limitation

It could largely be produced from one note and public knowledge. It proves
editorial intelligence better than it proves why a person should entrust a
life corpus to Vesper.

## 5. Opening B — corpus-led

Raw-audited treatment:

```text
ACROSS TWO DINNERS

The menus made “local” visible at three scales.

GARDEN
Villa Zagara lemon · flowers and vegetables around the table

PENINSULA
Sorrento walnut · Monti Lattari beef · Provolone del Monaco

COAST
Amalfi Sfusato lemon · Re Fiascone, recovered in Tramonti

Six menu photos · two venues · Vesper compared and researched
```

Claim-local limit:

> **Menus and producers make these origin claims. They do not prove what you
> ordered or where every ingredient in these servings was grown.**

Optional apertures:

- `See the six menu photos`
- `How Re Fiascone came back`
- `Find one of these in New York` — a handoff to current Places, never a
  pre-created task or Plan

### What is genuinely new

The system compared separate menu artifacts and distinguished three meanings
of `local`: immediate garden, protected or traditional peninsula products, and
coast-wide agricultural recovery. The founder noticed pasta texture; this
three-scale structure was not supplied by the founder.

### Strength

This is an accumulated-evidence dividend. A menu first given to Vesper for
translation later becomes part of a useful comparison without requiring the
person to organize or revisit it.

### Limitation

The raw-source audit and external grounding are complete. Production must
still persist the original files, field-level spans, venue-resolution evidence,
and claim-local citations rather than relying on temporary chat previews.

## 6. Comparative judgment

| Test | Aeneas opening | Menu opening |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Adds beyond what the founder noticed | Strong | Strong |
| Requires accumulated personal corpus | Weak | Very strong |
| Concrete at a glance | Strong | Strong after concise treatment |
| Opens practical future value | Medium | Strong |
| Demonstrates editorial intelligence | Very strong | Strong |
| Demonstrates Life specifically | Medium | Very strong |
| Risk | Feels like a generated article | Overclaims provenance or becomes a list |

**Selection:** menu opening first; Aeneas nested in Rome.

The selection is not “food is more important than mythology.” It is that the
first Life opening should teach the product in use: things the person casually
gave Vesper can compound into a later, source-visible contribution. Rome can
then demonstrate a different medium and operation without crowding the door.

## 8. Product implications

The opening requires capabilities beyond recap generation:

1. artifact transcription with field-level provenance;
2. cross-artifact comparison that preserves each Source's wording;
3. entity resolution for place-named ingredients without silently asserting
   provenance;
4. claim construction with an attached limit;
5. source-local correction and suppression;
6. medium selection: compact comparison first, optional article second; and
7. cross-root projection that can ask Places for a current New York opening
   without putting current recommendations inside Life.

This is the Life spine in miniature:

> **Recover what happened. Derive something new across it. Let the result open
> the present without turning history into homework.**
