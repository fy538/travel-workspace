## 2. Product context to preserve

Vesper's August pivot broadened the product beyond travel planning. People give
it incomplete traces of their lives: questions, tickets, photographs, places,
observations, intentions, and contributions from others. Vesper should turn
those into understanding, practical help, possibilities, and useful continuity.

The four product moves are:

**Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.**

They are not four compulsory feed sections, one move per tab, or a funnel every
experience must complete.

| Root | Dominant responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Return worthwhile current value across my life, people, and world |
| Chat | Let me give, ask, explore, shape, and cause things with little friction |
| Places | Let me understand and explore the world spatially, including human perspectives |
| Life | Organize and recover what is held across time, places, people, and threads |

Home is an output surface, not a second intake form. It can expose contextual
actions without routing every experience into Chat. Life remains the durable
record; Home is not a duplicate archive. Places owns spatial exploration;
Home can still deliver a useful map or timely place-based result directly.

The founder's Europe journey is important provenance, not the permanent topic
of the product. Coming home means projecting life forward in New York, not
being kept inside a trip retrospective. Solo value must work without friends;
social value must extend beyond organizing a group trip.

available possibility feel like an assigned task. Reserve directive urgency
for situations that warrant it.

## 5. Composition principles for this pass

### Separate priority from inventory

One item may dominate; few may ask for decisions; several may be enjoyable or
useful without action. No universal item count or required section quota is
being introduced. Build the generous version first, then remove redundancy,
weak value, and excessive reading burden.

Do not make urgent, ordinary, and sparse Home equally long. A genuine emergency
can reduce foreground competition. It does not establish that every ordinary
visit should end after one item. Keep optional depth reachable and preserve
context across changing priority.

### Deliver range, not a miscellaneous capability showroom

Candidate value families include:

- **The coming days:** grounded alternatives for an evening, weekend, visit,
  or ordinary afternoon, including staying home and low-effort options.
- **Practical competence:** useful timing, a changed condition, a workable
  route, or a prepared adjustment tied to something the person actually cares about.
- **Understanding now:** a compact explanation, comparison, annotated image,
  or other substantive result worth consuming without going anywhere.
- **Something to try:** a usable technique, small experiment, or way to notice
  something in a familiar environment, not an assigned learning exercise.
- **Accumulated material made useful:** reconstruction, an actual photo
  sequence, or coherent material recovered from scattered contributions.
- **Human perspective and participation:** authored sharing, shared attention,
  useful local judgment, and shared arrangements.
- **A wider world:** relevant local life and adjacent subjects, without
  pretending every offering was inferred from a personal trace.

These are candidate sources of value, not seven required headings. Every unit
needs a reason to be here; the whole scroll needs contrast and coherence.
Do not confine someone permanently to pasta, waterfronts, mythology, or whatever
single artifact first entered their account.

### Substance need not mean long text

Show an actual useful finding, human contribution, comparison, or practical
answer on the surface. Deeper articles and maps can have destinations, but
their previews must provide a real payoff rather than only a tantalizing title.
Do not force the full article inline to satisfy “complete value.”

Use the existing visual language and whichever medium explains the result
best. This pass is not a new visual-language laboratory. Put design rationale,
source-production notes, and audit language outside the consumer phone frames.

### 5.1 Composition principles — hierarchy, containment, and text reduction

**Editorial describes selection and composition, not the absence of cards.**
Use cards selectively to make coherent objects and experiences recognizable.
Do not put every sentence into an identical rounded rectangle; equally, do not
reserve visual containment for operational urgency.

| Material | Candidate expression |
| --- | --- |
| Prepared afternoon or shared arrangement | A contained card with a readable shape and integrated people/timing |
| Friend's photograph and words | A coherent media-led unit, with authorship visibly belonging to that unit |
| Upcoming commitments | Compact, scannable rows, not paragraphs |
| Useful comparison | An instrument or comparison panel; text interprets rather than repeats it |
| Substantial reading | A satisfying editorial preview with owned depth behind it |
| Simple service update | One concise inline fact or compact row |
| Onboarding sample | The sample result itself, clearly labeled, with an optional way to try it |

These are design candidates, not seven required component implementations.
Existing components should be adapted before adding new ones. A static card
can deliver value without suggesting it is tappable; interactive cards need
clear, consistent destination behavior. Avoid ambiguous nested actions.

Give typography distinct jobs:

1. **Section heading:** occasional, clearly differentiated, plain-language
   navigation. Test a larger non-monospace treatment within the existing system.
2. **Content title:** the principal entry into the thing itself, not a third
   label following two near-identical headers.
3. **Metadata:** restrained use of monospace for time, distance, date, or source.
4. **Supporting text:** enough to deliver the useful distinction; not an essay
   justifying why the unit qualifies for Home.

Remove a section label when it adds no navigation value. A single technique does
not automatically need both `SOMETHING TO TRY` and `CARRIED FORWARD`. Do not fix
the conflict by shrinking type, weakening contrast, or adding more whitespace
between otherwise indistinguishable units. Verify at normal phone width and
larger text rather than only viewing a scaled-down full-scroll canvas.

Move expandable evidence detail and design rationale out of the resting surface.
Keep visible what the person needs to interpret or act safely: human authorship,
the fact that a sample is a sample, a material uncertainty, and an audience or
consequence boundary when relevant. Progressive disclosure must not hide those
essentials. The goal is less repetition, not less understandable authority.

For H1 and H2, retain multiple distinct payoffs while reducing the reading needed
to recognize them. For H3, make a ticket-shaped input and the resulting evening
visually understandable before the explanatory copy. Keep its optional actions
and sample label; replace the paragraph about the product with the product result.

The typography principle is supported by [Apple's Typography guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography):
type should communicate hierarchy and remain legible as it scales. This does
not validate any specific Vesper font size, card style, or composition.

### 5.2 Production principles — not a bespoke magazine

**A personalized Home does not require newly written personal prose for every
unit.** Selection, ranking, timing, composition, and an authorized human source
can make existing material personally valuable. Generation is one production
method, not the definition of the product.

Classify every visible unit outside the phone:

| Production class | Appropriate approach | Example from the boards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human-authored material | Retrieve and render the authorized original; do not rewrite by default | Maya's photograph or Theo's note |
| Existing structured state | Compose from its current owner; use direct data presentation where sufficient | Ticket, arrangement, commitment, week row |
| Current world information | Retrieve from a suitable source and reuse within its freshness and licensing limits | Service notice, event, hours |
| Reusable researched content | Reuse a sourced explanation or content object when its claims and rights permit | A general cooking technique or explanatory mechanism |
| Personal relevance | Select, rank, connect, or lightly adapt; use generation only where it adds value | A relevant technique shown because of the person's actual observation |
| Bespoke synthesis | Targeted generation from authorized evidence when a new result earns the work | A substantive cross-source connection not already available |
| Optional depth | Reuse existing depth first; otherwise generate selectively or on explicit request | An extended reading or audio treatment |

No percentages, daily quotas, or model choices are prescribed here. These are
proposed production responsibilities for engineering review, not a claim that
the current system already implements all of them. Retrieval, media delivery,
third-party data, and ranking can also cost money; “not generated” does not mean
free. Do not replace bespoke prose with a large unbudgeted research pipeline.

Design toward these operating behaviors:

- Opening or scrolling Home primarily retrieves and composes available value;
  it does not launch a new research-and-writing job for every visible slot.
- Meaningful source changes, stale facts, new relevant contributions, and
  deliberate exploration may justify new work. Repeated visits alone should
  not automatically regenerate valid results.
- Keep useful generated results and reuse them while valid. Separate live
  facts from durable explanation so an hours or route update does not require
  rewriting an entire article.
- Reuse public/common content only where authorized. Personal evidence and
  friend-derived results stay within their audience and purpose boundaries;
  revocation or source correction must invalidate affected derivatives.
- If deeper generation is unavailable, delayed, or over budget, the surface
  should remain useful with existing material. Do not fill it with fabricated
  results, permanent loading promises, or a new assignment for the user.
- Fixed, clearly labeled onboarding demonstrations can be prepared and reused;
  their value does not depend on generating them anew for each account.

A limited code spot-check found per-call output, timeout, and retry limits in
the [source-contribution producer](../../travel-agent/backend/root_projection/v2/source_contribution_producer.py).
Those limits and its system-prompt caching do not establish an account-level
budget or sustainable Home economics. [Prompt caching](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)
reuses input processing; it is not the same as retaining and serving a finished
content result without another generation call.

For engineering handback, identify the likely generation trigger, reuse scope,
freshness/invalidation condition, and fallback for each production class. Flag
unknowns rather than inventing unit costs. Subsequent engineering evaluation
needs actual generation frequency, token/tool usage, reuse rate, and cost per
active user, not an affordability claim inferred from static boards.

The latest review read H0's revised decision/production ledger and inspected

or participant validation and not a runtime cost audit.

#### A. H1 and H5 — alternatives are not an itinerary

The numbered `Today` and `This afternoon` cards combine mutually alternative
ways to spend time: the bakery/water, a different walk, or staying in. Numbering
them 1–3 makes them resemble successive steps or an assigned plan.

Preserve the compact contained choice set, but make its optionality legible
without a disclaimer. Use differentiated unnumbered options, grouping, or another
light treatment within the existing system. Reserve sequencing cues for a
genuine sequence within one option or an arrangement the person has adopted.
Opening an option must not silently adopt or schedule it. Do not compensate by
adding mandatory selection controls to a previously consumable card.

**Review check:** can someone tell whether these are three things to do or three
different ways the day might go, before reading the explanatory text?

#### B. H2 — preserve the person, remove the echoes

Dana's bookshop appears in her In motion row, the Sunday recommendation, her
authored share, and the closing weekly summary. These serve different purposes,
but their cumulative repetition adds reading without equivalent new value.

Keep her actual contribution and its useful place/timing context as the primary
expression. Shorten or remove the paraphrases elsewhere. A compact arrangement
reference can coexist with the share when it conveys a genuinely distinct fact;
this is not a ban on all cross-section references. The coda should not recite
every item the person just saw or promote optional possibilities into commitments.

The conditional From your people treatment is now a plausible composition.
Refine it rather than commissioning another conceptual social redesign. Its
broader budget and intentional-browsing decisions remain proposed until resolved.

**Review check:** does each repeated reference add different useful information,
or is it merely another summary of the same contribution?

#### C. H3 — reveal the product before a long city briefing

Revision 2 still leads with weather, a microclimate explanation, transit service,
and registration information before the sample. This risks making the first
impression a city briefing rather than Vesper's distinctive value exchange.

Bring the ticket-to-evening demonstration earlier, alongside one worthwhile
city offering. Keep the remaining city material lower where useful; do not
replace output with a promotional hero or mandatory intake prompt. Preserve the
clear Sample label and optional inspection/try actions.

The cold-start account only supplied a city. Replace `your walk` with truthful
nonpersonal language such as `waterfront streets`; do not manufacture a known
routine. Likewise, a sample must not imply knowledge of a user's workplace,
starting point, or attendance. Personal language should follow actual context.

**Review check:** can a new user encounter what one contribution becomes before
reading several unrelated notices, while receiving useful value without input?

#### D. H3 and H5 — replace analytical framing with the useful result

Some remaining copy sounds like our internal reasoning rather than a human
benefit. Rework these examples without weakening material uncertainty:

- `The way back is the constraint`: explain the practical return issue directly,
  using only the source-supported route or timing consequence.
- `A dish for three is the honest test`: show a useful cooking possibility,
  without assigning an experiment or making a dinner into validation work.
- `The only leg documented from departure to arrival`: foreground the actual
  journey or a worthwhile reconstruction, not the completeness of ingestion.

For the journey, tickets and photographs may support a useful account with
gaps. Do not invent missing events or treat a departure ticket as proof of
boarding. Evidence quality belongs in supporting detail unless it is the
reason the person asked. The correction is about the value proposition, not
permission to obscure uncertainty.

**Review check:** is the unit enjoyable or useful as an experience, rather than
a report about Vesper's reasoning, data coverage, or quality-control process?

#### E. H0 — distinguish reuse from prediction, inference, and measured cost

The production ledger is improved, but qualify these remaining shortcuts:

| Ledger shortcut | Required qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Reusable cold mechanism plus personal numbers | A general explanation of exposure does not establish an exact four-degree difference on a particular route. Name the data/model that would support the prediction; otherwise use an appropriately bounded explanation. |
| Photograph finding classified as personal relevance | Mechanism matching and personal callouts may require vision, retrieval, inference, and uncertainty handling. Reusable prose does not eliminate the initial processing work. |
| Cost unknown marked `None new` | If intended, say `no new generation expected`; identify retrieval, permission checks, media, refresh, and provider costs as applicable. Do not imply zero total cost. |
| Prepared sample marked no cost | Separate one-time preparation from delivery and maintenance; reuse is a cost strategy, not evidence of free operation. |

Keep **design intent**, **verified implementation**, and **measured economics**
separate in the handback. Missing measurements should remain unknown. Do not
commission infrastructure, a mechanism library, or new data providers merely
to make the fixture ledger appear complete.

These qualifications do not establish that the fuller Home is unaffordable.
They prevent a static classification from becoming an unsupported affordability
claim. The desired default remains retrieval and composition of available
value, with new inference or generation reserved for justified work.

## 6. Multiplayer: make the present social world visible

Home is a good place to encounter multiplayer. It must not be the only route
to shared material when ranking does not select it.

Distinguish these experiences rather than calling them all activity:

| Experience | Home value | Continuation / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authored share | A friend's actual image, words, experience, or featured Status | Source object or person; optional response |
| Directed handoff | Something deliberately given to this person, useful before replying | Addressed material and the relevant Place or object |
| Situated judgment | Theo's advice changes what works about the market visit | Places for spatial detail |
| Shared understanding | Authorized perspectives produce a genuinely substantive comparison | Attributed artifact or reading, not a fabricated group opinion |
| Shared arrangement | What is taking shape, a useful contribution, or a viewer-relevant change | Existing Plan/Occasion arrangement |
| Relationship continuity | Something held together becomes enjoyable or relevant again | Life People / shared record |

**People are not merely data sources for the AI.** Their authored contribution
can itself be interesting, warm, funny, or illuminating. It need not improve a
route or be rewritten into an AI insight to earn space. Conversely, adding an
avatar to an otherwise identical recommendation is not sufficient social value.

Pressure-test the older “one relational opening” rule. It may govern dominance
or interruption; do not assume it limits all social material throughout a long
Home. Report any tension with current canon rather than declaring a new rule.

Compare two Home treatments using the same social material:

1. **Distributed:** social content integrated into the practical, editorial,
   and prospective units it changes, with a clear way to find more.
2. **Distributed plus a conditional grouping:** an identifiable “From your
   people” region when enough meaningful material exists, while important
   shared consequences can still appear elsewhere on Home.

The grouping must disappear naturally when empty. It must not duplicate the
same note already presented in full above. Do not artificially weaken the
distributed comparison to make the grouped version win.

Show where a person goes when asking, “What else have my people shared?” Reuse
existing person/shared-record and Places destinations where suitable. If a
focused present-tense destination is needed, sketch the minimum boundary and
entry/return behavior as an open cross-lane decision. Do not create a fifth tab
or redesign Life People as part of this assignment.

Essential safeguards should feel natural, not dominate the design:

- Authored sharing is not passive activity telemetry. Saves, searches, views,
  attendance, and location do not automatically become feed content.
- Explicitly shared travel Status is not live location or confirmed availability.
- An optional contribution is not a pending decision. A comment is not an edit;
  an edit is not authority to change someone else's commitment.
- No forced reply, relationship score, popularity ranking, synthetic intimacy,
  or public nonresponse signal.
- Preserve audience and authorship. Withdrawal must not leave a paraphrase of
  material the viewer can no longer access.

## 7. Value-first onboarding and capability discovery

Not knowing someone personally does not mean knowing nothing useful about the
world. Separate three sources explicitly:

1. **Nonpersonal world value:** useful material based on a city the person
   selected or another legitimate current context; no invented personalization.
2. **Clearly labeled demonstration:** sample input and sample result that the
   person can inspect without uploading their own data.
3. **Personal return:** an actual result from material deliberately supplied,
   with an understandable connection between input and value.

Do not assume location permission or infer attendance/enjoyment from a ticket.
If current world data is unavailable, label a demonstration; do not fake a live
result. The first screen should not say that absence of personal history makes
an otherwise empty Home the only honest design.

Demonstrate the exchange through a few concrete outcomes, for example:

- ticket → an understandable evening and useful practical context;
- photograph or observation → a grounded explanation beyond a caption;
- friend's note → a useful perspective or shared possibility;
- scattered material → a coherent, recoverable Life record.

These examples describe design targets, not shipped capability claims. In the
fixture ledger, identify what exists, what needs engineering verification, and
what remains proposed. Do not claim autonomous checking or guaranteed updates
because a prototype can display those words.

The invitation should follow visible value: **“Try this with yours,”** not a
blank request to document the person's life. Keep experimentation optional.
No onboarding checklist, completion meter, mandatory friend invite, account
connection campaign, or persistent tutorial carousel.

Once the person contributes, show a meaningful change in Home and an appropriate
destination in Life. Do not require another input before returning value. Later
capability discovery should appear when relevant and retire when no longer
helpful; the user should not have to guess the entire product from one example.

Research anchors, not proof of this composition:

- [Google PAIR — Mental Models](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapter/mental-models/)
  recommends staged onboarding, realistic expectations, benefits over technology,
  and opportunities to experiment. Apply this to visible sample outcomes and
  contextually introduced capabilities, not an exhaustive feature tour.
- [Apple — Shared with You](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102197) demonstrates
  shared content appearing in relevant consumption surfaces with sender context.
  Borrow object-native social delivery, not its particular permission model.

## 9. Interaction and cross-lane boundaries

For each important unit, specify what the tap does, the existing destination,
whether anything persists, whether anyone else is affected, and how the person
returns to their prior Home position. Reading a unit is not permission to save,
invite, share, schedule, or subscribe to a monitor.

For a later open, distinguish meaningful updates from gratuitous reshuffling.
An ignored item creates no debt; it also is not conclusive negative preference.
Keep useful material recoverable through its owner without freezing Home forever.

Adjacent owners remain intact:

- **Life:** durable organization, People/shared records, and retrieval destinations.
- **Components and Plan:** arrangement composition, contribution/editing,
  participation, and real commitments; no parallel Home planning workspace.
- **Entities:** canonical object pages and source/research presentation.
- **Contribution and Capture:** intake and immediate readback; this pass owns
  Home's presentation of the resulting value, not a new capture pipeline.
- **Retirement:** booking contraction and legacy operational surfaces. Use
  external provider handoff where needed; do not restore retired ambitions.

Report needed data and integration seams without inventing new canonical
objects, background jobs, audience permissions, or APIs in the design project.

## 10. Evaluation and deliverables

These are design reviews, not participant validation or production readiness.

For every complete page, answer:

1. Is the first glance useful and understandable without reading design notes?
2. If its leading offering does not interest me, are there other good reasons to stay?
3. What did I receive before being asked to contribute, decide, reply, or save?
4. Does the longer scroll add different value rather than more prose and chrome?
5. Can I recognize ordinary practical competence without a rare travel emergency?
6. Is multiplayer apparent through people and their contributions, not only avatars?
7. Can I deliberately find shared material beyond what Home selected?
8. Does solo or thin-context Home remain worthwhile without fabricated intimacy?
9. Do new users understand concrete benefits and how to try them?
10. Does a genuine priority remain unmistakable within the richer product?
11. Are calm aesthetics compatible with anticipation, pleasure, and curiosity?
12. Can I stop freely without completion pressure, while continuing remains worthwhile?
13. Can I distinguish a section, an object, its title, and metadata without
    reading every word? Do card boundaries clarify rather than clutter?
14. Is capability demonstrated through a result rather than a paragraph about it?
15. Which units can appear without new generation, and what would actually trigger
    new work? Does the page stay worthwhile when no bespoke result is ready?
