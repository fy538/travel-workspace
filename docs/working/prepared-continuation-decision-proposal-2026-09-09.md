# Decision proposal: one prepared continuation

## 1. Status

**Resolved: full bounded exception adopted for design** by the
[September 9 decision](../decisions/2026-09-09-select-design-convergence-and-prepared-continuations.md#2-amend-sentence-6-one-optional-prepared-message-door).
The founder delegated selection after the review. That decision, not this packet,
amends sentence 6 and specifies the exact Social receiving treatment. Remove
PROPOSED only from the prepared-door choice in I1/J2f when the designs are updated;
retention, guest and following proposals remain distinct. No export or runtime
has been changed by resolving this packet.

Sections 2–7 preserve the original proposal, alternatives and dated pending
states as rationale. They are not a current decision queue or authorization to
use the old ambiguous Social controls.

## 2. What is being asked

Sentence 6 of the accepted seven sentences reads:

> **Vesper states facts.** It recommends when asked, or once, in one sentence, when
> a change defeats what you said you wanted. No recommendation carries a button.
> It can be turned off.

The requested amendment adds one bounded exception, in these exact words:

> **Vesper states facts.** It recommends when asked, or once, in one sentence, when
> a change defeats what you said you wanted. A recommendation carries no button —
> **except that when acting on it means sending words to people already involved,
> one door may open that message, already addressed and editable. The person sends.**
> It can be turned off.

Nothing else in the seven sentences changes.

## 3. The tradeoff, in work rather than controls

Two fixtures already drawn, each measured as steps the person performs.

| | Accepted sparse route | With the amendment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kyoto, 09 J2f.** Ben moved Tōdai-ji to eleven, after the quieter window. | Read the implication · open the entry · restate the request in words · read what Vesper composes · send | Read the implication · open the prepared message · send or edit |
| **Saturday, 11 I1.** Sam cannot do eight; keeping seven with Maya joining late is the supported possibility. | Read the possibility · open the entry · retype the decision · name the two recipients · read · send | Read the possibility · open the prepared proposal · send or edit |

**Saved:** one reconstruction — restating a decision Vesper has just stated — and
one addressing step, naming recipients Vesper already knows.
**Not saved, and not intended to be:** the reading, the editing and the send.

**If adopted,** a door appears only on a result that already carries a stated
implication and only where acting means sending words. The risk is door
proliferation; the bounds in §4 are what hold it to one.

**If declined,** the retyping stands. Vesper's help ends at the observation in
precisely the cases where it has already done the thinking, and the person
reconstructs the request each time.

## 4. Bounds that hold in either outcome

- Send is always human; the words are always editable.
- Preparation changes nothing: not the arrangement, not provider truth, not any
  participant's answer. Sending a proposal is a message, not an adoption.
- One door, never a set. No command catalog, recommendation inventory or
  management surface. No door on pages that carry no stated implication.
- Recommendations still appear only when asked, or once after a problem, and can
  be turned off.
- Owner-instructed change stays distinct: Plans 05 D3's **Update & send** moves the
  arrangement and announces it, because the owner instructed it. 11 I1b's
  **Send proposal** changes nothing until people answer. The label names the effect.
- Unaffected: PR-1 remains deferred; PR-2 remains withdrawn; the continuing-help
  terms of `12 W1–W5` remain separately proposed and are not activated here.

## 5. The three answers

1. **Adopt as written.** Sentence 6 gains the exception; Plans 11 I1–I1c and 09
   J2f lose their PROPOSED label; Social may take the receiving state in §6.
2. **Adopt narrower.** The same wording limited to a result the person's own
   request produced, excluding results caused by another person's change. This
   keeps 11 I1 and drops the 09 J2f case.
3. **Decline.** Sentence 6 is unchanged; the frames stay illustrative and labelled
   as a rejected option; Social keeps §6's pending state permanently.

## 6. Social's receiving state, exactly

Social 03.5 currently offers **Keep seven** and **Move to eight** as two equal
selections. They are not equal: one is an owner-instructed change, the other is a
message that changes nothing until people answer.

- **While this decision is pending:** 03.5 keeps the owner-instructed change as its
  one direct action, and keeping-and-telling is expressed in words through the
  contextual entry. The two must not stand side by side as equal defaults, and
  Social must not adopt an independent default in the meantime.
- **If adopted (1 or 2):** 03.5 may show one door that prepares the proposal, on
  Plans 11 I1b's reading — a message that changes nothing until people answer,
  labelled by its effect (**Send proposal**), never presented as a peer of the
  update.
- **If declined:** 03.5 keeps the direct owner action only; keeping-and-telling
  stays a typed request.

Social leads the actor-by-actor work comparison. This proposal does not create a
second assistance system, and no Plan canvas is reopened to accommodate it.

## 7. Evidence and limits

The comparison in §3 counts steps in two static, illustrated fixtures. Nothing is
wired: Plans board 92's prototype uses a finite regex phrase set, has no free-text
routes for the guest, and marks the longer Chat handoff unbuilt, so it exercises
neither the I1 nor the W sequences. No native or participant evidence exists for
either route. The step counts are design arithmetic, not measured task times.
