# The record is a first class product benefit

## Context and authorization

In the Strategy thread `01a08d4e-3ee2-7d80-9add-a5179aaf3b8e`, the founder
identified visible memory, contributing and multiplayer as the product center.
The September 28 follow-up made the resulting record a benefit in itself.
On September 29, the founder authorized the documentation reconciliation
following the 24-hour audit. The [direction note](../working/product-direction-2026-09-28.md#11-september-28-follow-up-the-record-is-worth-having)
preserves that reasoning; the [research follow-up](../working/memory-as-product-direction-research-2026-09-28.md#14-september-29-collection-experience-follow-up)
keeps evidence separate from proposals.

## Decision

1. Making the record should require no separate documentation routine.
   The resulting record can nevertheless be a first-class reward: recognizable
   originals, enjoyable personal or shared collections, and useful refinding.
   Contribution still costs effort and must earn it.
2. Understanding, revisiting, sharing and practical action are possible
   continuations, not mandatory stages. The September 27 action-oriented loop
   is a complete example, not the test every kept thing must pass.
3. Vesper's subject remains encounters and relationships with the world.
   Collections organize the consumer experience; specialized owners retain
   source, occurrence, reservation, audience and action truth. Multiplayer can
   enrich the material without manufacturing one shared voice.

## Boundaries and consequences

Reconcile Product Thesis and Product Model without rewriting the
[September 27](2026-09-27-documenting-core-loop-and-one-composer.md) or
[September 28](2026-09-28-collections-are-the-spine.md) records. The four product
moves and practical capabilities remain; novelty is required of claimed AI
editorial additions, not every original or retrieval.

This adopts no new retention grant, automatic sharing, correction-learning
default, catalog mechanism, notification cadence, launch scope or schema.
The September 29 formation, correction and stability refinements remain
proposals in the direction note. No runtime implementation or commercial
advantage is certified by this amendment.
