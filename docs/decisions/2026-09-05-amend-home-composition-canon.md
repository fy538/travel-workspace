# Amend the Home composition canon

## Context

The September 4 Home-only project (`Vesper — Home`, revisions 1–3 of the
generous-value pass) was reviewed on September 5 against every product,
system, and decision document created or updated since August 14. The
principles held: one dominant, a strict demand budget and a generous
possibility budget, value before input, interpret the experience not the
person, social material inside the object it changes, Life holds evidence
while Home delivers consequence, silence as a first-class result.

Four points did not hold, and in each the accepted boards were right and the
written canon was stale:

1. The kernel's first composition law, **containment equals completion**
   (§5.1), was written when the only carded thing on Home was a finishable
   crown. The founder's September 4 review withdrew the derived rule that a
   card renders only when something operational is at stake, and revision 2
   cards a friend's photograph and words and a prepared afternoon. Neither is
   finishable; both are coherent objects.
2. The August 30 anatomy decision and the Home root contract say **a visible
   people chapter appears only when the gathering itself is the dominant
   subject** (R1). That sentence predates authored shares drawn at full size.
   The September 4 handoff explicitly reopened the question; the H2
   comparison recommended a conditional grouping and reported the tension as
   D-H2 / D-H3 rather than ruling.
3. The kernel's **two kicker registers per screen** (§12.7 c) was superseded in
   practice by revision 2's four typographic roles, which the founder's review
   accepted as the working baseline (D-H10).
4. The **closed 31-kind Home union** ("adding a kind is a canon event") was
   drawn before the possibility-field and generous-value work. Revision 3 uses
   four forms that are not in the union.

## Decision

### 1. Containment marks a coherent object

Kernel §5.1 is amended from "a card marks something you can complete here; a
row takes you somewhere else" to:

> **A card marks a coherent object or experience — something finishable here,
> or something whole enough to be held as one thing (an authored share, a
> prepared afternoon, a shared arrangement, a reading preview). A row takes
> you somewhere else. Findings, techniques, and facts stay bare.**

Containment remains selective. It is not reserved for urgency, and it is not
applied to every sentence. The crown keeps its meaning: the one dominant,
finishable thing.

### 2. The people region is conditional, not forbidden — and holds only what was addressed

R1's clause "a visible people chapter appears only when the gathering itself
is the dominant subject" is replaced by:

> **Social material renders inside the state, possibility, or continuity it
> changes. An `Addressed to you` region may additionally render when two or
> more contributions deliberately given to this person are not already spent
> in full above it. It never duplicates a contribution shown in full
> elsewhere on the page, it disappears when empty, and it ends with the pull
> door. Shared consequences (an arrangement, a decision) still appear where
> they land, above the region.**

The MP2 "one relational opening" law is read as a **dominance and demand**
rule: at most one social cause may lead the page, and at most one social unit
may ask anything. It is not a page-wide count of authored material. This
resolves D-H2 and D-H3.

**The social split (ruled later the same day, founder).** Multiplayer material
divides by root as follows, and this governs where §2's region and the pull
door point:

| Material | Root | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Directed and shared-consequential: addressed to me, an invitation, a contribution to our arrangement, a decision asked of me, a plan edit under a grant, a change to a shared occasion | **Home** | The consequence, never the notification; inside the object it changes, or in the `Addressed to you` region; one ask per page |
| Casual sharing: a friend's photograph, note, or place made visible to friends; a featured Status | **Places**, as a `From friends` scope over the same map and field | Finite, situation-ranked, never person-ranked; marks at the precision the author chose (Place, neighborhood, city); city-level statuses in an `Elsewhere` band; ends |
| The record: what each person addressed, shared, contributed, withdrew, under which grant and epoch | **Life · People** | Stable, indexed by person, never reordered by visits |

The friends map is **semantic, never biometric** (social aperture §9.8): every
mark answers where they were or what they shared or what is in a plan, who
authored it, at what precision, for which audience, until when. No live
location, no presence inferred from anything, no location permission required
of anyone.

Home's pull door ("What your friends have shared") lands on Places in the
friends scope. **This closes D-H4**: no present-tense band on Life People and
no fifth surface; a separate Shared World aperture stays a hypothesis until the
friends scope proves insufficient. Casual shares with no Place keep their two
existing homes: a Status doorway on Home while featured, and Life People after.

### 3. Four typographic roles replace the two-kicker rule

Kernel §12.7 (c) is superseded:

> **Section heading:** sans 13/600, sentence case, with a hairline; used only
> where it navigates. **Content title:** serif, the first line of every unit.
> **Metadata:** one mono line at the 10px floor, for time, distance, date, or
> source only. **Supporting text:** one or two sentences. The oxblood status
> line stays a different atom (sans + dot) on the crown only. Gold mono
> survives only as the Door law's text and as a unit's metadata line when the
> unit is tappable.

### 4. Four kinds join the Home union

| Kind | Region | Containment | Evidence gate | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `horizon_prepared_alternatives` | Horizons (may lead on a quiet day) | card | two to four grounded, mutually alternative ways into an open window, each with its own time and reason; unnumbered; opening one adopts nothing | BUILD |
| `horizon_world_fact_row` | Horizons | uncarded fact row | a city-scoped notice with a date, hour, or service consequence inside the person's window; marked by its source; never a standing section; never inferred from a trace | BUILD |
| `people_authored_region` | With people | banded region of cards | the condition in §2 above; holds only material addressed to this person | BUILD |
| `now_sample_demonstration` | Now (Cold and Thin postures) | dashed card, stamped SAMPLE | a fixed, reusable demonstration of one capability with a made-up input and its result; never personal; retires after tried or ignored twice | BUILD |

The Home union is now **35 kinds**. The build manifest remains the count
authority.

## Not decided here

- Production classes and cost (D-H11). The H0 ledger's separation of design
  intent, verified implementation, and measured economics stands as a review
  obligation, not a claim.
- Any change to the seven postures, the orientation ladder, the Quiet floor,
  or the demand budget. Those rulings are unchanged.

## Consequences

- The kernel doc carries the amended §5.1 and §12.7 (c) text with pointers
  here.
- The build manifest lists the four kinds under their regions.
- The Home root contract's rules 1, 3, 4, and 7 cite this decision.
- The August 30 anatomy decision carries a revision note; its text is not
  rewritten.
- The `Vesper — Home` project's H0 marks D-H1 withdrawn, D-H2/D-H3/D-H10
  ruled, and the four kinds admitted.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit if the people region is observed rendering as a social module (more
than one screen in three, or with material already shown above), if carded
non-finishable objects blur the crown's meaning in native testing, or if the
world-fact row drifts into a nearby feed.
