# Adopt the Life v1 behavior sequences

## Decision

Promote the following four families to **consumer-proven at 1:1 fixture
scale in their owning surfaces**:

1. **Together** — the six-state sequence on design board 21 is accepted.
   Arrival changes one attribution; withdrawal removes the dependent region
   and its lineage; blocking changes viewer-relative visibility without
   rewriting either person's independent record.
2. **Human refinding** — board 22 is accepted. Search begins with the most
   likely target, shows containment and match reason, states limits honestly,
   and may maintain continuity within the current search session. Search is
   retrieval, not a write or a resurfacing event.
3. **Return arbitration** — board 23 is accepted. A shared evidence cluster
   has at most one active present-delivery seat across roots. When Home or
   Places owns the current consequence, Life yields without an empty state;
   the durable dossier and search path remain.
4. **Contribution and consequence** — board 24 is accepted after the D5
   truth/value correction. The response delivers value before retention,
   a receipt appears only when durable state changed, placement follows the
   person's authored meaning, later consequence supplies new substance, and
   one correction repairs every dependent projection.

The corrected heat comparison on board 13C is also promoted at fixture scale.
It compares two authorized responses to heat without asserting that they are
the same mechanism or that either caused the other.

## What this status does and does not mean

This decision closes the **experience-shape question** for these fixtures. It
does not claim usability proof, accessibility proof, visual polish, model
reliability, contract completion, or production implementation.

The two axes remain independent:

- Experience: architecture-resolved → fixture-composed → directionally
  accepted → **consumer-proven at 1:1 fixture scale** → canonically integrated
  → build-ready.
- Build: unimplemented → implemented dark → integrated behind flag → shipped.

The families above still require canonical integration into one compact Life
v1 experience, followed by the engineering work in the Life transformation
sequence. Only refinding has a dark implementation slice today.

## Binding behavior

### Together

- Every shared rendering names the contributor and the audience relationship.
- A grant change recompiles every dependent rendering before the next read.
- Withdrawal removes derived use but does not rewrite independent history.
- Blocking is a viewer-relative visibility overlay, not deletion or ownership
  transfer.
- Shared records appear inside the same Time, Places, Threads, and People
  corpus; there is no separate social inbox.

### Refinding

- Results are doors to existing objects, never a competing object type.
- The result tells the person what matched and where it lives.
- Exact lookup and semantic recovery share presentation but retain distinct
  truth: the system must not imply semantic coverage it does not have.
- Search does not silently save, promote, infer a preference, or create a
  thread.

### Return arbitration

- Admission requires authority, truth, novelty, a material trigger, and fresh
  world facts.
- Engagement probability, dwell, elapsed time alone, and available screen
  space are never triggers.
- Compatible jobs may merge; redundant siblings suppress.
- A losing candidate is not queued as engagement inventory. It may remain in
  its dossier or search path, or fall silent.
- Silence is a complete state.

### Contribution and consequence

- The immediate response must be useful even if the person never retains the
  contribution.
- Ask is no-write unless the person also contributes or authorizes a change.
- Receipts explain actual durable change and expose Correct/Undo when those
  actions are meaningful.
- Later projections must add current evidence, comparison, reconstruction, or
  practical use—not replay the person's own observation.
- Corrections target the underlying claim or relation and propagate causally.
