# Adopt structured contribution use grants

## Context

The accepted [Contribution and Consequence
Contract](../systems/contribution-and-consequence.md) established five
independent authority axes—Use, Retention, Inference, Audience, and Action—and
three visible treatments. Research and fixture review exposed seven decisions
needed before those axes can govern ordinary Chat, Intake, behavioral learning,
multiplayer projection, Life inspection, and connected services consistently.

The supporting [Source, Claim, Scoped Learning, and Multiplayer Purpose
research](../working/source-claim-scoped-learning-and-multiplayer-purpose-research-2026-08-29.md)
found that the seven questions are one information-flow problem rather than
seven separate permission products.

## Decision

Adopt a structured **Contribution Use Grant** as the policy meaning carried by
the existing contribution envelope. It is a coordination decision, not a new
universal durable object or automatic database table.

The five authority axes retain their names and gain these meanings:

1. **Use:** immediate job, allowed purposes, and eligible consumers.
2. **Retention:** independent Source, claim, and projection lifecycles.
3. **Inference:** allowable semantic distance, learning target, and learning
   level.
4. **Audience:** contributor, subject or affected principal, custodian, and
   recipients—not a single private/shared flag.
5. **Action:** owner, externality, materiality, reversibility, and authority.

Visibility, relevance, confidence, repetition, successful operation, and
following do not widen purpose or authority.

## Seven rulings

### 1. Ask and Bring are inferred from gesture and affordance

- A Source accompanying a question supports **Ask** and is transient by
  default.
- A Source deliberately sent alone in Chat is **Point/Bring** and may receive
  private reversible custody after value.
- A share sheet, forwarded message, or explicit Add affordance is **Bring**.
- **Keep** or explicit authored language admits only the named Source or claim.
- Ambiguity falls back to useful T0 behavior unless retention itself was the
  invoked job.

No classification question precedes value. T1 leaves a compact scope and Undo
receipt; T2 remains reserved for material boundaries.

### 2. Expiry follows the owning lifecycle

Natural completion, Occasion end, provider deadline, Moment end, revocation,
or correction is the primary expiry event. Time-to-live is a conservative
backstop. Stored evidence and eligibility for future influence are separate.

The v1 defaults live in the system contract and may be tuned through product
evidence without reopening this decision.

### 3. Outcome learning preserves a causal ladder

The system distinguishes exposure, deliberate relation, commitment, provider
confirmation, Occurrence, and authored Outcome. No stage proves the next.

Every learning update names its target:

- world;
- product performance;
- current situation;
- person; or
- relationship.

Implicit signals default to product or situation learning. Durable person and
relationship claims require governed authored or well-supported evidence.

### 4. Co-owned material separates custody from projection

One contributor may privately retain a Source without acquiring unilateral
authority to publish every affected person's identity, location, relationship,
or experience. Wider projections use the least permissive applicable
affected-person boundary. Narrowing, omission, redaction, or abstraction is
preferred to a social negotiation workflow.

### 5. Inspectability is contextual, not a dossier

Life exposes Sources, Occurrences, Outcomes, episode membership, audience, and
repair in context. Consequential projections expose “Why this?” using concrete
evidence. Global settings own connectors, mandates, retention, and complete
Source access. Vesper does not create a universal inferred biography or
personality dashboard.

### 6. Human verbs communicate purpose

Ask, Bring/Keep, Send/Address, Contribute, Feature as Status, Publish to Place,
and Act are distinct authority-bearing verbs. Users do not manage an abstract
purpose matrix. The invoked verb provides the default; a receipt or exact
boundary preview makes the consequence legible.

### 7. Connected services are narrow Source and action adapters

Permission to retrieve from Maps, email, calendar, photos, messaging, booking,
location, or health systems does not authorize retention, inference, sharing,
or write-back. Adoption proceeds from user-directed import, to invoked narrow
retrieval, to Plan/Occasion-bounded monitoring. Ambient continuous ingestion is
deferred until a narrow value and permission model are proven.

## Product consequence

The visible experience remains simple:

```text
Ask or Point
  -> Vesper contributes value
  -> stop, apply a small private consequence with Undo,
     or preview one material boundary
```

The policy complexity remains behind the interface. It exists so Home and
Places can confidently deliver value, Chat can remain the low-friction input
surface, Life can preserve continuity without becoming maintenance work, and
multiplayer can enrich each loop without creating a group mind.

## Implementation consequence

- Extend policy decisions before changing storage schemas.
- Keep Intake v2's custody, observation, candidate, and capability separation.
- Gate legacy observation, synthesis, behavioral, social, and connector paths.
- Require causal readback and invalidation across Home, Places, Life, Chat,
  Occasion, social projections, and pending actions.
- Certify the expanded contribution fixture portfolio before promoting a
  universal data shape.

## Deferred

- Exact expiry values may change after operational and human evidence.
- Co-owned media conflict resolution beyond conservative projection is not P0.
- Full-inbox, camera-roll, ambient location, health-history, and relationship-
  inference connectors remain outside the initial integration posture.
- Model-weight machine unlearning is distinct from product-level deletion,
  correction, revocation, and projection invalidation.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit if people cannot predict Ask versus Bring after seeing value and a T1
receipt; if affected people cannot safely narrow future projection; if a later
occasion cannot explain which governed evidence improved it; or if a connected
service requires broader authority than its named job to provide material
value.
