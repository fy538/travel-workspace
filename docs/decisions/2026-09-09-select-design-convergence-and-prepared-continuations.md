# Select coherent destinations and bounded prepared continuations

## Context and acceptance boundary

After the six-project source and representative-render review, the founder asked
the orchestrator to make the recommended design decisions and update the handoffs
with residual corrections. This records that delegated selection. It is not a
claim that the founder separately inspected each resulting frame or that the
designs have passed native or participant acceptance.

The inspected projects contain 108 HTML boards including historical/reference
material. Home 15's steering, Places 03's correction and 08's encounter, Life 02/06's
continuity comparisons, Entity 14's destination proposal and the
[prepared-continuation packet](../working/prepared-continuation-decision-proposal-2026-09-09.md)
are delivered comparisons, not requests to invent those designs again.

## 1. Select the purpose-responsive Entity destination

Accept Entity 14's direction: **stable identity and shell, purpose-responsive
reading**. This rejects the lab's invariant-body rule, not the restrained
photographic/minimal-body design. Use supported material and current state:

- Discovery can explain character or an earned relationship.
- Assessing Saturday leads with facts relevant to that actual visit.
- An existing reservation leads with its confirmation, useful approach and
  arrangement; do not keep selling Reserve as the primary next action.
- Opening a friend's contribution reaches the exact original first, with a
  separate Place door and human Reply; optional private Ask requires eligible use.
- Understanding an unvisited place may complete the benefit without routing there.

This selects the treatment, not every fixture sentence or illustrated capacity,
access claim or kitchen cutoff. Correct unsupported claims before treating those
frames as the selected specification. No generation on opening, new owner,
provider operation or universal body template is adopted. Entity reconciles
active 09/11/12 and its indexes with 14 rather than leaving two competing specs.

## 2. Amend sentence 6: one optional prepared-message door

Adopt the packet's full bounded exception, not the request-only alternative.
Replace sentence 6's no-button clause with the following:

> **Vesper states facts.** It recommends when asked, or once, in one sentence,
> when a change defeats what you said you wanted. A recommendation carries no
> button—except that when acting on it means sending words to people already
> involved, one door may open that message, already addressed and editable.
> The person sends. It can be turned off.

The reason is practical: when a change defeats an expressed purpose, requiring
the person to re-prompt for the useful message recreates work Vesper can already
prepare. The exception covers Plans I1 and J2f under the existing recommendation
conditions; it is not permission for unsolicited social outreach or a button
on every recommendation. All other seven-sentence rules retain their scope.

The door opens private preparation. It does not send, change a Plan, accept
participation, contact a provider or establish another person's answer. Recipients
and disclosed material must be eligible; knowing a name is not permission to
include private context. Show the message and audience, allow editing or leaving,
then use an explicit human Send. Sent proposals remain proposals; replies alone
do not silently adopt an arrangement. Existing owners apply authorized changes.

| Current intent | Chosen continuation | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consider a supported logistical possibility | Prepare proposal → editable message → **Send proposal** | Sends words only; arrangement and participant commitments remain unchanged |
| Owner explicitly instructs an arrangement change and announcement | Existing D3 preview → **Update & send** | Applies that authorized owner change and sends the announcement; authoritative readback reports actual results |
| Read an original or understand a situation | Read / optional contextual question / stop | No compulsory proposal, reply or durable consequence |

For Social 03.5's inferred option, select the proposal route, not ambiguous
“Move to eight…”. The owner-instructed D3 route remains available when actually
requested, not an equal recommendation button beside it. Use exact effect labels
and truthful pending/failure readbacks; “Send proposal” is never an update alias.

## 3. Select the receiving compositions

**Home:** retain the generous ordinary/post-return 02/03 compositions. On a
nonurgent page, the strongest admitted benefit leads; weather is supporting
orientation unless its consequence is itself the strongest value. Select 15.3's
learning-through-received-value and ordinary controls; the explicit sample remains
an optional bounded demonstration, not an input tax. Accept 15.5–15.7's local
shortening treatment without adopting persistent preference/retention policy.
14 remains the bounded-supply stress case. This does not amend commitment/crown
arbitration, admit proposed new kinds or change social placement.

**Places:** preserve the substantial field and purpose-sensitive access. Select
08.12's natural stop; lunch stays in the existing nearby exploration, not an
automatic continuation. Keep 08.13's unvisited understanding, correcting its
diagram or using concise prose until a truthful diagram exists. Keep the explicit
mistake/correction/later-use comparison; lead the corrected screen with useful
help and a brief acknowledgment, not a scope-management table.

**Life:** preserve four lenses, ordinary formation, direct originals, precise
retrieval and quiet non-use. Contextual and no-history comparisons receive equal
present information and practical competence. Historical behavior cannot make
a current route feasible. No archive expansion or compulsory insight follows.

**Social:** select original-first receiving and the existing guest-hospitality /
asymmetric-photo sequence as the core exemplars. A share may end in enjoyment;
an outing is optional. Keep D7 as a corrected matched-evidence comparison, not a
claim of superiority. Use ordinary authorized assets; placeholders do not establish
pleasure. No public performance metrics, automatic private-use reporting, mandatory
reply or reciprocal contribution is introduced.

## 4. Service and residual-correction rules

Preserve the actual requested target and window. In the current W2 example,
“before Saturday” ends when Saturday begins in the stated venue-local timezone,
not Saturday at 5 pm. Any different window or near-match notification requires
an explicit choice; do not make correction the price of a faithful readback.
Correcting that specimen does not adopt or activate its pending following service.

Places' “Check seats, then ask” is a current check and prepared question, not an
implicit watch. When seats are unavailable, preserve the arrangement and state
the result without promising a later Home update. A later check is explicitly
user-invoked unless a separately supported bounded mandate exists.

Residual work must preserve a consistent clock, current origin, source/permission
inputs and action meaning through each sequence. Visuals must carry the same
distinctions as the prose: corrected sailing versus issued ticket, moving time
versus waiting, and pumps versus a raised sill. Remove repeated process narration,
not useful evidence or material responsibility. The
[shared handoff](../working/vesper-five-project-design-consolidation-2026-09-08.md)
owns frame-level corrections and closure evidence, not another set of policy rules.

## Boundaries, consequences and revisit triggers

These choices reduce reconstruction and conflicting design specifications. They
cost some projection variation and require discipline about when a prepared door
is appropriate. Revisit if people mistake a proposal for a changed arrangement,
doors proliferate, destination variation obscures identity, or rich receiving
creates repetitive reading rather than useful choice.

Existing [contribution authority](../systems/contribution-and-consequence.md)
and [exact-original display](2026-09-09-exact-original-recipient-display.md)
remain binding. This accepts no conversational-retention agreement, independent
recipient-copy right, broader Friends/guest audience, watch service, pre-Plan
schema, new Home placement, provider booking or notification permission. It
does not promise deletion of already received bytes or screenshots.

Update design handoffs and selected/proposed labels only for the choices above.
Other flags and proposals stay distinct. No product implementation, deployment,
publication, recruitment or restart of the paused Integration lane is authorized
by this documentation decision. Preserve immutable earlier decisions and their
rationale; this record amends sentence 6 only where expressly stated.
