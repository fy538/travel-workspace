# Decision: an exact original sent to one recipient may be viewed

## Context

Original human material is complete value. A friend should be able to enjoy
what someone deliberately sent without asking Vesper a question, keeping a
copy, replying, or contributing equivalent effort. Existing handoff metadata
and recipient-only display intent do not alone authorize an arbitrary original
byte read. The September 9 investigation identified the missing exact selection
and current source/share checks in the existing Relationships and Source owners.

The founder approved the proposed bounded rule and implementation packages with
“Let's do it” following the recommendation to complete original receiving and
meaning-based discovery. This decision records only the display rule.

## Decision

Explicitly sending a selected original to one named, currently eligible account
recipient authorizes that recipient to view that exact material while both the
share and source remain valid. The effective selection identifies the original
owner, source/object or component, and applicable revision. Replacing the
selected original is not an automatic consequence of editing a note.

The existing Relationships owner records the addressed share and selected-source
binding; the existing original owner retains custody and supplies authorized
material. One current reader policy checks the recipient, selection, share,
source and applicable affected-person/precision restrictions before access.
An owner-only Intake route must not be broadened or called with an impersonated
sender to bypass recipient authorization.

This grants display, not AI use, inference, onward distribution, access to the
rest of a source bundle, or an independent retained recipient copy. Existing
permission names such as `contextual_only` must not manufacture those grants.
Opening creates no sender-visible read receipt and requires no Keep or reply.

Share withdrawal/expiry, source loss/expiry and applicable access restrictions
deny subsequent access. Consumers revalidate and remove unavailable material;
the product cannot promise erasure of screenshots or bytes already received.
Source content, share/audience and recipient attention remain distinct states.
Independently authorized material can remain useful when this original closes.

## Consequences and boundaries

- Implement exact selection, sender readback/control, recipient receiving and
  lifecycle repair through the existing owners and receiving components.
- Preserve current applicable connection, subject and precision restrictions;
  this does not settle broader disconnect-versus-old-share semantics.
- Do not backfill display permission from ambiguous historical `artifact_ref`
  strings or a source anchor's entire attachment bundle.
- Do not fabricate a Place or conversation to fit a legacy schema. If a
  selected non-spatial original cannot use the existing specialization, report
  the exact owner/schema boundary before generalizing it.
- Multi-recipient audiences, guests, general sharing architecture, friend-source
  AI use, later composition and Life serving remain separate work/decisions.
- This approves implementation, not production exposure, deployment, branch
  landing, a feature-flag change, or resumption of Integration.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit through a superseding decision if receiving requires additional rights,
an independent copy, materially different source-version behavior, broader
audiences or a different canonical owner. Test comprehension and receiving
quality separately from authorization correctness.
