# Use Hermene as the working company, product, and intelligence name

## Decision

**Hermene** is the working name for the company, the user-facing product, and
the intelligence that helps people build a relationship with places.

For current strategy, design, and product-copy work, use one public noun:

> Hermene helps the world become more yours—through the places you encounter,
> the people you are with, and what stays with you.

This is a working brand decision, not a public rename, incorporation decision,
domain purchase, trademark filing, or production copy migration. Existing
Vesper names, bundle IDs, domains, code identifiers, legal notices, and
release surfaces remain unchanged until a separately approved migration.

## Why one name

The product is not a portfolio of a parent company, a generic assistant, and a
separate world-model engine. Its value is a continuous relationship: grounded
world context, individual and group perspective, lived encounter, editable
memory, and a carefully timed future opening.

Giving each layer a different public name would make that relationship feel
like implementation architecture. A single name keeps the promise legible:

- people can ask Hermene for an interpretation or a plan;
- Hermene can make a proposal without pretending to own the group's decision;
- a person's **My World** remains their accumulated artifact, not a profile
  owned by the system;
- Places, Plans, conversation, routes, memory, and openings remain connected
  expressions of one product rather than separate apps.

Hermene is an interpretive name rather than a travel, map, ranking, or
notification name. It can therefore carry the broader company thesis as the
travel specialization grows into ordinary life and repeated relationships with
places.

## Product language

The following terms are product vocabulary, not competing master brands:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **My World** | The user-controlled, accumulating artifact of places, people, encounters, and returns that have become meaningful. |
| **Openings** | Timely, optional invitations to act, notice, revisit, or continue; silence is an intentional alternative. |
| **Motifs** | Cross-place threads in a person's curiosity or experience. |
| **Counterpoint** | Multiple valid group perspectives held without forced consensus. |
| **Reprises** | Meaningful returns to a place, memory, or unresolved thread. |
| **Resonance** | The philosophical outcome: a person is affected by the world, responds to it, and carries something forward. It is not the master brand. |

## Interaction guardrails

Use Hermene as a situated, accountable product—not as a fictional person that
claims ownership of the user's life.

- Say **"Hermene's proposal"**, show why and source/uncertainty where
  applicable, and keep human approval visible for commitments.
- Say **"Hermene remembers what you chose to keep"**, not that it possesses
  the user or secretly knows them.
- In groups, name a proposal, its scope, and its authority. Hermene can
  facilitate shared reality; it cannot manufacture consensus or expose private
  context.
- Let the user edit, correct, hide, or remove remembered material. The system
  may support interpretation, never dictate a final meaning of a place or
  encounter.

## Naming research disposition

The associated naming screen found that the direct **Reson** / **Rezon** /
**Resono** family is too occupied to carry the master brand. In particular,
RESON is a long-standing Teledyne marine-acoustics and sonar business with the
exact `reson.com` domain and global mapping/navigation associations. Retain
*resonance* as product philosophy, not a public company name.

Hermene's interpretation root is a better fit for the product's central claim:
places do not arrive with one final meaning; people encounter and interpret
them through situation, history, companions, and return. The familiar Hermes
association is evocative but etymologically uncertain, so public copy must not
present it as a literal origin claim.

## Required before any public adoption

1. Run a professional trademark knockout/clearance in the intended markets and
   relevant software, AI, travel, and consumer-service classes.
2. Verify domain, app-store, social-handle, and company-registration options
   at the time of a decision; no point-in-time web result is availability.
3. Conduct a small blind language test for pronunciation, spelling after
   hearing it, recall, and category association.
4. Approve a public naming and migration plan before changing code, bundles,
   legal copy, product UI, domains, or infrastructure.

## Non-goals

- A global find-and-replace of Vesper.
- An implication that trademark clearance has happened.
- A claim that the current travel product already implements the full company
  model.
- Turning resonance, Motifs, Counterpoint, or My World into separate brands.
