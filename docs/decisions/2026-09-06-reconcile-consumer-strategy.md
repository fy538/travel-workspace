# Reconcile Vesper's consumer strategy

## Context and acceptance boundary

The founder requested reconciliation of launch wedge, conversational continuity,
practical responsibility and monetization, then asked to update the documents
after reviewing the research and recommendations. This accepts the strategic
direction below. It does not certify demand, select prices, establish a memory
agreement with any user, approve the pending history/intention schemas, enable
watches, or authorize production deployment.

The August four-move philosophy and four-root structure remain. Earlier identity
decisions are amended only where they prescribe an exclusive group-travel-first
launch. Travel's canonical shared operational truth is unchanged. Commercial
arguments from the older Monetization Strategy and Venture Path are preserved
as archives, not additional current roadmaps.

## 1. Everyday usefulness is the product; travel is a specialization

Vesper starts from attention and intention in ordinary life, alone or with
other people. Local use, questions, understanding, casual sharing, practical
help and continuity have intrinsic value; they are not merely a bridge back to
travel or transaction revenue. A general input surface does not promise equal
specialist competence in every domain.

The initial market strategy is a bounded, reachable audience with supported
world coverage, not a mandatory Trip. People who already ask questions, save
places, exchange recommendations and make experiences happen are a candidate
cohort. A founder-accessible NYC cohort is a hypothesis, not a selected launch
geography or a claim that city-only Home supply is ready.

Separate audience, acquisition entrance, immediate payoff, natural return and
payment. A question, Source, friend's contribution or invitation may be an
entrance; none must traverse every product move or tab. Individual usefulness
must survive low social density. A recipient need not contribute equally or
become an organizer to receive value.

Group travel remains the first high-resolution backend specialization and a
strong acquisition candidate. It does not gate ordinary-life product value.
Compare travel-led and everyday-led acquisition with honest complete experiences;
do not declare either validated. Limit initial exposure and coverage rather
than derive the system from one behavior loop.

## 2. Optional conversational continuity belongs in the intended product

Relevant ordinary conversation should be able to improve later private help
under an understandable optional agreement, without per-turn saving. Manual
Keep is not intended to be the only continuity path. A discussion can remain
evidence of an exploration without becoming a preference, identity, intention,
Life entry, generated article or notification.

This is an accepted product direction, **not an accepted retention policy**.
Ask/T0 and current expiry remain binding until a separate contract amendment
settles material, duration, eligible later uses, sensitive and third-party
exclusions, inference limits, controls and dependency repair. Continuing to use
Vesper is not consent. No historical backfill follows from this decision.

Distinguish readable history, Source/derivative custody and later use. Private
Chat/Home/Places continuity is the intended scope to evaluate; sharing,
monitoring and external action remain separate. Preserve current-purpose
priority, conversation exclusion, correction and revocation without requiring
a memory-management workflow. Retention duration is deliberately unresolved.

The [existing history/source proposal](../working/conversation-history-source-expiry-decision-proposal-2026-09-06.md)
owns the next policy review. Evaluate understandable history behavior as well
as non-reconstruction: retain independent explanations where permitted, do not
launder released source content through generated answers, and do not make
unrelated conversation disappear. The proposal's particular treatments remain
unadopted.

## 3. Own bounded assistance, not universal execution

Vesper can understand evidence, assess feasibility, prepare alternatives,
retain intent, coordinate authorized changes and follow a supported condition
under an explicit bounded mandate. It does not resume provider transaction
execution. Booking retirement retains exact evidence and external continuation,
not checkout, payments, rebooking or autonomous restaurant contact.

| Relationship with the user | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Help me now | Useful grounded assessment; no implied future checking |
| Keep this in mind | Named retention and eligible later use; no watch by implication |
| Follow this for me | Supported subject/condition, sources, checking window, permitted treatment, stop and failure behavior |
| Make this change | Named authorized owner command and authoritative result; no permission expansion |

These are service expectations, not additional product grammar, roots or a
universal service. The live engine preserves the purpose of an experience, not
only its schedule. Useful preparation exists before disruption. A watch may
not become active through Keep, ordinary browsing, payment or inferred interest.
If accepted monitoring loses capability, convey the limitation where reliance
would otherwise be misleading. Notification controls and action authority remain
separate from monitoring.

The [pre-Plan intention proposal](../working/retained-intention-before-plan-decision-proposal-2026-09-06.md)
is directionally compatible; its owner/schema choice still requires adoption.
There is no instruction here to add a general scheduler or another truth owner.

## 4. Consumer-funded assistance is the base business hypothesis

The primary economic direction is payment for useful depth, capacity and bounded
assistance. Membership is the leading model to investigate for everyday value;
an occasion/travel pass is an alternative for episodic demand. Neither pricing,
conversion nor willingness to pay is established. Do not build both billing
systems merely because both remain plausible.

Replace "planning is free, always; pay for the experience" with complete,
credible free participation and bounded paid expansion. Complete is not
unlimited. Basic social participation, core correctness, correction, deletion
and access to entrusted material must not become coercive upgrade mechanisms.
Paid assistance must not require every recipient to subscribe or leave shared
truth inconsistent across tiers. No service accepts ongoing work beyond a
clearly represented allowance or period.

Booking commissions, sponsored ranking, personal-data commercialization, wallets
and travel financial rails are not the base business case or automatic future
stages. External referrals are optional supplementary revenue, require actual
commercial terms and honest attribution, and do not change recommendation
quality or authorize provider execution. Partnerships must enrich the same
consumer product without becoming bespoke operations software.

Evaluate net revenue against model/tool/data work, refreshing and monitoring,
storage/delivery, free-participant cost and variable support. Rich Home value
must not require bespoke generation on every visit. Subscription success is
not identical to daily use, and absence must not create content or response debt.

## Consequences and implementation boundary

Canonical owners are Product Thesis, Growth Strategy, Product Model, Product
Vision and Scope, Monetization Strategy and Venture Path. The contribution
contract continues to own actual authority; the integration roadmap owns
execution. Existing release manifests and flags do not expand automatically.

Do not rewrite the architecture or wait for every commercial detail to settle.
Continue bounded integration, correction, retained-evidence readers and
value-first surfaces under current contracts. New conversational retention,
pre-Plan persistence, watches, billing and release exposure require their
specific accepted contracts and verification.

## Evidence and revisit triggers

Desk research informs these choices; it is not Vesper user evidence. Sources,
limitations and documentary corrections are recorded in the
[consumer research brief](../working/vesper-consumer-promise-and-adaptive-value-research-2026-09-05.md#13-consequential-strategy-reconciliation--september-6).

Revisit audience/entry when newcomers cannot identify a use, travel produces
materially stronger demand, or local supply cannot support worthwhile value.
Revisit continuity if it requires memory homework, surprises people, or fails
to improve later help. Revisit responsibility when people misread what is being
followed or cannot rely on accepted work. Revisit payment when actual purchase,
renewal, recipient access or measured costs contradict the model. None of these
failures alone proves that all four product moves or roots should be discarded.
