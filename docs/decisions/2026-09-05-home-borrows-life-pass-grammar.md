# Home borrows Life's pass grammar; the crown is arbitrated

## Context

The Home project (`Vesper — Home`, `03538beb`, board `08 - Seam with Life`)
was read against the Life project (`Vesper — Life & Anchors`, `f524c7f0`,
boards 01A, 01A2, 01A6, 09B, 16, 17C, 19, 32, 33). The two projects share
one fixture world and one loop: Life holds the source, the record, and the
governed foundation; Home delivers the present consequence once; Places
delivers the place-scoped present; Chat continues; the receipt returns to
Life as lineage.

Board 08 drew one ticket and one flight at every scale from Life's pass to
Home's row, and the founder accepted it ("it brings home to life"). Two
kernel laws stood in the way, and a third question surfaced: once kept
objects can elevate into the crown, several objects can qualify as
dominant on the same afternoon, and nothing said which wins.

## Decision

### 1. Kind marks on object rows (amends kernel §12.7 g)

A Home row whose subject is a kept object (a ticket, a booking, a claim)
leads with Life's kind mark: a 15px inline glyph in ink, one stroke family
(Life 01A2 / 01A6). The mark is print, not an icon plate; §12.7 (g)'s "no
icon plates" stands. State stays in the words. People rows keep the
facepile; ephemeral rows (weather, a dentist) keep the status dot. One
lead per row. Kind inks (Life's charcoal, rust, olive, sepia, violet,
gold-deep) are illustration ink and never UI chrome.

### 2. Chip lettering is object print (amends the §11.2 floor's scope)

The chip (Life 01A6 v3: one line, h26, mark + identity + at most one dot;
ring = upcoming, green = live, grey = unused; held says nothing) may render
on Home only where a kept object is named: the KEPT receipt with Undo, and
the shelf under the Life door. Its interior lettering (8.5px mono, 11px
serif for a named place) is object print, exempt from the 10px UI floor the
way the pass's stub is. Never for UI labels, never as a list, never on a
row.

### 3. The pass on Home

The L1 pass (Life 09B signatures) renders on Home only inside its live
window, and there it is the crown: elevation, oxblood threshold, provider
facts replacing static ones, a stub that says when they were last true
(Life 01A, live state). Before the window the object is a chip and a mark,
and the day-before crown is Home's consequence with the ticket as one kick
line and a door; no provider fact is claimed before a provider supplies
it. After occurrence the pass never renders on Home; FLOWN is Life's word.

### 4. Crown arbitration (provisional: ruled on the drawing, to be confirmed on board 08 row three)

When more than one object qualifies as the dominant on one Home page, the
crown goes to the highest rank present:

1. **A recovery.** A held commitment inside its window whose world
   changed (the oxblood register).
2. **A live commitment.** A held commitment inside its window, unchanged
   (the pass as the crown).
3. **A decision asked of this person with a deadline.**
4. **A shared arrangement forming, or a prepared possibility.**

Ties within a rank go to the earlier deadline. When ranks 1 and 2 are the
same object they merge: the pass in the recovery register, the original
span struck through, the new one gold. The loser never becomes a second
crown: it folds to one row directly beneath the crown, carrying its
deadline, and returns to full form when the crown clears. The demand
budget holds throughout: at most one ask on the page, and it belongs to
the crown. When the crown's ask is spent (the message sent, the check-in
done), the next-ranked object's ask may become the page's one ask, on its
row.

### 5. Timeliness on the Addressed-to-you region

The region condition in decision 2026-09-05 §2 gains a clause: two or more
addressed contributions, unspent above, **and timely inside the person's
window** (Life 17C: an addressed note rests in Life People and Home admits
it only when it earns present attention).

### 6. The seat law, cited

Life 19: one present-delivery seat across surfaces. When Home delivers a
consequence from some evidence, Life's matching Return is absent, with no
redirect shell. Home's ledger (board 07) and the Home root contract cite
it.

## Not decided here

- Whether the kind inks enter Home's kit palette (the violet admission
  band is the only one drawn on Home so far).
- Large-text renders of chips and marks.
- Whether Places uses chips.

## Consequences

- Kernel: §12.7 (g) carries the mark note; §11.2 carries the object-print
  exemption; new §11.16 records the arbitration.
- Home root contract: rule 3 cites §11.16; rule 7 admits the mark and the
  chip.
- Board 08 row three draws the arbitration: the same flight delayed, the
  group's consequence, a pending decision that folds, and the ask moving
  once the crown's ask is spent.

## Revisit trigger

A mark or chip observed on a non-object row; two crowns on one page; a
folded decision whose deadline passes unseen; a provider fact shown before
a provider supplied it.
