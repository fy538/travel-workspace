# Adopt the lived-world product identity

## Context

The August 9 decision correctly moved Vesper beyond itinerary generation and a
travel-concierge category. It identified Experience as the human product unit,
group travel as a wedge, Place as more than inventory, proactivity as earned,
and outcomes as a compounding substrate.

Continued founder dogfood and product work exposed a deeper center. Rome in
August showed that abundant places, explanations, tours, and transactions still
leave the person responsible for joining meaning, physical condition, another
person's perspective, and practical action. A recent movie ticket could make
Troy, Aeneas, and Rome newly intelligible; heat, food, fatigue, and companionship
could determine whether that connection should become an action at all.

The same pattern extends beyond cultural interpretation. A photograph, menu,
question, invitation, reservation, message, book, changing condition, or
unfinished human thread can already hold attention. Requiring the person to
translate it into a travel, discovery, planning, social, or memory workflow
recreates the fragmentation Vesper is meant to remove.

Multiplayer work also moved beyond shared planning. An invitation creates a
temporary social world whose host intent, recipient relevance, participation,
permissions, and common consequences must become legible without exposing
private context or merging subjective experience.

## Decision

Vesper is **personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world**.

Its subject is the evolving, permissioned relationship among a person, other
people, and the physical world. Its universal doorway is whatever already has
attention. Vesper connects that fragment to the relevant people, Place, Source,
Occasion, Moment, and prior experience, then helps the smallest useful
consequence happen: immediate relief, interpretation, a Move, a human opening,
a governed shared action, continuity, or deliberate silence.

The product does not require every input to become a Plan, every encounter to
be captured, every shared episode to produce one subjective story, or every
useful response to compound. AI should deepen the encounter and reduce burden,
then return attention to the world and the people present.

The authority portfolio includes Person, Relationship, Place, Source,
Occasion, Plan, Commitment, Moment, and Outcome. Occurrence is distinct from
plural Outcomes. Attention, Opening, Move, Encounter, and artifact are
interaction or projection contracts; none automatically authorizes a new
table, service, or root surface.

Group travel remains the launch and distribution wedge and the first demanding
specialization. “Vesper helps groups have better trips—not just plan them” is a
valid wedge sentence, not the company definition. Inside a Trip, the living
itinerary remains canonical operational truth.

## Consequences

- Company and investor materials lead with the lived-world identity and make it
  concrete through an input, contextual connection, and consequence before
  explaining the travel wedge.
- The canonical authority list lives in `travel-agent/docs/product/README.md`.
  Company orientation begins with Product Thesis and Product Model; execution
  draws from the full architecture and behavior portfolio rather than one M1
  journey or proof loop.
- Product architecture is derived systematically across personal,
  multiplayer, interpretive, practical, local, travel, continuity, expiry, and
  silence behavior rather than from one proof loop.
- Multiplayer design treats Occasion as a bounded shared world and preserves
  private, relational, Occasion, and public visibility spaces.
- Source artifacts carry experience and provenance; domain authorities carry
  truth. A ticket or book may connect occasions without becoming the authority
  for place, attendance, identity, or meaning.
- Home, Vesper, Places, Life, Plans, maps, notifications, and handoffs remain
  interfaces into one intelligence. Conceptual importance alone does not earn
  a new root.
- Travel-specific release language may remain narrower when it is clearly
  labeled as release scope rather than company category.
- Product proof requires a representative portfolio. Architecture coherence,
  implementation conformance, human value, and release readiness remain
  separate gates.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit this identity if people cannot understand the product after a concrete
input-to-consequence demonstration, if non-travel doorways fail to produce
distinct value, or if the integrated personal and multiplayer product cannot
remain coherent without splitting into unrelated products. Revise through a
new decision; do not rewrite this record to imply the newer position was always
held.
