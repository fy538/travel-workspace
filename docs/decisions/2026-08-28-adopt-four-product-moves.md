# Adopt four product moves as the whole-product spine

## Context

The August product canon became coherent before it became easy to hold in one
mental model. It correctly contains a detailed personal lifecycle, multiplayer
authority, Place relationships, attention arbitration, artifacts, adaptive
treatments, four proposed roots, and durable domain owners. But several shorter
grammars compete to explain that whole:

- `See more / Live it well / Carry it forward / Experience it together`;
- `Bring / Understand / Keep, share, or act`;
- the formal Initiator–Verb–Object–Job–Context interaction sentence;
- the detailed personal experience lifecycle; and
- root shorthand such as `Home = attention` and `Chat = transformation`.

Recent Home, editorial, consequence, and social work exposed the missing
abstraction. The product needs one small set of responsibilities that explains
what value Vesper contributes without pretending every experience follows one
funnel or every root owns one feature family.

## Decision

The canonical whole-product spine is:

> **Make sense. Open possibility. Help it work. Carry forward.**

In full:

1. **Make sense.** Turn fragments, current reality, and authorized perspectives
   into a more accurate and useful representation. This requires an actual
   contribution—reconstruction, explanation, contrast, relational mapping, or
   clarified judgment—not storage exposure, paraphrase, or a personality read.
2. **Open possibility.** Make a grounded, optional way of noticing, entering,
   doing, making, continuing, or sharing newly available. A possibility is
   relative to the person, people, Place, Moment, and constraints; it is not an
   inventory item or an obligation.
3. **Help it work.** Once something matters, absorb the practical and
   coordinative burden required for it to become livable: shape, decide,
   prepare, authorize, route, coordinate, act, adapt, repair, or deliberately
   withdraw. Human meaning, social expression, and protected consequences stay
   with the appropriate people and authorities.
4. **Carry forward.** Preserve intent, evidence, outcomes, authorship, and
   unresolved continuity only when they can change a later surface, decision,
   capability, relationship, return, or Occasion. Carrying forward is
   prospective usefulness, not accumulation or retrospective display.

These moves are **recursive and non-mandatory**. A practical answer may make
sense and stop. A disruption may begin at Help it work. A past Outcome may
carry forward by opening a new possibility. One unit should have one dominant
job even when the containing root or experience composes several moves.

## Layering rule

The four moves do not replace the richer product machinery:

| Layer | Governing question |
|---|---|
| **Product moves** | What kind of value is Vesper contributing? |
| **Interaction grammar** | Who acts on what, for which immediate job, in what context, through which treatment and consequence? |
| **Personal and multiplayer lifecycles** | How does the experience unfold, stop, compose across people, reconcile, or return? |
| **Canonical authorities** | Which object owns durable truth, permission, and correction? |
| **Root postures** | How should several moves be composed for this mode of attention? |
| **Presentation treatments** | Which native visual or conversational form makes the job legible? |

The existing fundamental interaction sentence remains the mechanical product
grammar. The four moves are its **value grammar**.

## Terminology consequences

- **Opening** retains a narrow meaning: a grounded, optional possibility made
  available now. It is one possible output of **Open possibility**, not a name
  for every answer, insight, invitation, notification, or Home unit.
- **World Opening** remains interpretive content within an Opening. It does not
  become a durable object or the universal unit of product value.
- **Social and multiplayer are cross-cutting**, not a fifth move. Another
  person may help make sense, originate a possibility, participate in making
  it work, and separately authorize what carries forward.
- **Home, Chat, Places, and Life mix all four moves unevenly.** They are stable
  postures over one intelligence, not one-move-per-tab containers.
- The previous four promises remain historical provenance. `See more` divides
  more usefully into Make sense and Open possibility; `Live it well` becomes
  Help it work; Carry forward remains; `Experience it together` becomes a
  condition across the whole product.

## Product and surface law

> **One dominant job per unit. Several product moves per root. One coherent
> attention hierarchy per screen. One durable owner per consequence.**

Home additionally has a strict demand budget and a generous possibility
budget. The four moves are an admission and composition vocabulary; they do
not require four modules, equal representation, or a full loop on every page.

## Relationship to external messaging

This decision does not supersede the
[experiential-responsibility messaging hierarchy](2026-08-24-adopt-experiential-responsibility-messaging.md).
External explanations should still begin with the familiar human problem and
Vesper's responsibility. The four moves are the compact behavioral explanation
underneath that pitch, not a slogan a new listener must decode first.

## Consequences

- Product Thesis owns the four moves as the canonical whole-product spine.
- Product Model distinguishes the value grammar from interaction syntax,
  lifecycle, authorities, roots, and treatments.
- Personal Experience Loop maps its detailed stages onto the moves without
  becoming a four-step funnel.
- Surfacing Strategy and the experience constitution prevent one move per root
  and narrow the meaning of Opening.
- Multiplayer Strategy makes the social contribution visible across all four.
- Editorial Canon treats its epistemic operations primarily as Make sense and
  requires any offered continuation to earn Open possibility or Help it work.
- Current mobile contracts that describe the legacy three-root shell or the
  combined Vesper workbench remain honest implementation authorities, not
  target product canon.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit if complete scenario compositions repeatedly require a fifth product
responsibility, if users cannot explain the behavior after experiencing it, or
if the four moves cause teams to erase important authority, lifecycle, social,
or surface distinctions.
