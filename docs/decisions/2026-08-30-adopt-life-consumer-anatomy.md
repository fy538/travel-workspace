# Adopt the Life consumer anatomy

## Context

The Life continuity decision (2026-08-29) established Life as the governed
continuity and return layer without claiming a conforming surface. Through
2026-08-30 the anatomy was designed, rendered at 1:1 across every state, and
founder-ruled in the Claude Design project "Vesper — Life & Anchors"
(boards 00–11, with rejected alternatives preserved as evidence). The full
detail lives in the production spec
(`docs/working/life-root-production-spec-2026-08-30.md`); this decision
records what is now canonical.

## Decision

1. **Life has four first-class lenses — Time · Places · Threads · People**
   — one corpus, four explorations, never copies. This supersedes the
   earlier lens list (Timeline / Map / Artifacts-Sources / People /
   category filters): Threads is promoted to a co-equal exploration of
   pursuits; Artifacts ceased to be a lens and became nested renderings
   (chips, drawers) plus the Vault. The header is the speaking header:
   eyebrow, a four-pane facet instrument (the front pane walks by mode),
   an action capsule, a generated read in the standfirst register, and a
   mode sub-line — only Time carries an instrument (the years band, and
   only once the record spans more than a year), because only time is a
   line.

2. **Every lens page wears one anatomy**: digest (4–5 deterministic
   blocks) → one scroll door with an honest count → reflections
   (keepsake + specimen, both conditional) → up to two FIXED windows into
   other lenses → utilities. Windows change only when the record changes —
   rotation is Home's job. The root page is the front page of a mode,
   never the archive.

3. **The entry grammar**: mono section bars are the metronome; expanded
   entries are uncarded paper groups; children are serif lines led by
   their anchor's kind mark; collapsed entries are plain rows. **No cards
   on Life pages.** Below the pass, the miniature is one line — **the
   chip** (kind mark + identity + at most one exception dot); tiles,
   plates, and miniature status words are retired. On Life, held says
   nothing; dots mark only exceptions.

4. **Every click lands on the dossier** — one held-thing grammar at every
   grain (journey, chapter, cluster, thread, person, hosted night):
   everything the record holds, where every organ shows a taste, an
   honest count, and a door to all of it. Chips exit to the object page —
   the only surface where the full pass (L1) renders, with
   provenance-backed truths and minimal workflows. Scroll doors lead to
   the full scroll — the pure archive with its two-level metronome.
   Search returns doors whose containment chain is always visible.

5. **The Vault is the custody surface** behind "Everything kept" (user-language ruled 2026-08-31; "the Vault" is internal shorthand) — a utility
   room, not a fifth lens: everything held by kind with reconciling
   counts, loose artifacts as fact not chore, arrivals as receipts, the
   grants and withdrawal ledgers, and the custody verbs (destructive verbs in plain ink —
   oxblood is the live-threshold register only, ratified 2026-08-31).

6. **No hero photography anywhere in Life.** Life is a raw collection of
   artifacts, not a polished feed; imagery enters only as specimens, one
   riso keepsake per page, or a tapped photo as an object. **Thin is a
   state of the same grammar**: the degradation ladder gates instruments,
   never content; absence is never an ask.

## Rationale worth preserving

The lens system is the product's own best pitch: each mode is a different
exploration of the lived world, and together they demonstrate — better
than any onboarding — why contributing to Vesper compounds. This is where
the compounding-context loop's requirement that "future contribution feel
worthwhile" is actually manufactured.

## Supersessions (deliberate, founder-ruled)

The design phase re-ruled four positions taken by canon adopted on
2026-08-29. The design wins; this section exists so no reader mistakes
the older text for the standing law:

1. **Four co-equal lenses supersede the single period-and-episode
   spine** of the Life index contract (§Executive decision). The Time
   lens carries the period spine inside it; Timeline/Map/Sources as
   subordinate lenses are superseded by Time · Places · Threads ·
   People.
2. **In Motion is banned on Life** ("belongs in Home or Places"),
   superseding the index contract §2.2's In-motion region. The state
   continuity it carried is owed instead by: Home (live consequence),
   the digest's upcoming/live dots, and the dossier's
   plan-vs-occurrence block (spec §5).
3. **The Combined/Mine/Together toggle is removed from chrome**,
   superseding index contract §2.1's placement — but NOT its authority
   semantics, which still bind: they are owed a rendering by the
   Together-layer design (the largest open design arc) and, interim, by
   search scope filters (spec §5g).
4. **Threads ships as a first-class lens** ahead of the fixture
   report's P1 sequencing; the seven entrance gates of the continuity
   decision still apply to every thread door.

## Consequences

- The compact build-facing Life v1 cut is now owned by
  `docs/contracts/life-v1-experience.md`; this decision continues to own the
  underlying lens, entry, destination, and custody anatomy.
- The Together, refinding, arbitration, and contribution sequences have been
  accepted at 1:1 fixture scale by the 2026-09-01 behavior decision. That
  experience status does not imply implementation or usability proof.

- Seven Life primitives are registered provisional in the travel-app
  component catalog; `LifeChip`, the dossier organs, the masthead glue,
  the scroll and object surfaces, and the Vault are designed, unbuilt.
- Data contracts owed by the build are enumerated in the production spec
  §10 (deterministic pickers, artifact index, window invalidation, one
  counting service, person/group entity, chat-thread↔episode linkage).
- The four-root contract §6.4 is amended to match this decision.
- The do-not-resurrect list in the production spec §11 is binding.
- Deliberately deferred: motion specification (against the real build)
  and the dark canon (a kernel-level question).
