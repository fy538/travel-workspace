# Adopt the experiential-responsibility messaging hierarchy

## Context

The August product identity is settled: Vesper is personal and multiplayer
intelligence for the lived world. Recent product, multiplayer, brand, demo, and
fundraising work made the underlying product responsibility more legible:
existing products organize information, inventory, transactions, or
communication around an experience, while Vesper is organized around helping
the experience work for the people actually living it.

The current documents already contain this idea, but external openings still
lead with the abstract category and explain the responsibility afterward. That
ordering makes a new category harder to understand than necessary.

## Decision

The company identity does not change. The canonical external explanation now
follows this order:

```text
responsibility gap
  -> attention as the doorway
  -> person/place/moment/relationship context
  -> smallest worthwhile consequence and withdrawal
  -> private understanding composing into coherent shared action
  -> group-travel wedge
  -> category name and company ambition
  -> outcome-backed continuity
```

The preferred short company description is:

> **Vesper helps a person—and the people around them—turn whatever already has
> their attention into a real-world experience worth having.**

The preferred competitive translation is:

> **Other products organize information, inventory, transactions, or
> communication around an experience. Vesper is organized around helping the
> experience work for the people actually living it.**

“Personal and multiplayer intelligence for the lived world” remains the
canonical category and north-star label. It should follow a concrete behavior
when the audience does not already understand the phrase.

## Guardrails

- “Responsibility” describes the product's organizing objective and continuity
  across context, action, reality, and outcome. It does not promise a good
  outcome, replace a provider's authority, create legal or safety liability, or
  authorize action without consent.
- Multiplayer must be described as private understanding producing safe shared
  consequences while personal experience remains plural.
- Group travel remains the launch wedge and first demanding specialization, not
  the company boundary.
- The category, ambition, and moat remain hypotheses where the evidence ledger
  marks them as hypotheses. Code, founder dogfood, and document volume are not
  external traction.
- Release-facing copy remains constrained to behavior the submitted build can
  honestly demonstrate.

## Consequences

- [Investor Narrative and FAQ](../../travel-agent/docs/product/Investor%20Narrative%20and%20FAQ.md)
  owns the exact investor and outreach messaging stack.
- [Product Thesis](../../travel-agent/docs/product/Product%20Thesis.md) owns the
  underlying product meaning and may use the responsibility translation without
  becoming a script library.
- [Brand Identity](../../travel-app/docs/Brand%20Identity.md) owns tone and
  public expression; it should inherit the hierarchy without replacing the
  brand essence.
- Application, deck, demo, and app-store materials are derivatives. They may
  reorder or shorten the stack for their audience, but may not invent a second
  company definition.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit this decision if a concrete-input demonstration still leaves new users
unable to explain what Vesper does, if the responsibility language causes
material overclaiming or liability confusion, or if external evidence shows a
different product responsibility is more predictive of value.
