# Close whole-product V1 Wave 0

## Context

The August pivot produced a coherent product philosophy before it produced a
closed implementation contract. Home, Chat, Places, Life, the real-world
engine, contribution authority, generated expression, and multiplayer were
each explored deeply, but several working documents still treated root
promotion, Opening ownership, saved compositions, episode identity, navigation
return, and tool migration as open.

The first twelve agentic fixtures then exposed the cross-owner requirements of
Chat and the real-world engine. They did not adequately exercise quiet Home
value, everyday contribution, asynchronous social perception, post-return
re-entry, Life as an index, or compounding over time. Six surface-first cases
were therefore derived from the Nice, Sorrento, Capri, Amalfi, Rome, and New
York fieldwork.

Wave 0 closes product definition far enough to begin shared semantic and read
architecture. It does not freeze visual composition, activate runtime shadow,
authorize dogfooding, or claim product proof.

## Decision

Accept the eighteen-case whole-product portfolio in the [Whole-Product V1
Conformance Workbook](../working/whole-product-v1-conformance-workbook-2026-08-29.md)
as the representative architecture portfolio for V1.

The portfolio contains two complementary suites:

1. **A01–A12: agentic operation** — contribution, contextual entry, shaping,
   comparison, correction, group coordination, external consequence,
   monitoring, explanation, and return; and
2. **A13–A18: surface and compounding** — quiet Home value, everyday
   contribution, asynchronous social value, post-return re-entry, Life
   re-finding, and later causal reuse.

No single case, loop, Trip, surface, or implementation slice becomes the
product model merely because it ships first.

## 1. Fieldwork adjudication of A13–A18

### A13 — Quiet New York Home: accepted

The fieldwork explicitly says New York owns the next viewport after return and
that quiet should mean low coercion rather than low value. This case is the
user-invoked return on opening Home, not permission for ambient interruption.
Ignore creates no debt, preference, or unfinished work.

### A14 — Everyday contribution: accepted with Ask/Bring distinction

Menus, a pasta observation, a hotel bill, tickets, a film, a caption, and
ordinary practical questions prove that one transport can carry different
jobs. An attachment does not itself mean Bring. If the person is asking a
question, answer under T0 unless retention is separately justified. A
deliberate, clearly owned Bring may receive T1 source-bound private continuity
only after immediate value.

### A15 — Asynchronous social value: accepted with no new social owner

The friend-in-Paris juxtaposition and lightweight return Status support social
perception beyond coordination. Durable authority remains with Source custody,
author, audience/use grant, Place, Occasion, and any accepted consequence.
“Social contribution” is a projection role, not a universal owner. The value
must change an object, possibility, participation, or coordination; it does
not create an activity feed or response obligation.

### A16 — Post-return re-entry: accepted with practical recovery

The return included cancellation, rerouting, compensation, rescheduling, and
ordinary New York life—not only retrospective editorial material. Home is
current-life-first. Life owns complete episode and Source depth. Places owns
spatial continuation. Practical recovery may outrank both editorial synthesis
and forward possibility while it remains consequential.

### A17 — Life re-finding: accepted

The trip produced tickets, bills, photographs, menus, routes, Occasions,
provider receipts, uncertain associations, corrections, and plural Outcomes.
Time, Map, people, Sources, and Occasions must therefore be lenses over the
same identities. Planned-only, occurred, source-only, Mine, Together,
disputed, revoked, and unknown state remain legible without asking the person
to maintain folders or an ontology.

### A18 — Longitudinal compounding: accepted without forced root traversal

The Nice interactions often terminated after a useful answer; the product
opportunity is justified later reuse, not compulsory continuity. A fixture may
exercise all four roots when the causal chain warrants it, but no contribution
must traverse Home, Chat, Places, and Life. The pass condition is a materially
better later judgment, capability, coordination, action, or silence decision
with inspectable causal evidence and unchanged authority.

## 2. Resolved product rulings

### 2.1 Four roots

Home, Chat, Places, and Life are the accepted target navigation direction and
stable human orientations:

- **Home:** what matters now;
- **Chat:** what the person wants to give, understand, shape, change, or cause;
- **Places:** what the world affords here, now, or next, and why; and
- **Life:** what exists across the person's Vesper world and remains true and
  usable.

This does not claim that the current mobile shell conforms. Exact page anatomy,
visual hierarchy, and component composition remain experimental.

### 2.2 Opening ownership and promotion

An Opening is an ephemeral, expiring read assessment by default. It retains a
projection lineage to the evidence and conditions that justified it but is not
a durable owner.

An Opening becomes durable only through an explicit person action or an
existing narrow mandate accepted by a canonical owner. The target may be a
saved intention, Plan, Occasion, Commitment, deliberately saved/shared
composition, or later Outcome. Ranking, display, dwell, ignore, or model
confidence cannot promote it.

### 2.3 Generated composition lifecycle

Generated compositions are ephemeral by default. Explicit save or share
creates versioned manifest identity containing ingredient Source/Claim refs,
authorship roles, audience, generated time, model/process version where needed,
and correction lineage. Regeneration creates a new version; it does not mutate
the historical snapshot or turn generated language into evidence.

### 2.4 Life episode fallback

A Life episode is normally a viewer-relative read projection. A Journey,
Occasion, or another canonical domain may own stable return identity when it
passes the accepted entrance gates. Otherwise Life may assign a stable
projection key for re-finding without creating a universal episode writer.

### 2.5 Permission vocabulary

Use, Retention, Inference, Audience, and Action are the five normative axes.
May-name, may-show, may-contact, affected principals, named purpose, mandate
reference, threshold, protected consequence, delivery destination, expiry, and
reversibility are typed subfields. They are not alternate top-level authority
models or ad hoc enum values.

### 2.6 Direct UI and Chat

Use direct UI for one visible, familiar, low-risk operation or precise spatial
manipulation. Use Chat when intent is ambiguous, compositional, explanatory,
cross-owner, coordinative, monitored, or reparative. Both paths operate on the
same typed owner and selection state; Chat is not mandatory ceremony and the
UI is not an authority bypass.

### 2.7 Life and global settings

Life owns contextual Source inspection, provenance, correction, detachment,
scope change, release, and deletion entry. Global settings own connectors,
complete Source access, standing mandates, export, account-wide retention, and
destructive global scope. Both route mutations to canonical owners.

### 2.8 Home and Places placement

Home foregrounds cross-domain value because of **why now**. Places owns depth
because of **why here, there, or along this path**. The same object may project
into both while preserving identity: Home previews timely value; Places owns
spatial explanation, current affordance, alternatives, and exploration.

### 2.9 Social admission

Social material is admitted only when it materially changes the object,
understanding, possibility, participation, or coordination being rendered.
Authorship and audience appear inside that contribution. Vesper does not create
a generic friend-activity feed, synthetic group meaning, or reaction debt.

### 2.10 Monitor sequence

Define Monitor semantics now, but implement the durable Monitor owner after
owner-aware reads, Commitment semantics, command authority, Activity identity,
and delivery receipts are stable. A notification rail is not a Monitor owner,
and a transcript promise is not a durable job.

### 2.11 Trip migration

Keep proven Trip and itinerary readers, writers, CAS, provider, repair, and
receipt machinery behind semantic Plan/Commitment adapters until general
owners demonstrate parity. Change model-facing semantics before retiring
backend authority. No repository-wide noun migration is authorized.

### 2.12 Cross-root return

- Inspect depth returns to the exact origin object, scroll or map state, and
  composition revision when still valid.
- Chat receives typed entry context and returns to the origin or canonical
  owner after completion.
- Explicit root switches enter the root's stable workspace posture rather than
  replaying a stale overlay.
- Background completion targets the owner or active instrument through Home or
  Push according to delivery policy.
- A stale origin preserves object identity and explains that the prior
  projection changed.

## 3. Authority normalization for the fixture portfolio

Fixture vocabulary must use the canonical five axes and typed subfields.

The following free-form values are retired:

| Retired value | Normalized representation |
| --- | --- |
| `source_bound_private` | retention source `retained_private`; scoped governed Claim only if justified; audience `private` |
| `group_safe_effect_only` | Occasion audience plus minimum-safe projection rule; private Source remains excluded |
| `named_occasion` | audience `occasion` plus `occasion_id` |
| `affected_participants` | audience scope plus affected-principal refs |
| `commit_requested` | action `commit` or `propose` plus exact authority evidence |
| `existing_mandate` | action `mandate` plus mandate ref, scope, and authority evidence |
| `monitor_scope` | ordinary retention fields plus Monitor owner, purpose, expiry, and Stop |
| `threshold_only` | inference `current_job` plus typed threshold and protected consequence |
| `monitor_mandate` | action `mandate` plus Monitor owner and explicit authority source |

Opening projection lineage, Life projections, dependents, cards, instruments,
and generated prose are not mutation owners. A05 corrects the owning Outcome or
Occurrence relation and invalidates dependent projections. A03, A11, and A12
read Opening projection evidence without granting an Opening durable ownership.

## 4. Working-document disposition

The following earlier working claims are superseded by this decision:

- that the four-root direction still awaits product-level promotion;
- that Home, Chat, Places, and Life may be judged as independent product
  grammars;
- that an Opening owns durable pre-acceptance truth;
- that a Life projection or rendered Artifact may own correction;
- that generated composition becomes durable merely because it rendered;
- that root silence implies a low-value or empty surface;
- that Wave 0 requires live dogfood or runtime shadow evidence; and
- that the first implementation slice may define the whole ontology.

The following remain experimental rather than rejected:

- Home regions, card hierarchy, and density;
- Places page anatomy and map/list composition;
- Life lens naming and visual organization;
- Chat empty-state prompts and artifact treatment;
- detailed medium choice and visual language; and
- ranking and admission calibration.

## 5. Consequences

- Wave 0 is closed.
- Wave 1 may implement shared ResourceRef, entry/return, authority-source, and
  fixture contracts without waiting for visual design.
- A01–A18 govern architecture breadth; bounded implementation slices remain
  necessary but do not become product proof.
- Runtime shadow and dogfood remain off until the accepted surface-stability
  gate passes.
- Product experiments may falsify prioritization or treatment hypotheses. They
  do not silently rewrite ownership, authority, correction, or social safety.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit if several complete experiences require a fifth durable human
orientation; if ephemeral Opening lineage cannot support correction and
promotion without a durable owner; if saved compositions cannot preserve
authorship and correction without becoming evidence; or if direct UI and Chat
cannot operate on the same owner state without duplicated authority.
