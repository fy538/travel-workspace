# Adopt the Life continuity and return contract

## Context

The Life fixture and research work established four human jobs—Find, Return,
See anew, and Continue—and separated the stable record from the dynamic return
layer. It also exposed choices that cannot safely be left to ranking models or
later screen design:

- what earns an independent Life entrance;
- how much context a search result should reconstruct;
- whether leaving a shared Occasion erases historical access;
- how blocks, revocations, and depicted-person boundaries propagate;
- how source identity survives generated and edited compositions;
- what old material may enter Home or interrupt the person;
- what search behavior is retained;
- how deletion reaches derivatives; and
- how unresolved intentions become dormant or return.

Without one decision, a coherent-looking Life surface could still manufacture
occurrence, consensus, relationship state, autobiographical meaning, or action
authority.

## Decision

Life is Vesper's **governed continuity and return layer**. It does not own every
underlying object and is not a universal memory database. It compiles a
viewer-safe, repairable experience over Sources, Plans, Occasions, Occurrences,
Outcomes, Places, people, and generated compositions owned by their canonical
domains.

Its product contract is:

> Make accumulated life easy to find, return to, understand anew, and carry
> forward without turning existence into resurfacing, resurfacing into
> interruption, or relevance into authority.

Life preserves evidence and lineage; Home and Places deliver present
consequence. Life may make prior experience newly intelligible, but it does not
compete with Home for temporal attention or Places for current spatial depth.

## 1. Entrance eligibility

### 1.1 Stable doors use hard gates

An object or inferred episode becomes a stable Life door only when it passes:

1. governed existence;
2. return identity;
3. truth adequacy;
4. nonredundant return value;
5. stability;
6. viewer-safe plurality; and
7. repairability.

No weighted relevance score may compensate for a failed gate.

### 1.2 Minimum evidence for an inferred Journey

An explicitly authored Trip, Journey, Plan, or Occasion may provide return
identity directly. An inferred Journey may receive a neutral descriptive door
only when it has:

- at least two independently supported Occurrences connected by a material
  Place or time transition, or one supported multi-day episode with several
  independent evidence types;
- a bounded represented period;
- at least one human-authored or first-party Source anchor;
- no material unresolved contradiction about whether the episode occurred;
- a merge, split, rename, detach, and correction path; and
- value beyond the nearest existing container.

Its generated name stays descriptive—such as `Nice → Rome · Aug 14–27`—unless
the person explicitly authors a title. Vesper does not name inferred episodes
with emotional or identity interpretations.

### 1.3 Prior return is not required

A stable door does not require a prior successful return. That would make a
useful initial record undiscoverable and turn engagement into ontology. Prior
return may affect ordering inside an already eligible region; it never creates
truth or eligibility.

### 1.4 Pinning is access only

A pin creates a user-authored shortcut. It does not change semantic type,
truth, occurrence, ownership, audience, stability, or automatic-return policy.
The interface may say `Pinned`, but it should not expose an ontology lesson.

## 2. Re-finding and contextual reconstruction

### 2.1 Target first

Search returns the requested Source or governed answer first. It may attach an
**Around this** trail containing at most three default landmarks:

1. the parent episode or relationship;
2. the strongest preceding or causal landmark; and
3. the strongest following or consequence landmark.

The trail expands only on request or when the query explicitly asks for a
sequence or explanation. It must not become a recap around every known item.

### 2.2 Search refinements are ephemeral

Raw queries and reformulations remain session-local. Product recovery may keep
encrypted session state for at most 24 hours. Durable state requires an
explicit correction, saved result/query, named collection, authored statement,
or consequential action. Content-free aggregate diagnostics may persist; raw
query text must not become interest, emotion, relationship, or resurfacing
evidence.

## 3. Source identity and edited compositions

Compact reading exposes source role at the smallest unit where authorship or
epistemic operation materially changes:

- `You said` / `You noticed`;
- `You captured`;
- `[Person] contributed`;
- `The record shows`;
- `Vesper connected`;
- `Vesper suggests`;
- `External sources report`; or
- `Unresolved` / `Disputed`.

Repeated identical roles may be grouped at a paragraph, lane, or section.
Role changes, contested claims, and consequential claims remain visible
inline. Full lineage remains inspectable.

If a person substantially edits generated prose, the composition becomes
`Edited by you · based on a Vesper draft`. Human edits own the edited wording;
they do not retroactively make Vesper's inference or external claims
human-authored. Explicit publication may express endorsement of the rendered
composition, but it does not erase ingredient provenance.

## 4. Shared historical access and relationship safety

### 4.1 Leaving preserves a frozen historical shared core by default

When a participant leaves an occurred, ordinary social Occasion, they retain a
frozen projection of the shared core they were authorized to see during their
membership epoch. They do not receive later contributions, corrections beyond
what is necessary to prevent falsehood, current participant state, or new
generated social returns.

This default does not apply when:

- the Occasion was explicitly ephemeral or access-terminating;
- a Source author withdraws or revokes material;
- audience is narrowed;
- a block or safety boundary applies;
- law or platform safety requires removal; or
- continued access would expose current-sensitive information.

Leaving changes membership. It does not withdraw the leaver's prior
contributions, delete another participant's private history, or grant future
access. Rejoining or reconciliation creates a new epoch.

### 4.2 Social-periphery suppression is dependency-bounded

A block or no-contact boundary suppresses:

- direct person and relationship entrances;
- compositions, covers, snippets, reminders, suggestions, messages, and
  invitations whose value depends materially on the blocked relation;
- addressed social comparisons and friend-location/status treatments; and
- derived social-periphery paths that would predictably reintroduce the person.

It does not suppress independent Place facts, unrelated personal Occurrences,
or an episode that can remain coherent after the person's lane is removed.
The dependency graph, not mere co-occurrence, determines traversal.

### 4.3 Depicted-person control

A depicted person may remove their identity, face linkage, precise location,
and contribution from Vesper-managed shared, public, generated, and social
projections. This does not grant unilateral deletion of another person's
lawfully retained private original, but that private original cannot continue
to power managed social inference or resurfacing involving the depicted person.
Stronger legal and safety rights remain additive.

### 4.4 Death, minors, and inaccessible retention

Without an explicit posthumous directive, verified death freezes the account,
expands no audience, authorizes no synthetic first-person speech, and creates
no proactive relationship revival. Existing authorized historical projections
may remain only under their original grants and safety policy.

When a minor reaches the age of majority, external sharing, public projection,
managed generation for others, and new reuse of childhood contributions pause
until revalidated. Their private record remains available to them under the
applicable custody policy.

Legally retained but product-inaccessible data is represented only by an audit
tombstone. It is excluded from search, generation, ranking, return, export,
Continue, and ordinary product read paths.

### 4.5 Managed export

A managed export recompiles from currently authorized inputs. It closes when
the viewer is no longer authorized or when remaining inputs no longer support
a truthful, substantive rendering. Static downloads and screenshots cannot be
remotely revoked; the product must disclose that boundary before sensitive
sharing rather than promise impossible control.

## 5. Return and interruption

### 5.1 Home eligibility

Life material may enter Home automatically only as:

- a complete-value possibility supported by current context and current return
  authority; or
- an explicitly carried intention whose trigger is active.

It may not enter Home merely because it was kept, searched, emotionally
salient, recently generated, socially popular, or previously engaged with.
Retrospective push remains off by default.

### 5.2 Changed-world reopening

An old Plan may reopen as a possibility when:

- the entity or purpose still resolves;
- the prior state is unresolved, dormant, or explicitly kept—not rejected or
  completed;
- a material world condition has changed;
- current truth is refreshed;
- the result provides value before asking for input; and
- the treatment remains below reminder, commitment, or action authority.

### 5.3 Learning interruption preference

Explicit controls may narrow or expand return. Repeated dismissal may lower
ranking or create a reversible suggestion to suppress a class; it cannot
create consent, a trait, or broader interruption authority. Silence and
inactivity never expand authority.

## 6. Dormant intentions and returned consequences

Date-bound Plans become historical after their valid window unless explicitly
rescheduled. Condition-bound Plans re-evaluate only when the named condition
changes. Undated `someday` intentions become dormant and search/context-only by
default; they do not generate periodic reminders.

A destination action automatically returns only a content-free receipt and
canonical readback reference to the originating lineage. An Occurrence may
attach when supported by the domain owner. A personal or shared Outcome
requires a fresh authored or governed admission; action completion does not
manufacture meaning.

## 7. Deletion and derivative invalidation

All persisted, shared, action-bearing, or materially interpretive claims
require dependency lineage. Low-risk, unsaved display transformations may
remain ephemeral if they cannot be shared, cited, acted through, or reused as
evidence.

Deletion applies:

- an immediate logical tombstone and exclusion from reads, search, generation,
  ranking, return, and action;
- invalidation of online indexes, vectors, caches, thumbnails, snippets, and
  generated derivatives within 24 hours;
- closure or recompilation of managed exports on their next access; and
- exclusion from any backup restoration into product paths, with backup expiry
  targeted within 30 days unless a documented legal requirement prevents it.

Independent evidence may continue to support an independent claim. Generated
paraphrase is not independent evidence.

## 8. Value realization

### 8.1 Life's visible dividends and cross-root placement

Life visibly owns four primary dividends:

1. **cognitive relief** through retrieval and reconstruction;
2. **representational gain** through better organization than the contributing
   source systems provide;
3. **experiential return** through faithful, bounded re-entry; and
4. **accumulated understanding** whose evidence, limits, and lineage remain
   inspectable.

Life may preserve capability, relational, and possibility-bearing evidence,
but it does not foreground every later application. A current cross-domain
consequence belongs in Home. A current spatial application belongs in Places.
User-directed interrogation, authorship, and repair belong in Chat. The compact
law is:

> **Life holds the evidence and lineage. Home and Places deliver the
> consequence.**

### 8.2 Stable orientation with bounded adaptive returns

Life combines a stable period-and-episode spine with at most a small, finite
set of earned adaptive entrances. Eligible treatments include `Continue where
you left off`, `Newly useful`, `Recently completed`, `From someone you know`,
and `Worth returning to` when the specific item already passes its own value,
authority, and resurfacing gates.

The adaptive layer may change ordering among eligible Returns. It may not
reorder record truth, manufacture a stable door, become infinite scroll, or
optimize for consumption. A person may suppress the adaptive layer while
retaining search and stable access.

### 8.3 Operation-specific evidence gates

Evidence sufficiency is claim- and operation-local rather than one corpus-size
threshold:

| Operation | Minimum admission condition |
| --- | --- |
| **Recover** | One governed Source or owner fact supports the returned item. |
| **Reconstruct** | An explicit governed container, or at least two supported Occurrences joined by a material transition; a multi-day inferred episode also needs heterogeneous evidence. |
| **Understand** | At least two independent evidence items support a new relation or mechanism; consequential or outside claims expose grounding and limits. |
| **Explore** | One reliable bridge plus current or external grounding may be sufficient; the bridge does not become a durable interest from one exposure. |
| **Carry** | A supported prior Outcome or authored observation, a current target, a shared mechanism, and at least one material difference are all explicit. |
| **Together** | At least one attributable, authorized human contribution is relevant to another person or bounded group job; co-presence alone is insufficient. |
| **Persistent pattern** | Repetition spans distinct contexts and remains correctable; multiple traces from one event do not establish a trait. |

The evidence ladder is `Artifact -> episode -> pattern -> transferable
capability`. Usefulness may begin at the first level; claim strength must not
jump ahead of it.

### 8.4 Structural analogy and transfer language

Every transfer treatment makes three things legible:

1. the shared mechanism;
2. the material difference; and
3. the bounded present use.

Plain-language treatments use `This may be relevant because...`, `What differs
here is...`, and `The useful part to carry forward is...`. They expose the
prior and current Sources at a glance. They do not convert the analogy into a
personality claim, omit disconfirming structure, or present similarity as
identity.

### 8.5 Experiential return as complete value

Experiential return does not require insight, action, reflection, or sharing.
Faithful reconstruction, an evidence-bearing juxtaposition, attributed social
recollection, sensory return, ritual return, or a delightful factual pattern
may end on view. Generic totals, recap language, engagement bait, and prompts
that hand interpretation back to the person do not qualify.

The test is whether the composition lets the person re-enter something they
lived or see its evidence in a more useful form—not whether it reports their
data back to them.

### 8.6 Progressive resurfacing control

Controls begin on the surfaced unit with neutral, non-diagnostic choices:

- `Useful`;
- `Not now`;
- `Keep searchable`;
- `Less like this`; and
- `Never surface this`.

Only repeated explicit dismissal may justify offering a one-tap scoped rule,
such as fewer unsolicited returns from a Journey, person, Place, or return
type. Life may provide a deeper control center grouped by people, periods,
Places, return types, delivery surfaces, and notifications. Silence, dwell,
response time, and non-use do not create an emotional or preference claim.

### 8.7 Minimum transactive-memory proof

The smallest social proof is not a post or comparison feed. It is one
authorized contribution from one person becoming a useful route to that
person's knowledge in a later bounded situation. Vesper may say that a person
may know about the present question, show the attributable contribution that
supports the route, and let the viewer inspect it or open a human-authored
request. It does not impersonate the contributor or infer expertise from
co-presence alone.

A proof fixture needs two people, one authorized contribution, one later
relevant situation, one human choice to view, use, or contact, and an observable
benefit. This tests **relationship as an intelligent index**, not social content
volume.

### 8.8 Negative outcomes and failed transfer

Negative Outcomes remain context-specific boundaries rather than scores,
mistake recaps, or identity lessons. A useful treatment states what happened,
what was structurally different, and what to protect now. Failed analogy is
valid evidence that a mechanism does not carry. The treatment appears only
when it reduces current risk or work and retains a correction path for the
supposed cause.

### 8.9 The `Carried forward` causal receipt

When a prior contribution materially improves a later experience, Vesper may
create a compact `Carried forward` receipt containing:

1. the prior Source, Outcome, or governed episode;
2. the present suggestion, preparation, or changed consequence;
3. the resulting Occurrence or Outcome when known; and
4. a path to inspect, correct, or stop using the connection.

The receipt first appears at the consequence-owning surface—Home, Places,
Plan, or Occasion—and remains inspectable in Life under the applicable episode
or `What carried` return. Group-visible wording exposes only group-safe reasons;
private influences remain privately inspectable. The receipt proves causal
compounding without claiming that Vesper caused meaning or success.

### 8.10 Honest cold start

Life does not fabricate richness before governed history exists. At zero
evidence it explains its continuity, custody, correction, audience, and
deletion contract without asking the person to journal or complete a profile.
Cold-start value belongs to the product loop, not to every root independently.

The first contribution should create immediate representational gain: a ticket
becomes an accurate searchable Artifact, several supported items may propose a
Journey, a Place link retains useful context, or an authorized contribution
appears in its proper Occasion. Life shows the result and its owner; it does not
ask for reflection as the price of persistence. Richer episode, pattern,
transfer, and social treatments remain absent until their respective gates
pass.

## Consequences

- Life index and episode design must expose posture, truth, scope, and repair
  without showing a policy dashboard by default.
- Life must retain a stable spine beneath a finite adaptive Returns layer.
- Home, Places, and Chat must receive a typed Continue envelope and recheck
  current authority.
- Cross-root projections that use prior experience need inspectable causal
  lineage; material later improvement may emit a `Carried forward` receipt.
- Together must compile a frozen historical shared core, attributed lanes, and
  plural Outcomes for the current viewer and epoch.
- Search must return deterministic target and authority before optional
  generation.
- Current implementation remains unproven; this decision authorizes behavioral
  specification and read-model derivation, not new universal tables.

## Revisit triggers

Revisit when user testing shows the frozen historical shared core violates
reasonable expectations; three-landmark search context systematically fails
orientation; deletion targets cannot be met by the deployed infrastructure;
or the six fixtures require a fifth control plane or a new durable owner.
