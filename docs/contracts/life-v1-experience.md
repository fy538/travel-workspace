# Life v1 experience contract

## The promise

**Life turns what Vesper has held from living into something the person can
find, understand, and use again.**

It is the continuity root of Home · Chat · Places · Life. It is neither a feed
nor a passive archive. Home and Places deliver current openings; Chat receives
and operates; Life keeps the governed record, reveals shape across it, and
provides durable ways back in.

The test is not “did we store it?” The test is **“did living with Vesper make
the record more useful later, without giving the person filing work?”**

## The one corpus and its four lenses

Life has one corpus viewed through four first-class lenses:

- **Time** — periods, journeys, occasions, and episodes in sequence.
- **Places** — the person's history with places and place clusters.
- **Threads** — supported lines of attention that cross more than one isolated
  moment; never projects that demand completion.
- **People** — viewer-relative shared records, not dossiers about people or
  inferred intimacy rankings.

Changing a lens changes the exploration, not the underlying object. The same
object identity, authority, lineage, and correction state follows everywhere.

## The production root at rest

Every lens uses the same finite anatomy:

1. **Speaking header** — LIFE, labeled lens selector, Search, Everything kept,
   and one earned descriptive line or a factual statline. No inferred emotion,
   identity claim, or synthetic first-person voice.
2. **Digest** — four to five deterministic record blocks. Expanded entries are
   uncarded groups; collapsed entries are rows. The page must scan by section
   bars alone.
3. **One full-record door** — an honest route into the complete history for the
   selected lens, with a count or span.
4. **Returns, zero to three** — record-native reconstruction, explanation,
   differentiation, or re-entry. Each Return provides complete value before
   the tap and names a material reason. The region disappears when nothing
   qualifies.
5. **Reflections, zero to two** — a keepsake or specimen only when the evidence
   itself warrants one.
6. **Windows, zero to two** — fixed, record-driven doors into objects from other
   lenses. They change when the record or authority changes, never to manufacture
   novelty.
7. **Everything kept** and the tab bar.

There is no global Map utility on the v1 Life root. A map appears contextually
inside a journey, place cluster, or place dossier when spatial structure helps
the person understand that object. “Everything kept” is custody; it is not a
fifth lens.

## The coherent v1 system slice

Life v1 is not one isolated behavior loop. It is the smallest coherent system
that demonstrates the philosophy across the root and its consequences:

1. A real Life root can sit calmly at rest with a rich, ordinary, thin, or
   yielded record.
2. Search can recover a held thing and open the canonical object.
3. At least one record-native Return can add genuine understanding without
   repeating the person's input.
4. A shared contribution can live inside the same record with visible
   attribution and audience boundaries.
5. A current consequence can move to Home or Places while Life yields and
   preserves continuity.
6. A correction or withdrawal can repair every dependent rendering.

Removing any one of these reduces Life either to an archive, a feed, a search
screen, or a demo card. Together they establish the product.

## Entry and destination grammar

- A root entry is a **door**, not the whole object.
- Every door opens a **dossier** for a journey, episode, thread, person-shared
  record, hosted occasion, or place relationship.
- A dossier contains the complete bounded object: what happened, what was
  planned, sources and claims, people and grants, the shape Vesper can support,
  and doors to all evidence.
- Miniatures below the dossier use one compact chip grammar. Chips never become
  a competing card taxonomy.
- Search results carry their containment chain and open the same dossier.
- Contextual continuation into Chat carries the object handle and visible
  context; the person does not restate the subject.

## Returns: the value bar

A Return earns root space only when it does at least one of these:

- **Reconstructs** something difficult to see from individual artifacts.
- **Explains** a mechanism or context with support beyond the person's own note.
- **Differentiates** two things that looked similar or links things the person
  had not already connected.
- **Transfers** an authorized insight into a materially relevant possibility.
- **Re-enters** a held object with a useful new fact, source, or question already
  answered as far as the evidence allows.

A recap, poetic paraphrase, personality interpretation, generic prompt, or
“you noticed X” does not qualify. The title must contain the new distinction;
the substance must be useful before the tap. A door may deepen value but cannot
be required to discover what the row is offering.

Among substantially overlapping candidates, one active present-delivery seat
exists across roots. Home or Places wins when the value is current or spatial;
Life yields without residue. The dossier and search path remain.

## Contribution, Together, and repair

- Chat delivers immediate value before asking to keep anything.
- No classification, filing, profile construction, or “finish setting this up”
  is required from the person.
- A receipt appears only after durable state changed. It states what changed,
  where it went, the audience, and Correct/Undo where applicable.
- Placement follows authored meaning. A restaurant photograph offered as “the
  texture I meant” may nest in an existing pasta question; it does not become a
  rating, preference, or new thread.
- Another person's contribution is attributed wherever it materially affects
  output. Use, retention, inference, audience, and action authority remain
  independently enforceable.
- Withdrawal recompiles dependent output; blocking changes visibility without
  rewriting ownership or independent history.
- Corrections mutate the canonical relation or claim once, then repair search,
  dossiers, Returns, and current projections that depended on it.

## Root states

### Rich

Four to five digest blocks, one full-record door, one or two earned Returns,
and at most two fixed windows. Rich does not mean dense; it means the record
supports several distinct ways back in.

### Ordinary

Recent local periods, a few people or places, perhaps one thread, and no need
for travel spectacle. The same grammar holds.

### Zero-record

When no governed object exists anywhere in the Life corpus, the normal
masthead, four labeled lenses, and bottom tabs remain. The root may carry one
frameless threshold illustration and one indicative reassurance sentence. It
contains no CTA, prompt, example gallery, progress, or category-completion
target. This is a root-level exception for a genuinely empty corpus, not a
placeholder treatment for individual lenses or conditional organs. It yields
to the first real governed object.

### Thin

One or two honest entries, one full-record door if a history exists, and no
placeholder Returns. The page never asks the person to enrich it.

### Yielded

The root remains complete while a current consequence is active in Home or
Places. The corresponding Return is absent; no “see this in Home” shell or
empty module remains.

## Visual and interaction law

- No cards on Life root pages. Hierarchy comes from paper, section bars, type,
  spacing, hairlines, and bounded marks.
- No infinite feed, pull-to-refresh novelty, engagement rotation, or inbox.
- Labeled lens choices are always visible at the top. The condensed facet may
  switch lenses while scrolled; horizontal page swipe does not.
- One disclosure chevron means open. Gold text and one arrow mean a door.
- Oxblood is reserved for a live threshold, not destructive actions.
- Held state is implicit. Status marks appear only for meaningful exceptions
  such as planned, live, unresolved, disputed, withdrawn, or lapsed.
- Loading, error, offline, partial-authority, and empty states preserve the same
  page anatomy and never fabricate completeness. Missing conditional organs
  remain air. A nonempty corpus does not render illustrated empty-lens modules;
  a genuinely empty lens may use at most one plain indicative sentence.

## V1 cut

Ship in v1:

- Dedicated Life root replacing the legacy Atlas presentation behind a flag.
- Time lens first, with the four-lens contract and navigation structure intact.
- Canonical dossiers for the object kinds actually rendered.
- Everything kept custody path.
- Deterministic search/refinding with honest capability limits.
- One record-native Return family plus cross-root seat arbitration.
- One attributed shared-record path and causal correction/withdrawal repair.

Defer without weakening the contract:

- Hero or performed photographic composition.
- A global Life map.
- Learned ranking or engagement optimization.
- Generalized Occasion architecture or repository-wide noun migration.
- Full semantic recovery beyond the validated dark lane.
- Every possible object renderer; unsupported kinds degrade to a truthful
  dossier and source list.

## Acceptance contract

Life v1 is build-ready only when:

- the root is useful without a new contribution;
- every visible count reconciles with Everything kept;
- every result and projection opens the same object identity;
- every Return passes authority, truth, novelty, trigger, and freshness gates;
- present-delivery duplication across roots is zero;
- Ask performs no silent write;
- withdrawal and correction remove or repair all dependent projections before
  the next read;
- thin and yielded states contain no prompts or filler;
- mobile types come from the committed OpenAPI projection, not a handwritten
  mirror;
- real-device accessibility, touch, loading, partial, and failure states pass.
